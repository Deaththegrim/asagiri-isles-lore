Skills

The arts of the Asagiri Isles: what each covers and how hard things are.

archery

The bow, mounted or on foot, and the stillness it takes. A basic success hits what was aimed at; a great success hits the gap in the armour; a failure is an arrow wasted and a position given away.

Covers: the bow on foot or horseback, at a target, a fleeing rider or an enemy on the walls. Not: thrown weapons, which are hidden arms. Easy: hit a straw target at thirty paces. Medium: hit a running deer. Hard: hit a rider's gap in the armour from the saddle. Very hard: hit a tengu in flight at night. Extremely hard: hit the seal on a beast's brow through a storm.

Primary Attribute: Will

artifice

Locks, traps, mechanisms and machines: picking a lock, setting or springing a trap, keeping a foreign musket loaded and firing, rigging a bridge to fall. A basic success works it; a great success works it silently and leaves no sign; a failure jams it or springs it on yourself. The Artisan abilities roll it for crafting at a workshop or workbench.

Covers: locks, traps, mechanisms, clockwork, foreign firearms' loading and upkeep, rigging a door or a bridge. Not: smithing, which is forging, and puppets, which are puppetry. Easy: open a storehouse lock. Medium: set a snare that holds a man. Hard: pick a castle strongroom lock. Very hard: disarm a hidden village's trap corridor. Extremely hard: keep a foreign musket firing in the rain.

Primary Attribute: Guile

artistry

Kagura dance, music, poetry, calligraphy, tea: the arts the courts and shrines read people by. A basic success pleases; a great success moves a lord or a spirit; a failure is a public embarrassment.

Covers: dance, music, poetry, calligraphy and tea. Not: talismans and wards, which are warding. Easy: play a tea house song. Medium: write a poem a lord keeps. Hard: dance the kagura at a festival. Very hard: move the regent's court to tears. Extremely hard: dance a spirit to rest.

Primary Attribute: Will

awareness

Noticing with the senses: the ambush in the reeds, the scratch by the lock, the thing out of place in a searched room. A basic success notices it in time; a great success notices what the others missed and why it matters; a failure is a surprise.

Covers: noticing with the senses: ambushes, small details, searching a room, the physical evidence a scene leaves. Not: feeling Soul, which is sensing, and following a trail, which is tracking. Easy: spot a pickpocket in a market. Medium: find the loose board in an inn room. Hard: see the ambush before the arrows. Very hard: notice the guard who is not a guard. Extremely hard: find what a shinobi hid in a searched room.

Primary Attribute: Guile

beast-bond

Partnership with an animal or a lesser yokai: wolves, hawks, insect hives, a stubborn horse. A basic success gets the beast to do it; a great success fights as one with it; a failure is a beast that chose its own way.

Covers: partnership with animals and lesser yokai: wolves, hawks, hives, horses. Not: commanding summoned familiars, which is summoning. Easy: calm a frightened horse. Medium: send a hawk with a message. Hard: fight back to back with your wolf. Very hard: turn a hive on an army camp. Extremely hard: befriend a yokai beast in its own wood.

Primary Attribute: Will

blade

Swords, spears, naginata: anything with an edge on a haft or a hilt. A basic success lands the cut or turns one; a great success disarms, breaks a guard or ends the exchange; a failure leaves an opening a good fighter takes.

Covers: swords, spears, naginata and every edged weapon on a hilt or a haft, in a duel or a melee. Not: thrown blades and wire, which are hidden arms. Easy: cut down a bandit with a rusty spear. Medium: hold a samurai retainer to a draw. Hard: beat a house's sword master on Dawn Bridge. Very hard: cut a technique in half mid-flight. Extremely hard: win a duel against an awakened soul-blade's bearer.

Primary Attribute: Body

breathcraft

The breath hunters' forms: a breath drawn in a set way that pushes Soul through the body for one impossible movement. A basic success holds the form for a strike; a great success chains forms; a failure leaves the lungs empty at the worst moment.

Covers: breath forms that push Soul through the body for impossible movement. Not: raw Soul techniques, which are soulcraft. Easy: hold a breath form for one strike. Medium: cut faster than a samurai's draw. Hard: chain three forms in a fight. Very hard: fight a demon lord's general a full night. Extremely hard: a form no master has seen.

Primary Attribute: Body

command

Leading people in the open: rallying a broken line, giving orders a crew obeys, making an honest offer stick at a negotiation. A basic success is heard and followed; a great success turns a rout or a room; a failure is an order nobody takes.

Covers: rallying, leading troops and crews, open persuasion, negotiating terms and giving orders that are obeyed. Not: courtly manoeuvring, which is intrigue, and lies, which are deception. Easy: get a ferry crew rowing. Medium: talk a frightened village into barring its gates. Hard: rally ashigaru who are breaking. Very hard: turn a bandit band against its chief. Extremely hard: hold the Long Night Wall's line through a demon charge.

Primary Attribute: Will

cursecraft

Grudges given shape: laying, carrying, turning and feeding curses, and paying their price. A basic success lays a small curse or turns one; a great success lays a heavy one or breaks it; a failure turns it on the caster.

Covers: laying, carrying, turning, feeding and breaking curses. Not: purification and exorcism, which are warding. Easy: lay a minor ill-luck. Medium: turn a curse back on its caster. Hard: carry a curse that would kill another. Very hard: break a family curse three generations old. Extremely hard: lay a curse on a province.

Primary Attribute: Will

deception

Lies, false faces, misdirection, the feint in a fight and the forged pass at a barrier. A basic success is believed; a great success is believed and repeated; a failure is remembered.

Covers: lies, false faces, forged papers, feints and misdirection. Not: moving unseen, which is stealth. Easy: talk past an inn's bill. Medium: pass a toll barrier on forged papers. Hard: pose as a house retainer for a day. Very hard: convince a Veil Officer you are already dead. Extremely hard: deceive the regent to his face.

Primary Attribute: Guile

divination

Star reading, lot casting, omens and lucky days: seeing the shape of what is coming. A basic success reads the obvious omen; a great success names the hour and the danger; a failure is a confident wrong prophecy.

Covers: star reading, lots, omens and lucky days. Not: reading people, which is intrigue. Easy: name tomorrow's weather. Medium: read an ill omen in a birth. Hard: name the day an ambush will come. Very hard: read the regent's fate in the stars. Extremely hard: see the hour a sealed beast will break.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

footwork

Running, climbing, leaping roof to roof, water-walking, balance on a branch: getting there. A basic success arrives; a great success arrives first and unseen; a failure is a fall.

Covers: running, climbing, leaping roof to roof, water-walking and balance. Not: staying unseen while moving, which is stealth. Easy: outrun a toll guard. Medium: cross Miyako's roofs at night. Hard: water-walk across the lake. Very hard: climb Tengu Peak's face. Extremely hard: outrun a demon across the night valleys.

Primary Attribute: Body

forging

Smithing, sharpening, repairing armour and weapons, and at the masters' anvils, folding Soul into steel. A basic success holds; a great success is better than new; a failure cracks the blade. The Artisan abilities roll it for crafting at a forge, anvil or smithy.

Covers: smithing, sharpening, repairing arms and armour, and folding Soul into steel. Not: binding a spirit into a blade, which is sealwork. Easy: sharpen a dull blade. Medium: repair a cracked helmet on the road. Hard: forge a sword a samurai would carry. Very hard: fold Soul into a blade so it wakes. Extremely hard: reforge a broken soul-blade without losing its spirit.

Primary Attribute: Body

hidden-arms

Thrown blades, needles, wire, chain-sickle, poison on an edge: the weapons a guard never sees. A basic success hits or binds; a great success disables before the target knows; a failure shows the hand.

Covers: kunai, needles, shuriken, wire, chain-sickle, blowpipe and poisoned edges. Not: swords and spears, which are blade. Easy: pin a sleeve to a post. Medium: put a needle in a guard's neck. Hard: bind a running man in wire. Very hard: hit three lanterns in the dark with one throw. Extremely hard: kill through a ward's gap without breaking it.

Primary Attribute: Guile

horsemanship

Riding, mounted combat, the long gallop between post stations. A basic success stays in the saddle and arrives; a great success fights or shoots from it as if on foot; a failure is a thrown rider.

Covers: riding, mounted combat and long rides between post stations. Not: archery itself, which is archery. Easy: ride a post stage. Medium: fight from the saddle. Hard: outride a samurai patrol. Very hard: jump a toll barrier on horseback. Extremely hard: ride the Fifty Stations in five days.

Primary Attribute: Body

intrigue

Courts, clans and houses: etiquette, debts, who owes whom and who will betray whom. A basic success reads the room; a great success moves it; a failure makes an enemy who smiles.

Covers: courts, clans and houses: etiquette, debts, alliances and betrayals. Not: underworld dealings, which are streetwise. Easy: know which cup to drink first at a lord's table. Medium: learn who owes whom in a samurai house. Hard: turn a clan elder against a rival. Very hard: broker a truce between Kagegi and Hayate. Extremely hard: unseat the regent's chief retainer.

Primary Attribute: Guile

lore

The isles' histories, the clans and houses, yokai and their rules, curses and how they were made. A basic success recalls what is commonly known; a great success recalls what was buried; a failure is a confident wrong answer.

Covers: histories, clans and houses, yokai and their rules, curses and how they were made. Not: the living politics of a court, which is intrigue. Easy: name a samurai house's crest. Medium: know which yokai a fox shrine serves. Hard: recall how the last war's curse wells were made. Very hard: name the beast in the Sealing Vaults by its old title. Extremely hard: recall the Veil Court's founding pact.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

medicine

Field medicine, herbs, poisons and antidotes, setting bones, and Soul-led healing for the five healer lines. A basic success stabilises; a great success heals beyond the herbs; a failure leaves the wound worse. The Artisan abilities roll it for crafting at an apothecary, still or kitchen.

Covers: field medicine, herbs, poisons and antidotes, setting bones and Soul-led healing for the five healer lines. Not: purifying curses, which is warding. Easy: clean and bind a cut. Medium: set a broken arm on the road. Hard: name a Kirisame poison by its smell. Very hard: keep a gutted man alive to the next station. Extremely hard: draw a curse-rot out of living flesh.

Primary Attribute: Will

might

Raw strength: lifting, carrying, breaking, forcing a door, holding a gate, dragging a cart out of the mud. A basic success moves it; a great success moves what takes three people; a failure is a pulled back or a door that holds.

Covers: lifting, breaking, carrying, forcing doors, holding a gate, raw strength feats. Not: fighting bare-handed, which is open-hand, and endurance, which is stamina. Easy: carry a wounded friend a mile. Medium: force a barred door. Hard: lift a cart off a trapped porter. Very hard: hold a castle gate against a ram. Extremely hard: move a fallen torii stone alone.

Primary Attribute: Body

mindwalk

Entering another's mind through Soul: reading surface thoughts, planting a false memory, walking a dream. A basic success hears what they think now; a great success moves or hides something in there; a failure lets them into yours.

Covers: reading, moving and hiding things in another's mind, and walking dreams. Not: sensing Soul strength and kind, which is sensing. Easy: hear a surface thought. Medium: plant a small doubt. Hard: walk a sleeping guard's dream. Very hard: hide a memory from a trained shinobi. Extremely hard: hold a mind against a clan elder's entry.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

open-hand

Fists, feet, throws, joint locks and the body as a weapon. A basic success lands or escapes; a great success drops, pins or throws; a failure is a grapple lost.

Covers: fists, feet, throws, holds and the body as a weapon. Not: edged weapons, which are blade. Easy: put a drunk on the floor. Medium: throw an armoured guard. Hard: break a shinobi's hold. Very hard: fight an oni barehanded and stand. Extremely hard: stop a blade-strike with bare hands.

Primary Attribute: Body

puppetry

Puppets of wood, steel and wire moved by threads of Soul: fighting dolls, decoys, the hidden blade in a lacquered arm. A basic success moves the puppet as meant; a great success makes it act like a living thing; a failure tangles the threads in the middle of the fight.

Covers: puppets of wood, steel and wire moved by Soul threads. Not: binding spirits into objects, which is sealwork. Easy: make a puppet bow to a lord. Medium: fight a guard with a puppet from cover. Hard: run two puppets in one fight. Very hard: pass a puppet off as a living person for an evening. Extremely hard: fight ten puppets at once.

Primary Attribute: Guile

resolve

Holding steady: fear, pain, a curse's pull, a vow's limit, the thing inside. A basic success holds; a great success holds and steadies others; a failure gives ground.

Covers: holding steady against fear, pain, a curse's pull, a vow's limit and the thing inside. Not: physical endurance, which is stamina. Easy: keep your nerve in a haunted inn. Medium: stand your ground before an oni. Hard: refuse a curse's whisper for a night. Very hard: keep your vow with your life on the line. Extremely hard: hold your Hold at zero for one more breath.

Primary Attribute: Will

seafaring

Boats, ships, tides and weather at sea, and swimming when it goes wrong. A basic success makes the crossing; a great success makes it through a storm or a blockade; a failure is the sea's.

Covers: boats, ships, tides, sea weather and swimming. Not: the strait ferries' fares, which are the post road's. Easy: row across a harbour. Medium: sail a junk along the coast. Hard: cross Ember Strait in a gale. Very hard: run a merchant blockade. Extremely hard: cross White Strait in winter.

Primary Attribute: Body

sealwork

Hand seals, seal formulae, binding marks: Soul written into a fixed shape that holds. A basic success makes the seal; a great success makes it fast or makes it hold against something strong; a failure is a seal that breaks at the wrong moment.

Covers: hand seals, seal formulae, binding marks and the barriers they make. Not: talismans and purification, which are warding. Easy: seal a letter so only one hand opens it. Medium: make a barrier hold for a breath. Hard: bind a lesser yokai in a jar. Very hard: re-seal a cracked beast seal in a host. Extremely hard: seal a great beast into a living body.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

sensing

Feeling Soul: who is near, how strong, what kind, whether a mask or a beast hides behind a face, and where a presence came from. A basic success notices; a great success reads it exactly; a failure misses the thing in the room.

Covers: feeling Soul: presence, strength, kind, what hides behind a face, and the Soul a presence leaves behind (where it came from, how fresh, which way it went). Not: reading lies and motives, which is intrigue. Easy: feel someone in the next room. Medium: tell a shinobi from a farmer by their Soul. Hard: find a masked spirit in a crowd. Very hard: feel a sealed beast through its host's skin. Extremely hard: track a single Soul across a province.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

shapeshifting

A yokai's change of shape: a fox's illusion, an oni's horns grown, a cat's body, a tanuki's disguise. A basic success holds the shape; a great success fools those who know; a failure slips at the wrong moment.

Covers: a yokai-blooded body's change of shape and the illusions it casts. Not: disguise with clothes and paint, which is deception. Easy: grow ears or a tail at will. Medium: pass as a stranger for an hour. Hard: take a beast's shape in a fight. Very hard: fool a Veil Officer's sense. Extremely hard: hold a dragon's shape.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

soulcraft

Shaping raw Soul into force: a release, an elemental technique, a strike carried by the current. A basic success does what the technique says; a great success does more than it should; a failure spends the Soul and shows the shape.

Covers: shaping raw Soul into force: elemental techniques, releases, strikes carried by the current. Not: fixed seals and formulae, which are sealwork. Easy: light a lamp with a spark. Medium: throw a fist of wind across a room. Hard: hold a technique against an equal's. Very hard: break a fortress gate with one release. Extremely hard: turn a battlefield's weather.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

stamina

Enduring the road, the cold of the north, hunger, a long fight, a poison's slow work. A basic success endures; a great success endures and keeps others going; a failure is a collapse at the wrong time.

Covers: enduring the road, the northern cold, hunger, a long fight and a slow poison. Not: fear and curses, which are resolve. Easy: walk two post stages without rest. Medium: cross the Blight without water. Hard: fight through a night of the hunt. Very hard: survive a night on the Hyoga glacier. Extremely hard: march Fifty Stations in ten days.

Primary Attribute: Body

stealth

Moving unseen and unheard: shadows, crowds, a disguise held for a day. A basic success passes the careless; a great success passes the trained; a failure is a raised alarm.

Covers: moving unseen and unheard, shadows, crowds and a disguise held for a day. Not: lies spoken aloud, which are deception. Easy: slip past a sleepy gatekeeper. Medium: cross a samurai mansion's garden. Hard: pass Kagegi's outer checkpoint. Very hard: walk unseen through the regent's keep. Extremely hard: pass the Sealing Vaults' wardens.

Primary Attribute: Guile

streetwise

Ports, gambling dens, bandit roads and black markets: finding the fence, the bounty, the boat that asks no questions. A basic success finds it; a great success finds it cheap; a failure finds you.

Covers: ports, gambling dens, bandit roads and black markets. Not: court politics, which is intrigue. Easy: find a cheap inn in Saikai. Medium: find a fence for a stolen blade. Hard: get a boat that asks no questions. Very hard: buy passage with the bandit federation. Extremely hard: find the society that trades in sealed beasts.

Primary Attribute: Guile

summoning

Calling and commanding familiars: paper shikigami, contracted spirits, a bound animal from far away. A basic success calls a lesser servant; a great success calls a strong one that obeys; a failure calls one that does not.

Covers: calling and commanding paper familiars and contracted spirits. Not: animal partners, which is beast-bond. Easy: fold a paper bird that carries a note. Medium: call a shikigami to fight beside you. Hard: call two at once in a fight. Very hard: call a contracted spirit across a province. Extremely hard: bind a wild yokai into a contract mid-fight.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

tracking

Following a trail by sign, scent and broken twig; hunting man or beast. A basic success finds the trail; a great success finds the quarry before it knows; a failure loses it or walks into it.

Covers: following trails by sign, scent and broken ground; hunting. Not: feeling Soul, near or left behind, which is sensing. Easy: follow a cart's ruts. Medium: track a deer through the wood. Hard: track a shinobi across rooftops. Very hard: track a demon to its nest at night. Extremely hard: track one man across the Blight a week late.

Primary Attribute: Guile

warding

Talismans, purification, exorcism, wards on a door or a person. A basic success keeps a lesser thing out; a great success drives a greater one back; a failure is a ward that something has now tasted. The Artisan abilities roll it for writing talismans at a shrine or writing desk.

Covers: talismans, purification, exorcism and wards on doors, places and people. Not: binding formulae, which are sealwork. Easy: ward a room for a night. Medium: drive off a hungry ghost. Hard: exorcise a possessed farmer. Very hard: hold a shrine against a masked spirit's tide. Extremely hard: purify a curse well.

Primary Attribute: Will

wildcraft

Living off the land: foraging, shelter, fire, reading weather and ground, finding the way where no road runs. A basic success eats and sleeps dry; a great success leads a party through the wild faster than the road; a failure is a cold night or a wrong valley.

Covers: foraging, shelter, fire, reading weather and land, finding the way off the road. Not: hunting a quarry, which is tracking, and enduring cold, which is stamina. Easy: find water on a hillside. Medium: build a dry shelter in rain. Hard: read a typhoon a day before it lands. Very hard: lead a party through the Ayakashi Wood without a path. Extremely hard: keep a party alive a week on the Hyoga ice.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
