Crater Rim Shrine, The Rim of Many Blades

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 33-40 · Danger: Severe]

The crater rim shrine after the war, its altar crowded with war blades brought back to be cleansed, as well as first blades to be blessed. Priest Enju chants over both kinds from dawn to dusk, and his white robes have gone grey to the hem.

  • Rim Altar, After the War: The rim altar, war blades laid out beside first blades, all waiting to be blessed.
