Wayside Jizo Shrine

[Recommended Level: 1-5 · Danger: Low]

A row of small stone Jizo statues in red bibs by the road, where travellers leave coins for safe journeys and parents leave toys for lost children. Wind chimes tinkle over the Statue Row, a line of small stone figures in red bibs with coins and toys at their feet. Travellers stop to bow and set down a coin before walking on. Kichibei the Charm-Peddler usually works the spot, his pack hung with paper wards and lucky frogs, cheerfully selling protection to anyone who has just prayed for it. The shrine stands beside the road a short walk from Oiwake Station.
