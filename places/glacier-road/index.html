Glacier Road

[Recommended Level: 35-45 · Danger: Severe]

Beyond the last poles of Shirokawa's road, the marked way across the open glacier to the Vaults, crevasses on both sides. Past the last poles the road becomes a line of cairns across bare ice, and the wind never stops. Along the Marker Line, crevasses open blue on both sides, some only a pace from the cairns. Travellers rope together and probe the snow ahead with spears. Exile Kanbe, frost on his furs, often walks the line with a sled, a spear and a lantern. The road runs from Shirokawa to the Vaults.
