Old Battle Barrow, The Opened Barrow

[Recommended Level: 40-46 · Danger: Severe]

The old battle barrow after the war, open: the warriors who refused the ferry came out to fight in the war, and some of them have not gone back in.

Areas

  • Barrow Mouth, After the War: The barrow mouth, open, an armoured dead warlord seated in it.
  • The Open Door: The barrow's stone door, wide open now, cold air flowing out. Going in leads down to The Warlord's Mouth.
  • Warriors' Hall, After the War: The warriors' hall, half its benches empty.
