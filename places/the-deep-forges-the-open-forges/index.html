The Deep Forges, The Open Forges

[Recommended Level: 36-46 · Danger: Severe]

The deep forges after the war, no longer closed for repairs: the house works them openly, red light pours from the Mine Tunnels, and Onmyoji Kurenai pastes talismans faster than the rock can hold them. Tetsuo still works alone at the Soul Anvil, his hands worse.

Areas

  • Mine Tunnels, After the War: The mine tunnels, red light and fresh chains along the walls.
  • The Open Shaft: The deep forges' shaft, no longer hidden: a lift and a ladder, both worked day and night. Going in leads down to The Lift Landing.
  • The Soul Anvil, After the War: The soul anvil, glowing with no fire, Tetsuo bent over it.
