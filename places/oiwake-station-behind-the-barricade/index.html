Oiwake Station, Behind the Barricade

[Recommended Level: 20-29 · Danger: High]

The crossroads station after the war, right behind the Straw Mask barricade: the honjin inn turned into a peasant army's headquarters, the post stable full of captured horses, and the toll barrier thrown down and burned for warmth.

Areas

  • Captain Kajiwara's Stone: A stone beside the barricade, his toll list nailed to it: Captain Kajiwara was killed when the Straw Masks took the barrier.
  • Honjin Inn, After the War: The lord's inn, now the barricade's headquarters, straw masks at the door.
  • Post Stable, After the War: Stables of captured cavalry horses, branded with three different crests.
  • Toll Barrier, After the War: The toll barrier, thrown down, its timbers burned in the guards' fires.
