Exiles' Shore Towns, Towns Packing to Leave

[Recommended Level: 43-53 · Danger: Deadly]

The exiles' shore towns after the war, packing: boats loaded with nets and sealskin, families arguing on the Seal Hunters' Quay over whether to stay. The exiles are proud, but the ice is cracking under them.
