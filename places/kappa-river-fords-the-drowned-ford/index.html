Kappa River Fords, The Drowned Ford

[Recommended Level: 23-32 · Danger: High]

The fords after an army crossed them: the banks trampled, cucumbers rotting in heaps nobody remembers leaving, and more drowned men in the reeds than the kappa ever took. Travellers wait for a guide or go a day round.

Areas

  • Offering Bank, After the War: A bank heaped with cucumbers from frightened soldiers, most of them rotting.
  • Shallow Ford, After the War: The ford, deeper now where the banks gave way under cavalry.
  • The Trampled Steps: The kappa steps, broken by cavalry, leading down under the overhang into flooded holes. Going in leads down to The Flooded Undercut.
