Daylight Watch Ridge, The Ridge of Unburned Tracks

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 50-60 · Danger: Deadly]

The daylight watch ridge after the war, crossed by tracks every morning now: the Northern Court's army went south over it, and the Sunlit Snow is trampled flat. The red hunter's cord on the Ridge Cairn has been cut.

Areas

  • Ridge Cairn, After the War: The ridge cairn, its red cord cut and gone.
  • Sunlit Snow, After the War: The sunlit snow, trampled flat by an army's passing.
  • The Cut Cord Trail: The ridge trail, the hunters' red cord cut from the cairn at its top. Going in leads down to The Trampled Gold.
