Drifting Fish Market, Black Market Decks

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 20-25 · Danger: Moderate]

The floating market after the war: the fish are fewer, the decks carry rice, salt and stolen arms, and the Brine Tide runs the auctions openly now nobody has the ships to stop them.

Areas

  • Deck Auction, After the War: Lashed decks where rice and arms go to the highest bidder.
  • Gutting Pier, After the War: The gutting pier, half the knives now gutting crates instead of fish.
