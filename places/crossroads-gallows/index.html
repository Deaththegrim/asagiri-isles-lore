Crossroads Gallows

[Recommended Level: 1-10 · Danger: Low]

Where the plain's two great roads cross stands a gallows and a notice board of wanted faces. Travellers read the notices and do not look up. A rope creaks on the gallows in the wind, and crows wait on the crossbeam. Travellers slow at the Notice Board to read the wanted faces and pardons, then move on quickly. Swordsmith Masamura sets up his portable anvil beside the board, mending blades for coppers and tapping each one with a fingernail to hear its ring. Carts bound for Miyako pass through on both roads all day.
