Void Sanctum

[Recommended Level: 25-40 · Danger: Severe]

A sanctum of the void: a hall where the grey thread shrine's threads all end, lit by nothing, where something that is not there waits to be noticed. A shinobi learns the void style by being as absent as it is. Grey threads run in from every wall toward a point in the middle of the hall, and the eye slides off that point. There is light, from nowhere, and no shadows. Footsteps make no echo. The sense of being looked at is strong and comes from the one place a visitor cannot make themselves look.

Areas

  • The Point: The place the strands end; nothing is there, and it is hungry.
  • The Thread Hall: A hall of grey threads converging on a point that is not there.
  • The Threshold Thread: A grey thread across the doorway where the hall begins. Going in leads down to The Hall of Threads.
