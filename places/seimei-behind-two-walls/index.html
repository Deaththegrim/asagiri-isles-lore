Seimei, Behind Two Walls

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 23-36 · Danger: High]

The onmyoji city after the war, untouched and closed: a second star-shaped ward wall raised around the first, paper charms strung along both, and the lake gate opened twice a day. Refugees camp on the shore outside and are not let in.

Areas

  • Lake Gate Post, After the War: The lake gate, opened twice a day, refugees camped on the shore beyond it.
  • Star Tower, After the War: The star tower, lit all night, its readers never off the instruments.
  • Ward-Wall Walk, After the War: The wall walk, now between two walls, charms rustling on both.

People here

  • Refugee Mother Ogin (Commoner)
  • The Regent's Man Hosokawa Taku (Courtier)
  • The Woman Who Knocks at the Lake Gate (Oni)
