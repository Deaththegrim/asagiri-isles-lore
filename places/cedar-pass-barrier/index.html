Cedar Pass Barrier

[Recommended Level: 3-15 · Danger: Low]

A barrier where the road climbs into a pass: an inspection hall, a gate, and guards who check every pass and every woman's face for runaways. Nobody gets through without paying. Cedars close in on both sides as the road climbs, and the air turns cold and resinous. A long queue waits outside the Inspection Hall, where everyone kneels while passes are read and bundles opened; among them a slight young monk in a straw hat keeps their face down. Captain Nagai stands at the Pass Gate with spears racked behind him, studying each face before he takes the fee.

Areas

  • Inspection Hall: A hall where travellers kneel while their passes are read and their bundles opened.
  • Pass Gate: A heavy gate in a stone wall across the pass, with spears racked beside it.
