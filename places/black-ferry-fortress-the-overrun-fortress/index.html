Black Ferry Fortress, The Overrun Fortress

[Recommended Level: 29-46 · Danger: Severe]

The Veil Court's fortress after the war, overrun with work: ferry boats running day and night, barracks emptied to crew them, and the records wing writing the names of the dead faster than it can file them.

Areas

  • Black Ferry Fortress: Ferry Landing, After the War: The ferry landing, boats loading the dead without pause.
  • Division Barracks, After the War: Barracks emptied to crew the ferries.
  • Records Wing, After the War: The records wing, scrolls of the war dead stacked to the ceiling.
  • The Water Stair: A stair down from the fortress's ferry landing to the lower docks, flooded at the bottom. Going in leads down to The Lower Docks.
