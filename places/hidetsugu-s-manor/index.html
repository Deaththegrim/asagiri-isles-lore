Hidetsugu's Manor

[Recommended Level: 5-20 · Danger: Moderate]

The regent's nephew keeps a lively manor on the edge of the capital: a dueling yard full of young swords, a hall for speeches, and a stable of fine horses. Hidetsugu himself trains at dawn where anyone can watch. The clack of wooden swords carries into the street before the gate comes into view. In the Dueling Yard, young retainers spar and cheer each other's cuts, and Lord Takamori Hidetsugu trains among them at first light, sharing sake afterwards with whoever fought well. The Speech Hall stands open for lords and officials, gold-striped banners hanging behind its dais. Grooms walk fine horses up and down the stable lane.

Areas

  • Dueling Yard: A sanded yard where Hidetsugu's young swords spar from morning to night, cheering each other's cuts. The clack of wooden swords carries to the street.
  • Speech Hall: A hall with a raised dais where the nephew speaks to lords and officials about the reign to come. Banners with a single gold stripe hang behind him.
