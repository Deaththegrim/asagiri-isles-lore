Seventh Station Inn, The Last Inn East

[Recommended Level: 20-26 · Danger: Moderate]

The seventh station after the war, the last inn east of the barricade under the shogunate's flag: soldiers in the honjin rooms and refugees in the bath, the red lantern taken down and hidden.

Areas

  • Common Bath, After the War: A common bath shared by soldiers and refugees in shifts.
  • Honjin Rooms, After the War: The lord's rooms, quartered by an officer of the Regent's army.
