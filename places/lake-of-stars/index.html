Lake of Stars

[Recommended Level: 10-25 · Danger: Moderate]

The great lake the city sits on, so still on clear nights that the stars lie on its surface. Fishermen will not row out after dark. By day the lake is busy: fishing boats from Seimei work the shallows, ferries cross to the far shore, and children skip stones off the Night Shore. Out on the water the Island Shrine is small enough to hide behind a raised thumb. The air smells of weed and cold stone. Toward dusk the boats come in together, and the fishermen haul them high up the pebbles before the light goes.

Areas

  • Island Shrine: A tiny shrine on an island in the lake, its offering table empty and cracked.
  • Night Shore: A pebble shore where the stars are reflected so clearly that the water seems to vanish.
