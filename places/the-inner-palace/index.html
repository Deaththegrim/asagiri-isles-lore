The Inner Palace

[Recommended Level: 1-15 · Danger: Low]

Behind its own walls inside the capital, the emperor's palace keeps the old rites: cypress roofs, raked gravel, poets and priests and court ladies in layered silks. Nobody carries a sword inside, and every word is chosen. Raked gravel crunches underfoot past the Gate of Ceremonies, where swords are left behind and Grand Chamberlain Ochikane greets visitors with a soft smile and a scroll of names. Courtiers trade verses in the Hall of Poems while carp rise in the pond, and Dowager Empress Shoshi listens far more than she speaks. Captain Mori Sayo stands outside Princess Ayame's Rooms with her naginata, day and night.

Areas

  • Gate of Ceremonies: A vermilion gate where guards in court dress check names against a scroll. Visitors leave their swords and their manners are watched.
  • Hall of Poems: A pavilion over a pond where courtiers trade verses and allusions, and where a lot of politics is done in seventeen syllables. Carp rise for the crumbs of rice cakes.
  • Princess Ayame's Rooms: The Dowager's ward lives in quiet rooms behind paper screens, watched by her guards and her tutors. A lotus is painted on every lantern.
  • The Sleeping Chamber: The emperor's innermost chamber, dim with incense, where physicians and the Grand Chamberlain come and go and nobody else is let past the last screen.
