Hanging Bridge Pass

[Recommended Level: 8-22 · Danger: Moderate]

A high pass crossed by a long hanging bridge over a gorge, with a toll hut on each side kept by Hayate shinobi. Wind moans through the gorge, and the bridge ropes creak under every gust. The planks of The Hanging Bridge are worn pale, with a few newer, darker boards where someone patched them. Hayate shinobi in grey-blue keep the Toll Huts at either end and take coin, rice or news. Michiru of the Straw Hat crosses here often, face hidden under her deep hat, and the toll-keepers argue about her after she has gone. Grey wolves sometimes circle the huts at dusk.

Areas

  • The Hanging Bridge: A long rope bridge swaying over the gorge, its planks worn pale.
  • Toll Huts: Two huts at either end, each keeping its own tally and its own grudges.
