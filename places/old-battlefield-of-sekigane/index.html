Old Battlefield of Sekigane

[Recommended Level: 13-25 · Danger: Moderate]

A wide field of grave mounds where two houses fought a hundred years ago; rusted armour still surfaces after rain. Farmers will not plough it, and travellers cross it by day. Crows sit on the stones topping the Grave Mounds, rows of low grassy hummocks that stretch across the field. Scraps of rusted armour poke from the earth where the rain has washed it. On one side of The Great Mound a fresh hole has been dug, and cold air breathes out of it. Grave-Robber Hachi works nearby with a spade and a wrapped sword, glancing at the hole more often than he digs.

Areas

  • Grave Mounds: Rows of low grassy mounds, each topped with a stone. Crows sit on the stones and watch.
  • The Great Mound: A mound larger than the rest, freshly dug into on one side. Cold air breathes out of the hole.
