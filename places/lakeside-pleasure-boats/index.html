Lakeside Pleasure Boats

[Recommended Level: 5-12 · Danger: Low]

Lantern-hung boats on the lake where scholars and merchants drink, listen to music and say things they regret. Koto music and laughter carry across the water from the moored boats, and the air smells of warm sake and lamp oil. Kocho of the Lantern Boat pours for her guests and takes up the shamisen when the talk slows, saying little herself. Poet Ariwake drifts from boat to boat with his brush and flask, offering a verse for a coin. Small skiffs ferry guests out from the Seimei shore at dusk and bring them back unsteady before dawn.

Areas
