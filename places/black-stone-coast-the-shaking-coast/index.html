Black Stone Coast, The Shaking Coast

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 46-57 · Danger: Deadly]

The black stone coast after the war, where the fissure from the Vaults reaches the sea: the cliffs shake at night, and the sea eagles have left the Eagle Cliffs. The wreck in Wreck Cove has been joined by a Warden ship.

Areas

  • Eagle Cliffs, After the War: The eagle cliffs, empty nests, rock shaking at night.
  • Wreck Cove, After the War: Wreck cove, a Warden ship broken beside the old hull.
