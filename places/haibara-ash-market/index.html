Haibara Ash Market

[Recommended Level: 20-30 · Danger: High]

A market held at night in Haibara's back streets where cursed relics, bottled grudges and stranger things are sold. After dark grey awnings go up in the back streets of Haibara, and the Night Stalls open by candlelight. Buyers wear masks or hoods and speak low, and nobody asks anyone's name. Dolls, swords, mirrors and sealed bottles lie out on cloths, each with a price chalked beside it. Dealer Tomekichi keeps the best-lit stall, gloved and well dressed, and tells each relic's history at length. The market packs away before dawn.
