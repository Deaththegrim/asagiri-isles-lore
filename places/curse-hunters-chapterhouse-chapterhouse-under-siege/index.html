Curse-Hunters' Chapterhouse, Chapterhouse Under Siege

[Recommended Level: 33-46 · Danger: Severe]

The curse-hunters' chapterhouse after the war, its brothers worked to exhaustion: every curse the war left needs breaking, and the breaking hall never cools.

Areas

  • Breaking Hall, After the War: The breaking hall, a curse broken on its floor every hour.
  • Elder Kanemitsu's Seal: A sealed niche in the breaking hall: Elder Kanemitsu died breaking a war curse, and the curse is sealed in with him.
  • Record Room, After the War: A record room of curses broken since the war, the scrolls overflowing.
