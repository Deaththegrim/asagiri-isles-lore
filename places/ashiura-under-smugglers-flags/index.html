Ashiura, Under Smugglers' Flags

[Recommended Level: 26-40 · Danger: Severe]

The marsh town after the war, flying the Brine Tide's flags openly now House Mizuchi's navy is ash in the delta. Its stilt market sells navy timber, navy rope and navy swords, and the drowned shrine is gone under the water.

Areas

  • Drowned Shrine, After the War: Only the shrine's roof shows above the water now.
  • Reed Landing, After the War: The reed landing, smugglers' boats moored openly.
  • Stilt Market, After the War: A stilt market selling salvage from the burned navy.

People here

  • Brine Tide Captain Sakon (Coast Smuggler)
  • Headman Uozumi (Commoner)
  • Stilt-Market Crier Funao (Commoner)
