Shirokawa

[Recommended Level: 35-60 · Danger: Deadly]

The exiles' town on the ice isle's sheltered shore: black stone houses, smoke, and people who do not ask where you came from. Smoke drifts from black stone houses, and the White Strait ferry knocks against the floes at the Ice Pier. Warden hunters in furs and chains watch who comes ashore. Nobody asks newcomers their business, and the town's quarrels are settled over drink in the Exiles' Hall. On the Road to the Vaults, Hunter Captain Gaku, scarred and cold, stops travellers among the warning poles and seals.

Areas

  • Exiles' Hall: A hall where the exiles meet, drink and settle what passes for law.
  • Ice Pier: The pier where the White Strait ferry ties up between the floes.
  • Road to the Vaults: The road climbing the glacier toward the Sealing Vaults, marked by poles and warning seals.
