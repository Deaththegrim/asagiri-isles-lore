Seventh Station Inn

[Recommended Level: 1-10 · Danger: Low]

One of the busiest post stations: an official inn for lords, cheaper inns for everyone else, and a lantern gate that closes at dusk. Travellers swap news in the bath. Porters, pilgrims and samurai crowd the street between the inns, and maids call room prices from every doorway. The Common Bath is loud with news from both ends of the road, and Tsurube the Storyteller sits in the steam with his folding fan, telling ghost tales for a cup. Behind their own gate the Honjin Rooms stay hushed, and Innkeeper Jinbei bustles between them and the street, bowing and sweating.

Areas

  • Common Bath: A long wooden bath where porters, pilgrims and samurai soak and talk.
  • Honjin Rooms: The lords' inn, quiet and expensive, with its own gate and its own guards.
