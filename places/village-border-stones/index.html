Village Border Stones

[Recommended Level: 5-15 · Danger: Low]

A line of stones marking the old border between the two villages' lands, each carved with both villages' marks. The meadow is open and windy, with long grass that hides the bases of the stones. Each stone along the Stone Line bears both villages' marks, chipped and recut over the years. Herb-pickers from Kagegi Village and Hayate Ridge Village cross here without stopping, and patrols from both sides walk the line at dusk, each keeping to its own end and watching the other.

Areas

  • Stone Line: A line of carved border stones across a meadow, one freshly set, its earth still dark.
