The Ferry Stones

[Recommended Level: 15-30 · Danger: High]

A field of standing stones out on the flats, each cut with the names of the dead a ferryman carried across. The dead who were not carried wait between the stones, patient, and ask every passer-by for their name. The stones rise out of the flats taller than a man, grey on grey, their cut names filled with silt. Between them figures stand very still and turn their heads to follow the living. One asks, politely, for a name. A Veil Officer's hand goes to their blade here without being told to, and the blade is heavier than it was on the isles.

Areas
