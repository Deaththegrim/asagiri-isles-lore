Reed Channels

[Recommended Level: 12-26 · Danger: Moderate]

A maze of channels through tall reeds where fog sits for days. Only the rain clan and the smugglers know the way through. Inside the reeds the world shrinks to a lane of brown water between two green walls, and the fog muffles every sound but dripping. The Channel Maze turns and turns again with no landmark to steer by. Chidori the Runner is the one most often met here, poling a flat boat at speed with a message for the Brine Tide. Herons stand at the Heron Pool, a still opening deep in the reeds. The channels run between Ashiura and the Kirisame Stilt Village.

Areas

  • Channel Maze: Water lanes between reed walls taller than a man, every turn the same.
  • Heron Pool: A still pool where herons stand and the water is too clear.
