The Ash Plain, The Wider Plain

[Recommended Level: 36-50 · Danger: Severe]

The ash plain after the war, wider: it has crept north past the old road, and the war's burned villages have joined the old capital's outer city in the ash.

Areas

  • Ash Drifts, After the War: Ash drifts over the bones of burned war villages.
  • Dead Grove, After the War: A dead grove, newly dead, still standing.
  • The New Cairns: A new line of cairns, built since the war, leading out into wider ash. Going in leads down to The Village Drifts.

People here

  • Ash Plain Ghoul (Ashfall Ghoul)
  • Ash Plain Oni (Oni)
