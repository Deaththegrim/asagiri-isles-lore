Saltpan Village

[Recommended Level: 1-8 · Danger: Low]

A village of salt pans and drying racks on the shore south of the port. Everyone here is in debt to the guild for their pans. The glare off the Salt Pans is hard on the eyes, and the air tastes bitter. Families rake the white crust into heaps while Headwoman Oshio walks the banks between the pans, shouting orders that carry across the shore. Mule-Driver Hachibei loads sacks onto his mule, Princess, for the climb to the post road and chats with anyone who will stop. Most of the salt raked here is weighed and sold in Saikai, a short walk up the shore.

Areas
