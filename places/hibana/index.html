Hibana

[Recommended Level: 15-40 · Danger: Severe]

The largest forge town on the volcano's slopes: chimneys glowing all night, the ring of hammers, hot springs used for quenching, and masters who choose their customers. Hammers ring from Forge Row day and night, and the air tastes of coal smoke and hot iron. Each forge door carries its master's crest, and customers wait on benches outside to be accepted or turned away. Old Kanetsugu sits outside one of the forges every day, watching the younger smiths work. Down at the Ember Pier the ferry puts passengers ashore into ash and steam, where Troupe-Master Sanshiro and his actors unload a cart of costumes.

Areas

  • Ember Pier: The pier where the Ember Strait ferry from Oiwake puts in, in a haze of ash and steam.
  • Forge Row: A street of forges up the slope, each master's crest over the door.
  • Quenching Springs: Hot springs where blades are quenched and smiths bathe, steam rising into the dusk.

People here
