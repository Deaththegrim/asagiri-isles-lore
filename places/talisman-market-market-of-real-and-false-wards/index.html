Talisman Market, Market of Real and False Wards

[Recommended Level: 23-27 · Danger: Moderate]

The talisman market after the war, its trade tripled: every refugee and soldier wants a ward, and forgeries outsell the real ones four to one.

Areas

  • Back Lane Stalls, After the War: Back-lane stalls of forged wards sold by the armful.
  • Licensed Row, After the War: The licensed row, Bureau seals checked at every stall and still forged.
