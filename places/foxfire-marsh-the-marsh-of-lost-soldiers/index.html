Foxfire Marsh, The Marsh of Lost Soldiers

[Recommended Level: 29-40 · Danger: Severe]

Foxfire Marsh after the war, where deserters hiding from both armies followed the lights and have not walked out. The foxfire burns brighter on the Light Pools, and the newcomers circle them without knowing whose lights they follow.

Areas

  • Circle Path, After the War: The circle path, worn deep by soldiers' boots that never get anywhere.
  • Light Pools, After the War: Light pools burning brighter, a deserter's boots left at their edge.
