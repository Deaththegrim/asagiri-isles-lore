The Sealing Vaults

[Recommended Level: 40-60 · Danger: Deadly]

A prison fortress cut into the glacier, its doors sealed with paper and iron, its Wardens in furs. The bodies of the great beasts lie bound in its depths. The cold inside the glacier is dry and still, and every sound echoes off the ice. Visitors are stopped in the Outer Hall and searched by Seal-Master Hisame, who keeps the record of every beast. Beyond lies the Seal Gallery, door after door papered with talismans, where Keeper Yorikane walks the line in heavy furs, inspecting each seal. Wardens stand at every passage.

Areas

  • Outer Hall: A hall of ice where visitors are searched and seals checked.
  • Seal Gallery: A gallery of sealed doors, each papered with layers of talismans.
  • The Deep Vault: A vault so cold the air hurts, chains as thick as trees going down into dark.

People here
