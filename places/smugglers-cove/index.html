Smugglers' Cove

[Recommended Level: 10-25 · Danger: Moderate]

A narrow sea cave at the island's southern tip, hidden behind rocks: deep enough for a small boat to slip inside at high tide. Brine Tide smugglers use it every new moon.

Areas

  • Cove Mouth: The cave's mouth, half-flooded at high tide, a dark boat tied inside.
  • Smugglers' Tunnel: A tunnel running back under the island, stacked with crates and lit by a shuttered lantern.
