Ashiura Fish Docks

[Recommended Level: 10-15 · Danger: Low]

The working docks of the coast town where fishermen land their catch and mend their nets. Everyone here owes someone. Gulls scream overhead and the planks are slick with scales and brine. Fishermen land their baskets from dawn, gut fish on the boards and haggle with buyers from Ashiura's market, while others sit mending nets in the sun. Every basket and crate passes Tally-Man Kanroku on the Tally Pier, who counts it, marks his board and chews his brush. Moneylenders walk the docks at the end of each day to collect what they are owed.

Areas

  • Tally Pier: A slippery pier where the catch is counted and tallied, gulls diving and the tally man's numbers never quite matching the boats.

People here

  • Tally-Man Kanroku (Commoner)
