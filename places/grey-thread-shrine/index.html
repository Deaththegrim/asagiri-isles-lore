Grey Thread Shrine

[Recommended Level: 10-25 · Danger: Moderate]

A small shrine on a cliff path where pilgrims tie grey threads to a sacred rope and quiet keepers sweep the steps. Few pilgrims stay long. Wind combs through the Thread Rope, and the grey threads hiss against one another like rain on a roof. Keeper Ito sweeps the same steps over and over, glancing down the cliff path at every footfall. The shrine is small: a worn stair, an offering box, and a plain Keepers' Room behind a sliding door. Pilgrims buy a thread from the keepers, tie it on, bow once and go back down.

Areas

  • Keepers' Room: A plain room with a writing desk and a boy's wooden sword hung on the wall.
  • Thread Rope: A sacred rope hung with thousands of grey threads, stirring in the cliff wind.
