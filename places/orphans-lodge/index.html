Orphans' Lodge

[Recommended Level: 15-20 · Danger: Moderate]

A lodge where children orphaned by demon raids are raised and, when old enough, trained as hunters. Smoke rises from the long roof, and inside the lodge is loud with children's voices and the clack of wooden swords. In the Hearth Room, children eat, sleep and practise breathing forms in rows while an older hunter corrects their stance. Orphan Shiro, white-haired and thin, is usually the first up and the last to put his practice sword down. The lodge answers to the fortress above Asahi Gate, and every child here hopes to wear the uniform one day.

Areas
