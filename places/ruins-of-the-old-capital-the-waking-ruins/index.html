Ruins of the Old Capital, The Waking Ruins

[Recommended Level: 40-50 · Danger: Severe]

The old capital's ruins after the war, stirring: the struck-out names are being spoken again by the war's grudges, and the fallen gate is standing straighter every month.

Areas

  • Fallen Gate, After the War: The fallen gate, leaning upright again, a fresh name carved in it.
  • Ruined Palace, After the War: The ruined palace, whispering in a language nobody speaks.
  • The Rising Avenue: The old main avenue, the fallen gate leaning upright at its end. Going in leads down to The Upright Gate.
