Old Battle Barrow

[Recommended Level: 30-40 · Danger: Severe]

A barrow of warriors from a war against the dead, its stones carved with old officers' marks. Cold air flows from the Barrow Mouth even at midday, smelling of old iron and stone dust, and frost rims the grass around the opening. Nobody tends the mound. Beyond the entrance the Warriors' Hall runs back into the dark, lined with armoured skeletons seated in rows as if waiting to be called. The carvings on the outer stones are weathered but still sharp at the edges, and moorland birds do not nest on the barrow.

Areas

  • Barrow Mouth: A low stone door pulled aside, cold air flowing out.
  • The Pulled Stone: A low stone door pulled aside at the barrow's mouth, cold air flowing out. Going in leads down to The Frost Passage.
  • Warriors' Hall: A long chamber of armoured skeletons sitting in rows.
