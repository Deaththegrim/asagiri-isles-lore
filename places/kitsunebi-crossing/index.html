Kitsunebi Crossing

[Recommended Level: 10-40 · Danger: Severe]

A crossing town at the wood's edge where the human road and the yokai paths meet, kept by a fox shrine that both sides respect. Fried tofu and incense scent the Fox Shrine Post, and coaches stop just long enough to change passengers. Priestess Kohaku tends the shrine in red and white robes, greeting humans and yokai with the same small bow. Paper lanterns come up at dusk over the Lantern Market of the Wood, where odd stallkeepers call their wares in voices not quite human. Mame the Go-Between keeps the gate of the Refuge Go-Between House, apron on, cheerful with everyone.

Areas

  • Fox Shrine Post: The post stop beside the fox shrine, where travellers leave fried tofu and coaches leave quickly.
  • Lantern Market of the Wood: A night market where yokai sell to humans and humans try not to buy anything that costs a name.
  • Refuge Go-Between House: A walled house at the wood's edge where the half-yokai Refuge's go-betweens meet humans; the Refuge itself lies deep in the wood, and its way is not told.

People here

  • Lantern-Market Go-Between Konta (Commoner)
  • Mame the Go-Between (Tanuki Trickster)
  • Priestess Kohaku (Fox Spirit)
