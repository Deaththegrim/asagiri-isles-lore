Sun-Rite Shrine of the Plain

[Recommended Level: 1-12 · Danger: Low]

An old shrine of the sun rites where the court's priests come each spring to bless the plain's rice. Its torii glow red at dawn, and farmers leave the first sheaf of every harvest here. A line of vermilion gates, the Dawn Torii, faces the sunrise, and pilgrims pass under them at first light. The Sheaf Hall smells of straw and incense, stacked with the first sheaves of a hundred villages. Priest Ujibe Kiyonari tends it, listening to farmers ask for rain and for sons and writing their prayers on slips of paper. Priests in white sweep the gravel between rites.

Areas

  • Sheaf Hall: A plain hall stacked with the first sheaves of a hundred villages, smelling of straw and incense.
