Wardens' Watch Fort, The Fort Facing Both Ways

[Recommended Level: 46-53 · Danger: Deadly]

The Wardens' watch fort after the war, watching south as well as north: the Northern Court went past it in the war, and now the danger is in the Vaults behind it. Hornkeeper Yuzuru stands his watch with a spyglass turned the wrong way.

Areas

  • Barracks, After the War: The barracks, half the bunks empty, furs left folded.
  • Horn Tower, After the War: The horn tower, Yuzuru's spyglass turned toward the Vaults.
  • Takumi's Empty Bunk: His bunk, furs folded, a horn on the pillow: Warden Takumi of the Horn went into the Vaults and did not come out.
