Kurogane Armoury

[Recommended Level: 15-30 · Danger: High]

The house armoury, where every Kurogane warrior's harness is fitted and repaired. Hammers ring off lacquered plates, and the air smells of lacquer, oil and leather. In the Fitting Room, armourers strap Kurogane warriors into black iron harness piece by piece and chalk dents for repair. Clerk Mitsu sits at a desk by the door with the record books, entering every harness fitted or sent out for mending. The armoury serves the castle guard and every warrior of the iron house.

Areas

  • Fitting Room: A room of lacquered iron plates on racks and armourers fitting harness, hammers ringing.
