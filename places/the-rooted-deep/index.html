The Rooted Deep

[Recommended Level: 25-40 · Danger: Severe]

A tree so old its hollow trunk is a country, with roots going down past where a torch reaches. Something crowned keeps the deepest chamber and the kodama fall silent on the way down. The trunk's inside is wide enough to lose a house in and goes down in a spiral of roots that serve as a stair. Kodama cling to the walls at the top and there are none past the first landing. The air is wet and smells of leaf mould and something sweeter. Far below a sound like branches settling comes up at intervals, as if something large is making itself comfortable.

Areas

  • The King's Chamber: The deepest hollow, roots knotted into a throne.
  • The Trunk Door: A split in the great trunk where the root stair begins. Going in leads down to The Root Stair Down.
  • The Trunk Stair: A stair of roots spiralling down inside the trunk.
