Forge Guild Hall

[Recommended Level: 15-35 · Danger: High]

The swordsmiths' guild hall: inspection rooms where every blade is marked, the oldest forge on the isle, and a hall where masters wake soul-blades. Daylight pours through tall windows onto the long tables of the Inspection Room, where examiners turn blades under the light and stamp the guild mark into those that pass. Smiths queue outside with wrapped swords. Deeper in, the Waking Hall is kept silent; Fire Master Homura sits there with a blade across his knees for days at a time. Most guild members keep forges in Hibana.

Areas

  • Inspection Room: Long tables where blades are examined under daylight and stamped with the guild mark.
  • The Old Forge: The oldest forge on the isle, its hearth never let go cold, anvils and quenching troughs open to any guild smith.
  • Waking Hall: A quiet hall where a master sits with a sword across her knees for days.
