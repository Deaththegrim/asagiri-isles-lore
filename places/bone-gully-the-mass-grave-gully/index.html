Bone Gully, The Mass Grave Gully

[Recommended Level: 40-50 · Danger: Severe]

Bone Gully after the war, used by both armies to bury their dead in a hurry. The white stream runs grey, and the gashadokuro rises bigger than before.

Areas

  • Robbers' Camp, After the War: An abandoned robbers' camp, tools left where they fell.
  • The Mass Grave Rim: The gully's rim, fresh earth where the armies dug in a hurry. Going in leads down to The Hasty Graves.
  • White Stream, After the War: The stream, grey with ash from the mass graves.
