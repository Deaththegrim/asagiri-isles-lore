Kodama Grove

[Recommended Level: 10-25 · Danger: Moderate]

A grove of trees older than the empire where small pale spirits echo your voice back to you, a little wrong. Trunks wider than a house rise into green gloom, and the moss underfoot swallows footsteps. Along the Echo Path, every cough and word returns from the trees a beat late, slightly changed. The Elder of the Kodama watches from a root with its round head tilted. Near the Marked Trees, a cloud of giant hornets drones around a nest, and the sound carries through the whole grove.

Areas

  • Echo Path: A path where every word comes back, sometimes before you speak.
  • Marked Trees: Great trees with logger's marks painted on them, the paint running like sap.
