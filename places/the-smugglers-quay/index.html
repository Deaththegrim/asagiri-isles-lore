The Smugglers' Quay

[Recommended Level: 29-37 · Danger: High]

The sea wall used as a quay, smugglers' boats moored along it, the fort behind empty. Ways on: The Empty Barracks. The way back up and out is here, to Old Coast Fort, The Abandoned Fort.

Areas
