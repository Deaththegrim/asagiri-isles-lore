Miyako Castle

[Recommended Level: 5-25 · Danger: Moderate]

The regent's white castle above the capital: keep, councils and the crane banners of House Takamori. Officials in grey hurry through its gates with warrants and ledgers, and samurai watch every stair. Wet stone and ink smell strongest at the Grey Gate, where petitioners queue with their papers and clerks stamp or refuse them. Takamori spearmen in conical iron hats stand in threes, and Treasury Magistrate Sakuma Chiyo passes through with her ledgers, nodding to nobody. Deeper in, Lord Takamori Masanobu presides in the Council Hall, where councillors murmur in rows and every footstep echoes.

Areas

  • Council Hall: A long hall of polished boards where the regent's councillors sit in rows and argue in murmurs. Every footstep echoes; nobody raises a voice.
  • Grey Gate: The outer gate where petitioners queue with their papers and clerks in grey stamp them or turn them away. The smell of ink and wet stone never leaves it.
  • Keep Ramparts: The high stone walk around the keep, where Takamori retainers keep watch over the whole plain. Wind snaps the crane banners like whips.
