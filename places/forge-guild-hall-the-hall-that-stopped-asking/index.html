Forge Guild Hall, The Hall That Stopped Asking

[Recommended Level: 29-43 · Danger: Severe]

The forge guild hall after the war, richer than any house: the Inspection Room stamps war orders by the hundred, and the Waking Hall is no longer silent. Fire Master Homura sits there with a blade across his knees, and the blades around him whisper.

Areas

  • Inspection Room, After the War: The inspection room, war orders stamped by the hundred.
  • The Old Forge, After the War: The old forge, never cold, hammering war blades day and night for whoever pays.
  • Tsubaki's Bellows: Her bellows in the waking hall, nobody pumping them: Smith Apprentice Tsubaki went down to the deep forges to ask, and did not come up.
  • Waking Hall, After the War: The waking hall, whispering, Homura with a blade across his knees.

People here

  • Blade Inspector Kaji Sota (Commoner)
  • Fire Master Homura (House Samurai)
