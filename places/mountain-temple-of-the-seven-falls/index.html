Mountain Temple of the Seven Falls

[Recommended Level: 5-25 · Danger: Moderate]

The Order's oldest temple, built among seven waterfalls where ascetics fast under the cold water and the sutras are chanted day and night. Travellers are given rice and a mat without question. The roar of falling water covers every other sound, and cold spray drifts across the Falls Courtyard, where monks stand under the falls chanting. Grey-robed monks with naginata keep the gate and the courtyard. High Abbot Ryokai spends the hours before dawn in the Sutra Hall and the rest of the day greeting guests by name. In the Guest Cells, Old Nurse Tsuru, frail and white-haired, rests on her mat and accepts visitors.

Areas

  • Falls Courtyard: A courtyard ringed by seven falls, spray hanging in the air, monks under the water.
  • Guest Cells: Plain cells for travellers, a mat and a bowl in each.
  • Sutra Hall: A dim hall where monks chant in shifts so the sutras never stop.
