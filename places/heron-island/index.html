Heron Island

[Recommended Level: 14-28 · Danger: High]

An island in the marsh where herons nest by thousands and the old marsh clan buried its dead. The noise reaches a boat long before the island does: thousands of herons calling from the trees of the Heron Colony, which stand white with birds above ground white with droppings. The smell is strong. Further in, at the Old Graves, weathered posts of the old marsh clan lean in a ring, half sunk in mud and dark with moss. Smugglers and fishermen from Ashiura pass the island in the channels but rarely land.

Areas

  • Heron Colony: Trees white with nesting herons, the noise deafening, the ground white below.
  • Old Graves: Weathered grave posts in a ring, half sunk in mud.
  • The Mud Landing: A mud bank where boats run aground and the herons' noise begins. Going in leads down to The Nesting Trees.
