Old Battlefield of Sekigane, Twice Fought

[Recommended Level: 28-36 · Danger: High]

The old battlefield fought over again: fresh banners rotting beside rusted armour, new grave mounds beside the old ones, and a great mound of this war's dead raised in a week. Farmers will not cross it even by day now.

Areas

  • Grave Mounds, After the War: Rows of mounds, old grass beside raw earth, both kinds topped with stones.
  • The Great Mound, After the War: The great mound of this war's dead, raised in a week, already sinking.
  • The Robbers' Cut: A trench the grave robbers cut into the newest war mound, shored with broken spears. Going in leads down to The Spear-Shored Trench.

People here

  • Grave-Robber Hachi (Commoner)
  • Sekigane Grave-Robber Band (Highway Bandit)
