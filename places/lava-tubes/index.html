Lava Tubes

[Recommended Level: 25-40 · Danger: Severe]

Tunnels of old lava under the volcano, too hot to walk without wet cloths, home to magma crawlers. The heat hits like an open oven door, and anyone going in wraps a wet cloth over mouth and nose. The Glowing Tube runs ahead with orange walls and shimmering air, the rock ticking as it warms and cools. In the Crawler Nest, magma crawlers lie in heaps on the warm stone, their bellies glowing through the cracks in their plates. Hibana is the nearest town, and few come down from it without good reason.

Areas

  • Crawler Nest: A wide chamber where great lizards sleep on warm stone.
  • Glowing Tube: A tunnel whose walls glow orange, the air shimmering.
