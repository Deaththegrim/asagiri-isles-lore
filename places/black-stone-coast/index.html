Black Stone Coast

[Recommended Level: 40-55 · Danger: Deadly]

A coast of black stone and storm where sea eagles nest and ships wreck. Storm surf booms against black rock, and the screams of sea eagles carry over the wind. The Eagle Cliffs are streaked white with nests above the shore. Below them, Wreck Cove holds the broken hull of a ship, half buried in snow and ice. Shirokawa lies far back along the coast, and exiles come out this way only to scavenge driftwood after storms.

Areas

  • Eagle Cliffs: Cliffs white with eagle nests above the black stone.
  • Wreck Cove: A cove with a broken ship and footprints in the snow.
