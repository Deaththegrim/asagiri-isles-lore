Foreigners' Quarter

[Recommended Level: 1-12 · Danger: Low]

A walled quarter where foreign sailors and traders must live while in port: odd houses, odd food, and translators who sell every conversation twice. The smells of baked bread, pipe smoke and unfamiliar spices drift over the wall. At the Quarter Gate guards check passes in both directions, and Hendrik van Aal lounges nearby, a tall red-bearded sailor trading knots and songs for a meal and asking every captain who passes for a berth home. In the Translators' Tea House, Ume the Translator sits with a jade pin in her hair, taking work by the hour and listening to every table at once.

Areas

  • Quarter Gate: A guarded gate where foreigners show their passes and locals show their reasons.
  • Translators' Tea House: A tea house where translators sit waiting for work and listening to everything.

People here

  • Hendrik van Aal (Commoner)
