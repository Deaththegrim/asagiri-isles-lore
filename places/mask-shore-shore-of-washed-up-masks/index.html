Mask Shore, Shore of Washed-Up Masks

[Recommended Level: 33-46 · Danger: Severe]

The mask shore after the war, where the tide brings in masks every night: the war fed the masked devourers, and their masks wash up by the dozen.

Areas

  • Beachcomber's Hut, After the War: A hut hung with masks from the tide.
  • The Mask Dunes: Dunes above the shore, masks half buried in the sand. Going in leads down to The Mask Tide.
  • Tide Line, After the War: A tide line strewn with pale masks.
