The War Road

[Recommended Level: 20-30 · Danger: High]

A cut in the yard's eastern bank where a road the Otherworld never had runs down into mist. Its keeper says plainly what it is: the road home to a home that has changed, and it runs one way. Past the last lodge the yard's dust gives way to a cut in the bank and a road that was not there when the masters came. Banners in colours no house on the isles flies hang from stakes either side, limp. The mist below smells of wet ash, and now and then a sound comes up out of it that could be a bell or a gate. The keeper does not stop anyone; he only counts them, and says the road runs one way.
