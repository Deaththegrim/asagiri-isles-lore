Bureau of Yin and Yang

[Recommended Level: 5-25 · Danger: Moderate]

The college of onmyoji: lecture halls of talisman scribes, a library of star charts and calendars, and examination rooms where licences are won and lost. Students in white hurry between halls with ink-stained fingers. The first sound is brushes, dozens of them, scratching in long rooms with the shutters open. Talisman Master Gyokuen walks the rows of the Examination Hall, and a candidate whose talisman starts to smoke is sent out without a word. Ward-scribes in white carry cases of paper between the halls. Director Tachibana Seiran keeps to the Director's Garden between lectures and still teaches every first-year class himself. The doors of the Sealed Archive stay shut.

Areas

  • Director's Garden: A raked gravel garden arranged as a star map, where the Director walks alone at night.
  • Examination Hall: A silent hall of low desks where candidates write talismans under the eyes of examiners. A failed talisman smokes.
  • Sealed Archive: A locked archive of forbidden texts behind ward-papered doors. The air inside is cold.
