Kagegi Village

[Recommended Level: 5-30 · Danger: High]

The hidden village of the southwestern slopes, built into a mist-filled valley among black cedars: training grounds, clan compounds, and the mission hall that sells its shinobi to the realm. Mist lies in the valley until midmorning, and the thud of thrown kunai carries from the Cedar Training Grounds before the sun clears the ridges. Visitors meet Yumeno Kasumi first at the Outer Checkpoint, where she takes each traveller's hand in a drowsy greeting before anyone is escorted up. Kagegi Rin drills the younger apprentices hard among the scarred posts, and clients sit waiting in the Mission Hall for Kagegi Sojun to name a price.

Areas

  • Cedar Training Grounds: Clearings among the cedars, posts scarred by blades, where children learn to throw before they learn to read.
  • Mission Hall: Where contracts are posted, ranked and handed out, and where clients wait to be told the price.
  • Outer Checkpoint: The checkpoint below the village where the post road ends and every visitor is met, searched and escorted; a guarded path runs down to the north cove where the White Strait ferry puts in.
