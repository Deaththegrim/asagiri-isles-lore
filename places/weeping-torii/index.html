Weeping Torii

[Recommended Level: 10-20 · Danger: Moderate]

A lone torii on the moor that is always wet with dew, as if it were weeping. Pilgrims pray beneath it for the lost. The moor is quiet here except for wind in the heather and a slow drip from the crossbeam. Pilgrims kneel on the Torii Ground with their knees soaked through, holding written names or small belongings of the lost. Some tie folded paper to the posts, and the damp takes the ink within a day. Many of them walk out from the Pilgrims' Hostel in the morning and back again before dark.

Areas

  • Torii Ground: The ground beneath the lone torii, always damp, pilgrims kneeling in the wet grass to pray.
