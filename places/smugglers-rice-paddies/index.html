Smugglers' Rice Paddies

[Recommended Level: 10-16 · Danger: Moderate]

Rice paddies on the marsh edge where boats are hidden in the dikes and rice sacks hold more than rice. Frogs sing across the flooded paddies and fall silent in a wave as anyone walks the raised paths. The fields are worked like any others, farmers bent to the rice and egrets stepping through the shallows. Farmer Oyone is usually out on the Hidden Dike, muddy to the knees, leaning on her hoe to greet whoever passes. A small boat lies tucked into the reeds along her dike. The paddies sit on the marsh edge within reach of Ashiura.

Areas

  • Hidden Dike: An earth dike with a boat hidden in its reeds and rice sacks heavier than rice, frogs falling silent as anyone passes.
