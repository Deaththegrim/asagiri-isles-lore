The Lodges

[Recommended Level: 10-30 · Danger: High]

Eight low lodges in a half-ring, one for each tradition's master, with a long mess between them and a bunk hall behind where a party sleeps off a day's drills. Each lodge door carries its tradition's mark and nothing else. Smoke from the mess rises straight up, which is the first sign the wind here is not the isles' wind. Each lodge door is shut and marked; a student waits outside one with their hands folded, and has been waiting a while. From the mess comes the knock of a ladle on a cauldron and the voice of the keeper telling someone the board is for anyone who can read it.

Areas

  • Masters' Lodges: Eight doors in a half-ring, each marked with one tradition's sign.
  • The Bunk Hall: Rows of bunks, a stove, and the quiet of people too tired to talk.
  • The Mess: A long table, a cauldron that is never empty, and a board of jobs by the door.
