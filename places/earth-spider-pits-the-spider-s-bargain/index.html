Earth Spider Pits, The Spider's Bargain

[Recommended Level: 32-40 · Danger: Severe]

The earth spider pits after the war, where the spider of the old clans finally got its alliance: Hayate took it, and the pits are a Hayate stronghold now.

Areas

  • Clan Stones, After the War: The clan stones, Hayate's mark carved beside the spider's.
  • The Sentry Rim: The pits' rim, Hayate sentries posted on it, a way down between their posts. Going in leads down to The Marked Stones.
  • Webbed Pits, After the War: Webbed pits, Hayate sentries posted at the rims.

People here

  • Pit Clan Stone-Watcher (Shinobi Operative)
  • Pit Tsuchigumo (Tsuchigumo)
  • The Earth Spider of the Old Clans (Tsuchigumo)
