Burned Village of Kitamura, The First of Many

[Recommended Level: 36-50 · Danger: Severe]

Kitamura after the war, no longer the only burned village: it was the first, and the valleys north of it went the same way. The hunters have stopped chalking its doorways; there is no one left to search for.

Areas

  • Ash Street, After the War: Ash street under fresh snow, no chalk on the doorways now.
  • Old Well, After the War: The old well, its rope cut, its survivor gone south.
  • The Unchalked Gate: Kitamura's burned gate, no hunter's chalk on it any more. Going in leads down to The Unchalked Street.

People here

  • Kitamura Carrion Thrall (Blood-Sworn Thrall)
  • Survivor Fuki (Commoner)
  • The Mother on the Kitamura Well (Oni)
