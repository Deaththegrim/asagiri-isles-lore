Spider Woman's House

[Recommended Level: 20-35 · Danger: High]

A lonely house in the wood where music plays at night and a beautiful woman invites travellers in for tea. Koto music drifts through the trees at dusk, and the house stands tidy and well swept in a clearing that no path seems to reach. Lanterns glow in its windows. The Spider Woman of the Wood greets travellers at the door in a dark kimono patterned with webs and leads them into the Spider Woman's House: Tea Room, where the tea is excellent and the cushions faintly tacky. No birds sing near the house.
