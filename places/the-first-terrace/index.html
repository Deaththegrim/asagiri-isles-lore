The First Terrace

[Recommended Level: 10-20 · Danger: Moderate]

The lowest of a hundred terraces climbing a cliff, where every visitor lays their sword down for the keeper to look at before a step is taken up. Swords left by those who never came back line the wall. The cliff goes up in steps of swept stone, each terrace a little smaller than the last, to a top the eye cannot be sure of. A flat stone at the first terrace's centre is scored with the marks of swords laid on it. Along the back wall swords hang in rows, hundreds of them, and a few are rusting while most are bright.
