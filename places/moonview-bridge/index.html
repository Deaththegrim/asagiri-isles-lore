Moonview Bridge

[Recommended Level: 5-10 · Danger: Low]

An arched bridge over the lake's outflow where the city comes to watch the moon. Lovers leave ribbons on its rails. The bridge rises steeply in a single arch of grey wood, its rails knotted with ribbons of every colour, the oldest faded white. Couples, students and whole families from Seimei climb to the Bridge Crown on moon nights. On other evenings it is quiet except for Ryuan the Komuso, whose shakuhachi can be heard from either bank. Student Akitsu, a Bureau scribe with a weakness for bad poetry, is often at the rail with a scrap of paper and a brush.
