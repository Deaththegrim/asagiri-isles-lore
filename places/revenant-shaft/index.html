Revenant Shaft

[Recommended Level: 30-40 · Danger: Severe]

An old shaft where miners hear weeping and see a lantern moving below. An old winch stands over the Shaft Head, its rope hanging into the dark and swaying with no one on it. Miners down in Miners' Town will not work this shaft, and they talk of weeping heard from it at night. In the Lower Gallery far below, shapes with lanterns stand at old bellows and pump them, though no forge is lit. The air from the shaft smells of cold ash.

Areas

  • Lower Gallery: A gallery where figures work bellows that are not lit.
  • Shaft Head: A winch and a rope going down into dark, the rope moving.
  • The Winch Head: The old winch over the shaft, its rope swaying with no one on it. Going in leads down to The Swaying Winch.
