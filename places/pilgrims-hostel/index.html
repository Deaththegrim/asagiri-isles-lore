Pilgrims' Hostel

[Recommended Level: 10-18 · Danger: Moderate]

A hostel where mourners stay while they wait for word that their dead have crossed. Paper lanterns hang for each. Rice gruel, damp wool and candle smoke fill the low building, and conversations stay at a whisper. Mourners eat at a long table, then go back to sit under their lanterns in the Lantern Hall. Mourner Asa, young and calm in a white kimono, keeps the same place every evening and speaks to almost no one. The hostel charges little, feeds everyone, and lies a short walk from Black Ferry Fortress, whose towers show on a clear day.

Areas

  • Lantern Hall: A hall hung with paper lanterns, one for each soul awaited, mourners sitting quietly beneath them.
