Wind Shrine of the Pass

[Recommended Level: 5-15 · Danger: Low]

A shrine on the pass hung with prayer flags snapping in the wind, where travellers pray for good weather. Prayer flags crack in the wind like whips, hundreds of them, faded red, white and blue. The shrine itself is barely the size of a cupboard. Keeper Fuyo sits on her stool beneath the Prayer Flags, sweeping when the wind allows and chatting with every traveller who stops to pray. Most are on their way to or from Hanging Bridge Pass and want only fair weather for the crossing.
