Ghost Pine Hill, The Lantern Hill

[Recommended Level: 23-29 · Danger: High]

The ghost hill after the war, thick with empty lanterns: the war's unburied dead drift in from the road, and the hilltop shrine is lit every night by someone nobody sees.
