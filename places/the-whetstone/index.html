The Whetstone

[Recommended Level: 15-30 · Danger: High]

A terrace halfway up with a whetstone the size of a millwheel, turned by an armourer who remembers every blade he has ever touched. A duellist who died on the terraces keeps him company and offers bouts to anyone who looks bored. The whetstone is the size of a millwheel and turns with a sound like a long breath drawn in. Sparks go up in a fan and die on the stone floor. The armourer at the crank does not look up from the blade he is working, and a woman in a duellist's half-armour leans on the parapet beside him, translucent at the edges, watching the stair for someone to fight.
