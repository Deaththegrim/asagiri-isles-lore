Hot Spring Inn

[Recommended Level: 15-20 · Danger: Moderate]

An inn of hot springs where swordsmen wait for their commissions. Steam drifts over black rocks around the Steam Bath, and the smell of minerals hangs over the whole inn. Swordsmen with wrapped blades lodge here for weeks while the forges of Hibana finish their commissions, passing the days soaking, drinking and sizing each other up. Buyer Shinpei is usually in the water, his sword wrapped and within reach. Maids carry towels and sake between the bath and the rooms.

Areas

  • Steam Bath: An outdoor bath among black rocks, swordsmen soaking and eyeing each other through the steam.
