The Dark Side

[Recommended Level: 25-40 · Danger: Severe]

The side of Sun Peak the sun never reaches, a slope of black rock and old snow where demons come to test the hunters and the hunters come to be tested. A kill before dawn is forbidden on the mountain; the demons know it and use it. The light goes out of the air as if a door has shut. Black rock and old grey snow, cold enough to bite through boots, and a silence with something in it. Tracks cross the slope that are not a man's and not an animal's. Far up, a ledge shows against a sky that is dark at noon, and a shape on it is standing very still.

Areas

  • The Hunting Ledge: A ledge at the slope's top where a demon waits for the next hunter.
  • The Shadow Line: The line on Sun Peak where the light stops, as if a door has shut. Going in leads down to The Lightless Slope.
  • The Snow Slope: Black rock and old snow, no sun on it ever.
