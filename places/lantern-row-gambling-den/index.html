Lantern Row Gambling Den

[Recommended Level: 1-12 · Danger: Low]

The loudest den on Lantern Row: dice, cards, sake and a debt book kept by a smiling woman. Everyone loses here eventually, and some lose more than money. Rattling dice cups and the smell of spilled sake carry out under the red lanterns well before the door. The Dice Room is packed shoulder to shoulder with sailors, clerks and ronin. Smiling Okon sits by the debt book on its lacquer stand, lending to anyone with a cheerful word and writing every coin down. Mizuchi Toranosuke is usually at the busiest table in an open sea-blue kimono, flushed and certain the next throw is his. The den sits among the tea houses of Saikai's pleasure district.
