Bureau of Yin and Yang, The Silent Bureau

[Recommended Level: 23-36 · Danger: High]

The Bureau after the war, which sat the fighting out behind its wards and is hated for it by every side. Its examinations continue as if nothing happened; its archive is sealed tighter than ever.

Areas

  • Director's Garden, After the War: The Director's garden, where envoys wait to be refused.
  • Examination Hall, After the War: The examination hall, still examining students as if the realm were whole.
  • Sealed Archive, After the War: The archive, sealed with new wards and a guard on every stair.
