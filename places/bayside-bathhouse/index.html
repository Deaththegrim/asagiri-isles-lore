Bayside Bathhouse

[Recommended Level: 1-10 · Danger: Low]

A steaming bathhouse on the bay where merchants, sailors and ronin soak side by side. Nothing said in the bath is repeated, they say, which is why everything is. Steam rolls out of the door onto the bayside path, carrying the smell of hot cedar and soap. In the Great Bath, merchants, sailors and ronin sit shoulder to shoulder, keeping their voices low under the echoing beams. Oyuki the Bath Attendant moves among them with hot water, towels and warm sake, smiling at everyone. Men from Saikai come here after a long day on the quays and leave pink, loose-limbed and talkative.
