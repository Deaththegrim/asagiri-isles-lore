Hiwada Horse Fields, The Empty Pastures

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 21-32 · Danger: High]

The horse house's pastures after the cavalry rode to war and mostly did not come back: fences down, half the stables empty, the archery butts used for firewood. Widows of the riders work the remaining herd themselves.

Areas

  • Gallop Range, After the War: Open ground where a few riders still train, more of them women than before.
  • Hiwada Stables, After the War: Long stables, most stalls empty, a captured raider's chains still on one post.
  • Lord Hiwada's Barrow: A barrow on the horse fields, his saddle and bow laid on it: Lord Hiwada Tamenori fell at Sekigane leading his riders.
