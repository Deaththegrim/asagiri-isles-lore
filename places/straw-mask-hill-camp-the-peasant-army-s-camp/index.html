Straw Mask Hill Camp, The Peasant Army's Camp

[Recommended Level: 25-34 · Danger: High]

The hill camp grown into an army's: palisades, drilled ranks with captured spears, and banners of straw masks over a forge that turns ploughshares into blades. The villages below still feed it, and now they are proud to.

Areas

  • Flail Drill Clearing, After the War: A drill ground where farmers in straw masks practise with captured spears.
  • Masked Fire Ring, After the War: The council fire, wider now, where captains argue about what to do with a peace.
  • Old Gensai's Spear: His stolen spear stuck in the drill clearing, straw masks hung on it: Old Gensai fell taking the Oiwake barricade.
