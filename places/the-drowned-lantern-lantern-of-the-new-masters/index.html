The Drowned Lantern, Lantern of the New Masters

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 26-34 · Danger: High]

The smugglers' tavern after the war, the Brine Tide's open headquarters: captains drinking at the tap, the auction's crates in the back room, and nobody hiding anything.

Areas

  • Back Room, After the War: The back room, crates of sealed goods stacked to the beams.
  • Tap Room, After the War: A loud tap room of smuggler captains and salvage dealers.
