Lighthouse of the Dragon's Eye

[Recommended Level: 10-20 · Danger: Moderate]

A Mizuchi signal tower on the eastern cliff that watches for pirates and signals the fleet with fire. Wind hits the cliff top hard enough to lean against, and the smell of pine pitch hangs around the tower. A long stair climbs to the Signal Platform, where fire baskets stand ready beside stacked pitch and the whole eastern sea is in view. Keeper Sabu tends the baskets and complains about every step of the stair to anyone who has just climbed it. Sailors from the Mizuchi Naval Anchorage bring up his oil, food and pitch.

Areas

  • Signal Platform: A stone platform with fire baskets and a stack of pine pitch, the wind off the sea strong enough to lean on.
