Black Ferry Fortress

[Recommended Level: 15-40 · Danger: Severe]

The Veil Court's fortress on the fog shore east of Tasogare: black walls, officers in black coats, and the landing where the souls who pass the pale torii are ferried on into the fog. Fog clings to its towers. Black stone sweats in the fog, and the only sounds are boots on wet steps and the creak of the ferry at its moorings. Patrol officers drill outside the Division Barracks, soul-blades at their hips, and stop any living visitor to ask their business. In the Records Wing the doors stay shut; Lieutenant Mibu Kotaro passes between them smiling, gloves stained with ink, his notebook open in one hand. Nobody here hurries, and nobody explains anything.
