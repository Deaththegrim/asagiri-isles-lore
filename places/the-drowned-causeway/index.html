The Drowned Causeway

[Recommended Level: 25-40 · Danger: Severe]

A stone causeway running out under the grey tide, knee-deep then waist-deep, to a drowned court where what the ferrymen could not carry has gathered. The water does not move and the things in it do. The stones of the causeway are slick and the water over them is warm, which is wrong. Shapes move under the surface with no ripple above. At the far end a sunken roof shows above the tide, and from under it comes a voice reading charges in the isles' oldest legal form. A ferryman's lantern pole stands in the shallows with no lantern on it.

Areas

  • The Drowned Court: A sunken hall at the causeway's end, its roof just above the tide.
  • The Lantern Pole: A ferryman's lantern pole at the causeway's start, where the stones go under the tide. Going in leads down to The Warm Shallows.
  • The Shallows: The causeway's first reach, water to the knee, shapes under it.
