Sea Monk's Shallows

[Recommended Level: 18-30 · Danger: High]

Rich fishing grounds off the coast where the sea goes glassy-calm on certain nights and a vast black shape rises beside the boats. By day these are busy fishing grounds a short row from Ashiura, with boats spread across the swell and gulls following the nets. The water is deep blue and thick with fish. Barrel Rock, low and black and streaked white by the birds, marks the edge of the best grounds. On some evenings the wind drops away and the sea flattens into the Glass Water, so still and silent that the crews call to one another to start for home.

Areas

  • Barrel Rock: A rock where fishermen used to leave bottomless barrels, now bare.
  • Glass Water: Water so calm it reflects every star, with no wind and no sound.
