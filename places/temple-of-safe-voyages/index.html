Temple of Safe Voyages

[Recommended Level: 1-10 · Danger: Low]

A temple on the hill above the harbour where sailors pray for safe voyages and hang carved ships from the rafters. Its bell rings for every ship that comes home. Incense and sea wind meet at the top of the temple steps. Sailors climb up to clap and bow before they sail, and their families climb them again while they are away. In the Votive Hall hundreds of carved ships hang from the rafters, some draped in black, and Priest Kaison moves among them with his sleeves rolled up over tattooed arms. On the Bell Terrace the great bronze bell looks out over Saikai's bay, its rope worn smooth by his hands.

Areas

  • Bell Terrace: A terrace with a great bronze bell looking over the bay. Its rope is worn smooth.
  • Votive Hall: A hall hung with hundreds of carved ships, each for a voyage prayed over. Some are draped in black.
