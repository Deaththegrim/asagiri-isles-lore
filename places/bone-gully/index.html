Bone Gully

[Recommended Level: 30-45 · Danger: Severe]

A gully where the old capital's dead were thrown, so full of bones the stream runs white. A giant skeleton is said to rise from it. The smell reaches the rim first, old and dry, and below it the stream runs milky over a bed of bones. The White Stream leaves a pale crust on everything it touches. Picks clink from the Robbers' Camp on the gully floor, where Boss Dairoku drives a crew of tired, filthy diggers through the bone heaps and weighs every sack they bring him. What the diggers find goes back to Haibara to be sold.

Areas

  • Robbers' Camp: A camp of grave robbers with sacks of teeth and very little sleep.
  • The Gully Rim: The rim of the gully where the smell rises and a path drops toward the white stream. Going in leads down to The Rim Path.
  • White Stream: A stream running over bones, the water white with powder.
