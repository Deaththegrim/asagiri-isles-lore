Breath Masters' Retreat, The Emptied Retreat

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 36-50 · Danger: Severe]

The breath masters' retreat after the war, half empty: its students were sent to the wall and fewer came back. Master Genshin still sits before the Frozen Falls, breathing so slowly he seems asleep, and the ledge is lined with wounded hunters sent up to learn to breathe again.

Areas

  • Frozen Falls, After the War: The frozen falls, Genshin before them in the snow.
  • Haru's Place on the Ledge: His place on the meditation ledge, left empty, a robe folded on it: Novice Haru was sent to the wall and did not come back.
  • Meditation Ledge, After the War: The meditation ledge, wounded hunters breathing in their bandages.
