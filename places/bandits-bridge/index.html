Bandits' Bridge

[Recommended Level: 5-12 · Danger: Low]

A rope bridge over a gorge on the old road, used by those avoiding the barriers. Bandits like it for the same reason. The gorge is deep enough that the river at the bottom is heard more than seen. The Bridge Planks sway on frayed ropes and creak with every step. The few who take this old road are dodging the barriers, and they keep looking over their shoulders. A wiry, grinning young man often squats at the far end with a knife, humming and watching who crosses. The bridge lies on the old road inland from Saikai.

Areas
