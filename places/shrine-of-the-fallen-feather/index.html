Shrine of the Fallen Feather

[Recommended Level: 10-20 · Danger: Moderate]

A shrine where would-be students of the tengu leave a sword and wait to be noticed. A single black feather lies on the altar, and the only sound is the rustle of name tags on the Sword Rack. Swords of every quality hang there: farm-forged blades, fine heirlooms, even a wooden practice sword. Hopeful students camp nearby for days, sharpening, praying and staring up toward Tengu Peak. Some lose heart and take their blades back; most leave them and wait.

Areas

  • Sword Rack: A rack of swords left by hopeful students, each with a name tag fluttering, a black feather on the altar.
