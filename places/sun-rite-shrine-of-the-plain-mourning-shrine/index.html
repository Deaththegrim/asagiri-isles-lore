Sun-Rite Shrine of the Plain, Mourning Shrine

[Recommended Level: 20-27 · Danger: Moderate]

The sun shrine after the emperor's death: its vermilion gates hung with white, the first sheaves of a thin harvest left on an altar no priest blesses. The priests pray for an heir instead of a harvest.

Areas

  • Dawn Torii, After the War: The gates of the sunrise, hung with white mourning cloth.
  • Sheaf Hall, After the War: The harvest hall, a few thin sheaves on a long empty altar.
