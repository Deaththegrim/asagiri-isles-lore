Deserters' Gorge

[Recommended Level: 25-40 · Danger: Severe]

A gorge of caves and rope bridges where officers who fled the Court hide until the Pursuit Division finds them. Water runs loud at the bottom of the gorge, and the Rope Bridges creak between cave mouths high on both walls. Some bridges hang cut, their planks dangling against the rock. Near the entrance, Pursuer Ikaruga sits by a small fire with his coat buttoned and his eyes on the caves, as still as a heron. Nothing else moves in daylight; whoever is inside keeps fires out and voices down.

Areas

  • Hidden Cave: A narrow cave, one flat stone in its floor lifted and set back not long ago.
  • Rope Bridges: Swaying bridges between cave mouths, some cut on purpose.
