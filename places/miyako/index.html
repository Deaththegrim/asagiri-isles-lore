Miyako

[Recommended Level: 1-25 · Danger: Moderate]

The walled castle city: the regent's keep and the emperor's palace side by side, samurai mansions, markets, temples, and the river bridges where duels are fought at dawn. Temple bells, hawkers and the clatter of wooden sandals fill the streets under the white castle. The Castle Market sells silk, rice and blades beside stalls of gossip from every house, and the river docks smell of mud and fish. At the Riverside Post House, Ofuku of the Post House ladles soup for Warden riders and books coach seats at the top of her voice. White lotus badges and crane crests pass each other in the street without a word.

Areas

  • Castle Market: The market under the castle walls: silk, rice, blades, gossip from every house.
  • Dawn Bridge: The arched bridge where duels are fought at first light and the river carries the loser away.
  • Riverside Post House: The post house by the river docks, where road coaches depart.

People here

  • Magistrate's Scribe Oriha (Commoner)
