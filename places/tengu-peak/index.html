Tengu Peak

[Recommended Level: 20-40 · Danger: Severe]

A crag above the wood where the tengu keep their court and their sword dojo. Students climb for days to be thrown off again. Wind howls across the crag, and the clack of wooden swords carries from the Cliff Dojo, along with Kuro's insults and now and then the yell of a student going over the edge. Tengu swordsmen in yamabushi robes watch from the rocks. Swordsman Hayabusa, a quiet human in a straw hat, trains alone at the far end of the floor. Above it all, Tarobo the Great Tengu sits on the Feather Throne and laughs.

Areas

  • Cliff Dojo: An open dojo on a ledge, the floor polished by centuries of bare feet.
  • Feather Throne: A throne of black feathers where the great tengu sits, laughing at students.
