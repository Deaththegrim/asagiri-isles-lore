Hiwada Horse Fields

[Recommended Level: 3-18 · Danger: Moderate]

The horse house's open country: pastures to the horizon, a manor on a low rise, and archery butts where riders shoot at the gallop from dawn. The plain's farmers ride out here to beg help against bandits. Hooves drum like rain across the Gallop Range, where riders loose arrows at straw targets at full speed. Lord Hiwada Tamenori rides and shoots among them, laughing at misses louder than anyone. Grooms in the long Hiwada Stables know every horse by name, and Steward Kosaka Gen walks the aisles with an account book. The smell of hay, leather and horse sweat is everywhere.

Areas

  • Gallop Range: A long straight run lined with straw targets where riders loose arrows at full speed. Hooves drum the ground like rain.
  • Hiwada Stables: Long stables of fine horses and grooms who know every one by name. One stall is barred and guarded, and the horses near it are nervous.

People here

  • Hiwada Rider (Mounted Archer)
  • Lord Hiwada Tamenori (Mounted Archer)
  • Steward Kosaka Gen (Commoner)
