Moth Dell

[Recommended Level: 30-45 · Danger: Severe]

A dell where black moths cover every surface and the air is thick with wings. Sound dies in it. The ground dips into a hollow where every surface is black and velvety: stones, dead branches and grass all covered in resting moths. Each step raises a cloud of them. The Hatching Stones at the bottom are crusted with eggs and give off a faint warmth. Further in, the Wing Cloud closes overhead so thickly that daylight turns grey, and wings brush the face at every breath. Nothing else that lives has been found here.

Areas

  • Hatching Stones: Stones crusted with moth eggs, warm to the touch.
  • Wing Cloud: A cloud of black moths so thick the sun is grey.
