The Sanctum Steps

[Recommended Level: 10-20 · Danger: Moderate]

A landing where five stairs part, one to each Sanctum: stone, water, fire, wind and the void. A hermit keeps a hut at the parting and tells visitors which stair their Element will suffer them on. Five stairs leave the landing in five directions and five kinds of weather: warm stone, wet mist, ash heat, a tearing wind, and a stair that is simply dim. Moss has taken the hut at the parting and the hermit in it has let it. He asks each visitor their Element before he asks their name, and points with his chin.

Areas

  • The Parting: Five stairs leaving one landing, a moss-roofed hut at the join.
