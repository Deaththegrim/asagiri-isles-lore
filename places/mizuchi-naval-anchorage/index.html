Mizuchi Naval Anchorage

[Recommended Level: 10-28 · Danger: High]

The sea house's anchorage: warships with river-dragon pennants, a shipyard, and an admiral's hall on the bluff. Sailors drill on the decks from dawn. Pine tar, rope and salt are the smells, and mallets ring from the Shipyard, where half-built hulls stand on the slipways and Mizuchi marines in light armour keep guard. Admiral Mizuchi Naotora works among his charts in the Admiral's Hall on the bluff, while his elder son Mizuchi Hayato lies in a room there with a fever three days old. A quiet man from the marsh, Kirisame Raizo, waits near the slipways with a box of medicines and a white flag.

Areas

  • Admiral's Hall: A hall on the bluff with charts on every wall and a view of the whole coast.
  • Shipyard: Slipways and half-built hulls, the smell of pine tar, shipwrights hammering.
