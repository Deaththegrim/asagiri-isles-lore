Snowbound Trading Post

[Recommended Level: 15-25 · Danger: Moderate]

A trading post where trappers sell furs and buy salt, and where some things come south that should not. The post smells of tallow, salt and raw pelts drying by the stove. Trappers come in stamping snow off their boots, drop bundles of fur on the Fur Counter, and leave with sacks of salt. Trader Bunzo, wrapped in furs of his own, weighs every pelt twice and argues the price of each. Sledges bound for Asahi Gate stop here to trade gossip and warm up.

Areas

  • Fur Counter: A counter piled with furs and salt sacks, a trader weighing goods and a crate in the corner nobody looks at.
