Kurogane Iron Castle, The Iron Victor

[Recommended Level: 29-46 · Danger: Severe]

The iron house's castle after the war, the richest seat in the isles: new iron on the walls, warship banners beside the house crest, and the empty armour in the Lord's Hall joined by the harness of the war's dead. Kurogane Moritaka still commands the Iron Gate, and Kurogane Akane still duels anyone who calls her lady.

Areas

  • Iron Gate, After the War: The Iron Gate, ironclads under warship banners.
  • Lord's Hall, After the War: The lord's hall, new harness for the dead hanging among the old.
