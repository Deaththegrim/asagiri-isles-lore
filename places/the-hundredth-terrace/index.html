The Hundredth Terrace

[Recommended Level: 25-40 · Danger: Severe]

The top of the cliff, a terrace of swept stone where the sword saints hold their last lesson. A student fights a hundred bouts on the way up and the saint on arrival; the hundredth duellist, who never passed, fights anyone who makes it. The terrace is swept so clean the stone shows its grain. The cliff's edge is a step away on three sides and the drop is the whole climb. In a shaded corner a figure sits with a sword across their knees and does not move; before it stands another, in armour from a school nobody teaches now, who moves as soon as a foot touches the terrace.

Areas

  • The Last Stair: The last stair up the cliff to the hundredth terrace. Going in leads down to The Count Stone.
  • The Saint's Shade: A shaded corner where the saint sits and the duellist stands.
  • The Swept Stone: A terrace swept clean, bout marks on it.
