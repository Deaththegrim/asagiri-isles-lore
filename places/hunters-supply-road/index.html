Hunters' Supply Road

[Recommended Level: 15-20 · Danger: Moderate]

The road that carries rice and steel from the south to the Hold. Ox carts creak up the Cart Track in a slow line, loaded with rice bales and bundles of bar steel for the Hold. Drivers stamp their feet and beat their arms at every halt. Quartermaster Kuwabara rides alongside with his ledger, counting bales and grumbling about the cold. Recruiter Gonnosuke walks the other way, south toward the villages, in a sunrise haori. The road ends at Asahi Gate.

Areas

  • Cart Track: A rutted track of supply carts and oxen, the drivers stamping their feet in the cold.
