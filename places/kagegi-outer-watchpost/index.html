Kagegi Outer Watchpost

[Recommended Level: 8-20 · Danger: Moderate]

A cedar watchtower on the outer edge of Kagegi land, signalling the village with mirrors. The cedar tower creaks in the wind, and flashes of light from the Mirror Platform blink toward the valley at set intervals. Watcher Nezumi sits on the watcher's mat up top, describing everything below out loud whether anyone is listening or not: carts, birds, a woodcutter, the weather. The watchpost answers to Kagegi Village, and its ladder is pulled up when strangers approach.

Areas

  • Mirror Platform: A platform at the top of the cedar tower with signal mirrors and a watcher's mat, the valley spread out below.
