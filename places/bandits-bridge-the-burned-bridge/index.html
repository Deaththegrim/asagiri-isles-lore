Bandits' Bridge, The Burned Bridge

[Recommended Level: 23-27 · Danger: Moderate]

The bridge where the road was cut: burned in the war's last week and never rebuilt, its charred piles standing in the river. Travellers wade or pay a boatman, and the Red Lanterns still watch.
