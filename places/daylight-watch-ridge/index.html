Daylight Watch Ridge

[Recommended Level: 45-60 · Danger: Deadly]

A ridge where tracks are found in the snow that should have burned at dawn and did not. The ridge catches the first sunlight in the north, and at dawn the Sunlit Snow turns gold across the slope. Lines of tracks cross it, and hunters climb up to study them. At the crest, the Ridge Cairn stands with a red hunter's cord knotted round its stones as a marker. Wind scours the top, and the view runs a long way north over ice and black rock.

Areas

  • Ridge Cairn: A cairn of stones with a hunter's red cord tied to it.
  • Sunlit Snow: Snow lit gold at dawn, a line of tracks across it.
  • The Ridge Trail: A trail up the ridge, tracks in the snow that should have burned at dawn. Going in leads down to The Gold Snow Slope.
