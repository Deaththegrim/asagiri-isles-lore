Burned Village of Kitamura

[Recommended Level: 25-45 · Danger: Severe]

A village burned in the last raid, its people dead or taken. Hunters search the ash for survivors and tracks. The smell of wet ash and charred thatch reaches the road before the village does. On Ash Street, blackened beams stand up out of the snow, and hunters from Asahi Gate pick through the ruins in silence, chalking the doorways they have searched. Claw marks score the few walls still standing. At the Old Well at the end of the street, the rope sometimes shifts with no hand on it.

Areas

  • Ash Street: A street of burned houses, black beams pointing at the sky.
  • Old Well: A well with a rope that moves when no one touches it.
  • The Village Gate: The burned gate of Kitamura, its beams black, the street beyond under snow. Going in leads down to The Ash Street Ruins.
