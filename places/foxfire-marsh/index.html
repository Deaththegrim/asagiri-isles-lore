Foxfire Marsh

[Recommended Level: 15-30 · Danger: High]

A marsh lit by foxfire at night, where travellers following the lights walk in circles until dawn. Reeds hiss, frogs fall silent at dusk, and cold lights rise over the Light Pools in green, rose and pale violet. The Circle Path loops back to the same dead tree whichever way a traveller turns. Lord Shiranui lounges beside the pools in a dark kimono, silver tails spread behind him, watching passers-by with a contemptuous smile. Mud sucks at every step, and the water smells of rot and mint.

Areas

  • Circle Path: A path that always leads back to the same dead tree.
  • Light Pools: Pools where cold lights hover, each a different colour.
