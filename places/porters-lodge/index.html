Porters' Lodge

[Recommended Level: 1-8 · Danger: Low]

Where the road's porters and palanquin bearers sleep between jobs, and where they decide which travellers are worth carrying. Snoring, sweat and straw fill the Bunk Room, a long hall lined with bunks and empty palanquins. Bearers lie about between jobs, mending straps and telling stories about the people they carried. Big Ushi sits among them, huge and calm, settling who works tomorrow. A thin man in a borrowed porter's coat that does not fit keeps to the back corner and flinches when a samurai goes by. The lodge serves the traffic out of Oiwake Station.
