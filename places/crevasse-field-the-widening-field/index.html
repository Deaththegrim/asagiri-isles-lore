Crevasse Field, The Widening Field

[Recommended Level: 46-53 · Danger: Deadly]

The crevasse field after the war, widening every month as the fissure opens beneath it. The frost spirits chime louder over the Blue Depths, and the snow bridges no longer hold at all.

Areas

  • Blue Depths, After the War: The blue depths, open wide, frost spirits chiming over them.
  • Rope Anchor, After the War: The rope anchor, its rope pulled up, fresh boot prints beside it.
