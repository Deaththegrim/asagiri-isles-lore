Kappa River Fords

[Recommended Level: 5-18 · Danger: Moderate]

Shallow fords on the plain's great river where travellers cross on foot. Cucumbers are left on the bank, and children are told never to wade alone. Reeds rustle along the river, and the Offering Bank is strewn with cucumbers, some fresh and some soft and rotting. The Shallow Ford runs knee-deep over smooth stones, quick and cold, and something small and green ducks beneath the surface now and then. Travellers cross in groups, holding hands, and farmers set down a fresh cucumber before they step in.

Areas

  • Offering Bank: A grassy bank strewn with cucumber offerings, some fresh, some rotting.
  • Shallow Ford: Knee-deep water over smooth stones, quick and cold. Something moves under the surface.
  • The Kappa Steps: Worn steps cut into the bank where the river runs under an overhang into the kappa's holes. Going in leads down to The Undercut Bank.
