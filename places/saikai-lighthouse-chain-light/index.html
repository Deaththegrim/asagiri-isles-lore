Saikai Lighthouse, Chain Light

[Recommended Level: 20-26 · Danger: Moderate]

The lighthouse after the war, now the signal tower for the chain-boom: the keeper raises and lowers the harbour chain by lamp code, and nothing enters at night without the guild's word.

Areas

  • Lamp Room, After the War: The lamp room, shutters and chain-signals, a guild clerk beside the keeper.
  • Old Rokubei's Lamp: His old lamp, cold, set on the rocks below the lighthouse: Old Rokubei died at the lamp during the blockade.
