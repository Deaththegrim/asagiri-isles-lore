The Inner Palace, Without an Emperor

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 20-29 · Danger: High]

The emperor's palace with no emperor: roofs open to the sky where fire took them, raked gravel trampled by soldiers who were never meant to enter, court ladies in mourning white keeping the rites for a throne nobody sits on.

Areas

  • Captain Mori's Naginata: A naginata driven into the ground before the inner gate: Captain Mori Sayo fell holding it for Princess Ayame.
  • Chamberlain's Marker: A stone by the seal room's door: Grand Chamberlain Ochikane was cut down here keeping the seals from the Regent's men.
  • Gate of Ceremonies, After the War: The ceremonial gate, its doors taken for firewood during the siege.
  • Hall of Poems, After the War: The poetry hall, roofless, the last poems still pinned to the burned screens.
  • Princess Ayame's Rooms, After the War: Her rooms, kept swept by one loyal maid, the princess's brush still on the desk.
  • The Dowager's Ashes: A small shrine where the palace fire burned hottest: Dowager Empress Shoshi died here the night the capital was stormed. Her poems are pinned to its posts.
  • The Emperor's Tomb: A sealed tomb in the palace garden, its doors unpainted: Emperor Kiyohito, whose death began the war. Nobody tends it.
  • The Sleeping Chamber, After the War: The chamber where the emperor slept a year and died; the court keeps vigil over an empty bed.
