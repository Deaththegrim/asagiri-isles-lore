Monks' Herb Fields

[Recommended Level: 5-10 · Danger: Low]

Terraced fields where monks grow herbs for the temple and the poor. Narrow terraces step down the hillside, and the air smells of mint, bitter root and turned earth. Monks from the Mountain Temple of the Seven Falls work the Herb Terraces in straw hats, weeding and carrying water up on shoulder yokes. They hand out bundles of dried herbs to the poor who walk up from the valley, and a plain shed at the top holds the season's drying racks.

Areas

  • Herb Terraces: Terraced beds of medicinal herbs tended by monks, a failed patch brown and bare.
