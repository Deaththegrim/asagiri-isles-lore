Paper Familiar Workshop

[Recommended Level: 5-18 · Danger: Moderate]

Where Bureau students learn to fold and wake paper familiars: long tables of paper cranes, warriors and hounds that twitch when no one is looking. Paper rustles everywhere though no window is open, and the room smells of rice glue and scorched chalk. Bureau students sit shoulder to shoulder at the Folding Tables, creasing cranes and hounds from stacks of white sheets, while Instructor Shirokane walks behind them, his own hands folding as he corrects theirs. At the Waking Circle one student at a time kneels to wake a familiar under his eye. The workshop is part of the Bureau of Yin and Yang.

Areas

  • Folding Tables: Long tables covered in folded paper creatures in every stage of waking.
  • Waking Circle: A chalk circle on the floor where familiars are woken, scorched in places.

People here
