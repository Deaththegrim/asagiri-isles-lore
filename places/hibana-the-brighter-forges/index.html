Hibana, The Brighter Forges

[Recommended Level: 29-46 · Danger: Severe]

The forge town after the war, brighter: chimneys glowing all night and all day, hammers ringing without rest, and masters who no longer choose their customers because the Regent's agents choose for them. Old Kanetsugu still sits outside his forge, watching smiths younger than ever at work.

Areas

  • Ember Pier, After the War: The ember pier, warship iron loaded beside actors' carts.
  • Forge Row, After the War: Forge row, every chimney glowing, the Regent's agents at the doors.
  • Quenching Springs, After the War: The quenching springs, running too hot to touch, steam hiding the far bank.
