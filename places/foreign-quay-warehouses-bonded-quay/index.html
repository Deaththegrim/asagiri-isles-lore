Foreign Quay Warehouses, Bonded Quay

[Recommended Level: 23-33 · Danger: High]

The warehouses after the war, crammed with bonded goods: arms bought for one side and never collected, rice held against debts, and the black warehouse busier than it has ever been. Guild seals hang on every lock.

Areas

  • Loading Yard, After the War: A yard stacked with uncollected arms and guild-sealed rice.
  • The Black Warehouse, After the War: The masked auction house, its lacquer boxes sliding faster than ever.
