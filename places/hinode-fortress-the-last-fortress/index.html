Hinode Fortress, The Last Fortress

[Recommended Level: 29-50 · Danger: Severe]

The Breath Hunters' fortress after the war, the north's last wall: the sunrise banner still flies, the Breathing Yards drill children who would not have been called for three more years, and Commander Asahina Tetsu's map of the north has more black pins than white. Hinode Saya trains apart, as ever, and harder.

Areas

  • Breathing Yards, After the War: The breathing yards, drilling children in uniforms cut down to fit.
  • Commander's Tower, After the War: The commander's tower, black pins crowding the map of the north.
  • Sun-Steel Forge, After the War: The sun-steel forge, never cold, blades forged for hands too young.
