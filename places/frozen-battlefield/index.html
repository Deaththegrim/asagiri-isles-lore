Frozen Battlefield

[Recommended Level: 45-60 · Danger: Deadly]

A field where an army froze where it stood fighting the north, still standing under the ice. The field is silent, and the cold lies heavy and still. Under clear ice, the Ice Ranks stand where an army froze in mid-battle: soldiers with spears raised, banners stiff, faces visible a hand's depth down. Exiles from Shirokawa avoid the place. A ranked demon of the Northern Court in black armour has been seen walking slowly among the frozen ranks.

Areas

  • Cracked Line: Where the ice has cracked and a hand reaches out.
  • Ice Ranks: Ranks of frozen soldiers under clear ice.
  • The Field's Edge: The edge of the frozen field, the first rank of faces visible under the ice. Going in leads down to The Frozen Front.
