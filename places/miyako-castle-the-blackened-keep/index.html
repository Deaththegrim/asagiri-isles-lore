Miyako Castle, The Blackened Keep

[Recommended Level: 23-36 · Danger: High]

The Regent's castle after the siege: soot climbing the white walls to the third roof, a breach in the outer gate patched with timber, and crane banners flying beside the guild's coin-mark. Inside, clerks count debts instead of taxes, and the council hall never empties.

Areas

  • Council Hall, After the War: A council that sits day and night and decides nothing, guild factors in the back row.
  • Grey Gate, After the War: The petitioners' gate, half its doors burned, a timber barricade where the other half stood.
  • Keep Ramparts, After the War: Soot-black ramparts where archers watch the burned quarters below.
