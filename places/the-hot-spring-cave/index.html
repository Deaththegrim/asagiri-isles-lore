The Hot Spring Cave

[Recommended Level: 5-20 · Danger: Moderate]

A sea cave at the foot of the west cliff below the house, reached by a cliff path, where a hot spring steams into a rock pool before it runs to the sea. A kappa lives in it.

Areas

  • Cave Ledge: A dry ledge above the pool where clothes and towels are left, and the kappa's cucumber ledger is kept.
  • The Steaming Pool: A wide rock pool of hot water under the cave roof, steam rolling out over the sea.
