The Pebble Rings

[Recommended Level: 25-40 · Danger: Severe]

Rings of pebbles on the near shore that someone keeps repairing, mist so thick the far shore cannot be seen. Ways on: The Reader's Hut. The way back up and out is here, to Water Sanctum.
