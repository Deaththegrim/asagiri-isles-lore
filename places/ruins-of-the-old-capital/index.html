Ruins of the Old Capital

[Recommended Level: 30-45 · Danger: Severe]

Broken walls and fallen gates of a capital older than Miyako, half buried in ash. Grudge fog pools in its streets. The ash lies thinner among the ruins, and old paving shows along the main avenue until the Fallen Gate blocks it, a great gate lying face down from wall to wall. Roofless houses stand on either side, and fog thickens in the low streets. Beyond them the Ruined Palace rises in black beams open to the sky. Brother Seiji is often there with a lantern and an ink-smeared notebook, sketching the carvings and measuring the halls.

Areas

  • Fallen Gate: A great gate lying on its face across the old main avenue.
  • Ruined Palace: A palace of black beams with no roof, its throne room full of fog.
  • The Old Avenue: The old main avenue, its paving showing through thin ash. Going in leads down to The Face-Down Gate.
