The Great Gate Cliffs

[Recommended Level: 25-40 · Danger: Severe]

High cliffs over the Sea of Fog where the Veil is thinnest. On tide nights masked spirits climb out of the fog. The fog below moves in slow swells and breaks against the rock without a sound. A row of Watch Stones runs back from the Cliff Edge, each with a worn notch where a sentry's spear rests on tide nights. By day the cliffs are empty except for gulls that circle and will not land, and the few travellers who come up keep well back from the drop and speak quietly.

Areas

  • Cliff Edge: A sheer edge over a sea of white fog, the fog moving like water.
  • Watch Stones: A line of stones where the Court posts sentries on tide nights.
