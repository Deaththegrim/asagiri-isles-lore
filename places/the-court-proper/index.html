The Court Proper

[Recommended Level: 15-30 · Danger: High]

The yokai court itself, a hall of roots and lanterns where the courtiers hold session under their own law. Steel drawn here without a declared challenge is the worst manners the Court knows, and the Court has a long memory. Roots form the walls and the lanterns in them never gutter. Courtiers sit in session on cushions of moss, masked and unmasked both, and stop talking when the living enter, then resume as if they had not. A low table at the hall's side holds slips of paper weighed down with stones: favours, owed and owing. Steel at the hip draws every eye in the room.

Areas

  • The Favour Table: A low table where favours are weighed, written and owed.
  • The Session Hall: A hall of living roots, lanterns in every hollow, courtiers in session.
