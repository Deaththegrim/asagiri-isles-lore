River Ferry Crossing, The Only Crossing

[Recommended Level: 20-26 · Danger: Moderate]

The ferry crossing after the bridge burned: now the only way over the river for a day either side, its ferry running night and day, the queue on both banks a camp of its own.

Areas

  • Rider Kanbei's Post: A post-rider's marker on the riverbank, his dispatch bag hung on it: Rider Kanbei was shot carrying a dispatch across the river.
  • River Ferry Crossing: Ferry Landing, After the War: A crowded landing where the queue waits days for a place on the ferry.
  • Waiting Shed, After the War: A shed full of travellers sleeping in shifts.
