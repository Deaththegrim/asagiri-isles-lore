Grudge Well Sanctum, The Overflowing Wells

[Recommended Level: 36-50 · Danger: Severe]

The grudge wells after the war, overflowing: the war poured a generation of new grudges into them, and the sorcerers bottle faster than they can sell. Grey smoke stands over the sanctum like a second sky.
