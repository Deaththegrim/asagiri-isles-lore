Straw Mask Hill Camp

[Recommended Level: 8-22 · Danger: Moderate]

A camp in the wooded hills above the rice country where the Straw Masks gather between raids: lean-tos, hidden fires, and farmers practising with flails. The villages below feed it and say nothing. Smoke from hidden fires seeps through the trees, and lean-tos huddle under the canopy. In the Flail Drill Clearing, farmers swing flails and sickles under Old Gensai, an eyepatched veteran who curses them fondly. At the Masked Fire Ring, Ine of the Hundred Fields sits in her woven straw mask with the camp's leaders. Sukezo runs in and out with messages from the villages, always asking whether there is anything to eat.

Areas

  • Flail Drill Clearing: A clearing where farmers drill with flails, sickles and spears under a scarred old soldier. The drills are clumsy and getting less so.
  • Masked Fire Ring: A ring of logs around a low fire where the leaders talk behind their straw masks. Nobody uses a real name here.

People here

  • Ine of the Hundred Fields (Ashigaru)
  • Old Gensai
  • Sukezo (Commoner)
