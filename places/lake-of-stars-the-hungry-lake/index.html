Lake of Stars, The Hungry Lake

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 26-36 · Danger: High]

The star lake after the war, its serpent unfed for two years and growing bolder: boats cross only in convoys, and the island shrine is lit with offerings again, paid for by the refugees.

Areas

  • Island Shrine, After the War: The island shrine, relit, heaped with refugee offerings.
  • Night Shore, After the War: The night shore, refugee fires along the water's edge.
