Halls of the Nine-Tailed Lady, The Court of Refuge

[Recommended Level: 29-46 · Danger: Severe]

The vermilion halls after the war, crowded: yokai driven out of burned shrines and half-blood children from the houses' camps have come to the Lady's court, and the Hall of Bargains has never written so many favours. Lady Nanao's leaves fill baskets. The court holds the wood's edge as its own now, and humans who come without a gift are walked home before they arrive.

Areas

  • Foxfire Avenue, After the War: Foxfire Avenue, lit twice over, refugee yokai sleeping in its side halls.
  • Hall of Bargains, After the War: The Hall of Bargains, favours written on leaves by the basket.
  • The Lady's Pavilion, After the War: The Lady's pavilion, the Regent's envoy waiting outside it.
