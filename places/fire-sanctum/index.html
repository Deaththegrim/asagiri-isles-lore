Fire Sanctum

[Recommended Level: 25-40 · Danger: Severe]

A sanctum of fire: an ash field around a crack in the ground that breathes heat, where a sprite keeps the furnace and an ash-tender keeps the field. Water carried in is poured out at the edge; the furnace takes it as an insult. Ash lies to the ankle and is warm underneath. Heat comes up through it in breaths, and with each breath the crack in the ground glows and dims. The air smells of hot stone and nothing else. A tender in a leather apron rakes the ash into lines that the next breath disturbs, and rakes them again.

Areas

  • The Ash Edge: The edge of the ash field, where carried water must be poured out. Going in leads down to The Ember Field.
  • The Ash Field: Grey ash to the ankle, heat coming up through it.
  • The Furnace Crack: A crack in the ground that breathes orange, a sprite in it.

People here

  • Ash-Tender Kaen (Commoner)
