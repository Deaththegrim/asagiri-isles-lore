The Gate Ring

[Recommended Level: 10-30 · Danger: High]

Ten weathered posts in a ring, each carved with one isles gate's name: the torii, the grove, the fox road, the five shrines, the peak and the feather. A party that names a post and walks between them is home. The posts stand on bare earth worn into a ring by feet going home. Each name is cut deep and old, the torii's deepest of all. The keeper sits on a three-legged stool at the ring's edge with a kettle, asks every party which post they want, and does not get up until they have said it. Between the posts the air shivers like heat over a road.
