The Drowned Lighthouse

[Recommended Level: 15-25 · Danger: Moderate]

The island's northern rock, joined by a narrow causeway: a tall stone lighthouse whose lamp has been dark for thirty years, waves breaking at its foot and wreck timbers in the water below. Islanders do not climb the stair at night.

Areas

  • Lower Stair: The lighthouse's bottom floors: a stair spiralling up past storerooms, the keeper's log on a shelf.
  • The Lamp Room: The top of the tower: a great dead lamp, a ring of glass, the whole sea visible in every direction.
  • The Rock Causeway: A narrow causeway of rock over the breakers, slick with spray.
