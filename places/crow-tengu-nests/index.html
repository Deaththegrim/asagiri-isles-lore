Crow Tengu Nests

[Recommended Level: 20-35 · Danger: High]

Treetop nests of crow-headed tengu, glittering with stolen things. Harsh cawing comes from high in the pines, and things glitter up there: mirrors, coins and hairpins strung along the Hoard Branches. Crow-headed tengu squabble over them and dive at anyone who stares too long. General Karasuba keeps watch from the Lookout Pine in lacquered armour, spear in hand, and orders humans out of the wood in a harsh, carrying voice.

Areas

  • Hoard Branches: Branches hung with stolen mirrors, coins and hairpins.
  • Lookout Pine: The tallest pine, a crow tengu always on watch.
  • The Climbing Pine: A pine with notches cut into its trunk, the way up to the nests. Going in leads down to The Lower Boughs.
