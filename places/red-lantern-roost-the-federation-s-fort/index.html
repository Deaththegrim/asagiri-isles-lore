Red Lantern Roost, The Federation's Fort

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 23-29 · Danger: High]

The bandit roost after the war, fortified with the war's leftovers: captured palisades, stolen spears and a watchtower taller than the lantern trees. The Federation plays both halves of the road.

Areas

  • Lantern Trees, After the War: Trees hung with red lanterns above a new palisade.
  • Mother's Log, After the War: The Federation's council log, a war map carved into its top.
