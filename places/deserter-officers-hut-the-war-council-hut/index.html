Deserter Officers' Hut, The War Council Hut

[Recommended Level: 43-50 · Danger: Severe]

The deserter officers' hut after the war, the exiles' war council: the council meets every night now, arguing over whether to help the Wardens hold the Vaults or to leave the isle. Kuze Ibuki still listens more than he speaks.
