Moorland Graves

[Recommended Level: 10-22 · Danger: Moderate]

A graveyard on the moor where the poor of the coast are buried. Wisps in cracked masks drift over it at night. Heather and low grave markers stretch to a gravedigger's hut at the field's edge, a lantern hanging by its door. By day the Grave Rows are quiet but for spades and crows. Gravedigger Hyozo digs in a slow stoop, and Nun Seigan walks between the graves with her prayer beads, stopping to chant over the newest mounds. Families from the coast villages bury their poor here because the ground costs nothing.

Areas
