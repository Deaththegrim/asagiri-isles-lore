The Thin Air Steps

[Recommended Level: 10-20 · Danger: Moderate]

Steps cut into the mountain above the ascetics' peak, where the air is thin enough that the first hundred leave a hunter gasping. A cook keeps a fire at the first landing and feeds anyone who makes it up. The first fifty steps are easy and the next fifty are not; breath comes short and the head goes light. Cloud lies below and the peak above is a shape in brighter cloud. At the landing a cook's fire burns small and blue in the thin air, with a row of bowls beside it and the smell of barley and dried fish.

Areas

  • The First Landing: A landing with a cook's fire, bowls and a view of nothing but cloud.
