Mask-Maker's Workshop, Workshop of Borrowed Faces

[Recommended Level: 25-34 · Danger: High]

The mask-maker's workshop after the war, doing a trade it never had: soldiers who want to go home as someone else, and widows who want one more night with a face.

Areas

  • Shop Front, After the War: A shop front with a queue of soldiers and widows.
