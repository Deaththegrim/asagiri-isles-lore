Curse-Hunters' Chapterhouse

[Recommended Level: 20-40 · Danger: Severe]

A fortified chapterhouse on the edge of the Blight where the Curse-Hunter Brotherhood trains, breaks curses for a fee and keeps a record of every curse-maker it has killed. Iron bells hang in rows from the beams of the Breaking Hall and shiver now and then with no wind. Elder Kanemitsu drills novices there with an iron staff, on a floor scorched in circles where curses have been broken. Brother Jun moves quietly between the halls, courteous to all and close to none. Novice Tomo sweeps the Record Room and reads the ledgers when she thinks no one is watching. Shrine priests and frightened families come from Haibara to hire a hunter.

Areas

  • Breaking Hall: A hall hung with iron bells that ring when a curse is near, the floor scorched in circles.
  • Record Room: Shelves of ledgers naming curses and their makers, some crossed out in red.
