The Ash Plain

[Recommended Level: 25-45 · Danger: Severe]

A plain of grey ash where nothing grows and dead trees stand like grave markers. Ghouls walk it in packs at dusk. Grey stretches to every horizon, broken only by white dead trees, and the wind lifts the ash in thin veils that sting the eyes. In the Ash Drifts footprints fill in within minutes, so travellers from Haibara mark their way with piled stones. The Dead Grove creaks though the air is still. No birds sing and no insects hum. An Ash Plain Oni has been seen wading through the drifts, an iron club over its shoulder.

Areas

  • Ash Drifts: Drifts of fine grey ash that swallow footprints within minutes.
  • Dead Grove: A grove of white dead trees that creak with no wind.
  • The Cairn Line: A line of piled stones marking the way out into the ash. Going in leads down to The Swallowing Drifts.
