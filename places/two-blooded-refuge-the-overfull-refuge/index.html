Two-Blooded Refuge, The Overfull Refuge

[Recommended Level: 26-40 · Danger: Severe]

The Two-Blooded Refuge after the war, overfull: half-blood children from every burned village have been carried here, and the Great Tree's branches hold more lanterns than leaves. Old Kagari teaches in shifts, and Kiyo of the Refuge has stopped pretending newcomers are welcome.

Areas

  • Great Tree, After the War: The Great Tree, lanterns crowding its branches, children sleeping in its roots.
  • Ward Ring, After the War: The ward ring humming loud, one stone cracked, Kiyo guarding it.
