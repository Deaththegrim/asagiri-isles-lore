Crossroads Gallows, Hanged Crossroads

[Recommended Level: 20-26 · Danger: Moderate]

The crossroads after the war: the one gallows has become a row, the notice board lists traitors to three different claimants, and the crows no longer bother to fly away.

Areas

  • Notice Board, After the War: A board of traitors' faces from every side of the war, pasted over each other.
