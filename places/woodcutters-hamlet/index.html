Woodcutters' Hamlet

[Recommended Level: 5-12 · Danger: Low]

A hamlet of woodcutters who fell the range's cedars and float them down to the plain. They pay both villages for safety. Axes ring on the cedar slopes above, and the Log Yard smells of fresh sap and sawdust. Woodcutters work the sawpits in pairs, stripping bark and dragging logs to the stream to float them down toward Miyako. Headman Mokichi hovers over the stacks with a sweaty face and a travel bag he never seems to put down. Children carry water between the sheds, and smoke from cooking fires hangs in the trees.

Areas

  • Log Yard: A yard of stacked cedar logs and sawpits, woodcutters at work and the smell of fresh sap.
