Valley of the Rank Exams

[Recommended Level: 10-25 · Danger: Moderate]

A deep cedar valley below Kagegi where the rank exams are held among traps and posts, far from the daily training grounds. Outsiders who wander in are escorted out, or not. The cedars grow close and dark, and every breeze sets small bells ringing somewhere in the Trap Grove. Wires catch the light at ankle height along the paths. Proctor Kagegi Isshin walks the Exam Clearing between exam days, resetting traps and checking posts, polite to anyone he finds and firm about walking them back to the trail. Out of season the valley stays empty but for birds and the proctor.

Areas

  • Exam Clearing: A clearing with a stone where examinees kneel, stained dark.
  • Trap Grove: A grove of cedars strung with wires and bells, every path a test.
