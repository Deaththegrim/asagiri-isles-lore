Saikai

[Recommended Level: 1-20 · Danger: Moderate]

The free port town on the bay: warehouses, quays, foreign ships at anchor among the junks, and lantern-lit streets that never close. The merchant guild's flag flies over the customs house and the regent's does not. Tar, fish and incense hang over the quays, and gulls work the rubbish behind the warehouses. Coaches rattle out of the Harbour Post yard, where Harbourmaster Watanuki stands with spyglass and ledger, checking anchor fees against the hulls in the bay. Foreign sailors in striped shirts haggle along the Foreign Quay, and after dark the crowd drifts up to Lantern Row for dice, music and tea. Guild clerks in dark blue coats are everywhere.

Areas

  • Apothecary Lane: A narrow lane of herb sellers and apothecary shops behind the quays, foreign remedies beside the isles' own.
  • Foreign Quay: Where the square-sailed ships tie up: strange cargo, strange sailors, and guild inspectors who can be paid.
  • Harbour Post: The post-horse stable and ferry office at the head of the quays, where coaches leave east along the post road and foreign ships put in.
  • Lantern Row: The pleasure district: gambling halls, tea houses, musicians, and the debts that start here.
