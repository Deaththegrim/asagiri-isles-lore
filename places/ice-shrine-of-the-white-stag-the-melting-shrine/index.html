Ice Shrine of the White Stag, The Melting Shrine

[Recommended Level: 46-57 · Danger: Deadly]

The ice shrine of the white stag after the war, melting: the carvings drip, the thousand antlers in the Antler Hall have lost their tines, and water pools at the Melting Altar. Priestess Shirane carves new stags as fast as the old ones fade.

Areas

  • Antler Hall, After the War: The antler hall, its fine tines melted to stubs.
  • Melting Altar, After the War: The melting altar, water pooling at its foot.
  • Shizuku's Stag: A small stag carved fresh in the melting wall: Acolyte Shizuku of the Stag drowned in the melt under the shrine.
