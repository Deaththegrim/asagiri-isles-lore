Fog Watch Tower

[Recommended Level: 15-30 · Danger: High]

A tower where the Court rings a bell when the tide begins. The tower is a tall timber frame on a stone base, and its great bronze bell hums faintly whenever the wind strikes it. Up in the Bell Loft, Bellkeeper Oto sits wrapped in a patched coat, watching the fog line with one hand near the rope. She climbs down only for food and water, and lets visitors up if they mind their feet on the ladder. From the loft, the lanterns of Tasogare show as a smudge of light along the cliffs.

Areas

  • Bell Loft: A loft with a great bronze bell and a rope that has been spliced twice, fog pressing against the open sides.
