Asahi Gate, Under the Broken Wall

[Recommended Level: 29-50 · Danger: Severe]

The hunters' town after the war, under siege in all but name: the dawn bell rings twice now, once for the hunt riding out and once for the count of those who did not come back. Refugees from the Daylight Valleys sleep in the Hunters' Mess, and Physician Ogata stitches cuts at the side table from dawn to dawn under more names cut in the beams.

Areas

  • Hunters' Mess, After the War: The hunters' mess, refugees sleeping between the tables, new names cut in the beams.
  • Snow Road Post, After the War: The coach yard, carts of the wounded going south and rice coming north.
  • Sunrise Wall, After the War: The town's sunrise wall, raised higher with timber since the outer wall fell.
