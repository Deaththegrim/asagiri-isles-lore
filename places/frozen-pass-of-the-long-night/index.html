Frozen Pass of the Long Night

[Recommended Level: 30-45 · Danger: Severe]

The pass beyond the Long Night Wall that the demons come through on the Demon Nights, frozen nine months of the year. The wind is the first thing, a steady scream between the ice walls that drowns out speech. The Pass Floor is a narrow, drifted road, and snow sliding off the cliffs fills old tracks within the hour. Grey-skinned thralls in farmers' clothes shelter in the lee of the rocks after dark. By day, travellers have met Hakuro the Daylight Demon walking the pass in a dark cloak, and he lets hunters go on their way.
