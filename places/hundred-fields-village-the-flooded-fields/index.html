Hundred Fields Village, The Flooded Fields

[Recommended Level: 20-26 · Danger: Moderate]

A rice village after the dykes broke: half the paddies are mud, half the thatch is new, and the village feeds a Straw Mask patrol and a Regent's tax post on the same day. Nobody looks at anyone.

Areas

  • Headman Tahei's Grave: A grave by the burned tax house: Headman Tahei stayed when the village burned. The register lies on the stone, unburned.
  • Headman's House, After the War: The headman's house, a tax post nailed to one wall and a straw mask hung on the other.
  • Paddy Dikes, After the War: Broken dykes and drowned paddies, villagers mending them with bare hands.

People here

  • Headwoman Kinu of the Hundred Fields (Commoner)
  • Tax Collector Mizobe Hachiro (Ashigaru)
