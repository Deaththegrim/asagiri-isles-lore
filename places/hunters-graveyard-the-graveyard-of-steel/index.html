Hunters' Graveyard, The Graveyard of Steel

[Recommended Level: 29-40 · Danger: Severe]

The hunters' graveyard after the war, spread down the slope: the Blade Rows run twice as far, and the pale steel rings in the wind like a field of bells. The hunters stopped cutting names when they ran short of time.

Areas

  • Blade Rows, After the War: Blade rows running down the slope, unnamed steel among the named.
  • Empty Grave, After the War: The empty grave, three more beside it now.
  • Kurogi's Blade: His own blade planted at the head of the rows: Grave-Warden Kurogi was killed guarding the empty graves.
