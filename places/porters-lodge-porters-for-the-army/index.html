Porters' Lodge, Porters for the Army

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 20-25 · Danger: Moderate]

The porters' lodge after the war, half empty: the armies took the strongest porters and paid them in promises. Those left carry the wounded home for a few coins.

Areas

  • Big Ushi's Carrying Pole: His great carrying pole set upright by the lodge door: Big Ushi died carrying the wounded off the road.
  • Bunk Room, After the War: A half-empty bunk room, a porter's crutch by every other bed.
