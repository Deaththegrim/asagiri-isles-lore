Hundred Fields Village

[Recommended Level: 1-10 · Danger: Low]

A rice village of thatched roofs and flooded paddies, typical of the hundred that feed the capital. Its people are tired, taxed and quiet when samurai pass. Frogs croak in the flooded paddies, and the air smells of mud and woodsmoke. Villagers walk the Paddy Dikes in single file and bow low when samurai pass; at night wild boars root along the dikes. Headman Tahei keeps the tax register in the Headman's House and greets every visitor with a nervous smile and a long memory. Like the hundred villages around it, it sends its rice to Miyako.

Areas

  • Headman's House: The largest thatched house, where the headman keeps the tax register and a nervous smile.
  • Paddy Dikes: Narrow earth dikes between flooded paddies, where everyone walks in single file.
