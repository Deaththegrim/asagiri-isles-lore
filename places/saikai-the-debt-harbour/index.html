Saikai, The Debt Harbour

[Recommended Level: 20-33 · Danger: High]

The free port after the war, untouched by fire and richer than ever: new warehouses on every quay, war-junks moored among the foreign tall ships, and an iron chain-boom across the harbour mouth that the guild raises at dusk. Every house in the isles owes this town money, and the town knows it.

Areas

  • Apothecary Lane, After the War: The apothecary lane, every shop selling war salves at war prices.
  • Foreign Quay, After the War: Crowded quays of foreign ships and war-junks, guild clerks counting every crate.
  • Harbour Post, After the War: The post house, busier than ever, selling passage to anyone fleeing the plain.
  • Harbourmaster's Marker: A marker on the harbour post's steps: Harbourmaster Watanuki drowned when the blockade burned the pilots' boats.
  • Lantern Row, After the War: The lantern quarter, brighter and louder; soldiers spend their pay here.
