Pirate Graves Reef, The Wreck Reef

[Recommended Level: 26-33 · Danger: High]

The reef after the war, crowded with new wrecks: war-junks that ran from the chain-boom in the dark, and a guild silver ship still stuck on the rocks with guards camped on its deck.

Areas

  • Gibbet Rock, After the War: The gibbet rock, a new pirate hanging beside the old bones.
  • The Guild Landing: A rope landing the guild's guards rigged to reach the silver ship. Going in leads down to The Fogged Wrecks.
  • Wreck Field, After the War: A field of new wrecks among the old, a guarded silver ship in the middle.
