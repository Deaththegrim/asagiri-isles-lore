The Warm Shallows

[Recommended Level: 25-40 · Danger: Severe]

The causeway's first reach, water to the knee and wrongly warm, shapes moving under it with no ripple above. Ways on: The Docket Steps. The way back up and out is here, to The Drowned Causeway.

Areas

  • Gripping Water: Warm water over slick stones, pale fingers just under the surface.
