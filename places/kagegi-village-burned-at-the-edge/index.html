Kagegi Village, Burned at the Edge

[Recommended Level: 23-40 · Danger: Severe]

The hidden village after the war, no longer hidden: a burnt patch on its eastern slope where Hayate fire-talismans fell, smoke rising from the cedars, and every genin on the walls. The cold war ended the year the realm went to war, and became a hot one.

Areas

  • Cedar Training Grounds, After the War: Training grounds turned to drill for war, burnt cedars at their edge.
  • Mission Hall, After the War: The mission hall, every mission now against Hayate.
  • Outer Checkpoint, After the War: The checkpoint, doubled, nobody let through without a seal.
