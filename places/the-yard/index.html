The Yard

[Recommended Level: 10-30 · Danger: High]

A wide trampled yard under five different skies, one for each quarter at the horizon. Sparring circles are scraped in the dirt, racks of wooden blades stand along the edge, and the sound is always the same: breath, feet and wood on wood. Dust hangs in the air at knee height and never quite settles. In one circle two shinobi trade feints without a sound; in the next a samurai drills the same cut forty times while a breath hunter counts for her. Nobody watches the sky, which is five skies, and nobody mentions it. A master crosses from the lodges now and then, says four words to a student, and goes back.

Areas

  • Blade Racks: Racks of wooden blades, staves and padded fists, worn smooth by hands.
  • Sparring Circles: Rings scraped in the dirt, each with its own rule chalked on a post.
