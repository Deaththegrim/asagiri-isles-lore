Hidetsugu's Manor, Camp-Lord's Hall

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

[Recommended Level: 23-33 · Danger: High]

The nephew's manor turned into a war camp: the duelling yard churned to mud by drilled levies, the speech hall full of maps, and banners of the claimants who backed the nephew hung upside down in the stable where his enemies can see them.

Areas

  • Dueling Yard, After the War: Mud and drill-lines where young swords once duelled for show.
  • Kanzaki Ryo's Practice Post: His practice post in the dueling yard, a death tablet tied to it: Young Sword Kanzaki Ryo died in his first battle at Sekigane.
  • Speech Hall, After the War: Maps of the plain on every wall, pins where every village still holds rice.
