Salt Marsh Shrine

[Recommended Level: 10-18 · Danger: Moderate]

A small shrine on a boardwalk over the salt marsh, where fishermen leave cucumbers for the river folk. A long boardwalk runs out over the salt marsh, grey planks over grey water, with reeds whispering on both sides. At its end stands the Boardwalk Altar, a small roofed shrine with cucumbers laid on it, some fresh and some going soft. Priest Mokuren keeps it, barefoot and thin, and blesses the nets of fishermen who stop on their way out from Ashiura. The marsh around is quiet except for frogs and the odd splash too heavy for a fish.
