Tasogare, The Long Procession

[Recommended Level: 26-46 · Danger: Severe]

The torii city after the war, in mourning: black banners on every street, twice the ghost-lanterns drifting out over the fog sea, and the war's dead queueing at the great pale torii faster than the officers can ferry them. The living walk quietly here.

Areas

  • Lantern Cliff, After the War: The lantern cliff, lanterns drifting out in an unbroken line.
  • Pilgrim Post, After the War: The pilgrim post, busy with families looking for their dead.
  • The Great Pale Torii, After the War: The great torii, a queue of the dead stretching back from it.
