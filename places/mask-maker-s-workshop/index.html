Mask-Maker's Workshop

[Recommended Level: 8-22 · Danger: Moderate]

A small workshop in a back alley of the lake city where an old woman carves theatre masks of rare quality. Actors and collectors queue for her work; she turns most of them away. The Shop Front is so crowded with hanging masks that the walls seem to be made of faces: demons, maidens, foxes and old men in fresh lacquer. The smell of lacquer and cedar shavings comes through the curtain from the Carving Room, with the tap of a chisel. Customers are met by an old woman who studies each face a long while and then, more often than not, refuses the order. Actors from Seimei's theatres wait in the alley outside anyway.

Areas

  • Carving Room: A back room of chisels, lacquer pots and a locked chest. A fox's tail hangs over the door.
  • Shop Front: A narrow shop hung with finished masks: demons, maidens, old men, foxes. Their eyes follow you.

People here
