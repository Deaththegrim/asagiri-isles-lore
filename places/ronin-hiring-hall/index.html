Ronin Hiring Hall

[Recommended Level: 3-18 · Danger: Moderate]

A big drinking hall where masterless swords wait for work and clients come to hire them. Contracts are pinned to boards by the door, and the best bands have their own tables. Sake, sweat and lamp oil fill the long hall, and the din rises each time a client comes through the door. Gonroku the Broker stands at the Contract Boards with his one arm, pinning up escort and guard jobs and taking the hall's tenth. Masterless swords in faded kimono and straw hats read the boards or sleep against the wall. In the corner, Captain Ishimura laughs at the best of the Captains' Tables, where a sword laid flat means business.

Areas

  • Captains' Tables: Corner tables where band captains drink and bargain with clients. A sword laid on the table means the talk is serious.
  • Contract Boards: Boards covered in pinned contracts: escorts, collections, guard duty, and a few no one will touch. A clerk takes the hall's cut.
