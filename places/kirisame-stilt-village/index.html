Kirisame Stilt Village

[Recommended Level: 12-28 · Danger: High]

Houses on stilts deep in the reeds, joined by plank walks and hidden by fog most of the day. The rain clan lives here and lets very few visitors leave by a different channel than they came. Fog hides the village until a boat is already among the stilts, and then voices go quiet on every porch. The Plank Walks are narrow and wet, and Kirisame Nagi waits on them with a tea tray and a smile for each new face. Herbs and gourds hang drying from the eaves of the Poison House, where the matriarch Kirisame Shigure receives anyone the clan allows that far. Clan members in rain-patterned clothes mend nets and watch strangers without speaking.

Areas

  • Plank Walks: Narrow walks between stilt houses, slick with fog, with water dark below.
  • Poison House: A stilt house hung with drying herbs and gourds, where nobody eats.

People here
