Grudge Well Sanctum

[Recommended Level: 25-45 · Danger: Severe]

A ring of curse wells inside a broken wall, tended by sorcerers in grey who draw up grudges in long iron ladles and bottle them for sale. The air tastes of ash and old anger. The broken wall encloses a bare yard and seven wells, and the first thing noticed is a low murmur, like many voices far away, rising from the Well Ring. A Grudge Well Sorcerer in ash-grey robes stands watch at each well beside its chained ladle. Master Hai runs the Bottling House, a long shed where she inspects each dark bottle before it is sealed and shelved. Buyers come from Haibara and further, and they do not linger once their business is done.

Areas

  • Bottling House: A long shed of dark bottles on shelves, each holding a whispering grudge.
  • The Deepest Well: A well wider than the rest, its rope cut, a cold wind rising from it.
  • Well Ring: Seven stone wells in a ring, each with a ladle chained to it and a sorcerer watching.
