Tanuki Market

[Recommended Level: 10-20 · Danger: Moderate]

A night market in a clearing where tanuki sell anything for money that turns to leaves at dawn. Lanterns, roasting chestnuts and loud bargaining fill the clearing once night falls. The Leaf-Money Stalls are run by round-bellied tanuki in straw hats, who pour sake for customers and swear every item is genuine. Ponta of the Tanuki Market holds the biggest stall in the middle, laughing louder than the rest. The market lies a long night's walk into the wood from Kitsunebi Crossing and packs up before first light.

Areas

  • Leaf-Money Stalls: A clearing of lantern-lit stalls run by round-bellied tanuki, piles of goods priced in coins that smell faintly of leaves.
