The Court of the Dead

[Recommended Level: 15-30 · Danger: High]

The Veil Court's true seat: a pale hall of clerks who were dead before the isles had a name, keeping black ledgers of every soul ferried and every soul owed. The living are tolerated here if they pay a memory at the door. The hall is lit by nothing and is not dark. Clerks in grey write in black ledgers at desks that run out of sight, and the scratch of their pens is the only sound. At the door a clerk holds out a hand for the living visitor's memory and takes it like a coin, and for a breath the visitor cannot recall what they paid.

Areas

  • The Ledger Hall: Rows of desks, black ledgers, and clerks who do not look up.
  • The Toll Door: A door where a clerk takes a memory from each living visitor and writes it down.
