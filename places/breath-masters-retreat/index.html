Breath Masters' Retreat

[Recommended Level: 25-45 · Danger: Severe]

A retreat in the mountains where breath masters train the hunters who will succeed them, under a frozen waterfall. The only sound up here is the wind, since even the waterfall is frozen in mid-fall. The Frozen Falls hang in a pale wall of ice over a cave, and Master Genshin sits before it bare-chested in the snow, breathing so slowly he seems asleep. Students on the Meditation Ledge sit for days without moving, wrapped in a single robe each. Supplies and new students climb the mountain from Asahi Gate.

Areas

  • Frozen Falls: A waterfall frozen in mid-fall, a cave behind the ice.
  • Meditation Ledge: A ledge where masters sit in the snow for days.
