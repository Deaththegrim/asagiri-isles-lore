Frozen Pass of the Long Night, The Open Pass

[Recommended Level: 40-50 · Danger: Severe]

The frozen pass after the war, open: the demons come through it every night now, not only on Demon Nights, and the Pass Floor is trampled to ice by thralls marching south. Footprints that did not burn at dawn cross it, and the hunters who see them do not follow.

Areas

  • Frozen Pass of the Long Night: Ice Caves, After the War: The ice caves, emptied of packs, a cold light deep inside.
  • Pass Floor, After the War: The pass floor trampled to ice by the marching dead.
  • The Trampled Marker: The last hunters' marker, knocked flat by marching thralls. Going in leads down to The Marching Road.
