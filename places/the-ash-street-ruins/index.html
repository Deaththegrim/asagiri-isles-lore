The Ash Street Ruins

[Recommended Level: 25-45 · Danger: Severe]

Blackened beams standing out of the snow, claw marks on the few walls left, chalk on the searched doorways. Ways on: The Burned Register House. The way back up and out is here, to Burned Village of Kitamura.
