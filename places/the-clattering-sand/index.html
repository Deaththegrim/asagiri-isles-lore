The Clattering Sand

[Recommended Level: 20-40 · Danger: Severe]

Grey sand strewn with broken white masks like teeth, waves dragging over them with a dry clatter. Ways on: The Mask Collector's Shack. The way back up and out is here, to Mask Shore.
