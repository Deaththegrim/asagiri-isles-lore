Haibara, On the Grudge Line

[Recommended Level: 33-50 · Danger: Severe]

The blight's town after the war, no longer at the blight's edge but deep inside it: the grey has spread north past the road, the sealed well smokes day and night, and a ring of new shrines, the Grudge Line, has been raised a day's walk north to hold it. The Vow Temple has never had so many penitents.

Areas

  • Grey Banner Waystation, After the War: The waystation, crowded with soldiers come to atone at the temple.
  • Lady Shizune's Grave: A grave behind the Vow Temple, ringed with dog charms: the inugami found Lady Shizune.
  • Sealed Well, After the War: The well in the square, smoking through a broken seal.
  • Vow Temple, After the War: The vow temple, a queue of penitents from every army.
