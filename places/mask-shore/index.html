Mask Shore

[Recommended Level: 20-40 · Danger: Severe]

A grey beach where broken white masks wash up on the tide line. Beachcombers sell them; most regret it. Waves drag across grey sand with a dry clatter, because the shallows are thick with broken white masks. The beach smells of kelp and cold ash. Beachcomber Sute works the shoreline with cloth-wrapped hands, sorting pieces into baskets, and sells the best of them from the Beachcomber's Hut, where masks cover every wall and face the door. Collectors come down from Tasogare to haggle; locals will not touch the masks bare-handed.

Areas

  • Beachcomber's Hut: A driftwood hut with masks hung on every wall.
  • The Tide Line Path: A path down the dunes to where the masks begin. Going in leads down to The Clattering Sand.
  • Tide Line: A line of broken white masks along the grey sand, like teeth.
