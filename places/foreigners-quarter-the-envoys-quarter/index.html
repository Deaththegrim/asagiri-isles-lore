Foreigners' Quarter, The Envoys' Quarter

[Recommended Level: 20-27 · Danger: Moderate]

The foreign quarter after the war, full of envoys as well as traders: the foreign captains sell to whoever wins, and every claimant has sent someone to buy their guns.

Areas

  • Quarter Gate, After the War: The quarter gate, guarded by foreign marines as well as guild men.
  • Translators' Tea House, After the War: The tea house where envoys and captains bargain through translators who sell both sides.
