Orphans' Lodge, The Crowded Lodge

[Recommended Level: 29-33 · Danger: High]

The orphans' lodge after the war, crowded past its walls with the valleys' orphans, sleeping three to a mat. Orphan Shiro is the oldest now, and teaches the breathing forms to the small ones.
