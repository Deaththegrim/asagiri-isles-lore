Smugglers' Caves

[Recommended Level: 8-20 · Danger: Moderate]

Sea caves under the cliffs north of the bay, flooded at high tide and dry at low. Fishermen avoid them; smugglers do not. The Tide Mouth booms like a drum as the sea surges in and drains away, and the rocks are slick with weed. At low water a path climbs to the Dry Gallery, where crates sit above the tideline beside a fire that is laid but never lit. Small bare footprints cross the sand up there, and Hana, a thin girl with tangled hair, watches from the shadows, ready to bolt at the sound of boots. The caves are a long scramble along the shore from Saikai.

Areas

  • Dry Gallery: A high gallery above the tideline where crates are stacked and a fire is laid but never lit.
  • The Low-Tide Path: A weed-slick path down the cliff that only shows at low water, leading into the booming caves. Going in leads down to The Booming Mouth.
  • Tide Mouth: The cave mouth where the sea surges in and out, booming like a drum.
