Polishers' Street

[Recommended Level: 15-22 · Danger: Moderate]

A quiet street of sword polishers who spend weeks on a single blade. After the hammers of the forge town, the quiet is the first thing noticed: only the soft scrape of steel on whetstone. The Polishing Shops stand open-fronted along the street, each with a polisher bent over a blade on a low bench. Master Polisher Togi, fussy and bespectacled, checks his apprentice's work under lamplight. Hagane Suzu sits at the next stone, murmuring to the sword in her hands. Blades come here from the forges of Hibana for their finish.
