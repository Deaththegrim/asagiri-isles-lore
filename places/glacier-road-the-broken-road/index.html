Glacier Road, The Broken Road

[Recommended Level: 43-50 · Danger: Severe]

The glacier road after the war, broken by the fissure halfway to the Vaults: the Marker Line ends at a crack too wide to jump, and Exile Kanbe ropes travellers across it one at a time.

Areas

  • Marker Line, After the War: The marker line, ending at a fissure, Kanbe's rope across it.
  • The Fissure Lip: The edge of the fissure that broke the road, ropes and markers dangling into it. Going in leads down to The Rope Descent.
