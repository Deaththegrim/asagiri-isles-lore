Two-Blooded Refuge

[Recommended Level: 10-30 · Danger: High]

A hidden village under a great tree where children of humans and yokai live together, behind wards no hunter finds. Lanterns hang in the branches of the Great Tree, and children with ears, tails or scales play among roots as thick as houses. The air smells of leaf mould and cooking fires. Old Kagari sits at the roots with the small ones around her, teaching in a slow voice. At the Ward Ring the carved stones hum faintly, and Kiyo of the Refuge stands guard there with her ears uncovered, studying every newcomer with open suspicion.

Areas

  • Great Tree: A tree so vast the village lives in its roots, lanterns hung in its branches.
  • Ward Ring: A ring of carved stones that hums when hunters come near.

People here

  • Gatekeeper Heisuke (Commoner)
  • Kiyo of the Refuge (Fox Spirit)
  • Old Kagari (Commoner)
