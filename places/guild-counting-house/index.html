Guild Counting House

[Recommended Level: 1-15 · Danger: Low]

The Saikai Merchant Guild's counting house: three floors of clerks at abacuses, money changers at the front counters, and a vault of ledgers at the back. Half the isles' debts are written down in this building. The clack of abacus beads reaches the street before the door does. Downstairs, changers at the Money Counters weigh coin, bite silver and write receipts for a queue of captains, farmers and shrine stewards. Upstairs, Guildmaster Daikokuya Sohei sits on his cushion in the Council Room, laughing with whoever is in favour while the windows show him every quay. Clerks run between floors with ledgers under their arms and talk only in figures.

Areas

  • Council Room: An upstairs room with a long table where the Guild Council meets. The screens are painted with ships, and the windows look down on every quay.
  • Ledger Vault: A fireproof storehouse of ledgers behind iron doors, with a clerk who knows where every page is. There is a gap on one shelf.
  • Money Counters: Long counters where changers weigh coin, test silver with their teeth and write receipts. The clack of abacus beads never stops.
