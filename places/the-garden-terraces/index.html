The Garden Terraces

[Recommended Level: 5-15 · Danger: Low]

Stepped garden beds on the south slope below the house, and an orchard of three old persimmon trees. Ruined until restored: the beds are thick with weeds and sea-bramble, and only the persimmons are tended.

Areas

  • Persimmon Orchard: Three old persimmon trees, one far older than the others, a grave marker in its roots.
  • Terrace Beds: Stone-walled beds stepping down the slope, the soil black and good under the weeds.

People here

  • Old Goro (Commoner)
