Ferrymen's Rest

[Recommended Level: 15-25 · Danger: Moderate]

A teahouse below the Black Ferry Fortress where off-duty officers drink and do not talk about work. Steam, roasted tea and the smell of wet wool fill the Ferrymen's Rest: Tea Room, where off-duty officers sit on cushions with their black coats open. Talk runs to weather, dice and the price of sake. Ferryman Gin nurses a cup in the corner, grey-stubbled and tired, and nods at newcomers without inviting them over. The teahouse stands under the walls of Black Ferry Fortress and serves anyone who pays, though the living get looked at.
