Game modes

How a story in the Asagiri Isles leans.

Clan War

WAR · Hard
The feuds boil over: Kagegi and Hayate, the houses, the bands. Every faction is arming.
Expect · battles, sides, betrayals
Lighter on · quiet downtime
Starts · (Party) The Exam Team · (Party) The Lot in the Black Warehouse · Kurosugi Range: The Outer Checkpoint · Shinobi: The Cedar Training Grounds

Court Intrigue

INTRIGUE · Medium
The succession is the only story: the regent, the nephew, the princess, the hidden heir.
Expect · secrets, leverage, whispered deals
Lighter on · open combat
Starts · (Party) The Mirror and the Dowager · Hostage: The Ward of the Inner Palace · Masks: The Face at the Grey Gate

Ghost Road

HORROR · Hard
The Fifty Stations Road after dark: an inn that is not there, the dead who do not arrive, lanterns with no one holding them.
Expect · dread, ghosts, curses
Lighter on · politics
Starts · Lantern: The Road After Dark · (Party) The Note at the Jizo Shrine · Festival: Obon at Tasogare

Hardcore

HARDCORE · Very hard
The isles do not forgive. Wounds linger, enemies remember, and nobody comes to save you.
Expect · lethal fights, scarce help, real stakes
Lighter on · rescues
Starts · Bounty: A Price on Your Name · Beast Host: The Crate That Opened · Ashfall Blight: The Grey Banner Waystation

Hunt Night

HUNT · Hard
Night falls on the isles and the monsters come out: demons in the north, spirits on the road, beasts in the wood.
Expect · monster hunts, dread, survival
Lighter on · court politics
Starts · Breath Hunter: The Breathing Yards · (Party) The Sword Under the Great Mound · Hinode Hold: The Sunrise Wall

Multiplayer

TABLE · Medium
Two to five players, one story: every character gets the spotlight, nobody is talked over.
Expect · shared scenes, split parties, party choices
Lighter on · one hero's plot
Starts · any (Party) start

Rising Legend

POWER FANTASY · Easy
The party is the story the isles will tell: techniques that land, rivals who fall, crowds that remember names.
Expect · big fights, fast fame, spectacle
Lighter on · grinding setbacks
Starts · Tournament: The Open Bouts · Duelist: The Ninety-Nine · Samurai: The Waking Hall · Delver: The Salvage Licence · The Otherworld: Behind the Gates

Shinobi Contracts

CONTRACTS · Medium
The hidden villages' trade: missions taken, scrolls stolen, targets watched, and no questions asked.
Expect · stealth, missions, betrayal
Lighter on · open battles
Starts · Shinobi: The Cedar Training Grounds · (Party) The Lot in the Black Warehouse · Toll: The Cedar Pass Queue

Slow Burn

ROMANCE · Easy
A story of people: bonds, trust and slow-burning love among the isles' cast, with the world's dangers behind it.
Expect · relationships, quiet scenes, devotion
Lighter on · constant danger
Starts · Slow Burn: The Long Watch · (Party) The White Hare · Guest: The Lord's Retinue

Teahouse Days

SLICE OF LIFE · Very easy
Lanes, inns, markets and festivals: the isles' everyday life, with danger far off the road.
Expect · small jobs, neighbours, food, festivals
Lighter on · fights and dread
Starts · Teahouse: The Tea Benches · Fifty Stations Road: The Post Stable · Pilgrim: The Spring Sun Rite

The Long Night

LATE WAR · Very hard
The emperor is dying, the seals are failing, and the Lord of the Long Night is coming south.
Expect · war in the north, beasts, last stands
Lighter on · quiet life
Starts · Hinode Hold: The Sunrise Wall · Orphan: The Lodge on the Wall · Hyoga Reach: The Ice Pier · Breath Hunter: The Breathing Yards

The Tournament

TOURNAMENT · Medium
The Capital Tournament is the centre of the isles: bouts, bets, scouts and the houses' champions.
Expect · duels, rivals, honour
Lighter on · wilderness travel
Starts · Tournament: The Open Bouts · Duelist: The Ninety-Nine · Ronin: The Captains' Tables

Wanderer

SANDBOX · Medium
The isles as they are: no push toward any line, every road open, the world moving on its own.
Expect · travel, chance meetings, jobs you choose
Lighter on · authored plots pressing in
Starts · Miyako Plain: Dawn on the Dawn Bridge · Saikai Free Port: The Foreign Quay · Fifty Stations Road: The Post Stable
