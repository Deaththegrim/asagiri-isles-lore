The Asagiri Isles, After the War

[Recommended Level: 20-60 · Danger: Deadly]

The Asagiri Isles after the Succession War: a hollow Regent's peace. The Regent won the field and lost the realm: the emperor is dead with no heir, Miyako half-burned and ruled on Saikai's debt, the Fifty Stations Road cut by Straw Mask barricades, Hinode's valleys held by the demon court, Kagegi and Hayate still at war, the Sealing Vaults cracked.
