The Otherworld

[Recommended Level: 10-40 · Danger: Severe]

One country behind the isles' oldest gates, five faces around a trampled yard: The Training Ground: The trampled yard at the Otherworld's heart, where the five quarters meet: lodges for eight masters, a mess, a bunk hall and the ring of gate posts that lead home. A party that stays together trains here while any one of them explores. The Far Shore: The dead's side of the Weeping Torii: a grey tide that never turns, ferry stones, the Veil Court's true seat and a drowned causeway where what the ferrymen could not carry waits. The Hidden Court: The yokai country behind the kodama grove and the fox road: lanterns in old trees, a walk where the masked promenade, a court with its own law, and a hollow tree that goes down further than it should. The Five Sanctums: Five nature worlds in one quarter, one for each Element: stone, water, fire, wind and the void between, each kept by what the Element made, each a sanctum and a trial. Sun Peak: The breath ascetics' mountain, where the air thins until the lungs change: steps, a boulder every hunter has tried to cut, and a dark side where demons test what the breath is worth. The Hundred Blades: The sword saints' terraces, a hundred of them climbing a cliff, a whetstone that remembers every blade, and a hundredth terrace where the saint waits.
