Tomoshibi Isle

[Recommended Level: 5-25 · Danger: Moderate]

Tomoshibi Isle, the lamplight isle, lies a morning's row west of Saikai: a curled leaf of cliff, pine and terrace with a dead lighthouse on its northern rock. The keeper's family left thirty years ago, after the night the lamp went dark and three ships went down beneath it. Saikai calls it unlucky. A widow on the harbour post holds the deed.
