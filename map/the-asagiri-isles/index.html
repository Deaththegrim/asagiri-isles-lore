The Asagiri Isles

[Recommended Level: 1-60 · Danger: Deadly]

The Asagiri Isles are a crescent of mountains and rice plains under a morning mist, with a volcanic isle to the south and an ice isle to the north. A puppet emperor reigns in Miyako and a regent rules in his name; samurai houses hold the plains, two hidden shinobi villages sell their blades from the cedar mountains, and on the northwest cliffs the Veil Court's gate takes in the dead. Yokai courts keep the deep woods, onmyoji ward the cities, breath hunters keep the night out of the north, and curse-bearers walk the ash of an old war. Every art in the isles draws on one current, the Soul: shinobi shape it with seals, samurai forge it into blades, Veil Officers are made of it, yokai are its wild overflow. No one rules all of it, and the post road runs between them all.
