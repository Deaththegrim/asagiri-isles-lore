Granary: The Counted Shifts

GRANARY · Miyako Plain · guided
📍 Shogunate Rice Granary, The Asagiri Isles. A great timber granary on stilts where the plain's tax rice is stored before it goes to the capital.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 3-15)

The Shogunate's rice granary feeds the castle while the villages starve, and the Straw Masks have been counting its guards for months.
🎭 Sergeant Ishida, Male, Sergeant of the granary guard. Tired eyes, padded armour with a crane crest. Show him the pledge papers mean his granary feeds the guild, not the plain, and he looks the other way one night.
