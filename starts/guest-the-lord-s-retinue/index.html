Guest: The Lord's Retinue

GUEST · Fifty Stations Road · guided
📍 Seventh Station Inn, The Asagiri Isles. One of the busiest post stations: an official inn for lords, cheaper inns for everyone else, and a lantern gate that closes at dusk.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 1-10)

A lord's retinue arrives at the Seventh Station Inn tomorrow, the innkeeper is too friendly, and the Red Lanterns know the route.
🎭 Innkeeper Jinbei, Male, keeper of the Seventh Station Inn. Receding hair, brown apron over a kimono. Warn the lord's retinue, or stand guard, and he will never forgive you; or he will thank you forever, if the bandits lose.
🎭 Old Hisa of the Seventh Station, Female, the innkeeper's mother. Bright-eyed elderly woman in a plain kimono waiting at a post-road inn gate with a paper crane. Ask about the couriers of twenty years ago and she smiles at one name.
