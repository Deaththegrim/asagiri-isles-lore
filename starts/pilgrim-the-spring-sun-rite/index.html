Pilgrim: The Spring Sun Rite

PILGRIM · Miyako Plain · guided
📍 Sun-Rite Shrine of the Plain, The Asagiri Isles. An old shrine of the sun rites where the court's priests come each spring to bless the plain's rice.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 1-12)

You came to the Sun-Rite Shrine to pray for the emperor's waking, and the priest who leads the prayer has stopped believing it.
🎭 Priest Ujibe Kiyonari, Male, a priest of the sun rites. Kind worried eyes. Tell him what the burned talisman means and he tells you what the priests fear about the emperor's death.
🎭 Shrine Maiden Hayashida Yae, Female, a shrine maiden of the plain's sun shrine. Help fund the dawn rite and she teaches you a sun sutra.
