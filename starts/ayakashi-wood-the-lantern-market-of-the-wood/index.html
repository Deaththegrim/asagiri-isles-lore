Ayakashi Wood: The Lantern Market of the Wood

AYAKASHI WOOD · any tradition · guided
📍 Kitsunebi Crossing, The Asagiri Isles. A crossing town at the wood's edge where the human road and the yokai paths meet, kept by a fox shrine that both sides respect.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 10-40)

Kitsunebi Crossing, where humans and yokai trade at night under lanterns and nobody's money is quite what it seems.
🎭 Priestess Kohaku, Female, the priestess of the fox shrine at Kitsunebi Crossing. Bring her a dispute between wood and road and she brokers it fairly.
🎭 Lantern-Market Go-Between Konta, Male, Go-between of the lantern market. Ask him who he warns and he grins and asks what it is worth.
