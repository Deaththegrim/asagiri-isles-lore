Seimei Bureau: The Ward-Wall Walk

SEIMEI BUREAU · any tradition · guided
📍 Seimei, The Asagiri Isles. The onmyoji city on the lake, laid out on a grid that is itself a ward, with an observatory tower at its heart and talismans on every gate.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 5-25)

Seimei, city of onmyoji, where every wall is warded and every ward is failing a little more each month.
🎭 Bureau Petition-Clerk Tokiwa, Female, Petition-clerk at Seimei's lake gate. Settle a petition she renamed and she tells you whose familiar it really was.
🎭 Gate Guard Isozaki Gonzo, Male, the lake gate's guard. Win at dice against him and he tells you about the fox.
