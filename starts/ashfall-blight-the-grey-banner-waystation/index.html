Ashfall Blight: The Grey Banner Waystation

ASHFALL BLIGHT · any tradition · guided
📍 Haibara, The Asagiri Isles. The one town on the edge of the Blight, a waystation built from the timbers of the war's fortresses, where curse-bearers and the desperate trade.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 20-45)

Haibara, the last town before the Blight's ash: pilgrims, curse-hunters, relic-sellers and the dead who will not lie down.
🎭 Fukube Sanzaemon, Male, a rich patron who keeps a lacquered house in Haibara and summers on a pleasure boat. A broad delighted smile that never reaches his eyes, a plum silk kimono under a black haori with a gold gourd crest. He will pay for your tea before you sit down, and ask for nothing. Yet.
🎭 Yukiha, Female, a curse-bearer of Haibara. Long pale hair, sad calm grey eyes, long-sleeved dark kimono. She warns you off; stay anyway, carefully, and she starts to warn you less.
🎭 Keeper Hisako, Female, keeper of the Grey Banner Waystation in Haibara. Cropped grey hair, grey banner apron. Find another way to feed the sealed well and she owes you her life.
🎭 Waystation Clerk Sumi, Female, Clerk of the grey banner waystation in Haibara. End the horror that killed her husband and she gives you his blade.
