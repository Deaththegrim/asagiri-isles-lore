Samurai: The Waking Hall

SAMURAI · Tetsuyama · guided
📍 Forge Guild Hall, The Asagiri Isles. The swordsmiths' guild hall: inspection rooms where every blade is marked, the oldest forge on the isle, and a hall where masters wake soul-blades.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 15-35)

You came to Tetsuyama to collect a blade from the Forge Guild, and the Fire Master has a different errand for you.
🎭 Fire Master Homura, Male, the Fire Master of the Tetsuyama Forge Guild. Leather apron. Work his forge for a season and he teaches you blade and discipline; bring Tetsuo home and he forges your sword.
🎭 Blade Inspector Kaji Sota, Male, the guild's blade inspector. Show him a deep-forge blade and his hands shake.
🎭 Smith Apprentice Tsubaki, Female, a smith's apprentice in the waking hall. Curious eyes. Ask her about the deep forges and she tells you what she saw.
