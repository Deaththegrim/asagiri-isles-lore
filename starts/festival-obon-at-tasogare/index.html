Festival: Obon at Tasogare

FESTIVAL · Veilgate · guided
📍 Tasogare, The Asagiri Isles. The shrine city on the cliffs, its streets climbing to the great pale torii at the edge where the dead pass through, its lanterns drifting out over the Sea of Fog at night.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 10-40)

For three nights the dead come home to Tasogare. Lanterns float out from the cliffs, and one floats against the current.
🎭 Captain Shirasagi Ren, Female, a captain of the Veil Court's Ferry Division. Cold violet eyes, immaculate black officer's coat. Bring her a soul she failed to reach and she looks at you for the first time.
🎭 Officer Kurose Akio, Male, an officer of the Veil Court's Records wing. White officer's coat with a records crest. Show him the cut rope's end and he tells the party exactly whose order he followed, as if that ends it.
🎭 Lantern-Seller Oiwa, Female, a lantern-seller at the Pilgrim Post in Tasogare. Look at her list of lanterns that turn back.
