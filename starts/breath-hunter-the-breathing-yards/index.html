Breath Hunter: The Breathing Yards

BREATH HUNTER · Hinode Hold · guided
📍 Hinode Fortress, The Asagiri Isles. The Breath Hunters' fortress on the crag above Asahi Gate: barracks, training yards for the eight breathing schools, and a forge where sun-steel is worked.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 15-45)

You are a sworn hunter of Hinode Hold, and a village the daylight demon gave no warning of has burned.
🎭 Commander Asahina Tetsu, Female, Commander of the Breath Hunters of Hinode. Cropped grey hair, hard eyes, dark hunter's uniform with a sunrise haori. Go north for her and ask the question she cannot, and she trains you.
🎭 Hinode Saya, Female, a Blossom Breath hunter. Sad steady eyes, dark hunter's uniform with a blossom-pattern haori. Survive a Demon Night at her side.
