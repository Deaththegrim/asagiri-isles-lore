Ronin: The Captains' Tables

RONIN · Saikai Free Port · guided
📍 Ronin Hiring Hall, The Asagiri Isles. A big drinking hall where masterless swords wait for work and clients come to hire them.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 3-18)

You are a masterless sword in Saikai with an empty purse and a reputation, good or bad, that walked in before you.
🎭 Tonbo the Upside-Down, Male, a lanky ronin who hangs around the Hiring Hall upside down on a beam more often than. Sleeveless kimono open at the chest. Make him laugh in a fight; he teaches the ones who do.
🎭 Captain Ishimura, Male, captain of the Ishimura band. Help him turn the two bands on the people who sent them and you have a seat at his table.
🎭 Gonroku the Broker, Male, Gonroku, broker of the Ronin Hiring Hall, seventy, one-armed. Worn dark kimono. Take his small contracts and keep them, and he teaches you the road; break one and the boards close to you.
