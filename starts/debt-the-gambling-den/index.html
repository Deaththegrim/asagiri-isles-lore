Debt: The Gambling Den

DEBT · Saikai Free Port · guided
📍 Lantern Row Gambling Den, The Asagiri Isles. The loudest den on Lantern Row: dice, cards, sake and a debt book kept by a smiling woman.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 1-12)

A Mizuchi captain's son owes the Lantern Row Gambling Den more than his life is worth, and you sat down at his table.
🎭 Smiling Okon, Female, the smiling woman who keeps the debt book at the loudest gambling den on Lantern Row. Bright red kimono. Win at her table honestly and she likes you; buy the captain's son's debt and you own a Mizuchi heir.
🎭 Utsugi of the Nails, Female, a curse-bearer the same age as the character. A grey layered kimono with black prayer strips. She will show you her scars, and then ask to see yours.
🎭 Mizuchi Toranosuke, Male, the admiral's youngest son. Open sea-blue kimono with a wave crest. Get him out of the debt and he owes you a ship; tell his father and he never forgives you.
