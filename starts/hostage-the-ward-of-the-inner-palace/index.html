Hostage: The Ward of the Inner Palace

HOSTAGE · Miyako Plain · guided
📍 The Inner Palace, The Asagiri Isles. Behind its own walls inside the capital, the emperor's palace keeps the old rites: cypress roofs, raked gravel, poets and priests and court ladies in layered silks.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 1-15)

You were given to the court as a hostage-ward for your family's good conduct. Tonight the princess you guard asked you a question.
🎭 Captain Mori Sayo, Female, captain of Princess Ayame's sworn guard. Short black hair, stern eyes, dark armour over a white lotus surcoat. Show her you would take Ayame's side against the court and she lets you past the screens.
🎭 Dowager Empress Shoshi, Female, the emperor's widowed elder sister-in-law and the senior lady of the court. Calm dark eyes, a white lotus embroidered at the collar. Serve the Lotus Ward without asking about Ayame's past and she trusts you with her letters; ask about the past and she decides what you are.
🎭 Princess Ayame, Female, the Dowager's ward. Bright mischievous dark eyes. She tests you with teasing and small betrayals offered as gifts; refuse to use her when you could and the game stops.
