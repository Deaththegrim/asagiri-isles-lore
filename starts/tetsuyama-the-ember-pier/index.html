Tetsuyama: The Ember Pier

TETSUYAMA · any tradition · guided
📍 Hibana, The Asagiri Isles. The largest forge town on the volcano's slopes: chimneys glowing all night, the ring of hammers, hot springs used for quenching, and masters who choose their customers.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 15-40)

Hibana under the volcano: forges, soul-steel, a theatre troupe on the pier, and a mask that has been worn too often.
🎭 Troupe-Master Sanshiro, Male, the master of a travelling theatre troupe. Colourful coat. See the play and ask about the masks.
🎭 Old Kanetsugu, Male, the master of the forge on Hibana's Forge Row that has been cold for twenty years. Learn from him how to put a spirit to rest.
