Beast Host: The Crate That Opened

BEAST HOST · Saikai Free Port · guided
📍 Smugglers' Caves, The Asagiri Isles. Sea caves under the cliffs north of the bay, flooded at high tide and dry at low.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 8-20)

A smuggling crate opened at sea and a child walked out of it. You found her in the caves, and now everyone is hunting you both.
🎭 Hana, Female, a girl of ten hiding in the Smugglers' Caves. Tangled black hair, wary large eyes, ragged oversized shirt. Win her trust with food and patience, and keep the boots away, and she lets you take her somewhere safe.
