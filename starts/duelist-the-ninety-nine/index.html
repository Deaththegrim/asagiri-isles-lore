Duelist: The Ninety-Nine

DUELIST · Fifty Stations Road · guided
📍 Duelists' Teahouse, The Asagiri Isles. A teahouse on the road where the Hundred-Duel Circuit's duelists stop to sign books and find opponents.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 3-15)

A duelist with ninety-nine duels waits at the Duelists' Teahouse for an opponent no one will accept. You just walked in.
🎭 Kagero the Sheathed, Male, a thin, polite swordsman in a faded travelling coat who sits in the teahouse's signing. Faded travelling coat over a plain grey kimono. Sit with him through a night without moving.
🎭 Ochiba, Female, a wandering duelist of the Hundred-Duel Circuit. Short ragged black hair, tired sharp eyes, worn dark hakama and a faded haori. Spar with her and she rates you; save her from the house's assassins and she does not know what to do with you.
