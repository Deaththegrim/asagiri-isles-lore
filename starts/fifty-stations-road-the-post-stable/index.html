Fifty Stations Road: The Post Stable

FIFTY STATIONS ROAD · any tradition · guided
📍 Oiwake Station, The Asagiri Isles. The crossroads station where the highway forks: north for the capital, south down to Ember Strait, east for the marsh coast, west along the ash for the port.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 1-15)

Oiwake Station, the busiest stop on the post road: toll barriers, red lanterns in the pines, and riders who do not come back.
🎭 Station Master Ogiwara Tokuzo, Male, the post master of Oiwake Station's stables. Stable master's jacket and apron. Name the rider in front of his men and his hands go to the water bucket.
🎭 Post Clerk Tsuge Hanbei, Male, Clerk of Oiwake Station's barrier board. Earn his trust on three bounties and he hands you the missing list.
