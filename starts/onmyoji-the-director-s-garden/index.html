Onmyoji: The Director's Garden

ONMYOJI · Seimei Bureau · guided
📍 Bureau of Yin and Yang, The Asagiri Isles. The college of onmyoji: lecture halls of talisman scribes, a library of star charts and calendars, and examination rooms where licences are won and lost.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 5-25)

You hold a Bureau licence, or are about to, and the Director has called you to his garden on a matter he cannot put in writing.
🎭 Director Tachibana Seiran, Male, Tachibana Seiran. Long white hair, calm wise eyes. Bring him a question he cannot answer and he takes you as a student; bring him Sorei and he owes you.
