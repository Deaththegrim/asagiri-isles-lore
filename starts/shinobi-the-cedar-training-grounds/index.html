Shinobi: The Cedar Training Grounds

SHINOBI · Kurosugi Range · guided
📍 Kagegi Village, The Asagiri Isles. The hidden village of the southwestern slopes, built into a mist-filled valley among black cedars: training grounds, clan compounds, and the mission hall that sells its shinobi to the realm.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 5-30)

You are Kagegi-trained, or Kagegi-tolerated, on the morning after the joint rank exam went wrong.
🎭 Kagegi Sojun, Male, head of Kagegi Village. Calm unreadable eyes. Take his scroll contract and keep his rule (read none of it), and he trains you.
🎭 Kagegi Rin, Female, a Kurobane shadow-binder, twenty, top of her year at Kagegi and certain of it. Cold dark eyes. She calls you dead weight; carry your share on a mission and she stops.
🎭 Kagekiri Jin, Male, a shinobi of the hidden villages the same age as the character. A sleeveless charcoal shinobi jacket. He will tell you exactly how many times he has beaten you, and offer to make it one more.
