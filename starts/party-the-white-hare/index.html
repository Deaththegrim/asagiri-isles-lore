(Party) The White Hare

PARTY · 2-5 players · Seimei Bureau · guided
📍 Bureau Dormitories, The Asagiri Isles. Long dormitories where Bureau students sleep in rows, study by candle and break curfew by the back window.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 5-10)

A Bureau student keeps seeing a white hare on the Lake of Stars, and boats keep sinking. She has asked the only people who listened.
🎭 Hiiragi Nanase, Female, an onmyoji of the Bureau the same year as the character. A white onmyoji robe with blue cords. She will point out the flaw in your last talisman, then ask, quietly, how you hold your nerve.
🎭 Student Kunimitsu, Male, a senior Bureau student. Short black hair, white robes with a knotted white sash. Argue the creed with him honestly and he listens, for the first time in a year.
🎭 Student Hotaru, Male, a first-year Bureau student. Messy hair, sleepy eyes. Follow him on a sleepwalk and you see what the hare wants.
