Masks: The Face at the Grey Gate

MASKS · Miyako Plain · guided
📍 Miyako Castle, The Asagiri Isles. The regent's white castle above the capital: keep, councils and the crane banners of House Takamori.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 5-25)

The Treasury Magistrate is forgetting her own name, and she has just asked you whether you have met her before.
🎭 Hayase Ayane, Female, a samurai of a minor house the same age as the character. Sharp grey eyes, a white kimono under black-lacquered do and kote. She will bow, give you her name and her house's, and ask yours as though it were a challenge. It is.
🎭 Roll-Keeper Tadokoro, Male, a dry, careful archivist of the Council Hall who keeps the rolls of retainers of every. Ask about House Ikoma's roll and he shows you the scraped-out name.
🎭 Treasury Magistrate Sakuma Chiyo, Female, Sakuma Chiyo, a treasury magistrate of the Shogunate, forty, grey-robed and ink-stained. Tired sharp eyes. Take her convoy job and she pays from her own purse; find out who has been wearing her face and she owes you her name.
