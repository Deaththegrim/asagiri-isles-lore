(Party) The Lot in the Black Warehouse

PARTY · 2-5 players · Kurosugi Range · guided
📍 Hayate Ridge Village, The Asagiri Isles. The wind village on the ridges above the passes: houses lashed against the gales, wind chimes on every eave, wolf pens and herb gardens.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 5-28)

The forbidden Kagegi scroll has been sold to an auction in Saikai. Hayate wants it, and hires outsiders to beat a Kagegi team to it.
🎭 Hayate Oboro, Male, head of Hayate Village. Wind-swept grey-streaked hair, laughing eyes. Find his missing genin and he tells you a name worth a war.
🎭 Hayate Tsumugi, Female, a Hayate ridge-runner. Short messy light brown hair, bright laughing eyes. Run the ridges with her and keep the secret she tests you with.
