After the War: The Demon Peace

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

AFTER THE WAR · envoys · Hinode Hold · a truce
📍 Asahi Gate, Under the Broken Wall, The Asagiri Isles, After the War. The hunters' town after the war, under siege in all but name: the dawn bell rings twice now, once for the hunt riding out and once for the count of those who did not come back.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 29-50)

The north has a truce with the Northern Court, and you carry it: the Regent's escort for the demon envoy, at a broken wall where every hunter wants the envoy dead.
🎭 Kasane of the Long Night, Female, a ranked demon of the Northern Court. Red eyes, black and white court armour. She is the only demon in the south under a truce banner, and she will answer any courteous question once.
🎭 Master Chizuru, Female, a Breath Master of the Hold. Hunter's black coat and scarf. Ride north with her and the party sees where the patrol rolls go.
🎭 Child Hunter Kaito, Male, a Breath Hunter recruit. Messy dark hair, oversized dark uniform. Find out what became of his brother and he will follow you north.
