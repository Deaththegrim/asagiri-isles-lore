Courier: The Riderless Horse

COURIER · Miyako Plain · guided
📍 Miyako, The Asagiri Isles. The walled castle city: the regent's keep and the emperor's palace side by side, samurai mansions, markets, temples, and the river bridges where duels are fought at dawn.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 1-25)

The Bureau's talisman courier vanished on the post road. His horse came home alone to Miyako, and you were the one who caught it.
🎭 Magistrate's Scribe Oriha, Female, Scribe of the capital magistrate's public board at the riverside post house. Neat hair, pale blue official kimono. Ask her about the names on the regent's bounties and she goes quiet, then asks if you can keep a secret.
🎭 Ofuku of the Post House, Female, keeper of the Riverside Post House at Miyako's coach yard. Apron over a blue kimono. Eat her food and listen to her gossip and she tells you what came back to the yard without its rider.
