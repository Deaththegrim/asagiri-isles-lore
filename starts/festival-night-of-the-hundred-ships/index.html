Festival: Night of the Hundred Ships

FESTIVAL · Saikai Free Port · guided
📍 Saikai, The Asagiri Isles. The free port town on the bay: warehouses, quays, foreign ships at anchor among the junks, and lantern-lit streets that never close.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 1-20)

Saikai blesses the trading fleet tonight: a hundred lit ships, a temple bell, and every smuggler in the port pretending to be a merchant.
🎭 Harbourmaster Watanuki, Male, the guild's harbourmaster. Sharp eyes, dark blue guild coat with brass buttons. Find the escort that took the humming chest north and he shows you the second ledger.
🎭 Notice-Keeper Kagura, Female, keeper of the Saikai harbour post's notice board. Indigo work kimono. Bring back proof of a bounty and she shows you the back board.
🎭 Widow Toki, Female, an old widow who sells bait and mends sails at the harbour post. A faded indigo kimono with tied-back sleeves. Sit down at her bait stall and she will tell you about the island nobody wants, and what it would cost to take it.
