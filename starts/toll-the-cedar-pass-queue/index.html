Toll: The Cedar Pass Queue

TOLL · Fifty Stations Road · guided
📍 Cedar Pass Barrier, The Asagiri Isles. A barrier where the road climbs into a pass: an inspection hall, a gate, and guards who check every pass and every woman's face for runaways.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 3-15)

You are in the queue at the Cedar Pass Barrier behind a monk who is not a monk, carrying a letter a lord would kill to keep.
🎭 Captain Nagai, Male, captain of the Cedar Pass Barrier. Flat eyes, Warden's dark coat. Pay him, or make him believe the courtesan is worth less caught, and the gate opens.
🎭 Shinonome Tatsumi, Male, a breath hunter of the character's cohort who counts every demon he kills out loud. Short wild red-brown hair, a black hunter's uniform with the Hinode sun crest. He will tell you his count and demand yours, then challenge you to beat it by dawn.
🎭 Komurasaki, Female, a young monk in a straw hat waiting in the barrier queue. Get her through the barrier and she gives you the letter she stole.
