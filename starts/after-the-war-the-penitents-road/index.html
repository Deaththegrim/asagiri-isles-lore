After the War: The Penitents' Road

AFTER THE WAR · veterans · Ashfall Blight · a penance road
📍 Haibara, On the Grudge Line, The Asagiri Isles, After the War. The blight's town after the war, no longer at the blight's edge but deep inside it: the grey has spread north past the road, the sealed well smokes day and night, and a ring of new shrines, the Grudge Line, has been raised a day's walk north to hold it.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 33-50)

You fought in the war and lived. Now you walk the penitents' road in grey with a thousand others, and the road is making you sick.
🎭 Bearer of the Shared Mark, Yukiha, Female, a curse-bearer of Haibara after the war. Long pale hair, sad calm grey eyes, a long dark coat. She warns you off; stay anyway, carefully, and she starts to warn you less.
🎭 Keeper Sanae of the Grudge Line, Female, a keeper of the Grudge Line. Hold a failing shrine with her for one night and she will show you the whole line.
🎭 The Girl Who Walks at Night, Bone-Picker's Daughter Sae, Female, the bone-picker's daughter after the war. Watch her at night and you see what the bite is doing.
🎭 The Keeper Holding the Well, Keeper Hisako, Female, keeper of the Grey Banner Waystation after the war. Cropped grey hair, grey banner apron. Find another way to feed the sealed well and she owes you her life.
