Hinode Hold: The Sunrise Wall

HINODE HOLD · any tradition · guided
📍 Asahi Gate, The Asagiri Isles. The town under the breath hunters' fortress: hunters' mess halls, forges, and the gate through which every hunt rides out at dawn.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 15-45)

Asahi Gate, the Hold's southern door: hunters, orphans, sun-steel, and the long north beyond the wall.
🎭 Master Rensuke, Male, a Breath Master of the Hold. Hunter's black coat. Sit with him through a night watch and the party hears him not breathe.
🎭 Physician Ogata, Male, the fortress physician. Blood-spotted apron over dark clothes. Watch the sleepers with him and the two masters' secret is yours.
