After the War: The Regent's Camp

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

AFTER THE WAR · camp followers · Miyako Plain · intrigue
📍 Tournament Grounds, The Regent's Camp, The Asagiri Isles, After the War. The tournament grounds turned into the Regent's field camp: tents where the house pavilions stood, a quartermaster's stockade over the bettors' stands, and the sand ring used for drill.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 23-36)

The war is won and the Regent's army is camped on the old tournament grounds. You follow it: you cook, carry, sing, bet or steal for it, and the camp is starving.
🎭 Watch-Captain Okami Tenzen, Male, a lean, wolf-faced captain of the capital's watch with a pipe always in his teeth. Dark watch uniform with a haori. Take his thrust and stay standing.
🎭 Hazuki the Camp Singer, Female, a singer of the Regent's field camp. Warm brown eyes, faded patched kimono with a soldier's scarf. She sings about you before she speaks to you, and the song is never quite kind.
🎭 Otama the Bookmaker, Female, the Tournament's leading bookmaker. Clever eyes, bright patterned kimono. Bring her a champion's secret and she pays in coin or in odds; fight in the ring and she sells you the field.
