Rice: The Hidden Seed

RICE · Miyako Plain · guided
📍 Hundred Fields Village, The Asagiri Isles. A rice village of thatched roofs and flooded paddies, typical of the hundred that feed the capital.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 1-10)

Hundred Fields Village has hidden its seed rice, and the tax collector arrives tomorrow with soldiers.
🎭 Headman Tahei, Male, Headman of Hundred Fields Village. Thin grey hair, patched brown kimono. Stand between the village and the tax collector tomorrow and the village hides you forever after.
🎭 Widow Okiku of the Fields, Female, a farmer's widow who works her husband's paddy alone and cooks for the headman's house. Tired young widow in a plain kimono holding a paper receipt in a farmhouse kitchen. Ask about her husband and she shows you the paid receipt.
