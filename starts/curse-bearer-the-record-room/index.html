Curse-Bearer: The Record Room

CURSE-BEARER · Ashfall Blight · guided
📍 Curse-Hunters' Chapterhouse, The Asagiri Isles. A fortified chapterhouse on the edge of the Blight where the Curse-Hunter Brotherhood trains, breaks curses for a fee and keeps a record of every curse-maker it has killed.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 20-40)

You carry a curse, or hunt them, and the Brotherhood's elder has seen something on the Ash Plain he does not like.
🎭 Brother Jun, Male, a curse-hunter of the Brotherhood, twenty-six, the elder's best student. Calm distant grey eyes. Hunt a curse-maker at his side and he trusts your hands; ask about his sister and the vow's length shrinks.
🎭 Novice Tomo, Female, a novice of the Curse-Hunter Brotherhood. Short hair. Read the founder's journal with her and the Brotherhood's secret is yours.
