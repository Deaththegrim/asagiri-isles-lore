Tomoshibi: The Lamp Relit

TOMOSHIBI ISLE · any tradition · 1-5 players · a household to run
📍 The Workshop, Tomoshibi Isle. A long low workshop on the lane up from the quay: a forge with a cold chimney, a workbench under a collapsed skylight, a stillroom of cracked jars, and an open counter facing the lane where the keeper's family once sold rope, lamp oil and repairs.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 5-20)

The island has been yours for a season. The house has a roof, the forge has a fire, two islanders work for you, and the rest of the ruins are waiting. Run the household: pay it, send it out, and choose who else to bring home.
🎭 Boatwright Hanzaemon, Male, a one-eyed boatwright. Grey cropped hair, a sawdust-covered work apron over a dark kimono. Show him your hands and he will decide whether you are worth teaching how to fix the forge.
