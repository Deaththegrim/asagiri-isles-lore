Mistmarsh Coast: The Stilt Market

MISTMARSH COAST · any tradition · guided
📍 Ashiura, The Asagiri Isles. The largest stilt town of the marsh, built over reed channels on poles of black timber, where the road ends and the boats begin.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 10-30)

Ashiura on its stilts: fish, smugglers, navy patrols, and a rain clan whose poisons nobody can cure.
🎭 Headman Uozumi, Male, Headman of Ashiura. Practical indigo jacket. Help him keep all three masters happy one more month and the town is yours.
🎭 Stilt-Market Crier Funao, Male, Crier of Ashiura's stilt market. Catch him singing a verse wrong and he begs you to take the real bounty instead.
