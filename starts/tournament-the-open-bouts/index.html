Tournament: The Open Bouts

TOURNAMENT · Miyako Plain · guided
📍 Tournament Grounds, The Asagiri Isles. The Capital Tournament's grounds: a sand ring under the castle walls, ringed by stands and the pavilions of the great houses.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 5-25)

The Capital Tournament's open bouts begin today: ronin, village champions and you, for a place before the regent.
🎭 Otama the Bookmaker, Female, the Tournament's leading bookmaker. Clever eyes, bright patterned kimono. Bring her a champion's secret and she pays in coin or in odds; fight in the ring and she sells you the field.
🎭 Ring-Crier Okabe Hisashi, Male, the tournament's crier. Ask him which bout is fixed and he names one, loudly, by accident.
