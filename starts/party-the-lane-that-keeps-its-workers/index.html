(Party) The Lane That Keeps Its Workers

PARTY · 2-5 players · Seimei Bureau · guided
📍 Haunted Teahouse Lane, The Asagiri Isles. A lane of abandoned teahouses on the city's edge where lights are seen at night and laughter heard from empty rooms.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 10-22)

A merchant's workers keep vanishing on Haunted Teahouse Lane, and the lane's old umbrella has asked the party, very politely, to listen.
🎭 Shizume of the Two Shadows, Female, a quiet woman in dark travelling clothes who keeps the empty teahouse on the haunted lane. Short dark hair, sharp calm eyes. Fight through her teahouse in the dark without touching a wall.
🎭 Old Umbrella, Non-binary, a hundred-year-old paper umbrella with one eye and a long tongue that hops on its handle. Persuade the merchant to let the objects stay and Old Umbrella frees the workers.
