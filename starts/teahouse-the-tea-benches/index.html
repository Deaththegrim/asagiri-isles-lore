Teahouse: The Tea Benches

TEAHOUSE · Fifty Stations Road · guided
📍 Rice-Cake Peddlers' Rest, The Asagiri Isles. A cluster of stalls selling rice cakes and tea to walkers, the best gossip on the road for the price of a cup.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 1-6)

Your feet gave out on the post road and Granny Kiku put you to work for your tea. The rice cakes are good, the gossip is better, and nobody is chasing you.
🎭 Granny Kiku, Female, the old woman at the end stall of the Rice-Cake Peddlers' Rest. Patched kimono. Buy a cake and listen and she tells you where the courier went.
