Half-Yokai: The Great Tree

HALF-YOKAI · Ayakashi Wood · guided
📍 Two-Blooded Refuge, The Asagiri Isles. A hidden village under a great tree where children of humans and yokai live together, behind wards no hunter finds.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 10-30)

You are two-blooded, and the Refuge in the Ayakashi Wood took you in. Old Kagari has a favour to ask.
🎭 Old Kagari, Female, the founder of the Two-Blooded Refuge, who looks seventy and has looked it for a century. Bring the coast child home and the Refuge is yours to call on.
🎭 Kiyo of the Refuge, Female, a fox-blooded guardian of the Refuge's ward ring. Long red-gold hair, sharp amber eyes. She tests you with a trick; see through it and stay kind.
