Veil Officer: The Great Pale Torii

VEIL OFFICER · Veilgate · guided
📍 Tasogare, The Asagiri Isles. The shrine city on the cliffs, its streets climbing to the great pale torii at the edge where the dead pass through, its lanterns drifting out over the Sea of Fog at night.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 10-40)

You are an officer of the Veil Court on your first watch at the Great Pale Torii, and the commander has a quiet order for you.
🎭 Commander Kirigaya Shion, Female, Commander of the Veil Court's post at Tasogare. Calm tired eyes, black officer's coat with a white sash. Help her ferry the backlog and she trains you as one of her living recruits.
