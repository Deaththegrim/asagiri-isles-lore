Smuggler: The Tally Pier

SMUGGLER · Mistmarsh Coast · guided
📍 Ashiura Fish Docks, The Asagiri Isles. The working docks of the coast town where fishermen land their catch and mend their nets.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 10-15)

You count crates for the Brine Tide on the Ashiura Tally Pier. One crate counted as tuna is ticking.
🎭 Tally-Man Kanroku, Male, the dock tally man at Ashiura. Open the ticking crate with him and he owes you everything.
