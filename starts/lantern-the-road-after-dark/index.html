Lantern: The Road After Dark

LANTERN · Fifty Stations Road · guided
📍 Ghost Pine Hill, The Asagiri Isles. A hill where the road runs through a cutting, notorious for lanterns that float along it at night with no one carrying them.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 6-15)

Night has fallen between two post stations on Ghost Pine Hill, and a lantern-lit inn is waiting where no inn should be.
🎭 The Grey Pilgrim, Non-binary, a pilgrim in grey robes and a deep straw hat who walks the cutting on Ghost Pine Hill. A plain white mask under it. Walk it gently to its burned shrine and it remembers its name and goes on.
