After the War: The Writ

AFTER THE WAR · landholders · Fifty Stations Road · a district to rebuild
📍 Oiwake Station, Behind the Barricade, The Asagiri Isles, After the War. The crossroads station after the war, right behind the Straw Mask barricade: the honjin inn turned into a peasant army's headquarters, the post stable full of captured horses, and the toll barrier thrown down and burned for warmth.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 20-29)

You fought beside the Straw Masks in the war, and they pay their debts: Fifty Stations Road is yours, if you can hold it. The bridge is burned, the inns are empty and the hill bandits own the ford. Rebuild it.
🎭 Barricade Captain Tora, Female, the Straw Masks' captain of the Oiwake barricade. Short tousled hair, straw mask pushed up on her head. Find the guard who sells papers and the barricade is yours to cross whenever you like.
🎭 Deserter Hanzo of the Road, Male, a deserter from both armies. Straw in his hair. Get him a pardon from either side and he will tell you both sides' passwords.
🎭 Post Clerk Tsuge Hanbei, Clerk Behind the Barricade, Male, Clerk of Oiwake's board after the war. A straw mask hung on the board's corner. Earn his trust on three bounties and he hands you the missing list.
🎭 Gensuke the Medicine-Seller, Medicine-Seller of the Barricade, Male, the travelling medicine-seller after the war. Buy a remedy and he tells you which house on the plain is hiding someone.
