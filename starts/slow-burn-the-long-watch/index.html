Slow Burn: The Long Watch

SLOW BURN · Seimei Bureau · guided
📍 The Old Observatory, The Asagiri Isles. The Bureau's first observatory, a lone tower of bronze instruments on the hills south of the city, kept by a few star-readers who prefer its dark to the city's lanterns.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 5-15)

The Bureau has sent you to keep the star-reader at the Old Observatory company through a season of night watches. She read your death in the stars the first time she saw you, and told you so.
🎭 Tsukimi, Female, a star-reader of the Bureau. Long silver-grey hair, half-lidded dreamy eyes. She reads your death at first meeting; change one fate she read and she stops reading yours.
