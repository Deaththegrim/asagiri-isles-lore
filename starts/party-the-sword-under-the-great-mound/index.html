(Party) The Sword Under the Great Mound

PARTY · 2-5 players · Miyako Plain · guided
📍 Crossroads Gallows, The Asagiri Isles. Where the plain's two great roads cross stands a gallows and a notice board of wanted faces.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 1-10)

Grave robbers at the Old Battlefield of Sekigane woke something under the great mound. A swordsmith at the crossroads knows what.
🎭 Kuchinashi Mio, Female, a Veil Court officer of the character's own intake. Short black hair, a black officer's robe over white. She will quote the regulation you broke, and then ask how it felt.
🎭 Swordsmith Masamura, Male, an itinerant swordsmith. Let him mend your blade and ask about Tetsuyama.
