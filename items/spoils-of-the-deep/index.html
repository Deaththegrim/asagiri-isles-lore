Spoils of the Deep

A ledger-keeper's notes on what comes out of the lairs, and what never comes out of anywhere else.

Every boss that goes down gives up one piece of loot, rolled on the guilds' own quality ladder: Plain 50%, Fine 28%, Superior 15%, Master-made 5.5%, Named 1.5%. A long hunt adds a hoard roll on the way out, a Bounty Head run a second roll for the head, and a Rich Hoard run leans every roll better. The ledger remembers bad luck: each roll that comes up Plain or Fine makes the next one at that lair a little better, until something good comes out. Until a lair has been cleared once, its boss never pays worse than Fine.

Each lair also hides one unique piece, a weapon or armour of its boss's own, found nowhere else and never sold. A boss kill gives it up about one time in 4; the ledger counts the misses, and by the fourth kill it always comes. Once a lair's piece has been taken, that lair pays ordinary loot only. The storyteller names the piece when it drops.

The pieces, as hunters tell them: Kappa River Fords: Kawataro's Riverstone Gauntlet; Smugglers' Caves: The Wreck-Raider's Gaff; Bear Den Ridge: Gonbei's Sake-Barrel Club; Pirate Graves Reef: The Umibozu's Tide Mantle; Old Coast Fort: The Night-Coast Cutlass; Heron Island: The Nure-onna's Scale Leggings; Earth Spider Pits: Broodmother's Silk Haori; Crow Tengu Nests: Karasuba's Black-Feather Yari; Mask Shore: The Devourer's Mask; Burned Village of Kitamura: The Carrion Thrall's Chain-Sickle; The Ash Plain: The Ash Oni's Kanabo; Old Battle Barrow: Gashadokuro Bone Kabuto; Revenant Shaft: The Tsuchigumo's Lantern Gauntlets; Bone Gully: Dairoku's Grave-Digger Spade; Ruins of the Old Capital: The Old Court Wraith-Blade; Frozen Pass of the Long Night: The Night Hunter's Fang Tanto; Frozen Battlefield: The Demon Noble's Frost Armour; Daylight Watch Ridge: The Great Mask's Faceguard; The Drowned Causeway: The Drowned Magistrate's Seal-Staff; The Rooted Deep: The Rooted King's Crown of Roots; Earth Sanctum: The Stone Warden's Bracers; Water Sanctum: The Tide Serpent's Coil Whip; Fire Sanctum: The Furnace Sprite's Ember Blade; Wind Sanctum: The Gale Lord's Cloud Sandals; Void Sanctum: The Hungry Nothing's Empty Cloak; The Dark Side: The Dark-Side Demon's Moon-Claw; The Hundredth Terrace: The Hundredth Duellist's Blade.

After the war the lairs changed hands, and so did their pieces: Kappa River Fords: The Drowned Captain's Sashimono; Smugglers' Caves: The Drowned Bosun's Pike; Bear Den Ridge: The Oni-Hunter's Iron Bow; Pirate Graves Reef: The War-Wreck Lantern Helm; Old Coast Fort: The Powder Store Gauntlets; Heron Island: The Sailor's-Face Mask; Earth Spider Pits: The Eldest's Venom Tanto; Crow Tengu Nests: Karasuba's War-Plunder Mantle; Mask Shore: The War-Fat Devourer's Jaw; Burned Village of Kitamura: The Second Thrall's Sun-Ward Coat; The Ash Plain: The Burned Villages' Tetsubo; Old Battle Barrow: The Warlord's Terms; Revenant Shaft: The New Tsuchigumo's Carapace; Bone Gully: The War Gashadokuro's Rib-Shield; Ruins of the Old Capital: The Greater Wraith's Grudge-Blade; Frozen Pass of the Long Night: The Cold Light Lantern-Staff; Frozen Battlefield: The Thawed Noble's Court Blade; Daylight Watch Ridge: The Army-Drawn Great Mask; Old Battlefield of Sekigane: The Levy General's Standing Banner; The Long Night Wall: The Tunnel-Mother's Digging Claws; Glacier Road: The Unbound Beast's Shed-Skin Coat; The Deep Forges: The Unforged Soul-Blade; Black Ferry Fortress: The Ferry-Sergeant's Mooring Pole; Sunken Well Village: The Well's Stolen Voice. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
