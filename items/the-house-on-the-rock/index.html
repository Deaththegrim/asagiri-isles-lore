The House on the Rock

The household ledger kept at the Workshop counter, with the rules written on its first page.

Every room mended on Tomoshibi Isle opens a post, and the islander who lives there takes it first. A specialist from across the isles can take a post over and do it better; the islander stays on to help.

  • Smith (The Workshop): Boatwright Hanzaemon at 6 Mon a week; Swordsmith Masamura at 12
  • Steward (The Cliff House): Sora of the Lamp House at 4 Mon a week; Ledger-Girl Hosoi Fuku at 10
  • Spring (The Hot Spring Cave): Sarumaru of the Spring at 3 Mon a week; Physician Ogata at 8
  • Cook (The Garden Terraces): Old Goro at 3 Mon a week; Old Hisa of the Seventh Station at 8
  • Drill (The Training Yard): Uncle Kesaburo at 5 Mon a week; Captain Ishimura at 12
  • Priest (The Shrine on the Rock): Shrine-Keeper Akari at 3 Mon a week; Brother Ganseki at 8
  • Boat (Fishers' Huts): Iwa the Net-Mender at 4 Mon a week; Hendrik van Aal at 10
  • Fence (Smugglers' Cove): Captain Uosuke at 5 Mon a week; Gonroku the Broker at 12

Wages are due every 7 days, paid at the Cliff House, with 2 days' grace. Paid, each one warms by 3; each week overdue costs 8, and paying the arrears gives back 4 for every week owed. Nobody is paid or owed while away on an errand. A day's first scene with someone adds 1; every 10 days away from the island costs everyone 2. What you do in the isles reaches them too: each has things they admire and things they hate. A specialist steward keeps the books and says what is due.

At 40 or below they grumble and the post gives half. At 20 or below they leave in 3 days unless the arrears are paid or the grievance put right, and a steward at home warns you first. An islander never leaves; they go quiet and the post gives nothing. A specialist hired for wages alone can walk out, and one who reaches nothing, or leaves with wages owed, betrays you on the way. A specialist who joined through their own quest is sworn: they never fall below 70 and never betray. After the war, they serve under the names the war gave them.

Send one at a time on one errand: gather (2 days), deliver (3 days), trade (2 days), scout (4 days), spy (5 days). At most 3 away at once, and nobody hurt. Skill, loyalty and whether the errand suits them decide how it goes; a boatmaster at home makes scouting safer. Scouting and spying can go badly wrong, and someone taken must be brought home within 10 days or they are lost. Rescued, they never forget it. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
