Blood on the Ledger

A ledger kept at Haibara's Vow Temple, copied for every traveller: what the isles do to a killer.

Infamy is a counter from 0 to 100, kept for each character. Every needless killing adds 15: someone who posed no threat, a surrendered, fleeing or helpless foe, a bystander, a merchant, a servant, a guard doing their job, a child, a quest-giver, anyone in a peaceful scene. It never counts self-defence or defending others, an ordered execution, a contract or bounty the story set up, an accepted duel, a battle or raid, a villain the story set up as a target, or demons, spirits, beasts and yokai. A victim already counted is not counted again.

At 25 you are Notorious: recognised, prices rise, nobles refuse you. At 50 you are Barred: no coach or ferry will carry your party while you travel with it, and lawful offices refuse you. At 75 you are Kill on Sight: guards attack on sight, and bounty hunters come for you every 30 turns. Each brand lifts again only below a lower mark (Notorious below 15, Barred below 40, Kill on Sight below 65).

The way back is penance at Haibara's Vow Temple: confess and atone there, and infamy falls by 10, at most once every 30 turns. One penance never erases one killing. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
