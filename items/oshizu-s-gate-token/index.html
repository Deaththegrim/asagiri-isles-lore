Oshizu's Gate Token

A refugee's wooden entry token, cut for a gate that never opened, swollen with water and warm. Doors, gates and watchmen give way to the bearer a little more readily. Left when the Woman Who Knocks was let in. Found, never sold: Seimei, Behind Two Walls.
