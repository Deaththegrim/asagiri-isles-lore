The Drowned Bosun's Pike

A navy boarding pike, salt-rotted at the haft and sharp at the head, that the Regent's drowned bosun never let go of. The one unique piece of the depths under Smugglers' Caves after the war, left by the drowned bosun of the Regent's sailors. Found, never sold: Smugglers' Caves after the war.
