Tatsuzo's Last Die

A soldier's bone die, worn smooth, the six a little deeper than the rest. Luck runs a little straighter for the bearer, and they always know when the table is crooked. Left when the Soldier in the Black Book was paid out. Found, never sold: Lantern Row Gambling Den, Soldiers' Den.
