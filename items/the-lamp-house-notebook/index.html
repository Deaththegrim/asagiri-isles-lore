The Lamp-House Notebook

A notebook found in the Cliff House, in the keeper's widow's hand, and finished by whoever holds the deed now.

Tomoshibi Isle lies a morning's row west of Saikai. Widow Toki on the harbour post holds the deed and sells it for 150 Mon, or gives it to whoever finishes the island's opening work for her. Boats leave from her post; the crossing back lands at Saikai's harbour. After the war the island is still yours, and the crossing runs from post-war Saikai.

Every room is a ruin until a restoration quest mends it, and each mended room gives something:

  • Soak at The Hot Spring Cave: 15 Health, 10 Soul, once in 8 turns.
  • Drill at The Training Yard: 60 XP, once in 10 turns.
  • Pray at The Shrine on the Rock: 15 Hold, once in 10 turns.
  • Harvest at The Garden Terraces: 2 Rice Ball Bundle and 1 Ginseng Tonic, once in 3 days.
  • Rest at The Cliff House: 40 Health, 20 Soul, 30 Hold, once a day.

The Workshop is a shop front once restored, and its Master's Bench is a second restoration on top of it.

The Workshop takes one order at a time at its counter: a customer, an item, a price in Mon and a day it is due. Deliver it there to be paid and to raise Shop Standing; a late order earns 50 per cent of the standing. Better orders come with standing: Back-Alley Bench at 0, Known Workshop at 100, Sought-After at 250, House of Masters at 450.

Once the Cliff House has a roof again, up to 4 people can live there: a love interest at Oath-Bound or closer, or a wanderer travelling in your party at Comrade or closer. Ask them, one at a time, wherever you are.

Stay away 40 turns or more and the island misses you: a raid on the Net Shed, a storm over the terraces, or smoke at the dead lighthouse, waiting when you row back. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
