The Ferry-Sergeant's Mooring Pole

A drowned ferry-sergeant's iron-shod pole. Pushed against the dead, it moves them back into line. The one unique piece of the depths under Black Ferry Fortress after the war, left by the drowned ferry-sergeant who keeps the lower docks. Found, never sold: Black Ferry Fortress after the war.
