Grave Ash

A twist of paper holding grey ash that stays cold. Sealers mix it into ink that binds the restless dead. Taken from a beaten Ashfall Ghoul, Grudge Wraith, Drowned Grudge, Mud Wraith, Funayurei, Gaki, Well Horror, Grudge Swarm; a market pays 6 Mon.
