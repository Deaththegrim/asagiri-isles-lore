The Second Thrall's Sun-Ward Coat

A thrall's coat lined with stolen sunrise talismans; the Long Night sent it out in daylight in this. The one unique piece of the depths under Burned Village of Kitamura after the war, left by the thrall the Long Night sent to replace the first. Found, never sold: Burned Village of Kitamura after the war.
