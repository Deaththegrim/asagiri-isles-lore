The Post Road

The post road's own guide, sold at every station.

Coaches run between the district towns for 12 Mon a seat, ferries across the straits for 30 Mon. Ask at the station yard to board, name where you are going, and pay. A clerk or ferryman refuses a party travelling with anyone Barred.

Every district's hub keeps a public notice board of bounties, open to anyone, sworn or not: ask the clerk for bounty work, take one, bring proof and be paid.

The isles are twelve districts on one map, from the gentle plain around Miyako to the deadly ice of Hyoga Reach. Each district's danger band covers everything in it: the further from the capital, the harder the road. Some places are on no map: you hear of them before you find them. Outfitters in every town sell garb and masks, and you start with a purse of Mon to spend. On your first day you also find a few goods your past left you: up to 3, at most 20 Mon in all, chosen from your background. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
