The Sea Ledger

A salt-stained ledger from the Landing's net shed, Boatwright Hanzaemon's hull drawings in the back.

Hanzaemon builds three hulls at the Landing, one after another:

  • The fishing boat: 30 hull, 2-6 hands, 2 lots of cargo, coastal lanes only, no guns
  • The coaster: 60 hull, 6-16 hands, 6 lots of cargo, every lane, 1 gun
  • The war junk: 100 hull, 12-30 hands, 4 lots of cargo, every lane, 3 guns

A hull is mended at The Landing, Saikai, Mizuchi Naval Anchorage for 2 Mon a point. A ship sunk at sea is a wreck: the cargo is lost, half the hands drown, and Hanzaemon rebuilds her for 40 Mon (the fishing boat), 150 Mon (the coaster), 400 Mon (the war junk).

Hands are hired at Saikai, Ashiura Fish Docks, Mizuchi Naval Anchorage for 1 Mon a head a week, paid in port on the household's terms (7 days, 2 days' grace). Their morale rises with pay, prizes and quiet crossings; at 30 or below, a quarter of them walk off at the next port.

Four officers can sign on, each through their own quest (sworn) or for wages:

  • Navigator: Old Captain Hamada Gennai, 12 Mon a week; takes a day off any lane of three days or more and calms the weather
  • Gunner: Mizuchi Toranosuke, 12 Mon a week; makes the broadside hit harder
  • Bosun: Aoi of the Wreck, 8 Mon a week; patches hull after a fight and loses fewer hands
  • Pilot: Chidori the Runner, 10 Mon a week; keeps the navy off a smuggling run and helps the ship slip a fight

Ports and what they trade (prices drift week to week; contraband sells for about 2.5 times its price where it is wanted, and raises infamy):

  • The Landing: makes dried fish; wants timber, rice, iron
  • Saikai: makes sake, silk; wants iron, dried fish; buys under the counter: forbidden books
  • Ashiura Fish Docks: makes dried fish, salt; wants rice, timber
  • Foreign Quay Warehouses: makes foreign glass; wants silk, sake; buys under the counter: forbidden books, yokai relics
  • Mizuchi Naval Anchorage: makes timber; wants iron, salt, rice
  • Drifting Fish Market: makes salt; wants sake, silk, foreign glass
  • Black Sand Beach: makes timber; wants salt, dried fish; buys under the counter: foreign powder
  • Heron Island: makes rice; wants iron, sake; buys under the counter: yokai relics
  • Pirate Graves Reef: an anchorage, no market
  • Hibana: makes iron; wants timber, rice, salt; buys under the counter: untaxed salt-iron, foreign powder

14 lanes join the ports, one to four days each. Every crossing makes one roll: a quiet passage, weather, a wreck to pick over, pirates, the drowned, or a navy patrol if the hold carries contraband. A fight lasts at most 3 rounds; order broadside, board, run or ward, and bribe or submit to a patrol (20 Mon and 10 more a contraband lot, or lose the contraband and pay 30 Mon). Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
