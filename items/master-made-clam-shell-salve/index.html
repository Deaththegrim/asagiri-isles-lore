Master-made Clam-Shell Salve

Master-made, from an exotic material at a master's station. A grey-green salve of crushed healing herbs in beeswax, carried in a clam shell. Spread on a wound, it restores a potent amount of health. 24 Mon. Made with the Artisan abilities. Restores 40 Health when used (the engine adds it).
