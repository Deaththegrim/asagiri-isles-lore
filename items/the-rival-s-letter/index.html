The Rival's Letter

A duellist's letter, copied and passed along the circuit: on the rival who grows with you.

Every tradition has one rival: a peer who tests you and is not cruel. You meet yours four times as you grow, when you reach levels 5, 15, 25, 40, each time stronger than before, and never two meetings with any rival closer together than 20 turns. The meeting comes to you wherever you are, on the isles before or after the war.

Each meeting is a bout. Win it, lose it, or win and spare them out of respect: the isles keep the record. The last meeting decides how it ends. Spare them then and they become a friend for good. Win without sparing and they leave the roads. Lose and they walk away the better, and tell you so.

Each of the eight traditions has its own: Shinobi, Samurai, Ronin, Veil Officer, Half-Yokai, Onmyoji, Breath Hunter, Curse-Bearer. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
