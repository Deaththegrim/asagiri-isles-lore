Paths and Ladders

A temple scroll on how every fighter grows.

Every art has a ladder of eight rungs, at skill 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 15 / 18 / 21 / 24. When an art crosses a rung you may buy that rung's ability with an ability point: the engine offers, you choose. A rung you have not taken is not yours.

Your own picks climb too: every Tradition, Element, Birthright and Path opens 4 techniques of its own as its key skill reaches 6 / 12 / 18 / 24, and some pairs of picks open a technique only the two together can learn, and a Veil Officer's blade releases at Blade 12 and takes its final shape at Blade 24. Your creator page lists them under each pick.

Each tradition branches: at level 10 you choose one of its two paths, at 30 the path's elder form arrives, and at 50 the tradition's apex, reachable from either elder form. A Named Soul-Blade has its own two stages, at 15 and 35, as its spirit wakes. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
