The Fief Ledger

A writ-holder's ledger, the first page a copy of a writ with the seal cut out.

After the war, a house or band the party stands at 20 or more with will write them a writ to one broken district (the best standing writes it; no lawful house writes one for the Barred). Claim it at the district's hub, in front of its steward. One writ is all a party gets.

Three projects open at the claim, and three more once two of the first are done. Each is a quest from the steward: pay its Mon, bring its materials (the ship's cargo counts), and do one deed. A finished project stands as a new place beside its ruin and pays every day from then on:

  • Fifty Stations Road, held from Oiwake Station (Barricade Captain Tora keeps the count; Innkeeper Jinbei speaks for the people):
  • Rebuild the Burned Bridge (tier 1): 120 Mon and two lots of timber; pays 3 Mon a day
  • Reopen the Last Inn East (tier 1): 150 Mon and two lots of timber; pays 4 Mon a day and a lot of rice every 7 days for the ship
  • Raise the Cedar Pass Toll (tier 1): 100 Mon and one lot of iron; pays 3 Mon a day
  • Restore the Wayside Jizo (tier 2): 80 Mon and one lot of timber; pays 2 Mon a day
  • Reopen the Porters' Lodge (tier 2): 120 Mon and two lots of rice; pays 4 Mon a day and a lot of timber every 7 days for the ship
  • Light the Ghost Pine Road (tier 2): 90 Mon and one lot of salt; pays 2 Mon a day
  • Ashfall Blight, held from Haibara (Keeper Hisako keeps the count; Headman Gosuke speaks for the people):
  • Dig New Wells (tier 1): 100 Mon and one lot of iron; pays 3 Mon a day
  • Terrace the Ash Plain (tier 1): 150 Mon and two lots of timber; pays 4 Mon a day and a lot of rice every 7 days for the ship
  • Reopen Haibara's Market (tier 1): 120 Mon and one lot of salt; pays 4 Mon a day
  • Clear the Pilgrims' Road (tier 2): 80 Mon and one lot of timber; pays 2 Mon a day
  • Quiet the Grudge Wells (tier 2): 130 Mon and one lot of salt; pays 3 Mon a day
  • Lay the Gully's Dead (tier 2): 90 Mon and one lot of timber; pays 2 Mon a day
  • Mistmarsh Coast, held from Ashiura (Headman Uozumi keeps the count; Farmer Oyone speaks for the people):
  • Dyke the Drowned Village (tier 1): 140 Mon and two lots of timber; pays 4 Mon a day
  • Raise the Stilt Village (tier 1): 110 Mon and two lots of timber; pays 3 Mon a day
  • Replant the Paddies (tier 1): 100 Mon and one lot of salt; pays 4 Mon a day and a lot of rice every 7 days for the ship
  • Build a New Harbour (tier 2): 160 Mon and two lots of iron; pays 5 Mon a day and a lot of salt every 7 days for the ship
  • Retake the Coast Fort (tier 2): 130 Mon and one lot of iron; pays 3 Mon a day
  • Relight the Dragon's Eye (tier 2): 90 Mon and one lot of salt; pays 2 Mon a day

The take piles up day by day; collect it from the steward. Unrest starts at 20 out of 100. It rises 4 for every 3 days the party stays away and 2 a day for every squatting threat still standing; it falls 10 with every finished project, and 20 once when the reeve's own trouble is settled. At 60 the take halves. At 90 the newest work is wrecked and pays nothing until the party puts it right at the steward (30 Mon), which also calms the district to 80. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
