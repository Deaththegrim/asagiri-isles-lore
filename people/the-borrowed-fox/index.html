The Borrowed Fox

A fox spirit who comes to Moonview Bridge on full-moon nights in the shape of the Moon Fox, who died in the war. The student who waits for the Moon Fox has not noticed, or will not. In person: young woman; fox spirit; long silver hair; faint fox ears; pale kimono with moon patterns.

Home Ground: Bridge Crown, After the War at Moonview Bridge, The Mourning Bridge, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Sweet and teasing, a moon fox's voice that is almost right, and a word too careful. Her sister's lilt, almost right, a word too careful each time. Tell: when the boy says her sister's name she answers to it a beat late.
Combat Role: illusions and fox-fire, then gone across the water.
Relationship Hook: Tell the student the truth with her, or keep it, and the Fox Court owes you either way.

Personality

  • Core: will not cross the bridge's crown; ties ribbons to the rail; asks about the Bureau too often
  • Quirk: imitates her sister's laugh, slightly wrong
