Officer Kurose Akio, Clerk of the Endless Lists

A Records officer, thirty-two, who no longer has to cut the bell rope: the bell rings all night for the war's dead, and he writes their names until his fingers bleed. In person: veil court officer; gaunt; black hair cut short; a white officer's coat ink-stained to the elbows; three ledgers and a blade.

Home Ground: Lantern Cliff, After the War at Tasogare, The Long Procession, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Correct and cold, every word by the book. Correct and cold, every sentence a regulation, his pen tapping once per clause. Tell since the war: he still never rings a bell, and does not look up when the tower's bell rings.
Combat Role: an officer's blade by the book, then a seal.
Relationship Hook: Show him the cut rope's end and he tells the party exactly whose order he followed, as if that ends it.

Personality

  • Core: quotes the Court's law; writes until he bleeds; flinches at the bell
  • Quirk: writes everything down
