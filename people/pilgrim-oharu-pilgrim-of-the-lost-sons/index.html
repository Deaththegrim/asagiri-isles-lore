Pilgrim Oharu, Pilgrim of the Lost Sons

The pilgrim after the war, sixty-two, still walking the road shrine by shrine, now asking at each one for every mother's son. In person: pilgrim in white; straw hat with a sutra; staff with a bell; a stamp book thick with seals.

Home Ground: Shaded Verge, After the War at Pine Avenue, The Deserters' Avenue, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Cheerful, chatty and devout; she tells strangers about every shrine she has visited. Chatty and devout, lists shrines on her fingers, bows her words. Laughs warmly at her own sore feet. Tell since the war: she shows the two extra stamps to anyone who will look, and taps the second one twice.
Combat Role: none; a pilgrim's staff and a loud prayer.
Relationship Hook: Help her find her son's last station and she gives you the stamp from the station that is not on the map.

Personality

  • Core: prays at every stone; feeds the crows; talks about her son in the present tense
  • Quirk: shows everyone her stamp book
