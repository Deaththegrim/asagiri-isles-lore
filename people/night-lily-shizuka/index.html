Night-Lily Shizuka

A court dancer, twenty-six, in layered night-lily silks who dances at the poetry evenings and is always where the princess turns to look. In person: elegant court dancer; long black hair with a white lily ornament; layered night-blue silks; a folding fan.

Home Ground: Hall of Poems at The Inner Palace, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Soft and sweet, a dancer's laugh; nothing she says is quite an answer. Soft and sweet, a dancer's breathy laugh behind the fan, never a straight answer. Tell: when the princess's guard captain enters, the laugh stops and she counts the room aloud under her breath.
Combat Role: the fan in close, the needle once, and gone in the crowd.
Relationship Hook: Catch her fan before the dance ends and she offers the party the name of whoever paid her.

Personality

  • Core: laughs behind the fan; never eats at a feast; dances closer each evening
  • Quirk: counts the guards in every room
