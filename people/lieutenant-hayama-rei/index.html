Lieutenant Hayama Rei

Captain Mori's lieutenant, twenty-nine, captain of the princess's guard since Mori fell holding the inner gate. She sleeps across Princess Ayame's door and has not taken a day's leave since the fire. In person: samurai guard captain; short black hair; scar; dark lacquered armour with a white mourning sash; a naginata; stern.

Home Ground: Princess Ayame's Rooms, After the War at The Inner Palace, Without an Emperor, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Hard and clipped, talking like a soldier giving a report; she softens only when the princess is out of earshot. Every sentence a report: who, where, how many. Tell: when someone speaks of the princess's birth she answers too quickly, and then not at all.
Combat Role: a naginata in a doorway, the guard behind her.
Relationship Hook: Prove you would never use the princess and Rei becomes the best ally in the palace.

Personality

  • Core: checks every window twice; calls the princess 'my lady' and nothing else; carries Mori's sash
  • Quirk: sleeps sitting up
