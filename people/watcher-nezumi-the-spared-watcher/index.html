Watcher Nezumi, The Spared Watcher

A Kagegi watcher after the war, twenty-four, who signals the war-line from the outer watchpost and narrates everything he sees, quieter. He was found selling Kagegi movements to a house and spared. In person: watcher shinobi; messy hair; sharp frightened eyes; dark gear; a signal mirror.

Home Ground: Mirror Platform, After the War at Kagegi Outer Watchpost, Watchpost on the Line, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Bored, fast and cheeky; he narrates what he sees through the mirrors. Bored and fast, narrating what he sees like a running commentary, cheeky asides included. He chews something constantly. Tell since the war: his running commentary leaves out the Regent's colours, as if he did not see them.
Combat Role: kunai from height, then running.
Relationship Hook: Catch him spying and he becomes your spy.

Personality

  • Core: polishes mirrors; bets on everything; hides coins in the tower
  • Quirk: narrates out loud
