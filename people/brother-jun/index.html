Brother Jun

A curse-hunter of the Brotherhood, twenty-six, the elder's best student: tall, quiet, bound in prayer strips over a curse he carries on purpose so he can taste others. Shrines hire him; curse-layers know his face. He is courteous to everyone and close to no one, and he keeps every person at a vow's length. In person: tall quiet curse-hunter; black hair tied back; calm distant grey eyes; dark robes with arms bound; an iron-capped staff; reserved.

Home Ground: Record Room at Curse-Hunters' Chapterhouse, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, courteous and distant; every sentence keeps a vow's length between you. When it slips, he sounds his age. Courteous distance, formal phrasing, a vow's measured cadence. Tell: when he cares about someone, the formality slips and he sounds young and tired.
Combat Role: a curse turned back, iron strips that bind, and a staff; methodical and very hard to stop.
Relationship Hook: Hunt a curse-maker at his side and he trusts your hands; ask about his sister and the vow's length shrinks.
Courtship Style: Formal, careful and kind at arm's length; his courtship is letting you rewrap his prayer strips.
Jealousy Style: Goes perfectly courteous to the rival and very quiet with you; asks, later, whether you would rather he left.

Personality

  • Core: rewraps his prayer strips at night; eats standing; says his sister's name only when he is alone
  • Quirk: keeps a vow's length
