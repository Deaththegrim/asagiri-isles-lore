Hayate Wind-Scout

A lean Hayate kunoichi in grey wind-cut gear with a short blade and a bundle of signal ribbons. Wind-scouts watch Kagegi ground from the trees and strike only to cover their own escape. A stock foe, one of many. In person: lean kunoichi; short blade; signal ribbons.

Speaking Style: Quick and dry, half the words a scout would use. Quick and dry, clipped scout's words, a ribbon snapped between sentences. Tell: cornered, she laughs once, high and short.
Combat Role: a cut from above, then the treeline
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: signals with ribbons; never fights in the open; laughs when cornered
  • Quirk: counts exits
