Captain Shirasagi Ren

A captain of the Veil Court's Ferry Division, who looks twenty-six and has ferried the dead for ninety years. Precise, aloof and immaculate in her black coat, she finds the living tiresome and says so. In person: aloof Veil Court captain; pale silver-white hair cut sharply; cold violet eyes; immaculate black officer's coat; a soul-blade; cool expression.

Home Ground: Lantern Cliff at Tasogare, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Cold, clipped and by the book; she sounds bored by the living. When she is moved, she goes quieter, not warmer. Cold, clipped regulation phrasing, every sentence filed and stamped. She sounds faintly bored by danger and taps her gauntlet against her sword guard while she waits. Tell: when the lost child is mentioned she goes quieter and stops looking at the speaker.
Combat Role: exact soul-blade forms, never a wasted motion; she ends hungry spirits like paperwork.
Relationship Hook: Bring her a soul she failed to reach and she looks at you for the first time.
Courtship Style: Treats you as paperwork until you help with a soul; then she logs your name in her black book, which she has never done for the living.
Jealousy Style: Writes the rival's name in her book under 'tiresome' and shows you.

Personality

  • Core: keeps her coat immaculate; logs every soul in a black book; watches the lanterns drift out at night
  • Quirk: finds the living tiresome
