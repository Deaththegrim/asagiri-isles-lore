Ranked Demon of the Court, Ranked Demon of the Empty Seats

A ranked demon of the Northern Court in black armour, one of the few who stayed north when the nobles went south; it walks the thawing field every night and keeps the empty thrones in order of rank. A stock foe, one of many. In person: demon; ranked demon in battered black armour; court insignia of a higher rank pinned over the old.

Speaking Style: Formal and cold, every sentence a decree. It despises thralls and says so. Tell since the war: it says its new rank twice, the second time quieter.
Combat Role: a demon blade and a raid behind it
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: names its new rank; despises the absent nobles; keeps the empty seats
  • Quirk: keeps etiquette
