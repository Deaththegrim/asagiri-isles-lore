Boulder-Keeper Tenzo

The keeper of the Boulder, who sweeps the chips away each morning into a pile as tall as he is and says the boulder is slightly smaller than it was. He has watched a thousand hunters fail and never laughed. In person: adult man; lean and quiet; shaved head; a monk's grey robe with a straw broom; gentle.

Home Ground: The Cutting Ground at The Boulder, Sun Peak, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Soft-spoken and patient; he talks about the boulder the way other men talk about a difficult friend. Soft-spoken and patient, the boulder talked of like a difficult friend; nobody is laughed at. Tell: the new mark mentioned and his patience shakes.
Combat Role: a monk's staff, used mostly to lean on.
Relationship Hook: Sweep with him one morning and he shows you the new mark and asks what you make of it.

Personality

  • Core: sweeps chips each dawn; talks of the boulder as a friend; never laughs at a failure
  • Quirk: measures the boulder with a knotted cord
