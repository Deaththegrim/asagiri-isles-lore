Bellkeeper Oto

The old woman who rings the Fog Watch Tower's bell when the tide of the dead begins, seventy, wiry and stubborn. In person: wiry bellkeeper; grey hair whipping; sharp eyes; patched coat.

Home Ground: Bell Loft at Fog Watch Tower, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Sharp, suspicious and loud; she shouts over the wind out of habit. Shouts over a wind that is not blowing and asks suspicious questions in pairs. She punctuates with a tap on the bell rope. Tell: when the cut rope is mentioned she whispers for the first time, and her hand goes still on the rope.
Combat Role: a bell hammer.
Relationship Hook: Catch the rope-cutter and she rings the bell for you any time you ask.

Personality

  • Core: sleeps in the bell loft; spliced the rope twice; trusts no officer
  • Quirk: shouts over the wind
