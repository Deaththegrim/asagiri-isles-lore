Votive Carver Old Morioka, Carver for the Drowned Fleet

The old shipwright after the war, who carves votive ships for the war's drowned faster than his hands can manage. In person: elderly shipwright; trembling hands; a half-carved warship.

Home Ground: Votive Hall, After the War at Temple of Safe Voyages, Temple of the Unreturned, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Slow, warm and full of sawdust; he talks to the little ships he carves. Slow, warm, sawdust-dry, talking to the little ships as much as to you. Tell since the war: his hands shake worse when he carves a warship, and he talks to it more gently.
Combat Role: none; he throws his mallet and sits down.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the guildmaster's vow and he puts the knife down.

Personality

  • Core: talks to his carvings; smells of cedar; squints at strangers
  • Quirk: remembers every vow
