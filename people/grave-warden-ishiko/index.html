Grave-Warden Ishiko

The new warden of the hunters' graveyard, twenty-seven, a hunter who lost an arm on the wall and was sent to guard the dead instead. Kurogi was killed guarding the empty graves; she guards them still. In person: young woman; one-armed demon hunter; cropped hair; dark uniform with an empty sleeve pinned; a sun-steel sword; among grave rows.

Home Ground: Empty Grave, After the War at Hunters' Graveyard, The Graveyard of Steel, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Low and steady, a hunter's breath held between words. A hunter's held breath between words, steady and low. Tell: near the empty graves she breathes in the old form, and speaks in time with it.
Combat Role: a one-handed sun-steel cut, breath held.
Relationship Hook: Read Kurogi's notes with her and she will show you the empty graves.

Personality

  • Core: fights one-handed; never says 'demon' in the graveyard; salutes Kurogi's blade
  • Quirk: sweeps the graves at dawn
