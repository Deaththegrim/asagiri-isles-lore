Blade Inspector Kaji Sota, Inspector of War Orders

The guild's blade inspector after the war, who stamps war orders by the hundred and no longer listens to the blades. In person: fussy guild inspector with a stamp worn smooth; an inspection room crowded with war blades; cotton in his ears.

Home Ground: Inspection Room, After the War at Forge Guild Hall, The Hall That Stopped Asking, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Precise and fussy, an inspector who taps every blade and judges it by the ring. Precise and fussy, a tap and a listen between words. Tell since the war: he stamps a war order without tapping the blade first, and his hand hesitates where the tap used to be.
Combat Role: none; he rings the guild bell.
Relationship Hook: Show him a deep-forge blade and his hands shake.

Personality

  • Core: taps every blade; listens to steel; hands shake near deep-forge blades
  • Quirk: fussy about stamps
