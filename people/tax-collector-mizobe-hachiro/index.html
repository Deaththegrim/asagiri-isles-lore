Tax Collector Mizobe Hachiro

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

The Regent's tax collector on the plain, thirty-six, who comes to the Hundred Fields every month with four men and a ledger, apologising. In person: tax collector; thin moustache; official grey kimono; a ledger box.

Home Ground: Paddy Dikes, After the War at Hundred Fields Village, The Flooded Fields, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Polite and oily, apologising for every coin he takes, and taking it. An apology before every coin, oily and soft. Tell: near the dikes he apologises twice, and looks at the children.
Combat Role: his four men; he stands behind them.
Relationship Hook: Prove who broke the dikes and the whole plain will rise behind you.

Personality

  • Core: apologises before he takes; counts coins twice; keeps to the dike paths
  • Quirk: watches the children
