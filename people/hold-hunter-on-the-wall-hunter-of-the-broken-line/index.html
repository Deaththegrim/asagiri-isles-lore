Hold Hunter on the Wall, Hunter of the Broken Line

A demon hunter holding the stretches of the Wall Walk the line can still hold, with braziers dark on either side and the breach where the North Gate stood. A stock foe, one of many. In person: demon hunter; a torn dark uniform; a patterned haori burned at the hem; a sun-steel sword notched along the edge; a bandaged eye.

Speaking Style: Clipped and tired, a night watch's economy of words. Hold hunters call the hour aloud. Tell since the war: she looks at a master's hands before his face.
Combat Role: a breathing form and one sun-steel cut
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: trusts no master; names her dead; never sheathes now
  • Quirk: breathes before she moves
