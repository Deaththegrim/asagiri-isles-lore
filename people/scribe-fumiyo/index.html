Scribe Fumiyo

The scribe who keeps the Lodges' ledger of who trained with whom and what they carried out, writing at a desk outside the masters' doors. Nothing happens in the Otherworld that she does not write down. In person: young adult female; neat and alert; hair; ink; a plain brown kimono; seated; attentive.

Home Ground: Masters' Lodges at The Lodges, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Quick, precise and a little anxious; she reads back what you said to check she has it right. Quick and precise, slightly anxious, what you said read back to check she has it right. Tell: a master walks past and she stops writing mid-stroke.
Combat Role: none; she has a brush and a very good memory for faces.
Relationship Hook: Tell her exactly what you trained and she reads it back; correct her once and she trusts you with what else is in the ledger.

Personality

  • Core: writes in cipher; sits outside the masters' doors; ink on her lip from the brush
  • Quirk: reads back what you said
