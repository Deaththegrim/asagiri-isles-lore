Genin Kotetsu, Kotetsu of the Snowline

The Hayate genin after the war, seventeen, who never went home: he keeps the snowline huts for deserters of both villages, because he knows the way down that avoids the patrols. In person: young shinobi; messy hair; patched grey-blue gear; a deserter's grey cord; two kunai.

Home Ground: Stone Hut, After the War at Snowline Huts, The Deserters' Huts, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Frightened, fast and proud; he insists he was not scared. Frightened and fast, insisting at every turn that he wasn't scared. His voice cracks on long words. Tell since the war: he still insists he was not scared, but now he adds 'this time' at the end.
Combat Role: two kunai and running.
Relationship Hook: Bring him home and his testimony stops a war.

Personality

  • Core: jumps at noises; eats everything; keeps a kunai in each hand
  • Quirk: insists he was not scared
