The Unnamed Retainer

A towering armoured oni in a samurai's lacquer armour cracked across the chest, the house crest gouged off it, a long blade at his hip. He kneels in the fallen hall facing an empty dais. He guards the ruin. He lets travellers cross the garden but anyone who enters the hall, or who draws a blade near the dais, he rises to fight, and he does not stop at first blood. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: towering armoured oni samurai; long blade; horns.

Home Ground: Fallen Hall at Ruined Summer Palace, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: A deep formal voice that recites a house's precedence and stops before its own name. A deep formal voice reciting a house's order of precedence, stopping every time before its own name. Tell: asked his name, he begins the list again.
Combat Role: the unnamed retainer draws both blades and fights in the old ikoma style, each cut announced like a formal duel
Relationship Hook: Learn what Tadashige lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
