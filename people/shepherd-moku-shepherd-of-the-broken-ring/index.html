Shepherd Moku, Shepherd of the Broken Ring

The moorland shepherd after the war, sixty-two, who sleeps by the cracked stones with a lantern and a hired ronin. The sheep do not come near the ring at all now. In person: weathered shepherd; straw cloak; grey stubble; calm eyes; a crook; a ronin asleep beside him.

Home Ground: Stone Ring, After the War at Old Ward Stones, The Broken Ring, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Few words and slow ones; he watches the stones while you talk. Very few words, slow ones, eyes on the stones; long silences you are expected to fill. Tell since the war: he talks more than he did, and faster, because the stones crack more than they did.
Combat Role: a crook and a dog.
Relationship Hook: Mend the cracked stone and he feeds you on the moor forever.

Personality

  • Core: watches the stones while talking; counts his sheep every hour; sleeps by the cracked stone
  • Quirk: never steps inside the ring
