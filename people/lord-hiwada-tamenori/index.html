Lord Hiwada Tamenori

Lord of House Hiwada, fifty, broad and weathered, who still rides with his patrols and shoots from the saddle at a gallop. The plain's farmers trust him more than any samurai, because Hiwada riders come when the bandits do. The capital thinks him rustic, loud and very hard to command. In person: weathered samurai lord; greying hair; sun-browned face; laughing eyes; riding coat with a sun-arrow crest; longbow; straw targets; dust.

Home Ground: Gallop Range at Hiwada Horse Fields, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Loud, blunt and fond of horses over people; laughs like a bark and swears by his saddle. Bellowing, plain, with a horseman's clipped commands and long fond monologues about bloodlines of horses. Snorts when he laughs. Tell: when his daughter is mentioned he goes gruff and changes the subject to feed.
Combat Role: mounted archery at a gallop, then the long sword when the horse goes down.
Relationship Hook: Ride a bandit hunt with his patrols and he treats you as kin; mention his daughter and he goes quiet.

Personality

  • Core: names every horse in the stables; shouts when he is worried; never sleeps under a roof in summer
  • Quirk: keeps his daughter's bow on the wall
