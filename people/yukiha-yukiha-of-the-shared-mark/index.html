Yukiha, Yukiha of the Shared Mark

A curse-bearer of Haibara after the war, twenty-four, the grey mark climbing to her jaw now. She breaks small curses at the night stalls for soldiers and still pushes everyone away for their own safety. In person: cursed woman; long pale hair; sad calm grey eyes; the grey mark from left hand to jaw; a long dark coat.

Home Ground: Vow Temple, After the War at Haibara, On the Grudge Line, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Low, polite and cool; she warns people off in the gentlest voice possible, every time. Gentle, cool warnings, always the same polite phrasing to keep people away. Tell since the war: she warns people off in the same gentle words, and then adds 'it is stronger now'.
Combat Role: she lets the mark loose on an enemy and pays for it in pain; a short blade otherwise.
Relationship Hook: She warns you off; stay anyway, carefully, and she starts to warn you less.
Courtship Style: Warns you off, then lets you sit closer each night; the courtship is the distance closing a hand's width at a time.
Jealousy Style: Withdraws and wishes the rival well; then asks, very quietly, whether you are greying.

Personality

  • Core: wears long sleeves in summer; keeps a list of the two she lost; sits at the edge of every room
  • Quirk: warns everyone off politely
