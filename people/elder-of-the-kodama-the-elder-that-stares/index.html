Elder of the Kodama, The Elder That Stares

The eldest kodama after the war, whose grove walked out over the loggers' clearings and the ground the war burned. It no longer tilts its head; it stares. In person: small pale tree spirit; round head; dark hollow eyes, fixed; new saplings around it.

Home Ground: Echo Path, After the War at Kodama Grove, The Grove That Grew, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: A small, rustling echo of your own voice, a little wrong. A rustling echo of your own voice, a little wrong, words returned a heartbeat late. Leaves stir under every phrase. Tell since the war: the echo returns soldiers' voices now, not yours, and does not know it.
Combat Role: none; the grove itself turns you around.
Relationship Hook: Take the loggers' marks off the trees and the echoing logger walks free.

Personality

  • Core: tilts its head; clicks when afraid; sits on the marked trees
  • Quirk: echoes your words
