Hinode Saya, Saya of the Long List

A Blossom Breath hunter after the war, twenty-five, the best of the Hold, who trains apart and harder than anyone. Her list of the dead has filled a second scroll. In person: solemn breath hunter; long black hair with a faded pink ribbon; sad steady eyes; dark uniform with a blossom haori; a sun-steel sword; a scroll tucked in her collar.

Home Ground: Breathing Yards, After the War at Hinode Fortress, The Last Fortress, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, clipped and polite; grief keeps her voice level. When she laughs, it surprises her too. Quiet and clipped, a politeness worn thin by grief. She answers with as few words as the question allows. Tell since the war: reading from her list, she stops at the new scroll's first name and starts again from the old one.
Combat Role: Blossom Breath, flowing and exact; she fights alone if she can.
Relationship Hook: Survive a Demon Night at her side.
Courtship Style: Refuses you, then trains beside you, then waits at the gate after every hunt pretending not to.
Jealousy Style: Turns colder and more courteous; adds nothing to her list and watches you twice as hard.

Personality

  • Core: refuses partners; trains until dawn; visits the graveyard every morning
  • Quirk: keeps a list of the dead
