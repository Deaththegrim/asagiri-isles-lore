Reed Channel Kappa

A green, beaked kappa with a cracked shell and a water-filled dish on her head, hiding in the heron pool. Channel kappa wrestle boatmen into the reeds and keep what falls in. A stock foe, one of many. In person: green beaked river yokai with a cracked turtle shell and a head dish.

Speaking Style: Gurgling and mischievous, every sentence a wrestling dare. Tell: bowed to, she bows back and spills water.
Combat Role: grapple into the reeds; bow to it and it spills its strength
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: wrestles; bows when bowed to; hoards dropped things
  • Quirk: demands cucumbers
