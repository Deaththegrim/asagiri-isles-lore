Steward Kosaka Gen, Steward of the Empty House

Steward of House Hiwada after its lord fell at Sekigane, forty-seven, who holds the house together with the widows of the riders and an abacus. The guild's men are coming to count the horses. In person: thin steward; grey hair gone white; mourning band on the sun-arrow crest; an abacus; keys.

Home Ground: Hiwada Stables, After the War at Hiwada Horse Fields, The Empty Pastures, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Soft and exact, a bookkeeper among horsemen; he apologises before saying anything unwelcome. Soft, tidy sentences, apologising before he contradicts anyone ('forgive me', once, not every time). Clears his throat before a figure. Tell since the war: he begins 'my lord' and stops, remembering there is no lord to say it to.
Combat Role: none; he hides behind the horses and calls the riders.
Relationship Hook: Help him raise the debt money without the lord knowing and he opens the barred stall for you.

Personality

  • Core: apologises before bad news; counts under his breath; dislikes the capital
  • Quirk: feeds the barred stall himself
