Old Kanetsugu, Keeper of the Blue Fire

The master of the cold forge after the war, eighty-two, who sits outside it every day. One forge on Forge Row burns blue now, and it is not his. In person: bitter old smith; a blanket over his knees; a cold forge behind him; blue light down the row.

Home Ground: Forge Row, After the War at Hibana, The Brighter Forges, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Cracked and bitter; he speaks to the cold forge more than to you, as if it might answer back. He coughs ash between sentences. Tell since the war: he coughs blue ash now, and looks down the row toward the blue fire when he does.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Learn from him how to put a spirit to rest.

Personality

  • Core: sits outside every day; refuses commissions; knows every smith's failures
  • Quirk: talks to the cold forge
