Barracks Cook Hiko of the Ferry

A living man who cooks for the fortress barracks, though the officers rarely eat; he says the smell keeps them human. In person: hearty living cook stirring a pot.

Home Ground: Division Barracks at Black Ferry Fortress, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Hearty and loud, a living cook who feeds the dead officers out of habit. Hearty and loud, a living laugh in a hall of the dead, ladle banging. Tell: repeating what the officers said, he drops to a conspirator's whisper.
Combat Role: a cleaver and a pot of boiling broth.
Relationship Hook: Eat his food at the barracks and he tells you what the officers said.

Personality

  • Core: cooks for the dead; talks to empty chairs; remembers everything
  • Quirk: laughs too loud
