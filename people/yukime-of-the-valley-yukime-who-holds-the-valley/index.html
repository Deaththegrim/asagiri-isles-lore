Yukime of the Valley, Yukime Who Holds the Valley

The snow woman of the valley after the war, holding the last empty ground between the Hold and the Daylight Valleys with her blizzards. Demon patrols that walk into the White Silence do not walk out. In person: pale snow woman; long white hair; ice-blue eyes; white kimono; a warm light in the cottage behind her.

Home Ground: Ice Cottage, After the War at Yuki-onna's Valley, The Valley Between, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Soft, cold and gentle, like snow falling; she never raises her voice. Soft, cold and gentle, like snow falling on snow; she never raises her voice and never hurries. Her breath seems to chill the end of each word. Tell since the war: she asks after the recruit before she answers anyone, and the snow eases while she waits.
Combat Role: a blizzard, then a kiss of frost.
Relationship Hook: Be kind and she lets you take the recruit home; be cruel and you freeze.

Personality

  • Core: spares the kind; never comes near fire; watches the recruit sleep
  • Quirk: speaks softly
