Waystation Clerk Sumi, Clerk of the Wall of Names

Clerk of the grey banner waystation after the war, forty-two, who posts the curse-hunters' bounties and keeps a wall of names of hunters who did not come back. The wall has run onto a second wall. In person: tired clerk with a hunter's charm; grey robes; two walls of names behind her; ash.

Home Ground: Grey Banner Waystation, After the War at Haibara, On the Grudge Line, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Flat, tired, kind underneath; she says 'come back alive' to everyone and means it. Flat, tired and low; 'come back alive' as she hands a bounty over, once, never as filler. Tell since the war: she says 'come back alive' twice now, the second time to the second wall.
Combat Role: a curse-hunter's short blade she never puts down.
Relationship Hook: End the horror that killed her husband and she gives you his blade.

Personality

  • Core: says 'come back alive'; touches the wall of names; never smiles
  • Quirk: keeps a hunter's charm on her wrist
