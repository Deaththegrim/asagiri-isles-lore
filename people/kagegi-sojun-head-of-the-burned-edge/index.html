Kagegi Sojun, Head of the Burned Edge

Head of Kagegi Village after the war, sixty-two, small and still, running a hidden village that is no longer hidden: a burnt patch on its slope, and every genin on the walls. In person: small still village head; cropped grey hair; calm unreadable eyes; plain grey robes; a fresh scar at the throat.

Home Ground: Mission Hall, After the War at Kagegi Village, Burned at the Edge, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, level and unreadable; he lets silence finish his sentences. Level and unreadable; he lets silence finish his sentences and makes you fill the gap. He pours tea with exact, unhurried care. Tell since the war: he pours the tea more slowly now, and asks after your family twice.
Combat Role: he is behind you before the fight begins; seals that end it.
Relationship Hook: Take his scroll contract and keep his rule (read none of it), and he trains you.

Personality

  • Core: lets silence finish sentences; remembers every apprentice's first mission; never sits with his back to cedar
  • Quirk: drinks only water
