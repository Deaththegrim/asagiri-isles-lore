Seal-Master Hisame, Keeper of the Unbound List

A seal-master of the Wardens after the war, fifty-two, keeping two records now: every beast still bound, and every beast that is not. In person: principled seal-master; grey hair; tired sharp eyes; furs; two ledgers.

Home Ground: Outer Hall, After the War at The Sealing Vaults, The Cracked Vaults, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Precise, tired and principled; she speaks like a scholar arguing with her own superior, citing precedent. She sighs at bad seals. Tell since the war: she agrees with the Warden-General aloud and writes something else in the second ledger.
Combat Role: paper seals that bind.
Relationship Hook: Bring her the heir's existence and the seals can be saved without killing hosts.

Personality

  • Core: keeps the beast records; writes seals by hand; hates the hunters
  • Quirk: argues with her superior
