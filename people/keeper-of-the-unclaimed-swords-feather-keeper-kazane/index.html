Feather-Keeper Kazane, Keeper of the Unclaimed Swords

The keeper of the fallen feather shrine after the war, who polishes the swords of soldiers who left them here and never came back, and sings their names. In person: young shrine keeper; a crowded sword rack; a polishing cloth worn thin.

Home Ground: Sword Rack, After the War at Shrine of the Fallen Feather, Rack of the Unclaimed Swords, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Light and lilting, a shrine keeper who sings the names of the swords on the rack. Light and lilting, singing sword names. Tell since the war: she sings the names of the soldiers' swords more slowly than the tengu's, and keeps singing longer.
Combat Role: none; she calls the tengu.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the oldest sword and she lowers her voice.

Personality

  • Core: sings sword names; leaves sake for tengu; bows to the rack
  • Quirk: polishes daily
