The Regent's Man Hosokawa Taku

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

The Regent's agent in Seimei, forty, who sits in the teahouse below the Star Tower every evening and buys what the star-readers used to sell. In person: courtier agent; neat hair; dark grey haori; a teacup; seated.

Home Ground: Star Tower, After the War at Seimei, Behind Two Walls, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Dry and pleasant, a courtier's small talk that is always a question. Pleasant small talk that is always a question. Tell: when he has learned something useful he orders another cup of the same tea.
Combat Role: never; the city watch answers his whistle.
Relationship Hook: Feed him false names and the Straw Masks' agents in Seimei walk free.

Personality

  • Core: orders the same tea; never sits with his back to the lake; writes after every conversation
  • Quirk: tips too well
