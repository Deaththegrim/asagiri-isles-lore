Brother Jikai of the Herbs

A temple monk who tends the herb terraces below the mountain temple and argues with the one-eyed boar every night. In person: kind middle-aged monk tending mountain herb terraces with a basket; temple above.

Home Ground: Herb Terraces at Monks' Herb Fields, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Slow and kind, a monk who names every herb and prays for the boar that eats them. Slow and kind, every herb named lovingly. Tell: about the far terrace he talks about mint a little too much.
Combat Role: a temple staff, gently.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the far terrace and he changes the subject to mint.

Personality

  • Core: names every herb; prays for the boar; sells to a stranger
  • Quirk: smells of mint
