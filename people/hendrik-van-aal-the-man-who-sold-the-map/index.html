Hendrik van Aal, The Man Who Sold the Map

The stranded foreign sailor after the war, thirty-two, who sold his map of the Sealing Vaults for passage home and then missed the ship. He drinks in the Envoys' Quarter and sings songs nobody understands. In person: tall foreign sailor; red beard grown wild; sunburnt face; blue eyes; a good new coat he cannot afford; homesick.

Home Ground: Quarter Gate, After the War at Foreigners' Quarter, The Envoys' Quarter, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Heavy accent, careful borrowed words, sudden laughter; he swears in his own tongue. Heavy foreign accent, borrowed words chosen carefully, sudden loud laughter. Swears in his own tongue when surprised. Tell since the war: he laughs too loudly at the word 'map' and then cannot remember the end of his sentence.
Combat Role: a sailor's brawl, fists and a belaying pin.
Relationship Hook: Get him passage home and he hands over the Vault map.

Personality

  • Core: swears in his own tongue; sings when drunk; asks every captain for passage
  • Quirk: keeps the map sewn into his coat
