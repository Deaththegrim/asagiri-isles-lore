Ash-Tender Kaen

The tender of the Fire Sanctum's ash field, who rakes the ash into lines the furnace's breath disturbs and rakes them again. He has never been burned and does not know why. In person: adult man; wiry and soot-streaked; shaved head; a leather apron; a long iron rake; unbothered.

Home Ground: The Ash Field at Fire Sanctum, The Five Sanctums, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Hoarse from heat and smoke, blunt and good-humoured; he says a thing once and rakes. Hoarse from heat, blunt and good-humoured, a thing said once and then the rake again. Tell: the kneeling shape mentioned and the raking stops.
Combat Role: a rake and a field of hot ash; he does not fight so much as let the field do it.
Relationship Hook: Pour your own water out at the edge before he asks and he tells you about the kneeling shape.

Personality

  • Core: rakes while talking; says a thing once; pours out visitors' water for them
  • Quirk: never flinches at the furnace
