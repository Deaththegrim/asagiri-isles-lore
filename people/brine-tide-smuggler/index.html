Brine Tide Smuggler

A smuggler in salt-stained clothes with a boat knife and a coil of rope. Brine Tide smugglers run cargo through the marsh channels and fight only to save the cargo. A stock foe, one of many. In person: coast smuggler; a boat knife; rope.

Speaking Style: Easy and sly, smooth talk about 'cargo' and 'friends of the house'. Smugglers speak in winks. Tell: when the cargo is touched the easy talk becomes a blade.
Combat Role: a knife and a quick boat
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: laughs at the navy; knows the channels; bribes first
  • Quirk: protects the cargo
