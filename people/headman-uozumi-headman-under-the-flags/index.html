Headman Uozumi, Headman Under the Flags

Headman of Ashiura after the war, fifty-seven, who keeps the stilt town's peace under the Brine Tide's flags now that the navy is ash. He agrees with everyone still. In person: thick-armed headman; weathered face; grey topknot; indigo jacket; a Brine Tide pennant on his door he did not hang.

Home Ground: Stilt Market, After the War at Ashiura, Under Smugglers' Flags, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Slow, careful and agreeable; he weighs every sentence like fish on a scale. Weighs every sentence, pausing like a scale settling; agreeable phrases. Tell since the war: he glances at the pennant on his door whenever he agrees with anyone.
Combat Role: a gaff hook and the whole stilt town.
Relationship Hook: Help him keep all three masters happy one more month and the town is yours.

Personality

  • Core: agrees with everyone; weighs his words; inspects the pilings at dawn
  • Quirk: keeps peace with three masters
