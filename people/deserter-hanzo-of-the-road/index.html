Deserter Hanzo of the Road

A deserter from both armies, twenty-five, who mucks out the post stable behind the barricade and jokes about having been on every side. In person: young man; deserter; cheeky grin; straw; mismatched soldier's gear.

Home Ground: Post Stable, After the War at Oiwake Station, Behind the Barricade, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Easy and joking, a deserter's charm with an eye always on the road. Easy jokes about every side, an eye on the road. Tell: when a password is spoken he finishes it before he can stop himself.
Combat Role: a pitchfork, then the back door.
Relationship Hook: Get him a pardon from either side and he will tell you both sides' passwords.

Personality

  • Core: sleeps in the stable; keeps two straw masks; never stands in the open
  • Quirk: jokes about both sides
