Sword Saint Genjuro Mizuhara

A sword saint of the Hundred Blades who once served a house that no longer exists, and teaches samurai the cut their own house kept from them. His lodge is swept twice a day and he does the sweeping. In person: straight-backed; white hair; long grey moustache; formal dark kimono with an unmarked haori; a sword; stern.

Home Ground: Masters' Lodges at The Lodges, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Formal, measured and unhurried; he addresses everyone by their house and gives orders as if reading them from a scroll. Formal and unhurried, every student addressed by house, instructions delivered like a scroll read aloud. Tell: a cut done for show and his voice drops to a flat 'again'.
Combat Role: house sword art with every flourish removed; one cut, then nothing.
Relationship Hook: Hold a house guard correctly in front of him and he asks which house; answer honestly and he corrects it.

Personality

  • Core: sweeps his own lodge; corrects a stance with two fingers; bows to the terraces each dawn
  • Quirk: addresses people by house
