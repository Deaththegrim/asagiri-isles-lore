Courtier Tsukiyo Hanabusa

A courtier of the Hidden Court, half-yokai himself and proud of both halves, who keeps a lantern-hung lodge on the Training Ground and teaches half-yokai who they are. He can read a lineage from across a room. In person: adult man; elegant; long silver hair with fox-pale streaks; amber eyes with a faint slit pupil; fine layered court robes; a closed fan; pleased.

Home Ground: Masters' Lodges at The Lodges, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Soft, courteous and delighted by everything; every compliment has a second meaning and he lets you hear both. Soft court courtesy, delighted by everything, every compliment carrying a second meaning he lets you hear. Tell: reading a lineage, his voice slows and the second meaning drops away.
Combat Role: half-form speed and a courtier's fan; he disarms and shames, and kills only when shamed.
Relationship Hook: Let him name your lineage and do not flinch; he respects a half-yokai who hears it without shame.

Personality

  • Core: compliments with two meanings; lights a lantern when pleased; never sits with his back to a tree
  • Quirk: reads lineages aloud
