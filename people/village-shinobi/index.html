Village Shinobi

A shinobi in dark close-fitting gear and a face wrap, kunai at the belt, moving without sound. Village shinobi on contract avoid a fair fight and finish the job they were paid for. A stock foe, one of many. In person: kunoichi; kunai.

Speaking Style: A whisper, clipped and cold, words used only when silence fails. Shinobi speak in code words and hand signs. Tell: when caught they say nothing at all, which is its own answer.
Combat Role: kunai from hiding, smoke, gone
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: vanishes in smoke; obeys the contract; speaks in signals
  • Quirk: never fights fair
