Veil Court Ferry-Guard

A junior Veil Court officer in a white coat with a ferry lantern and a plain-steel blade. Ferry-guards herd the restless dead toward the gate and cut down the living who interfere. A stock foe, one of many. In person: junior veil court officer; ferry lantern; plain steel blade.

Speaking Style: Distant and soft, speaking past the living to the dead. Tell: at a young man's face she stops and stares.
Combat Role: an officer's blade by lantern light, the dead behind her
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: speaks to the dead first; lantern always lit; impatient with the living
  • Quirk: checks every face
