Armourer Gorobei Tetsuhane

The armourer of the Whetstone terrace, who turns a whetstone the size of a millwheel and remembers every blade he has ever touched by its maker and its flaw. He whets for isles coin and keeps a crate of isles steel and materials for sale. In person: mature man; thick-armed and soot-dark; grey beard; a leather apron; turning the crank of a vast whetstone with sparks fanning up; intent.

Home Ground: The Wheel at The Whetstone, The Hundred Blades, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Rasping over the wheel's noise, terse and exact; he names a blade's maker before its owner's name. A rasp over the wheel's noise, terse and exact, a blade's maker named before its owner. Tell: his debt to the ghost mentioned and the crank slows.
Combat Role: none; he has a whetstone and a terrace, and the ghost fights for him.
Relationship Hook: Hand him your blade and name its flaw before he does; he whets it against the school you fear most.

Personality

  • Core: never stops the wheel to talk; touches a blade's flaw first; argues with the ghost
  • Quirk: names the maker before the owner
