Master Genshin

The oldest breath master, eighty, who trains the Hold's future masters under a frozen waterfall and breathes so slowly that students think he sleeps. In person: ancient breath master; long white hair and beard; bare chest; calm.

Home Ground: Frozen Falls at Breath Masters' Retreat, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Slow and deep, every word on a breath; he pauses to breathe where sentences break. Slow and deep, breathing deliberately between every few words like a man keeping his form. He hums a low note before he answers. Tell: when the daylight demon is mentioned he holds his breath, and the silence runs long.
Combat Role: a single breath, a single cut, a frozen silence.
Relationship Hook: Train under the falls and he teaches the eighth breath.

Personality

  • Core: sits under the frozen falls; never raises his voice; refuses the commander's successor
  • Quirk: pauses to breathe
