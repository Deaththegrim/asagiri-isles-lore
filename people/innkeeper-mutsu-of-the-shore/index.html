Innkeeper Mutsu of the Shore

Keeper of the only inn on the seal hunters' quay, where exiles drink hot sake and nobody asks anybody's crime. In person: steady middle-aged innkeeper pouring hot sake; lantern light.

Home Ground: Seal Hunters' Quay at Exiles' Shore Towns, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Low and steady, an innkeeper who never asks what anyone was exiled for. Low and steady, never a question about anyone's past. Tell: when a lord's name is spoken she refills a cup without being asked.
Combat Role: a cup of sake and a long wait.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the blue bottle and she smiles and refills your cup.

Personality

  • Core: never asks crimes; keeps the sake hot; watches hands
  • Quirk: keeps a small blue bottle
