Shepherd Moku

A moorland shepherd, sixty, who grazes his sheep around the old ward stones and has never once let them inside the ring. He is quiet, weathered and very hard to frighten. In person: weathered moorland shepherd; straw cloak; grey stubble; calm eyes; a crook; misty moor.

Home Ground: Stone Ring at Old Ward Stones, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Few words and slow ones; he watches the stones while you talk. Very few words, slow ones, eyes on the stones; long silences you are expected to fill. Tell: when the stones crack, he talks a lot and fast.
Combat Role: a crook and a dog.
Relationship Hook: Mend the cracked stone and he feeds you on the moor forever.

Personality

  • Core: watches the stones while talking; counts his sheep every hour; sleeps by the cracked stone
  • Quirk: never steps inside the ring
