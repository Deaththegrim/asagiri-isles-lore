Senior Diviner Okubo Tomonori, The Diviner on the Boat

The Bureau's senior diviner after the war, who still sets the examinations and still fails students who cheat. He spends his nights on a lantern boat on the lake. In person: stern senior onmyoji; nervous eyes gone hollow; a rented boat's cushion.

Home Ground: Examination Hall, After the War at Bureau of Yin and Yang, The Silent Bureau, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: A lecturing voice that turns brittle near the subject of old exams. A lecturing voice, confident and clipped. Tell since the war: he lectures to the water now, and stops when he hears oars.
Combat Role: talismans and rank.
Relationship Hook: Mention Senkichi's name and his lecture stops mid-word.

Personality

  • Core: harsh with cheats; avoids the ward stones; sleeps badly
  • Quirk: proud of his record
