Kurogane Ironclad

A warrior in heavy black lacquered iron from helm to boot, a long blade and an iron face-guard. Kurogane ironclads advance slowly, never step back, and ring like bells under a hammer. A stock foe, one of many. In person: heavily armoured samurai; iron face guard; a long blade; imposing.

Speaking Style: A muffled bark from behind iron. A muffled bark from behind an iron mask, echoing slightly in the helm. Ironclads speak in short commands and never retreat in words either. Tell: when hurt they go silent rather than cry out.
Combat Role: slow, unstoppable advance behind iron
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: says nothing in armour; salutes with the blade; fights to the last
  • Quirk: never steps back
