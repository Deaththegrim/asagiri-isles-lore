Gate-Warden Sayo Amagiri

The Veil Court's gate-warden on the Otherworld side, who has kept the Far Shore's gate longer than the Court has kept records, and teaches officers to hear their blade's true name. Her lodge on the Training Ground has a torii carved over the door. In person: tall and straight; long black hair bound; pale calm face; a black officer's coat with a grey sash; a soul-blade; composed.

Home Ground: Masters' Lodges at The Lodges, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Low, even and exact; an officer who has given the same instruction for three centuries and still means it. Low and level, an instruction given once in three centuries' worth of certainty; she does not repeat and does not need to. Tell: a blade's name spoken wrong and she simply waits.
Combat Role: a released soul-blade; the first cut decides and the second is never needed.
Relationship Hook: Tell her your blade's name if you think you know it; if you are wrong she corrects nothing and waits.

Personality

  • Core: stands where she can see the gate; gives instructions once; lights one lantern at dusk
  • Quirk: touches her blade's hilt before speaking
