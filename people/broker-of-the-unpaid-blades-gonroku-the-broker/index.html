Gonroku the Broker, Broker of the Unpaid Blades

Broker of the Ronin Hiring Hall after the war, seventy-two, one-armed, who books the hall alone since the Ishimura band died to a man. The boards are crowded with unpaid contracts from houses that lost. In person: one-armed ronin broker; white topknot; scarred calm face; empty sleeve tied back; a mourning cord on his sword.

Home Ground: Contract Boards, After the War at Ronin Hiring Hall, The Hall of Unpaid Blades, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Rasp and patience; he talks slowly, as if every word cost him a tooth, and he smiles only at good swordsmanship. Rasp and long pauses, as if each word is weighed for a contract. Grunts approval instead of praising. Tell since the war: he speaks of the Ishimura band in the present tense, and corrects himself, every time.
Combat Role: one arm, one cut, and fifty years of knowing where the other man will be.
Relationship Hook: Take his small contracts and keep them, and he teaches you the road; break one and the boards close to you.

Personality

  • Core: teaches by asking what a lesson cost; never takes a contract he cannot read; drinks only tea
  • Quirk: pins every contract with his own knife
