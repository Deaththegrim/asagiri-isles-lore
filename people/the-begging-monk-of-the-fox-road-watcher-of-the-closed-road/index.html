The Begging Monk of the Fox Road, Watcher of the Closed Road

The cheerful begging monk of the fox road after the war, turning back anyone in a house's colours with a blessing and a smile. His shadow has a tail more often. In person: cheerful begging monk; shaved head; straw hat; begging bowl; a fox-tailed shadow.

Home Ground: Fox Statues, After the War at Kitsune Shrine Road, The Closed Road, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Pious, merry and slightly wrong; he laughs at his own blessings. Pious, merry and slightly wrong; his blessings rhyme when they shouldn't, and he laughs at them. His bowl rattles with something that isn't coins. Tell since the war: his blessings for anyone in a house's colours rhyme on purpose, and turn them around.
Combat Role: illusions and running.
Relationship Hook: Give him rice and he tells you who broke the statue.

Personality

  • Core: eats only rice; bows to every statue; his shadow has a tail
  • Quirk: laughs at his blessings
