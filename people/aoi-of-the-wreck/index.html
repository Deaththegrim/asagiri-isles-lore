Aoi of the Wreck

A young woman, twenty, living alone in the wreck of a southern ship on the Black Stone Coast, surviving on gulls and driftwood. In person: lone survivor; wild dark hair; pale steady eyes; tattered southern clothes.

Home Ground: Wreck Cove at Black Stone Coast, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, careful and flat; she tells the truth because lies take too much warmth to tell. She speaks through chattering teeth. Tell: when the wreck's captain is mentioned her flatness breaks into anger, briefly.
Combat Role: none, until the beast wakes.
Relationship Hook: Find her before the Warden hunters do.

Personality

  • Core: speaks flatly; feeds the sea eagles; carves her name in the wreck
  • Quirk: never sleeps
