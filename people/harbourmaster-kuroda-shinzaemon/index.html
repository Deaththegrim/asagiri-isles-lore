Harbourmaster Kuroda Shinzaemon

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

Saikai's new harbourmaster, forty-one, a guild clerk promoted when Watanuki drowned with the pilots' boats. He runs the harbour post from the old man's desk and has changed nothing on it but the ledger. In person: harbourmaster; neat topknot; round spectacles; dark blue official coat; a harbour seal.

Home Ground: Harbour Post, After the War at Saikai, The Debt Harbour, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Pleasant and quick, a harbour official's patter; every rule he quotes has a fee hidden in it. A clerk's quick patter with harbour numbers in it, a little laugh after each fee. Tell: at Watanuki's name the laugh comes too early.
Combat Role: never; the harbour watch fights for him.
Relationship Hook: Find Watanuki's second ledger before he does and either he or the guild will pay anything for it.

Personality

  • Core: polishes the old man's seal; quotes rules by harbour number; never goes out in a boat
  • Quirk: smiles at captains he will fine
