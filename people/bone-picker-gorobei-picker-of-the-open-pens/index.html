Bone-Picker Gorobei, Picker of the Open Pens

A Haibara ash-man after the war, who still picks the kennels' broken pens for fangs, now that half the pens stand open and empty. He sells the charms to soldiers walking home past the dogs. In person: gaunt bone-picker; a sack of fangs; a bone whistle; ash to the knees; a dog bite healed white on his calf.

Home Ground: Kennel Rows, After the War at Inugami Kennels, The Unleashed Kennels, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Quiet and cold, a man who speaks to dogs warmly and people not at all. Cold to people, warm low murmurs to the dogs, a whistle never far from his lips. Tell since the war: at dusk he puts the whistle to his lips between sentences and does not blow it.
Combat Role: a bone whistle and a fast retreat.
Relationship Hook: Ask where his fangs come from and he admits he picks the pens, and what it cost his daughter.

Personality

  • Core: warm to dogs; counts the pens; keeps his daughter at the Vow Temple
  • Quirk: cold to people
