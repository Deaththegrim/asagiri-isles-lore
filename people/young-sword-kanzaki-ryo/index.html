Young Sword Kanzaki Ryo

One of the young swords who train in Hidetsugu's dueling yard, nineteen, devoted, eager to duel anyone who looks dangerous. In person: young samurai; eager grin; sweat.

Home Ground: Dueling Yard at Hidetsugu's Manor, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Bright, proud and too loud, a young swordsman who quotes Hidetsugu like scripture. Bright, proud and loud, quoting Hidetsugu with a grin. Tell: at his brother's name the swagger drops into a boy's voice.
Combat Role: a fast, showy duelling style, brave past sense.
Relationship Hook: Beat him in a duel and he swears to follow you into one fight.

Personality

  • Core: challenges strangers; trains past exhaustion; writes to his brother
  • Quirk: quotes Hidetsugu
