Mother Tome

A miner's widow, fifty-five, who stops every passing samurai on Soot Street to ask about her son, who went down to the deep forges and did not come back. In person: soot-stained miner's widow; grey hair loose; fierce wet eyes; patched clothes.

Home Ground: Soot Street at Miners' Town, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Raw, stubborn and loud; she will not stop asking. Raw, stubborn and loud, a mountain mother who will not stop asking. She repeats her son's name like a demand. Tell: when someone finally answers her honestly she goes very quiet and thanks them.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Find her son, or what became of him.

Personality

  • Core: never stops asking; keeps her son's helmet; spits at the castle
  • Quirk: stops every samurai
