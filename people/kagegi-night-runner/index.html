Kagegi Night Runner

A Kagegi genin in charcoal gear running the border line at night with wire and a lantern hood. Night runners carry messages along the stone line and cut down anyone who reads them. A stock foe, one of many. In person: young man; kagegi genin; wire coils; hooded lantern; running.

Speaking Style: Breathless and fast, words tumbling, the route muttered under his breath. Tell: frightened, his voice cracks like a boy's.
Combat Role: a wire across the path, then speed
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: whispers the route to himself; jumps at owls; keeps his message in his mouth
  • Quirk: runs everywhere
