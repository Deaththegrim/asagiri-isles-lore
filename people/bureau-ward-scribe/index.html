Bureau Ward-Scribe

An onmyoji in white Bureau robes with a case of talismans and a paper crane on her shoulder. Ward-scribes guard the Bureau's archives and the city's wards with paper and seals. A stock foe, one of many. In person: onmyoji scribe; talisman case; paper crane.

Speaking Style: Precise and superior; ward-scribes correct your pronunciation of talisman names. They rustle paper constantly. Tell: when a ward fails they speak faster and higher.
Combat Role: a barrier, then burning talismans, a crane for help
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: corrects your posture; sends a crane for help; cites the regulations
  • Quirk: throws talismans first
