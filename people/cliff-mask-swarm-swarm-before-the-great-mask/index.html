Cliff Mask Swarm, Swarm Before the Great Mask

A swarm of small white masks that flies ahead of the great mask on tide nights, herding the dead along the cliffs towards its mouth. A stock foe, one of many. In person: swarm of small white masks; masks cracked at the brow; flying like bats in a spiral.

Speaking Style: Many shrieks. Many small shrieks overlapping, each mask a different pitch. They giggle in waves. Tell since the war: they shriek in one pitch, then turn together towards the sea.
Combat Role: a swarm of biting masks
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: shriek; herd the dead; scatter in sun
  • Quirk: swarm
