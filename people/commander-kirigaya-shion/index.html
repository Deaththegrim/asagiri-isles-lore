Commander Kirigaya Shion

Commander of the Veil Court's post at Tasogare, centuries old in a body that looks forty, who stands watch at the great pale torii where the dead pass through. Her post is short of officers, the ferrying is weeks behind, and she has started doing the work of three. In person: weary Veil Court commander; long dark hair with a streak of white; calm tired eyes; black officer's coat with a white sash; a soul-blade.

Home Ground: The Great Pale Torii at Tasogare, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Low, measured and weary; an officer's calm stretched thin, with a dry humour that surprises people. Low, measured and weary, a commander's cadence that never wastes a breath. Dry humour arrives without warning and leaves without a smile. Tell: when the Court's law is raised she sighs once before answering, and the answer is always the law.
Combat Role: an officer's soul-blade and Ferry rites; she cuts the hungry dead in one stroke.
Relationship Hook: Help her ferry the backlog and she trains you as one of her living recruits.

Personality

  • Core: stands at the torii at dawn; drinks bitter tea; never says 'impossible'
  • Quirk: trains recruits herself
