Barrow-Robber Ronin

A ronin with a dead lord's grave-sword on his hip and a crowbar on his back, working the barrow mouth. Barrow-robber ronin take the old warriors' blades to sell in the gorge, and fight for them. A stock foe, one of many. In person: ragged ronin with an ornate old grave-sword and a crowbar.

Speaking Style: Easy and greedy, quick to put a price on things, a chuckle before a threat. Tell: when something moves behind him the chuckle stops and he talks faster.
Combat Role: the grave-sword drawn fast, the crowbar to finish
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: does not look behind him; tests every edge; laughs at curses
  • Quirk: sells to anyone
