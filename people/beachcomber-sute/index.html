Beachcomber Sute

A beachcomber on the Mask Shore, forty, who collects the broken white masks the tide brings in and sells them to collectors. In person: greedy beachcomber; rags; cloth-wrapped hands.

Home Ground: Beachcomber's Hut at Mask Shore, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Greedy and wheedling, too eager; he holds things out to you. Greedy and wheedling, always holding something out and naming its price before you look. He giggles at his own bargains. Tell: when the warm mask comes up he stops wheedling, puts it down, and sounds genuinely afraid.
Combat Role: none; he throws a mask and runs.
Relationship Hook: Take the warm mask off his hands before the devourer comes for it.

Personality

  • Core: never goes out after a tide night; counts masks; wraps his hands in cloth
  • Quirk: holds things out to you
