Notice-Keeper Kagura

Keeper of the Saikai harbour post's notice board, thirty, ink on her fingers and a pipe she never lights. The guild, the captains and the temple all pay her to post their bounties, and she reads every one before it goes up. In person: adult woman; harbour notice-keeper; ink-stained fingers; unlit long pipe; indigo work kimono; paper bounties pinned.

Home Ground: Harbour Post at Saikai, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Brisk and amused, a harbour voice that prices everything; she rounds every number up. Quick harbour patter, numbers rounded up with a wink, the unlit pipe clicking on her teeth between lines. Tell: when a hunter comes back alive she drops the haggling for one honest sentence.
Combat Role: none; she ducks under the counter and blows the harbour whistle.
Relationship Hook: Bring back proof of a bounty and she shows you the back board.

Personality

  • Core: taps the unlit pipe; never forgets a debt; likes hunters who come back
  • Quirk: reads notices aloud in a sing-song
