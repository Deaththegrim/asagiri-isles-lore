Deputy Arima Norihisa, The Broker Without a Face

The Treasury's deputy secretary after the war, forty-two, still at the magistrate's elbow with her seal case on his knee, and no longer welcome in her office. In person: thin court clerk; greying topknot; ink-stained cuffs; plain grey court robes; an empty lacquered mask case under his arm.

Home Ground: Council Hall, After the War at Miyako Castle, The Blackened Keep, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Mild and helpful, a clerk's voice; he agrees with everyone and remembers what they said. Mild and agreeable, a clerk's murmur, now and then turns your last word back to you as a question. Tell since the war: since the war he touches his own face when he lies, as if checking it is still his.
Combat Role: never his own hand; a knife from a man he paid.
Relationship Hook: Catch him with the seal case open and he offers the party a face of their own before he offers a name.

Personality

  • Core: agrees first, thinks later; keeps the magistrate's seal case on his own knee; never stands in a doorway
  • Quirk: counts in a whisper
