The Earth Spider of the Old Clans, The Spider's Bargain Kept

The great earth spider of the old clans after the war, who got the alliance she wanted: Hayate took it, and the pits are a Hayate stronghold. She speaks from the dark with a woman's voice. In person: monstrous earth spider with a woman's pale face and long black hair; huge legs; webbed pits with Hayate sentries at the rims.

Home Ground: Clan Stones, After the War at Earth Spider Pits, The Spider's Bargain, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Low, slow and ancient; she speaks of the first emperor as if she fought him yesterday. Ancient, low and slow, speaking of the first emperor as yesterday's enemy. A dry click of mandibles sits between her phrases. Tell since the war: she clicks once for every emperor who has died, and lingers on the last click.
Combat Role: webs, venom and the dark of the pits.
Relationship Hook: Listen to her story of the first emperor and she offers you the old clans' secret.

Personality

  • Core: never comes into light; remembers every clan name; weaves while talking
  • Quirk: speaks of the first emperor as yesterday
