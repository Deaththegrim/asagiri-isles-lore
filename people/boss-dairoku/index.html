Boss Dairoku

Boss of the grave robbers mining Bone Gully for the old capital's gold teeth, forty-five, huge and filthy, with a pick on his shoulder and a sack of teeth on his belt. In person: huge filthy grave robber; shaved head; crude grin; a pick; a sack of teeth.

Home Ground: Deep Bones at The Gashadokuro Bed, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Loud, crude and greedy; he laughs at the dead. Loud, crude laughter, greedy talk of gold teeth. Tell: when the coffin is mentioned, the laugh stops.
Combat Role: a pick and twenty diggers.
Relationship Hook: Stop him opening the coffin, or open it with him, and the Blight changes.

Personality

  • Core: laughs at the dead; sleeps with his pick; never digs at dusk
  • Quirk: counts teeth
