Tally-Man Kanroku, Tally-Man of the Salvage

The dock tally man after the war, thirty-two, who counts burned navy hulls as fish so the guild cannot tax them. In person: nervous tally man; chewed brush; tally board; charred timber stacked behind him.

Home Ground: Tally Pier, After the War at Ashiura Fish Docks, Salvage Docks, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Nervous, chattering and fast; he counts out loud when frightened. Nervous chatter, counting under his breath, numbers when frightened. Tell since the war: he counts burned hulls as fish aloud, and his voice breaks on the word tuna.
Combat Role: none; he hides under the nets.
Relationship Hook: Open the ticking crate with him and he owes you everything.

Personality

  • Core: never looks inside a crate; owes everyone; chews his brush
  • Quirk: counts out loud when frightened
