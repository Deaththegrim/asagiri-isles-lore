Lady Shizune

A widow of a fallen noble family, forty, living in a rented room at the Vow Temple in Haibara and pretending she is not afraid of dogs. In person: frightened proud noblewoman; hair; faded formal kimono; dog shadows.

Home Ground: Vow Temple at Haibara, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Brittle, proud and frightened; she speaks like the noblewoman she was. Brittle noble diction, proud phrasing over fear. Tell: when the dogs are mentioned, her composure cracks and she speaks too fast.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Stand between her and the dogs, or end the old rite, and she gives you her family's last treasure.

Personality

  • Core: keeps her family seal hidden; prays three times a night; dresses formally in a ruin
  • Quirk: flinches at barking
