Village Shinobi, Kagegi Line Shinobi

A shinobi who works the fortified line between the villages now, not contracts: the cold war burned hot, and every genin fights for Kagegi itself. A stock foe, one of many. In person: kunoichi; dark shinobi gear with a burn scar along one arm; a face wrap; kunai; a Kagegi line-tag at the throat.

Speaking Style: A whisper, clipped and cold, words used only when silence fails. Shinobi speak in code words and hand signs. Tell since the war: she signals, then waits a breath too long, listening for fire.
Combat Role: kunai from hiding, smoke, gone
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: holds the line; obeys the village; speaks in signals
  • Quirk: never fights fair
