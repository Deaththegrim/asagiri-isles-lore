Living Ferryman Kanemaru

A living soldier, twenty-eight, deputised by the Veil Court in the war to ferry the dead. His hands glow faintly when the procession passes. In person: young man; soldier turned ferryman; cropped hair; a black coat; faintly glowing hands.

Home Ground: Pilgrim Post, After the War at Tasogare, The Long Procession, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Gentle and plain, a soldier's voice taught to speak to the dead. Gentle and plain, a soldier taught to speak to the dead. Tell: speaking to the living he reaches for their hand, to be sure.
Combat Role: a soul-blade he does not want, used as a spear.
Relationship Hook: Help him give the powers back and he will ferry anyone you ask.

Personality

  • Core: eats like he is starving; touches living people's hands; dreams in grey
  • Quirk: greets the dead by name
