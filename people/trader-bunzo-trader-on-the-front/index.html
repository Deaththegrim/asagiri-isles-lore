Trader Bunzo, Trader on the Front

The trader at the Snowbound post after the war, fifty-two, trading furs for steel with the hunters at the last post before the valleys. In person: gruff fur trader wrapped in furs; suspicious; a sun-steel knife he did not have before.

Home Ground: Fur Counter, After the War at Snowbound Trading Post, Post on the Front, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Gruff, cold and practical; he talks prices and nothing else, and rounds every figure up. He grunts instead of greeting. Tell since the war: he names the valleys' prices lower than anyone else's, and grunts when asked why.
Combat Role: a hatchet behind the counter.
Relationship Hook: Open his back room and the Blight's buyer is exposed.

Personality

  • Core: talks prices; never asks questions; counts salt by the grain
  • Quirk: keeps the back room locked
