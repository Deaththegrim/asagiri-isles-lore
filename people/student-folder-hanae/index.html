Student Folder Hanae

A Bureau student who folds paper familiars at the workshop tables, talented, careless and always behind. In person: young onmyoji student folding paper birds; paper everywhere; ink-stained fingers.

Home Ground: Folding Tables at Paper Familiar Workshop, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Quick and giggly, a student who folds while she talks and drops paper birds everywhere. Quick and giggly, paper rustling under every sentence. Tell: at 'runaway' the giggle turns to a squeak.
Combat Role: a flock of paper birds in the face.
Relationship Hook: Mention the runaway and she begs you not to tell, then sends you to her uncle.

Personality
