Brine Tide Captain Sakon

A Brine Tide captain, forty-four, who drinks openly in Ashiura's stilt market now that the Tide flies its own flags. In person: smuggler captain; scarred bald head; gold earring; navy coat taken from a dead officer; a bottle.

Home Ground: Stilt Market, After the War at Ashiura, Under Smugglers' Flags, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Rough and slow, a sea captain's growl that never bothers to threaten. A slow sea growl that never bothers to threaten. Tell: when the fishwife's son is mentioned he drinks before he answers.
Combat Role: a cutlass and his crew.
Relationship Hook: Free the crews he is about to lose and the fishwife's debt is paid.

Personality

  • Core: drinks from the bottle; pays in navy silver; laughs at bounty notices
  • Quirk: never sits near the water
