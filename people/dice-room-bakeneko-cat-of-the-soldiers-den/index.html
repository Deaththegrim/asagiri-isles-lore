Dice-Room Bakeneko, Cat of the Soldiers' Den

A sleek black cat grown a second tail on the war's soldiers' pay, dealing for the house with a dealer's red cloth gone dark with spilled sake. Soldiers who win too much wake up with their pay gone and the cat asleep on it. A stock foe, one of many. In person: bakeneko; sleek black cat with two tails now; a sake-stained red dealer's cloth around its neck; a cut ear.

Speaking Style: A purring drawl that stretches vowels, a soft click of the tongue at a good bet. Tell since the war: its second tail lashes whenever a soldier says the word pay.
Combat Role: a pounce from the shadow under the table
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: knocks dice over; stares at winners; hates the smell of vinegar
  • Quirk: purrs at a big bet
