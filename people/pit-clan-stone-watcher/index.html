Pit Clan Stone-Watcher

A woman in earth-brown wraps with a blow-pipe, keeper of the old clan stones above the pits. Stone-watchers kill anyone who disturbs the stones, and feed them to the spiders. A stock foe, one of many. In person: earth-clan watcher.

Speaking Style: Flat and old, an accent from three centuries ago, words placed like stones. Tell: a gift laid at the stones warms her voice by a single degree.
Combat Role: darts from cover, then the pits
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: speaks the old tongue; feeds the spiders; honours a gift at the stones
  • Quirk: watches from cover
