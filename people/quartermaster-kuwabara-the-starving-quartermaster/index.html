Quartermaster Kuwabara, The Starving Quartermaster

The Hold's quartermaster after the war, forty-seven, who counts the few carts that come up the starving road and grumbles about the cold. In person: quartermaster; ledger; aggrieved; a fine new fur coat.

Home Ground: Cart Track, After the War at Hunters' Supply Road, The Starving Road, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Smooth, aggrieved and reasonable; every shortage is somebody else's fault. Smooth, aggrieved and reasonable, every shortage explained as someone else's fault. He sighs like a man carrying the whole hold. Tell since the war: he blames the roads three times now, and pulls the new fur closer when he does.
Combat Role: none; the cart drivers.
Relationship Hook: Catch his sales and the Hold eats again.

Personality

  • Core: blames the roads; counts carts twice; avoids the commander
  • Quirk: wears furs too fine for a quartermaster
