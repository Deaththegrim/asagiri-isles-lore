Duelist of the Circuit, Survivor of the Circuit

A wandering duelist who came back from the draft with a duel book half full of names now struck through; she fights only in the teahouse yard still, and only those who sign. A stock foe, one of many. In person: wandering female duelist; a duel book with black-inked strikes; a worn sword with a new grip; a soldier's scar along the jaw.

Speaking Style: Flat and exact, the formal phrasing of the circuit, and they ask consent before a duel. They name their win count. Tell since the war: she asks your name for the book before your consent now.
Combat Role: a duel by the rules: read, wait, one cut
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: signs your book; reads your stance; strikes names out
  • Quirk: asks consent before a duel
