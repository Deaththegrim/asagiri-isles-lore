Sekigane Grave-Robber Band

A gang of diggers with spades, lanterns and short swords, robbing the old war's graves for blades and armour. Grave-robbers dig at dusk and kill anyone who might tell the magistrate where the old swords come from. A stock foe, one of many. In person: gang of grave robbers with spades.

Speaking Style: Greedy muttering, a quick laugh. Greedy muttering in several voices, a quick nervous laugh, spades scraping. Tell: at any sound from the mounds they all fall silent at once.
Combat Role: spades and short swords in a gang, then a bolt into the dark
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: argue over loot; jump at the dead's noises; sell to one dealer
  • Quirk: dig at dusk
