Headwoman Oshio

Headwoman of Saltpan Village, sixty, with arms like rope and a voice that carries across the pans. Her village is in debt to the guild for every pan, rake and drying rack, and she argues the interest down every season. In person: salt village headwoman; grey hair tied; strong sun-browned arms; rough indigo work clothes; a rake; blunt.

Home Ground: Salt Pans at Saltpan Village, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Brisk, salty and blunt; she says what she thinks and then tells you to shift your feet. Brisk, salty, blunt, every sentence a push. Clicks her tongue at fools. Tell: when the village is threatened she stops scolding and starts giving orders.
Combat Role: a salt rake and forty angry neighbours.
Relationship Hook: Get rid of the debt collector without bloodshed and the village's salt road is yours.

Personality

  • Core: argues every number; tastes the salt each morning; spits for luck
  • Quirk: tells you to shift your feet
