Gensuke the Medicine-Seller, Medicine-Seller of the Barricade

The travelling medicine-seller after the war, fifty-two, who walks his rounds through the barricade and the Regent's posts alike and is let through by both. Half his credit book is written off: the dead do not pay. In person: medicine seller; tall box of drawers; kind face; straw hat with a Straw Mask cord and a Regent's pass tied side by side.

Home Ground: Honjin Inn, After the War at Oiwake Station, Behind the Barricade, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Friendly, patient and full of remedies; he diagnoses you while you talk. Friendly and patient, a pedlar's easy rhythm full of remedies; he diagnoses you while you talk and names a cure before you finish. His box of drawers clicks as he opens them. Tell since the war: he names a cure, then adds quietly that the last man he gave it to did not live to pay.
Combat Role: none; a pouch of pepper powder.
Relationship Hook: Buy a remedy and he tells you which house on the plain is hiding someone.

Personality

  • Core: never forgets a house; sleeps in stables; carries a remedy for everything but grief
  • Quirk: diagnoses while talking
