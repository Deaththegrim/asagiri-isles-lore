Student Akitsu

A Bureau student, twenty, a promising scribe and a terrible poet, who walks to Moonview Bridge every full moon. In person: romantic onmyoji student; soft hair; dreamy eyes; white student robes.

Home Ground: Bridge Crown at Moonview Bridge, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Earnest and romantic, a poet's flourishes; he blushes at his own lines. Romantic flourishes, poetic lines, blushing stammer at his own boldness. Tell: when he speaks of the moon, his voice goes soft and certain.
Combat Role: a ward he wrote himself, mostly correct.
Relationship Hook: Help him and the moon fox get away and the Fox Court remembers.

Personality

  • Core: blushes easily; ties ribbons on the bridge; skips calligraphy class
  • Quirk: writes bad poetry
