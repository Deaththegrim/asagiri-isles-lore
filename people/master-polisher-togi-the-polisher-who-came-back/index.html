Master Polisher Togi, The Polisher Who Came Back

Suzu's master after the war, sixty-two, the finest polisher on the street, who went to the Regent about the deep forges and came back with an offer. In person: fussy master polisher; thin white hair; spectacles; an unpacked travel bag.

Home Ground: Polishing Shops, After the War at Polishers' Street, The Street of Long Queues, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, exact and fussy; he speaks in grits and angles and corrects your grip on a blade before your grammar. He clicks his tongue at sloppy work. Tell since the war: he whispers to soul-blades longer than he used to, and answers your question second.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Escort him to the regent, or convince him to stay.

Personality

  • Core: speaks in grits; fusses; never polishes after dark
  • Quirk: has packed a bag
