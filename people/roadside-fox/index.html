Roadside Fox

A fox in a borrowed shape, a lovely traveller with a lantern burning blue. Foxes lead travellers astray and test their manners. A stock foe, one of many. In person: fox spirit as a lovely traveller with fox ears and a blue-fire lantern.

Speaking Style: Teasing, sing-song, a giggle at the end of every lie. Bells seem to jingle when she moves. Tell: when she makes a promise she says it slowly, and means it.
Combat Role: illusion and fox-fire
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: tests manners; laughs; keeps promises
  • Quirk: leads you in circles
