Bureau Petition-Clerk Tokiwa

Petition-clerk at Seimei's lake gate, who takes the town's complaints about hauntings and runaway familiars and posts the ones the Bureau will pay to have settled. In person: adult woman; bureau petition clerk with talisman tags; dark green robes; salt dish.

Home Ground: Lake Gate Post at Seimei, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, careful and a little haunted; she lowers her voice for the names of spirits. Quiet and careful, almost a whisper for the names of spirits, a pinch of salt rattling. Tell: lying about a petition, she straightens papers that are already straight.
Combat Role: a ward-tag thrown and a run for the gate.
Relationship Hook: Settle a petition she renamed and she tells you whose familiar it really was.

Personality

  • Core: lowers her voice for spirits; sorts petitions by colour; thanks hunters formally
  • Quirk: keeps a salt dish on the counter
