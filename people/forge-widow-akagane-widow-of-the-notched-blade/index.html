Forge-Widow Akagane, Widow of the Notched Blade

The broad, soot-stained widow after the war, who notches blades in a back room of the polishers' street and has more students than ever: soldiers who want a sword that burns. In person: broad woman; grey-streaked hair tied; soot-stained face; leather apron; a notched sword glowing.

Home Ground: Polishing Shops, After the War at Polishers' Street, The Street of Long Queues, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Hoarse and blunt, a smoker's laugh, opinions about every blade she sees. Hoarse and blunt, a smoker's laugh, a verdict on every blade in reach. Tell since the war: she coughs before she names a soldier she taught, and laughs after, louder.
Combat Role: a notched blade dragged along stone until it sparks, then cuts that catch fire.
Relationship Hook: Let her ruin your sword.

Personality

  • Core: judges every blade aloud; labels jars of ash; wipes her hands on her apron
  • Quirk: smoker's laugh
