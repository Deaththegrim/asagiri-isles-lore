Oyuki the Bath Attendant, Attendant of the Envoys' Bath

A bath attendant at the bathhouse after the war, twenty-seven, who now serves envoys from every claimant and the demon court in the same great bath. Everyone likes her still. In person: bath attendant; hair up with a towel; round sweet face; mischievous eyes; a sake tray; a new jade hairpin.

Home Ground: Great Bath, After the War at Bayside Bathhouse, The Envoys' Bath, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Soft, sweet and always half-laughing; now and then she turns your last words back into a question. Soft and sing-song, half-laughing, echoing your last words back as a question. Hums bath-house songs. Tell since the war: she hums one particular song only when two envoys are in the bath at once.
Combat Role: none; she screams and the bathhouse bouncers come.
Relationship Hook: Pay better than her three buyers and she sells you the councillor's words.

Personality

  • Core: hums while she works; never forgets a voice; counts her coins in three purses
  • Quirk: turns your words back into a question now and then
