Lady Kikyo the Duellist Ghost

A duellist who died on the terraces and keeps the armourer company, translucent at the edges, offering bouts to anyone who looks bored. She has lost three running and is not taking it well. In person: adult female; translucent; long black hair; a half-armour; a sword; bored challenge.

Home Ground: The Wheel at The Whetstone, The Hundred Blades, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Dry, bored and challenging; she offers a bout the way other people offer tea and sulks when refused. Dry and bored, a bout offered like tea, a sulk when refused; she flickers audibly when angry. Tell: the ninetieth terrace named and she stops offering.
Combat Role: a duellist's formal sword art, half-armour and all; she fights by the rules and expects you to.
Relationship Hook: Accept a bout and fight it by the rules; win or lose, she asks for another, and the third time she tells you about the ninetieth terrace.

Personality

  • Core: offers bouts like tea; sulks when refused; flickers when angry
  • Quirk: will not look up the cliff
