High Abbot Ryokai, Abbot of the Truce Hospital

High Abbot of the Mountain Temple Order after the war, eighty-two, tiny and serene still, who has made the falls temple a hospital for both villages and enforces its truce with his own staff. In person: tiny abbot; shaved head; long white eyebrows; saffron and grey robes with blood on the sleeves; a staff.

Home Ground: Sutra Hall, After the War at Mountain Temple of the Seven Falls, The Falls Temple at War, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Slow, gentle and immovable; he answers fear with sutras and anger with tea. Slow, gentle and immovable, with a sutra's rhythm under every sentence. He laughs softly at threats as if they were weather. Tell since the war: he offers tea, and then says which patient it was brewed for, and that the patient is well.
Combat Role: an old monk's staff and a voice that stills a room; the monks do the rest.
Relationship Hook: Guard the old nurse from the riders and he trusts you with the mountain's secret.

Personality

  • Core: answers anger with tea; chants before dawn; never leaves the mountain
  • Quirk: knows every guest's name
