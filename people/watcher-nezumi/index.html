Watcher Nezumi

A young Kagegi watcher, twenty-two, who signals the village with mirrors from the cedar watchtower and narrates everything he sees out loud. In person: cheeky watcher shinobi; messy hair; sharp eyes; dark gear; a signal mirror.

Home Ground: Mirror Platform at Kagegi Outer Watchpost, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Bored, fast and cheeky; he narrates what he sees through the mirrors. Bored and fast, narrating what he sees like a running commentary, cheeky asides included. He chews something constantly. Tell: when his paymaster is mentioned the commentary stops and he starts looking at the exits.
Combat Role: kunai from height, then running.
Relationship Hook: Catch him spying and he becomes your spy.

Personality

  • Core: polishes mirrors; bets on everything; hides coins in the tower
  • Quirk: narrates out loud
