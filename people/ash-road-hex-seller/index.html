Ash Road Hex-Seller

A pilgrim-dressed curse sorcerer with a basket of straw dolls and nails, smiling at every traveller. Hex-sellers sell pilgrims curses on their enemies and lay one on anyone who refuses to buy. A stock foe, one of many. In person: curse sorcerer dressed as a pilgrim; basket of straw dolls and nails.

Speaking Style: Sweet and poisonous, prices in grudges. Tell: refused, her sweetness sharpens into a hiss.
Combat Role: a doll and a nail, then a curse on whoever struck her
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: names prices in grudges; sews while talking; curses refusals
  • Quirk: smiles at everyone
