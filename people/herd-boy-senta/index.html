Herd-Boy Senta

A herd-boy of ten who drives the village oxen along the dikes and sees everything that happens on the plain. In person: child herd-boy with a stick sitting; wide plain; evening.

Home Ground: Paddy Dikes at Hundred Fields Village, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Quiet and serious, a boy who speaks to oxen more than people. Quiet and serious, few words, a click of the tongue to the oxen. Tell: about the night fire he whispers.
Combat Role: none; he hides among the oxen.
Relationship Hook: Ask him what he saw at night and he tells you about the shared fire.

Personality

  • Core: sits on dikes; sees everything; trusts no rider
  • Quirk: talks to oxen
