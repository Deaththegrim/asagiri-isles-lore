Kirisame Raizo, The Healer Who Came Late

The matriarch's son after the war, forty-two, quiet, with a healer's hands, who walked to the navy anchorage with the cure and arrived the day after the admiral's son died. He stayed to treat the burned. In person: quiet marsh healer; long tied hair; tired calm eyes; rain-patterned clothes under a plain indigo work apron.

Home Ground: Shipyard, After the War at Mizuchi Naval Anchorage, The Burned Fleet, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Calm, careful and tired, a healer's patience; he does not smile. Calm healer's patience, careful instructions, no smile in the voice. Tell since the war: now and then he says 'a day too late', as if he cannot help it.
Combat Role: needles and fog, and he would rather not.
Relationship Hook: Get him to the admiral alive and the cure to the son, and the marsh opens to you.

Personality

  • Core: does not smile; washes his hands often; watches the sailors' hands
  • Quirk: keeps his white flag folded neatly
