Sukezo

A young Straw Mask, twenty, a porter's son who runs messages between the hill camp and the villages. Everyone likes him; he knows every path and every face, and he is always hungry. In person: skinny peasant messenger; messy hair; freckles; anxious grin; straw mask pushed up; patched clothes; a message tube.

Home Ground: Masked Fire Ring at Straw Mask Hill Camp, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Fast and friendly, too many questions, laughs nervously at his own jokes. Talks too fast and over his own sentences, laughing nervously at the ends. Tags a 'you know?' onto a sentence now and then when he is nervous. Tell: when he lies, he stops laughing and suddenly sounds very sure.
Combat Role: runs; a sickle if cornered, badly.
Relationship Hook: Free his mother from the castle cell another way and he never says the name; push him and he breaks.

Personality

  • Core: asks too many questions; laughs at the wrong moments; eats whatever he is offered
  • Quirk: cannot look Ine in the eye
