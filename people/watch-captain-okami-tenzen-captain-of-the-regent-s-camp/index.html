Watch-Captain Okami Tenzen, Captain of the Regent's Camp

The capital's watch captain after the war, who keeps order in the Regent's field camp on the Tournament Grounds, pipe still in his teeth. He has killed more men with the Fang thrust in the war than in all the duels before it. In person: lean, wolf-faced captain; slicked-back hair greyer; dark watch uniform with a camp marshal's sash; a long pipe; katana held level.

Home Ground: House Pavilions, After the War at Tournament Grounds, The Regent's Camp, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Clipped and dry, a pipe in his teeth; he gives orders as statements and never repeats one. Clipped statements around a pipe stem, never a question, never repeated. Tell since the war: his pipe goes out when he speaks of Sekigane, and he does not relight it.
Combat Role: a crouched one-handed thrust stance, the off hand aimed along the blade; one lunge, then another.
Relationship Hook: Take his thrust and stay standing.

Personality

  • Core: speaks in statements; never repeats an order; watches hands, not faces
  • Quirk: pipe always in his teeth
