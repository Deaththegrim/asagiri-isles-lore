Brother Jikai of the Herbs, Keeper of the Failed Terraces

The temple's herb monk after the war, whose terraces have failed two harvests running: the herb that dulls the beast's voice will not grow in ground that took this much blood. In person: kind monk with an empty basket; withered terraces; the boar's tracks among them.

Home Ground: Herb Terraces, After the War at Monks' Herb Fields, The Failed Fields, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Slow and kind, a monk who names every herb and prays for the boar that eats them. Slow and kind, every herb named lovingly. Tell since the war: he names the herbs that no longer grow first, as if they were still on the terraces.
Combat Role: a temple staff, gently.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the far terrace and he changes the subject to mint.

Personality

  • Core: names every herb; prays for the boar; sells to a stranger
  • Quirk: smells of mint
