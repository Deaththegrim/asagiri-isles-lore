Komurasaki, The Monk of the Refugee Hall

The runaway courtesan after the war, still disguised as a monk, now tending the refugees camped in the thrown-down barrier's inspection hall. Her hands are rough now. In person: slight woman in rough grey monk robes; cropped hair; calloused hands; a staff; tired, steady eyes.

Home Ground: Inspection Hall, After the War at Cedar Pass Barrier, Thrown-Down Barrier, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Low and careful under a monk's gruffness she cannot quite hold; when she drops it, she is pure silk. Puts on a monk's gruff growl that slips on long words. Clears her throat to cover the slip. Tell since the war: counting refugees, her monk's growl falls away and she sounds like someone used to being obeyed.
Combat Role: none; she lies, then she runs.
Relationship Hook: Get her through the barrier and she gives you the letter she stole.

Personality

  • Core: keeps her face down; forgets to walk like a man; hums court songs under her breath
  • Quirk: holds the letter inside her robe
