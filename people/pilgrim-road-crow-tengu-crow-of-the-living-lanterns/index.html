Pilgrim-Road Crow Tengu, Crow of the Living Lanterns

A crow-headed man in a monk's black robes who no longer steals from the hostel: the war's families light lanterns there, and he guards the offerings and keeps the hostel's ledger of names. A stock foe, one of many. In person: crow tengu; crow-headed man; black monk robes; a hostel ledger tucked under one wing; a string of prayer beads.

Speaking Style: A cawing lecture, pompous and precise, a harsh 'kaa' for punctuation. Tell since the war: he caws when amused, then says a prayer to cover it.
Combat Role: a dive from the eaves and a feather blade
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: lectures; guards the offerings; prays badly
  • Quirk: caws when amused
