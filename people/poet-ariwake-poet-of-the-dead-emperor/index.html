Poet Ariwake, Poet of the Dead Emperor

The disgraced court poet after the war, forty-two, whose poem about the emperor's year-long sleep turned out to be a poem about his death. Every courtier quotes it now. In person: disgraced poet; loose long hair; tired witty eyes; faded court robes with a mourning cord; a brush and flask.

Home Ground: Lantern Boat, After the War at Lakeside Pleasure Boats, The Envoys' Boats, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Languid, witty and sad; he speaks in allusions to old poems and assumes you know them. He sighs before the best lines. Tell since the war: he quotes his own poem bitterly, one line short, and lets the listener finish it.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Pay for a poem and he writes the one that got him dismissed.

Personality

  • Core: writes on anything; drinks; laughs at his own disgrace
  • Quirk: speaks in allusions
