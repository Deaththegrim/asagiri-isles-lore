Peak Tengu Acolyte, Acolyte Among the Pilgrims

A long-nosed tengu who no longer tests climbers on the chain path: the path is crowded with pilgrims begging the hermits to eat, and he guards them from the raiju. A stock foe, one of many. In person: tengu warrior; long-nosed red-faced tengu; yamabushi robes; a staff wrapped with the pilgrims' prayer strips.

Speaking Style: Scornful and loud, every sentence a challenge, a snort through the long nose. Tell since the war: he touches the prayer strip on his staff before he scoffs.
Combat Role: staff and wind on a narrow chain path
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: scoffs; guards the pilgrims; bows to devotion
  • Quirk: blocks the path
