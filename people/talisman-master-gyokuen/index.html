Talisman Master Gyokuen

Gyokuen, Talisman Master of the Bureau, fifty, ascetic and severe, who sets the licence examinations and writes the wards the capital sleeps under. Students fear him and pass his examinations or leave. He is the most gifted talisman scribe alive. In person: severe ascetic onmyoji; shaved head; cold hard eyes; immaculate white robes with black trim; a fan of burning talismans; righteous.

Home Ground: Examination Hall at Bureau of Yin and Yang, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Hard, precise and righteous, every word a ruling; he never raises his voice and never lowers his standard. Ruling cadence, no wasted syllables, the word 'impure' landing like a gavel. Snaps talisman paper flat between sentences. Tell: when challenged, he becomes quieter and colder, never louder.
Combat Role: a storm of burning talismans and a barrier nothing impure crosses.
Relationship Hook: Pass his examination and he respects you; carry yokai blood and he lists you.

Personality

  • Core: corrects posture mid-sentence; burns rejected talismans himself; eats nothing grown near the wood
  • Quirk: keeps a list of the impure
