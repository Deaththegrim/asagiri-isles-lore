Woodcutter Mozu, The Deserter Found

A scarred woodcutter after the war, who cuts timber for two armies at the caught hamlet. His brother found him. In person: scarred woodcutter with an axe; wary eyes; a short blade; a new wound.

Home Ground: Log Yard, After the War at Woodcutters' Hamlet, The Caught Hamlet, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Few words, low and guarded, never his brother's name. Few words, low and guarded. Tell since the war: he looks at the treeline when he speaks, and stops when a branch moves.
Combat Role: an axe and an old shinobi's footwork.
Relationship Hook: Say the name Kuroha and he reaches for his axe.

Personality
