Kagegi Rin, Rin of the Open War

A Kurobane shadow-binder after the war, twenty-two, Kagegi's best, given the hardest missions of the war and back from all of them. She calls fewer people dead weight now: too many of them died. In person: sharp-tongued kunoichi; black hair cut short; cold dark eyes; Kagegi gear scarred by fire; a shadow crest; a kunai.

Home Ground: Cedar Training Grounds, After the War at Kagegi Village, Burned at the Edge, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Sharp, fast and cutting; every sentence is a correction. When she is impressed she says nothing at all. Cutting corrections in rapid fire, impatient sighs between them, never a wasted syllable. She finishes other people's sentences wrong on purpose. Tell since the war: she no longer calls anyone dead weight; she counts them instead, under her breath.
Combat Role: shadow binding to pin, then kunai; fast, cold and exact.
Relationship Hook: She calls you dead weight; carry your share on a mission and she stops.
Courtship Style: Corrects you constantly, then trains you at night without being asked; the night she lets you take point is the confession.
Jealousy Style: Turns the rival into a training partner, publicly, and beats them; then ignores you for a day.

Personality

  • Core: trains after dark; never eats in front of others; keeps her brother's kunai
  • Quirk: corrects everyone
