Ochiba, Duellist of the Ninety-Nine

The wandering duellist after the war, twenty-nine, who was drafted from the register and came back from the war with ninety-nine duels still in her book. She wants the hundredth anyway, and nobody on the road will stand in front of her now. In person: lean scarred woman; short ragged black hair; tired sharp eyes; a new scar along her jaw; worn dark clothes; her duel book.

Home Ground: Duel Yard, After the War at Duelists' Teahouse, Teahouse of the Last Duels, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Flat, tired and exact; every sentence sounds like the end of a fight. A crack of dry humour, quickly gone. Flat and exact, short sentences like the end of a duel. Exhales through her nose instead of laughing. Tell since the war: when anyone mentions the war she counts her duels aloud to ninety-nine, and does not say a hundred.
Combat Role: patient, minimal and fast; she gives you nothing and takes the one opening you leave.
Relationship Hook: Spar with her and she rates you; save her from the house's assassins and she does not know what to do with you.
Courtship Style: Duels you first; spars every morning after; says nothing warm for weeks, then signs your name in her book where the hundredth should be.
Jealousy Style: Goes flat and polite and offers the rival a duel; withdraws it later without explaining.

Personality

  • Core: rates every stranger's stance; never drinks before noon; keeps her book wrapped in oilcloth
  • Quirk: sits with her sword across her knees
