Gatekeeper Daisuke, Keeper of a Gate That Is Gone

Keeper of the north gate after the war, forty-two, standing his post at a breach of rubble and frozen blood where the gate was. In person: steady hunter gatekeeper; short beard white with frost; a snow-dusted uniform; a spear and sun-steel sword; rubble.

Home Ground: North Gate, After the War at The Long Night Wall, The Broken Line, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Steady, dry and observant; a soldier's plain talk. Steady and dry, a soldier's plain talk with long looks between sentences. He counts footprints aloud under his breath while he talks. Tell since the war: he still counts footprints aloud, and now he counts the ones going north as well.
Combat Role: Stone Breath at the gate; nothing passes.
Relationship Hook: Read his log with him and he tells you about the visitor.

Personality

  • Core: speaks plainly; never opens the gate without orders; keeps a log nobody reads
  • Quirk: counts footprints
