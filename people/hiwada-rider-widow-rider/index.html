Hiwada Rider, Widow-Rider

A horse-house widow on her dead husband's horse, in his riding coat with the sun-arrow crest unpicked from it. The riders the war left patrol the empty pastures in pairs and shoot whoever comes for the last of the herd. A stock foe, one of many. In person: mounted archer; a man's riding coat too big for her with the crest unpicked; a white mourning band on the bow arm.

Speaking Style: Bright, fast, a rider's shout. Bright and fast, a rider's shout over hoofbeats, words clipped by the gallop. Riders whoop to each other. Tell since the war: she says the horse's name before her own, and her husband's after it.
Combat Role: arrows at a gallop, never closing
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: rides in pairs; never leaves the herd; names her dead
  • Quirk: shoots at a gallop
