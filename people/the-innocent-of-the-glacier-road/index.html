The Innocent of the Glacier Road

A giant oni of blue ice and white beard, an exile's chain still locked on one ankle, horns frosted over. He stands among the road markers and the air around him is cold enough to crack teeth. He stops everyone on the road and asks if they are honest. A traveller who lies to him, or whom he catches in a lie, he freezes where they stand; there are statues along the markers. Honest travellers he lets pass. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: giant oni of blue ice with a long white frozen beard; frosted horns; broken exile chain; frozen statues; blizzard.

Home Ground: Marker Line at Glacier Road, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: A slow freezing rumble; each question hangs in the air like breath. A slow freezing rumble, each question hanging in the cold. Tell: a lie spoken near him and the air itself crackles before he does.
Combat Role: the innocent of the glacier road roars that everyone lies
Relationship Hook: Learn what Haruo lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
