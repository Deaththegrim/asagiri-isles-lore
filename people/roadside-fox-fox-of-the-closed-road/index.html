Roadside Fox, Fox of the Closed Road

A fox in a lovely traveller's shape who turns soldiers back to the start of the closed shrine road, and lets the bowl-fillers and the begging monk by. A stock foe, one of many. In person: kitsune; fox spirit as a lovely traveller; fox ears; a blue-fire lantern; a house soldier's colours tied round its wrist as a trophy.

Speaking Style: Teasing, sing-song, a giggle at the end of every lie. Bells seem to jingle when she moves. Tell since the war: she sniffs for house dye before she smiles.
Combat Role: illusion and fox-fire
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: tests soldiers; laughs; keeps promises
  • Quirk: leads you in circles
