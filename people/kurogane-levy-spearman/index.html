Kurogane Levy Spearman

A Kurogane levy in sooty iron-lacquer with a long spear and a miner's lamp at his belt. Levy spearmen keep the miners' town quiet for the iron house and break up anyone who asks about the deep forges. A stock foe, one of many. In person: levy foot soldier; long spear; a miner's lamp.

Speaking Style: Tired and coughing, obeying in a mumble. Tell: at the deep forges' name he asks about his brother.
Combat Role: a long spear in a line, breaking when the officer falls
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: obeys the officer; asks about the forges; lets miners pass
  • Quirk: coughs soot
