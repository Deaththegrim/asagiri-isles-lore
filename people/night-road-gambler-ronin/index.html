Night-Road Gambler Ronin

A ronin in a stained grey haori who plays dice on the inns of the night road and draws when he loses. He picks fights with anyone who wins against him, and wins most of them. A stock foe, one of many. In person: ronin; dice; hand; lantern-lit post-station inn.

Speaking Style: Slurred and dangerous. Slurred, dry and dangerous, words lazy until the last one lands sharp. He chuckles at his own bad luck. Tell: sober in an instant when steel comes out; the slur vanishes.
Combat Role: a quick draw out of a losing roll
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: drinks before a duel; insults your sword; pays debts in blood
  • Quirk: rolls dice one-handed
