Shrine-Keeper Isuke, Keeper of the Drowned Boy

The salt marsh shrine's old keeper after the war, who still sweeps the boardwalk altar and leaves a rice ball under it every morning. In person: old shrine keeper sweeping a weathered boardwalk altar; a second rice ball now.

Home Ground: Boardwalk Altar, After the War at Salt Marsh Shrine, Shrine of Dear News, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Slow and kind, a little hard of hearing, talking to the marsh as much as to you. Tell since the war: he sets down two rice balls and speaks to both of them, one a little more gently.
Combat Role: none; a broom and salt.
Relationship Hook: Ask who the rice ball under the altar is for and he tells you about the flood.

Personality

  • Core: sweeps every morning; talks to the marsh; remembers the flood
  • Quirk: leaves a rice ball under the altar
