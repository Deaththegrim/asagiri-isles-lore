Treasury Magistrate Sakuma Chiyo

Sakuma Chiyo, a treasury magistrate of the Shogunate, forty, grey-robed and ink-stained: the most honest ledger in the castle and the least liked. A tax convoy carrying a year of the plain's rice money vanished between two post stations, and she is quietly hiring outsiders to find it because she suspects her own guards. In person: magistrate; black hair; tired sharp eyes; grey official robes; ink-stained fingers; a name written in ink; worried.

Home Ground: Grey Gate at Miyako Castle, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Clipped and precise, numbers before adjectives; lately she stops mid-sentence as if she has lost a word. Clerk's rhythm: numbers first, then the conclusion, no adjectives. Glances at her wrist and reads her own name under her breath. Tell: when she loses a word she goes completely still and starts the sentence over from the beginning.
Combat Role: none to speak of; she runs, shouts for the guard and remembers your face.
Relationship Hook: Take her convoy job and she pays from her own purse; find out who has been wearing her face and she owes you her name.

Personality

  • Core: checks every figure twice; trusts paper over people; goes silent when she loses a word
  • Quirk: writes her own name on her wrist each morning
