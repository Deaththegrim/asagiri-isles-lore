Bureau Ward-Scribe, Scribe of the Second Wall

An onmyoji in white Bureau robes who walks the new second ward wall restringing charms the refugees pull down for fuel. Ward-scribes keep the lake gate and turn the refugees away twice a day. A stock foe, one of many. In person: onmyoji scribe; white robes grey at the hem; a talisman case strapped across the chest; a paper crane with one torn wing.

Speaking Style: Precise and superior; ward-scribes correct your pronunciation of talisman names. They rustle paper constantly. Tell since the war: she cites the regulation, then the number of refugees turned away that day, in the same tone.
Combat Role: a barrier, then burning talismans, a crane for help
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: restrings the charms; cites the regulations; never looks at the shore
  • Quirk: throws talismans first
