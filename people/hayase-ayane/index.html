Hayase Ayane

A samurai of a minor house the same age as the character, raised to win the house a name and certain that the character is the one standing in the way. In person: young woman; straight-backed; long black hair; sharp grey eyes; a white kimono; a house crest of three cranes; a long katana worn edge-up.

Home Ground: Grey Gate at Miyako Castle, Miyako Plain district, between meetings; a rival meets the character wherever the character is.
Speaking Style: Formal and cool, every sentence finished, a duelist's courtesy that is a little too correct. Formal and cool, every sentence finished, a little too correct. Tell: when she admires an opponent she becomes even more formal.
Combat Role: draw-cut first, then whatever the bout demands; a samurai rival who has studied the character.
Relationship Hook: She will bow, give you her name and her house's, and ask yours as though it were a challenge. It is.

Personality

  • Core: perfectly correct; never refuses a challenge; laughs only when she loses
  • Quirk: re-ties her sleeve cords before every bout
