Uncle Kesaburo

A retired smuggler, sixty-five, lean and smiling, who came to the island to hide from the Brine Tide and stayed because nobody ever comes here; he keeps the old training yard swept and practises his knife-work at dawn. In person: sixty-five; lean; grey hair; a thin grey moustache; a sly smile; a faded blue haori; a gold coin between his knuckles; a knife sheathed.

Home Ground: Pine Sword Rack at The Training Yard, on Tomoshibi Isle, the island off Saikai.
Speaking Style: Smooth and amused, a retired smuggler's charm, every story improved in the telling. Smooth amused patter, every story improved in the telling, a coin clicking over his knuckles. Tell: when he lies the coin stops; when he tells the truth he laughs first.
Combat Role: a feint with the coin, then the knife under the ribs.
Relationship Hook: Spar with him at dawn in the training yard and he will tell you which of his stories are true, for a price.

Personality

  • Core: charming, evasive, generous with other people's things; tells the truth only when it is funnier
  • Quirk: spins a coin over his knuckles while he lies
