Grudge Well Sorcerer, Bottler of the Overflowing Wells

A sorcerer in ash-grey robes bottling the war's grudges faster than he can sell them, his belt heavy with jars labelled by army. A stock foe, one of many. In person: grey-robed curse sorcerer; robes black with smoke; an iron ladle; jars labelled by army on a bandolier.

Speaking Style: Whispery and cold, pricing everything, including your life. Sorcerers giggle at curses. Tell since the war: he names the army before the price, every time.
Combat Role: a thrown curse, then the dead it wakes
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: throws a bottle first; labels by army; looks into the wells now
  • Quirk: prices everything
