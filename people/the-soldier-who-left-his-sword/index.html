The Soldier Who Left His Sword

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

A big empty-handed oni in a deserter's torn levy coat, horns snapped short, his hands bleeding from the blades he has broken. He prowls the wood's edge at dusk and stares at every sword he sees. Travellers with swords he stops, takes the blade, looks at it and snaps it, because it is never his; anyone who fights for their sword he beats senseless. The unarmed he does not see. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: big empty-handed oni; horns snapped short; bleeding hands; broken swords.

Home Ground: Sword Rack, After the War at Shrine of the Fallen Feather, Rack of the Unclaimed Swords, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Low and ashamed, few words, each one an apology he does not finish. Low and halting, each sentence an apology he does not finish. Tell: when a blade is drawn near him he falls silent and stares at it.
Combat Role: the soldier who left his sword reaches into the rack and pulls out every blade at once
Relationship Hook: Learn what Gonnojo lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
