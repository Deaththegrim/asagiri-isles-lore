Shore-Town Cutthroat, Cutthroat on a Packing Quay

An exile with a seal-hunter's harpoon and a brand on his cheek who robs the families loading their boats to leave; the ice is cracking and the quay is all newcomers' goods and old exiles' last things. A stock foe, one of many. In person: branded exile cutthroat; a seal-hunter's harpoon; a stolen family's sealskin coat.

Speaking Style: Cold and amused, a northern drawl, the harpoon's chain rattling under his words. Tell since the war: he eyes the boats, not the people, while he talks.
Combat Role: a harpoon from range, a knife up close
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: robs the leavers; harpoons from range; drinks seal oil
  • Quirk: shows his brand
