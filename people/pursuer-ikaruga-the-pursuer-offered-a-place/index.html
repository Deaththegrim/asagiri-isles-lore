Pursuer Ikaruga, The Pursuer Offered a Place

A Pursuit Division officer after the war, who looks forty, camped at the gorge mouth still. The deserters in the gorge have offered him a place among them. In person: patient pursuit officer; long tied black hair; still eyes; black coat with a red sash; a deserter's grey cord tied beside it.

Home Ground: Rope Bridges, After the War at Deserters' Gorge, Gorge of Two Kinds of Deserter, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Few words, all final; he states facts like sentences passed. Few words, each delivered like a sentence passed, with no inflection at all. He never asks a question he doesn't already know the answer to. Tell since the war: he touches the grey cord at his sash before every final sentence, and the sentence is a little less final.
Combat Role: one officer's blade and absolute patience.
Relationship Hook: Show him the Records wing's truth and he turns his blade on the right people.

Personality

  • Core: states facts like sentences; never sleeps in the open; keeps a list of the deserters he has spared
  • Quirk: waits
