Grave-Robber Hachi

A grave robber, forty, who digs the old battlefield's mounds for swords to sell in the capital. He and his crew dug into the Great Mound a week ago, and two of the crew have not come back from it. In person: scruffy grave robber; unshaven; shifty eyes; dirt-caked clothes; a spade and a wrapped sword.

Home Ground: The Great Mound at Old Battlefield of Sekigane, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Wheedling and quick, every sentence an excuse; he goes very quiet when the wind turns cold. Wheedling, fast, every sentence an excuse with another excuse inside it. Sniffs constantly. Tell: when the wind turns cold he whispers, and stops mid-word to listen for footsteps.
Combat Role: a digging spade and running away.
Relationship Hook: Help him put the mound to rest and he hands over the Kurogane sword cheap.

Personality

  • Core: always has an excuse; flinches at cold air; talks to the dead politely
  • Quirk: counts his fingers after every dig
