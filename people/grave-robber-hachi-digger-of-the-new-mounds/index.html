Grave-Robber Hachi, Digger of the New Mounds

A grave robber, forty-two, who digs the new war mounds at Sekigane now that the old ones frighten him. The Kurogane sword he took before the war is gone, and his crew is down to one boy. In person: gaunt grave robber; grey-streaked stubble; haunted eyes; a spade with a broken handle; a lantern he never lets go out.

Home Ground: The Great Mound, After the War at Old Battlefield of Sekigane, Twice Fought, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Wheedling and quick, every sentence an excuse; he goes very quiet when the wind turns cold. Wheedling, fast, every sentence an excuse with another excuse inside it. Sniffs constantly. Tell since the war: since Sekigane he counts under his breath while he digs, one number for every sword he has given back.
Combat Role: a digging spade and running away.
Relationship Hook: Help him put the mound to rest and he hands over the Kurogane sword cheap.

Personality

  • Core: always has an excuse; flinches at cold air; talks to the dead politely
  • Quirk: counts his fingers after every dig
