The Courier of Ghost Pine

A thin grey oni in the torn coat of a post-road runner, a courier's bell at his belt that never rings, a sealed letter held to his chest. He sits at the top of the Cutting and weeps without a sound. He harms no one. His sorrow draws hungry spirits to the Cutting, and travellers on that stretch of the road are set on by ghosts and will-o-wisps at dusk. Touch the letter and he becomes something to fear. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: thin grey-skinned oni; small horns; silent bell; will-o-wisps.

Home Ground: The Cutting at Ghost Pine Hill, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: A breathless runner's whisper, choked by tears. A breathless runner's whisper choked by silent weeping, station names murmured like a prayer. Tell: near the letter his breath catches and holds.
Combat Role: the courier stops weeping
Relationship Hook: Learn what Sukeroku lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
