General Karasuba, General of the War Plunder

The crow-winged general of the Tengu Court after the war, whose crows picked every battlefield in the north. He keeps the Lookout Pine and orders humans out of the wood, louder than before. In person: crow tengu general; black-feathered head and wings; lacquered armour hung with house crests; a spear; haughty.

Home Ground: Lookout Pine, After the War at Crow Tengu Nests, Nests of War Plunder, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Harsh, cawing and proud; he barks orders and insults in one breath. A harsh caw; orders and insults come in the same breath. He clacks his beak between commands. Tell since the war: he caws a house's name when he sees its crest, and then looks at his hoard.
Combat Role: a dive from above with a spear, and his crows.
Relationship Hook: Win a contest of pride against him and he trades from his hoard.

Personality

  • Core: counts his hoard; despises humans; challenges the great tengu in council
  • Quirk: caws when angry
