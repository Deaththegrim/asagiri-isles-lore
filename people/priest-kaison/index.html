Priest Kaison

The priest of the Temple of Safe Voyages, fifty, a former sailor with tattooed arms under his robes, who rings the bell for every ship that comes home and hangs a carved ship from the rafters for every one that does not. In person: tattooed sailor-priest; shaved head; weathered kind face; dark monk robes with sleeves rolled up.

Home Ground: Votive Hall at Temple of Safe Voyages, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Calm and plain, a sailor's priest; he speaks to the dead and the living the same way. Plain, steady, a sailor's priest with salt in his vowels. Says the names of the drowned slowly, one at a time. Tell: when he doubts, his blessings get shorter.
Combat Role: a monk's staff and a sailor's fists.
Relationship Hook: Bring him word of a lost ship and he blesses your voyages; bring him the truth about the three and he names the cargo.

Personality

  • Core: rings the bell himself; carves ships at night; speaks to the drowned by name
  • Quirk: rolls his sleeves up for work
