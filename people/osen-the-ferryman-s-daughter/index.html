Osen the Ferryman's Daughter

The ferryman's daughter, eighteen, who poles the flat ferry across the great river when her father's back is bad, which is most days. She knows the river's moods and every traveller's business. In person: cheeky ferrywoman; hair tied with a red cloth; sun-browned face; rolled sleeves; poling a flat ferry; singing.

Home Ground: River Ferry Crossing: Ferry Landing at River Ferry Crossing, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Bright, quick and cheeky; she sings ferry songs under her breath. Bright, cheeky, singing ferry songs under her breath. Answers with a verse when she does not want to answer. Tell: when the magistrate's list comes up, the singing stops.
Combat Role: the ferry pole, and the river.
Relationship Hook: Keep the magistrate's list from crossing and she ferries you free for life.

Personality

  • Core: teases passengers; reads the river's colour; never lets a samurai touch the pole
  • Quirk: sings ferry songs
