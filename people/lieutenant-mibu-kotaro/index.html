Lieutenant Mibu Kotaro

A lieutenant of the Records and Research division, who looks thirty and has studied the dead for two centuries: courteous, curious and always smiling, with ink-stained gloves and a notebook he never closes. In person: smiling clinical Veil Court lieutenant; neat black hair; spectacles; ink-stained gloves; black officer's coat; a notebook; unsettling.

Home Ground: Records Wing at Black Ferry Fortress, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Soft, delighted and clinical; he talks about people as results. Soft delight and clinical vocabulary; he talks about people as results and pain as data. He hums while he writes and taps his notebook with the brush when pleased. Tell: when exposed he keeps the same gentle delight, and starts bargaining in exactly the same tone.
Combat Role: binding seals and a soul-blade he prefers not to dirty.
Relationship Hook: Bring him a subject or a secret and he trades; become a subject and you learn too much.

Personality

  • Core: never closes his notebook; smiles at screams; thanks his subjects
  • Quirk: calls people results
