Step-Warden Taiga

A breath hunter who guards the Thin Air Steps and counts who makes it up gasping. He lets hunters through when the isles have learned their names and sends the rest down with a word about their lungs. In person: adult man; big-shouldered; cropped black hair; wind-reddened face; a hunter's dark uniform with a patterned haori; a blade; scowling.

Home Ground: The First Landing at The Thin Air Steps, Sun Peak, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Loud in thin air, which takes effort; short barked sentences between breaths and no patience for excuses. Short barked sentences between breaths, loud because thin air demands it, no patience for excuses. Tell: the Boulder named and he changes the subject in the same breath.
Combat Role: a hunter's blade and the first breath form; loud, direct, and short of air.
Relationship Hook: Make it up without stopping and he shares the fire; tell him you failed the Boulder and he tells you how many times he did.

Personality

  • Core: barks between breaths; counts steps aloud for climbers; shares the cook's fire with anyone who made it up
  • Quirk: will not look toward the Boulder
