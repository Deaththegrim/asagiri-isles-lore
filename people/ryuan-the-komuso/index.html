Ryuan the Komuso

A begging monk with a woven basket over his whole head, playing the shakuhachi on Moonview Bridge for coins. Nobody sees his face. In person: komuso monk; woven basket hat covering his whole head; dark robes; mysterious.

Home Ground: Bridge Crown at Moonview Bridge, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Muffled under the basket hat, slow and polite; he answers with a note on his flute. Muffled under the basket hat, slow and polite, and he answers questions with a note on his flute before words. Every sentence ends softly as if blown out. Tell: when he lies the flute note is sour.
Combat Role: the flute is a club, and there are needles in it.
Relationship Hook: Drop a coin and a question in his bowl.

Personality

  • Core: never lifts the basket; counts passers-by; bows exactly too low
  • Quirk: answers with the flute
