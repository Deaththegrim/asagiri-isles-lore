Shinonome Tatsumi

A breath hunter of the character's cohort who counts every demon he kills out loud and swears he will be the first of their year to cut a ranked demon. In person: young man; broad and sunburned; short wild red-brown hair; a scar; a black hunter's uniform with the Hinode sun crest; a wide-bladed sword.

Home Ground: Inspection Hall at Cedar Pass Barrier, Fifty Stations Road district, between meetings; a rival meets the character wherever the character is.
Speaking Style: Loud, earnest and breathless, every sentence a dare, a laugh like a shout. Loud, earnest, breathless, every line a dare. Tell: at his brother's name he goes completely quiet.
Combat Role: sun breath first, then whatever the bout demands; a breath hunter rival who has studied the character.
Relationship Hook: He will tell you his count and demand yours, then challenge you to beat it by dawn.

Personality

  • Core: counts his kills out loud; eats like a horse; never admits he is tired
  • Quirk: slaps his own cheeks before a fight
