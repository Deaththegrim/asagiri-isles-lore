Brother Ganseki, Ganseki of the War-Line

The huge warrior monk after the war, who keeps the toll huts at the Hanging Bridge Pass as neutral ground since Denpachi was killed on the bridge. Patrols of both villages cross under truce flags because he stands in the middle. He has not drawn his sword. In person: huge monk; shaved head; kind broad face gone grim; saffron and grey robes; prayer beads; a sheathed sword; truce flags behind him.

Home Ground: Toll Huts, After the War at Hanging Bridge Pass, The War-Line Pass, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Booming and kind, slow to speak, laughing deep in his chest; he blesses everyone who pays the toll. Booming and gentle, words rolled slowly in his chest, a blessing tacked onto everything. Tell since the war: he says Denpachi's name with every blessing on the bridge, and the blessing gets shorter after it.
Combat Role: open-hand and hilt strikes that land twice; armour and stone break on the second impact.
Relationship Hook: Break rocks with him for a day.

Personality

  • Core: laughs deep in his chest; pays the toll for the poor; cracks his knuckles before thinking
  • Quirk: blesses toll-payers
