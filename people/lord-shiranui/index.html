Lord Shiranui

A beautiful fox lord with silver hair and seven tails, who keeps a court of young foxes in the foxfire marsh and leads travellers in circles until dawn for sport. He took his name from the shiranui, the unknown fire that walks the sea at night. He calls himself the lady's son and the lady does not deny it. In person: beautiful fox lord; long silver hair; golden slit eyes; fox ears; seven silver tails; elegant dark kimono with foxfire patterns; contemptuous smile.

Home Ground: Light Pools at Foxfire Marsh, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Silky, amused and contemptuous; he laughs at humans gently, the way you laugh at a dog. Silky and contemptuous, with gentle laughter at everything mortal. He drawls the ends of words as if they bored him. Tell: when his mother is mentioned the drawl vanishes and his voice sharpens like a blade drawn.
Combat Role: circles of fox-fire and illusion; he never stands where he seems to.
Relationship Hook: He offers power over the wood; the price is the seals.

Personality

  • Core: laughs at humans gently; calls his mother 'the old woman'; collects human names
  • Quirk: leads travellers in circles
