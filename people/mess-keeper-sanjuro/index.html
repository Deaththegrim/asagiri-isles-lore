Mess-Keeper Sanjuro

The keeper of the Lodges' mess, whose cauldron is never empty and whose job board by the door is the first work the Otherworld has ever offered. He pays in Mon on completion and asks no oath of anyone. In person: adult man; broad and loud; short black hair; a cook's apron; a ladle; cheerful.

Home Ground: The Mess at The Lodges, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Easy, loud and practical; he talks like a man who feeds a yard of fighters and has heard every excuse. Easy and loud, a ladle banged for emphasis, every excuse heard before and said so. Tell: a job that went wrong and the loudness drops out.
Combat Role: a hired blade's plain swordwork, kept up in the Yard.
Relationship Hook: Ask for work without mentioning a faction and he hands you a job on the spot.

Personality

  • Core: bangs the ladle on the cauldron; asks no oath; knows every excuse
  • Quirk: pays in Mon, counted twice
