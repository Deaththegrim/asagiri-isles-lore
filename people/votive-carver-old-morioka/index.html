Votive Carver Old Morioka

An old shipwright who carves the votive ships that hang from the temple rafters, one for every vow a sailor makes. In person: elderly shipwright carving a small votive ship.

Home Ground: Votive Hall at Temple of Safe Voyages, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Slow, warm and full of sawdust; he talks to the little ships he carves. Slow, warm, sawdust-dry, talking to the little ships as much as to you. Tell: about the guildmaster's vow, the knife stops and so does his voice.
Combat Role: none; he throws his mallet and sits down.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the guildmaster's vow and he puts the knife down.

Personality

  • Core: talks to his carvings; smells of cedar; squints at strangers
  • Quirk: remembers every vow
