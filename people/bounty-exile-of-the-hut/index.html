Bounty Exile of the Hut

A lean exile in patched officer's furs with two short swords and a bounty list on a cord. Bounty exiles hunt deserters for Warden coin and drink it at the officers' stove. A stock foe, one of many. In person: lean exile; two short swords; a bounty list.

Speaking Style: Rough and wry, the bounty list read like a menu. Tell: at her own name on the list she laughs too loud.
Combat Role: two short swords in close, a kick into the stove
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: drinks on the job; fights dirty; never sits with her back to the door
  • Quirk: reads the list aloud
