Ine of the Hundred Fields

The masked leader of the Straw Masks, the peasant rising of the plain: a tall woman in a woven straw mask who fights with a flail and plans like a general. Villages hide her, the Shogunate has a pardon on her name, and the young fighters of the camp would follow her into the capital's granaries tomorrow. In person: tall peasant rebel leader; woven straw mask; strong shoulders; patched farmer's clothes with a sash of rope; a flail; a bow.

Home Ground: Masked Fire Ring at Straw Mask Hill Camp, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Low, plain and steady, a farmer's words with a soldier's timing; she never says more than the fire needs. Even and low, a voice for a fire ring, never pitched to carry further than it needs. Uses farm proverbs as orders. Tell: under stress a rider's clipped noble vowel slips through, and she corrects it.
Combat Role: flail and terrain, the villages' knowledge of every dike and ditch, and a bow from the saddle when she must.
Relationship Hook: Bring the camp food, weapons that do not come from the Red Lanterns, or the name of the traitor, and she listens.

Personality

  • Core: never takes off the mask; eats last; listens to the youngest fighters first
  • Quirk: rides better than any farmer should
