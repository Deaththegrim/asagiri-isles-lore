Tenma of Two Faces

A half-yokai who lives as both and neither, the same age as the character, who thinks the character is hiding half of themselves and means to drag it out. In person: young man; slender; white hair with a single black streak; one gold eye and one brown; fox-like ears half hidden; an elegant red-and-black haori; a folding fan; bare feet.

Home Ground: Lantern Boat at Lakeside Pleasure Boats, Seimei Bureau district, between meetings; a rival meets the character wherever the character is.
Speaking Style: Charming and slippery, a fox's laugh, two registers that swap mid-sentence. Charming, slippery, two registers that swap mid-sentence. Tell: serious, he snaps his fan shut and speaks in one voice.
Combat Role: fox face first, then whatever the bout demands; a half-yokai rival who has studied the character.
Relationship Hook: He will tell you which half of you is winning, and offer to prove it.

Personality

  • Core: teases until it stings; never lies, never tells the whole truth; switches faces mid-sentence
  • Quirk: snaps his fan shut when he is serious
