Grey Mouth

The sorcerer called Grey Mouth, the most feared of the Grudge Well sorcerers: a gaunt man in grey with ash-coloured lips who went down the deepest well a month ago and has not come back up. The other sorcerers will not draw from that well now. At night something answers from it in his voice. In person: gaunt cursed sorcerer; grey skin cracking like ash; colourless eyes; grey lips; tattered grey robes with iron ladles and bottles; ash fog; menacing.

Home Ground: The Deepest Well at Grudge Well Sanctum, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: A whisper that comes from the bottom of a well, patient and hungry, with other voices murmuring under it. A whisper from deep down, other voices murmuring under it, words that echo slightly late. Tell: when the massacred names are spoken, the murmuring voices fall silent and he hesitates.
Combat Role: curses first, the Blight's dead second, and the well's whole hatred when cornered.
Relationship Hook: Go down the well after him and he talks to you, kindly, about the throne that owes the dead.

Personality

  • Core: speaks with other voices under his own; never blinks; smiles at fear
  • Quirk: names the dead he has swallowed
