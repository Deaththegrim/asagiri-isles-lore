Rider Tomoe, Rider of the Cut Road

A post rider after the war, twenty-four, who still carries the road's mail on her grey mare Fog, now through a barricade, two sets of posts and a lantern hill. Rider Kanbei's route is hers now. In person: post rider; short hair; bright eyes; Warden's blue coat patched at the shoulder; two dispatch bags.

Home Ground: Rider Yard, After the War at Road Commissioner's Office, The Office of a Cut Road, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Brisk, bright and breathless, talking like a rider giving a report. Breathless reports, sentences clipped by the gallop, every message in order: who, where, how many. Clicks her tongue to an imagined horse. Tell since the war: she glances over her shoulder when she reports, as if someone behind her might correct the count.
Combat Role: a short bow at a gallop, then away.
Relationship Hook: Ride the dangerous stretch with her at dusk and she shows you what she saw.

Personality

  • Core: reports in a breathless rush; talks to her horse; keeps a dead rider's whistle
  • Quirk: never rides between two particular stations at dusk
