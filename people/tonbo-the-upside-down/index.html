Tonbo the Upside-Down

A lanky ronin who hangs around the Hiring Hall upside down on a beam more often than standing, and takes jobs for food. He fights like nobody taught him, because nobody did. In person: adult man; lanky; messy black hair tied up badly; sleeveless kimono open; bandaged forearms; a battered katana; hanging upside down from a beam; grinning.

Home Ground: Contract Boards at Ronin Hiring Hall, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Loose, cheerful and never still, humming between sentences and laughing at his own jokes before he finishes them. Loose and sing-song, a hum between sentences, laughing before the punchline lands. Tell: a formal stance in front of him and the humming stops.
Combat Role: acrobatic, improvised, kicks and cuts from every angle, upside down half the time.
Relationship Hook: Make him laugh in a fight; he teaches the ones who do.

Personality

  • Core: hangs upside down to think; laughs before the punchline; eats whatever is offered
  • Quirk: hums between sentences
