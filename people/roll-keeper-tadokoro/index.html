Roll-Keeper Tadokoro

A dry, careful archivist of the Council Hall who keeps the rolls of retainers of every house, living and dissolved. In person: dry middle-aged archivist; brush and seal.

Home Ground: Council Hall at Miyako Castle, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Dry and exact, quoting rules by number. Tell: at House Ikoma's name he lowers his voice.
Combat Role: none; he calls the guard.
Relationship Hook: Ask about House Ikoma's roll and he shows you the scraped-out name.

Personality

  • Core: never guesses; loves old ink; afraid of the regent's office
  • Quirk: dry and careful
