Yukiha

A curse-bearer of Haibara, twenty-two, with a grey curse mark climbing her left arm and the side of her neck. She lives in a room at the Vow Temple, works the night stalls breaking small curses for coppers, and pushes everyone away for their own safety: anyone who stays near her long starts to grey. In person: cursed woman; long pale hair; sad calm grey eyes; a grey curse mark spreading up her left arm and neck; long-sleeved dark kimono; guarded.

Home Ground: Vow Temple at Haibara, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Low, polite and cool; she warns people off in the gentlest voice possible, every time. Gentle, cool warnings, always the same polite phrasing to keep people away. Tell: when someone stays anyway, her voice shakes.
Combat Role: she lets the mark loose on an enemy and pays for it in pain; a short blade otherwise.
Relationship Hook: She warns you off; stay anyway, carefully, and she starts to warn you less.
Courtship Style: Warns you off, then lets you sit closer each night; the courtship is the distance closing a hand's width at a time.
Jealousy Style: Withdraws and wishes the rival well; then asks, very quietly, whether you are greying.

Personality

  • Core: wears long sleeves in summer; keeps a list of the two she lost; sits at the edge of every room
  • Quirk: warns everyone off politely
