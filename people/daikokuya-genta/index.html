Daikokuya Genta

The guild master's son and young master of the foreign quay, twenty-eight, sleek, clever and impatient, who wears a foreign coat over his kimono and runs the quay's warehouses at a profit his father calls reckless. In person: sleek merchant heir; slicked black hair; clever narrow eyes; foreign coat; a pocket watch; smug.

Home Ground: Loading Yard at Foreign Quay Warehouses, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Easy, modern and dismissive; he uses foreign words to sound clever and is. Easy and dismissive, foreign words for effect, a little bored. Taps a ledger with a brush. Tell: when his father is mentioned, the boredom cracks and he sounds young and angry.
Combat Role: a foreign pistol he has fired twice, and a lot of porters.
Relationship Hook: Help him hide the ledger and he makes you rich; give it back to his father and he never forgives you.

Personality

  • Core: uses foreign words; despises the old council; never carries money
  • Quirk: checks his foreign pocket watch
