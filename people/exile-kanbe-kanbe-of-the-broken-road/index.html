Exile Kanbe, Kanbe of the Broken Road

An exile after the war, fifty-two, who ropes travellers one at a time across the fissure that broke the glacier road halfway to the Vaults. In person: weathered exile; frost in his beard; heavy furs; a spear; a lantern; a rope across a crack behind him.

Home Ground: Marker Line, After the War at Glacier Road, The Broken Road, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Hoarse and steady; he talks like someone conserving warmth, a few words per breath. He blows on his hands between sentences. Tell since the war: he looks down into the fissure before every sentence, as if checking the frozen hand is still there.
Combat Role: a spear and the ice itself.
Relationship Hook: Help him find the patrol and keep his secret.

Personality

  • Core: checks every marker; never hurries on ice; hums old marching songs
  • Quirk: conserves words
