Officer Tsubame

A Veil Court officer who has come to Hyoga, the first in a century, sitting in the exiles' hall in her black coat and asking about deserters. In person: unreadable Veil officer; short black hair; cold eyes; black officer's coat with a white sash; taking notes.

Home Ground: Exiles' Hall at Shirokawa, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Flat, formal and unreadable; she asks questions like a ledger. Flat, formal and unreadable; she asks questions like entries in a ledger, one at a time. She waits a full beat after every answer. Tell: when an answer is true she writes it down; when it is a lie she doesn't bother.
Combat Role: a soul-blade, precise.
Relationship Hook: Convince her the exiles are not her enemy and she carries their proof south.

Personality

  • Core: keeps her coat buttoned; never eats with exiles; takes notes
  • Quirk: asks like a ledger
