Night-Road Gambler Ronin, Gambler at the Barricade

A ronin who plays dice with the Straw Mask army in the honjin inn they took for a headquarters, and keeps the peace for them, badly, for a mat and rice. A stock foe, one of many. In person: ronin; a stained grey haori with a straw mask hung at the belt; dice in one hand; a bandaged sword hand.

Speaking Style: Slurred and dangerous. Slurred, dry and dangerous, words lazy until the last one lands sharp. He chuckles at his own bad luck. Tell since the war: he rolls the dice one-handed and covers the bandaged hand when a captain looks.
Combat Role: a quick draw out of a losing roll
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: drinks before a duel; insults your sword; pays debts in blood
  • Quirk: rolls dice one-handed
