Shiba Tadaoki

A disgraced samurai, thirty-five, who has joined a highwayman's band in the pines on market days. He robs politely, quotes poetry, and never takes a traveller's last coin. In person: disgraced samurai highwayman; unkempt topknot; stubble; tired eyes; faded formal kimono with the crest cut off; katana; bitter smile.

Home Ground: Shaded Verge at Pine Avenue, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Dry, bitter and educated; he quotes poetry while he robs you. Educated, bitter diction with a classical line of poetry dropped into every robbery. Sighs before he draws. Tell: when he speaks of the farmer he refused to kill, the bitterness goes and he sounds ashamed.
Combat Role: the draw-cut, clean and sad.
Relationship Hook: Give him a reason to put the sword to better use and he follows you.

Personality

  • Core: never takes the last coin; keeps his crest hidden; bows to his victims
  • Quirk: quotes poetry while robbing
