Weeping Cedar Jorogumo, Spider of Many Nails

A beautiful woman in a web-pale kimono who plays her biwa under a cedar nailed with dolls from every army, fat on a war's worth of curses and weeping sap with it. A stock foe, one of many. In person: jorogumo; beautiful woman; a sap-stained web kimono; a biwa with a broken string; a spider shadow larger than the tree.

Speaking Style: Sweet, slow, hungry. Sweet, slow and hungry, each word drawn out like silk, a plucked biwa note under the pauses. Tell since the war: she plays a war song now, and smiles at the soldiers who know it.
Combat Role: a lure, silk, then the bite
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: never steps into sun; counts her guests; feeds on every nail
  • Quirk: plays to strangers
