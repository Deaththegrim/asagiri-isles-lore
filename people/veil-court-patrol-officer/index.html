Veil Court Patrol Officer

An officer of the Veil Court in a black coat with a soul-blade, pale and calm. Patrol officers cut down the hungry dead and question the living who see too much. A stock foe, one of many. In person: Veil Court officer; pale; black officer's coat; a soul-blade.

Speaking Style: Formal, distant. Formal and distant, Court phrasing, every sentence an entry in a case file. Patrol officers state your status before your name. Tell: when you are alive they sound faintly surprised.
Combat Role: a soul-blade, exact, on the dead first
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: sees the dead; asks for names; never sweats
  • Quirk: speaks formally
