Kirisame Shigure, The Matriarch Deciding

Matriarch of the Kirisame rain clan after the war, seventy-two, tiny and straight-backed still, who sold poison to every side and has outlived another navy. The clan's elders are deciding whether to leave the marsh for good. In person: tiny matriarch; white hair; cold half-closed eyes; dark grey kimono; a grey-marked child at her knee.

Home Ground: Poison House, After the War at Kirisame Stilt Village, Village of the Grey Mark, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Soft, slow and utterly without warmth; she speaks of death like weather. Slow and soft, death spoken of like rain, no warmth at all. Taps her fingernail on a poison jar. Tell since the war: her fingernail stops tapping the poison jar when a marked child coughs.
Combat Role: the fight was over three days ago.
Relationship Hook: Bring her something the curse wells cannot give and she listens; threaten her and you have three days.

Personality

  • Core: speaks of death like weather; brews tea she does not drink; names every child born marked
  • Quirk: never leaves the Poison House
