The Grey Pilgrim, The Pilgrim Among Many

A pilgrim in grey robes and a deep straw hat on Ghost Pine Hill after the war, no longer alone: the war's dead walk the cutting with it, and it asks them all the way to the burned shrine. In person: ghostly pilgrim; grey robes; deep straw hat; plain white mask; a staff with a bell; a line of lanterns behind it.

Home Ground: Hilltop Shrine, After the War at Ghost Pine Hill, The Lantern Hill, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Slow, gentle and lost, a pilgrim's polite questions about a road that changed long ago. Slow, polite, faintly echoing questions, each one ending in a sigh like wind in pines. Repeats the name of a station that does not exist. Tell since the war: it speaks of 'we' now, never 'I', and the lanterns behind it flicker when it does.
Combat Role: none, until it learns it is dead; then the hunger.
Relationship Hook: Walk it gently to its burned shrine and it remembers its name and goes on.

Personality

  • Core: bows to every traveller; never steps off the road; forgets each answer
  • Quirk: asks the way to a burned shrine
