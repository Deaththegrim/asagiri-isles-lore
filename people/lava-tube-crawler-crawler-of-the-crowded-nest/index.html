Lava Tube Crawler, Crawler of the Crowded Nest

A segmented beast of cooling rock driven out of the tubes the house carts through at night, crowded into the nest and angry; it ambushes the carts. A stock foe, one of many. In person: magma crawler; segmented rock beast; a belly glowing furious red; cart-wheel gouges along its back.

Speaking Style: A grinding rumble, stone on stone, words as slow as cooling rock. Tell since the war: it rumbles at the sound of cart wheels on stone.
Combat Role: a molten bite
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: glows; ambushes carts; burns
  • Quirk: burrows
