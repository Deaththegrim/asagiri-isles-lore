Bureau Petition-Clerk Tokiwa, Clerk of the Shut Gate

Petition-clerk at the lake gate after the war, who takes petitions from refugees on the shore through a slot in the gate and posts the ones the Bureau will pay to settle. Most it will not. In person: petition clerk; talisman tags; dark green robes faded; a salt dish; a stack of petitions she has not posted.

Home Ground: Lake Gate Post, After the War at Seimei, Behind Two Walls, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, careful and a little haunted; she lowers her voice for the names of spirits. Quiet and careful, almost a whisper for the names of spirits, a pinch of salt rattling. Tell since the war: she reads a refugee's petition aloud in full before she says no, every time.
Combat Role: a ward-tag thrown and a run for the gate.
Relationship Hook: Settle a petition she renamed and she tells you whose familiar it really was.

Personality

  • Core: lowers her voice for spirits; sorts petitions by colour; thanks hunters formally
  • Quirk: keeps a salt dish on the counter
