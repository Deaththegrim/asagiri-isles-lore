Fishwife Nagase Kayano, Fishwife of the Black Decks

The drifting market's fishwife after the war, who guts fish on the same pier while the decks around her sell rice, arms and salvage. In person: elderly broad fishwife; gutting knife; a black mourning band on her apron; laughing less.

Home Ground: Gutting Pier, After the War at Drifting Fish Market, Black Market Decks, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Loud, salty and fond, a gutting knife's rhythm in her talk; she calls everyone 'eel'. Loud and salty, an eel turning up in her talk now and then, the thunk of a gutting knife keeping time. Tell since the war: she grips the gutting knife harder when she says 'one hand', and then makes a joke of it.
Combat Role: a gutting knife and the whole pier behind her.
Relationship Hook: Help her son out of the smugglers' crew and she feeds you for life.

Personality

  • Core: calls everyone 'eel'; guts while she talks; laughs at the guild
  • Quirk: throws scraps to cats
