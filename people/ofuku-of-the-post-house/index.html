Ofuku of the Post House

Keeper of the Riverside Post House at Miyako's coach yard, fifty, round and loud, who feeds the Warden riders, books coach seats and knows every traveller on the Fifty Stations Road by their luggage. In person: plump innkeeper; hair tied; rosy cheeks; apron; a ladle; warm laugh.

Home Ground: Riverside Post House at Miyako, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Warm, loud and motherly; she gossips like other people breathe. Loud, warm, gossipy, with a habit of answering her own questions. Calls everyone 'dear'. Tell: when she is frightened, the gossip stops dead and she whispers.
Combat Role: a ladle and a very loud voice.
Relationship Hook: Eat her food and listen to her gossip and she tells you what came back to the yard without its rider.

Personality

  • Core: feeds everyone too much; knows everyone by their luggage; scolds Warden riders like sons
  • Quirk: keeps the dispatch book under the rice pot
