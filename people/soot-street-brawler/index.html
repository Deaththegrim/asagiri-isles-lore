Soot Street Brawler

A miner as wide as a door, soot to the elbows, who fights strangers for their boots on payday. Brawlers run the soot street on payday nights and settle every argument with fists. A stock foe, one of many. In person: huge soot-covered miner brawler with bare fists.

Speaking Style: Loud and cheerful about it. Loud, cheerful and coughing, laughing between punches, soot in his throat. Tell: hit hard, he laughs louder and calls you friend.
Combat Role: fists, a headbutt, soot in the eyes
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: laughs at blood; shares the winnings; respects a hard hit
  • Quirk: cracks his knuckles
