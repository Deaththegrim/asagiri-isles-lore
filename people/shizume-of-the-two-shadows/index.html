Shizume of the Two Shadows

A quiet woman in dark travelling clothes who keeps the empty teahouse on the haunted lane, two short swords crossed at the small of her back. In person: adult woman; short dark hair; sharp calm eyes; dark close-fitting travelling clothes; two short swords crossed; watchful.

Home Ground: Empty Teahouse at Haunted Teahouse Lane, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Low and level, few words, every one of them practical; she watches doors while she talks. Low, level and practical, eyes on the door while she talks. Tell: asked who she guarded, she pours a second cup instead of answering.
Combat Role: two short swords, one guarding while the other cuts, then a spinning flurry in tight spaces.
Relationship Hook: Fight through her teahouse in the dark without touching a wall.

Personality

  • Core: sets out a second cup; never sits with her back to a window; speaks only of what is useful
  • Quirk: watches doors
