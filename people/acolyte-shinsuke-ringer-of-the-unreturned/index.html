Acolyte Shinsuke, Ringer of the Unreturned

The temple's acolyte after the war, who rings the bell for every ship that comes home and now counts more that do not than ever. In person: young shaven-headed monk with rope-burned hands; a great bronze bell hung with mourning cloth; sea wind.

Home Ground: Bell Terrace, After the War at Temple of Safe Voyages, Temple of the Unreturned, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Gentle and earnest, a young monk who apologises to the bell before he rings it. Gentle and earnest, a small apology before anything bold, a prayer murmured under the bell. Tell since the war: he says the name of every ship that did not come home under his breath, and loses his place in what he was saying.
Combat Role: a temple staff, swung with apologies.
Relationship Hook: Listen to the bell for a night and you hear it ring a beat late.

Personality

  • Core: apologises to the bell; counts ships; climbs the bell rope barefoot
  • Quirk: prays for sailors by name
