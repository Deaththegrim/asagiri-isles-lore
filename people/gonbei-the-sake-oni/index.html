Gonbei the Sake Oni

An oni who has moved into the largest bear den on the ridge: red-skinned, horned, twice a man's height, and very fond of sake. He has started trading the woodcutters bearskins for it. In person: huge red-skinned horned ogre; wild hair; big grin; tiger-skin loincloth; an iron club.

Home Ground: Sake Store at The Den of Jars, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: A huge rumbling laugh; he talks about sake like poetry. A huge rumbling laugh and sake poetry, with hiccups landing in the middle of lines. He toasts everything, including insults. Tell: when the northern demons are mentioned the laugh stops and he sets his cup down very carefully.
Combat Role: a huge iron club and a laugh.
Relationship Hook: Bring sake and a good song and he fights beside you.

Personality

  • Core: drinks sake by the barrel; names the bears he evicted; sings badly
  • Quirk: laughs at everything
