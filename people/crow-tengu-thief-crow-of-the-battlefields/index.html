Crow Tengu Thief, Crow of the Battlefields

A crow-headed tengu that picked the northern battlefields clean, its wings hung with house crests and war fans. A stock foe, one of many. In person: crow tengu; crow-headed yokai with black wings; a stolen general's war fan; a string of house crests round its neck.

Speaking Style: Cawing laughter and chattering boasts, words chopped short like pecks. It clacks its beak at shiny things. Tell since the war: it caws a house's name each time it touches a crest.
Combat Role: dive from above, grab, gone
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: caws insults; fights in numbers; hoards crests
  • Quirk: steals shiny things
