Crevasse Frost Spirit

A drifting shape of ice crystals and cold light. Frost spirits drift over crevasses and freeze the lost. A stock foe, one of many. In person: frost spirit.

Speaking Style: A chiming hum, ice bells shaped into words. It follows warmth and says so. Tell: when someone is dying of cold it chimes softly, like a lullaby.
Combat Role: a freezing touch
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: freezes; hums; follows warmth
  • Quirk: drifts
