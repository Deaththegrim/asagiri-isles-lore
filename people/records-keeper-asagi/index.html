Records-Keeper Asagi

A Veil Officer of Lieutenant Mibu's records division who keeps the fortress's lists of the dead ferried and of the dead the ferry never received. In person: veil court officer.

Home Ground: Records Wing at Black Ferry Fortress, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Low and precise, an officer who recites the dead's names like a ledger. Low and precise, names recited like ledger entries. Tell: at the never-received list she slows and reads each name twice.
Combat Role: an officer's blade, precise and rare.
Relationship Hook: Tell her what Mibu makes in his wing and she shows you her copy.

Personality

  • Core: recites names; never forgets a soul; distrusts the lieutenant
  • Quirk: inks her fingers black
