Tonbo the Upside-Down, Tonbo of the Unpaid Blades

The lanky ronin after the war, who still hangs upside down from the Hiring Hall's beams and keeps two bands from opposite sides laughing at the same table. He fought in the war for food and never lost to a schooled swordsman. In person: lanky man; messy hair tied up worse; sleeveless kimono; bandaged forearms; a notched katana; hanging from a beam; grinning.

Home Ground: Contract Boards, After the War at Ronin Hiring Hall, The Hall of Unpaid Blades, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Loose, cheerful and never still, humming between sentences and laughing at his own jokes before he finishes them. Loose and sing-song, a hum between sentences, laughing before the punchline lands. Tell since the war: before a fight he hums the same three notes, and stops humming only when the other man is good.
Combat Role: acrobatic, improvised, kicks and cuts from every angle, upside down half the time.
Relationship Hook: Make him laugh in a fight; he teaches the ones who do.

Personality

  • Core: hangs upside down to think; laughs before the punchline; eats whatever is offered
  • Quirk: hums between sentences
