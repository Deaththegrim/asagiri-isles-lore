Student Kunimitsu

A senior Bureau student, nineteen, in a white sash he ties in the Talisman Master's knot, who leads the dormitory's morning recitations. In person: young man; earnest onmyoji student; short black hair; white robes with a knotted white sash.

Home Ground: Sleeping Rows at Bureau Dormitories, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Earnest and fierce, a believer quoting his teacher. Earnest and fierce, creed phrases in his mouth, a student's quick breath. Tell: doubted, he recites louder and his voice cracks on the Master's name.
Combat Role: fire talismans from behind a ward line of students.
Relationship Hook: Argue the creed with him honestly and he listens, for the first time in a year.

Personality

  • Core: ties the white sash in the Master's knot; lends notes to everyone; cannot sleep before a rite
  • Quirk: recites the creed at meals
