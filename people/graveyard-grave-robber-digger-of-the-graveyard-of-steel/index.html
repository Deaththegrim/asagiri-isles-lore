Graveyard Grave-Robber, Digger of the Graveyard of Steel

A disgraced hunter who no longer sells the dead's blades south: she digs them up and carries them to the broken wall, where the living have none. A stock foe, one of many. In person: disgraced demon hunter; a cracked breathing mask; a shovel; a bundle of grave blades on her back; frostbitten fingers.

Speaking Style: Flat, ashamed, hunter's discipline in her clipped breathing. Tell since the war: she touches the hidden crest in her coat when anyone says the Wall.
Combat Role: an old breath form with a dead hunter's blade
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: breathes in forms; carries blades north; never runs twice
  • Quirk: digs at night
