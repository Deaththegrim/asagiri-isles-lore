Grand Chamberlain Ichijo Sanemori

The new Grand Chamberlain, fifty-five, appointed by the Regent the week after Ochikane was cut down at the seal room's door. He keeps the empty palace in perfect order and receives every claimant's envoy with the same bow. In person: middle-aged man; grand chamberlain; thin grey moustache; black court cap; layered formal court robes; a brush; precise.

Home Ground: Hall of Poems, After the War at The Inner Palace, Without an Emperor, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Soft, ceremonious and exact; he speaks of an empty throne as if the emperor were in the next room. Each sentence opens with an honorific and closes with a small bow of the voice. Tell: when the seals come up he answers a question nobody asked, about the gardens.
Combat Role: never; the palace guard stands between him and any blade.
Relationship Hook: Find Ochikane's hidden seals and he will owe you the court; bring them to anyone else and he will owe you a grave.

Personality

  • Core: bows exactly as deep as rank requires; never sits on any cushion near the throne; remembers every slight in court order
  • Quirk: keeps a brush behind his ear
