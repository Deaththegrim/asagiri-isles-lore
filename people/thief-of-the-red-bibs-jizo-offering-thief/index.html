Jizo Offering Thief, Thief of the Red Bibs

A thin youth stealing the coins heaped at the feet of the jizo dressed for the lost sons; he takes the coins and leaves the notes, and cannot read them. A stock foe, one of many. In person: teen; thin offering thief; a stolen red bib knotted as a scarf; a stolen soldier's straw cape.

Speaking Style: Fast, guilty patter. Quick, guilty patter in a cracking teen voice, apologising and lying in the same breath. Tell since the war: he touches the red scarf at his throat whenever someone mentions the war.
Combat Role: stones, then his heels
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: apologises to the statues; throws stones; lies badly
  • Quirk: eats while he runs
