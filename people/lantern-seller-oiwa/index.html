Lantern-Seller Oiwa

A lantern-seller at the Pilgrim Post in Tasogare, fifty, who sells the paper lanterns mourners set drifting over the Sea of Fog and writes each name on them in a neat hand. In person: kind lantern seller; hair tied; ink-stained fingers.

Home Ground: Pilgrim Post at Tasogare, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Warm, chatty and gently sad; she asks every customer who the lantern is for and remembers the answer. She hums while she folds paper. Tell: when she lies she busies herself with the lanterns and talks to them instead of you.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Look at her list of lanterns that turn back.

Personality

  • Core: asks who the lantern is for; writes every name herself; watches the lanterns drift
  • Quirk: keeps a list of the ones that turn back
