Hazuki the Camp Singer

A singer of the Regent's field camp, twenty-four, who sings in the bettors' stands for coins and carries songs, letters and rumours between the camp's tents. Every soldier knows her voice; no one knows where she sleeps. In person: adult woman; twenty-four; camp singer; warm brown eyes; long black hair loose with a red cord; faded patched kimono with a soldier's scarf; a small shamisen; teasing smile.

Home Ground: Bettors' Stands, After the War at Tournament Grounds, The Regent's Camp, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Teasing and light, a song in every other sentence; she makes you ask twice for anything true. Light and teasing, half-sung, the end of a sentence turned into a tune. Tell: when something is true she speaks it flat, without the tune, and then laughs too quickly.
Combat Role: none in the open; a knife and a song to cover the noise.
Relationship Hook: She sings about you before she speaks to you, and the song is never quite kind.
Courtship Style: Teases first and sings about you to the whole camp; the first real gift is a song she stops halfway, because it is true.
Jealousy Style: Teases the rival sweetly in front of you, then sings nothing at all for a night and asks you which one you meant.

Personality

  • Core: sings the last line of every song wrong on purpose; never eats in front of anyone; remembers every soldier's name
  • Quirk: teases hardest when she is afraid
