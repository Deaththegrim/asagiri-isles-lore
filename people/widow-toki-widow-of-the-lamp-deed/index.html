Widow Toki, Widow of the Lamp Deed

The old widow at the harbour post's bait stall after the war, seventy-two, thinner, still straight-backed: the war took the harbour's young men and left her selling bait to soldiers. In person: small old fishwife; grey hair under a blue cloth; a black mourning band on one sleeve; a twist of net in her hands.

Home Ground: Harbour Post, After the War at Saikai, The Debt Harbour, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Slow and practical, an old fishwife's plain words, a dry laugh at anything grand. Slow and practical, a fishwife's plain words with a dry laugh at anything grand, the creak of a net knot between sentences. Tell since the war: she knots the net too tight whenever a sail comes into the harbour, and unpicks it when it is not his.
Combat Role: none; she throws the bait bucket and calls the harbour guard.
Relationship Hook: Sit down at her bait stall and she will tell you about the island nobody wants, and what it would cost to take it.

Personality

  • Core: says the price first and the story after; feeds anyone who sits down; never raises her voice
  • Quirk: knots a twist of net while she talks and unknots it when she is done
