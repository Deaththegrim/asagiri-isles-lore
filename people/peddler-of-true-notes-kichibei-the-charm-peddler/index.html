Kichibei the Charm-Peddler, Peddler of True Notes

The charm peddler after the war, forty-two, who sells fake wards to frightened soldiers and leaves true notes at the Jizo shrine. In person: shifty charm peddler; thin moustache; a pack of paper wards; a deserter's coat.

Home Ground: Statue Row, After the War at Wayside Jizo Shrine, Jizo of the Lost Sons, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Fast, oily patter; every charm is the last one and every price a bargain. Oily rapid patter, blessings and prices tumbling together, a rattle of charms in his hand. Calls everyone 'lucky friend'. Tell since the war: he stops mid-patter whenever anyone in a general's colours passes, and calls them lucky friend too late.
Combat Role: runs, throwing charms.
Relationship Hook: Catch him at the shrine and he tells you which station master, if you keep his name out of it.

Personality

  • Core: swears every charm is real; never stays two nights; pockets coins by habit
  • Quirk: leaves notes instead of speaking
