Spring Snow Monkey Troop

A troop of red-faced snow monkeys around a steaming pool, led by a scarred old male. The troop mobs anyone who comes to the pools with food or a weapon. A stock foe, one of many. In person: troop of red-faced snow monkeys; scarred old male leader.

Speaking Style: Screeching from every side at once, the old male's bark over it. Tell: when he barks twice the troop goes quiet.
Combat Role: a screeching mob from every side
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: steals packs; throws snow; mobs together
  • Quirk: screeches
