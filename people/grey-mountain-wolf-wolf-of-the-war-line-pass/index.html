Grey Mountain Wolf, Wolf of the War-Line Pass

A big grey wolf that follows the patrols crossing the pass under truce flags and takes whoever falls behind. A stock foe, one of many. In person: large grey wolf with frost; a Hayate patrol's torn collar on its neck.

Speaking Style: Growls and snarls. Low growls rolling into snarls, short huffing breaths between. Wolves call to each other with yips. Tell since the war: it falls in behind the last man of any group, every time.
Combat Role: pack circles, then the throat
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: follows the patrols; howls for the pack; flees fire
  • Quirk: circles
