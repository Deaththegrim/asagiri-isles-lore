The Drafted Duellist

A tall lean oni in a duellist's faded white, a register page nailed through his chest-plate, two horns curving back like a drawn bow and a long blade that drips grey water. He waits in the duel yard with his eyes closed. He challenges every sword that enters the yard to a duel to the death and counts each one aloud. He has killed eleven since the war. Anyone who refuses he lets go with contempt; anyone who runs mid-duel he cuts down from behind. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: tall lean oni duellist; a torn register page nailed; two horns curving back like a drawn bow; long blade dripping grey water.

Home Ground: Duel Yard, After the War at Duelists' Teahouse, Teahouse of the Last Duels, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: A clipped formal challenge, then numbers, counted slowly. A duellist's formal challenge, clipped and polite, then a count. Tell: he says ninety-seven before every number, as if the rest did not exist.
Combat Role: the drafted duellist opens his eyes
Relationship Hook: Learn what Shinza lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
