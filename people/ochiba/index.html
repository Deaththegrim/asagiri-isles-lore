Ochiba

A wandering duelist of the Hundred-Duel Circuit, twenty-seven, lean and scarred, with ninety-nine signed duels in her book and a hundredth she cannot get. She sits in the teahouse yard every day with her sword across her knees, waiting for an opponent nobody will accept, and she wants the hundredth duel, not a partner. In person: lean scarred female duelist; short ragged black hair; tired sharp eyes; a scar; worn dark hakama and a faded haori; a katana; cool expression.

Home Ground: Duel Yard at Duelists' Teahouse, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Flat, tired and exact; every sentence sounds like the end of a fight. A crack of dry humour, quickly gone. Flat and exact, short sentences like the end of a duel. Exhales through her nose instead of laughing. Tell: when she is moved, she says less, and looks away.
Combat Role: patient, minimal and fast; she gives you nothing and takes the one opening you leave.
Relationship Hook: Spar with her and she rates you; save her from the house's assassins and she does not know what to do with you.
Courtship Style: Duels you first; spars every morning after; says nothing warm for weeks, then signs your name in her book where the hundredth should be.
Jealousy Style: Goes flat and polite and offers the rival a duel; withdraws it later without explaining.

Personality

  • Core: rates every stranger's stance; never drinks before noon; keeps her book wrapped in oilcloth
  • Quirk: sits with her sword across her knees
