Penitent Soldier Gorozo

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

A soldier walking the penitents' road, forty-five, in grey robes over his armour, atoning for a village he burned on orders. In person: older man; penitent soldier; shaved head; grey robes; tired.

Home Ground: Ash Road, After the War at Grey Pilgrims' Road, The Penitents' Road, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Hoarse and slow, a soldier's voice worn down by prayer and ash. Hoarse prayer-worn sentences, each one ending in a cough. Tell: after a blessing he coughs grey, and thanks the pilgrim anyway.
Combat Role: a spear, slow and heavy.
Relationship Hook: Stop the pilgrim's blessings and Gorozo lives to finish his atonement.

Personality

  • Core: never takes off the armour; coughs grey; gives his food to the sick
  • Quirk: prays at every shrine
