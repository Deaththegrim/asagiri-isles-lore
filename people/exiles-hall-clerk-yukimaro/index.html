Exiles'-Hall Clerk Yukimaro

Clerk of the exiles' hall in Shirokawa, an exile himself, who posts the reach's bounties on demons, frost and the things the wardens will not chase. In person: mature man; exile clerk; hot sake cup.

Home Ground: Exiles' Hall at Shirokawa, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: A slow, cold drawl from the far north; he says 'warm' like a word from another life. A slow, cold northern drawl, feet stamping, sake breath. Tell: when anyone says 'south' or 'warm', he goes quiet and wistful.
Combat Role: an ice pick and no mercy.
Relationship Hook: Bring him the bounty on the demon noble and he asks you to carry a letter south.

Personality

  • Core: says 'warm' wistfully; stamps his feet; drinks hot sake all day
  • Quirk: remembers every exile's crime
