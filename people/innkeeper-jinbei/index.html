Innkeeper Jinbei

Keeper of the Seventh Station Inn, forty-five, who runs the official honjin rooms for lords and the cheaper inns for everyone else, and closes the lantern gate at dusk. In person: nervous plump innkeeper; receding hair; sweaty smile; brown apron; bowing.

Home Ground: Honjin Rooms at Seventh Station Inn, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Hearty, nervous and eager to please; he agrees with everyone and sweats. Hearty, eager, too many apologies and too much agreement. Wipes his hands on his apron audibly. Tell: when he is lying his laugh goes high and his sentences get longer.
Combat Role: none; he hides in the kitchen.
Relationship Hook: Warn the lord's retinue, or stand guard, and he will never forgive you; or he will thank you forever, if the bandits lose.

Personality

  • Core: sweats when lords arrive; polishes the red lantern himself; counts the rooms twice
  • Quirk: agrees with everyone
