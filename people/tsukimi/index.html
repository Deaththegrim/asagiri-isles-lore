Tsukimi

A star-reader of the Bureau, twenty-four, who keeps the instruments of the Old Observatory on the hills south of the city and prefers its dark to the lanterns. Pale, short-sighted and blunt, she reads the death written in your stars and tells you so, the first time you meet. In person: pale short-sighted star-reader; long silver-grey hair; half-lidded dreamy eyes; oversized white onmyoji robes with star embroidery; aloof.

Home Ground: Instrument Deck at The Old Observatory, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, matter-of-fact and slightly too slow, as if she is reading the end of the sentence before she says it. Slightly behind the moment, as if reading ahead; long pauses where others breathe. Counts stars under her breath. Tell: when she has seen someone's death, she stops looking at them and speaks very gently.
Combat Role: none she likes; a ward, a crane, and knowing exactly where the blow will fall.
Relationship Hook: She reads your death at first meeting; change one fate she read and she stops reading yours.
Courtship Style: Tells you your death, then keeps checking whether it is still coming; that checking is the courtship.
Jealousy Style: Reads the rival's stars aloud, flatly, in front of you; then apologises, badly.

Personality

  • Core: squints at faces; forgets to eat; talks to the instruments
  • Quirk: tells people how they die
