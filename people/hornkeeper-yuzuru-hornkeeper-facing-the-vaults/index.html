Hornkeeper Yuzuru, Hornkeeper Facing the Vaults

The hornkeeper of the watch fort after the war, twenty-seven, who stands his watch with his spyglass turned toward the Vaults behind the fort. In person: hornkeeper; furs; a huge horn; a spyglass turned the wrong way; tired.

Home Ground: Horn Tower, After the War at Wardens' Watch Fort, The Fort Facing Both Ways, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Nervous, honest and quick; he is sure of what he saw and says so three ways. He fiddles with the horn's cord. Tell since the war: he no longer blows the horn by accident; he grips its cord and keeps his voice level.
Combat Role: seal-chains, nervously.
Relationship Hook: Go out on the ice and see what he saw.

Personality

  • Core: sleeps in the horn tower; counts the ice; keeps a spyglass
  • Quirk: sure of what he saw
