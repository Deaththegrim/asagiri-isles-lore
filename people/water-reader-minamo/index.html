Water-Reader Minamo

A reader of the black pool who keeps a hut at the Water Sanctum's shore and arranges the pebbles in rings the pool disturbs. She says what the water shows and never what it means. In person: adult female; slight and still; long wet-dark hair loose; grey eyes; a plain pale blue kimono damp; calm.

Home Ground: The Shore at Water Sanctum, The Five Sanctums, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Quiet, even and unhurried, with long pauses she does not fill; she talks the way a pool reflects. Quiet and even, long pauses left unfilled, saying what the water shows and never what it means. Tell: noon mentioned and the pause does not end.
Combat Role: none; she steps into the mist and is not there.
Relationship Hook: Sit with her at the shore for an hour without speaking; she tells you what the pool showed while you sat.

Personality

  • Core: leaves long pauses; rebuilds the pebble rings; never explains what the water means
  • Quirk: will not look at the pool at noon
