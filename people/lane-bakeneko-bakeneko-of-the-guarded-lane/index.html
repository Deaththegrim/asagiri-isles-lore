Lane Bakeneko, Bakeneko of the Guarded Lane

A dog-sized cat on its hind legs, red cloth at its throat, that no longer raises the dead to dance: Shizume's two blades taught the lane manners, and the bakeneko keeps to the teahouse roofs. A stock foe, one of many. In person: bakeneko; large cat yokai with a single long tail; a red cloth at its throat; a notch cut clean from one ear.

Speaking Style: Purring under every word, threats delivered sweetly. It drags out the 'r's. Tell since the war: it flattens its ears at the sound of steel on a whetstone.
Combat Role: claws and the dancing dead
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: watches from the roofs; steals fish; raises no dead
  • Quirk: dances on hind legs
