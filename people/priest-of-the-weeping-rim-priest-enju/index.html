Priest Enju, Priest of the Weeping Rim

The crater rim's priest after the war, sixty-two, chanting over war blades and first blades from dawn to dusk, his white robes grey to the hem. In person: haunted volcano priest; ash-grey robes; a crowded altar; listening.

Home Ground: Rim Altar, After the War at Crater Rim Shrine, The Rim of Many Blades, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Soft and haunted; he stops to listen to something you cannot hear. Soft and haunted; he stops mid-sentence to listen to something you cannot hear, then carries on as if nothing happened. He rings a hand bell to close a thought. Tell since the war: he covers both ears now, not one, and keeps speaking.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Carry his letter off the isle.

Personality

  • Core: blesses every blade; sleeps on the rim; writes letters
  • Quirk: stops to listen
