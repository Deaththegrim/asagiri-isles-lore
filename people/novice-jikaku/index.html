Novice Jikaku

A young temple novice, eighteen, shaven and quick, who copies sutras faster than anyone and listens at the Sutra Hall floor at night. In person: young man; shaven temple novice; bright eager eyes; grey novice robes; a stack of copied sutras.

Home Ground: Sutra Hall at Mountain Temple of the Seven Falls, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Eager and quick, a novice who finishes other people's sutras for them. Eager and quick, finishing other people's sentences, a whisper as if at the Sutra Hall floor. Tell: asked about the beast, he lowers his voice to a reverent hush.
Combat Role: a stave and a novice's fast feet.
Relationship Hook: Ask him what he hears through the floor and he tells the party more than the abbot ever has.

Personality

  • Core: finishes sutras too fast; asks the abbot questions he already knows; sleeps in the Sutra Hall
  • Quirk: presses his ear to the floor
