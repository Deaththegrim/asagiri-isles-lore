Cook Umeno

The cook at the Thin Air Steps' first landing, who keeps a small blue fire going and a row of bowls full for anyone who makes it up gasping. She has kept one bowl warm for three days. In person: sturdy; grey hair tied; wind-chapped cheeks; a padded jacket; a ladle; warm.

Home Ground: The First Landing at The Thin Air Steps, Sun Peak, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Warm, brisk and motherly in thin air; she feeds first and asks later. Warm and brisk, feeding first and asking later, a mother's voice in thin air. Tell: the warm bowl mentioned and her briskness goes soft.
Combat Role: a hunter's first breath form, rusty, and a very hot ladle.
Relationship Hook: Eat what she gives you and ask whose bowl the warm one is; she tells you, and then asks if you would go and look.

Personality

  • Core: feeds first, asks later; talks to the fire; knows every hunter's name who passed
  • Quirk: keeps a bowl warm for the missing
