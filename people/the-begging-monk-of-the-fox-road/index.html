The Begging Monk of the Fox Road

A cheerful young begging monk walking the road of fox shrines, blessing travellers and asking for rice. His shadow sometimes has a tail. In person: cheerful begging monk with a fox-tailed shadow; shaved head; straw hat; begging bowl; mischievous.

Home Ground: Fox Statues at Kitsune Shrine Road, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Pious, merry and slightly wrong; he laughs at his own blessings. Pious, merry and slightly wrong; his blessings rhyme when they shouldn't, and he laughs at them. His bowl rattles with something that isn't coins. Tell: when called by a fox's name he laughs a beat too late.
Combat Role: illusions and running.
Relationship Hook: Give him rice and he tells you who broke the statue.

Personality

  • Core: eats only rice; bows to every statue; his shadow has a tail
  • Quirk: laughs at his blessings
