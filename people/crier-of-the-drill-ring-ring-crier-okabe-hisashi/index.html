Ring-Crier Okabe Hisashi, Crier of the Drill Ring

The tournament's crier after the war, who now calls drill in the Regent's camp and announces the odd bout in the sand ring when the soldiers need one. In person: adult crier with a hoarse voice; a fan with a torn rib; a soldier's coat over his crier's colours.

Home Ground: The Sand Ring, After the War at Tournament Grounds, The Regent's Camp, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: A booming crier's voice that can fill the stands, every name announced like a legend. A booming crier's voice even at a whisper, names announced like legends. Tell since the war: his booming voice cracks on the names of companies that did not come back from Sekigane.
Combat Role: none; he roars for the ring guards.
Relationship Hook: Ask him which bout is fixed and he names one, loudly, by accident.

Personality

  • Core: booms names; makes legends of nobodies; winks at the bookmaker
  • Quirk: drinks honey for his throat
