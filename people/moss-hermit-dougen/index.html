Moss Hermit Dougen

A hermit grown over with moss who keeps a hut at the parting of the five stairs and knows which stair will suffer whom. He lets through those of the Elements and any shinobi, when the isles have learned their names. In person: moss growing; bald sun-browned head; kind squinting eyes; a weathered monk's robe; a staff; seated before a moss-roofed hut where five stairs part; calm.

Home Ground: The Parting at The Sanctum Steps, The Five Sanctums, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Slow, warm and a little deaf, or pretending; he asks your Element before your name and points with his chin. Slow and warm, a little deaf or pretending, the Element asked before the name; he points with his chin and says 'that one'. Tell: the void stair mentioned and his warmth goes out of his voice.
Combat Role: a staff and the patience of moss; he turns strikes aside and waits.
Relationship Hook: Mend the first step with him one dawn and he tells you what each stair did to him.

Personality

  • Core: points with his chin; asks the Element first; mends the stairs' first step each dawn
  • Quirk: sits so still moss grows on him
