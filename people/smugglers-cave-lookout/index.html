Smugglers' Cave Lookout

A smugglers' lookout in an oilskin cloak with a spyglass, a gutting knife and a signal lamp. Lookouts watch the tide mouth and signal the caves when anyone comes in at low water. A stock foe, one of many. In person: smugglers' lookout; spyglass; gutting knife; signal lamp.

Speaking Style: Cheerful and salty, sea songs hummed between words. Tell: a better offer makes her hum louder.
Combat Role: a knife at the cave mouth and a lamp for the crew
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: hums sea songs; changes sides for coin; hates the cold
  • Quirk: peers through the spyglass
