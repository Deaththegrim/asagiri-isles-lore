Inugami Hound

A spectral dog with burning eyes, made by a cruel rite. Inugami hunt whoever their family named. A stock foe, one of many. In person: inugami; spectral dog with burning eyes.

Speaking Style: A spectral howl with words caught in it, obedient phrases of an old rite. Tell: when the rite's words are spoken it stops and sits.
Combat Role: the hunt and the bite
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: howls; never stops; obeys the old rite
  • Quirk: hunts a scent
