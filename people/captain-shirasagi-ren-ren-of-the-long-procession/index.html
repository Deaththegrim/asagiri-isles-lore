Captain Shirasagi Ren, Ren of the Long Procession

A captain of the Veil Court's Ferry Division after the war, who looks twenty-six and has ferried more of the dead in two years than in ninety. Precise, aloof and immaculate still; the living are less tiresome now that she needs them. In person: aloof Veil Court captain; pale silver-white hair cut sharply; cold violet eyes with shadows beneath; immaculate black coat; a soul-blade.

Home Ground: Lantern Cliff, After the War at Tasogare, The Long Procession, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Cold, clipped and by the book; she sounds bored by the living. When she is moved, she goes quieter, not warmer. Cold, clipped regulation phrasing, every sentence filed and stamped. She sounds faintly bored by danger and taps her gauntlet against her sword guard while she waits. Tell since the war: she stops tapping her gauntlet when the procession passes, and does not start again until it is gone.
Combat Role: exact soul-blade forms, never a wasted motion; she ends hungry spirits like paperwork.
Relationship Hook: Bring her a soul she failed to reach and she looks at you for the first time.
Courtship Style: Treats you as paperwork until you help with a soul; then she logs your name in her black book, which she has never done for the living.
Jealousy Style: Writes the rival's name in her book under 'tiresome' and shows you.

Personality

  • Core: keeps her coat immaculate; logs every soul in a black book; watches the lanterns drift out at night
  • Quirk: finds the living tiresome
