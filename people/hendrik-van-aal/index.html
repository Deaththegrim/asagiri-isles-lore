Hendrik van Aal

A foreign sailor, thirty, stranded in the Foreigners' Quarter when his ship sailed without him. Tall, red-bearded and homesick, he trades sailor's knots and foreign songs for meals and asks every captain for passage home. In person: tall foreign sailor; red beard; sunburnt face; blue eyes; striped shirt; homesick grin.

Home Ground: Quarter Gate at Foreigners' Quarter, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Heavy accent, careful borrowed words, sudden laughter; he swears in his own tongue. Heavy foreign accent, borrowed words chosen carefully, sudden loud laughter. Swears in his own tongue when surprised. Tell: when he is homesick his sentences trail off into his own language.
Combat Role: a sailor's brawl, fists and a belaying pin.
Relationship Hook: Get him passage home and he hands over the Vault map.

Personality

  • Core: swears in his own tongue; sings when drunk; asks every captain for passage
  • Quirk: keeps the map sewn into his coat
