Otama the Bookmaker

The Tournament's leading bookmaker, thirty-five, who runs the betting in the stands from a lacquered box with a tally board and four runners. Merchants, farmers and lords all lay money with her, and she always seems to know a champion's odds before the champion does. In person: sly bookmaker woman; black hair pinned with a writing brush; clever eyes; bright patterned kimono; a tally board and abacus; playful smirk.

Home Ground: Bettors' Stands at Tournament Grounds, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Bright, rapid patter and a merchant's charm; she quotes odds like poetry. Rapid patter that turns odds into little rhymes, never drawing breath until the price is named. Clicks her abacus as she talks. Tell: when she actually knows something, she slows down and lowers her price.
Combat Role: none; her runners scatter and she bribes whoever is left.
Relationship Hook: Bring her a champion's secret and she pays in coin or in odds; fight in the ring and she sells you the field.

Personality

  • Core: quotes odds for everything; tips runners in rice cakes; keeps a second tally in her sleeve
  • Quirk: never bets on her own book
