Hotarubi of the Foxfire Mask, Lantern of the Lost Soldiers

A half-blood in a cracked fox-fire mask, twenty-two, who leads deserters into the marsh with blue flames and leaves them circling; his lord's cause went to war and he went to the marsh. In person: young man; half-yokai; fox ears; a white fox-fire mask cracked through one eye; blue flames; dark robes torn at the hem.

Home Ground: Circle Path, After the War at Foxfire Marsh, The Marsh of Lost Soldiers, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Soft and mocking; he laughs like foxfire flickers. Soft and mocking behind the mask, a flicker of laughter like a flame. Tell since the war: he no longer laughs behind the mask; he goes silent where the laugh would be.
Combat Role: foxfire in the reeds, then the mask's blank face up close.
Relationship Hook: Pull the mask off and he stops laughing for the first time.

Personality

  • Core: never takes the mask off; lights blue fire to talk; doubts Lord Shiranui
  • Quirk: laughs behind the mask
