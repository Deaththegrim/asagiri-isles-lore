Mother Tome, The Mother Who Found Him

A miner's widow after the war, fifty-seven, who stopped asking samurai about her son: she found him, walking in the Revenant Shaft with a lantern. In person: soot-stained widow; grey hair loose; fierce dry eyes; patched clothes; a lantern of her own.

Home Ground: Soot Street, After the War at Miners' Town, The Hollowed Town, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Raw, stubborn and loud; she will not stop asking. Raw, stubborn and loud, a mountain mother who will not stop asking. She repeats her son's name like a demand. Tell since the war: she says her son's name and then 'I found him', as if daring you to argue.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Find her son, or what became of him.

Personality

  • Core: never stops asking; keeps her son's helmet; spits at the castle
  • Quirk: stops every samurai
