Kagero the Sheathed

A thin, polite swordsman in a faded travelling coat who sits in the teahouse's signing room with a sheathed sword across his knees. Nobody on the road has seen him draw it and remembered it. In person: adult man; thin; long dark hair tied low; faded travelling coat; a sheathed katana; calm.

Home Ground: Signing Room at Duelists' Teahouse, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Quiet and courteous, every word chosen; he never raises his voice and leaves long pauses nobody dares fill. Soft, courteous, every sentence followed by a pause nobody dares fill. Tell: anger in front of him and his voice drops below a whisper.
Combat Role: stillness, then a single invisible draw-cut and a second strike from the scabbard.
Relationship Hook: Sit with him through a night without moving.

Personality

  • Core: never takes his hand off the scabbard; pours tea for strangers; bows before he answers
  • Quirk: leaves long pauses
