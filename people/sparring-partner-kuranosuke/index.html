Sparring Partner Kuranosuke

A ronin who sparse with anyone in the Yard for the price of a meal and the chance to talk. He has fought every tradition's students and can imitate each one badly. In person: adult man; lean and grinning; messy topknot; a sleeveless gi with a wooden blade; cocky.

Home Ground: Sparring Circles at The Yard, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Cheerful, cocky and generous with advice nobody asked for; he loses well and wins worse. Cheerful and cocky, advice nobody asked for between exchanges, losses taken well and wins taken worse. Tell: the terraces mentioned and the cockiness goes flat.
Combat Role: a ronin's plain swordwork with borrowed flourishes from every student he has fought.
Relationship Hook: Spar with him and lose on purpose once; he notices, and tells you which master would take you.

Personality

  • Core: gives unasked advice; imitates every tradition badly; eats while sparring
  • Quirk: wins worse than he loses
