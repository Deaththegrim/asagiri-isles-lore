Buyer Shinpei, The Buyer Who Breaks Them

A swordsman soaking in the inn's spring after the war, thirty-two, among house agents and foreign captains bidding openly for soul-forged blades. He outbids them all. In person: cool swordsman; wet hair; a faint smile gone thin; a wrapped sword; a broken blade in a box beside him.

Home Ground: Steam Bath, After the War at Hot Spring Inn, The Buyers' Inn, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Easy, cool and amused; he talks to the steam. Easy, cool and amused, talking half to you and half to the steam rising from the bath. He hums a gambler's tune. Tell since the war: his gambler's tune stops when he outbids someone, and he sounds sorry about it.
Combat Role: a fine blade and a buyer's patience.
Relationship Hook: Tell him there is another buyer and he panics.

Personality

  • Core: soaks for hours; watches the other buyer; never names his lord
  • Quirk: talks to the steam
