Hayatomo the Crow-Taught, The Crow in the Drill Ring

A lean swordsman, twenty-four, who drills the Regent's levies in the old Sand Ring for rice and hates every hour of it; the open bouts are gone and his borrowed name sits on a muster roll now. In person: young man; lean swordsman in a drill-master's sash; messy black hair cropped short; a black feather braided into it; a healed cut across the bridge of the nose; straw sandals at last.

Home Ground: The Sand Ring, After the War at Tournament Grounds, The Regent's Camp, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Bright and cocky, a grin in every line. Cocky and bright, quick jokes, a crow's caw of a laugh. Tell since the war: he glances up at every crow that lands nearby and looks away before it can look back.
Combat Role: leaps and cuts from above, never a straight line.
Relationship Hook: Cross blades with him and he grins and names the peak before he names himself.

Personality

  • Core: laughs when cut; bows to crows; never gives his real name
  • Quirk: fights barefoot
