Breach Wrecker

A raider in salt-stiff leathers with a boarding axe and a coil of grapnel rope, climbing in through the fort's breach. Wreckers strip the old fort of iron and bronze at low tide and gut anyone who finds them at it. A stock foe, one of many. In person: sea raider; climbing.

Speaking Style: Short orders over the surf. Short orders pitched over the surf, every word a count or a command, a two-note whistle for the crew. Tell: near the guns' old house crest she drops to a mutter.
Combat Role: up the breach on a grapnel, then the axe
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: weighs metal by hand; whistles to the crew; cuts rope rather than lose it
  • Quirk: climbs before she walks
