Ghost Pine Nue

A monkey-faced, tiger-limbed, snake-tailed chimera that cries like a thrush in the pine cutting at night. Nue bring sickness and bad dreams to whoever hears their cry and fight from the dark. A stock foe, one of many. In person: nue; monkey-faced chimera with tiger limbs and a snake tail.

Speaking Style: A thrush's eerie cry. An eerie thrush's cry between words. Tell: at the sound of a bowstring it goes silent.
Combat Role: the cry first, then claws from the dark
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: hides in the dark; brings bad dreams; hates arrows
  • Quirk: cries like a bird
