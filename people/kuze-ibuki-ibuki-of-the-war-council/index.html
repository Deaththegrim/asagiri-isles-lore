Kuze Ibuki, Ibuki of the War Council

Head of the exiles' council after the war, who looks forty-two and has been an exile for a century, running a war council around the stove every night: help the Wardens hold the Vaults, or leave the isle. He listens more than he speaks. In person: weathered deserter officer; grey-streaked black hair; calm cold eyes; patched black coat; a council arguing around him.

Home Ground: Council Stove, After the War at Deserter Officers' Hut, The War Council Hut, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Short, quiet and warm under the frost; he weighs every word against the cold before he spends it. He breathes into his hands between sentences. Tell since the war: he uses a given name twice now, to the four hosts, and never to anyone else.
Combat Role: an officer's soul-blade, rusty and sure.
Relationship Hook: Help hide the shore host from the Warden hunters and the council trusts you.

Personality

  • Core: weighs every word; never wears his officer's sash; remembers every exile's crime
  • Quirk: keeps the stove fed
