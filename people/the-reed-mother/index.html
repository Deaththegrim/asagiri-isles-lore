The Reed Mother

A hunched oni-woman with long wet hair and reed-green skin, horns like broken reeds, wading the channels at night and singing a lullaby that breaks off halfway. She searches the channels every night. Anyone who wades the reeds after dark she seizes and turns to the lamplight to look at their face, and those who struggle she holds under. By day she sleeps in the mud. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: hunched oni-woman with long dripping black hair and reed-green skin; broken-reed horns; tattered kimono; wading; mournful.

Home Ground: Channel Maze at Reed Channels, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: A hoarse broken lullaby between every few words. A hoarse lullaby that breaks off halfway, words slipping between its lines. Tell: a child's voice anywhere near and she stops singing to listen.
Combat Role: the reed mother's lullaby turns into a scream
Relationship Hook: Learn what Ayu lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
