Kiyo of the Refuge, Kiyo of the Cracked Ward

The fox-blooded guardian of the Refuge after the war, twenty-six, who has stopped pretending newcomers are welcome: the Refuge is overfull, and a ward stone has cracked. In person: fox-blooded woman; long red-gold hair; fox ears; a red-gold tail; sharp tired amber eyes; red and white clothes with a spear now.

Home Ground: Ward Ring, After the War at Two-Blooded Refuge, The Overfull Refuge, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Light, mocking and quick; she talks through an illusion's smile and drops it when she is sure of you. Light mockery through an illusion's smile; her voice seems to come from slightly to the left of her. She teases first and decides later. Tell since the war: she speaks from where she stands more often now, and the mockery comes after, not before.
Combat Role: illusion first, fox-fire second, claws and a short blade if you are still there.
Relationship Hook: She tests you with a trick; see through it and stay kind.
Courtship Style: Tricks you, then tricks you kindlier; one day she drops the illusion and shows her real face, which is the whole confession.
Jealousy Style: Turns into the rival, perfectly, and asks you which one you prefer.

Personality

  • Core: hides her tail from humans; brings the children fox-fire to play with; never eats human food first
  • Quirk: tests visitors with illusions
