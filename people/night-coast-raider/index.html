Night-Coast Raider

A pirate in a sea-worn coat with a boarding axe and a cutlass, gold rings in her ears. Sea raiders strike the coast at night and are gone by dawn. A stock foe, one of many. In person: pirate raider; sea-worn coat; gold earrings; a boarding axe.

Speaking Style: Laughing, dangerous. Laughing and dangerous, sea shanty rhythms in their threats. Raiders cheer before boarding. Tell: when outnumbered they laugh louder.
Combat Role: boarding axe and a crew, gone by dawn
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: laughs in the fight; never stays past dawn; takes rings
  • Quirk: boards first
