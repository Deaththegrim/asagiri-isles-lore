Kurogane Akane

The lord's elder daughter, twenty-two, a Kurogane warrior in full black iron who duels anyone who calls her 'lady' and has not lost in four years. She is proud, armoured, loud and very good. In person: proud armoured samurai woman; long black hair; fierce red eyes; full black lacquered iron armour; a long sword; challenging grin.

Home Ground: Iron Gate at Kurogane Iron Castle, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Loud, proud and challenging; every greeting is a dare. When she means something gentle she gets louder, not softer. Loud, proud and challenging; every greeting is a dare and every compliment a provocation. She laughs like a sword clearing its sheath. Tell: when she means something gentle she gets louder, not softer.
Combat Role: an iron wall that advances; she never steps back and never stops.
Relationship Hook: Beat her once, fairly, and do not boast.
Courtship Style: Duels you every time you meet; the day she lets you see her without the gauntlets is the confession.
Jealousy Style: Challenges the rival to a duel on the spot, loudly; wins; sulks.

Personality

  • Core: never takes off her gauntlets in public; eats with the guard; keeps count of duels won
  • Quirk: duels anyone who calls her 'lady'
