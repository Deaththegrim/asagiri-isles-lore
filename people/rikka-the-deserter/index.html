Rikka the Deserter

A young woman hiding in a fisher's loft in Hanging Lantern Village, who keeps her hands wrapped and never stands near the lanterns. In person: short dark hair; wary eyes; plain fisher's clothes; wrapped hands.

Home Ground: Cliff Path at Hanging Lantern Village, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Hushed, careful and polite; she listens for footsteps between sentences. Hushed and polite, sentences trimmed short so she can listen for footsteps between them. She says 'sorry' when she is not, out of habit. Tell: when the Records Wing is named her breathing quickens and she starts edging toward a door.
Combat Role: a soul-blade she does not want to draw.
Relationship Hook: Get her evidence to someone who will use it and she fights at your side.

Personality

  • Core: keeps her hands wrapped; avoids lanterns; repays every meal with work
  • Quirk: listens for footsteps
