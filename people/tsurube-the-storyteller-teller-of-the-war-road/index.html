Tsurube the Storyteller, Teller of the War Road

The travelling storyteller after the war, fifty-two, whose hundred tales are now two hundred: the war gave him ghosts enough for a lifetime. He tells them in inn baths still. In person: storyteller; bald with a grey beard; expressive face; patched haori; a folding fan with a burned edge.

Home Ground: Common Bath, After the War at Seventh Station Inn, The Last Inn East, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: A storyteller's rich, rolling voice, every sentence a cliffhanger. A rolling performer's voice, dramatic pauses, and a slap of the fan at every turn of a tale. Lowers to a whisper for the frightening parts. Tell since the war: he tells war stories without the fan, and his voice stays level all the way through.
Combat Role: none; he tells a story until the danger gets bored.
Relationship Hook: Buy him a drink and he tells you how to survive the night road.

Personality

  • Core: ends every sentence as a cliffhanger; snaps his fan for effect; never tells the same story twice in a town
  • Quirk: goes pale at night lanterns
