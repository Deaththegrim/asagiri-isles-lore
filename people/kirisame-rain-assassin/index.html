Kirisame Rain Assassin

A rain-clan assassin in oiled reed-grey wraps with a blowpipe and a poisoner's ring. Rain assassins wait in the reeds for a boat that comes too close and leave no wound bigger than a thorn. A stock foe, one of many. In person: a bamboo blowpipe; a poison ring; hidden.

Speaking Style: Soft and wet, like rain on a roof. Soft as rain on a roof, every word dropped not spoken. Tell: before she strikes she says the target's name, gently.
Combat Role: a thorn from the reeds, then gone underwater
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: smells of wet reeds; counts heartbeats; bows to her target
  • Quirk: never speaks first
