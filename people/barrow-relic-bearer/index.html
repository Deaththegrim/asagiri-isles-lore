Barrow Relic-Bearer

A grave-robber owned by the cursed sword he took from the barrow, its hilt grown into his hand. Relic-bearers fight for the relic and protect it from anyone who would take it. A stock foe, one of many. In person: grave robber holding a cursed sword grown.

Speaking Style: Two voices at once: a robber begging and a general commanding. Tell: when the sword wins, only the general speaks.
Combat Role: the sword's old form through a robber's arms
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: cannot let go; begs between blows; fights like an old general
  • Quirk: speaks for the sword
