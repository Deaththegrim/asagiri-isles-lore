Mess-Hall Keeper Tetsuji

Keeper of the hunters' mess at Asahi Gate, a one-armed veteran who posts the hold's bounties on demons, thralls and the beasts that follow them. In person: mature man; one-armed veteran demon hunter mess-keeper; scarred face; fur-trimmed coat; tapping a bounty board.

Home Ground: Hunters' Mess at Asahi Gate, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: A quartermaster's bark that has shouted over storms; gentler with the young. A storm-trained bark, gentler for the young, the stump of his arm tapping the board. Tell: at the word 'night hunter' his bark drops to a growl.
Combat Role: a hunter's blade in his one hand, still fast.
Relationship Hook: End the night hunter on the pass and he gives you everything he saved.

Personality

  • Core: barks orders; taps the board with his stump; never sits with his back to the door
  • Quirk: feeds the young first
