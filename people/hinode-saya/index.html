Hinode Saya

A Blossom Breath hunter, twenty-three, orphaned by a raid and raised in the Hold, the best of her year: graceful, solemn and all duty. She refuses partnership, friendship or anything else with anyone who might die on her, which in the Hold is everyone. In person: solemn breath hunter; long black hair tied with a pink ribbon; sad steady eyes; dark hunter's uniform with a blossom-pattern haori; a sun-steel sword.

Home Ground: Breathing Yards at Hinode Fortress, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, clipped and polite; grief keeps her voice level. When she laughs, it surprises her too. Quiet and clipped, a politeness worn thin by grief. She answers with as few words as the question allows. Tell: when she laughs it surprises her, and she stops herself as if caught.
Combat Role: Blossom Breath, flowing and exact; she fights alone if she can.
Relationship Hook: Survive a Demon Night at her side.
Courtship Style: Refuses you, then trains beside you, then waits at the gate after every hunt pretending not to.
Jealousy Style: Turns colder and more courteous; adds nothing to her list and watches you twice as hard.

Personality

  • Core: refuses partners; trains until dawn; visits the graveyard every morning
  • Quirk: keeps a list of the dead
