Oyuki the Bath Attendant

A bath attendant at the Bayside Bathhouse, twenty-five, who brings hot water, towels and sake to the merchants, sailors and ronin soaking in the great bath. Everyone likes her; nobody remembers what they said in front of her. In person: bath attendant; hair up with a towel; round sweet face; mischievous eyes; light cotton yukata with sleeves tied back; carrying a sake tray; sly smile.

Home Ground: Great Bath at Bayside Bathhouse, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Soft, sweet and always half-laughing; now and then she turns your last words back into a question. Soft and sing-song, half-laughing, echoing your last words back as a question. Hums bath-house songs. Tell: when she is passing on something dangerous she stops humming and her voice gets very matter-of-fact.
Combat Role: none; she screams and the bathhouse bouncers come.
Relationship Hook: Pay better than her three buyers and she sells you the councillor's words.

Personality

  • Core: hums while she works; never forgets a voice; counts her coins in three purses
  • Quirk: turns your words back into a question now and then
