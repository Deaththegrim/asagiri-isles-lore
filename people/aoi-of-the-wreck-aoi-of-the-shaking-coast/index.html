Aoi of the Wreck, Aoi of the Shaking Coast

The lone survivor of the southern wreck after the war, twenty-two, living in Wreck Cove beside a broken Warden ship. The cliffs shake at night where the fissure meets the sea. In person: lone survivor; wild dark hair; pale steady eyes that glow at night; Warden furs taken from the wreck.

Home Ground: Wreck Cove, After the War at Black Stone Coast, The Shaking Coast, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, careful and flat; she tells the truth because lies take too much warmth to tell. She speaks through chattering teeth. Tell since the war: her teeth stop chattering when she is angry now, and the air around her warms.
Combat Role: none, until the beast wakes.
Relationship Hook: Find her before the Warden hunters do.

Personality

  • Core: speaks flatly; feeds the sea eagles; carves her name in the wreck
  • Quirk: never sleeps
