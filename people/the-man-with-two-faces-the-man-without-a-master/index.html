The Man with Two Faces, The Man Without a Master

A mourner at the Pilgrims' Hostel after the war, ordinary and forgettable, who lights a lantern every night for a different face he has worn. His master died in the war. In person: forgettable man; plain face; mourning clothes; a paper lantern; a theatre mask.

Home Ground: Lantern Hall, After the War at Pilgrims' Hostel, Hostel of the Living Lanterns, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Pleasant, forgettable and smooth; he sounds like whoever he last spoke to, accent and all. He mirrors your phrases back. Tell since the war: his own voice comes through more often now, and stays for a whole sentence.
Combat Role: a hidden blade and someone else's face.
Relationship Hook: Notice the lanterns' names and you have found him.

Personality

  • Core: sounds like the last person he spoke to; never eats in company; touches his face
  • Quirk: lights a lantern each night
