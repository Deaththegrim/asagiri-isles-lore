Lantern-Seller Momiji

A cat-eared lantern-seller on the Lantern Walk who sells paper lanterns for a favour apiece and never for coin. Every lantern she sells holds the favour that bought it. In person: young adult female; cat ears and slit green eyes; short russet hair; a maple-red kimono with lantern patterns; a pole of hanging paper lanterns; cheerful.

Home Ground: The Promenade at The Lantern Walk, The Hidden Court, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Bright, chatty and always mid-sale; she purrs on long vowels without noticing. Bright and mid-sale, long vowels purred without noticing, weighing kindness as favour owed. Tell: the dark lantern mentioned and the purr stops.
Combat Role: claws if cornered; she would rather owe you a favour.
Relationship Hook: Buy a lantern with an honest favour and she tells you whose debt hangs next to yours.

Personality

  • Core: purrs on long vowels; sells for favours only; knows every lantern's debt
  • Quirk: pretends the dark lantern is decoration
