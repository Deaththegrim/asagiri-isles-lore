Tsukimi, Star-Reader in the Cellar

A Bureau star-reader after the war, twenty-six, kept in the Old Observatory's cellar for trying to send the warning north. The instruments above her are trained on Hyoga day and night, and she reads the stars through a crack in the cellar roof. In person: pale short-sighted star-reader; long silver-grey hair unbound; half-lidded eyes; oversized white robes; a star chart scratched on the cellar wall.

Home Ground: Instrument Deck, After the War at The Old Observatory, Watch of the Cracked Seals, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, matter-of-fact and slightly too slow, as if she is reading the end of the sentence before she says it. Slightly behind the moment, as if reading ahead; long pauses where others breathe. Counts stars under her breath. Tell since the war: she counts the days aloud, softly, at the end of a conversation.
Combat Role: none she likes; a ward, a crane, and knowing exactly where the blow will fall.
Relationship Hook: She reads your death at first meeting; change one fate she read and she stops reading yours.
Courtship Style: Tells you your death, then keeps checking whether it is still coming; that checking is the courtship.
Jealousy Style: Reads the rival's stars aloud, flatly, in front of you; then apologises, badly.

Personality

  • Core: squints at faces; forgets to eat; talks to the instruments
  • Quirk: tells people how they die
