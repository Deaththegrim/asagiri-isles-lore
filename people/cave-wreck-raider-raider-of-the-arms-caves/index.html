Cave Wreck-Raider, Raider of the Arms Caves

A salt-scarred raider in a sharkskin vest guarding the Brine Tide's stacks of arms in the caves instead of looting wrecks. A stock foe, one of many. In person: scarred sea raider; sharkskin vest under a captured lacquered breastplate; a boarding axe; a wet rope coil.

Speaking Style: Hoarse and amused. Hoarse, amused, salt in every syllable, laughing through her teeth. She whistles signals between lines. Tell since the war: she taps the stolen breastplate and names the army it came from.
Combat Role: snare and axe in the surf
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: grins with gold teeth; guards arms; never fights uphill
  • Quirk: tests the tide
