Palace Straw Doll, Doll of the Storm

A straw doll nailed to a lightning-split pine in the storm-drowned garden, walking on its nail between strikes. Since the emperor's death the dolls hunt any courtier who comes to grieve. A stock foe, one of many. In person: straw doll curse; rain-soaked straw effigy scorched by lightning; two iron nails.

Speaking Style: A dry rustle that sounds like a name. A dry rustle that sounds almost like a family name, repeated, scratching like straw on wood. Tell since the war: it rustles in time with the thunder, and goes still in the silences between.
Combat Role: the nail, from behind
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: rustles; carries its nail; burns badly
  • Quirk: moves only unseen
