Nun Seigan

A wandering exorcist nun, forty, who walks the coast's graveyards laying the restless dead to rest with sutras and patience. In person: calm wandering nun; shaved head; grey robes; prayer beads.

Home Ground: Grave Rows at Moorland Graves, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Calm, warm and unshockable; she speaks to the dead and the living alike, in the same tone. She counts prayer beads under her words. Tell: when someone confesses she stops counting and listens.
Combat Role: a staff and a sutra; she prefers to talk.
Relationship Hook: Lay a wisp to rest with her.

Personality

  • Core: speaks to the dead; unshockable; eats little
  • Quirk: gives too much
