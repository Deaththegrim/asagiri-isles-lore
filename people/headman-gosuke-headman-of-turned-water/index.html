Headman Gosuke, Headman of Turned Water

Headman of Sunken Well Village after the war, fifty-two, rich and hated: every well has turned, the village drinks rainwater, and he sells the turned water to every army's assassins as poison. In person: bald headman; sweaty brow; a fine new coat; an ash mask; a son at his side who does not speak.

Home Ground: Well Square, After the War at Sunken Well Village, The Turned Village, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Cheerful, hoarse and shifty; he talks fast through his ash mask. Hoarse, muffled through an ash mask, fast and shifty. Coughs to buy time. Tell since the war: his cough comes before every lie and now before every mention of his son, and he cannot tell them apart.
Combat Role: none; the villagers' buckets.
Relationship Hook: Cleanse the turned well and he never charges you for water again.

Personality

  • Core: talks fast; never takes off his mask; tastes every barrel
  • Quirk: hides the turned well's bucket
