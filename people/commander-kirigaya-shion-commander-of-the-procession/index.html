Commander Kirigaya Shion, Commander of the Procession

Commander of the Veil Court's post at Tasogare after the war, centuries old in a body that looks forty-five now, standing watch at the great pale torii while the war's dead queue faster than her officers can ferry them. In person: weary Veil Court commander; long dark hair with more white; calm, exhausted eyes; black coat with a white sash; a soul-blade.

Home Ground: The Great Pale Torii, After the War at Tasogare, The Long Procession, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Low, measured and weary; an officer's calm stretched thin, with a dry humour that surprises people. Low, measured and weary, a commander's cadence that never wastes a breath. Dry humour arrives without warning and leaves without a smile. Tell since the war: her single sigh before the law has become two, and the second is for herself.
Combat Role: an officer's soul-blade and Ferry rites; she cuts the hungry dead in one stroke.
Relationship Hook: Help her ferry the backlog and she trains you as one of her living recruits.

Personality

  • Core: stands at the torii at dawn; drinks bitter tea; never says 'impossible'
  • Quirk: trains recruits herself
