The Mother on the Kitamura Well

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

A huge ash-grey oni-woman squatting on the lid of the village well, her farmer's apron burned to rags, horns like charred branches, her arms wrapped around the well as if it were a child. She will not leave the well. Anyone who comes near it she drives back; demons she tears apart and throws into the ash. She hums a children's counting song and stops it whenever someone speaks. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: huge ash-grey oni-woman squatting; burned farmer's apron; charred branch horns; arms wrapped around the well; protective.

Home Ground: Old Well, After the War at Burned Village of Kitamura, The First of Many, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: A mother's hum broken by growls, a counting song that stops at seven. A mother's hum under a growl, a counting song that stops at seven. Tell: when a girl's voice is heard anywhere near, she stops growling altogether.
Combat Role: the mother on the kitamura well stands up off the lid for the first time
Relationship Hook: Learn what Tsuta lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
