Boss Ganzo, Boss of the Swallowed Camp

Boss of the loggers' camp after the war, fifty-two, broad and impatient still, cutting at the edge of a wood that grew back over his road. A handful of men stayed. In person: broad logger boss; beard gone grey; rolled sleeves; a notched axe; saplings shoulder-high behind him.

Home Ground: Saw Line, After the War at Loggers' Camp, The Swallowed Camp, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Loud, impatient and greedy; he talks about deadlines. Loud and impatient, talking in deadlines when work is on, his fist on the table for emphasis. He calls workers by their crew numbers. Tell since the war: he counts saplings aloud the way he once counted crews, and swears at each one.
Combat Role: an axe and thirty loggers.
Relationship Hook: Tell him what the fox gift means before he insults the Court by ignoring it.

Personality

  • Core: swears at trees; counts logs; keeps the fox gift unopened
  • Quirk: talks about deadlines
