Quay Strongarm, Bonded-Quay Guard

A thick-armed dock tough in guild colours now, guarding the bonded warehouses where arms bought for one side were never collected. A stock foe, one of many. In person: burly dock tough; guild-colour tabard; a cargo hook on a chain; a new spear he does not know how to hold.

Speaking Style: Low, unhurried threats. Slow gravel, every threat in three words or fewer, a grunt for a full stop. He cracks his neck between sentences. Tell since the war: he keeps glancing at the black warehouse's door between sentences.
Combat Role: hook and chain at reach, then a shoulder into the water
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: swings the hook idly; counts heads; works for whoever pays tonight
  • Quirk: leans on crates
