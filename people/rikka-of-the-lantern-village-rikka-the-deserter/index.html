Rikka the Deserter, Rikka of the Lantern Village

The deserter officer after the war, hiding in a fisher's loft still, who ferries the village's own dead herself now that the Court is too busy to collect them. The village would hide her with their lives. In person: young woman; short dark hair; wary eyes; plain fisher's clothes; unwrapped hands, glowing faintly.

Home Ground: Cliff Path, After the War at Hanging Lantern Village, Village of Lanterns for Sons, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Hushed, careful and polite; she listens for footsteps between sentences. Hushed and polite, sentences trimmed short so she can listen for footsteps between them. She says 'sorry' when she is not, out of habit. Tell since the war: she says the village's dead by their names as she ferries them, never 'fare'.
Combat Role: a soul-blade she does not want to draw.
Relationship Hook: Get her evidence to someone who will use it and she fights at your side.

Personality

  • Core: keeps her hands wrapped; avoids lanterns; repays every meal with work
  • Quirk: listens for footsteps
