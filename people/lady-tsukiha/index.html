Lady Tsukiha

The nine-tailed lady of the Fox Court, who has watched three dynasties rise and fall from a vermilion pavilion lit by cold foxfire. She receives humans with good manners and a gift, trades in favours and promises, and never forgets either. She appears as a tall woman in white and gold with nine pale tails that are not always there. In person: nine-tailed fox lady; tall and regal; long white hair with gold ornaments; golden eyes; fox ears; nine pale tails; layered white and gold court robes; seated; knowing smile.

Home Ground: The Lady's Pavilion at Halls of the Nine-Tailed Lady, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Amused, unhurried and very old; every sentence is a bargain stated as a courtesy. Amused and unhurried, every bargain dressed as a courtesy, every threat as a kindness. She laughs behind her sleeve and lets you wonder at what. Tell: when her son is mentioned the amusement thins to something tired and protective.
Combat Role: nobody who fights her finds the right room; fox-fire and illusion, then nine tails of cold fire.
Relationship Hook: Bring a gift and good manners and she offers a bargain; take it and you owe her.

Personality

  • Core: states bargains as courtesies; never forgets a promise; laughs behind her sleeve
  • Quirk: counts favours owed in tails
