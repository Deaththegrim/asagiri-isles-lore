Mizuchi Nami

The admiral's daughter, twenty-six, a hard, brilliant captain who has sworn to find out which of her father's captains sold the sleeping warship. She is inspecting the Lighthouse of the Dragon's Eye. In person: fierce naval captain; short dark hair; sharp eyes; sea-blue armour coat with a river-dragon crest; a spear.

Home Ground: Signal Platform at Lighthouse of the Dragon's Eye, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Sharp, fast and furious; she speaks in charges and verdicts. Fast, sharp, verdicts and charges, no softening words. Tell: when her father's spies are mentioned, she goes silent with fury.
Combat Role: spear and grapple, then her marines.
Relationship Hook: Help her catch a bought captain and she trusts you; show her what her father does and she breaks.

Personality

  • Core: speaks in verdicts; never sits; keeps a list of the bought
  • Quirk: inspects everything twice
