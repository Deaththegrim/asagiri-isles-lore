Captain Arakawa Jinnai

The Regent's new captain at the thrown-down Cedar Pass barrier, forty, sent to rebuild it with twenty men and no money. Captain Nagai died when it fell; his lieutenant fled with the treasury. In person: samurai captain; grim weathered face; plain dark armour; a spear; rebuilding a broken mountain barrier gate.

Home Ground: Pass Gate, After the War at Cedar Pass Barrier, Thrown-Down Barrier, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Flat and sparing, a man who answers in as few words as the question allows. As few words as the question allows, then silence. Tell: at the word treasury he says one word more than he needs to.
Combat Role: a spear behind the half-built gate.
Relationship Hook: Help him take back the treasury from the fort and the pass is open to you for good.

Personality

  • Core: counts his men morning and night; rebuilds with his own hands; drinks alone
  • Quirk: keeps Nagai's marker clean
