Ferryman Gin, The Ferryman Who Stopped Counting

An off-duty ferryman after the war, who looks fifty-five, asleep at a tea-room table between crossings with his coat still on. In person: exhausted Veil Court ferryman; grey stubble; black coat; an untouched cup of tea.

Home Ground: Ferrymen's Rest: Tea Room, After the War at Ferrymen's Rest, Rest of the Tired Ferrymen, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Tired and dry, but after the third cup he counts aloud. Tired and dry, short sentences with a sip of sake between each one. He calls passengers 'fare' instead of their names. Tell since the war: he no longer counts after the third cup; he says 'three thousand' and stops there.
Combat Role: a ferryman's blade, patient and heavy.
Relationship Hook: Buy him the third cup and he tells you where the dead go missing.

Personality

  • Core: never sits facing the fog; knows every ferryman by name; tips badly
  • Quirk: counts aloud after the third cup
