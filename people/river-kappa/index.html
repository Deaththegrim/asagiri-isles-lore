River Kappa

A green, beaked, shell-backed river imp with a water-filled dish on its head. Kappa wrestle travellers who insult the river and drown those who break a bargain. A stock foe, one of many. In person: small green beaked river yokai with a turtle shell and head dish.

Speaking Style: Dry and wet at once, a polite croak with a click in the throat, every bargain spoken like a rule of etiquette. It slurps between sentences. Tell: offered a deep bow, it bows back before it can stop itself.
Combat Role: grapple and drown; bow to it
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: wrestles; bows when bowed to; remembers bargains
  • Quirk: demands cucumbers
