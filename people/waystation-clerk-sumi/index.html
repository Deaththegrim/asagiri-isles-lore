Waystation Clerk Sumi

Clerk of the grey banner waystation in Haibara, forty, who posts the curse-hunters' bounties on the blight's horrors and keeps a wall of names of hunters who did not come back. In person: mature woman; tired waystation clerk with a hunter's charm; grey robes; before a wall of names and a bounty board; ash.

Home Ground: Grey Banner Waystation at Haibara, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Flat, tired, kind underneath; she says 'come back alive' to everyone and means it. Flat, tired and low; 'come back alive' as she hands a bounty over, once, never as filler. Tell: when her husband's killer is named, she stops breathing and speaks very fast.
Combat Role: a curse-hunter's short blade she never puts down.
Relationship Hook: End the horror that killed her husband and she gives you his blade.

Personality

  • Core: says 'come back alive'; touches the wall of names; never smiles
  • Quirk: keeps a hunter's charm on her wrist
