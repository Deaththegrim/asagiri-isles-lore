Rakudai of the Ward Stones

A gaunt oni in a Bureau student's robe gone grey with age, ink-black horns, his fingers stained to the wrist. He crouches by the ward stones scratching characters into them and then scratching them out. He hates onmyoji. He cracks a ward stone a little more each night, letting spirits slip toward Seimei, and any onmyoji who comes near he attacks; others he quizzes with old exam questions and lets go if they answer. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: gaunt oni; ink-black horns; ink-stained hands; spirits drifting.

Home Ground: Cracked Stone at Old Ward Stones, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: A precise scholar's voice that turns every sentence into an exam question. Precise and scholarly, every sentence turned into an exam question. Tell: a wrong answer makes his voice shake with old fury.
Combat Role: rakudai tears open the cracked ward stone
Relationship Hook: Learn what Senkichi lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
