Lord Kurogane Tadakatsu, The Iron Victor

Lord of House Kurogane after the war, sixty-two, a mountain in black iron, holding the richest seat in the isles. Warship banners fly beside his crest. In person: massive samurai lord; grey beard; stern heavy face; seated under rows of empty harness; warship banners.

Home Ground: Lord's Hall, After the War at Kurogane Iron Castle, The Iron Victor, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Heavy, slow and final; he speaks as his armour moves, one plate at a time, and never repeats himself. A hinge-creak of iron sits between his clauses. Tell since the war: his armour creaks between clauses, and goes still whenever his son's slate scrapes.
Combat Role: advance, never retreat; a long blade behind an iron wall.
Relationship Hook: Prove yourself in a test of endurance and he hires you; ask about the deep forges and his son finds you first.

Personality

  • Core: speaks one plate at a time; inspects every blade himself; never goes below the castle
  • Quirk: never steps back
