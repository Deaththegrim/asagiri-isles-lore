Director Tachibana Seiran

Tachibana Seiran, Director of the Seimei Bureau, seventy, tall and thin in white robes, who has run the college for thirty years from a garden of raked sand and star-charts. He licenses every onmyoji in the isles and keeps the great wards on the capital, and he teaches every first-year class himself. In person: tall thin onmyoji director; long white hair; calm wise eyes; layered white and black robes with a yin-yang crest; a tall black hat; serene.

Home Ground: Director's Garden at Bureau of Yin and Yang, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Slow, gentle and precise; he teaches in questions and never answers his own. Every sentence a question that leads you to the answer he wanted. Pours tea while he talks. Tell: when he is hiding something he asks more questions, faster.
Combat Role: wards that close a room and familiars that fill it; he never needs to move.
Relationship Hook: Bring him a question he cannot answer and he takes you as a student; bring him Sorei and he owes you.

Personality

  • Core: teaches in questions; rakes the sand garden himself; never says a thing is impossible
  • Quirk: reads the stars before every decision
