Master Sorei

The mask-maker of a back-alley workshop in Seimei, whose theatre masks are the finest in the isles. Customers meet an old woman at the shop front and are mostly turned away. In the carving room she is a woman of twenty-nine with ink on her hands, a fox's patience and very little trust in anyone who wants a face. In person: mask-maker woman; long black hair tied back loosely; sharp tired amber eyes; ink-stained fingers; plain indigo work kimono with sleeves tied back; fox-fire glow; wary.

Home Ground: Carving Room at Mask-Maker's Workshop, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Low, dry and unhurried; she answers a question with the price of answering it. When she is honest her voice drops and slows. Ink-and-patience cadence, pauses to blow dust off a carving, every price stated softly. A fox's soft laugh in the back of her throat. Tell: when she tells the truth, she stops carving and looks at you.
Combat Role: a glamour first, a ward second, a carving knife last; she fights only when cornered and then without mercy.
Relationship Hook: Ask her for nothing, keep the Talisman Master off her, and the wall comes down a mask at a time.
Courtship Style: Wary, dry and mercenary to start; she prices every kindness until one day she stops charging you.
Jealousy Style: Gives the rival a mask, as a joke, of your face; takes it back that night and apologises in her own voice.

Personality

  • Core: turns most customers away; answers questions with prices; carves when she cannot sleep
  • Quirk: talks to the fox in her chisel
