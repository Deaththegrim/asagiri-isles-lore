Ward-Stone Guardian

A lion-dog of grey stone the size of an ox, cracked down one flank, that wakes when the ring is crossed. It guards the old ward ring against anyone, the Bureau's own included, since the ward broke. A stock foe, one of many. In person: stone guardian; huge cracked grey stone lion-dog komainu standing.

Speaking Style: A grinding roar with no breath behind it, stone on stone, then dead silence. Tell: before a true ward it lowers to a deep rumble and is still.
Combat Role: jaws and weight, never leaving the ring
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: circles the ring; never leaves the stones; bows to a true ward
  • Quirk: roars without breath
