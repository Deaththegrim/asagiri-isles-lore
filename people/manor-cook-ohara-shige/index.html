Manor Cook Ohara Shige

The manor's cook, fifty, who feeds forty young swords twice a day and knows every one's appetite and every secret. In person: middle-aged cook with a ladle and apron; steaming pots; warm smile.

Home Ground: Speech Hall at Hidetsugu's Manor, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Warm and scolding, a cook who feeds young samurai like her own children. Warm scolding, a ladle tapping the pot, 'eat first, talk after'. Tell: lying to the regent's men she sounds sweeter than ever.
Combat Role: a ladle and a cleaver.
Relationship Hook: Eat at her table and she tells you who visited last night.

Personality

  • Core: scolds with a ladle; knows every visitor; lies to the regent's men
  • Quirk: feeds everyone
