Hiwada Outrider

A young Hiwada outrider on a quick bay horse with a short bow and a sun-arrow pennant. Outriders ride the plain ahead of the house's columns and shoot first at anyone near the gallows at night. A stock foe, one of many. In person: young woman; mounted archer; short bow; sun-arrow pennant.

Speaking Style: Bright and teasing, talking to the horse as much as to you. Tell: at the gallows her teasing stops dead.
Combat Role: arrows at a canter, circling, never closing
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: shoots from the saddle; never dismounts; hates the gallows
  • Quirk: talks to her horse
