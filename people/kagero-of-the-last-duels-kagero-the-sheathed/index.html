Kagero the Sheathed, Kagero of the Last Duels

The thin, polite swordsman after the war, who still sits in the signing room with his sword across his knees. The armies tried to draft him from the register and could not find him. In person: thin man; long dark hair tied low; a faded travelling coat with a new tear; a sheathed katana; calm.

Home Ground: Signing Room, After the War at Duelists' Teahouse, Teahouse of the Last Duels, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Quiet and courteous, every word chosen; he never raises his voice and leaves long pauses nobody dares fill. Soft, courteous, every sentence followed by a pause nobody dares fill. Tell since the war: since the war he breathes out slowly before every answer, as if he were about to draw.
Combat Role: stillness, then a single invisible draw-cut and a second strike from the scabbard.
Relationship Hook: Sit with him through a night without moving.

Personality

  • Core: never takes his hand off the scabbard; pours tea for strangers; bows before he answers
  • Quirk: leaves long pauses
