Road Guard of the Wardens, Guard of a Thrown-Down Barrier

A road guard in a faded Warden's blue coat, standing where the toll barrier burned, checking papers for a commissioner who cannot reach this station any more. A stock foe, one of many. In person: post-road guard; faded Warden's blue coat; a spear and a soot-stained ledger; a straw mask armband forced on her.

Speaking Style: Bored orders, sharp when ignored. Bored orders recited for the hundredth time, sharp when ignored. She drums her fingers on the papers. Tell since the war: she still asks for papers, and apologises for asking.
Combat Role: spear behind a barrier, a whistle for the riders
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: checks papers nobody needs; counts nothing; looks away for coin
  • Quirk: checks papers slowly
