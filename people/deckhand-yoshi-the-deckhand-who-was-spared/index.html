Deckhand Yoshi, The Deckhand Who Was Spared

A Brine Tide deckhand after the war, twenty-one, who survived the night his crew was to be drowned and never knew how close it came. He has stopped telling the story about the girl in the crate. In person: young sailor; messy hair; a new scar; a navy salvage coat; quieter.

Home Ground: Tap Room, After the War at The Drowned Lantern, Lantern of the New Masters, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Loud, cheerful and young; he tells the story of the crate to anyone. Loud, cheerful, retells the crate story with new details each time. Tell since the war: he counts his crewmates on his fingers when he talks, and the count is shorter each time.
Combat Role: a knife and a lot of enthusiasm.
Relationship Hook: Get his crew out of Ashiura before the drowning and he follows you anywhere.

Personality

  • Core: drinks too fast; buys rounds he cannot afford; means well
  • Quirk: tells the crate story to anyone
