Kitamura Ash Gaki

Hungry ghosts of the burned villagers, thin-necked and ash-grey, crawling out of the cold hearths on Ash Street. Kitamura's gaki beg every visitor for the meal the raid took from them and claw when refused. A stock foe, one of many. In person: gaki hungry ghost; emaciated ash-grey spirit with a thin neck and swollen belly; wrapped; crawling from cold hearths.

Speaking Style: A hollow begging moan, the village's old names in it. Tell: at a funeral prayer the moaning stops.
Combat Role: a swarm of ash-grey grasping hands
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: crawls from hearths; claws; weeps for home
  • Quirk: begs
