Red Lantern Horse-Bandit

A bandit on a stolen warhorse with a red lantern tied to the saddle and a hunting bow. Horse-bandits run down travellers on the open road and drive them to the roost for ransom. A stock foe, one of many. In person: bandit; hunting bow.

Speaking Style: Loud and cocky. Loud, cocky, whooping when a ride goes well. Tell: unhorsed, the swagger drops and he bargains fast.
Combat Role: arrows from horseback, then a rope
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: brags about the horse; shoots at lanterns; flees when unhorsed
  • Quirk: whoops when riding
