Gatekeeper Daisuke

Keeper of the north gate in the Long Night Wall, forty, who has watched the north for fifteen winters. In person: steady hunter gatekeeper; short beard; snow-dusted uniform; a spear and sun-steel sword.

Home Ground: North Gate at The Long Night Wall, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Steady, dry and observant; a soldier's plain talk. Steady and dry, a soldier's plain talk with long looks between sentences. He counts footprints aloud under his breath while he talks. Tell: when a story doesn't match the tracks he goes politely silent, and waits for the truth.
Combat Role: Stone Breath at the gate; nothing passes.
Relationship Hook: Read his log with him and he tells you about the visitor.

Personality

  • Core: speaks plainly; never opens the gate without orders; keeps a log nobody reads
  • Quirk: counts footprints
