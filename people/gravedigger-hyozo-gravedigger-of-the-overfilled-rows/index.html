Gravedigger Hyozo, Gravedigger of the Overfilled Rows

Gravedigger of the Moorland Graves after the war, sixty-two, digging new rows for soldiers carried home and walking them at night with a lantern. He does not remember why. In person: stooped gravedigger; empty eyes; a lantern and shovel; new graves to the horizon.

Home Ground: Grave Rows, After the War at Moorland Graves, The Overfilled Graves, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Slow, patient and gloomy; he talks to the graves like neighbours. Slow and gloomy, the cadence of someone who talks more to the dead than the living. He calls the graves 'the neighbours' and greets them by name. Tell since the war: he greets the new graves as neighbours too, and gets their names wrong, and apologises.
Combat Role: a shovel, slowly.
Relationship Hook: Give the wisps a memory of your own and he tells you what they know.

Personality

  • Core: talks to graves; forgets names; walks the rows at night
  • Quirk: leaves rice on the stones
