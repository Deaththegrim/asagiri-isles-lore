Refugee Mother Ogin

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

A refugee mother on Seimei's lake shore, thirty-three, with four children and a petition the Bureau will not post. She teaches the shore children their letters with a stick in the sand. In person: refugee mother; tied hair; patched travelling clothes; a stick drawing letters; a lake shore.

Home Ground: Lake Gate Post, After the War at Seimei, Behind Two Walls, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Warm and practical, a mother's quick voice that turns every question into a plan. Warm and quick, every question turned into a plan. Tell: at the words paper warrior she counts her children aloud.
Combat Role: none; she gets the children away.
Relationship Hook: Get her petition read and she will tell you where the paper warrior walks.

Personality

  • Core: counts her children twice; argues with the gate guard daily; keeps the petition dry
  • Quirk: teaches in the sand
