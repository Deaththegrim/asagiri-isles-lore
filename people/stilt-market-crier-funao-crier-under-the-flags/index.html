Stilt-Market Crier Funao, Crier Under the Flags

Crier of Ashiura's stilt market after the war, who sings the coast's bounties and the Brine Tide's prices from the same barrel. In person: market crier on a barrel; rolled bounty notices; a hoarser voice.

Home Ground: Stilt Market, After the War at Ashiura, Under Smugglers' Flags, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: A market crier's big cheerful voice that carries across the water; he sings the bounties. A big singing crier's voice that rolls across water, every notice set to a tune. Tell since the war: he sings the bounties on the Tide's own captains loudest, and checks the water afterwards.
Combat Role: a pole off the barrel and a leap into the water.
Relationship Hook: Catch him singing a verse wrong and he begs you to take the real bounty instead.

Personality

  • Core: sings every notice; balances on the barrel; drinks with the fishermen
  • Quirk: knows every boat by its sail
