The Moon Fox

A young woman who meets a Bureau student on Moonview Bridge every full moon, laughing, with ribbons in her hair and a lantern that burns blue. In person: fox spirit; long silver hair with ribbons; fox ears half-hidden; golden eyes; pale blue kimono; a lantern burning blue fire; teasing smile.

Home Ground: Bridge Crown at Moonview Bridge, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Teasing, light and rhythmic, almost singing; she laughs at her own riddles. Sing-song riddles, giggles at her own wordplay, words bouncing like a game. Tell: when she is sincere, the rhythm stops and she speaks one plain sentence.
Combat Role: illusions and fox-fire, then she is gone.
Relationship Hook: Protect her meetings from the Talisman Master's followers and the Fox Court notices you.

Personality

  • Core: speaks in riddles; ties ribbons on the rails; never crosses the bridge's centre
  • Quirk: laughs at her own jokes
