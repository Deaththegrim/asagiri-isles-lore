Forge-Row Tally-Keeper Hinoe

Tally-keeper of Hibana's forge row, who posts the mines' and forges' bounties on crawlers, revenants and the things in the deep tunnels, and pays in iron or coin. In person: adult woman; forge-row tally-keeper with ear plugs; soot-smudged cheeks; leather apron.

Home Ground: Forge Row at Hibana, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Bright and quick over the hammers, counting in rhythm with the forges. Bright and quick, counting in time with the hammers, humming forge songs between notices. Tell: talking about the deep forges she loses the rhythm.
Combat Role: a smith's hammer, swung in rhythm.
Relationship Hook: Clear a forbidden bounty and she tells you what the guild keeps in the deep forges.

Personality

  • Core: counts to the hammer rhythm; pays in iron if asked; hums forge songs
  • Quirk: wears ear plugs on a string
