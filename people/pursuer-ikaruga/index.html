Pursuer Ikaruga

A Pursuit Division officer camped at the mouth of Deserters' Gorge, who looks forty, patient as a heron. Deserters do not come out while he is there, and he is always there. In person: patient pursuit officer; long tied black hair; still eyes; black officer's coat with a red sash.

Home Ground: Rope Bridges at Deserters' Gorge, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Few words, all final; he states facts like sentences passed. Few words, each delivered like a sentence passed, with no inflection at all. He never asks a question he doesn't already know the answer to. Tell: when his spared list is mentioned he pauses a full breath before denying it.
Combat Role: one officer's blade and absolute patience.
Relationship Hook: Show him the Records wing's truth and he turns his blade on the right people.

Personality

  • Core: states facts like sentences; never sleeps in the open; keeps a list of the deserters he has spared
  • Quirk: waits
