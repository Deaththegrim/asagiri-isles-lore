The Hanged Debtor of the Crossroads

A gaunt grey oni in a farmer's mud-stiff jacket, a cut rope still knotted at his throat, short horns through a straw hat gone black. He stands under the gallows tree with one hand held out, palm up, as if asking for something back. He lets farmers pass. Anyone carrying a ledger, a tax seal or a collector's staff he stops under the tree and lifts by the throat, and three collectors have been found hanging from the same branch since the war. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: gaunt grey-skinned oni farmer; cut rope knotted; short horns; one hand held out palm up.

Home Ground: Notice Board, After the War at Crossroads Gallows, Hanged Crossroads, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: A strangled farmer's rasp that counts sacks of rice and never reaches the last one. A rasp through a crushed throat, counting rice sacks under his breath and losing the count. Tell: at the sight of a ledger his hand comes out, palm up, before he speaks.
Combat Role: the hanged debtor tears the rope from his throat and swings it like a flail; the gallows tree creaks and every noose on it drops at once, reaching for the party
Relationship Hook: Learn what Yasuke lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
