Sekigane Grave-Robber Band, Mound-Diggers of the Twice-Fought Field

A gang working the fresh war mounds of Sekigane for this war's swords and armour, which sell better than the old. They dig by daylight now, because the field is worse at dusk. A stock foe, one of many. In person: gang of grave robbers; mud to the thigh; spades and short swords; scavenged war armour worn mismatched.

Speaking Style: Greedy muttering, a quick laugh. Greedy muttering in several voices, a quick nervous laugh, spades scraping. Tell since the war: they all stop talking at once when the wind turns, then argue louder to cover it.
Combat Role: spades and short swords in a gang, then a bolt into the dark
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: dig by day; sell to the quartermaster; never look at the great mound
  • Quirk: dig at dusk
