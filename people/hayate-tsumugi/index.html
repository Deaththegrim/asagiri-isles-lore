Hayate Tsumugi

A Hayate ridge-runner, twenty-one, the village's fastest scout and loudest laugh, who takes nothing seriously, least of all strangers, and tests every newcomer with a secret to see if they keep it. In person: bubbly wind kunoichi; short messy light brown hair; bright laughing eyes; light grey-blue shinobi gear with a herb pouch; grinning.

Home Ground: Healers' Gardens at Hayate Ridge Village, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Bubbly, teasing and quick, laughing at everything; the laugh stops for real things. Bubbly teasing, laughs at everything including herself, nicknames for everyone. She drums her fingers in a run of three. Tell: when something real is said, the laugh stops and her voice goes very small and serious.
Combat Role: speed, herbs and a short blade; she fights running and patches you after.
Relationship Hook: Run the ridges with her and keep the secret she tests you with.
Courtship Style: Teases, tests, laughs; the first serious sentence she says to you is the confession.
Jealousy Style: Laughs louder at the rival's jokes, then disappears up the ridge until you follow.

Personality

  • Core: tests people with secrets; runs instead of walking; hums healing songs when nobody listens
  • Quirk: laughs at everything
