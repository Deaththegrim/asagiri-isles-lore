Shiba Kagemori

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

The new leader of the deserters on Pine Avenue, twenty-four, son of a house that lost the war, who took the samurai recruit's place when he fell. The deserters follow him because he knows how to make the road pay. In person: young man; deserter samurai; long hair tied loosely; faded house crest; an undrawn sword.

Home Ground: Shaded Verge, After the War at Pine Avenue, The Deserters' Avenue, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Easy and bitter, a lord's son's polish worn thin by the road. Court polish worn thin by the road, bitter jokes in good grammar. Tell: when his brother is named he recites a line of the poem instead of answering.
Combat Role: his deserters at the trees, his own sword only at the last.
Relationship Hook: Clear the Shiba name and the deserters of Pine Avenue will follow whoever did it.

Personality

  • Core: speaks court Japanese to bandits; never robs the poor; polishes a sword he does not draw
  • Quirk: reads the poem on the pine
