Frozen Battlefield Demon Noble, Noble Who Stayed

A demon in black court robes over armour who stayed on the frozen field when the Court went south; the ice is thawing and his raised ranks wake slower each night. A stock foe, one of many. In person: demon noble; black court robes torn and frosted; an iron war fan cracked along one rib.

Speaking Style: Courtly and bored, a fan snapping shut at the end of a sentence, old court words. Tell since the war: he fans himself, then looks south a long moment.
Combat Role: the iron fan in a duel while the dead hold the line
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: fans himself; duels the worthy; despises the Court that left
  • Quirk: walks the ranks
