Deep-Forge Stone Guardian, Guardian of the Open Forges

A guardian of black iron-stone whose forge heart burns hotter since the house opened the deep forges; it guards the Soul Anvil's tunnel against anyone without the guild's mark, the house's own agents included. A stock foe, one of many. In person: stone guardian of black iron-stone; a forge heart glowing white-hot; cracks leaking red light; talismans pasted over its shoulders.

Speaking Style: A forge's roar with no words, bellows breathing, iron ringing. Tell since the war: its heart flares white whenever a house agent steps into the tunnel.
Combat Role: fists of iron-stone in a narrow tunnel
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: never speaks; obeys only the guild's mark; burns hotter
  • Quirk: glows when angry
