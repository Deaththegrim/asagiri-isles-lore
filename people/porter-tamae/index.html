Porter Tamae

A mountain porter who carries loads across the hanging bridge three times a day, singing to keep her feet. In person: sturdy cheerful mountain porter with a huge load crossing a swaying rope bridge; singing.

Home Ground: The Hanging Bridge at Hanging Bridge Pass, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Cheerful and breathless, a porter who sings to keep her balance on the bridge. Cheerful and breathless, a work song under every sentence. Tell: when the ropes are mentioned the song stops.
Combat Role: a carrying pole and a push off the edge.
Relationship Hook: Cross with her and she shows you the thinning rope.

Personality

  • Core: carries twice her weight; counts the ropes; laughs at the wind
  • Quirk: sings on the bridge
