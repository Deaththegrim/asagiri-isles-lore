The Lantern Bride of Gibbet Rock

A tall oni-woman in a sea-rotted wedding kimono, two short horns through her veil, a paper lantern burning green in her hand. She stands on Gibbet Rock every night looking out to sea. She waits on the rock. A sailor who looks like the man she married, broad, bearded, with a captain's coat, she wades out to meet and drags under; anyone else she ignores unless they touch the lantern. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: tall pale oni-woman with two short horns; sea-rotted white wedding kimono; green paper lantern; drowned beauty.

Home Ground: Gibbet Rock at Pirate Graves Reef, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Soft and drowned, every sentence a question to the sea. Soft and drowned, a bride's voice under water; every line ends as a question to the sea. Tell: a man's footstep on the rock and she turns, hope breaking her voice.
Combat Role: the lantern bride drops the veil
Relationship Hook: Learn what Okiyo lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
