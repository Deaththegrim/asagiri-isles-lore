Plain Boar

A tusked wild boar, bristling and mud-caked. Boars raid the paddies at night and charge anyone who corners them. A stock foe, one of many. In person: tusked wild boar; mud-caked.

Speaking Style: Grunts, quick and wet, rising into a squeal when it turns. It snorts at the ground. Tell: a long squeal means it is about to run, not fight.
Combat Role: a straight charge
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: charges straight; never turns; squeals
  • Quirk: roots at night
