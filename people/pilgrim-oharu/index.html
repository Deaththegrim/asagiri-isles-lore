Pilgrim Oharu

A pilgrim, sixty, walking the Fifty Stations Road to visit every shrine on it before she dies. She rests under the pines with a white pilgrim's robe, a staff and a stamp book full of shrine seals. In person: cheerful pilgrim; white pilgrim robes; a straw hat with a sutra; a staff with a bell.

Home Ground: Shaded Verge at Pine Avenue, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Cheerful, chatty and devout; she tells strangers about every shrine she has visited. Chatty and devout, lists shrines on her fingers, bows her words. Laughs warmly at her own sore feet. Tell: when her son is mentioned, the chatter stops and she prays under her breath.
Combat Role: none; a pilgrim's staff and a loud prayer.
Relationship Hook: Help her find her son's last station and she gives you the stamp from the station that is not on the map.

Personality

  • Core: prays at every stone; feeds the crows; talks about her son in the present tense
  • Quirk: shows everyone her stamp book
