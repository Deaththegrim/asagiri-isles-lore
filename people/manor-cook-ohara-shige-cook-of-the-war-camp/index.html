Manor Cook Ohara Shige, Cook of the War Camp

The manor's cook after it became a war camp, fifty-two, feeding two hundred levies instead of forty young swords. She knows who died at Sekigane by which bowls come back to the kitchen. In person: middle-aged cook; sleeves tied back; an army cauldron; steam; a list of names chalked on the kitchen wall.

Home Ground: Speech Hall, After the War at Hidetsugu's Manor, Camp-Lord's Hall, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Warm and scolding, a cook who feeds young samurai like her own children. Warm scolding, a ladle tapping the pot, 'eat first, talk after'. Tell since the war: she counts bowls under her breath as she talks, and stops at the ones that come back full.
Combat Role: a ladle and a cleaver.
Relationship Hook: Eat at her table and she tells you who visited last night.

Personality

  • Core: scolds with a ladle; knows every visitor; lies to the regent's men
  • Quirk: feeds everyone
