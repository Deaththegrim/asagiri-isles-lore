Mother Urushi, Mother of the Bonded Quay

The black warehouse's owner after the war, fifty-two, richer than ever: the war bought every sealed host she could sell, and some never came back to be resold. Porters still call her Mother. In person: heavy-lidded auctioneer; black lacquer jewellery; a new dark red kimono; a sliding lacquer box; a smile like a closed door.

Home Ground: The Black Warehouse, After the War at Foreign Quay Warehouses, Bonded Quay, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Husky and slow, every word priced; she calls everyone 'little one' and means it as a valuation. Husky, lazy vowels, a price at the end of every sentence. Taps a lacquer box with a fingernail as she talks. Tell since the war: when a crate creaks anywhere near her she stops mid-price and listens.
Combat Role: she never fights; her porters close the doors and the foreign captains' men do the rest.
Relationship Hook: Bring her the runaway child and she pays more than the guild ever will; bring her a buyer's name and she trades you a box.

Personality

  • Core: taps her lacquer rings while she thinks; never sits facing a door she did not choose; remembers every price ever paid
  • Quirk: calls everyone 'little one'
