Graveyard Frost Spirit, Frost Over the Crack

A swirl of grave-frost with many faces, risen from the exiles' graveyard where the fissure split the markers; the warm grave at its edge has made it restless. A stock foe, one of many. In person: frost spirit; many faint faces swirling; one face warm and flushed among the frozen.

Speaking Style: Many voices, very cold, speaking a heartbeat apart like an echo. Tell since the war: one of its faces is warm and keeps trying to speak.
Combat Role: frost that holds the feet, then shards
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: wears many faces; freezes the crack's edge; thins at a name
  • Quirk: rises from markers
