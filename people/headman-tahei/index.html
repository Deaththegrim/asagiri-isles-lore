Headman Tahei

Headman of Hundred Fields Village, sixty, who keeps the tax register and a nervous smile for every samurai who passes. His village is tired, taxed and quiet, and he has kept it alive through two famines by bowing low and remembering everything. In person: rice farmer headman; thin grey hair; weathered face; nervous smile; patched brown kimono; flooded paddies.

Home Ground: Headman's House at Hundred Fields Village, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Polite, nervous and full of agreeable nothings; under it, a farmer's stubbornness. Bows in his voice: lots of 'yes, yes' and 'of course, honoured one'. Rubs his hands audibly. Tell: when the seed rice is threatened the agreeable words stop, and a flat farmer's 'no' comes out.
Combat Role: none; he bows and hides the young ones.
Relationship Hook: Stand between the village and the tax collector tomorrow and the village hides you forever after.

Personality

  • Core: bows too often; talks about the weather when frightened; feeds strangers before asking their names
  • Quirk: keeps two tax registers
