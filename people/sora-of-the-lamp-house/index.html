Sora of the Lamp House

Widow Toki's grandson, twenty, lean and sunburned, who rows out to the island against her wishes and sleeps in the empty house because it is the only thing his family owns. In person: young man; twenty; lean; sunburned; short messy black hair; a cut lip healing; sleeveless indigo work jacket; rope belt; rolled trousers; bare feet; a boat hook.

Home Ground: Keeper's Hearth at The Cliff House, on Tomoshibi Isle, the island off Saikai.
Speaking Style: Eager, quick and a little too loud, a sailor's slang he learned on the quay. Quick and loud, quay slang, two fingers drumming on whatever is near. Tell: asked about his grandfather he goes quiet and the drumming stops mid-beat.
Combat Role: a boat hook swung wide, then a shoulder to the gut.
Relationship Hook: He will show you every room of the house that is still standing, and every room that is not, if you help him patch the roof.

Personality

  • Core: volunteers before he is asked; boasts, then works twice as hard to make the boast true
  • Quirk: drums on whatever is nearest with two fingers
