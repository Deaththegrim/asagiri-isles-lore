Priestess Kohaku

The priestess of the fox shrine at Kitsunebi Crossing, who keeps the place where the human road and the yokai paths meet. She is serene, patient and respected by both sides. In person: serene fox priestess; long black hair tied with white paper; faint fox ears; red and white shrine robes.

Home Ground: Fox Shrine Post at Kitsunebi Crossing, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Serene, warm and gently amused; she has been answering the same questions for three hundred years. Serene and warm, gently amused, as if she already knows how the conversation ends. She rings a small bell to close her blessings. Tell: when the dark shrine is mentioned she sighs and the bell stays silent.
Combat Role: the shrine's wards, and fox-fire if pushed.
Relationship Hook: Bring her a dispute between wood and road and she brokers it fairly.

Personality

  • Core: answers patiently; never eats meat in public; smiles at small children
  • Quirk: keeps both sides' secrets
