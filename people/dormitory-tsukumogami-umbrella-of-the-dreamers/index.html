Dormitory Tsukumogami, Umbrella of the Dreamers

A dark crimson paper umbrella that no longer licks the sleepers: it opens over the students who dream the white hare's dream, and its one eye stays open all night. A stock foe, one of many. In person: karakasa tsukumogami; faded crimson paper umbrella with a white hare painted over its crest; one wide unblinking eye; a long tongue rolled tight.

Speaking Style: Giggling and sulky. A giggling, sulky voice that hops with the umbrella, tongue slapping on the hard consonants. Tell since the war: it creaks open whenever a sleeper whimpers, wherever it is.
Combat Role: a tongue lash and a spin
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: shields sleepers; steals nothing now; never closes its eye
  • Quirk: hops on its handle
