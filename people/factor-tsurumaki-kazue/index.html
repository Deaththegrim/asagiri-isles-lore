Factor Tsurumaki Kazue

A guild factor, thirty-eight, in a dark merchant's coat with an abacus on a cord, who handles the counting house's road accounts and smiles at every debtor. In person: merchant guild factor; sharp eyes; hair pinned with a silver comb; dark merchant's coat; an abacus.

Home Ground: Money Counters at Guild Counting House, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Pleasant and quick, every sentence a price. Bright and quick, money talk never far off, abacus beads clicking under her words. Tell: when frightened the clicking stops and she starts to round her numbers.
Combat Role: she never fights; she sends for help and leaves by the back stair.
Relationship Hook: Show her the copied ledger and she will talk money before she talks threats.

Personality

  • Core: smiles before every refusal; never writes a name she can say; tips well
  • Quirk: flicks the abacus beads while others talk
