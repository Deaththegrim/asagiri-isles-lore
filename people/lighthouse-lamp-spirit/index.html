Lighthouse Lamp-Spirit

An old bronze lantern that has grown eyes and spindly legs and gutters with a greedy flame. A tsukumogami lamp shines ships onto the rocks for the joy of watching them break. A stock foe, one of many. In person: old bronze lantern with eyes and spindly legs; greedy flame; inside a lighthouse lamp room.

Speaking Style: A crackling giggle. A crackling, sputtering giggle that flares louder with excitement, words hissing like oil on a wick. Tell: thanked, it falls quiet and burns a steady, warm hum.
Combat Role: flares and scalding oil, then a skitter into the dark
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: giggles at wrecks; hates being dimmed; remembers every keeper
  • Quirk: flickers when lied to
