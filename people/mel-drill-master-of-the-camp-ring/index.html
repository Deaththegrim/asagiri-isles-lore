Mel, Drill-Master of the Camp Ring

The foreign fighter after the war, who stayed when the tournament became the Regent's camp and now drills its recruits in the sand ring by day and runs bare-knuckle night bouts for soldiers' pay. In person: dark-skinned foreign woman; black hair fading to sea-green, longer now; sea-green eyes; a cigarillo; a soldier's padded jacket under a black haori; red-lacquered armoured sleeve; the rune great-sword across her back.

Home Ground: The Sand Ring, After the War at Tournament Grounds, The Regent's Camp, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: A low, tired register: clipped phrasing, dry sarcasm, guarded pauses, and an intensity she keeps on a short leash. Low and tired, clipped to the bone, every kindness wrapped in dry sarcasm, a guarded pause before anything true. Tell since the war: since Sekigane she touches the blade's hilt before she speaks of the war, as if checking it is still asleep.
Combat Role: the great-sword swung two-handed like a club, then a gate opens behind you and the pommel finds your back.
Relationship Hook: Come to the night drill in the Sand Ring and she will teach you to take a hit, then ask what you are running from.

Personality

  • Core: guarded; volatile in a bout, patient with beginners; drinks and does not sleep; shields anyone weaker
  • Quirk: turns the memorial pendant at her throat when she lies
