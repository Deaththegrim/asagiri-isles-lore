Ine of the Hundred Fields, Lady of the Straw Masks

The masked leader of the Straw Masks after the war: the rising won ground, held the Oiwake barricade and drove the Red Lanterns out of its camp, and now the villages call her lady. She still fights with a flail and still wears the straw mask, and she has not decided whether to take it off. In person: tall rebel leader; straw mask with a new red cord; a captain's sash over patched farmer's clothes; a captured sword on her back beside the flail; scar across one wrist.

Home Ground: Masked Fire Ring, After the War at Straw Mask Hill Camp, The Peasant Army's Camp, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Low, plain and steady, a farmer's words with a soldier's timing; she never says more than the fire needs. Even and low, a voice for a fire ring, never pitched to carry further than it needs. Uses farm proverbs as orders. Tell since the war: when anyone calls her lady she waits half a breath before she answers, as if the word belonged to someone else.
Combat Role: flail and terrain, the villages' knowledge of every dike and ditch, and a bow from the saddle when she must.
Relationship Hook: Bring the camp food, weapons that do not come from the Red Lanterns, or the name of the traitor, and she listens.

Personality

  • Core: never takes off the mask; eats last; listens to the youngest fighters first
  • Quirk: rides better than any farmer should
