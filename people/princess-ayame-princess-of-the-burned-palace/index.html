Princess Ayame, Princess of the Burned Palace

The Dowager's ward after the war, twenty-one, the claimant with the best blood and no army still, holding the inner court together in mourning white since the Dowager died in the fire. The plain still wears white lotuses for her, and fewer of them now. She is sharp, curious and mischievous yet, and has learned to be afraid. In person: princess; long black hair cut to the shoulder after the fire; a white lotus pin; mourning white kimono with a burn mark on one sleeve; clever, tired eyes.

Home Ground: Princess Ayame's Rooms, After the War at The Inner Palace, Without an Emperor, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Light, teasing and quick, every line a test; when she means something, she stops smiling and speaks slowly. Teasing lilt, questions that sound like dares, a quick bright laugh she uses as a shield. Hums court songs off-key when she thinks she is alone. Tell since the war: at the word gate her voice catches, and she changes the subject with a joke that does not land.
Combat Role: a naginata she is not supposed to own, swung with real training and no experience.
Relationship Hook: She tests you with teasing and small betrayals offered as gifts; refuse to use her when you could and the game stops.
Courtship Style: Tests first, teases always; the first real gift is an hour where she drops the court voice.
Jealousy Style: Smiles harder and gets crueller with her wit; then asks, once, plainly, whether she is being used again.

Personality

  • Core: keeps a list of who has lied to her; sneaks out at night to the Hall of Poems; laughs at the wrong moments
  • Quirk: teases to find out who flinches
