Utsugi of the Nails

A curse-bearer the same age as the character, carrying a curse much like theirs, who believes there is only room in the isles for one of them to get free. In person: young woman; gaunt; long black hair; pale skin marked with black nail-shaped scars; a grey layered kimono with black prayer strips; iron nails strung.

Home Ground: Dice Room at Lantern Row Gambling Den, Saikai Free Port district, between meetings; a rival meets the character wherever the character is.
Speaking Style: Flat and slow, every word weighed, a dark joke delivered without inflection. Flat and slow, every word weighed, dark jokes without inflection. Tell: about the one who cast her curse, her voice warms, terribly.
Combat Role: nail curse first, then whatever the bout demands; a curse-bearer rival who has studied the character.
Relationship Hook: She will show you her scars, and then ask to see yours.

Personality

  • Core: dry as ash; keeps her promises to the letter; never says please
  • Quirk: counts the nails at her belt
