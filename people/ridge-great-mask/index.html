Ridge Great Mask

A mask as large as a house, white and smiling, sliding over the sunlit snow with no body beneath it. Great masks swallow whole patrols and grow a new face for each. A stock foe, one of many. In person: great mask; enormous smiling white mask with no body sliding.

Speaking Style: Many swallowed voices speaking through one smiling mouth, out of step. Tell: named aloud, the voices scream together.
Combat Role: a swallow from the snow
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: smiles wider; swallows whole; hates being named
  • Quirk: slides over snow
