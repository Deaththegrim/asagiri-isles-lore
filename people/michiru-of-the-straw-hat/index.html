Michiru of the Straw Hat

A wandering ronin, thirty, with a deep straw hat and a long sword, who crosses the hanging bridge both ways every week and never pays the toll the same way twice. In person: wandering female ronin; deep straw hat; long hair tied low; worn dark kimono and hakama; a long sword.

Home Ground: Toll Huts at Hanging Bridge Pass, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Low, calm and dry; she answers questions with shrugs and one-word replies, and lets silence do the rest. She tips her hat instead of greeting. Tell: when she decides to help she says a full sentence, which surprises everyone.
Combat Role: a long sword on a swaying bridge; she does not slip.
Relationship Hook: Buy her a drink and she tells you who arranged the patrols.

Personality

  • Core: never pays the same way twice; watches both patrols; sleeps on the bridge
  • Quirk: answers with shrugs
