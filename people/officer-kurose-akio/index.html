Officer Kurose Akio

An officer of the Veil Court's Records wing, thirty, in the Court's white coat with the Records crest, who audits the ferry lists at Tasogare and smiles at nobody. In person: stern veil court officer; black hair tied back; white officer's coat with a records crest; a ledger and a blade.

Home Ground: Lantern Cliff at Tasogare, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Correct and cold, every word by the book. Correct and cold, every sentence a regulation, his pen tapping once per clause. Tell: caught out, he quotes the order's exact wording and whose seal is on it.
Combat Role: an officer's blade by the book, then a seal.
Relationship Hook: Show him the cut rope's end and he tells the party exactly whose order he followed, as if that ends it.

Personality

  • Core: quotes the Court's law; straightens other people's coats; never rings a bell
  • Quirk: writes everything down
