Pass Night Hunter

A pale demon with long claws who hunts in packs on Demon Nights. Night hunters raid the south on the long winter nights. A stock foe, one of many. In person: pale night hunter demon with long claws and red eyes.

Speaking Style: Courtly and cruel, refined greetings before the kill. She compliments her prey. Tell: when wounded, the courtesy cracks and she hisses.
Combat Role: pack claws on the long night
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: toys with prey; flees sun-steel; speaks courtly
  • Quirk: hunts in packs
