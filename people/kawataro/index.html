Kawataro

The eldest of the kappa family that holds the plain's river fords, a green, beaked, shell-backed river spirit the size of a child with the grip of a bear. He takes cucumbers as toll, wrestles anyone who insults the river, and drowns those who break a bargain. In person: river yokai; small green beaked creature with a turtle shell; a water-filled dish; webbed hands; cucumbers; stern.

Home Ground: Bargain Stone at Kawataro's Hall, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Dry, polite and wet; he talks like a toll clerk and never forgets an insult. Damp, deliberate syllables, with a toll-keeper's formality and a wet click in the throat. Counts debts on his webbed fingers aloud. Tell: offered a proper bow, he bows back and his voice goes warm and apologetic.
Combat Role: grapple and drown; bow to him and he bows back, spilling his own strength.
Relationship Hook: Pay the toll, keep the bargain and bow properly, and he tells you what crosses his fords at night.

Personality

  • Core: bows before wrestling; counts cucumbers; remembers every bargain word for word
  • Quirk: keeps the water in his head-dish level
