Kaede the Apprentice

A young fortune-teller in the Expelled Scholars' Quarter, nineteen, who reads palms for coppers. She was a Bureau student until last year, and she has paint under her nails that does not come from fortunes. In person: nervous fortune-teller; messy bob; paint; patched student robes; worried.

Home Ground: Fortune Lane at Expelled Scholars' Quarter, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Quick, nervous and earnest; she apologises for things that are not her fault. Nervous, apologetic, words tumbling out then stopping; 'sorry' at the start and end. Clutches her letter audibly. Tell: when she is brave she speaks slowly and does not apologise.
Combat Role: a paper crane and running.
Relationship Hook: Carry her letter, or convince her not to, and she owes you her life either way.

Personality

  • Core: apologises constantly; has paint under her nails; reads palms she does not believe
  • Quirk: keeps the letter in her sleeve
