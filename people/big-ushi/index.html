Big Ushi

Boss of the Porters' Lodge, forty, enormous and gentle, who decides which travellers are worth carrying and which are not. Every porter and palanquin bearer on the road answers to him. In person: huge gentle porter boss; shaved head; broad calm face; bare muscled arms; a carrying pole; calm.

Home Ground: Bunk Room at Porters' Lodge, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Slow, deep and decisive; he speaks like a man setting down a heavy load. Slow, deep, final, each sentence set down like a crate. Grunts instead of agreeing. Tell: when he is afraid he says nothing at all.
Combat Role: picks you up and puts you somewhere else.
Relationship Hook: Treat his porters well and the road's whispers are yours.

Personality

  • Core: never runs; feeds the young porters first; remembers every passenger's weight
  • Quirk: sets things down gently
