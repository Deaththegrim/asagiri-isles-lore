Widow Toki

An old widow who sells bait and mends sails at the harbour post, seventy, small and straight-backed, who has held the deed to a haunted island for thirty years and cannot give it away. In person: seventy; small and straight-backed; grey hair tied back with a blue cloth; deep sun lines; a faded indigo kimono with tied-back sleeves; a fishwife's apron; a twist of net.

Home Ground: Harbour Post at Saikai, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Slow and practical, an old fishwife's plain words, a dry laugh at anything grand. Slow and practical, a fishwife's plain words with a dry laugh at anything grand, the creak of a net knot between sentences. Tell: when the lighthouse comes up she stops knotting and the laugh goes.
Combat Role: none; she throws the bait bucket and calls the harbour guard.
Relationship Hook: Sit down at her bait stall and she will tell you about the island nobody wants, and what it would cost to take it.

Personality

  • Core: says the price first and the story after; feeds anyone who sits down; never raises her voice
  • Quirk: knots a twist of net while she talks and unknots it when she is done
