Lantern-Market Go-Between Konta

Go-between of the lantern market, a half-tanuki who keeps the crossing's board where humans and yokai post bounties on each other's troublemakers. In person: young man; half-tanuki go-between with round ears and a striped tail; changing hats.

Home Ground: Lantern Market of the Wood at Kitsunebi Crossing, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Fast, friendly, half a laugh in every sentence; he speaks to yokai and humans in the same breath. Fast and friendly, a laugh tucked inside every sentence, switching from human talk to yokai cadence mid-phrase. Tell: when he is hiding a warning he laughs too long.
Combat Role: a puff of leaves and a tanuki's sprint.
Relationship Hook: Ask him who he warns and he grins and asks what it is worth.

Personality

  • Core: laughs mid-sentence; changes his hat often; talks to lanterns
  • Quirk: warns friends
