Kiyo of the Refuge

A fox-blooded guardian of the Refuge's ward ring, twenty-four, with red-gold hair, fox ears she does not hide inside the wards, and a tail she does. She distrusts humans by default and tests every visitor with an illusion to see what they do. In person: fox-blooded half-yokai woman; long red-gold hair; fox ears; a red-gold fox tail; sharp amber eyes; shrine-maiden style red and white clothes; wary smile.

Home Ground: Ward Ring at Two-Blooded Refuge, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Light, mocking and quick; she talks through an illusion's smile and drops it when she is sure of you. Light mockery through an illusion's smile; her voice seems to come from slightly to the left of her. She teases first and decides later. Tell: when she is sure of you the mockery drops and she speaks from exactly where she stands.
Combat Role: illusion first, fox-fire second, claws and a short blade if you are still there.
Relationship Hook: She tests you with a trick; see through it and stay kind.
Courtship Style: Tricks you, then tricks you kindlier; one day she drops the illusion and shows her real face, which is the whole confession.
Jealousy Style: Turns into the rival, perfectly, and asks you which one you prefer.

Personality

  • Core: hides her tail from humans; brings the children fox-fire to play with; never eats human food first
  • Quirk: tests visitors with illusions
