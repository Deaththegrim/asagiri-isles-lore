Bridge Paper Crane Swarm

A swarm of folded paper cranes woken by a careless student, slicing at anything on the bridge. Runaway crane swarms attack whoever crosses the bridge until the folder calls them back. A stock foe, one of many. In person: swarm of folded paper cranes with ink eyes slicing.

Speaking Style: A rustle of paper, a hundred tiny voices. Tell: near the letter the rustling softens.
Combat Role: a rustling swarm of paper cuts
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.
