Yumeno Kasumi, The Dreamer Who Waited

Head of the Yumeno mind-walkers after the war, forty-two, pale and drowsy-eyed, keeping Kagegi's doubled checkpoint and reading every visitor with a touch. In person: dreamy mind-walker; long pale hair; half-closed lilac eyes; dark kimono with a moth crest; a hand outstretched.

Home Ground: Outer Checkpoint, After the War at Kagegi Village, Burned at the Edge, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Soft, dreamy and unsettling; she answers what you were thinking, not what you said. Soft and dreamy, answering what you were thinking rather than what you said. Her sentences drift off and return somewhere else. Tell since the war: she answers the dead now as often as the living, and does not always notice which.
Combat Role: she makes you dream mid-fight.
Relationship Hook: Let her read you and she tells you what she saw in the dead man's dream.

Personality

  • Core: touches hands to greet; sleeps in the afternoon; hums lullabies
  • Quirk: answers unspoken thoughts
