Smiling Okon

The smiling woman who keeps the debt book at the loudest gambling den on Lantern Row, forty, round-faced and cheerful. She lends to anyone at the dice table and remembers to the coin what they owe. In person: cheerful gambling den keeper; round smiling face; hair; bright red kimono; a debt book with a red cord.

Home Ground: Dice Room at Lantern Row Gambling Den, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Bright, giggling and merciless; she sings the numbers of your debt like a children's rhyme. Giggling and sing-song, counting debts like a nursery rhyme. Now and then closes a threat with 'isn't that nice?', never twice in a scene. Tell: when she is truly angry the giggle stops and her voice goes flat and adult.
Combat Role: never; her bouncers and the guild collect.
Relationship Hook: Win at her table honestly and she likes you; buy the captain's son's debt and you own a Mizuchi heir.

Personality

  • Core: giggles at bad rolls; never gambles; keeps a debt book with a red cord
  • Quirk: sings debts like nursery rhymes
