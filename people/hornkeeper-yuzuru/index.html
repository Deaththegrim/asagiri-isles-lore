Hornkeeper Yuzuru

The young hornkeeper of the Wardens' Watch Fort, twenty-five, who sounds the horns when anything comes over the ice towards the Vaults. In person: nervous hornkeeper; furs; a huge horn; spyglass.

Home Ground: Horn Tower at Wardens' Watch Fort, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Nervous, honest and quick; he is sure of what he saw and says so three ways. He fiddles with the horn's cord. Tell: when doubted he blows a short note on the horn without meaning to.
Combat Role: seal-chains, nervously.
Relationship Hook: Go out on the ice and see what he saw.

Personality

  • Core: sleeps in the horn tower; counts the ice; keeps a spyglass
  • Quirk: sure of what he saw
