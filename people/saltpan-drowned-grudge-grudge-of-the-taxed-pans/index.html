Saltpan Drowned Grudge, Grudge of the Taxed Pans

A pale, salt-crusted figure that rises from the flooded pans the guild taxes; it drags the collectors under now, not the workers. A stock foe, one of many. In person: drowned grudge; pale salt-crusted ghost; hair full of brine; a guild tax tag nailed through one hand.

Speaking Style: A wet whisper. A wet, bubbling whisper, words dissolving at the ends like salt in water, the same name repeated. Tell since the war: it hisses at the sound of a brush on paper.
Combat Role: a grip on the ankle and the water over the face
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: weeps salt; drags collectors by the ankle; spares the salt workers
  • Quirk: rises at dusk
