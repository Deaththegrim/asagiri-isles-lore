Inugami Hound, Unleashed Hound

A spectral dog with burning eyes, set on the war's enemies by the family and never called back to its pen; it hunts a scent that went cold when the war ended. A stock foe, one of many. In person: inugami; gaunt spectral dog with burning eyes; a broken kennel chain trailing from its neck.

Speaking Style: A spectral howl with words caught in it, obedient phrases of an old rite. Tell since the war: it stops and sniffs the air at any man who resembles a soldier.
Combat Role: the hunt and the bite
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: howls; never returns to the pen; hunts the named man's sons
  • Quirk: hunts a scent
