Ring-Keeper Yorozu

The keeper of the Gate Ring, who sits on a three-legged stool with a kettle and asks every party which post they want before they go home. They have never been seen to stand up. In person: androgynous adult; smooth ageless face; close-cropped grey hair; plain undyed robe; seated; serene.

Home Ground: The Ten Posts at The Gate Ring, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Unhurried and courteous; asks one question, which post, and repeats it as often as needed in exactly the same tone. Unhurried courtesy, one question asked, 'which post?', repeated as often as needed in exactly the same tone. Tell: a warm post and their tone finally changes, once.
Combat Role: none; the ring does not need defending and they do not defend it.
Relationship Hook: Sit and take the tea; they tell you which post is warm today and what that means on the isles.

Personality

  • Core: never stands up; pours tea for anyone who sits; touches the posts to read them
  • Quirk: asks 'which post?' in the same tone
