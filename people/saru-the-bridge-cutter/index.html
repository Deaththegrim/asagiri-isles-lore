Saru the Bridge-Cutter

A wiry little bandit, twenty-five, who lives on the rope bridge over the gorge and cuts its planks to break under a heavy load. He robs whatever falls. In person: wiry giggling bandit; messy hair; monkey grin; ragged clothes; a knife.

Home Ground: Bridge Planks at Bandits' Bridge, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: High, giggling and twitchy; he narrates his own tricks as he does them. Giggles in short bursts between words and narrates himself in the third person mid-trick. Hums while he saws rope. Tell: when cornered, the giggle turns into a whine and he starts bargaining.
Combat Role: snares, falls and running up cliffs.
Relationship Hook: Catch him before the mule train crosses and he trades you the convoy's hiding place for his neck.

Personality

  • Core: giggles; climbs everything; hides shiny things
  • Quirk: narrates his own tricks
