Bath-Keeper Nanae

A woman who keeps the bathing hut at the snow monkey springs and lets hunters soak off the cold for a coin. In person: warm bath-keeper; snowy mountains; wooden hut.

Home Ground: Snow Monkey Springs: Steaming Pools at Snow Monkey Springs, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Warm and drowsy as the steam, a bath-keeper who shares the pools with the monkeys. Warm and drowsy as the steam, a hum between words. Tell: about the old monkey's knife her hum stops.
Combat Role: none; the monkeys take her side.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the old monkey's knife and she names its owner.

Personality

  • Core: shares with the monkeys; feeds the troop; knows every hunter's scars
  • Quirk: hums in the steam
