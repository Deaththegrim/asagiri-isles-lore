Shore Devourer, Devourer Grown Fat

A huge many-armed masked spirit fat on the war's hungry masks; their old masks wash up on the shore by the dozen behind it. A stock foe, one of many. In person: masked devourer; huge many-armed spirit; a great white mask; dozens of smaller masks fused into its back.

Speaking Style: A deep hungry rumble that drags its words, asking everyone for their face. Tell since the war: it plucks a washed-up mask from the sand and fits it to its back each time it pauses.
Combat Role: many arms and a devouring mask
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: eats masks; slow then fast; still searches for its face
  • Quirk: hunts after tides
