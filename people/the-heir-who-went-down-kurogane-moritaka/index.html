Kurogane Moritaka, The Heir Who Went Down

The lord's elder son after the war, thirty-two, broad and dutiful still, commanding the iron gate under warship banners. He went down to the deep forges to see for himself, and came back up not speaking. In person: broad samurai heir; short black hair; honest eyes gone distant; black iron armour; a slate he writes on.

Home Ground: Iron Gate, After the War at Kurogane Iron Castle, The Iron Victor, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Measured, honest and troubled; he says exactly what he means and no more. Measured and honest, he says exactly what he means and no more, then waits. He clears his throat before disagreeing with his father. Tell since the war: he clears his throat, picks up the slate, and writes instead of speaking.
Combat Role: shield-wall and long blade, immovable.
Relationship Hook: Go down to the deep forges for him.

Personality

  • Core: says exactly what he means; commands from the gate; keeps his father's letters
  • Quirk: visits the miners' town at night
