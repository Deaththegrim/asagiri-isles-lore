Hold Hunter on the Wall

A demon hunter in the Hold's dark uniform and a patterned haori, a sun-steel sword at her hip. Breath hunters push Soul through the body for one impossible movement and cut demons with sun-steel. A stock foe, one of many. In person: demon hunter; dark uniform and patterned haori; sun-steel sword.

Speaking Style: Clipped and tired, a night watch's economy of words. Hold hunters call the hour aloud. Tell: when a demon is near they stop calling the hour.
Combat Role: a breathing form and one sun-steel cut
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: trusts nobody on Demon Nights; names her dead; never sheathes at night
  • Quirk: breathes before she moves
