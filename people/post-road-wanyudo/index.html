Post-Road Wanyudo

A burning ox-cart wheel with a tormented monk's face at the hub, rolling down from the hill shrine at night. Wanyudo take the souls of those who look at them; shut eyes and running water save travellers. A stock foe, one of many. In person: wanyudo; burning ox cart wheel with a tormented monk's face; rolling down a hill.

Speaking Style: A tormented groan, a monk's sutra scorched. Tell: with eyes shut against it, its voice fades.
Combat Role: a burning roll and a stare that takes souls
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: burns; stares; screams
  • Quirk: rolls
