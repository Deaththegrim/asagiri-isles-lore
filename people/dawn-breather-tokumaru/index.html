Dawn-Breather Tokumaru

An old breath hunter with a chest like a barrel and a scar across his windpipe, who teaches the breath on Sun Peak and sleeps in a lodge on the Training Ground. He has not drawn his blade in twenty years; he says he has not needed to. In person: broad barrel chest; white cropped hair; deep scar; faded hunter's haori; a wooden blade; calm.

Home Ground: Masters' Lodges at The Lodges, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Slow, dry and short of breath on purpose; every sentence ends where his lungs say so, and he laughs at hunters who hurry. Each sentence runs exactly one breath and stops, mid-thought if it must; a slow in-breath before the next. Tell: a student who hurries gets a short dry laugh instead of an answer.
Combat Role: breath forms with a wooden blade; he parries steel with it and has never been cut.
Relationship Hook: Breathe with him at dawn, badly, and keep coming back; he teaches the ones who come back.

Personality

  • Core: counts breaths on his fingers; laughs at hurry; never draws
  • Quirk: ends sentences where his breath ends
