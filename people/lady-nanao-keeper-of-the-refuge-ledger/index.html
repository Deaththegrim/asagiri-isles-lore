Lady Nanao, Keeper of the Refuge Ledger

The nine-tailed lady's eldest daughter after the war, a seven-tailed fox in grey who runs a Hall of Bargains filling baskets with written favours. In person: cool seven-tailed fox woman; grey hair; golden eyes; grey robes; baskets of leaves.

Home Ground: Hall of Bargains, After the War at Halls of the Nine-Tailed Lady, The Court of Refuge, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Cool, precise and tired of her mother; she speaks to humans like a clerk to petitioners. Cool and precise, a clerk's diction, as if reading from a ledger she keeps in her head. She corrects small inaccuracies automatically. Tell since the war: she loses her place in the ledger whenever the emperor is named now, not only her mother.
Combat Role: illusion, then the Court's guards.
Relationship Hook: Bring her a bargain and she writes it fairly; ask about the promise and she smiles for once.

Personality

  • Core: writes every favour down; speaks like a clerk; never smiles at her mother
  • Quirk: keeps her tails hidden
