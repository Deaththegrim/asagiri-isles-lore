The Quiet Widow Osen

A curse-bearer who outlived her curse and keeps a quiet lodge on the Training Ground with a kettle always on. She teaches curse-bearers to turn what is killing them into something that works for them. In person: small and neat; white hair; faded indigo kimono; a kettle; dark curse marks like ink veins up her wrists; kind smile.

Home Ground: Masters' Lodges at The Lodges, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Gentle, unhurried and kind in a way that makes people nervous; she never says a curse's name aloud and never needs to. Gentle and unhurried, a kettle-side murmur that makes frightened people more frightened; she never says a curse's name. Tell: she hums when others are afraid.
Combat Role: the curse as a blade; she does not move and the foe's own grudge does the harm.
Relationship Hook: Drink her tea and let her hold your wrist; she tells you what your curse wants and does not pity you.

Personality

  • Core: keeps a kettle on; never names a curse aloud; hums while others are afraid
  • Quirk: touches a visitor's wrist to read their curse
