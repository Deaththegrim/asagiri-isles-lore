Kazakiri Daigo, Daigo of the War He Wanted

The Kazakiri heir after the war, twenty-seven, the best wind-blade in Hayate, who got his open war with Kagegi and has led it from the front. He is proud, scarred and tired. In person: tall wind shinobi; wild dark hair; two scars now; fierce tired eyes; dark blue gear with the Kazakiri crest; iron war fans.

Home Ground: Wolf Pens, After the War at Hayate Ridge Village, Burned on the Ridge, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Hard, proud and hot-tempered; he speaks in challenges, and in private, rarely, in questions. Hard challenges in a proud cadence, hot-tempered bursts that end in a demand. He cracks his neck before a duel. Tell since the war: his challenges end in a pause now, as if he were counting who is left to answer them.
Combat Role: iron fans throwing wind blades from range, then up close, hard.
Relationship Hook: Challenge him and he respects you; make him choose his clan over his grudge and he follows you.
Courtship Style: Challenges you, then spars with you, then asks your advice in private and denies it in public.
Jealousy Style: Challenges the rival to a duel, in front of everyone; afterwards, alone, asks you plainly where he stands.

Personality

  • Core: sharpens his fans at night; feeds the wolves himself; never says his father's name
  • Quirk: speaks in challenges
