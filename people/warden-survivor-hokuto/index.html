Warden Survivor Hokuto

A Vault Warden, forty, the only man of his patrol to come back out of the Vaults after the crack. He sits at the road's head and warns anyone going in. In person: vault warden; frostbitten face; heavy furs; a coil of paper-tagged chain wound around one forearm; hands empty.

Home Ground: Road to the Vaults, After the War at Shirokawa, Behind the Fissure, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Low and level, a man who speaks of the Vaults as if they were listening. Low and level, as if the Vaults were listening. Tell: at dusk he says his patrol's names, and stops at one he cannot say.
Combat Role: seal-chains and a spear.
Relationship Hook: Go into the Vaults with him and he will show you where it spoke.

Personality

  • Core: never sleeps facing the Vaults; says his patrol's names at dusk; eats little
  • Quirk: warns everyone
