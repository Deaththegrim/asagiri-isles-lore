Summer Palace Nue, Nue of the Unending Storm

A beast with a monkey's face, a tiger's legs and a snake for a tail, swollen on the storm that has hung over the ruined palace since the emperor's death; its cry rides the thunder now. A stock foe, one of many. In person: nue; larger chimera with a monkey face; storm-soaked fur crackling with static; tiger legs; a snake tail; a broken arrow in its flank.

Speaking Style: A thrush cry gone wrong. A thrush's cry twisted into something sick and low, a growl under it, the snake tail hissing. Tell since the war: its thrush-cry comes a breath before each lightning strike.
Combat Role: drop from the beams, claws and the snake together
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: hates fire; watches from the beams; flees an arrow from a sacred bow
  • Quirk: cries like a thrush at night
