Keeper Tobei of the Grudge Line

An old keeper of the Grudge Line's road shrines, sixty, who sweeps ash off the ward stones along the penitents' road and feeds the soldiers who walk it. In person: old monk; ash-dusted grey robes; a broom.

Home Ground: Ash Road, After the War at Grey Pilgrims' Road, The Penitents' Road, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Slow, kind and wheezing, a monk who coughs ash and apologises for it. Kind and wheezing, an apology for every cough. Tell: when the grey pilgrim passes he sweeps harder and stops talking.
Combat Role: a broom and a ward, slowly.
Relationship Hook: Help him prove what the grey pilgrim carries and the road's grudges quiet for a season.

Personality

  • Core: coughs ash; gives away his food; watches the grey pilgrim
  • Quirk: sweeps while talking
