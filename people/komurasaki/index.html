Komurasaki

A young monk in a straw hat waiting in the barrier queue, slight, quiet and keeping their face down. Their hands are too soft for a monk's. In person: runaway courtesan disguised as a monk; delicate face; rough grey monk robes; soft hands holding a staff; nervous.

Home Ground: Inspection Hall at Cedar Pass Barrier, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Low and careful under a monk's gruffness she cannot quite hold; when she drops it, she is pure silk. Puts on a monk's gruff growl that slips on long words. Clears her throat to cover the slip. Tell: when she drops the act, her voice falls into a slow, silken court cadence that turns heads.
Combat Role: none; she lies, then she runs.
Relationship Hook: Get her through the barrier and she gives you the letter she stole.

Personality

  • Core: keeps her face down; forgets to walk like a man; hums court songs under her breath
  • Quirk: holds the letter inside her robe
