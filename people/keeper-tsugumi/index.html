Keeper Tsugumi

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

A keeper of the Grey Thread Shrine, forty-five, in plain grey keeper's robes, who keeps the shrine's thread rope and the boy's meals and has not slept well since the war. In person: middle-aged woman; tired shrine keeper; grey hair; grey keeper's robes; a knotted thread rope.

Home Ground: Keepers' Room, After the War at Grey Thread Shrine, Shrine of the Hidden Heir, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Flat and tired, a keeper who has said every prayer too many times. Flat and tired, prayers said too many times, knot-tying under every sentence. Tell: at her son's name her voice breaks and the knotting stops.
Combat Role: never; she hides behind the shrine's doors.
Relationship Hook: Mention her son and she tells the party who has the Regent's token before she can stop herself.

Personality

  • Core: brings the boy's meals early; flinches at riders; prays for her son by name
  • Quirk: ties knots in the thread rope while she talks
