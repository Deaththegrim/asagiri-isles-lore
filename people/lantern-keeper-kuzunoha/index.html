Lantern-Keeper Kuzunoha

A fox older than the Fox Court who keeps the Lantern Gate with a tally stick and does not hide her tail. She lets half-yokai and onmyoji through when the isles have learned their names, and finds the rest very funny. In person: adult female; fox ears and a full russet tail unhidden; amber eyes; a lantern-orange kimono with a white collar; a notched tally stick; teasing smile.

Home Ground: The Arch at The Lantern Gate, The Hidden Court, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Light, teasing and quick; she makes every refusal sound like a favour and every welcome like a dare. Light and quick, every refusal made to sound like a favour, every welcome like a dare; a laugh arrives before the no. Tell: notching her tally, she goes quiet and counts aloud.
Combat Role: fox-fire and a tail's worth of tricks; she has never needed to finish a fight.
Relationship Hook: Ask what the second notch means and she tells you, if you promise to come back and prove it.

Personality

  • Core: does not hide her tail; laughs before refusing; lights lanterns early when expecting guests
  • Quirk: notches a tally stick
