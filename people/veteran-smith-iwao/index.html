Veteran Smith Iwao

A soldier who came home to Hibana's forge row, thirty-seven, carrying a soul-forged blade he fought the whole war with. He cannot put it down; when he tries, it calls him back. In person: veteran swordsman; tired eyes; burn scars; a dark sword that faintly whispers.

Home Ground: Forge Row, After the War at Hibana, The Brighter Forges, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Rough and grim, a smith's voice that goes quiet near steel. Rough and grim, quiet near steel, a mutter to the blade between sentences. Tell: when someone reaches for the blade he says the miner's name.
Combat Role: the soul-blade, which fights better than he does.
Relationship Hook: Free the miner in his blade and he will forge for you, his last blade without a soul.

Personality

  • Core: sleeps with the blade; will not let others touch it; drinks to sleep
  • Quirk: talks to it under his breath
