Ford River Serpent

A silver-scaled lesser river-dragon coiled in the shallow ford, demanding an offering for the crossing. River serpents flood the ford against anyone who crosses without paying. A stock foe, one of many. In person: river serpent; silver-scaled lesser river dragon coiled.

Speaking Style: A deep watery rumble, offerings named like rent. Tell: paid, it rumbles almost fondly.
Combat Role: a flood across the ford, then the coil
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: coils; floods; moody
  • Quirk: demands offerings
