Sea Wall Drowned Sentry, Sentry of the Abandoned Fort

A drowned soldier who still climbs the sea wall at high tide to walk his post, now among smugglers using his wall as a quay; nobody living knows the old password. A stock foe, one of many. In person: drowned soldier; a rusted cuirass hung with smugglers' rope; a waterlogged spear; barnacles on the helmet.

Speaking Style: A wet, slow challenge, the same three words each round, water in the throat between them. Tell since the war: he asks for the password, waits, and asks again more quietly.
Combat Role: a grip on the wall's edge, then into the sea
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: drips on the stones; keeps the rounds alone; hates the smugglers' lanterns
  • Quirk: asks for the password
