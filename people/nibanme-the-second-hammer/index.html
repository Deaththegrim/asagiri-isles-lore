Nibanme the Second Hammer

A huge oni smith, skin cracked like cooling iron, a broken hammer in one fist and the stump of a horn burned black. He works an anvil of raw rock in the steam field, beating at nothing. He challenges any smith or swordsman who comes near: strike his anvil and beat him at it, or fight him. He has killed three apprentices who lost. Anyone who refuses he chases from the field. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: huge oni blacksmith with cracked iron-grey skin; one burned horn stump; leather apron; broken forge hammer; rock anvil; sparks.

Home Ground: Steam Field at Ember Vent Fields, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: A grinding growl that breaks into a smith's shouted counts. A grinding growl broken by a smith's shouted counts, hammer rhythm in every sentence. Tell: at the word 'first' the count stops dead.
Combat Role: nibanme throws down the broken hammer and pulls a white-hot blade from the vent itself; every blow now sprays molten iron and he fights like the contest is happening again
Relationship Hook: Learn what Kanemasa lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
