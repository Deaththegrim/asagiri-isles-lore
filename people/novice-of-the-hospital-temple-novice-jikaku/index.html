Novice Jikaku, Novice of the Hospital Temple

A temple novice, twenty, who nurses both villages' wounded under the abbot's truce and no longer listens at the Sutra Hall floor: the war showed him what a weapon does. In person: young man; shaven temple novice; tired eyes; grey novice robes with bloodstained sleeves; bandage rolls at the belt.

Home Ground: Sutra Hall, After the War at Mountain Temple of the Seven Falls, The Falls Temple at War, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Eager and quick, a novice who finishes other people's sutras for them. Eager and quick, finishing other people's sentences, a whisper as if at the Sutra Hall floor. Tell since the war: he presses his ear to the floor out of habit, then lifts it quickly as if burned.
Combat Role: a stave and a novice's fast feet.
Relationship Hook: Ask him what he hears through the floor and he tells the party more than the abbot ever has.

Personality

  • Core: nurses both sides; asks the abbot nothing now; sleeps by the wounded
  • Quirk: presses his ear to the floor
