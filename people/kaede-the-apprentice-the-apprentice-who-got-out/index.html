Kaede the Apprentice, The Apprentice Who Got Out

The runaway mask apprentice after the war, twenty-one, who got out of the ring and now teaches refugees on the shore to make cheap wards that actually work. She is the only one in Seimei who can tell a forged Bureau seal from a real one. In person: young fortune-teller; messy bob; ink, not paint, under her nails; patched student robes; a satchel of blank wards.

Home Ground: Fortune Lane, After the War at Expelled Scholars' Quarter, Quarter of the Turned Away, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Quick, nervous and earnest; she apologises for things that are not her fault. Nervous, apologetic, words tumbling out then stopping; 'sorry' at the start and end. Clutches her letter audibly. Tell since the war: she has stopped apologising when she corrects a forged seal; she just corrects it.
Combat Role: a paper crane and running.
Relationship Hook: Carry her letter, or convince her not to, and she owes you her life either way.

Personality

  • Core: apologises constantly; has paint under her nails; reads palms she does not believe
  • Quirk: keeps the letter in her sleeve
