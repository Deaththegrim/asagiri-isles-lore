Station Master Ogiwara Tokuzo

The post master of Oiwake Station's stables, fifty, broad and red-faced, who knows every rider on the road by name and every horse by temper. In person: middle-aged man; broad red-faced post master; short thick beard; stable master's jacket and apron.

Home Ground: Post Stable at Oiwake Station, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Hearty and loud with travellers, flat and quiet with his own men. Hearty and loud with travellers, a back-slapping laugh, horses' names in every story. Tell: near the rider's name his voice goes flat and he looks for water to wash in.
Combat Role: a heavy stable hook and his men around him.
Relationship Hook: Name the rider in front of his men and his hands go to the water bucket.

Personality

  • Core: slaps backs; names every horse; drinks with the riders he sends out
  • Quirk: washes his hands too often
