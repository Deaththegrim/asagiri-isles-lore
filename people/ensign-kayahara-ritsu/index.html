Ensign Kayahara Ritsu

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

An archer-ensign of a house that lost at Sekigane, twenty-six, walking the penitents' road in grey over a scorched lacquer breastplate. She carries her lord's broken bow on her back and corrects how every soldier around her walks. In person: adult woman; twenty-six; archer ensign; sharp grey eyes; black hair cut short and ragged; grey penitent's robe; a broken longbow strapped to her back; stern.

Home Ground: Ash Road, After the War at Grey Pilgrims' Road, The Penitents' Road, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Clipped and sharp, every sentence a reprimand; she corrects your grip before she says your name. Barked and clipped, a drill-ground edge under the words. Tell: when she is pleased her scolding gets longer, never softer.
Combat Role: a longbow from cover, then a short blade when they close.
Relationship Hook: Walk the penitents' road with her and she will tell you everything you are doing wrong, and nothing about herself.
Courtship Style: Scolds first and mends your gear without asking; the first real gift is letting you carry the broken bow.
Jealousy Style: Goes cold and formal, uses your full name and rank; then picks a fight over nothing and loses it on purpose.

Personality

  • Core: corrects everyone's posture; eats last; laughs once a day, at something cruel
  • Quirk: never takes off the breastplate
