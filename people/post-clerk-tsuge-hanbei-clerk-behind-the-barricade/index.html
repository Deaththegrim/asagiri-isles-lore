Post Clerk Tsuge Hanbei, Clerk Behind the Barricade

Clerk of Oiwake's board after the war, fifty-two, who posts the Straw Masks' notices now that they hold the station, and stamps every claim twice still. In person: post-road clerk with seal and ink stone; plain grey robes; a straw mask hung on the board's corner.

Home Ground: Toll Barrier, After the War at Oiwake Station, Behind the Barricade, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Dry, patient and official; he reads every notice in the same flat voice, deaths included. Flat and official, every sentence ends like a stamp coming down, distances in ri and stations. Tell since the war: he stamps a Straw Mask notice with the same flat voice as the Regent's, and pauses only on the guard's name.
Combat Role: none; he closes the barrier and calls the guards.
Relationship Hook: Earn his trust on three bounties and he hands you the missing list.

Personality

  • Core: stamps twice; speaks in road distances; dislikes the commissioner
  • Quirk: keeps tea for hunters
