Ledger-Girl Hosoi Fuku, Clerk of the Second Book

A clerk of sixteen at the Seat of the Debt, promoted when the war took the older clerks, who now carries the second book herself. In person: young clerk; ink to the elbow; hair pinned with a brush; eager, frightened eyes.

Home Ground: Ledger Vault, After the War at Guild Counting House, Seat of the Debt, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Eager and precise, a junior clerk who reads numbers aloud like poetry. Eager, precise, numbers read like verse, words tumbling when excited. Tell since the war: she has stopped reading numbers aloud like verse; she says them flat now, and only once.
Combat Role: none; she runs and hides among the shelves.
Relationship Hook: Ask her about the second book and she tells you everything, then panics.

Personality

  • Core: reads numbers aloud; runs everywhere; remembers every page
  • Quirk: asks too many questions
