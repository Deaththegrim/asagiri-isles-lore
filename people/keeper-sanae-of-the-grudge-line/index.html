Keeper Sanae of the Grudge Line

A keeper of the Grudge Line, thirty-six, the chain of shrines the curse-hunters raised across the blight after the war to keep its grudges from spreading. She walks the line from Haibara to the pilgrims' road. In person: grudge line keeper; long hair tied with prayer strips; grey robes; a staff hung with ward tags.

Home Ground: Vow Temple, After the War at Haibara, On the Grudge Line, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Low and certain, each word placed like a ward stone. Each word set down like a ward stone, low and sure. Tell: when a shrine fails she names it, and her voice drops a stone's weight lower.
Combat Role: wards first, then a curse-breaking staff.
Relationship Hook: Hold a failing shrine with her for one night and she will show you the whole line.

Personality

  • Core: never eats in the blight; speaks to grudges by name; keeps a count of failing shrines
  • Quirk: touches every ward stone she passes
