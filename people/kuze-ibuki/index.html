Kuze Ibuki

Head of the Hyoga exiles' council, who looks forty and has been an exile for a century: a deserted Veil Court officer in a patched black coat who runs the council around a stove in a hut on the ice. In person: weathered deserter officer; grey-streaked black hair; calm cold eyes; patched black officer's coat without a sash; council around him.

Home Ground: Council Stove at Deserter Officers' Hut, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Short, quiet and warm under the frost; he weighs every word against the cold before he spends it. He breathes into his hands between sentences. Tell: when he trusts someone he uses their given name, once, and never again.
Combat Role: an officer's soul-blade, rusty and sure.
Relationship Hook: Help hide the shore host from the Warden hunters and the council trusts you.

Personality

  • Core: weighs every word; never wears his officer's sash; remembers every exile's crime
  • Quirk: keeps the stove fed
