Captain Ishimura

Captain of the Ishimura band, forty, broad, scarred and cheerful, who holds the best table in the Ronin Hiring Hall. His band is booked a year ahead, and he wants more than bookings. In person: burly ronin captain; wild topknot; scarred grinning face; open dark haori; a long sword; charismatic.

Home Ground: Captains' Tables at Ronin Hiring Hall, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Big, laughing and persuasive; he talks like a man selling a dream to people who can afford it. Big laughing voice, salesman's rhythm, every sentence ends on a promise. Slaps the table for emphasis. Tell: when a deal goes bad, the laugh stays but his eyes and words go flat.
Combat Role: a long blade and sixty friends; he leads from the front.
Relationship Hook: Help him turn the two bands on the people who sent them and you have a seat at his table.

Personality

  • Core: buys the room a round; calls every ronin brother; keeps the contracts in his boot
  • Quirk: never sits with his back to Gonroku
