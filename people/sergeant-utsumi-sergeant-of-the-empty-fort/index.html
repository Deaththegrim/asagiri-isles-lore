Sergeant Utsumi, Sergeant of the Empty Fort

Sergeant of the old coast fort after the war, forty-seven, who stayed when his garrison sold the last powder and walked home. The smugglers use his sea wall as a quay, and he lets them. In person: gruff sergeant; unshaven; worn sea-blue coat; a sea wall with smugglers' boats moored along it.

Home Ground: Powder Store, After the War at Old Coast Fort, The Abandoned Fort, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Gruff, bored and evasive; he complains about the damp to change the subject. Gruff, bored, complaining about damp to dodge questions. Tell since the war: he complains about the damp, then about the smugglers' boats, and never about the crate.
Combat Role: a spear behind a ruined wall.
Relationship Hook: Help him fill the powder store before the inspection and he names the buyer.

Personality

  • Core: counts empty kegs; drinks on watch; never looks at the sea wall
  • Quirk: complains about the damp
