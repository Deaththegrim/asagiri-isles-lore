Hagane Suzu, Suzu Who Hears the Blades

A sword polisher's apprentice after the war, twenty-two, small, soot-smudged and shy, who murmurs to the swords in her hands more often than before: the war blades talk back. In person: shy sword polisher; short messy dark hair; soot smudge; big earnest eyes with shadows; work apron; a war blade on her stone.

Home Ground: Polishing Shops, After the War at Polishers' Street, The Street of Long Queues, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Shy, blunt and quiet; she talks to blades more easily than people, and says exactly one honest thing at a time. Shy, blunt and quiet; she talks to blades more easily than people and says exactly one honest thing at a time. She hums when she polishes. Tell since the war: she hums a different tune for war blades, slower, as if to calm them.
Combat Role: none, but she knows exactly where your blade is weak.
Relationship Hook: Bring her a blade worth weeks of polishing and work beside her.
Courtship Style: Ignores you for your sword, then knows your sword better than you; the day she asks about you instead of it is the confession.
Jealousy Style: Polishes the rival's blade badly on purpose, and admits it immediately.

Personality

  • Core: blunt to the point of rude; forgets to eat; notices your sword before your face
  • Quirk: talks to blades
