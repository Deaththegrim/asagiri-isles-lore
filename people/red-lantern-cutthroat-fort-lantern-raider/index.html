Red Lantern Cutthroat, Fort-Lantern Raider

A bandit in captured war armour with a red lantern charm, garrisoning the Federation's new fort and raiding both halves of the cut road. A stock foe, one of many. In person: bandit raider; red lantern charm; a captured lacquered breastplate; a war spear instead of a hatchet.

Speaking Style: Rough, greedy and quick, demands shouted over each other. Cutthroats laugh at their own threats. Tell since the war: he checks the convoy list in his sleeve before he draws.
Combat Role: ambush from cover, a hatchet, flight if it goes badly
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: raids by the convoy list; garrisons the fort; boasts
  • Quirk: checks for a red lantern
