Fire Master Homura, Fire Master of the Whispering Hall

The Fire Master of the Forge Guild after the war, sixty-two, burn-scarred and broad, who sits in a Waking Hall that is no longer silent: the blades around him whisper. In person: burn-scarred master swordsmith; bald; white beard; broad bare arms; leather apron; a blade across his knees.

Home Ground: Waking Hall, After the War at Forge Guild Hall, The Hall That Stopped Asking, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Rough, warm and short; he talks like a hammer, with long pauses for the steel. Rough, warm and short; he talks like a hammer, with long pauses where the steel would ring. He calls every blade 'she'. Tell since the war: he calls the blades around him 'she' still, and says it more quietly when they whisper.
Combat Role: a smith's strength and a perfect blade; he fights rarely and finishes quickly.
Relationship Hook: Work his forge for a season and he teaches you blade and discipline; bring Tetsuo home and he forges your sword.
