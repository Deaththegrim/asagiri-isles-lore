Daikokuya Genta, Master of the Debt Harbour

Master of the Saikai Merchant Guild since his father died the week the debts were called in, thirty, sleek and clever still, and no longer impatient: he has everything he wanted. The Regent owes him twenty years. In person: sleek merchant lord; slicked hair; foreign coat over a mourning kimono; his father's abacus at his belt; a pocket watch he checks too often.

Home Ground: Loading Yard, After the War at Foreign Quay Warehouses, Bonded Quay, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Easy, modern and dismissive; he uses foreign words to sound clever and is. Easy and dismissive, foreign words for effect, a little bored. Taps a ledger with a brush. Tell since the war: he checks his pocket watch whenever the old guildmaster is named, and snaps it shut too hard.
Combat Role: a foreign pistol he has fired twice, and a lot of porters.
Relationship Hook: Help him hide the ledger and he makes you rich; give it back to his father and he never forgives you.

Personality

  • Core: uses foreign words; despises the old council; never carries money
  • Quirk: checks his foreign pocket watch
