Troupe-Master Sanshiro

The master of a travelling theatre troupe, forty, landing at Hibana's Ember Pier with six actors, a cart of costumes and a play about a smith who forged his own soul. In person: flamboyant theatre troupe master; painted eyebrows; colourful coat; a theatre mask; volcanic town behind.

Home Ground: Ember Pier at Hibana, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: A booming actor's voice; every sentence is a performance with an entrance and an exit. He announces himself in the third person. Tell: when the show is in danger he drops the stage voice entirely and sounds small.
Combat Role: stage swords and a lot of noise.
Relationship Hook: See the play and ask about the masks.

Personality

  • Core: never travels without masks; counts his actors; laughs at storms
  • Quirk: performs every sentence
