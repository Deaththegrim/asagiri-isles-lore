Moorland Gaki

Thin-necked, swollen-bellied hungry ghosts that crawl from the forgotten graves, mouths too small to eat. Gaki swarm the graves nobody makes offerings at and chew at the living for food they cannot swallow. A stock foe, one of many. In person: gaki hungry ghosts with thin necks; swollen bellies and tiny mouths; crawling from forgotten graves.

Speaking Style: A starving moan, begging for anything. Tell: offered food, the moan becomes a sob.
Combat Role: a swarm of grasping hands
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: crawls; begs; swarms
  • Quirk: starving
