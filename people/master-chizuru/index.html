Master Chizuru

A Breath Master of the Hold, forty, who keeps the Snow Road Post and trains the scouts that ride the road north. In person: breath master scout; dark hair; pale skin; hunter's black coat and scarf; a bow.

Home Ground: Snow Road Post at Asahi Gate, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Low and patient, a hunter who wastes no word. Low and patient, a scout's few words, muffled by her scarf. Tell: when the north is mentioned she speaks of it warmly, then catches herself and goes curt.
Combat Role: arrows from the snow, then the hunters' forms up close.
Relationship Hook: Ride north with her and the party sees where the patrol rolls go.

Personality

  • Core: rides north alone; never eats in the mess; knows every scout's family
  • Quirk: keeps her scarf over her mouth
