Farmer Oyone

A rice farmer on the marsh edge, forty, cheerful and muddy, whose paddies have very good dikes. In person: cheerful muddy rice farmer; headscarf; rolled trousers; a hoe; marsh reeds.

Home Ground: Hidden Dike at Smugglers' Rice Paddies, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Cheerful and sly; she talks about the weather while lying about everything else. Cheerful weather talk wrapped around every lie. Laughs from the belly. Tell: when the dike is mentioned, she talks about the weather even more.
Combat Role: none; a rice hoe and her neighbours.
Relationship Hook: Get the patrol off her dikes and the Tide owes you.

Personality

  • Core: lies cheerfully; feeds the patrol; never steps on the hidden dike
  • Quirk: talks about the weather
