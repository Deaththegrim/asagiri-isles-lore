Book-Keeper Senzo

The Book-Keeper of the Hundred-Duel Circuit, sixty, stooped and ink-fingered, who lodges at the Duelists' Teahouse and keeps the record of every duelist alive: their fights, their wounds, their styles. Duelists bring him their books to copy into the great register. In person: stooped scholarly book-keeper; thin grey hair; round spectacles; ink-stained fingers; plain grey robes; nervous.

Home Ground: Signing Room at Duelists' Teahouse, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Dry, fussy and precise; he quotes duel records by number and sighs at sloppy calligraphy. Fussy, dry, quotes duel numbers and sighs at bad calligraphy. Clears his throat before each number. Tell: when frightened, he starts listing duels he has recorded, to calm himself.
Combat Role: none; he hides under the register table.
Relationship Hook: Bring him a duelist's signed book and he copies it; corner him about the copies and he begs you to keep his name out.

Personality

  • Core: quotes records by number; sighs at bad handwriting; eats alone in the signing room
  • Quirk: flinches when a duelist draws
