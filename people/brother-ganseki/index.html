Brother Ganseki

A huge warrior monk who keeps the toll huts at the Hanging Bridge Pass and breaks the boulders that fall on the road with his bare hands. He carries a sword he has not drawn since he took his vows. In person: adult man; huge muscular monk; shaved head; kind broad face; saffron and grey monk robes with one sleeve tied back; prayer beads; a sheathed sword; smiling.

Home Ground: Toll Huts at Hanging Bridge Pass, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Booming and kind, slow to speak, laughing deep in his chest; he blesses everyone who pays the toll. Booming and gentle, words rolled slowly in his chest, a blessing tacked onto everything. Tell: an insult and he cracks his knuckles before he forgives it.
Combat Role: open-hand and hilt strikes that land twice; armour and stone break on the second impact.
Relationship Hook: Break rocks with him for a day.

Personality

  • Core: laughs deep in his chest; pays the toll for the poor; cracks his knuckles before thinking
  • Quirk: blesses toll-payers
