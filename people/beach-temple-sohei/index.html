Beach Temple Sohei

A warrior monk of the ferry temple, a woman in black monk's robes and a white head wrap, with a naginata and a string of iron prayer beads. Beach sohei guard the ferry landing and turn back anyone carrying soul-steel. A stock foe, one of many. In person: female sohei warrior monk; black buddhist monk robes; white cloth hood wrapped around the head; naginata; iron prayer beads.

Speaking Style: Low and steady, half a prayer. Low and steady, every warning half a prayer. Tell: near soul-steel her prayer quickens.
Combat Role: a naginata sweep at the landing, a prayer first
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: checks every blade; offers a toll before a fight; never pursues into the water
  • Quirk: prays aloud before a fight
