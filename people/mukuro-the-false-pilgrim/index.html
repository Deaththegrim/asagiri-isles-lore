Mukuro the False Pilgrim

A pilgrim in grey who walks the Grey Pilgrims' Road with the others, praying for the cursed and helping the weak along. In person: kindly-looking grey pilgrim; shaved head; gentle smile; grey robes and straw hat; a staff; a bottle hidden.

Home Ground: Ash Road at Grey Pilgrims' Road, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Gentle, pious and slow; the voice of a kind pilgrim, with something cold underneath. Gentle, pious, slow blessings, something cold under the warmth. Tell: when someone sees through him, the warmth simply stops, like a lamp blown out.
Combat Role: a curse whispered into a prayer, then a knife.
Relationship Hook: Catch him at his bottle and the pilgrims' road is safe again.

Personality

  • Core: prays loudly; helps the weak carry their packs; never drinks with others
  • Quirk: touches a bottle under his robe
