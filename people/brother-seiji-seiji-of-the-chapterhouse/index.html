Brother Seiji, Seiji of the Chapterhouse

The young curse-hunter after the war, twenty-six, who leads the chapterhouse since Elder Kanemitsu died breaking a war curse. He is too young for it and knows it. In person: curse-hunter scholar; messy hair cut short; a notebook; prayer-strip sleeves; the elder's staff.

Home Ground: Ruined Palace, After the War at Ruins of the Old Capital, The Waking Ruins, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Excited, fast and reckless; he talks about the dead like a scholar on a dig. Excited, fast, scholar's enthusiasm, interrupts himself with discoveries. Tell since the war: he reads the names on his list in a whisper, and checks the doorway before each one.
Combat Role: a curse-breaking chant and a short staff; he runs at the wrong moment.
Relationship Hook: Get the list out of the Blight safely and he gives it to whoever will use it to lay the dead to rest.

Personality

  • Core: talks like a scholar; copies everything; forgets to be afraid
  • Quirk: keeps the list on his body
