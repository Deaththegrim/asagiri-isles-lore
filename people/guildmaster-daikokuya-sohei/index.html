Guildmaster Daikokuya Sohei

Daikokuya Sohei, master of the Saikai Merchant Guild, sixty-five, round, bald and genial, who has run the free port for twenty years from a cushion on the counting house's third floor. He lends to lords, farmers and shrines alike, forgives small debts loudly and collects large ones quietly, and is the most respected man in Saikai. In person: plump bald merchant master; genial round face; shrewd small eyes; rich brown silk haori with an abacus crest; carp pond; warm smile.

Home Ground: Council Room at Guild Counting House, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Warm, generous and patient, the voice of a man who has never needed to hurry; numbers come out of him like kindness. Round, warm, unhurried, every sum delivered like a gift. Chuckles softly before refusing anything. Tell: when his son is mentioned the warmth stays but the words go cold and exact.
Combat Role: never; a dozen hired ronin and the harbour watch fight for him.
Relationship Hook: Recover the stolen ledger unread and he owes you a favour the size of a house; read it first and you are his problem.

Personality

  • Core: forgives small debts in public; never writes a number he can say instead; feeds the carp in the council room himself
  • Quirk: smiles hardest at people he is ruining
