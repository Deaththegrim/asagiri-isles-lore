Runaway Paper Warrior, Paper Soldier Who Came Back

A paper warrior folded for a house's war, sent home when its house fell, that will not go back to paper and stands guard over the folding tables of the workshop that made it. A stock foe, one of many. In person: shikigami construct; a scorched and rain-warped folded paper warrior; a house crest torn off its chest; talisman script half washed away.

Speaking Style: Rustling paper shaped into short orders it was given long ago. It repeats its last command. Tell since the war: its paper rustles in the shape of a name, over and over.
Combat Role: paper blades until burned
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: rustles; never tires; obeys no one
  • Quirk: obeys its folder
