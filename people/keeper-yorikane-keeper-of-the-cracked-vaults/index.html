Keeper Yorikane, Keeper of the Cracked Vaults

Warden-General of the Sealing Vaults after the war, sixty-two, white-bearded and wrapped in furs, walking the Seal Gallery and replacing talismans that burn out in a night. In person: stern warden general; heavy furs; seal-chains; ash on his gloves; cold.

Home Ground: Seal Gallery, After the War at The Sealing Vaults, The Cracked Vaults, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Cold, hard and exact; he speaks like a seal being read aloud, each clause locking the next. He recites rules by their number. Tell since the war: he recites the seals' rules faster than before, and the numbers skip.
Combat Role: seal-chains and wardens; he binds rather than kills.
Relationship Hook: Bring him a host and he pays; bring him a new anchor and he listens.

Personality

  • Core: speaks like a seal read aloud; never sleeps through a horn; counts the dreaming beasts
  • Quirk: renews the seals himself
