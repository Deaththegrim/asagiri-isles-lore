Beachcomber Sute, The Beachcomber Who Wears One

A beachcomber on the Mask Shore after the war, forty-two, who collects the masks the tide brings in by the dozen now. He wears one himself. In person: beachcomber in rags; cloth-wrapped hands; a pale mask pushed up on his forehead that he will not take off.

Home Ground: Beachcomber's Hut, After the War at Mask Shore, Shore of Washed-Up Masks, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Greedy and wheedling, too eager; he holds things out to you. Greedy and wheedling, always holding something out and naming its price before you look. He giggles at his own bargains. Tell since the war: he forgets the end of his own price mid-bargain, and touches the mask on his forehead.
Combat Role: none; he throws a mask and runs.
Relationship Hook: Take the warm mask off his hands before the devourer comes for it.

Personality

  • Core: never goes out after a tide night; counts masks; wraps his hands in cloth
  • Quirk: holds things out to you
