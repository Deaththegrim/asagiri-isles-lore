Hostel Empty Lantern

A paper lantern with no flame inside that still glows, drifting along the hostel's lantern hall. Empty lanterns lead pilgrims out of bed and down to the black water. A stock foe, one of many. In person: empty lantern spirit; glowing paper lantern with no flame drifting down a dark hostel hall.

Speaking Style: A hum like a far bell. A hum like a far temple bell, no words, rising and falling as it drifts. Tell: at a prayer the hum falters and goes out.
Combat Role: a cold glow that leads, then snuffs the light
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: glows colder when followed; goes out at a prayer; hums
  • Quirk: drifts at sleepers' height
