Kuro, Kuro of the Crowded Dojo

The crow-blooded tengu of the peak after the war, who looks thirty, running a cliff dojo twice as crowded and throwing twice as many students off. In person: crow tengu man; black wings; messy black hair; beak-mask pushed up; sharp yellow eyes; dark yamabushi robes; a sneer that slips.

Home Ground: Cliff Dojo, After the War at Tengu Peak, The Peak Above the War, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Dry, mocking and lazy-sounding; every compliment is an insult turned inside out. Lazy and dry, compliments delivered as insults, everything a little beneath him. He yawns at threats. Tell since the war: he stops yawning when a student mentions the plain, and looks down off the peak.
Combat Role: wings and a straight sword, from above; he fights like a bored god until you surprise him.
Relationship Hook: He throws you off the peak; climb back.
Courtship Style: Insults you, throws you, waits; the day he catches you instead of letting you fall is the confession.
Jealousy Style: Throws the rival off the peak, slightly harder; claims it was the wind.

Personality

  • Core: lounges on the cliff edge; counts the climbs; keeps a feather from every student who made it
  • Quirk: insults every student
