Fire Master Homura

The Fire Master of the Tetsuyama Forge Guild, sixty, burn-scarred and broad, the greatest swordsmith alive and one of the few who can wake a soul-blade's spirit. He teaches blade and discipline together and says you cannot have one without the other. In person: burn-scarred master swordsmith; bald with a white beard; broad bare arms; leather apron; stern and warm.

Home Ground: Waking Hall at Forge Guild Hall, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Rough, warm and short; he talks like a hammer, with long pauses for the steel. Rough, warm and short; he talks like a hammer, with long pauses where the steel would ring. He calls every blade 'she'. Tell: when a student does good work he says nothing and hands them the next job.
Combat Role: a smith's strength and a perfect blade; he fights rarely and finishes quickly.
Relationship Hook: Work his forge for a season and he teaches you blade and discipline; bring Tetsuo home and he forges your sword.

Personality

  • Core: talks like a hammer; pauses for the steel; tests students with heat
  • Quirk: never mentions Tetsuo by name
