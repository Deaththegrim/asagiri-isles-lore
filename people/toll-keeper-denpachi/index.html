Toll-Keeper Denpachi

A Hayate shinobi who keeps the toll huts at the hanging bridge, and charges a little more in wind and a lot more in fog. In person: chatty middle-aged toll-keeper.

Home Ground: Toll Huts at Hanging Bridge Pass, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Chatty and practical, a toll-keeper who prices the bridge by the weather. Chatty and practical, prices by the weather, a laugh at the wind. Tell: asked why he never crosses, he laughs too long.
Combat Role: a hidden blade, then he unhooks the guide rope.
Relationship Hook: Ask why he never crosses and he laughs too hard.

Personality

  • Core: prices by the weather; talks about wind; never crosses himself
  • Quirk: counts strands
