Breach Wrecker, Fort-Quay Wrecker

A raider who no longer climbs in through the breach: the garrison walked home and her crew works the abandoned fort's sea wall as a quay, landing Brine Tide cargo in daylight. A stock foe, one of many. In person: sea raider; salt-stiff leathers under a navy officer's stolen coat; a boarding axe; a coil of grapnel rope worn as a sash.

Speaking Style: Short orders over the surf. Short orders pitched over the surf, every word a count or a command, a two-note whistle for the crew. Tell since the war: she calls the fort my quay, and dares anyone to say otherwise.
Combat Role: up the breach on a grapnel, then the axe
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: weighs metal by hand; lands cargo in daylight; cuts rope rather than lose it
  • Quirk: climbs before she walks
