Runaway Paper Warrior

A paper warrior folded large enough to fight. Shikigami obey whoever folded them. A stock foe, one of many. In person: shikigami construct; folded paper warrior animated with talisman script.

Speaking Style: Rustling paper shaped into short orders it was given long ago. It repeats its last command. Tell: near fire the rustling goes frantic.
Combat Role: paper blades until burned
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: rustles; never tires; burns
  • Quirk: obeys its folder
