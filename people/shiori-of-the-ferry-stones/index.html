Shiori of the Ferry Stones

A drowned girl who waits among the Ferry Stones and asks every passer-by for their name, because she has forgotten her own and thinks one of them might be it. She is not hungry yet. In person: pale and wet; long black hair plastered flat; a plain soaked kimono; bare feet; puzzled.

Home Ground: The Stone Field at The Ferry Stones, The Far Shore, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Soft, polite and a little puzzled; she asks for names the way a child asks for a story. Soft, polite and puzzled, names asked for the way a child asks for a story, each one tried on aloud. Tell: her own name read to her and she says it twice, then nothing.
Combat Role: none yet; a drowned grudge that turns hungry is another matter.
Relationship Hook: Read her the names on the stones one by one; when you reach hers she knows.

Personality

  • Core: asks for names politely; drips without getting anything wet; cannot read
  • Quirk: counts the stones and gets a different number each time
