Old Kagari

The founder of the Two-Blooded Refuge, who looks seventy and has looked it for a century: small, brown, warm-handed, sitting at the roots of the great tree with children of every kind of blood around her. She teaches the two-blooded to live with what they are. In person: small warm founder; brown skin; white hair; kind eyes with a faint glow; simple earthen robes; gentle.

Home Ground: Great Tree at Two-Blooded Refuge, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Warm, slow and grandmotherly; under it, very rarely, a second voice breathes. Warm, slow and grandmotherly, with a second breath under hers that does not quite match. She calls everyone 'little cub'. Tell: when the beast stirs, the second breath grows louder and she stops mid-sentence to soothe it.
Combat Role: she does not; the wards close, and if they fail, the beast wakes.
Relationship Hook: Bring the coast child home and the Refuge is yours to call on.

Personality

  • Core: names every child; sleeps at the tree's roots; hums to the beast
  • Quirk: teaches by holding hands
