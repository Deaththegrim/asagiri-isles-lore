Channel Mud Wraith

A shape of marsh mud rising from the old paddies under the water. Mud wraiths drag boats and people down. A stock foe, one of many. In person: mud wraith; a shape of marsh mud rising from reed channels.

Speaking Style: A sucking gurgle, words drowned in mud. Tell: it breathes in when it means to drown you.
Combat Role: drag and smother
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: drags; slow; fills lungs
  • Quirk: rises
