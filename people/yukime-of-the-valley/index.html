Yukime of the Valley

A snow woman who lives in an ice cottage in the valley of blizzards, pale and lovely, who spares travellers who are kind. In person: pale snow woman; long white hair; ice-blue eyes; white kimono; gentle and eerie.

Home Ground: Ice Cottage at Yuki-onna's Valley, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Soft, cold and gentle, like snow falling; she never raises her voice. Soft, cold and gentle, like snow falling on snow; she never raises her voice and never hurries. Her breath seems to chill the end of each word. Tell: when someone she likes is cold, she apologises and moves away from them.
Combat Role: a blizzard, then a kiss of frost.
Relationship Hook: Be kind and she lets you take the recruit home; be cruel and you freeze.

Personality

  • Core: spares the kind; never comes near fire; watches the recruit sleep
  • Quirk: speaks softly
