Kagegi Sojun

Head of Kagegi Village, sixty, a small, still man in plain grey who has run the hidden village for twenty years from the mission hall. He sells his shinobi to the houses under contract, keeps the village's word absolutely, and trains the most promising apprentices himself. In person: small still shinobi village head; grey hair cropped short; calm unreadable eyes; plain grey shinobi robes.

Home Ground: Mission Hall at Kagegi Village, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, level and unreadable; he lets silence finish his sentences. Level and unreadable; he lets silence finish his sentences and makes you fill the gap. He pours tea with exact, unhurried care. Tell: when the scroll's secret is near he pours more tea, and asks about your family.
Combat Role: he is behind you before the fight begins; seals that end it.
Relationship Hook: Take his scroll contract and keep his rule (read none of it), and he trains you.

Personality

  • Core: lets silence finish sentences; remembers every apprentice's first mission; never sits with his back to cedar
  • Quirk: drinks only water
