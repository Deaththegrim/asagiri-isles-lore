Market Tanuki, Tanuki of the Back-Pay Market

A fat tanuki in a soldier's straw hat changing soldiers' back pay into leaf money and back again; some of the houses' pay was leaves all along, and the tanuki know which. A stock foe, one of many. In person: fat raccoon-dog yokai; a soldier's straw hat; an army paymaster's satchel stuffed with leaves.

Speaking Style: Jolly and quick, salesman patter with a belly-drum laugh. He rounds every price up and calls it a discount. Tell since the war: it bites every coin it is paid, even its own.
Combat Role: a trick and a laughing run
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: drums his belly; changes leaves for coin; runs
  • Quirk: laughs
