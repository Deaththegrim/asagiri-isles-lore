Genin Kotetsu

A Hayate genin, fifteen, the missing one from the ambushed team, sheltering in a stone hut at the snowline. He wrote his name in the hut book in a shaking hand. In person: frightened young shinobi; messy hair; torn light grey-blue gear; two kunai.

Home Ground: Stone Hut at Snowline Huts, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Frightened, fast and proud; he insists he was not scared. Frightened and fast, insisting at every turn that he wasn't scared. His voice cracks on long words. Tell: when Isshin is mentioned he drops to a whisper and checks the shadows.
Combat Role: two kunai and running.
Relationship Hook: Bring him home and his testimony stops a war.

Personality

  • Core: jumps at noises; eats everything; keeps a kunai in each hand
  • Quirk: insists he was not scared
