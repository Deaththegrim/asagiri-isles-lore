Drowned Village Grudge, Grudge of the Deeper Water

A drowned villager with seaweed hair rising at any tide now that the sea has taken the whole village but the roof path; with the navy gone it hates the Brine Tide's boats instead. A stock foe, one of many. In person: drowned grudge ghost; seaweed hair to the waist; a shrine bell rope wound round one arm.

Speaking Style: A gurgling whisper of many villagers, asking why. Water drips between words. Tell since the war: she rings the drowned bell at the sight of a Brine Tide flag.
Combat Role: drag under
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: rings bells; hates the Tide's boats; drags under
  • Quirk: rises at low tide
