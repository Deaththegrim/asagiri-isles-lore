Lamp-Keeper Gonji

The new keeper of the Saikai lighthouse, thirty-eight, a Brine Tide boatman put in the lamp room after old Rokubei died at his lamp during the blockade. In person: lighthouse keeper; rough stubble; oilskin coat; a lantern; lamp-lit tower room; the sea below.

Home Ground: Lamp Room, After the War at Saikai Lighthouse, Chain Light, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Gruff and short, a smuggler's grunt; he talks to the lamp more kindly than to people. Grunts and short words, softer when he speaks to the lamp. Tell: on foggy nights he lowers his voice as if someone might be listening from the rocks.
Combat Role: a boat hook and the lamp oil.
Relationship Hook: Learn the lamp codes from him and the smugglers' channels open to you.

Personality

  • Core: trims the wick too often; counts boats by their lights; sleeps by day
  • Quirk: never looks at the cold lamp
