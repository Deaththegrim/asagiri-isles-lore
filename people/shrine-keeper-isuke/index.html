Shrine-Keeper Isuke

An old keeper of the salt marsh shrine who sweeps the boardwalk altar every morning and leaves a rice ball under it. In person: old shrine keeper sweeping a weathered boardwalk altar.

Home Ground: Boardwalk Altar at Salt Marsh Shrine, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Slow and kind, a little hard of hearing, talking to the marsh as much as to you. Tell: asked about the flood, he looks at the altar.
Combat Role: none; a broom and salt.
Relationship Hook: Ask who the rice ball under the altar is for and he tells you about the flood.

Personality

  • Core: sweeps every morning; talks to the marsh; remembers the flood
  • Quirk: leaves a rice ball under the altar
