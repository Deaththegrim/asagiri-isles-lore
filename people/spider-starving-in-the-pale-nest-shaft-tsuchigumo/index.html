Shaft Tsuchigumo, Spider Starving in the Pale Nest

A huge pale earth spider, thin and failing, that can no longer hold the revenants below: they climb past it every night following the blades taken to war. A stock foe, one of many. In person: huge pale tsuchigumo earth spider; gaunt; legs missing on one side; torn webs.

Speaking Style: Clicking in the dark, slow and patient, silk whispering. Tell since the war: its legs tremble at the sound of the revenants' climbing.
Combat Role: the web, then the drop
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: waits in the dark; drags weakly; fails
  • Quirk: pale as bone
