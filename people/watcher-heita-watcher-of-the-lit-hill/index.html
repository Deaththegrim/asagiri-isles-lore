Watcher Heita, Watcher of the Lit Hill

The watcher on Watchfire Hill after the war, twenty-one, who keeps the fire burning every night now: it means the line still holds. He is older than his years. In person: watcher; scarf; hunter's coat; a great fire burning beside him.

Home Ground: Watchfire, After the War at Watchfire Hill, The Lit Hill, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Fast, defensive and upset; he swears he lit it. Fast, defensive and upset, talking over himself to swear he lit the beacon. His voice climbs when doubted. Tell since the war: he gives the same hour every time now, and his voice does not climb.
Combat Role: none; he runs for the Hold.
Relationship Hook: Believe him and look at the footprint.

Personality

  • Core: checks the fire every hour; jumps at noises; keeps the footprint covered
  • Quirk: swears he lit it
