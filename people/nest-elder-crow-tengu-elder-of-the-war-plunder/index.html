Nest Elder Crow Tengu, Elder of the War Plunder

An old crow tengu guarding the hoard branches heavy with war plunder, the regent's seal now one prize among hundreds. A stock foe, one of many. In person: elder crow tengu; grey feathers gone white; a fine stolen sword; a general's war fan in her other claw.

Speaking Style: Clipped and cold. Clipped, cold and aristocratic, every word a coin counted. Tell since the war: she counts the hoard aloud and stops at the regent's seal every time.
Combat Role: a dive with the blade and the flock behind
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: watches from the top branch; bargains; never gives a crest back
  • Quirk: counts the hoard
