Priest Ujibe Kiyonari, The Searching Priest

The sun priest after the emperor's death, fifty-two, who has left the shrine's rites to his maiden and walks the isles looking for the true heir. He comes back to the shrine between journeys, thinner each time. In person: gentle shinto priest; shaved head under a travel hat; white robes muddied to the knee; a pilgrim's staff; kind, worried eyes.

Home Ground: Sheaf Hall, After the War at Sun-Rite Shrine of the Plain, Mourning Shrine, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Mild and kind, with a stammer when he doubts himself, which is often now. Gentle temple cadence, prayers worn smooth from repetition. Stammers on the word 'answered'. Tell since the war: he asks every child he meets their age and their mother's name before he blesses them.
Combat Role: a purifying rite and a prayer staff; he would rather run.
Relationship Hook: Tell him what the burned talisman means and he tells you what the priests fear about the emperor's death.

Personality

  • Core: stammers when unsure; blesses everyone who passes; counts the sheaves each evening
  • Quirk: prays with his eyes open now
