Boatwright Hanzaemon

A one-eyed boatwright, sixty, thick-handed, who works out of the ruined workshop because the light is good and nobody charges him rent. In person: sixty; thickset; grey cropped hair; a black eye-patch; a sawdust-covered work apron; forearms like ropes; an adze.

Home Ground: The Counter at The Workshop, on Tomoshibi Isle, the island off Saikai.
Speaking Style: Dry and blunt, a craftsman's contempt for bad work, a rare grin for good work. Blunt and dry, a craftsman's verdicts, a rare short grin for good work. Tell: when the cove comes up he lowers his voice and rubs the scar under the patch.
Combat Role: the adze's edge, then the mallet on the hand that held the knife.
Relationship Hook: Show him your hands and he will decide whether you are worth teaching how to fix the forge.

Personality

  • Core: judges a person by their hands; says exactly what is wrong with your work and nothing about what is right
  • Quirk: rubs the scar under his eye-patch when he is thinking
