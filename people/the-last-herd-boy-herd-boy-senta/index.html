Herd-Boy Senta, The Last Herd-Boy

A herd-boy of twelve who drives the village's four surviving oxen along the broken dikes and still sees everything on the plain. In person: thin boy with a stick; patched coat too big for him; a straw mask hung at his belt; flooded paddies behind.

Home Ground: Paddy Dikes, After the War at Hundred Fields Village, The Flooded Fields, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Quiet and serious, a boy who speaks to oxen more than people. Quiet and serious, few words, a click of the tongue to the oxen. Tell since the war: at the sound of a tax collector's bell he goes silent mid-word and looks at the dikes.
Combat Role: none; he hides among the oxen.
Relationship Hook: Ask him what he saw at night and he tells you about the shared fire.

Personality

  • Core: sits on dikes; sees everything; trusts no rider
  • Quirk: talks to oxen
