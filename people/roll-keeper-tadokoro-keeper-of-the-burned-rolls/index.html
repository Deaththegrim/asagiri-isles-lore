Roll-Keeper Tadokoro, Keeper of the Burned Rolls

The Council Hall's archivist after the siege, who saved half the rolls of retainers from the fire and lost the rest. He is rewriting the lost ones from memory. In person: dry middle-aged archivist; singed sleeves; a brush; a stack of new rolls.

Home Ground: Council Hall, After the War at Miyako Castle, The Blackened Keep, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Dry and exact, quoting rules by number. Tell since the war: he touches his singed sleeve before he reads any name from a rewritten roll.
Combat Role: none; he calls the guard.
Relationship Hook: Ask about House Ikoma's roll and he shows you the scraped-out name.

Personality

  • Core: never guesses; loves old ink; afraid of the regent's office
  • Quirk: dry and careful
