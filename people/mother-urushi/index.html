Mother Urushi

A warehouse owner on the foreign quay, fifty, heavy-lidded and draped in black lacquer jewellery, who rents storage to foreign captains and asks no questions. Porters call her Mother and do what she says. In person: heavy-lidded auctioneer; black hair; black lacquer jewellery; dark red and black kimono; a sliding lacquer box; sly.

Home Ground: The Black Warehouse at Foreign Quay Warehouses, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Husky and slow, every word priced; she calls everyone 'little one' and means it as a valuation. Husky, lazy vowels, a price at the end of every sentence. Taps a lacquer box with a fingernail as she talks. Tell: when something is truly valuable, she stops calling it 'little one' and names it.
Combat Role: she never fights; her porters close the doors and the foreign captains' men do the rest.
Relationship Hook: Bring her the runaway child and she pays more than the guild ever will; bring her a buyer's name and she trades you a box.

Personality

  • Core: taps her lacquer rings while she thinks; never sits facing a door she did not choose; remembers every price ever paid
  • Quirk: calls everyone 'little one'
