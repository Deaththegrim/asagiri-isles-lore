Amenbo the Skimmer, Amenbo of the Grey Mark

The wiry messenger after the war, who runs letters across the marsh faster than any boat, twice an hour now. The clan's children carry the grey mark, and she carries their cure. In person: young woman; wiry; short windswept hair; bright eyes; light courier clothes; a short sword; blurred with speed; a satchel of medicine jars.

Home Ground: Plank Walks, After the War at Kirisame Stilt Village, Village of the Grey Mark, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Quick and teasing, out of breath only when she laughs; she finishes other people's sentences. Quick and teasing, finishing other people's sentences, breathless only when laughing. Tell since the war: she slows to a walk when she passes a marked child, and nowhere else.
Combat Role: speed above all: she is beside, behind and in front of a foe before they turn.
Relationship Hook: Keep up with her for a morning.

Personality

  • Core: never stands still; counts steps under her breath; laughs when out of breath
  • Quirk: finishes your sentences
