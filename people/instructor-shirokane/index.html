Instructor Shirokane

The instructor of the Paper Familiar Workshop, forty, who teaches Bureau students to fold and wake paper cranes, warriors and hounds. His hands are always folding something. In person: gentle onmyoji instructor; short greying hair; kind tired eyes; white robes with paper cranes; folding paper.

Home Ground: Waking Circle at Paper Familiar Workshop, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Patient, warm and tired; he talks to paper as if it could hear. Patient, tired, a teacher's gentle repetition; speaks to paper familiars like children. Sighs fondly. Tell: when a familiar escapes, he talks faster and calls it by its pet name.
Combat Role: a flock of paper hounds.
Relationship Hook: Find the runaway familiar quietly and he teaches you to fold your own.

Personality

  • Core: talks to the familiars; covers for his students; keeps a crane in his collar
  • Quirk: always folding something
