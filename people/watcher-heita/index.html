Watcher Heita

The young watcher on Watchfire Hill, nineteen, who keeps the fire ready to warn the south of a raid. In person: anxious young watcher; scarf; hunter's coat; guarding a big unlit watchfire pile.

Home Ground: Watchfire at Watchfire Hill, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Fast, defensive and upset; he swears he lit it. Fast, defensive and upset, talking over himself to swear he lit the beacon. His voice climbs when doubted. Tell: when asked exactly when he lit it, he gives a different hour each time.
Combat Role: none; he runs for the Hold.
Relationship Hook: Believe him and look at the footprint.
