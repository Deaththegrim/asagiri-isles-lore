Admiral Mizuchi Naotora, The Admiral in the Shipyard

The admiral of House Mizuchi after the war, sixty-two, whose fleet burned at its moorings. He hides in the silent shipyard, gathering the captains who stayed loyal, and the house thinks him dead. In person: broad salt-grey admiral; beard grown out; a scorched dark blue coat; the river-dragon crest cut off and kept in his sleeve; stern, grieving eyes.

Home Ground: Admiral's Hall, After the War at Mizuchi Naval Anchorage, The Burned Fleet, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: A quarterdeck voice, big and level; he gives orders like weather reports. Quarterdeck projection, level as a calm sea, orders delivered like forecasts. Tell since the war: he touches the cut-off crest in his sleeve before he gives an order, and gives it quieter.
Combat Role: spear and grappling hook on a rolling deck; a fleet behind him.
Relationship Hook: Bring him the truth of the sleeping warship and he gives you a ship's passage anywhere.

Personality

  • Core: gives orders like weather reports; walks the shipyard at dawn; never shows grief
  • Quirk: keeps a model ship of every vessel he lost
