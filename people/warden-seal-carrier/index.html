Warden Seal-Carrier

A Vault Warden in grey furs over plate armour with a sealing chain and an iron seal-case on his back. Seal-carriers walk the glacier road carrying fresh seals north and kill anyone who tries to open the case. A stock foe, one of many. In person: vault warden; small iron box bound with chains and paper talismans strapped to his back.

Speaking Style: Cold and exact, counts and orders only. Tell: asked about the seals, he answers a different question.
Combat Role: a sealing chain that binds, then a short blade
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: counts markers aloud; speaks only to Wardens; kills quickly
  • Quirk: never sets the case down
