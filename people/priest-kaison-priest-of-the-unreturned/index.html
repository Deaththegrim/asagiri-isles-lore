Priest Kaison, Priest of the Unreturned

The priest of the temple after the war, fifty-two, who has hung so many carved ships that the rafters bow. In person: tattooed sailor-priest; shaved head; weathered face; dark robes; a carved ship in his hand.

Home Ground: Votive Hall, After the War at Temple of Safe Voyages, Temple of the Unreturned, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Calm and plain, a sailor's priest; he speaks to the dead and the living the same way. Plain, steady, a sailor's priest with salt in his vowels. Says the names of the drowned slowly, one at a time. Tell since the war: he reads the names of the drowned in the same voice he once kept for blessings, and the blessings have got shorter.
Combat Role: a monk's staff and a sailor's fists.
Relationship Hook: Bring him word of a lost ship and he blesses your voyages; bring him the truth about the three and he names the cargo.

Personality

  • Core: rings the bell himself; carves ships at night; speaks to the drowned by name
  • Quirk: rolls his sleeves up for work
