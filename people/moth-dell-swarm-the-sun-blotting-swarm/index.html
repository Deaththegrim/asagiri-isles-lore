Moth Dell Swarm, The Sun-Blotting Swarm

A cloud of black moths grown so large it blots the noon sun over the dell and has reached the pilgrims' road, smothering the penitents' prayers. A stock foe, one of many. In person: grudge swarm; a towering cloud of black moths; grey wing-dust falling like snow.

Speaking Style: A rustle of wings with whispers inside it, many small grudges at once. Tell since the war: it goes utterly still when a prayer begins, then rushes toward the voice.
Combat Role: a smothering cloud
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: silences; follows prayers; burns
  • Quirk: smothers
