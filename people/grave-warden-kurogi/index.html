Grave-Warden Kurogi

An old hunter who guards the graveyard's blade rows, where every fallen hunter's sword is planted over their grave. In person: elderly demon hunter walking rows of swords planted; lantern.

Home Ground: Blade Rows at Hunters' Graveyard, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Grave and courteous, an old hunter who greets every grave by name. Grave and courteous, greeting graves by name as he talks. Tell: when his students come up his courtesy goes stiff.
Combat Role: an old breath form, slower but sure.
Relationship Hook: Catch the grave-robber and he gives you his own blade.

Personality

  • Core: greets every grave; walks the rows at night; suspects a student
  • Quirk: polishes the blades
