Watchfire Yamauba

A crone at the watchfire who asks travellers to share the fire and a story. Watchfire yamauba keep the fire lit for the Hold and eat those who will not share. A stock foe, one of many. In person: yamauba; old crone with wild white hair.

Speaking Style: Cackling and warm, asking for a story. Tell: a selfish answer turns her voice cold.
Combat Role: a lunge across the fire
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: shares fire; eats the selfish; hates demons
  • Quirk: asks for stories
