Tokoyami, Lord of the Long Night, Lord of the Half-Empty Court

The demon lord of the Northern Court after the war, on his throne of black ice in a hall half empty: his nobles have gone south to the Daylight Valleys, and he keeps their seats in order of rank. In person: demon lord; tall, ancient, beautiful; long white hair; crimson eyes; a crown of black ice; black and crimson imperial robes; seated among empty thrones.

Home Ground: Hall of Ice Thrones, After the War at Court of the Long Night, The Court Turned South, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Slow, vast and courtly; every word lands like a verdict from very far away. He addresses mortals by their bloodline rather than their name. Tell since the war: he names the empty seats in order of rank before he answers anything.
Combat Role: he does not fight; the Court does, and when he moves the night itself closes.
Relationship Hook: Reach his hall with a message and he receives you with perfect courtesy.

Personality

  • Core: speaks like a verdict; keeps the old court's etiquette; never raises his voice
  • Quirk: calls the emperor 'my little brother'
