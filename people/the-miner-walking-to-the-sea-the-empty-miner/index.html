The Empty Miner, The Miner Walking to the Sea

A miner's body after the war, climbed from the Revenant Shaft with its lantern, walking with the others toward the beach yards. In person: soulless miner revenant; grey skin; empty eyes; mining clothes; a lantern; a line of revenants behind.

Home Ground: Shaft Head, After the War at Revenant Shaft, The Climbing Dead, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: A slow, empty echo; he says only what he did every day, over and over. A slow, empty echo, repeating what he did every day, over and over, in the same order. A pick strikes stone between his words. Tell since the war: it says only the beach's name now, over and over, between strikes of a pick that is not there.
Combat Role: it walks through you towards its blade.
Relationship Hook: Follow it and it leads you to its blade.

Personality

  • Core: repeats his work day; carries a lantern; does not fight unless stopped
  • Quirk: walks towards his blade
