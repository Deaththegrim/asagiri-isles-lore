Old Umbrella

A hundred-year-old paper umbrella with one eye and a long tongue that hops on its handle through the abandoned teahouses, greeting visitors like a host. In person: an old red paper umbrella with one big eye and a long tongue; hopping; mischievous.

Home Ground: Empty Teahouse at Haunted Teahouse Lane, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Creaky, courteous and very old-fashioned, like a host from a hundred years ago. Creaky, courteous host's formality from another century, welcoming every guest by rank. Creaks between sentences. Tell: when the lane is threatened, the courtesy cracks and it snaps shut.
Combat Role: a whirl of paper and a long tongue; the lane's objects join in.
Relationship Hook: Persuade the merchant to let the objects stay and Old Umbrella frees the workers.

Personality

  • Core: greets everyone as a host; sticks out its tongue at rudeness; remembers the teahouses' best days
  • Quirk: hops on its handle
