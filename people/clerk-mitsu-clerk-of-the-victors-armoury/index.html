Clerk Mitsu, Clerk of the Victors' Armoury

The armoury's clerk after the war, thirty-two, with record books stacked to the beams: harness for the Regent's peacekeeping companies, fitted by the hundred. In person: armoury clerk; neat bun coming loose; ink brush; towers of record books.

Home Ground: Fitting Room, After the War at Kurogane Armoury, The Armoury of the Victors, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, careful and frightened; she whispers numbers to herself to stay calm. She apologises for her handwriting. Tell since the war: she stops whispering numbers and reads the names of the war's buyers instead, very softly.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Protect her and she gives you the true records.

Personality

  • Core: whispers numbers; never meets Lady Sen's eyes; hides papers in harness linings
  • Quirk: keeps copies of everything
