Sukezo, The Forgiven Messenger

A Straw Mask messenger after the war, twenty-two, who runs between the camp and the villages as he always did. He looks older, and he no longer jokes about being hungry. In person: thin messenger; messy hair; freckles; straw mask with a cut cord; a message tube; a healed arrow wound on his shoulder.

Home Ground: Masked Fire Ring, After the War at Straw Mask Hill Camp, The Peasant Army's Camp, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Fast and friendly, too many questions, laughs nervously at his own jokes. Talks too fast and over his own sentences, laughing nervously at the ends. Tags a 'you know?' onto a sentence now and then when he is nervous. Tell since the war: he flinches whenever Ine's name is said kindly, and laughs too late to cover it.
Combat Role: runs; a sickle if cornered, badly.
Relationship Hook: Free his mother from the castle cell another way and he never says the name; push him and he breaks.

Personality

  • Core: asks too many questions; laughs at the wrong moments; eats whatever he is offered
  • Quirk: cannot look Ine in the eye
