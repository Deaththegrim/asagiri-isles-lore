Iwa the Net-Mender

The last net-mender on the island, fifty, broad-armed, who never left when the others did and mends nets for boats that no longer come. In person: middle-aged woman; fifty; broad-armed; weathered face; grey-streaked hair; a patched brown work kimono with tied sleeves; a net shuttle.

Home Ground: Hamlet Drying Racks at Fishers' Huts, on Tomoshibi Isle, the island off Saikai.
Speaking Style: Warm and teasing, a fishwife's bark softened by a laugh. Warm teasing bark, everything measured in arm-lengths of net, a laugh that rolls. Tell: near her son's name she changes the subject to the weather, too fast.
Combat Role: the net thrown low, then the gutting knife.
Relationship Hook: Help her carry the drying racks in before the rain and she will tell you who used to live in every hut.

Personality

  • Core: teases strangers until they laugh, then puts them to work; never mentions her son first
  • Quirk: measures everything in arm-lengths of net
