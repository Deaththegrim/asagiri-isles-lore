Shaft Revenant

A miner's body walking without its soul, lantern in hand. Revenants walk the shafts, following their blades. A stock foe, one of many. In person: soulless miner revenant with a lantern.

Speaking Style: An empty echo, the same few words of a miner's shift, over and over. A pick strikes stone. Tell: when someone says 'rest' it pauses.
Combat Role: it walks through you
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: follows its blade; does not feel; walks
  • Quirk: repeats its work
