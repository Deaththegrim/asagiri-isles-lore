Debt Factor Okura Sada

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

A debt factor of the Saikai guild, thirty-two, who collects from the houses that lost the war: horses, swords, land and, lately, daughters. In person: guild debt factor; neat hair; grey kimono with the guild crest; a ledger and abacus.

Home Ground: Money Counters, After the War at Guild Counting House, Seat of the Debt, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Calm and courteous, figures given gently, the voice of someone who has never once lost her temper. Gentle figures, courteous bows in her voice, never a raised word. Tell: asked to collect a daughter, she says the sum and nothing else.
Combat Role: never; the guild's ronin collect what she cannot.
Relationship Hook: Tell her what the Hiwada heir is worth to you and she may sell it, or keep it.

Personality

  • Core: never raises her voice; remembers every house's crest; pays her own way
  • Quirk: bows before she names a sum
