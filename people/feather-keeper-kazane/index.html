Feather-Keeper Kazane

The keeper of the shrine where tengu leave the swords of those they defeated, who polishes them and sings their names. In person: young shrine keeper polishing swords.

Home Ground: Sword Rack at Shrine of the Fallen Feather, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Light and lilting, a shrine keeper who sings the names of the swords on the rack. Light and lilting, singing sword names. Tell: at the oldest sword she stops singing and lowers her voice.
Combat Role: none; she calls the tengu.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the oldest sword and she lowers her voice.

Personality

  • Core: sings sword names; leaves sake for tengu; bows to the rack
  • Quirk: polishes daily
