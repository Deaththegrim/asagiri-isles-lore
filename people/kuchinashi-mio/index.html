Kuchinashi Mio

A Veil Court officer of the character's own intake, top of the ferry exams, who believes the character cut corners to stand level with her. In person: young woman; tall; short black hair; pale serious face; a black officer's robe; a white haori with a division mark; a long officer's blade.

Home Ground: Notice Board at Crossroads Gallows, Miyako Plain district, between meetings; a rival meets the character wherever the character is.
Speaking Style: Low and precise, a court officer's clipped reports, a dry joke delivered without a smile. Low, precise, clipped like a report, a dry joke with no smile. Tell: about her silent blade her voice drops to a near whisper.
Combat Role: ferry cut first, then whatever the bout demands; a veil officer rival who has studied the character.
Relationship Hook: She will quote the regulation you broke, and then ask how it felt.

Personality

  • Core: by the book, to the letter; keeps a ledger of every soul she ferries; never raises her voice
  • Quirk: straightens her collar when she is unsure
