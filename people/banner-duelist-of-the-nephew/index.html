Banner Duelist of the Nephew

A swaggering duelist in the Nephew's Banner colours with two swords and a reform pamphlet in his sash. Banner duelists pick fights at the fords to prove the nephew's swords are the best on the plain. A stock foe, one of many. In person: swaggering samurai duelist; two swords; a pamphlet.

Speaking Style: Loud and sure of himself. Loud and sure, reform speeches quoted mid-fight. Tell: beaten fairly, he goes quiet and bows lower than he must.
Combat Role: two swords in the dueling-yard form, a formal challenge first
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: challenges anyone armed; polishes both swords; respects a clean win
  • Quirk: recites reform speeches
