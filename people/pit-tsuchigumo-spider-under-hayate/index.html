Pit Tsuchigumo, Spider Under Hayate

A long-legged spider the size of a horse that answers Hayate's bells now, the bargain of the old clans finally made; it drops on anyone who comes without the village's signal. A stock foe, one of many. In person: tsuchigumo; giant long-legged spider with a tiger-striped body; a Hayate wind-charm wired to one leg.

Speaking Style: Clicking. Dry clicking in a quickening rhythm, a hiss of silk drawn out. Tell since the war: it flinches at every bell but one.
Combat Role: a drop from the web and the drag down
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: drops on signal; drags into the pit; obeys a bell
  • Quirk: waits under the web
