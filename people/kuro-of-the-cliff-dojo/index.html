Kuro of the Cliff Dojo

A crow-blooded tengu of the peak, who looks twenty-eight, with black wings, a black beak-mask pushed up on his head and a sneer. He runs the cliff dojo's first lessons: he insults every student, throws them off the peak, and waits to see who climbs back up. In person: crow tengu man; black feathered wings; messy black hair; a black beak-mask pushed up; sharp yellow eyes; dark yamabushi robes; sneering.

Home Ground: Cliff Dojo at Tengu Peak, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Dry, mocking and lazy-sounding; every compliment is an insult turned inside out. Lazy and dry, compliments delivered as insults, everything a little beneath him. He yawns at threats. Tell: when his mother is mentioned he goes completely silent and stops pretending to be bored.
Combat Role: wings and a straight sword, from above; he fights like a bored god until you surprise him.
Relationship Hook: He throws you off the peak; climb back.
Courtship Style: Insults you, throws you, waits; the day he catches you instead of letting you fall is the confession.
Jealousy Style: Throws the rival off the peak, slightly harder; claims it was the wind.

Personality

  • Core: lounges on the cliff edge; counts the climbs; keeps a feather from every student who made it
  • Quirk: insults every student
