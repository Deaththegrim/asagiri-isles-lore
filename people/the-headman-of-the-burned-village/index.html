The Headman of the Burned Village

A stooped oni of ash and cinders, a village headman's tall hat burned to its frame, embers glowing where his eyes were. He walks behind the penitents in grey, a few steps back, and his breath is smoke. He never strikes first. He follows any soldier in armour who walks the penitents' road, close enough to breathe on, and the soldiers he follows cough grey and sicken. Anyone who stands between him and a soldier he burns. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: stooped oni of ash and cinders; a village headman's tall hat burned to its frame; glowing ember eyes.

Home Ground: Ash Road, After the War at Grey Pilgrims' Road, The Penitents' Road, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: A headman's polite murmur, choked with smoke, reading names. A headman's polite murmur through smoke, every greeting formal. Tell: asked anything, he answers with a villager's name from the register.
Combat Role: the headman of the burned village opens the register
Relationship Hook: Learn what Jinza lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
