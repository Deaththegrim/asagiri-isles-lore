Captain Mori Sayo

Captain of Princess Ayame's sworn guard, thirty-five, a plain-born samurai woman with a naginata and a scar through one eyebrow. She stands outside the princess's screens day and night, and the court ladies find her rude. In person: female samurai guard captain; short black hair; a scar; stern eyes; dark armour; naginata; lotus lanterns.

Home Ground: Princess Ayame's Rooms at The Inner Palace, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Short orders and shorter answers; she softens only when she talks about the plain. Parade-ground economy: subject, verb, done. Never uses a title she does not have to. Tell: when she speaks of the plain or the princess her vowels lengthen and she almost smiles.
Combat Role: naginata, reach and corridors; she holds a doorway against anyone.
Relationship Hook: Show her you would take Ayame's side against the court and she lets you past the screens.

Personality

  • Core: stands with her back to the wall; calls the princess by name in private; checks the screens twice at night
  • Quirk: hates poetry
