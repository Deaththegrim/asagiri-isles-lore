Tengu Swordsman, Swordsman Above the War

A red-faced, long-nosed tengu who sat the war out on the peak and trained twice the students; he duels down at the houses' envoys who climb to beg for the court's help. A stock foe, one of many. In person: red-faced long-nosed winged mountain spirit; a straight sword; new student cords knotted on the hilt.

Speaking Style: Haughty and loud, a crag-top voice that insults and teaches in one line. Feathers rustle when it gestures. Tell since the war: he laughs like wind at the word war, every time.
Combat Role: sword and wind from above
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: duels from above; laughs at envoys; respects skill
  • Quirk: insults students
