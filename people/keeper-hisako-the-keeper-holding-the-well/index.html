Keeper Hisako, The Keeper Holding the Well

Keeper of the Grey Banner Waystation after the war, fifty-two, built like a door still, renting beds to penitent soldiers and throwing out anyone who brings a fight inside. She is weaker than she lets anyone see. In person: sturdy waystation keeper; cropped grey hair; a grey pallor; bandages on both forearms; grey banner apron.

Home Ground: Grey Banner Waystation, After the War at Haibara, On the Grudge Line, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Low, firm and tired; she talks like someone guarding a door. Low, firm, guarding-a-door cadence; repeats the rules of the waystation. Tell since the war: she grips the doorframe while she lays down the rules, and her knuckles go grey.
Combat Role: a heavy bar and the waystation's guests.
Relationship Hook: Find another way to feed the sealed well and she owes you her life.

Personality

  • Core: guards the door herself; never goes near the square at night; binds her forearm
  • Quirk: counts the guests twice
