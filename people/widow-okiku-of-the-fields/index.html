Widow Okiku of the Fields

A farmer's widow who works her husband's paddy alone and cooks for the headman's house for rice. In person: tired young widow; rice fields outside.

Home Ground: Headman's House at Hundred Fields Village, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Soft and tired, a widow who apologises for the state of a house that is spotless. Soft and tired, apologies stacked one on another. Tell: showing the receipt, her voice steadies and hardens.
Combat Role: none; a hoe if cornered.
Relationship Hook: Ask about her husband and she shows you the paid receipt.

Personality

  • Core: apologises for everything; works till dark; keeps a receipt in her sleeve
  • Quirk: keeps a spotless house
