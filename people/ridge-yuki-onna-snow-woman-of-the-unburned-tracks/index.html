Ridge Yuki-onna, Snow Woman of the Unburned Tracks

A pale woman in a white kimono by the cairn whose red hunter's cord has been cut; the Court's army went south over her ridge, and she freezes the thralls who still cross it at dawn. A stock foe, one of many. In person: yuki-onna; pale woman in a white kimono; long black hair; the cut red hunter's cord tied round her wrist.

Speaking Style: A whisper with frost in it, every question ending on a held breath. Tell since the war: she asks your name, then which way the army went.
Combat Role: a whiteout, then her breath
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: stands by the cairn; freezes thralls at dawn; weeps ice
  • Quirk: asks names
