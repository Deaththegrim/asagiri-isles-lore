Lantern-Market Go-Between Konta, Go-Between of the Last Open Gate

The lantern market's go-between after the war, who keeps the crossing's board where humans and yokai post bounties on each other, busier than ever. In person: half-tanuki go-between; round ears; striped tail; a soldier's hat among his changing hats.

Home Ground: Lantern Market of the Wood, After the War at Kitsunebi Crossing, The Last Open Gate, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Fast, friendly, half a laugh in every sentence; he speaks to yokai and humans in the same breath. Fast and friendly, a laugh tucked inside every sentence, switching from human talk to yokai cadence mid-phrase. Tell since the war: he laughs too long more often now, because there are more warnings to hide.
Combat Role: a puff of leaves and a tanuki's sprint.
Relationship Hook: Ask him who he warns and he grins and asks what it is worth.

Personality

  • Core: laughs mid-sentence; changes his hat often; talks to lanterns
  • Quirk: warns friends
