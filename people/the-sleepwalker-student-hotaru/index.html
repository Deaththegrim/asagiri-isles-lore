Student Hotaru, The Sleepwalker

A Bureau student after the war, eighteen, who has walked in his sleep as far as the inner ward wall. He is tied by the wrist at night with the others. In person: young onmyoji student; messy hair; sleepy eyes; white robes; rope marks on one wrist.

Home Ground: Sleeping Rows, After the War at Bureau Dormitories, Dormitories of the Dreamers, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Earnest, high and quick; at night he speaks slowly in a voice that is not quite his. High, eager, questions on top of questions. Scratches notes as he talks. Tell since the war: by day he answers to his name a moment late, as if listening to another one first.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Follow him on a sleepwalk and you see what the hare wants.

Personality

  • Core: studies by candle; breaks curfew; talks in his sleep
  • Quirk: draws hares in his margins
