Lady Suzuran of Favours

A lady of the Hidden Court whose neck is longer than it should be, who sits at the Favour Table weighing what is owed. A favour from her opens any door in the Court; a favour owed to her closes several. In person: adult female; elegant; long black hair; a neck visibly too long and graceful; pale skin; layered court robes; seated; bored.

Home Ground: The Favour Table at The Court Proper, The Hidden Court, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Cultured, slow and faintly bored; every sentence is a transaction and she makes sure you notice. Cultured, slow and faintly bored, every sentence a transaction she makes sure you notice. Tell: a challenge answered without a cut and her voice warms for exactly one sentence.
Combat Role: none she admits to; her favours fight for her.
Relationship Hook: Answer a challenge in the Court without drawing and she writes you a favour; spend it wisely and she offers another.

Personality

  • Core: weighs favours on a small scale; bored by coin; writes every favour twice
  • Quirk: her neck lengthens when interested
