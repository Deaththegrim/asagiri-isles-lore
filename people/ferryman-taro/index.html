Ferryman Taro

The ferryman at Black Sand Beach, twenty-five, who lands the ferries from the main isle in rough weather and knows every passenger's business. In person: cheerful ferryman; headband; wet clothes.

Home Ground: Black Sand Beach: Ferry Landing at Black Sand Beach, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Cheerful, quick and nosy; he talks about the weather and everyone's luggage and guesses what's inside. He whistles at the oars. Tell: when cargo is heavier than it should be, he stops whistling and asks no more questions.
Combat Role: an oar.
Relationship Hook: He shows the crate's papers to someone he trusts.

Personality

  • Core: talks about luggage; hides things under his boat; laughs at storms
  • Quirk: reads the sea
