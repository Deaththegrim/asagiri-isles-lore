Webbed Pit Omukade

A giant man-eating centipede, long enough to coil the gully, with plates that turn a sword. Omukade hunt people and great serpents; even the earth spiders keep away from this one. A stock foe, one of many. In person: no humans; omukade; enormous segmented centipede with dozens of legs and dark armoured plates.

Speaking Style: A clicking rumble, a hundred legs in every pause. Tell: at a spitting sound it rears back.
Combat Role: a coil and a venom bite; spit on the point
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: clicks; hunts; hates spit
  • Quirk: coils
