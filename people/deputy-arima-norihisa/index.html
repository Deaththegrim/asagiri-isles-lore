Deputy Arima Norihisa

The Treasury's deputy secretary, forty, a quiet man with ink on his cuffs who stands one step behind the magistrate at every audit and carries her seal case. In person: thin court clerk; neat topknot; ink-stained cuffs; plain grey court robes; a lacquered seal case.

Home Ground: Council Hall at Miyako Castle, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Mild and helpful, a clerk's voice; he agrees with everyone and remembers what they said. Mild and agreeable, a clerk's murmur, now and then turns your last word back to you as a question. Tell: lying, he agrees a beat too soon and smooths his cuffs.
Combat Role: never his own hand; a knife from a man he paid.
Relationship Hook: Catch him with the seal case open and he offers the party a face of their own before he offers a name.

Personality

  • Core: agrees first, thinks later; keeps the magistrate's seal case on his own knee; never stands in a doorway
  • Quirk: counts in a whisper
