Master Chizuru, The Scout Who Opened the Road

A Breath Master, forty-two, who rides the Snow Road openly north now, her scarf gone and her teeth showing; the Hold names her traitor and her scouts hunt her. In person: turned breath master; dark hair loose; grey pale skin; red-rimmed eyes; a hunter's black coat with the Hold's crest cut away; a bow.

Home Ground: Snow Road Post, After the War at Asahi Gate, Under the Broken Wall, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Low and patient, a hunter who wastes no word. Low and patient, a scout's few words, muffled by her scarf. Tell since the war: she lowers the scarf that is no longer there, an old habit with nothing to cover.
Combat Role: arrows from the snow, then the hunters' forms up close.
Relationship Hook: Ride north with her and the party sees where the patrol rolls go.

Personality

  • Core: rides north openly; eats in no mess now; still knows every scout's family
  • Quirk: keeps her scarf over her mouth
