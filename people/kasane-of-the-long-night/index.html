Kasane of the Long Night

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

A ranked demon of the Northern Court, sworn blade of its envoy, who stands at the Asahi Gate's snow road post under the truce banner while the hunters stare at her. Pale, very still, a long sword across her back in a black scabbard. In person: adult woman; demon noble guard; very pale skin; long silver-black hair tied high; red eyes; two small dark horns; black and white court armour; a long sword.

Home Ground: Snow Road Post, After the War at Asahi Gate, Under the Broken Wall, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Cold and courteous, every word chosen; she never raises her voice and never lowers her guard. Slow, formal court speech, each courtesy complete. Tell: when she is hungry the courtesies get longer and her sentences end before the verb.
Combat Role: one long draw-cut from stillness, cold blood frosting the blade.
Relationship Hook: She is the only demon in the south under a truce banner, and she will answer any courteous question once.
Courtship Style: Courteous first and coldly distant; the first real gift is a question about you she did not need to ask.
Jealousy Style: Grows more formal and more polite, until the courtesy is a blade; then asks, once, if she frightens you.

Personality

  • Core: never sits in a hunter's presence; answers questions with courtesies; keeps a southern flower pressed in her sleeve
  • Quirk: watches the sun as if it might bite
