Coast Fort Deserter, Deserter Who Walked Home

A soldier of the abandoned fort who sold his share of the last powder and walked home to find no home; he squats on the sea wall now with the smugglers, robbing the coast road in a cuirass with no crest at all. A stock foe, one of many. In person: deserter foot soldier; a rusted cuirass with no crest; a matchlock with no powder; a fisherman's straw cape.

Speaking Style: Tired and bitter. Tired, bitter and low, a soldier's clipped words with the courtesy worn off. He sighs before he shoots. Tell since the war: he still scrapes at a crest that is no longer there.
Combat Role: a volley from the wall, then spears
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: jumps at drums; robs the coast road; has no home to go back to
  • Quirk: scrapes crests off
