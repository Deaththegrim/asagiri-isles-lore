Kazakiri Daigo

The Kazakiri clan's heir, twenty-five, the best wind-blade in Hayate: tall, scarred, proud and furious. He wants open war with Kagegi to settle a hundred years of insults and treats every outsider as a piece in that war. In person: tall proud wind shinobi; wild dark hair; a scar; fierce eyes; dark blue shinobi gear with a Kazakiri crest; iron war fans; intense.

Home Ground: Wolf Pens at Hayate Ridge Village, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Hard, proud and hot-tempered; he speaks in challenges, and in private, rarely, in questions. Hard challenges in a proud cadence, hot-tempered bursts that end in a demand. He cracks his neck before a duel. Tell: in private he asks questions quietly and listens to the answers, which he never does in public.
Combat Role: iron fans throwing wind blades from range, then up close, hard.
Relationship Hook: Challenge him and he respects you; make him choose his clan over his grudge and he follows you.
Courtship Style: Challenges you, then spars with you, then asks your advice in private and denies it in public.
Jealousy Style: Challenges the rival to a duel, in front of everyone; afterwards, alone, asks you plainly where he stands.

Personality

  • Core: sharpens his fans at night; feeds the wolves himself; never says his father's name
  • Quirk: speaks in challenges
