Hot Spring Ronin

A ronin with her sword in the bath beside her who takes strangers for the Kurogane's hired killers. She draws on anyone who comes too close in the steam. A stock foe, one of many. In person: ronin woman with wet hair and a towel; sword within reach; steaming hot spring bath; wary.

Speaking Style: Clipped and wary, short sentences, an ear always on the door. Tell: at the clank of metal her voice snaps to a whisper.
Combat Role: a draw from the steam
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: watches the door; jumpy at metal sounds; laughs when safe
  • Quirk: sword always within reach
