Magistrate's Scribe Oriha, Scribe of Three Notice Boards

Scribe of the capital's notice boards after the war, twenty-seven, who writes the Regent's bounties, the guild's debt notices and the names of the hanged in the same fine hand. In person: young woman; court scribe; ink up to the wrist; hair cut short; pale blue official kimono gone grey at the hem.

Home Ground: Riverside Post House, After the War at Miyako, Besieged, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Precise court Japanese with a scribe's patience; she corrects grammar mid-threat. Precise and patient, she pauses to correct a word mid-sentence and goes on. Tell since the war: she lowers her voice for the names of the hanged, as if they could still hear her read them.
Combat Role: none; she raises her voice for the magistrate's guards.
Relationship Hook: Ask her about the names on the regent's bounties and she goes quiet, then asks if you can keep a secret.

Personality

  • Core: corrects your grammar; writes while she talks; bows exactly as deep as rank demands
  • Quirk: keeps copies of everything
