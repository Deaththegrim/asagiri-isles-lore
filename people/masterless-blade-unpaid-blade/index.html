Masterless Blade, Unpaid Blade

A ronin whose house lost the war and never paid him, hiring out from the Hall of Unpaid Blades for whatever the board offers. Unpaid blades guard envoys, collect war debts and rob anyone who looks like a winner. A stock foe, one of many. In person: ronin swordsman; torn straw hat; a faded kimono with a house crest cut away; two swords, one chipped.

Speaking Style: Dry, bored, every word a price. Dry and bored; every word sounds like a price. Ronin size you up aloud. Tell since the war: he names his dead house's crest when he bows, then remembers and does not finish the bow.
Combat Role: the draw, then a road-fighter's dirty work
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: asks who is paying first and twice; trusts no house; drinks before jobs now
  • Quirk: sizes you up
