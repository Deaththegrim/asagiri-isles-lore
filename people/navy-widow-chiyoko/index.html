Navy Widow Chiyoko

A navy officer's widow, thirty-six, who runs the silent shipyard's kitchen and its message runners for the admiral nobody knows is alive. In person: navy widow; hair; dark blue kimono; sleeves tied back.

Home Ground: Shipyard, After the War at Mizuchi Naval Anchorage, The Burned Fleet, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Calm and exact, a navy wife's voice used to giving orders when the men are at sea. Calm, exact orders in a navy wife's voice. Tell: when the bought captains are named she straightens something that is already straight.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Help her give the silver back and she will tell the admiral the truth.

Personality

  • Core: keeps the kitchen fire low; counts the loyal captains; sends runners by the reed paths
  • Quirk: never mentions her husband
