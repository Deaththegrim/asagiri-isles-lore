Survivor Fuki, The Survivor Who Came Back

The girl from the well of Kitamura after the war, thirteen, who came out, went south, and came back to look for her mother among the ash. In person: thin girl; ash-smudged face; a hunter's cut-down coat; a wooden sword.

Home Ground: Old Well, After the War at Burned Village of Kitamura, The First of Many, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: A whisper, slow and frightened; she says the same thing again and again. A frightened whisper that loops back to the same few words. She flinches at sudden sounds and starts over. Tell since the war: she no longer screams the name; she says it once, clearly, and waits to see who believes her.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Bring her safely to the Hold and she tells the commander what she saw.

Personality

  • Core: hides from tall men; clutches a burned doll; will not sleep in the dark
  • Quirk: repeats herself
