Hill Bandit

A hill bandit in a patched cloak with a bow and a knife, face smeared with soot. Hill bandits rob the old road and cut the bridge ropes behind them. A stock foe, one of many. In person: hill bandit; soot-smeared face; patched cloak; a short bow.

Speaking Style: Sharp and hungry, short demands and no patience. Hill bandits whistle signals from the rocks. Tell: when food is offered, the threat softens immediately.
Combat Role: a bow from the rocks, then gone
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: knows every path; cuts ropes behind her; spares children
  • Quirk: shoots first
