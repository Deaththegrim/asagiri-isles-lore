Notice-Keeper Kagura, Keeper of the Debt Board

Keeper of the harbour post's notice board after the war, thirty-two, who posts bounties and debt notices side by side. The guild, the Regent's office and the temple all pay her to post. In person: harbour notice-keeper; ink-stained fingers; unlit long pipe; a mourning ribbon on the pipe.

Home Ground: Harbour Post, After the War at Saikai, The Debt Harbour, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Brisk and amused, a harbour voice that prices everything; she rounds every number up. Quick harbour patter, numbers rounded up with a wink, the unlit pipe clicking on her teeth between lines. Tell since the war: she taps the mourning ribbon on her pipe before she names a price for a death.
Combat Role: none; she ducks under the counter and blows the harbour whistle.
Relationship Hook: Bring back proof of a bounty and she shows you the back board.

Personality

  • Core: taps the unlit pipe; never forgets a debt; likes hunters who come back
  • Quirk: reads notices aloud in a sing-song
