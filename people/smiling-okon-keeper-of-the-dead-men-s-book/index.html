Smiling Okon, Keeper of the Dead Men's Book

The debt-book keeper of the Soldiers' Den after the war, forty-two, smiling still, who buys dead soldiers' debts cheap and collects from their widows. In person: cheerful den keeper; round smiling face; bright red kimono; a debt book with a black cord now.

Home Ground: Dice Room, After the War at Lantern Row Gambling Den, Soldiers' Den, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Bright, giggling and merciless; she sings the numbers of your debt like a children's rhyme. Giggling and sing-song, counting debts like a nursery rhyme. Now and then closes a threat with 'isn't that nice?', never twice in a scene. Tell since the war: at the Mizuchi name her giggle stops a moment too long before it starts again.
Combat Role: never; her bouncers and the guild collect.
Relationship Hook: Win at her table honestly and she likes you; buy the captain's son's debt and you own a Mizuchi heir.

Personality

  • Core: giggles at bad rolls; never gambles; keeps a debt book with a red cord
  • Quirk: sings debts like nursery rhymes
