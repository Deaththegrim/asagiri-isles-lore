Mess-Hall Keeper Tetsuji, Keeper of the Double Bell

Keeper of the hunters' mess after the war, a one-armed veteran who posts the Hold's bounties and the names of the dead on the same board. Refugees sleep between his tables. In person: one-armed veteran; scarred face; fur-trimmed coat; a bounty board crowded with names.

Home Ground: Hunters' Mess, After the War at Asahi Gate, Under the Broken Wall, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: A quartermaster's bark that has shouted over storms; gentler with the young. A storm-trained bark, gentler for the young, the stump of his arm tapping the board. Tell since the war: he taps the board twice for each new name, once for the hunter and once for the refugee who took their bed.
Combat Role: a hunter's blade in his one hand, still fast.
Relationship Hook: End the night hunter on the pass and he gives you everything he saved.

Personality

  • Core: barks orders; taps the board with his stump; never sits with his back to the door
  • Quirk: feeds the young first
