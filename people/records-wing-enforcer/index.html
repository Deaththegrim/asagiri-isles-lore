Records Wing Enforcer

A Records wing enforcer in a white coat with a black ribbon at the collar and a ledger chained to his wrist. Enforcers collect the souls the Records wing has listed and bind anyone who argues with the list. A stock foe, one of many. In person: veil court enforcer; a ledger chained to his wrist; among moorland graves.

Speaking Style: Even and bureaucratic, names read without looking. Tell: at a child's name his reading stumbles.
Combat Role: a binding ledger read aloud, then the blade
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: taps the ledger; never argues; flinches at children's names
  • Quirk: reads names without looking
