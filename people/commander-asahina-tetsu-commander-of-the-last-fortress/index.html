Commander Asahina Tetsu, Commander of the Last Fortress

Commander of the Breath Hunters after the war, forty-seven, iron-grey and more scarred, holding the north's last fortress with children in cut-down uniforms. Her map of the north has more black pins than white. In person: scarred breath hunter commander; cropped grey hair gone white at the temples; hard eyes; dark uniform with a sunrise haori; a sun-steel sword; a letter in her sleeve.

Home Ground: Commander's Tower, After the War at Hinode Fortress, The Last Fortress, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Low, steady and hard as frozen ground; she gives orders as if she has already counted the dead. Low and steady, a voice already counting the dead before the fight begins. She speaks in orders even when asking for tea. Tell since the war: she touches the letter in her sleeve before any order about the valleys, and the order comes later.
Combat Role: Stone Breath forms, immovable, and a sun-steel cut nothing survives.
Relationship Hook: Go north for her and ask the question she cannot, and she trains you.

Personality

  • Core: counts the dead before the battle; trains recruits herself; never sleeps on Demon Nights
  • Quirk: keeps a child's wooden sword on her desk
