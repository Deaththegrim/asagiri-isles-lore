Priestess Shirane

The keeper of the Ice Shrine of the White Stag, ageless and pale, who has tended the carvings in the ice for longer than the exiles remember. In person: ageless pale priestess; long silver hair; white robes; dreamy.

Home Ground: Antler Hall at Ice Shrine of the White Stag, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Distant, soft and slow; she speaks of the stag as if it were listening from the next room. Her voice echoes a little in the shrine. Tell: when the stag is threatened her calm turns to cold command.
Combat Role: none; the shrine's cold.
Relationship Hook: Read the carvings with her and learn how the stag was bound.

Personality

  • Core: never leaves the shrine; traces the carvings; smells of snow
  • Quirk: speaks to the stag
