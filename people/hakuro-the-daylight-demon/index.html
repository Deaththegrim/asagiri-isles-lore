Hakuro the Daylight Demon

A demon of the Northern Court who walks in daylight: tall, pale, handsome, in a dark travelling cloak, with eyes like frost on iron. Hunters who meet him on the pass in daylight are let go, which frightens them more than anything else on the pass. In person: tall pale handsome man; long black hair; frost-pale eyes; faint dark markings; dark travelling cloak; calm menace.

Home Ground: Pass Floor at Frozen Pass of the Long Night, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Soft, cultured and amused, with a cold rumble under it; he speaks like a guest who knows the house. Cultured and amused, with a cold rumble under the polish. He savours sentences like wine and names the year of each memory. Tell: when his sister is mentioned the rumble deepens and he stops smiling.
Combat Role: courteous until the moment he is not; frost and claws, then gone.
Relationship Hook: He lets you go in daylight and asks you to carry a message to his sister.

Personality

  • Core: speaks of his sister with fondness; never kills where she can see; keeps a child's wooden sword's twin
  • Quirk: lets hunters go in daylight
