Emperor Kiyohito

The emperor of the Asagiri Isles, eighty-one, last of the old line on the throne. For a year he has been seen only by his physicians and the Grand Chamberlain; the court says he is ill and resting, and every decree that leaves the palace carries his seal. In person: emperor; very old and frail; white hair loose; closed eyes; pale silk sleeping robes with a chrysanthemum crest; talismans pinned to the canopy; incense smoke.

Home Ground: The Sleeping Chamber at The Inner Palace, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: When he speaks at all, a whisper from a long way off, words from old poems. Breath-thin, with long gaps between words, each one carried as if it weighed too much. Murmurs half-lines of old court poetry and a woman's name. Tell: on the name, the whisper steadies and sounds young.
Combat Role: none; he has not lifted a hand in a year.
Relationship Hook: Reach his bedside and the court's whole lie is in front of you; what you do with it moves every claimant.

Personality

  • Core: sleeps; murmurs fragments of old poems; turns towards the sound of rain
  • Quirk: calls a woman's name in the night
