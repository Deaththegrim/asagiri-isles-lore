War Widow Sakiko of Kagegi

A Kagegi shinobi's widow, twenty-nine, who took her husband's place on the mission board and asks for the hardest Hayate missions. In person: young woman; kunoichi widow; short black hair; dark gear with a white mourning cord; sharpening a kunai.

Home Ground: Mission Hall, After the War at Kagegi Village, Burned at the Edge, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Flat and clipped, a shinobi's report about her own grief. A flat shinobi report about her own grief. Tell: when the elders are named she adds one word too many, on purpose.
Combat Role: kunai and shadow, at night.
Relationship Hook: Bring her proof of the Regent's contract and she will strike the Regent's house for you.

Personality

  • Core: sharpens his kunai, not hers; never speaks his name; watches the elders
  • Quirk: takes the hardest missions
