Cave Wreck-Raider

A salt-scarred raider in a sharkskin vest with a boarding axe and a coil of wet rope. Wreck-raiders loot what the reef breaks and cut the throats of anyone who saw the ship go down. A stock foe, one of many. In person: scarred sea raider; sharkskin vest; boarding axe; wet rope coil; sea cave mouth.

Speaking Style: Hoarse and amused. Hoarse, amused, salt in every syllable, laughing through her teeth. She whistles signals between lines. Tell: hurt, the laugh goes and the voice drops flat and fast.
Combat Role: snare and axe in the surf
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: grins with gold teeth; keeps a dead captain's ring; never fights uphill
  • Quirk: tests the tide
