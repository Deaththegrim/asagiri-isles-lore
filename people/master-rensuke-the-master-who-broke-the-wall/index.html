Master Rensuke, The Master Who Broke the Wall

A turned Breath Master, fifty-two, who opened the North Gate from inside the night the Wall broke, and stands at the Open Pass now in the north's colours; he no longer avoids the noon sun because he never sees it. In person: older man; turned breath master; grey-white hair; grey skin; red eyes; a Northern Court cloak over his old hunter's coat; a long blade.

Home Ground: Sunrise Wall, After the War at Asahi Gate, Under the Broken Wall, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Calm and sparing; a teacher's voice that never warms. Calm and sparing, a teacher's measured voice with no warmth in it, long pauses with no breath. Tell since the war: he calls the young hunters by name and corrects their stance before he strikes.
Combat Role: the hunters' forms, colder and faster than any living master.
Relationship Hook: Sit with him through a night watch and the party hears him not breathe.

Personality

  • Core: drills from the other side; speaks of the north as home; never sees the noon
  • Quirk: drills at dawn and never at noon
