Lieutenant Kozu Matabei

Captain Nagai's lieutenant, thirty-one, who fled the falling Cedar Pass barrier with its treasury and bought a seat at Kurokami's table with it. In person: young man; nervous soldier; untidy hair; a soldier's coat with the crest cut off; a heavy money box.

Home Ground: Mother's Log, After the War at Red Lantern Roost, The Federation's Fort, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Nervous and quick, laughing at his own cleverness, never quite meeting anyone's eye. Quick and nervous, laughing at his own lies before he tells them. Tell: at Nagai's name the laugh stops and he says 'he ordered it', and then denies it.
Combat Role: a short sword and the nearest door.
Relationship Hook: Tell him Nagai would forgive him and he will give the treasury back to the pass.

Personality

  • Core: counts the treasury every night; never goes near the pass; drinks to Nagai alone
  • Quirk: laughs at his own lies
