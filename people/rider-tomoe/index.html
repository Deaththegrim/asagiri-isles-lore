Rider Tomoe

A post rider of the Wardens, twenty-two, quick and fearless, who carries mail and dispatches the length of the Fifty Stations Road on a grey mare called Fog. In person: post rider; short hair; bright eyes; Warden's blue riding coat; a dispatch bag; energetic.

Home Ground: Rider Yard at Road Commissioner's Office, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Brisk, bright and breathless, talking like a rider giving a report. Breathless reports, sentences clipped by the gallop, every message in order: who, where, how many. Clicks her tongue to an imagined horse. Tell: when she is scared she reports faster.
Combat Role: a short bow at a gallop, then away.
Relationship Hook: Ride the dangerous stretch with her at dusk and she shows you what she saw.

Personality

  • Core: reports in a breathless rush; talks to her horse; keeps a dead rider's whistle
  • Quirk: never rides between two particular stations at dusk
