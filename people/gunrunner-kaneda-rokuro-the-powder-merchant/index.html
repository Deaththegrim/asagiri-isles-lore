Gunrunner Kaneda Rokuro, The Powder Merchant

A foreign-quay broker, forty-seven, who got the war he wanted and sold powder to every side of it; he wears silk under the oilcloth now and keeps two bodyguards. In person: weathered arms broker; grey-shot short beard; silk under an oilcloth coat; a pair of foreign pistols in his sash; a powder burn on one cheek.

Home Ground: Foreign Quay, After the War at Saikai, The Debt Harbour, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Dry and amused, a man who has heard every threat twice. Dry, amused, unhurried, every threat answered with a small laugh through the nose. Tell since the war: he still laughs at threats, but only after looking at the door first.
Combat Role: one pistol shot, then the water.
Relationship Hook: Bring him a better buyer than the rain clan's war party and he sells the party the next crate's route.

Personality

  • Core: smells of powder; pays in foreign coin; sleeps in a different room each night
  • Quirk: laughs at threats
