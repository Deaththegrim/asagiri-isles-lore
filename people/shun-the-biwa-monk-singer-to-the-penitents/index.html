Shun the Biwa Monk, Singer to the Penitents

The blind biwa monk after the war, sixty-two, singing the old capital's fall to soldiers on the penitents' road. They listen now. In person: blind biwa monk; shaved head; closed eyes; dark robes; a lute with a new string.

Home Ground: Ash Road, After the War at Grey Pilgrims' Road, The Penitents' Road, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: A storyteller's chant, slow and rhythmic; he speaks in verses of the old war. A storyteller's chant, slow and rhythmic, speaking in verses of the old war with a pluck of the biwa between lines. He never says 'I'; he says 'this monk'. Tell since the war: when this monk sings the last verse, he stops plucking and lets the road's quiet finish it.
Combat Role: his staff, and the dead stop to listen.
Relationship Hook: Feed him and hear the whole tale, last verse and all.

Personality

  • Core: tunes his lute when nervous; hears footsteps a mile off; never sings the last verse twice
  • Quirk: speaks in verses
