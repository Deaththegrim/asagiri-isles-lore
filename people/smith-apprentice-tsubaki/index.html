Smith Apprentice Tsubaki

A smith's apprentice in the waking hall, nineteen, who pumps the bellows and asks where the soul-blades come from. In person: young blunt smith apprentice pumping bellows; soot; curious eyes.

Home Ground: Waking Hall at Forge Guild Hall, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Bright and blunt, an apprentice who asks the questions the masters will not answer. Bright and blunt, questions fired between bellows pumps. Tell: about the deep forges she whispers for the first time.
Combat Role: a smith's hammer and a lot of nerve.
Relationship Hook: Ask her about the deep forges and she tells you what she saw.

Personality

  • Quirk: pumps bellows
