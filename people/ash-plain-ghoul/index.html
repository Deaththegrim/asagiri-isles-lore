Ash Plain Ghoul

A grey, ash-caked ghoul walking in a line with others at dusk. Ghouls walk the Ash Plain in packs and are walking towards Haibara. A stock foe, one of many. In person: ashfall ghoul; grey ash-caked ghoul.

Speaking Style: A groan with a chewing rhythm, words dragged out of a dry throat. It sniffs between groans. Tell: at sunlight it shrieks and the groan breaks.
Combat Role: grasping packs
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: moans; never stops; breaks in sunlight
  • Quirk: walks in lines
