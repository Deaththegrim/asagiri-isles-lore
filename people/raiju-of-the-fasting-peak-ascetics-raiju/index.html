Ascetics' Raiju, Raiju of the Fasting Peak

A wolf of lightning grown restless while the hermits fast to hold the beast under the temple; with no rice left for it, it prowls the chain path in every storm. A stock foe, one of many. In person: raiju; crackling wolf-shaped beast of blue lightning; ribs of sparks showing; scorched rice-offering bowls hung on its neck.

Speaking Style: Thunder in a throat. Thunder caught in a throat, crackling snarls, a sharp electric snap before each leap. Tell since the war: its crackle drops to a whine whenever it smells rice.
Combat Role: a leap of lightning at the nearest steel
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: prowls the chain path; leaps at metal; still fears the drum
  • Quirk: crackles
