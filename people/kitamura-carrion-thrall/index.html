Kitamura Carrion Thrall

A thrall in a looted hunter's coat picking through the ash street, red eyes behind a scrap of cloth. Carrion thralls come after a raid, never with it, to strip the dead and sell what they find north. A stock foe, one of many. In person: blood-sworn thrall; picking.

Speaking Style: A muttering scavenger. A scavenger's mutter, half to himself, cloth rustling as he digs. Tell: in sunlight his voice shrinks to a hiss.
Combat Role: a looted blade from the ash, then a run for the dark
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: talks to himself; wears dead men's coats; flees sunlight
  • Quirk: picks through ash
