Headman Mokichi, The Third Party's Man

Headman of the Woodcutters' Hamlet after the war, fifty-two, who pays both villages in timber for the hamlet's life. He did not flee; he could not. In person: nervous woodcutter headman; thinner; rough work clothes; the travel bag still packed by the door.

Home Ground: Log Yard, After the War at Woodcutters' Hamlet, The Caught Hamlet, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Nervous, hearty and sly; he talks about timber to change the subject. Nervous heartiness and too much talk about timber, prices and weather. He laughs at the end of every sentence like a full stop. Tell since the war: he no longer laughs at the end of sentences; he glances at the packed bag by the door.
Combat Role: an axe and his woodcutters.
Relationship Hook: Keep both villages off him and he sells you news instead.

Personality

  • Core: talks about timber; sweats; counts logs
  • Quirk: has packed a bag
