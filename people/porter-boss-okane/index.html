Porter Boss Okane

The porters' new boss, thirty-eight, broad as a door, who took up Big Ushi's carrying pole when he died bringing the wounded off the road. The lodge carries for the army now, for half the old rates. In person: broad porter woman; muscular arms; headband; short sleeves; a huge carrying pole; porters' lodge.

Home Ground: Bunk Room, After the War at Porters' Lodge, Porters for the Army, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Big-voiced and cheerful, a carrier's breath in every sentence, calls everyone by what they can lift. Big and breathless, every sentence carried like a load, calls people by what they can lift. Tell: asked about the scrip she lifts something heavy and answers with her back turned.
Combat Role: the carrying pole, swung like a club.
Relationship Hook: Get her porters paid in real coin and the lodge carries for you at old rates.

Personality

  • Core: sizes people up by their shoulders; never sits while others stand; keeps Ushi's pole upright
  • Quirk: sings carrying songs
