Priest Ujibe Kiyonari

A priest of the sun rites, fifty, keeper of the Sun-Rite Shrine where the court comes each spring to bless the plain's rice. Farmers leave the first sheaf of every harvest with him and ask him to pray for rain, for sons and for the emperor. In person: gentle shinto priest; shaved head; kind worried eyes; white priest robes with a tall black cap; vermilion torii.

Home Ground: Sheaf Hall at Sun-Rite Shrine of the Plain, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Mild and kind, with a stammer when he doubts himself, which is often now. Gentle temple cadence, prayers worn smooth from repetition. Stammers on the word 'answered'. Tell: when he is sure of something, the stammer vanishes and he speaks with sudden, frightening clarity.
Combat Role: a purifying rite and a prayer staff; he would rather run.
Relationship Hook: Tell him what the burned talisman means and he tells you what the priests fear about the emperor's death.

Personality

  • Core: stammers when unsure; blesses everyone who passes; counts the sheaves each evening
  • Quirk: prays with his eyes open now
