Bone-Picker's Daughter Sae, The Girl Who Walks at Night

The bone-picker's daughter after the war, eighteen, still at the Vow Temple, the bite on her wrist gone grey to the elbow. The temple ties her to her mat at night. In person: pale young woman; a grey-stained bandage from wrist to elbow; rope marks; dog shadows.

Home Ground: Vow Temple, After the War at Haibara, On the Grudge Line, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Soft and strange, a girl whose voice drops into a growl when she is upset. Soft and strange, a girl's voice that drops into a growl at the ends when upset. Tell since the war: she finishes sentences with a low sound she does not hear, and the dogs outside answer it.
Combat Role: at night, teeth.
Relationship Hook: Watch her at night and you see what the bite is doing.

Personality

  • Core: growls when upset; keeps to the temple; sleeps through the day
  • Quirk: feeds stray dogs by hand
