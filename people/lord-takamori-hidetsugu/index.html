Lord Takamori Hidetsugu

The regent's nephew, thirty, the finest young duelist in the capital and its best speaker. He trains at dawn where anyone can watch, shares his sake with common soldiers, and promises a reign where swords are rewarded for merit, not birth. Young Takamori swords and ambitious magistrates follow his single gold-striped banner, and he means to win the Capital Tournament himself. In person: handsome samurai lord; glossy black topknot; bright confident eyes; white kimono with a single gold stripe; katana; morning light; charismatic grin.

Home Ground: Dueling Yard at Hidetsugu's Manor, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Warm, quick and sure of the room; he speaks like a man already crowned, and laughs at his own near-misses. Speaks in rising phrases, like a speech building to applause, and uses 'we' for things only he wants. Drums two fingers on his sword hilt while he waits. Tell: when someone mentions his bloodline his laugh comes a beat too early.
Combat Role: fast, brilliant duelist who plays to the crowd; brutal when the crowd is gone.
Relationship Hook: Spar with him in the yard and win or lose well, and he wants you on his banner; find the archivist before he does and you hold his crown in your hands.

Personality

  • Core: laughs before he answers; greets soldiers by name; rehearses speeches aloud while sparring
  • Quirk: cannot leave a challenge unanswered
