Mule-Driver Hachibei, Driver of the Long Way Round

The salt trader after the war, forty-seven, who still carries salt from the pans with his mule Princess and now goes the long way round every barricade. In person: cheerful mule driver; straw hat with a hole in it; sun-browned; his old mule Princess.

Home Ground: Salt Pans, After the War at Saltpan Village, The Taxed Pans, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Slow, folksy and cheerful; he talks to his mule more than to you and translates the mule's opinions. He clicks his tongue to steer. Tell since the war: he tells the mule which barricade they are going round before he tells you.
Combat Role: Princess kicks.
Relationship Hook: Walk the road with him and learn every way around a barrier.

Personality

  • Core: whistles; never pays a toll he can walk around; shares his rice
  • Quirk: talks to his mule
