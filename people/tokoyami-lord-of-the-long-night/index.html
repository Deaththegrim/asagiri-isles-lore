Tokoyami, Lord of the Long Night

The demon lord of the Northern Court, whom no living person in the south has seen: tall, beautiful and ancient, seated on a throne of black ice in a palace where the night lasts most of the year. His court keeps ranks and etiquette as old as the empire. In person: demon lord; tall ancient beautiful man; long white hair; crimson eyes; a crown of black ice; layered black and crimson imperial robes; seated; blood fountain; regal menace.

Home Ground: Hall of Ice Thrones at Court of the Long Night, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Slow, vast and courtly; every word lands like a verdict from very far away. He addresses mortals by their bloodline rather than their name. Tell: when someone refuses him, he sounds delighted.
Combat Role: he does not fight; the Court does, and when he moves the night itself closes.
Relationship Hook: Reach his hall with a message and he receives you with perfect courtesy.

Personality

  • Core: speaks like a verdict; keeps the old court's etiquette; never raises his voice
  • Quirk: calls the emperor 'my little brother'
