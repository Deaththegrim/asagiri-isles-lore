Elder Kanemitsu

Elder of the Curse-Hunter Brotherhood, sixty-five, scarred, grey and grim, who runs the fortified chapterhouse on the edge of the Blight. He has broken more curses than anyone alive and killed the people who laid most of them, and he teaches novices to do the same. In person: grim scarred curse-hunter elder; short grey hair; burn-scarred hands; dark robes bound with iron-chained prayer strips; an iron staff.

Home Ground: Breaking Hall at Curse-Hunters' Chapterhouse, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Gravel and patience; he speaks like a man who has broken a thousand curses and remembers each one. Gravel and patience, long pauses as if listening to a curse. Tell: when the founder's pact comes up, he deflects with a story about an old curse.
Combat Role: a curse turned back on its maker, then an iron staff.
Relationship Hook: Break a curse with him and he teaches you; ask about the founder and he sends you out.

Personality

  • Core: remembers every curse he broke; makes novices carry stones; burns letters after reading
  • Quirk: never sits in the founder's chair
