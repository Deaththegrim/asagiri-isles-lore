Lord Takamori Masanobu, Regent of the Bought Peace

The Regent after the war: he won, and it shows on him. Sixty-two, thinner, still courteous, he rules the isles from the Blackened Keep on Saikai's credit and has not left it since the war ended. The common people call his peace order; the guild calls it collateral. In person: elderly samurai lord; grey hair cut short after the siege; formal black haori with the crane crest; a fan he no longer opens; tired, watchful eyes.

Home Ground: Council Hall, After the War at Miyako Castle, The Blackened Keep, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, unhurried, every sentence weighed before it leaves him. He never raises his voice; the room leans in instead. Pauses a full breath before any answer and lets the silence work for him. Clicks his fan shut on the last word of a decision. Tell since the war: he no longer clicks his fan shut; his hand closes on it and stays closed until he is alone.
Combat Role: one draw-cut from stillness, then the guards; he fights only when the fight is already decided.
Relationship Hook: Bring him the lost courier or the tax convoy and he remembers a sword with no house; take a side in the succession and he weighs you as a piece.

Personality

  • Core: answers a question with another question; remembers every name and every debt; lets silence do his threatening
  • Quirk: never sits with his back to a screen
