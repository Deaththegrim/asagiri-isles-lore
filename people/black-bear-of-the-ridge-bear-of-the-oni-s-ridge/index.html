Black Bear of the Ridge, Bear of the Oni's Ridge

A huge black bear driven from the great den, where the oni now sells sake to both villages' patrols; she raids the patrols' camps for what they bring. A stock foe, one of many. In person: huge black bear with a white crescent; an arrow scar on the shoulder; a torn patrol satchel in her jaws.

Speaking Style: A roar. A chest-deep roar in short bursts, then heavy snuffling. Its breath steams in grunts. Tell since the war: she huffs twice at the smell of sake.
Combat Role: a charge and a maul
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: raids camps; eats everything; charges
  • Quirk: warns with a huff
