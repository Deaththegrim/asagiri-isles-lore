The Nailed Doll, The Doll Nailed Again

A straw doll on the Weeping Cedar after the war, nailed back to the bark among the dolls of every army. It walked to the Vow Temple to finish its curse, was stopped there, and walked back. In person: cursed straw doll; small straw figure with two iron nails now; a scrap of paper with a name; eerie.

Home Ground: Nailed Bark, After the War at Weeping Cedar, The Cedar of Many Nails, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: A small dry rustle that speaks only its target's name, over and over, very patiently. A dry straw rustle shaped into one name, said with terrible patience and never louder than a breath. Between repetitions there is the soft knock of a nail settling. Tell since the war: the rustle catches on a second nail now, a dry click between every repetition.
Combat Role: it does not fight anyone but its target; it walks through you.
Relationship Hook: Find out whose name it speaks and decide whether to stop it.

Personality

  • Core: walks only at night; stops at every crossroads; does not attack anyone else
  • Quirk: repeats one name
