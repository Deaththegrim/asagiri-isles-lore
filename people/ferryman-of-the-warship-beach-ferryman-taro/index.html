Ferryman Taro, Ferryman of the Warship Beach

The ferryman at the black sand beach after the war, twenty-seven, who lands the ferries between warship keels on rollers and still knows everyone who comes ashore, shipwrights now. In person: cheerful ferryman; headband; wet to the knees; warship hulls behind.

Home Ground: Black Sand Beach: Ferry Landing, After the War at Black Sand Beach, The Warship Beach, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Cheerful, quick and nosy; he talks about the weather and everyone's luggage and guesses what's inside. He whistles at the oars. Tell since the war: he stops whistling whenever a warship hull is named, and starts again a beat too late.
Combat Role: an oar.
Relationship Hook: He shows the crate's papers to someone he trusts.

Personality

  • Core: talks about luggage; hides things under his boat; laughs at storms
  • Quirk: reads the sea
