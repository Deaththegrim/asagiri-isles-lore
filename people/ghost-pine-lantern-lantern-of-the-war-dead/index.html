Ghost Pine Lantern, Lantern of the War Dead

A paper lantern floating with nobody holding it, one of hundreds now: the war's unburied dead drift in from the road and every lantern carries one. They lead the living up to the hilltop shrine instead of off the road. A stock foe, one of many. In person: empty lantern; a soldier's name brushed on the paper; a faint figure inside the light.

Speaking Style: A flicker of whispers inside a lantern, asking to be followed. Tell since the war: its flame shapes a soldier's rank as it floats, a mouth trying a word.
Combat Role: a lure and ghost fire
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.
