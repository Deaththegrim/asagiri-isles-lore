Troupe-Master Sanshiro, Master of the Victory Play

The master of a travelling theatre troupe after the war, forty-two, playing the Regent's victory to Hibana's rich forge-masters. His actors wear masks better than masks should be. In person: flamboyant troupe master; painted eyebrows; colourful coat; a theatre mask; a crowded pier.

Home Ground: Ember Pier, After the War at Hibana, The Brighter Forges, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: A booming actor's voice; every sentence is a performance with an entrance and an exit. He announces himself in the third person. Tell since the war: he announces the dead lord's part in his stage voice, then whispers the actor's real name after.
Combat Role: stage swords and a lot of noise.
Relationship Hook: See the play and ask about the masks.

Personality

  • Core: never travels without masks; counts his actors; laughs at storms
  • Quirk: performs every sentence
