Takamori Spearman, Spearman of the Blackened Keep

A foot soldier in scorched lacquered armour with the crane crest half burned away, holding the timber-patched breach of the Grey Gate for a lord who never comes down to see it. Takamori spearmen hold in fours now and do not break, because there is nowhere left to run. A stock foe, one of many. In person: ashigaru foot soldier; dented conical iron jingasa hat; scorched lacquered armour with a half-burned crane crest; a long spear on a new shaft.

Speaking Style: A barked challenge, then nervous mutters. A barked challenge, then nervous muttering to the man beside him. Spearmen call to each other by village names. Tell since the war: he taps the gate's timber patch with his spear butt before he answers anyone, as if checking it still holds.
Combat Role: spear in a line of three; breaks when the officer falls
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: holds the breach; counts the scrip; no longer bows to anyone below the keep
  • Quirk: holds the line while watched
