Keeper Hisako

Keeper of the Grey Banner Waystation in Haibara, fifty, built like a door, who rents beds to curse-bearers and the desperate and throws out anyone who brings a fight inside. In person: sturdy waystation keeper; cropped grey hair; tired firm face; bandaged forearm; grey banner apron; ash.

Home Ground: Grey Banner Waystation at Haibara, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Low, firm and tired; she talks like someone guarding a door. Low, firm, guarding-a-door cadence; repeats the rules of the waystation. Tell: when the sealed well knocks, she stops talking and listens.
Combat Role: a heavy bar and the waystation's guests.
Relationship Hook: Find another way to feed the sealed well and she owes you her life.

Personality

  • Core: guards the door herself; never goes near the square at night; binds her forearm
  • Quirk: counts the guests twice
