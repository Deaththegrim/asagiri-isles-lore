Harpooner Kenmotsu, Harpooner Over the Crack

A grim exile harpooner after the war, the best on the ice, who pays for graves on a graveyard the fissure split. In person: grim weathered harpooner; harpoon; haunted eyes; a driftwood marker under his arm.

Home Ground: Rendering Yard, After the War at Whaling Station, The Station of Hungry Lamps, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Short, heavy words; long silences. Short heavy words and long silences. Tell since the war: he says Haruo's name now, once, and then the silence lasts longer than before.
Combat Role: a harpoon on the ice.
Relationship Hook: Ask why he never goes up the Glacier Road and he says nothing for a long time.

Personality

  • Core: grim and silent; best harpooner on the ice; never goes up the Glacier Road
  • Quirk: pays for graves
