Hermit Shirakumo

A white-bearded hermit who lives behind the Seven Falls and practises the same cut under the water every morning. The monks bring him rice and do not ask what the cut is for. In person: long white beard and hair; bare feet; simple grey hermit robes soaked from a waterfall; an old katana held low; serene.

Home Ground: Falls Courtyard at Mountain Temple of the Seven Falls, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Slow and warm, amused by everything, answering questions with smaller questions. Slow, warm and amused, questions answered with smaller questions. Tell: the war mentioned and the amusement goes out of him for one sentence.
Combat Role: precise angled cuts named as they fall, then all nine together.
Relationship Hook: Climb to him and stay after the first line.

Personality

  • Core: practises under the falls; never wears shoes; laughs at serious students
  • Quirk: answers with questions
