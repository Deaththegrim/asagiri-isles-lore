Master Rensuke

A Breath Master of the Hold, fifty, grey-haired, who drills the gate's young hunters on the Sunrise Wall at dawn. In person: older man; grey-haired breath master; pale skin; hunter's black coat; a long blade.

Home Ground: Sunrise Wall at Asahi Gate, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Calm and sparing; a teacher's voice that never warms. Calm and sparing, a teacher's measured voice with no warmth in it, long pauses with no breath. Tell: at night he forgets to breathe between sentences and nobody hears an inhale.
Combat Role: the hunters' forms, colder and faster than any living master.
Relationship Hook: Sit with him through a night watch and the party hears him not breathe.

Personality

  • Core: never sleeps in the barracks; refuses the physician; speaks of the north with respect
  • Quirk: drills at dawn and never at noon
