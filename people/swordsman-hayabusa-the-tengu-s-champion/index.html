Swordsman Hayabusa, The Tengu's Champion

The quiet human swordsman of Tengu Peak after the war, thirty-two, straw hat and unremarkable sword still. He fought in the war under the false name the great tengu gave him. In person: quiet swordsman; straw hat; plain clothes; an unremarkable sword with a notched edge; calm, older eyes.

Home Ground: Cliff Dojo, After the War at Tengu Peak, The Peak Above the War, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Calm, short and polite; he never boasts and never explains. Calm, short and polite, every sentence a single clean stroke. He bows his head before answering. Tell since the war: he bows before answering any question about the war, and the bow is longer than the answer.
Combat Role: the tengu's sword, plain and terrible.
Relationship Hook: Spar with him and he tells you nothing; beat him and he tells you why he is going to the capital.

Personality

  • Core: trains at night; eats like a farmer; bows to every opponent
  • Quirk: never boasts
