Bone-Picker Gorobei

A Haibara ash-man, the only one who goes to the ruined kennels willingly: he picks the broken pens for fangs and bone whistles and sells them as charms against the dogs. In person: cold middle-aged bone-picker with a sack and a bone whistle; ashen sky; dog shadows.

Home Ground: Kennel Rows at Inugami Kennels, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Quiet and cold, a man who speaks to dogs warmly and people not at all. Cold to people, warm low murmurs to the dogs, a whistle never far from his lips. Tell: at his daughter's name the cold breaks.
Combat Role: a bone whistle and a fast retreat.
Relationship Hook: Ask where his fangs come from and he admits he picks the pens, and what it cost his daughter.

Personality

  • Core: warm to dogs; counts the pens; keeps his daughter at the Vow Temple
  • Quirk: cold to people
