Student Folder Hanae, The Student Who Folded Too Well

A Bureau student after the war, talented, careless and further behind than ever. The runaway familiars write to her. In person: young onmyoji student; paper everywhere; ink-stained fingers; a folded paper message she will not open.

Home Ground: Folding Tables, After the War at Paper Familiar Workshop, The Folded Army, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Quick and giggly, a student who folds while she talks and drops paper birds everywhere. Quick and giggly, paper rustling under every sentence. Tell since the war: she holds the unopened message against her chest while she talks, and never puts it down.
Combat Role: a flock of paper birds in the face.
Relationship Hook: Mention the runaway and she begs you not to tell, then sends you to her uncle.

Personality

  • Core: folds while talking; drops paper birds; always late
  • Quirk: giggles when nervous
