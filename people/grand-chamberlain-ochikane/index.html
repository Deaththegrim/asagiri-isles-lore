Grand Chamberlain Ochikane

Ochikane Tadamori, Grand Chamberlain of the Inner Palace: keeper of the seals, the calendar and the doors. Nobody sees the emperor without him, and every decree passes through his hands. Plump, soft-spoken and always smiling, he is the most courteous man at court and the one everyone is careful with. In person: plump court chamberlain; shaved head; narrow smiling eyes; black and gold layered court robes; a sheaf of sealed decrees; sly courteous smile.

Home Ground: Gate of Ceremonies at The Inner Palace, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Soft, courteous and amused, as if every conversation were a joke only he knows the end of. Silken and unhurried, with a little sigh before bad news as if it pained him. Now and then ends a sentence on 'of course', which is never a question. Tell: when he is lying, he is more precise with names and dates than anyone needs.
Combat Role: he does not fight; he has a guard step in and a pardon written for whoever does the killing.
Relationship Hook: He offers small favours freely and collects them later; the first favour is always real.

Personality

  • Core: smiles while refusing; keeps everyone waiting exactly long enough; never writes anything in his own hand twice
  • Quirk: collects small favours like coins
