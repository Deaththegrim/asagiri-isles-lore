Gensuke the Medicine-Seller

A travelling medicine-seller, fifty, with a tall box of drawers on his back, who walks the isles leaving remedies at houses on credit and collecting payment a year later. He knows every household's ailments from the capital to the marsh. In person: travelling medicine seller; a tall wooden box of drawers; kind face; straw hat.

Home Ground: Honjin Inn at Oiwake Station, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Friendly, patient and full of remedies; he diagnoses you while you talk. Friendly and patient, a pedlar's easy rhythm full of remedies; he diagnoses you while you talk and names a cure before you finish. His box of drawers clicks as he opens them. Tell: when you lie about being well he hands you the remedy anyway, without comment.
Combat Role: none; a pouch of pepper powder.
Relationship Hook: Buy a remedy and he tells you which house on the plain is hiding someone.

Personality

  • Core: never forgets a house; sleeps in stables; carries a remedy for everything but grief
  • Quirk: diagnoses while talking
