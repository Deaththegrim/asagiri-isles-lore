Moth Dell Swarm

A cloud of black moths hatched from a curse. Grudge swarms smother sound and breath. A stock foe, one of many. In person: grudge swarm; a cloud of black moths.

Speaking Style: A rustle of wings with whispers inside it, many small grudges at once. Tell: near flame the rustle turns to a hiss.
Combat Role: a smothering cloud
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Quirk: smothers
