Dowager Empress Shoshi

The emperor's widowed elder sister-in-law and the senior lady of the court, seventy, small, gracious and feared. She raised Princess Ayame as her ward, leads the Lotus Ward's cause from behind paper screens, and has outlived three regents' patience. Courtiers say she has never once been surprised. In person: dowager empress; silver hair; calm dark eyes; layered purple and white junihitoe robes; a white lotus embroidered; a cat; serene.

Home Ground: Hall of Poems at The Inner Palace, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Gentle, exact and very old-fashioned; she wraps every refusal in a poem and every threat in a compliment. Old palace diction, every sentence finished with a soft falling cadence. Taps her poem-brush against the inkstone between thoughts. Tell: when the princess is endangered, the poetry drops away and she speaks plainly and fast.
Combat Role: she never fights; her guards and her letters do.
Relationship Hook: Serve the Lotus Ward without asking about Ayame's past and she trusts you with her letters; ask about the past and she decides what you are.

Personality

  • Core: answers in poems; never raises her eyes above the speaker's collar; pours tea for her enemies first
  • Quirk: keeps a cat that bites everyone else
