Chidori the Runner

A young smuggler's runner, eighteen, who poles a flat boat through the reed channels faster than anyone and carries messages for the Brine Tide. In person: cheeky smuggler runner; short hair; rolled sleeves; poling a flat boat.

Home Ground: Channel Maze at Reed Channels, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Quick, cheeky and breathless; she talks while poling, words coming in time with each push. She whistles to the gulls. Tell: when the patrol boats appear she stops talking mid-word and poles faster.
Combat Role: a pole and the fog.
Relationship Hook: Pay her and she takes you through the maze; pay double and she goes in at night.

Personality

  • Core: laughs at the navy; knows every channel; never goes in at night
  • Quirk: talks while poling
