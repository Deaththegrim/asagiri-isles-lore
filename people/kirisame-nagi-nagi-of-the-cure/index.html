Kirisame Nagi, Nagi of the Cure

The matriarch's granddaughter after the war, twenty-five, the clan's finest poisoner, who now brews the cure for the grey mark in the Poison House and still serves tea to every visitor on the plank walks. In person: beautiful smiling poisoner; long dark hair with needle hairpins; sly gentle eyes; rain-patterned kimono; stained fingers.

Home Ground: Plank Walks, After the War at Kirisame Stilt Village, Village of the Grey Mark, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Sweet, amused and unhurried; she smiles while she tests you, and the smile never tells you which cup it is. Unhurried sweetness, a teasing lilt, sips her own tea between lines. Tell since the war: she serves the tea, then drinks first from your cup before she hands it over.
Combat Role: poison before the fight, needles in it, and fog to leave by.
Relationship Hook: Drink her tea, trusting her, and she decides whether you live; that is how she says hello.
Courtship Style: Tests you with tea, then with secrets, then with her grandmother; every test you pass she smiles a little less and means it more.
Jealousy Style: Pours the rival a cup of tea, smiling; you never learn which cup it was.

Personality

  • Core: serves tea to everyone; hums in the rain; keeps a cure in her hair ornament
  • Quirk: smiles while testing
