Gate Guard Isozaki Gonzo

The lake gate's guard, who checks Bureau passes and watches the water for things that should not float. In person: adult gate guard with a spear and conical hat; bored; dice.

Home Ground: Lake Gate Post at Seimei, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Bored and dry, a gate guard who has seen every trick and is unimpressed by all of them. Bored, dry, unimpressed drawl, dice rattling. Tell: at the word 'fox' he coughs and changes the subject.
Combat Role: a spear across the gate.
Relationship Hook: Win at dice against him and he tells you about the fox.

Personality

  • Core: unimpressed by everything; plays dice; lucky lately
  • Quirk: watches the lake
