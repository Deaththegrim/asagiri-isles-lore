Rope-Boy Kanta, The Boy on the Black Decks

A rope-boy of thirteen who ties the black market's decks together before the dawn auctions, for a coin a day now instead of a fish. In person: barefoot boy; taller; a coil of rope over his shoulder; a dead man's sandal tied to his belt.

Home Ground: Deck Auction, After the War at Drifting Fish Market, Black Market Decks, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Breathless and cheeky, a boy who jumps between boats mid-sentence. Breathless boy's patter between jumps, boasts about knots. Tell since the war: he tugs the dead man's sandal on his belt when anyone from the guild walks by.
Combat Role: none; he dives into the sea.
Relationship Hook: Give him a fish and a promise and he shows you the sandal.

Personality

  • Core: knows every knot; steals a fish a day; keeps a sandal hidden
  • Quirk: jumps between boats
