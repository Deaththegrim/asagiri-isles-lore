Bamboo Kamaitachi

A weasel with sickle-claws riding a gust of wind. Sickle weasels cut travellers they never see. A stock foe, one of many. In person: kamaitachi; weasel yokai with sickle claws riding a gust.

Speaking Style: A whistling giggle that rides the wind, words sliced into pieces. It laughs mid-strike. Tell: three giggles in a row means three cuts.
Combat Role: three gusts, three cuts
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: cuts unseen; comes in threes; giggles
  • Quirk: rides the wind
