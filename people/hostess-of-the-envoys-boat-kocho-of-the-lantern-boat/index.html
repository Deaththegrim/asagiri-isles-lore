Kocho of the Lantern Boat, Hostess of the Envoys' Boat

Hostess of the finest lantern boat after the war, thirty-two, who hires it out to envoys who need to talk where no wall can hear. She earns more than ever and repeats less. In person: elegant boat hostess; knowing smile; a darker kimono; a shamisen.

Home Ground: Lantern Boat, After the War at Lakeside Pleasure Boats, The Envoys' Boats, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Silken, amused and musical; she laughs softly at everything and remembers it all. Silken, musical, soft laughter threaded through every line, repeating secrets as if they were jokes. Tell since the war: when the envoy's coin is mentioned she turns it over in her palm and does not laugh.
Combat Role: none; the boat leaves the shore.
Relationship Hook: Outbid her buyers and she tells you what the examiner said.

Personality

  • Core: never asks a question directly; plays the shamisen when thinking; keeps her own counsel
  • Quirk: laughs softly at everything
