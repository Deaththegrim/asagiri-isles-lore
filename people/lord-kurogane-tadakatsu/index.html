Lord Kurogane Tadakatsu

Lord of House Kurogane, sixty, a mountain of a man in black lacquered iron who has held Tetsuyama and its forges for thirty years. His house arms half the isles, sends the regent its tribute in swords, and never steps back. In person: massive samurai lord; grey beard; stern heavy face; seated; imposing.

Home Ground: Lord's Hall at Kurogane Iron Castle, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Heavy, slow and final; he speaks as his armour moves, one plate at a time, and never repeats himself. A hinge-creak of iron sits between his clauses. Tell: when his son defies him he goes quieter, not louder, and that is when the house trembles.
Combat Role: advance, never retreat; a long blade behind an iron wall.
Relationship Hook: Prove yourself in a test of endurance and he hires you; ask about the deep forges and his son finds you first.

Personality

  • Core: speaks one plate at a time; inspects every blade himself; never goes below the castle
  • Quirk: never steps back
