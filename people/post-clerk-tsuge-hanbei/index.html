Post Clerk Tsuge Hanbei

Clerk of Oiwake Station's barrier board, fifty, who posts the road commissioner's bounties beside the toll list and stamps every claim twice. In person: mature man; post-road clerk with a seal and ink stone; plain grey official robes.

Home Ground: Toll Barrier at Oiwake Station, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Dry, patient and official; he reads every notice in the same flat voice, deaths included. Flat and official, every sentence ends like a stamp coming down, distances in ri and stations. Tell: when the commissioner is named his voice goes a shade too careful.
Combat Role: none; he closes the barrier and calls the guards.
Relationship Hook: Earn his trust on three bounties and he hands you the missing list.

Personality

  • Core: stamps twice; speaks in road distances; dislikes the commissioner
  • Quirk: keeps tea for hunters
