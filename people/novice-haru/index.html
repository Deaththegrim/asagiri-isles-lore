Novice Haru

A novice of the breath masters' retreat who meditates on the frozen ledge and narrates his breath forms aloud. In person: earnest young novice demon hunter meditating; breath steaming.

Home Ground: Meditation Ledge at Breath Masters' Retreat, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: Earnest and breathless, a novice who narrates his own breathing. Earnest and breathless, narrating his breaths. Tell: after a forbidden form, a cough he swallows mid-word.
Combat Role: a forbidden breath form that costs him every time.
Relationship Hook: Watch him train and you see him cough blood.

Personality

  • Quirk: coughs blood quietly
