Lady Fuyutsuki, Fuyutsuki of the Trampled Ridge

One of the demon lord's three eldest after the war, a tall pale noblewoman in white furs, encamped on the daylight watch ridge where the northern army went south. In person: demon noble; tall pale aristocratic woman; long black hair; crimson eyes; white furs; a camp of thralls behind her.

Home Ground: Ridge Cairn, After the War at Daylight Watch Ridge, The Ridge of Unburned Tracks, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Icy, refined and amused; she speaks to mortals like servants who have forgotten their place. She laughs lightly through her nose. Tell since the war: she says the traitor's name in every third sentence, lightly, like a promise to herself.
Combat Role: frost and claws and centuries of practice.
Relationship Hook: She offers a bargain for the daylight demon's trail.

Personality

  • Core: treats mortals as servants; never hurries; laughs at sunrise from shelter
  • Quirk: follows tracks by scent
