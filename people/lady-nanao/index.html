Lady Nanao

The nine-tailed lady's eldest daughter, a seven-tailed fox who runs the Hall of Bargains: cool, precise and dressed in grey, she writes down every favour the Court is owed. In person: cool seven-tailed fox woman; grey hair; golden eyes; fox ears; grey layered robes; aloof.

Home Ground: Hall of Bargains at Halls of the Nine-Tailed Lady, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Cool, precise and tired of her mother; she speaks to humans like a clerk to petitioners. Cool and precise, a clerk's diction, as if reading from a ledger she keeps in her head. She corrects small inaccuracies automatically. Tell: when her mother is mentioned she sighs and loses her place in the ledger.
Combat Role: illusion, then the Court's guards.
Relationship Hook: Bring her a bargain and she writes it fairly; ask about the promise and she smiles for once.

Personality

  • Core: writes every favour down; speaks like a clerk; never smiles at her mother
  • Quirk: keeps her tails hidden
