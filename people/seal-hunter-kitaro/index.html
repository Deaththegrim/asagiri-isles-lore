Seal-Hunter Kitaro

An exile who hunts seals off the quay and knows which ice holds and which does not. In person: weathered exile seal hunter with a harpoon; furs; frozen sea.

Home Ground: Seal Hunters' Quay at Exiles' Shore Towns, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Short words, long silences, an exile seal hunter who talks to the ice. Short words, long silences, a murmur to the ice. Tell: at 'pardon' the silence stretches.
Combat Role: a harpoon on the ice.
Relationship Hook: Earn his trust and he shows you the pardon.

Personality

  • Quirk: shares seal oil
