Kurokami, Lord of the Federation's Fort

The bandit boss after the war, thirty-seven, who sold his band to the nephew, fought, and came out with a fort if not a title. The Red Lantern Roost is his now, bought with convoy silver. In person: cold bandit boss; long black hair; a lord's stolen armour mended with iron; a long sword; a new red-lantern banner.

Home Ground: Lantern Trees, After the War at Red Lantern Roost, The Federation's Fort, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Smooth and cold, with sudden bursts of temper; he talks about lordship as if it were already his. Smooth and cold with sudden flashes of temper, talking of 'my lordship' already. Cracks his knuckles mid-sentence. Tell since the war: he says 'when I am a lord' now, not 'my lordship', and his knuckles crack on the word when.
Combat Role: ambush from the trees, then a long blade and no mercy.
Relationship Hook: Find his buried convoy and every faction on the plain wants to talk to you.

Personality

  • Core: wears stolen armour; smiles before he hits; keeps a list of lords who owe him
  • Quirk: buries what he takes
