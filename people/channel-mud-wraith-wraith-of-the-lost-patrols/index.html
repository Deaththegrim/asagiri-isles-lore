Channel Mud Wraith, Wraith of the Lost Patrols

A shape of marsh mud risen from the old paddies, wearing what is left of a navy patrolman; the lost patrols' lanterns drift in the maze, and the wraiths carry them. A stock foe, one of many. In person: mud wraith; a shape of marsh mud; a navy patrolman's rotted cap; a drowned patrol lantern in one hand.

Speaking Style: A sucking gurgle, words drowned in mud. Tell since the war: a drowned patrolman's call comes out of it, the same three words.
Combat Role: drag and smother
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: drags; carries a lantern; wears the dead
  • Quirk: rises
