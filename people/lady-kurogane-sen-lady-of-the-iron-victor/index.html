Lady Kurogane Sen, Lady of the Iron Victor

The lord's sister after the war, fifty-two, who runs the richest house in the isles: the house sold soul-blades to every side and was paid by every side. In person: cold aristocratic woman; black hair with one white streak; sharp eyes; iron-grey silk; gloves; a ledger.

Home Ground: Lord's Hall, After the War at Kurogane Iron Castle, The Iron Victor, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Cool, dry and businesslike; she discusses souls and ore in the same tone. Cool, dry and businesslike, discussing souls and ore in the same tone and the same breath. She taps a tally brush against her teeth. Tell since the war: she corrects 'person' to 'soul' and then, once, lets it stand.
Combat Role: none; the house guard and the guild's ronin.
Relationship Hook: Bring her a buyer and she does business; bring her the armoury's true records and she bargains for her life.

Personality

  • Core: keeps the accounts herself; never visits the mines in person; wears gloves
  • Quirk: discusses souls like ore
