Mask Apprentice Ryuta, Carver of the Whispering Face

Master Sorei's apprentice after the war, nineteen, who now carves faces too: the workshop's trade grew, and she let him. In person: young apprentice; tired eyes; a carving knife; a white mask with a face half finished.

Home Ground: Carving Room, After the War at Mask-Maker's Workshop, Workshop of Borrowed Faces, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Easy and cheerful, an apprentice who talks to the masks he sands. Easy and cheerful, chatting to masks as he sands. Tell since the war: he whispers 'not you' to the masks when he thinks no one is listening.
Combat Role: none; a carving knife held wrong.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the face he carved and he shows you, shaking.

Personality

  • Core: talks to masks; sands while he talks; paints his hands by mistake
  • Quirk: sleeps badly
