Reef Funayurei, Ghost Crew of the Wreck Reef

A drowned war-junk sailor in the fog asking for a ladle, one of a whole crew risen from the new wrecks that ran the chain-boom in the dark. A stock foe, one of many. In person: funayurei; drowned ghost sailor in a soldier's sash; a ladle in each hand.

Speaking Style: A drowned whisper, many voices asking for a ladle, bubbling at the ends. Tell since the war: the whole crew asks for the ladle at once, in a soldier's count.
Combat Role: fill the boat with the sea
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: rises in fog; sinks boats; fooled by a bottomless ladle
  • Quirk: asks for a ladle
