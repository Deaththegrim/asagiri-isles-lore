The Soldier in the Black Book

A broad oni in a levy soldier's split lacquer chest-plate, his horns wrapped in a black cord, dice rattling in a fist the size of a bucket. He sits at the dice table every night with a stack of debt slips in front of him. He plays for his own debt and always loses. A gambler who beats him he follows home and stands outside their door till dawn; anyone caught cheating at his table he breaks over it. The den keeps his seat empty. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: broad oni; horns wrapped; dice; smoky den.

Home Ground: Dice Room, After the War at Lantern Row Gambling Den, Soldiers' Den, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: A dealer's patter in a soldier's growl, every line a wager. A gambler's easy patter in a soldier's growl, every line a bet. Tell: when a debt is named he rattles the dice and does not throw them.
Combat Role: the soldier in the black book overturns the table
Relationship Hook: Learn what Tatsuzo lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
