Haibara Curse-Peddler, Peddler of War Grudges

A smiling old woman with a tray of charms sold to war widows at the waystation, inside the blight now that the grey has spread past Haibara. A stock foe, one of many. In person: elderly woman; smiling curse-peddler; a tray of dark charms and hair-bound dolls; a mourning-white shawl she has no right to.

Speaking Style: Warm and poisonous. Warm grandmotherly comfort with poison under it, soft 'dear's and 'poor thing's. She hums a lullaby. Tell since the war: she dabs her eyes at every widow, and her eyes are dry.
Combat Role: a curse from the tray, then a word
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: offers a fair price; never touches iron; weeps with widows on cue
  • Quirk: smiles at the grieving
