Angry Kodama

A small pale tree spirit with a blank round face, its echo turned to a scream at the saw line. Angry kodama curse the loggers who fell their trees and lead them in circles. A stock foe, one of many. In person: small pale tree spirit with a blank round face; screaming.

Speaking Style: An echo of a voice, your words thrown back wrong. Tell: given an offering, the echo turns gentle.
Combat Role: an echo scream and roots underfoot
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: screams; curses loggers; accepts offerings
  • Quirk: echoes
