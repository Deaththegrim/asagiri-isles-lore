Ume the Translator

A translator in the Foreigners' Quarter tea house, thirty, who speaks four foreign tongues and every dialect of the isles. Captains, factors and magistrates hire her by the hour, and she sells every conversation twice. In person: translator; neat black hair with a jade pin; thoughtful eyes; plain grey kimono with a foreign shawl; calm.

Home Ground: Translators' Tea House at Foreigners' Quarter, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, careful and amused; she answers in whichever language you did not expect. Calm, amused, switching languages mid-sentence to see who follows. Repeats a word in three languages when it matters. Tell: when she is cornered she speaks only in the language the listener does not know.
Combat Role: none; she knows which door leads out.
Relationship Hook: Show her you would use the list to free the hosts, not sell them, and she gives it to you.

Personality

  • Core: answers in the unexpected language; pours tea before every answer; never translates an insult
  • Quirk: keeps a coded list in her sleeve
