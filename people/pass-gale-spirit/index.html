Pass Gale Spirit

A shape of whirling wind and snapping flags. Gale spirits throw travellers off the pass. A stock foe, one of many. In person: gale spirit; whirling shape of wind and prayer flags.

Speaking Style: A howl of wind that laughs, words carried off before they finish. Tell: when it respects you, the wind drops and it speaks clearly.
Combat Role: a gust that throws
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Quirk: whirls
