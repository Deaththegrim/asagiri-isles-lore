Warden Hunter

A hunter in furs and chains who tracks beast hosts across the ice. Warden hunters bring back every host they find, and do not much care whether alive. A stock foe, one of many. In person: fur-clad bounty hunter with seal-chains; cold.

Speaking Style: Flat, practical. Flat and practical, the talk of someone who never sleeps in a town. Hunters name the beast before the threat. Tell: when a host is a child, the flatness breaks.
Combat Role: chains and patience
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: oils her chains; pays informers; never sleeps in a town
  • Quirk: counts bounties
