River Kappa, Kappa of the Drowned Ford

A green, beaked river imp grown thin and angry since an army drowned in its ford; it no longer bows back, and it drags down anyone in armour. A stock foe, one of many. In person: kappa; gaunt green river yokai; cracked turtle shell; a chipped half-empty head dish; a broken spearhead in its grip.

Speaking Style: Dry and wet at once, a polite croak with a click in the throat, every bargain spoken like a rule of etiquette. It slurps between sentences. Tell since the war: it counts the drowned on its webbed fingers mid-sentence and starts again when it loses the place.
Combat Role: grapple and drown; bow to it
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: wrestles first; bows to no one now; keeps the drowned
  • Quirk: demands cucumbers
