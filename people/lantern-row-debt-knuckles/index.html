Lantern Row Debt-Knuckles

A heavy-handed debt collector with iron knuckles, a gambling den's tally stick and a missing ear. Debt-knuckles collect what the dice room is owed and break fingers when they cannot. A stock foe, one of many. In person: heavy debt collector; iron knuckles; a tally stick; one ear missing.

Speaking Style: Low and weary, food on his mind between threats. Tell: offered a meal, he softens.
Combat Role: iron knuckles in close, then the den's backup
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: counts on the tally stick; talks about food; never hits the face
  • Quirk: cracks his knuckles
