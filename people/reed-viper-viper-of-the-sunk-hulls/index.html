Reed Viper, Viper of the Sunk Hulls

A long brown viper nesting in the hulls of the sunk patrol boats; there are more of them, and fatter, since the navy fed the channels. A stock foe, one of many. In person: long brown marsh viper; thicker; a pale scar where a boat hook caught it.

Speaking Style: A hiss. A dry, steady hiss, sliding up and down like reeds in wind. Scales whisper on mud. Tell since the war: it hisses at the sound of a boat hook on wood.
Combat Role: one strike from the reeds
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: strikes once; hides in reeds; never chases
  • Quirk: waits
