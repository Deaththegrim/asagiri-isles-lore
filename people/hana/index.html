Hana

A girl of ten hiding in the Smugglers' Caves, thin, wary and fast, who steals fish from the Brine Tide's cache and runs from anyone in boots. In person: small thin runaway girl; tangled black hair; wary large eyes; ragged oversized shirt; barefoot; faint glow; frightened.

Home Ground: Dry Gallery at Smugglers' Caves, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: A small, careful voice; she answers questions with questions and goes silent when she hears boots. Small and careful, answering questions with questions, very polite. Breathes quickly when afraid. Tell: when she hears boots she goes completely silent, mid-word.
Combat Role: runs and hides; when cornered, the beast wakes.
Relationship Hook: Win her trust with food and patience, and keep the boots away, and she lets you take her somewhere safe.

Personality

  • Core: answers questions with questions; shares food with crabs; hums to the thing inside her
  • Quirk: hides at the sound of boots
