Ward-Ring Fox, Fox of the Broken Ring

A white fox with two tails who no longer sits on the cracked stone: two more stones broke the night the Vaults did, and she walks the ring at dusk keeping out whatever is coming through. A stock foe, one of many. In person: white two-tailed fox spirit; muddy fur; a scorched tail tip; a broken ward rope in her teeth.

Speaking Style: A lady's voice, teasing and musical, each riddle ending on a rising note, a laugh behind a sleeve. Tell since the war: she asks her riddle, then answers it herself before you can.
Combat Role: foxfire and an illusion over the stones
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: laughs less; hates rudeness; keeps promises still
  • Quirk: asks a riddle
