Shun the Biwa Monk

A blind biwa monk, sixty, who walks the Blight's pilgrim road with a lute on his back, singing the tale of the old capital's fall for anyone who feeds him. In person: blind biwa monk; shaved head; closed eyes; dark robes; a lute; solemn.

Home Ground: Ash Road at Grey Pilgrims' Road, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: A storyteller's chant, slow and rhythmic; he speaks in verses of the old war. A storyteller's chant, slow and rhythmic, speaking in verses of the old war with a pluck of the biwa between lines. He never says 'I'; he says 'this monk'. Tell: when a story touches the present he stops playing and speaks plainly.
Combat Role: his staff, and the dead stop to listen.
Relationship Hook: Feed him and hear the whole tale, last verse and all.

Personality

  • Core: tunes his lute when nervous; hears footsteps a mile off; never sings the last verse twice
  • Quirk: speaks in verses
