Heron Island Wrecker

A mud-caked wrecker with a boathook, a false beacon lantern and a grin full of gaps. Wreckers light false beacons off Heron Island and loot what the rocks break. A stock foe, one of many. In person: mud-caked wrecker; boathook; false beacon lantern; gap-toothed grin; among old graves.

Speaking Style: Cackling and wet, talking to the herons. Tell: at a storm he goes reverent and quiet.
Combat Role: a boathook from the mud, a lantern behind him
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: lights lanterns; loots first; talks to the herons
  • Quirk: grins at storms
