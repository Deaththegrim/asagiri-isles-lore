Kirisame Nagi

The matriarch's granddaughter, twenty-three, the clan's finest poisoner: a lovely, smiling woman in a rain-patterned kimono who serves tea to every visitor on the plank walks. Visitors who drink it leave by a different channel than they came. In person: beautiful smiling poisoner; long dark hair with needle hairpins; sly gentle eyes; rain-patterned kimono; teasing.

Home Ground: Plank Walks at Kirisame Stilt Village, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Sweet, amused and unhurried; she smiles while she tests you, and the smile never tells you which cup it is. Unhurried sweetness, a teasing lilt, sips her own tea between lines. Tell: when she has chosen to trust you, the teasing drops and she says your name plainly.
Combat Role: poison before the fight, needles in it, and fog to leave by.
Relationship Hook: Drink her tea, trusting her, and she decides whether you live; that is how she says hello.
Courtship Style: Tests you with tea, then with secrets, then with her grandmother; every test you pass she smiles a little less and means it more.
Jealousy Style: Pours the rival a cup of tea, smiling; you never learn which cup it was.

Personality

  • Core: serves tea to everyone; hums in the rain; keeps a cure in her hair ornament
  • Quirk: smiles while testing
