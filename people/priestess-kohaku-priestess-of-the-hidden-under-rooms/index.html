Priestess Kohaku, Priestess of the Hidden Under-Rooms

The fox priestess of Kitsunebi Crossing after the war, who bows to more yokai than humans now. She is serene still. In person: serene fox priestess; long black hair tied with white paper; fox ears showing more often; red and white robes.

Home Ground: Fox Shrine Post, After the War at Kitsunebi Crossing, The Last Open Gate, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Serene, warm and gently amused; she has been answering the same questions for three hundred years. Serene and warm, gently amused, as if she already knows how the conversation ends. She rings a small bell to close her blessings. Tell since the war: her closing bell rings twice now, once for the crossing and once for the children under it.
Combat Role: the shrine's wards, and fox-fire if pushed.
Relationship Hook: Bring her a dispute between wood and road and she brokers it fairly.

Personality

  • Core: answers patiently; never eats meat in public; smiles at small children
  • Quirk: keeps both sides' secrets
