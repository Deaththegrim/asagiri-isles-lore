The Nailed Doll

A straw doll pulled itself off the nails of the Weeping Cedar three nights ago and is walking, slowly, towards the Vow Temple in Haibara. It leaves a trail of straw and the smell of an old hatred. In person: cursed straw doll; small human-shaped straw figure with an iron nail; a scrap of paper with a name; eerie.

Home Ground: Nailed Bark at Weeping Cedar, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: A small dry rustle that speaks only its target's name, over and over, very patiently. A dry straw rustle shaped into one name, said with terrible patience and never louder than a breath. Between repetitions there is the soft knock of a nail settling. Tell: when its target is near, the rustle quickens like breathing, and the name comes faster.
Combat Role: it does not fight anyone but its target; it walks through you.
Relationship Hook: Find out whose name it speaks and decide whether to stop it.

Personality

  • Core: walks only at night; stops at every crossroads; does not attack anyone else
  • Quirk: repeats one name
