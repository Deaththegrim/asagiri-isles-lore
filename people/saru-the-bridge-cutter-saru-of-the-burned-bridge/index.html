Saru the Bridge-Cutter, Saru of the Burned Bridge

The wiry bandit after the war, twenty-seven, who lived on the rope bridge until the Straw Masks burned it, and now lives on the burned anchor. In person: wiry giggling bandit; singed hair; monkey grin; ragged clothes; a knife.

Home Ground: Bridge Planks, After the War at Bandits' Bridge, The Burned Bridge, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: High, giggling and twitchy; he narrates his own tricks as he does them. Giggles in short bursts between words and narrates himself in the third person mid-trick. Hums while he saws rope. Tell since the war: he giggles at fire now, and then stops, and looks at his singed sleeve.
Combat Role: snares, falls and running up cliffs.
Relationship Hook: Catch him before the mule train crosses and he trades you the convoy's hiding place for his neck.

Personality

  • Core: giggles; climbs everything; hides shiny things
  • Quirk: narrates his own tricks
