Director Tachibana Seiran, The Director Who Kept Silent

Tachibana Seiran, Director of the Seimei Bureau after the war, seventy-two, who kept the Bureau out of the war behind its wards and is hated for it by every side. He refused the Regent to his face. He still teaches every first-year class. In person: tall thin onmyoji director; long white hair; calm, guilty eyes; layered white and black robes; a cracked star-chart in his hand.

Home Ground: Director's Garden, After the War at Bureau of Yin and Yang, The Silent Bureau, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Slow, gentle and precise; he teaches in questions and never answers his own. Every sentence a question that leads you to the answer he wanted. Pours tea while he talks. Tell since the war: he pours a second cup of tea he does not drink, and asks no question about it.
Combat Role: wards that close a room and familiars that fill it; he never needs to move.
Relationship Hook: Bring him a question he cannot answer and he takes you as a student; bring him Sorei and he owes you.

Personality

  • Core: teaches in questions; rakes the sand garden himself; never says a thing is impossible
  • Quirk: reads the stars before every decision
