Acolyte Shinsuke

A young acolyte who rings the temple bell for every ship that comes home and counts the ones that do not. In person: young shaven-headed acolyte monk ringing a great bronze bell; sea wind.

Home Ground: Bell Terrace at Temple of Safe Voyages, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Gentle and earnest, a young monk who apologises to the bell before he rings it. Gentle and earnest, a small apology before anything bold, a prayer murmured under the bell. Tell: at the Brine Tide's name his gentleness thins.
Combat Role: a temple staff, swung with apologies.
Relationship Hook: Listen to the bell for a night and you hear it ring a beat late.

Personality

  • Core: apologises to the bell; counts ships; climbs the bell rope barefoot
  • Quirk: prays for sailors by name
