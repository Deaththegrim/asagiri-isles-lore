Exile Kanbe

An exile, fifty, walking the marked way across the glacier with a sled, a spear and a lantern, looking for an overdue Warden patrol. In person: weathered exile; frost; heavy furs; a spear and lantern.

Home Ground: Marker Line at Glacier Road, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Hoarse and steady; he talks like someone conserving warmth, a few words per breath. He blows on his hands between sentences. Tell: when he hears a hunter's horn he goes silent and stays silent.
Combat Role: a spear and the ice itself.
Relationship Hook: Help him find the patrol and keep his secret.

Personality

  • Core: checks every marker; never hurries on ice; hums old marching songs
  • Quirk: conserves words
