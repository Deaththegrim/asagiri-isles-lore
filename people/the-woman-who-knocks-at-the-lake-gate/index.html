The Woman Who Knocks at the Lake Gate

A thin drowned oni-woman, her refugee's bundle still strapped to her back, lake weed in her hair, two small horns like snapped reeds. She stands outside the lake gate every night and knocks, slowly, three times, and again. She leaves the refugees on the shore alone. When the gate's slot opens she reaches through it for whoever stands behind, and the cold water that runs from her floods the shore to the knee. Guards at the gate have drowned standing up. A Grudge-Born oni: it was a person once. In person: thin drowned oni-woman with a refugee's bundle; lake weed; small snapped-reed horns; knocking; flood water.

Home Ground: Lake Gate Post, After the War at Seimei, Behind Two Walls, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: A cold, patient whisper that asks to be let in, and asks again. A cold, patient whisper, water in every pause. Tell: she knocks three times on whatever is nearest before she answers.
Combat Role: the woman who knocks stops knocking
Relationship Hook: Learn what Oshizu lost, and it listens instead of fighting.

Personality

  • Core: repeats one fragment of its old life; stops for the one it waits for; unmoved by threats or bribes
  • Quirk: never says what it wants
