Old Umbrella, Host of the Guarded Lane

The hundred-year-old paper umbrella after the war, hopping on its handle through the lane's teahouses and greeting visitors as a host still. The merchant who bought the lane died in the war. In person: an old red paper umbrella with one big eye and a long tongue; a mourning ribbon tied to its handle.

Home Ground: Empty Teahouse, After the War at Haunted Teahouse Lane, The Guarded Lane, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Creaky, courteous and very old-fashioned, like a host from a hundred years ago. Creaky, courteous host's formality from another century, welcoming every guest by rank. Creaks between sentences. Tell since the war: it creaks a long note on the merchant's name, like a door that will not quite close.
Combat Role: a whirl of paper and a long tongue; the lane's objects join in.
Relationship Hook: Persuade the merchant to let the objects stay and Old Umbrella frees the workers.

Personality

  • Core: greets everyone as a host; sticks out its tongue at rudeness; remembers the teahouses' best days
  • Quirk: hops on its handle
