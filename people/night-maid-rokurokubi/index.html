Night-Maid Rokurokubi

A courteous inn maid whose neck stretches across the room at night. Rokurokubi frighten and bite sleepers. A stock foe, one of many. In person: inn maid with an impossibly long neck.

Speaking Style: Polite and strange, a maid's courtesy with words that come from slightly too high. She forgets what she was saying and starts again. Tell: when her neck stretches her voice drifts away and back.
Combat Role: the neck and the bite
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: stretches at night; bites; forgets
  • Quirk: courteous by day
