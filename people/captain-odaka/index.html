Captain Odaka

Captain Odaka of the Brine Tide, forty-five, one of its three senior captains: a big, laughing smuggler with gold teeth and a boarding axe, who settles quarrels and divides cargoes in the back room of the Drowned Lantern. In person: big laughing smuggler captain; gold teeth; braided beard; salt-stained coat; a boarding axe; lantern light.

Home Ground: Back Room at The Drowned Lantern, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Rough, laughing and dangerous; he speaks in toasts and threats in the same breath. Rough laughter and toasts, threats in the same breath as a cheer. Bangs his cup. Tell: when he is deciding whether to kill someone, he stops laughing and refills your cup.
Combat Role: boarding axe and a crew at his back; he prefers to leave by sea.
Relationship Hook: Find him a way to keep the auction's gold without drowning his own crew and he owes you a ship.

Personality

  • Core: bites coins; laughs at danger; never drinks with a crew he means to drown
  • Quirk: toasts before threats
