Old Nurse Tsuru

An old woman, eighty-five, dying, sheltering in the temple's guest cells. She was a nurse at the imperial palace for fifty years. In person: frail old palace nurse; thin white hair; kind wet eyes; plain grey robes.

Home Ground: Guest Cells at Mountain Temple of the Seven Falls, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Frail, kind and urgent; she has a confession and not much time. Frail and urgent, breathless with a confession she has carried for years. She clutches at sleeves and repeats 'listen, listen'. Tell: when the princess is named she weeps, and keeps talking through it.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Hear her confession and decide who else hears it.

Personality

  • Core: holds hands while she talks; drifts off mid-sentence; prays for forgiveness
  • Quirk: asks after Ayame
