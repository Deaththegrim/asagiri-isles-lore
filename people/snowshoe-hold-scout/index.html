Snowshoe Hold Scout

A Hold scout on snowshoes in a black hunter's coat with a sun-steel knife and a horn. Hold scouts check every cart on the supply road and treat strangers as demons until proven otherwise. A stock foe, one of many. In person: hold scout; black hunter's coat; sun-steel knife; horn.

Speaking Style: Short and watchful, every word a challenge. Tell: when she trusts you, she uses your name.
Combat Role: a sun-steel knife and a horn for the Hold
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: horn ready; suspicious of strangers; counts carts
  • Quirk: checks breath in the cold
