Shrine Maiden Hayashida Yae

A shrine maiden of the plain's sun shrine, who dances the dawn rite and blesses the first sheaf of every harvest. In person: young shrine maiden; sunlight; rice fields.

Home Ground: Dawn Torii at Sun-Rite Shrine of the Plain, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Calm and bright like early morning, a shrine maiden who greets the sun every dawn. Bright and calm as early morning, a soft bell-like lilt on the sun rite's words. Tell: when the masks are mentioned her brightness wavers.
Combat Role: a sun sutra and a bell.
Relationship Hook: Help fund the dawn rite and she teaches you a sun sutra.

Personality

  • Core: dances while she sweeps; blesses every sheaf; worries about the masks
  • Quirk: greets the sun
