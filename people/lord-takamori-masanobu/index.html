Lord Takamori Masanobu

The Regent: lord of House Takamori, sixty years old, who has ruled the isles in the dying emperor's name for twenty years. He is courteous, patient and tired, keeps the councils, the taxes and the great houses in balance by memory and nerve, and is said never to have lost a duel or an argument. The common people think him order itself; the other houses think him a thief with good manners. In person: elderly samurai lord; grey topknot; lined stern face; dark calculating eyes; layered formal kimono; crane crest; sword; lamplight.

Home Ground: Council Hall at Miyako Castle, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Quiet, unhurried, every sentence weighed before it leaves him. He never raises his voice; the room leans in instead. Pauses a full breath before any answer and lets the silence work for him. Clicks his fan shut on the last word of a decision. Tell: when he is cornered he becomes more courteous, not less, and his sentences get shorter.
Combat Role: one draw-cut from stillness, then the guards; he fights only when the fight is already decided.
Relationship Hook: Bring him the lost courier or the tax convoy and he remembers a sword with no house; take a side in the succession and he weighs you as a piece.

Personality

  • Core: answers a question with another question; remembers every name and every debt; lets silence do his threatening
  • Quirk: never sits with his back to a screen
