Collector Moriya Tatsuzo

A debt collector in a guild jacket with a ledger and a brush, thirty, who arrived at Saltpan two days ago and has not left. In person: neat debt collector; guild jacket; a ledger and a brush; a red thread round the wrist.

Home Ground: Salt Pans at Saltpan Village, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Polite and reasonable, always sorry for the trouble. Polite and sorry, a little bow in the voice, endless apologies. Tell: asked a guild question he cannot answer, he apologises twice and changes the subject to mules.
Combat Role: he runs; the Lanterns come later.
Relationship Hook: Ask him a guild question only a guild man would know and his smile slips.

Personality

  • Core: apologises before every question; writes down mule numbers; eats with the headwoman uninvited
  • Quirk: keeps a red thread on his wrist
