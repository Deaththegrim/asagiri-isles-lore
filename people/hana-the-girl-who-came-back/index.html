Hana, The Girl Who Came Back

A girl of twelve who hides in the Arms Caves and steals from the Brine Tide's cache, taller now and harder to catch. She has been to the Drowned Lantern asking for the captain who carried her crate. In person: thin runaway girl; tangled black hair; wary large eyes; a smuggler's coat cut down to fit; barefoot; faint glow under the skin.

Home Ground: Dry Gallery, After the War at Smugglers' Caves, The Arms Caves, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: A small, careful voice; she answers questions with questions and goes silent when she hears boots. Small and careful, answering questions with questions, very polite. Breathes quickly when afraid. Tell since the war: when the sea is out of earshot she hums to keep something in her quiet.
Combat Role: runs and hides; when cornered, the beast wakes.
Relationship Hook: Win her trust with food and patience, and keep the boots away, and she lets you take her somewhere safe.

Personality

  • Core: answers questions with questions; shares food with crabs; hums to the thing inside her
  • Quirk: hides at the sound of boots
