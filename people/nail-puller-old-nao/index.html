Nail-Puller Old Nao

An old woman who lives in the empty shrine under the weeping cedar and pulls the curse-nails from its bark one by one. In person: elderly woman pulling iron nails from a huge weeping cedar's bark with pliers; empty shrine; ash.

Home Ground: Empty Shrine at Weeping Cedar, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Flat and patient, an old woman who pulls curse-nails and talks to the tree. Flat and patient, talking to the tree between pulls, a grunt with every nail. Tell: when the biwa plays she stops speaking entirely.
Combat Role: pliers and salt, and a run for the shrine.
Relationship Hook: Help her pull the last nails and the jorogumo starves.

Personality

  • Core: talks to the tree; pulls one nail a day; counts nails
  • Quirk: never looks at the biwa player
