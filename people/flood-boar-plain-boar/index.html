Plain Boar, Flood Boar

A tusked boar grown fat on the war's ruined paddies, rooting the broken dikes by day now that nobody guards the fields. A stock foe, one of many. In person: tusked wild boar; heavier, with a spear scar down one flank; caked in flood mud.

Speaking Style: Grunts, quick and wet, rising into a squeal when it turns. It snorts at the ground. Tell since the war: it grunts at the sound of a spade, as if it has learned what spades mean.
Combat Role: a straight charge
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: charges straight; feeds by day; fears nothing on the dikes now
  • Quirk: roots at night
