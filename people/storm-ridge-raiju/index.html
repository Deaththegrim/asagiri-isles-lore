Storm-Ridge Raiju

A white thunder beast like a lynx of lightning with a crackling tail, sleeping in the steam until the storms wake it. Storm raiju fall from the clouds with the lightning and scorch whatever they land on. A stock foe, one of many. In person: raiju; white thunder beast like a lynx of lightning with a crackling tail.

Speaking Style: A crackling snarl, thunder under the words. Tell: in the steam it purrs like a cat.
Combat Role: a lightning pounce from the storm
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: howls at storms; sleeps in steam; chases thunder
  • Quirk: sparks when angry
