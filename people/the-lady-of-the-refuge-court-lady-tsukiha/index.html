Lady Tsukiha, The Lady of the Refuge Court

The nine-tailed lady of the Fox Court after the war, who has taken in yokai and half-bloods fleeing the war and holds the wood's edge as her own. She has kept the Regent's envoy waiting a month. In person: nine-tailed fox lady; tall and regal; long white hair with gold ornaments; white and gold robes; nine pale tails, always there now.

Home Ground: The Lady's Pavilion, After the War at Halls of the Nine-Tailed Lady, The Court of Refuge, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Amused, unhurried and very old; every sentence is a bargain stated as a courtesy. Amused and unhurried, every bargain dressed as a courtesy, every threat as a kindness. She laughs behind her sleeve and lets you wonder at what. Tell since the war: her laughter behind the sleeve comes later and lasts shorter, and she lets you see it.
Combat Role: nobody who fights her finds the right room; fox-fire and illusion, then nine tails of cold fire.
Relationship Hook: Bring a gift and good manners and she offers a bargain; take it and you owe her.

Personality

  • Core: states bargains as courtesies; never forgets a promise; laughs behind her sleeve
  • Quirk: counts favours owed in tails
