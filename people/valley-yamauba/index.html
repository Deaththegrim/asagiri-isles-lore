Valley Yamauba

A tiny bent crone with a frost-blue shawl and a bald, ice-flecked scalp in a snow-buried cottage, offering hot broth to the lost. The valley yamauba shelters the polite and eats the rude, and even the snow woman leaves her be. A stock foe, one of many. In person: elderly crone; yamauba; tiny bent bald old woman with a deeply wrinkled face; frost-blue shawl.

Speaking Style: Warm and creaking, broth offered the moment a guest sits. Tell: a rude guest makes her laugh go wide and cold.
Combat Role: a lunge from the hearth
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: laughs too wide; judges manners; speaks to the snow
  • Quirk: offers broth
