Ferryman Gin

An off-duty ferryman of the Veil Court, who looks fifty, drinking in the teahouse below the fortress and not talking about work. In person: tired Veil Court ferryman; grey stubble; black officer's coat open; a cup of tea.

Home Ground: Ferrymen's Rest: Tea Room at Ferrymen's Rest, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Tired and dry, but after the third cup he counts aloud. Tired and dry, short sentences with a sip of sake between each one. He calls passengers 'fare' instead of their names. Tell: after the third cup he starts counting the souls he lost on the crossing, softly, and cannot stop until he reaches the end.
Combat Role: a ferryman's blade, patient and heavy.
Relationship Hook: Buy him the third cup and he tells you where the dead go missing.

Personality

  • Core: never sits facing the fog; knows every ferryman by name; tips badly
  • Quirk: counts aloud after the third cup
