Mission-Hall Steward Kiri

Steward of Kagegi's mission hall, who posts the village's public work for outsiders beside the secret work she never shows them. In person: young woman; shinobi mission steward; needles.

Home Ground: Mission Hall at Kagegi Village, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Clipped and fast, a shinobi steward's whisper that never wastes a word. A clipped shinobi whisper, every word chosen, never a wasted breath. Tell: testing you with a lie, she adds one word too many.
Combat Role: a thrown needle and gone before you blink.
Relationship Hook: Finish three outsider bounties and she offers you one from the other board.

Personality

  • Core: whispers; reads lips across the hall; tests outsiders with small lies
  • Quirk: never sits
