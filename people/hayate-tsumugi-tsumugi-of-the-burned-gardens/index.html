Hayate Tsumugi, Tsumugi of the Burned Gardens

A Hayate ridge-runner after the war, twenty-three, the village's fastest scout still, who laughs louder than ever. The healers' gardens were trampled in the Kagegi raid and she works them now, wounded beside her. In person: bubbly wind kunoichi; short messy light brown hair; bright eyes with shadows; grey-blue gear with a full herb pouch; grinning too hard.

Home Ground: Healers' Gardens, After the War at Hayate Ridge Village, Burned on the Ridge, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Bubbly, teasing and quick, laughing at everything; the laugh stops for real things. Bubbly teasing, laughs at everything including herself, nicknames for everyone. She drums her fingers in a run of three. Tell since the war: her laugh is louder than before and stops sooner.
Combat Role: speed, herbs and a short blade; she fights running and patches you after.
Relationship Hook: Run the ridges with her and keep the secret she tests you with.
Courtship Style: Teases, tests, laughs; the first serious sentence she says to you is the confession.
Jealousy Style: Laughs louder at the rival's jokes, then disappears up the ridge until you follow.

Personality

  • Core: tests people with secrets; runs instead of walking; hums healing songs when nobody listens
  • Quirk: laughs at everything
