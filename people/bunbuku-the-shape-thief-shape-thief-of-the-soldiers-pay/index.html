Bunbuku the Shape-Thief, Shape-Thief of the Soldiers' Pay

A round young tanuki who no longer sells travellers' faces: he wears soldiers' faces now and collects back pay from the houses' paymasters, who paid half their armies in leaf money anyway. In person: round tanuki yokai; a soldier's straw hat over the leaf; a mischievous grin gone sly; a stolen paymaster's satchel.

Home Ground: Bamboo Corridor, After the War at Whispering Bamboo, The Bamboo of Written Names, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Giggling and fast, every sentence a dare. Giggling and fast, borrowed voices tried on mid-sentence, every line a dare. Tell since the war: he touches his sleeve, where the face is folded, whenever he lies.
Combat Role: a puff of smoke and someone else's face.
Relationship Hook: Show him the face he stole and he trades it back for a better joke.

Personality

  • Core: wears soldiers' faces; trades in back pay; flees from dogs
  • Quirk: giggles at his own jokes
