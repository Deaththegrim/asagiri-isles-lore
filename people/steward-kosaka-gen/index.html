Steward Kosaka Gen

Steward of House Hiwada, forty-five, thin and careful, who keeps the house's accounts, its grain and, these days, the barred stall at the end of the stables. Riders call him the lord's conscience and the lord's abacus. In person: thin steward; neat grey hair; careful eyes; plain brown kimono with a sun-arrow crest; an abacus; keys; nervous horses.

Home Ground: Hiwada Stables at Hiwada Horse Fields, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Soft and exact, a bookkeeper among horsemen; he apologises before saying anything unwelcome. Soft, tidy sentences, apologising before he contradicts anyone ('forgive me', once, not every time). Clears his throat before a figure. Tell: when he must deliver bad news to his lord he reads it as a list, as if a list could not be blamed.
Combat Role: none; he hides behind the horses and calls the riders.
Relationship Hook: Help him raise the debt money without the lord knowing and he opens the barred stall for you.

Personality

  • Core: apologises before bad news; counts under his breath; dislikes the capital
  • Quirk: feeds the barred stall himself
