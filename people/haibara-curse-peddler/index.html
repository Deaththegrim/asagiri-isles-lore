Haibara Curse-Peddler

A smiling old woman with a tray of charms that are not charms, sold to the bereaved by the waystation. Curse-peddlers sell revenge to the grieving and collect the price from the cursed. A stock foe, one of many. In person: smiling curse-peddler with a tray of dark charms and hair-bound dolls; grey banners; ashen waystation.

Speaking Style: Warm and poisonous. Warm grandmotherly comfort with poison under it, soft 'dear's and 'poor thing's. She hums a lullaby. Tell: refused, the warmth drops off like a mask.
Combat Role: a curse from the tray, then a word
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: offers a fair price; never touches iron; laughs at prayers
  • Quirk: smiles at the grieving
