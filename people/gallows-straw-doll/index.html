Gallows Straw Doll

A nailed straw doll that climbed off the gallows post and walks the crossroads at the hour of the ox. Gallows dolls hunt whoever the doll was nailed against and anyone who touches the nails. A stock foe, one of many. In person: straw doll curse with rusty nails.

Speaking Style: A dry rustle, one name repeated. Tell: at the magistrate's name its nails rattle.
Combat Role: rusty nails and a curse mark
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: nails rattle; hunts one name; burns easily
  • Quirk: walks at the hour of the ox
