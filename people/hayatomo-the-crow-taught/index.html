Hayatomo the Crow-Taught

A lean young swordsman, twenty-two, who fights the open bouts barefoot under a borrowed name and wins with jumps nobody in the Sand Ring has seen. In person: young man; lean barefoot swordsman; messy black hair; black feather tucked; plain hakama.

Home Ground: The Sand Ring at Tournament Grounds, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Bright and cocky, a grin in every line. Cocky and bright, quick jokes, a crow's caw of a laugh. Tell: asked his real name, he answers with someone else's and grins too wide.
Combat Role: leaps and cuts from above, never a straight line.
Relationship Hook: Cross blades with him and he grins and names the peak before he names himself.

Personality

  • Core: laughs when cut; bows to crows; never gives his real name
  • Quirk: fights barefoot
