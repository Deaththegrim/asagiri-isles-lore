Pilgrim-Post Monk Hakuun

Monk of Tasogare's pilgrim post, who keeps the board of the dead who would not go: the masked, the drowned and the lingering, with the temple's offering for each. In person: mature man; calm monk with prayer beads and shaved head; grey robes.

Home Ground: Pilgrim Post at Tasogare, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: A monk's slow calm with a dry joke at the end of every grim notice. Slow monk's calm with a dry joke landing at the end, beads clicking. Tell: asked about the Veil Court, the jokes stop.
Combat Role: a monk's staff and a ferry rite he swore never to use.
Relationship Hook: Bring proof a mask is ended and he tells you why he left the Veil Court.

Personality

  • Core: jokes about death; counts prayer beads; will not cross the Veil again
  • Quirk: watches the torii
