Watchfire Frost Spirit, Frost of the Lit Hill

A pale shape of drifting snow with a woman's face that no longer hisses at the watchfire: it burns every night as the sign the line holds, and she circles it as if guarding it. A stock foe, one of many. In person: frost spirit; pale woman's face; embers frozen in her drifting snow.

Speaking Style: A cold hiss like wind through a crack, words whispered between gusts. Tell since the war: she circles the fire sunwise now, as if keeping a vigil.
Combat Role: a whiteout and a freezing touch
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: guards the fire; freezes scouts; remembers warmth
  • Quirk: circles the fire
