Widow Tamaki of the Burned Fleet

A sailor's widow on Lantern Row, twenty-eight, whose husband burned with the Mizuchi fleet at its moorings. She mends nets for the Brine Tide now because nobody else pays. In person: young woman; sailor's widow; tied hair; a man's navy coat too big for her.

Home Ground: Lantern Row, After the War at Saikai, The Debt Harbour, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Soft and tired, a sailor's widow who apologises for the smell of the sea. Soft and tired, apologising for the smell of the sea. Tell: when the bought captain laughs nearby she pulls the net tighter.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Bring the bought captain to the admiral and she will mend your nets for life.

Personality

  • Core: wears her husband's coat; never takes Tide silver directly; watches the captain drink
  • Quirk: mends nets while she talks
