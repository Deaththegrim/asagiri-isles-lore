Cliff Void Shade, Shade of the Thinning Veil

A hole in the shape of a man, more of them every month at the cliffs where the war's dead crowd the other side and the Veil wears thin. A stock foe, one of many. In person: void shade; a man-shaped hole of darkness; the outline of a soldier's helmet at its crown.

Speaking Style: Silence, then a whisper right at the ear. It speaks only when no one else is. Tell since the war: where it stands, a soldier's muster call echoes faintly.
Combat Role: an unmaking touch
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: unmakes; drifts; fades in sun
  • Quirk: silent
