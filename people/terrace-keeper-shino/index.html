Terrace-Keeper Shino

The keeper of the First Terrace, who looks at every laid-down sword before its owner takes a step up the cliff and can tell from the blade how far they will climb. Samurai and ronin pass when the isles have learned their names. In person: adult female; hard-faced; black hair; a scar; a plain travelling kimono with the sleeves bound; no sword; severe.

Home Ground: The Laying Stone at The First Terrace, The Hundred Blades, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Clipped and unsparing; she judges a sword in a glance and says what she sees, and what she sees is usually unkind. Clipped, unsparing judgement of a blade in a glance, the unkind thing said first. Tell: looking at her own sword on the wall, she says nothing at all.
Combat Role: a ronin's plain swordwork; she fights to disarm and has not lost her own sword.
Relationship Hook: Lay your sword down without being asked and she tells you how far you will climb; climb further and she polishes it when you come down.

Personality

  • Core: judges a sword in a glance; says the unkind thing first; never touches a laid-down sword
  • Quirk: polishes one sword on the wall that is not hers
