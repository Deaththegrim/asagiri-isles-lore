Glacier Fort Warden

A warden in heavy furs with seal-chains and paper talismans, a spear and an iron lantern. Vault Wardens guard the Sealing Vaults and hunt anyone carrying a beast's power. A stock foe, one of many. In person: vault warden; seal-chains; paper talismans; spear and iron lantern.

Speaking Style: Cold and hard, words chipped like ice, little said and nothing repeated. Wardens stamp their feet against the cold. Tell: when the seal hums they all look down at once.
Combat Role: seal-chains, then the spear
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: checks seals; never trusts hosts; speaks little
  • Quirk: binds rather than kills
