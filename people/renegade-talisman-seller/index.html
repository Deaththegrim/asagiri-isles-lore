Renegade Talisman-Seller

A thin renegade onmyoji with star charts tucked in his sleeves and a bag of stolen Bureau talismans. Renegades sell the Bureau's stolen wards from the old observatory and bind anyone who comes to take them back. A stock foe, one of many. In person: thin renegade onmyoji; star charts; a bag of talismans.

Speaking Style: Nervous and wheedling, prices and star signs mixed together. Tell: threatened, he offers the whole bag.
Combat Role: a binding slip thrown, then running for the stairs
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: sells mid-fight; trips over his robes; begs before he binds
  • Quirk: reads the stars aloud
