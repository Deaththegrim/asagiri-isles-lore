Innkeeper Jinbei, Innkeeper of the Last Inn East

Keeper of the Seventh Station Inn after the war, forty-seven, who pays the Red Lanterns and the Regent's soldiers both and keeps a dead lord's baggage in his storeroom. In person: nervous plump innkeeper; thinner; sweaty smile; brown apron; two lanterns over his gate, one red.

Home Ground: Honjin Rooms, After the War at Seventh Station Inn, The Last Inn East, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Hearty, nervous and eager to please; he agrees with everyone and sweats. Hearty, eager, too many apologies and too much agreement. Wipes his hands on his apron audibly. Tell since the war: he lowers his voice near the storeroom, as if the dead lord's baggage could hear him price it.
Combat Role: none; he hides in the kitchen.
Relationship Hook: Warn the lord's retinue, or stand guard, and he will never forgive you; or he will thank you forever, if the bandits lose.

Personality

  • Core: sweats when lords arrive; polishes the red lantern himself; counts the rooms twice
  • Quirk: agrees with everyone
