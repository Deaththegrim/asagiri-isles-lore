Barrow-Robber Ronin, Robber of the Opened Barrow

A ronin who no longer needs a crowbar: the barrow opened from the inside, and he picks up what the risen warriors drop on the moor. A stock foe, one of many. In person: ragged ronin; two ornate old grave-swords now; a crowbar slung unused; frost on his shoulders that never melts.

Speaking Style: Easy and greedy, quick to put a price on things, a chuckle before a threat. Tell since the war: he glances over both shoulders before he speaks, one for each sword.
Combat Role: the grave-sword drawn fast, the crowbar to finish
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: looks behind him now; tests every edge; laughs at curses less
  • Quirk: sells to anyone
