Dealer Tomekichi, Dealer in War Relics

A relic dealer of the ash market after the war, forty-two, selling relics from the battlefields: cursed swords pulled from the dead, banners that still bleed, grudge-jars labelled by army. In person: slick relic dealer; gloved hands; neat moustache; a fine coat with ash at the hems; a stall of battlefield swords wrapped in prayer paper.

Home Ground: Night Stalls, After the War at Haibara Ash Market, Market of War Relics, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Smooth and quick; he describes curses like vintages. Smooth vintner's descriptions of curses, savouring the words. Tell since the war: describing a Sekigane blade, he talks too fast and wipes his gloves after.
Combat Role: none; he throws a relic and runs.
Relationship Hook: Trace the killing comb and he names his guild buyer to save himself.

Personality

  • Core: describes curses like vintages; wears gloves always; laughs politely
  • Quirk: never touches his own stock
