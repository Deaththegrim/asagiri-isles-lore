Tarobo the Great Tengu, The Peak Above the War

The great tengu after the war, who kept his court out of the fighting and trained twice as many students. He laughs from the Feather Throne at the houses' envoys climbing up to hire his swordsmen. In person: great tengu; huge winged mountain spirit; red face; long nose; wild white hair; black wings; yamabushi robes; a great sword; seated.

Home Ground: Feather Throne, After the War at Tengu Peak, The Peak Above the War, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: A huge, slow voice like wind on a crag; he insults and teaches in the same breath. Huge and slow, like wind across a crag, every lesson delivered as an insult and every insult as a lesson. He snorts at weakness. Tell since the war: he insults the houses' envoys in the same breath as his students, and only the students learn anything.
Combat Role: wind and sword; you are thrown off the peak before you finish drawing.
Relationship Hook: Climb the peak and survive his lessons and he teaches you; complain and you fall.

Personality

  • Core: insults while teaching; laughs like a gale; never repeats a lesson
  • Quirk: throws students off the peak
