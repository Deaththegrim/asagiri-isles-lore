Granary Sergeant Yamabe Kihachi

The granary's new sergeant, thirty-four, who took Ishida's spear from the gate stone and keeps the doors under the guild's seal now. The guild owns the rice; his men only guard it. In person: foot soldier sergeant; short beard; conical iron hat; worn lacquered cuirass; an old spear with a cloth tied to it.

Home Ground: Granary Floor, After the War at Shogunate Rice Granary, Under Guild Seal, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Blunt and tired, orders shouted as if from the gate, never a word of thanks to anyone. Shouts as if across a gate, then drops to a mutter mid-word. Counts under his breath. Tell: near the night sacks he stops shouting altogether.
Combat Role: a spear at the gate with two men either side.
Relationship Hook: Help him feed the plain one night's rice and he looks away from anything else.

Personality

  • Core: counts sacks aloud; eats standing up; hates the guild's clerks
  • Quirk: salutes Ishida's stone each morning
