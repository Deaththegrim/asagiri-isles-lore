Hermit Shirakumo, Shirakumo Under the Falls

The white-bearded hermit after the war, who still practises the same cut under the falls every morning while the temple below nurses the wounded of both villages. In person: long white beard and hair; bare feet; grey robes soaked; an old katana held low; serene.

Home Ground: Falls Courtyard, After the War at Mountain Temple of the Seven Falls, The Falls Temple at War, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Slow and warm, amused by everything, answering questions with smaller questions. Slow, warm and amused, questions answered with smaller questions. Tell since the war: he asks every student how old they are before he asks what they want to learn.
Combat Role: precise angled cuts named as they fall, then all nine together.
Relationship Hook: Climb to him and stay after the first line.

Personality

  • Core: practises under the falls; never wears shoes; laughs at serious students
  • Quirk: answers with questions
