Master Hai, Master of the Overflowing Wells

The oldest master of the Grudge Well sorcerers after the war, seventy-two, running a bottling house that cannot keep up: the war poured a generation of grudges into the wells. In person: severe grey sorceress; ash-dusted grey robes; an iron ladle; tired eyes.

Home Ground: Bottling House, After the War at Grudge Well Sanctum, The Overflowing Wells, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Dry, old and businesslike; she prices curses like rice. Dry, businesslike, prices curses like rice, never wastes a word. Tell since the war: she names a curse's price and then, under her breath, how many it came from.
Combat Role: a bottled curse thrown, then her apprentices.
Relationship Hook: Help her stop Grey Mouth and she sells you a curse at cost.

Personality

  • Core: prices curses like rice; never looks into the deepest well; keeps accounts in ash
  • Quirk: seals every bottle herself
