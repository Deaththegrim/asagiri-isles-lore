Student Kunimitsu, Dreamer of the White Hare

A Bureau student, twenty-one, who wakes every dawn from the white hare's dream with tears on his face and no longer ties the Talisman Master's knot. In person: young man; gaunt onmyoji student; short black hair grown out; white robes; a white sash worn untied; dark rings under the eyes.

Home Ground: Sleeping Rows, After the War at Bureau Dormitories, Dormitories of the Dreamers, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Earnest and fierce, a believer quoting his teacher. Earnest and fierce, creed phrases in his mouth, a student's quick breath. Tell since the war: he starts every sentence about the creed and stops halfway.
Combat Role: fire talismans from behind a ward line of students.
Relationship Hook: Argue the creed with him honestly and he listens, for the first time in a year.

Personality

  • Core: lends notes to everyone; sleeps badly; questions the creed now
  • Quirk: recites the creed at meals
