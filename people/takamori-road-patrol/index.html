Takamori Road Patrol

A young Takamori retainer in blue road armour with the crane crest, a sword and a stack of travel papers. Road patrols ask for papers in the regent's name and take a bribe in their own. A stock foe, one of many. In person: young man; samurai retainer; a sword and papers.

Speaking Style: Proud and young, regent's phrases worn like armour. Tell: offered a duel, his voice rises with hope.
Combat Role: a samurai's cut by the book, the column called if he loses
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: quotes the regent; itches for a duel; pockets bribes
  • Quirk: checks papers twice
