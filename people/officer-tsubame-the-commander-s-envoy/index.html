Officer Tsubame, The Commander's Envoy

A Veil Court officer in Shirokawa after the war, the first in a century to come north. She has stopped asking about deserters. In person: Veil officer; short black hair; cold eyes; black coat with a white sash; a sealed pardon in her hand.

Home Ground: Exiles' Hall, After the War at Shirokawa, Behind the Fissure, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Flat, formal and unreadable; she asks questions like a ledger. Flat, formal and unreadable; she asks questions like entries in a ledger, one at a time. She waits a full beat after every answer. Tell since the war: she writes down true answers faster now, and stops waiting the full beat.
Combat Role: a soul-blade, precise.
Relationship Hook: Convince her the exiles are not her enemy and she carries their proof south.
