Admiral Mizuchi Naotora

Admiral of House Mizuchi, sixty, broad, salt-grey and patient, whose river-dragon fleet holds the straits and hangs pirates at Ashiura. He runs the anchorage like a ship and the house like a fleet. In person: broad salt-grey admiral; short beard; stern sea-weathered face; dark blue armoured coat with a river dragon crest.

Home Ground: Admiral's Hall at Mizuchi Naval Anchorage, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: A quarterdeck voice, big and level; he gives orders like weather reports. Quarterdeck projection, level as a calm sea, orders delivered like forecasts. Tell: when his son's fever comes up, the voice stays level but the sentences get shorter.
Combat Role: spear and grappling hook on a rolling deck; a fleet behind him.
Relationship Hook: Bring him the truth of the sleeping warship and he gives you a ship's passage anywhere.

Personality

  • Core: gives orders like weather reports; walks the shipyard at dawn; never shows grief
  • Quirk: keeps a model ship of every vessel he lost
