Masterless Blade

A ronin in a faded kimono and a deep straw hat, a long sword and a short one. Masterless blades guard caravans, collect debts and rob travellers when the contracts dry up. A stock foe, one of many. In person: ronin swordsman; deep straw hat; faded kimono; two swords.

Speaking Style: Dry, bored, every word a price. Dry and bored; every word sounds like a price. Ronin size you up aloud. Tell: when outmatched they laugh, once, and sheathe.
