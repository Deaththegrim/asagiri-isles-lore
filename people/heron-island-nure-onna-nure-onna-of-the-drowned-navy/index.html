Heron Island Nure-onna, Nure-onna of the Drowned Navy

A woman's head on a serpent's long body in the channels choked with sunk warships; she hunts fatter prey now, salvagers and deserters, and the bundle she carries wears a navy sash. A stock foe, one of many. In person: nure-onna; woman's head on a long serpent body; a bundle wrapped in a navy sash; barnacles along her coils.

Speaking Style: Pleading and wet, a soft voice from the shallows asking for help. Water laps under her sentences. Tell since the war: she hums a navy drill call while the bundle grows heavy.
Combat Role: pin and coil
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: grows the bundle heavy; nests in the flagship; waits in the hulls
  • Quirk: asks for help
