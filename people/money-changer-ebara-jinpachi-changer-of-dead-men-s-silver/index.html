Money-Changer Ebara Jinpachi, Changer of Dead Men's Silver

A money changer at the Seat of the Debt after the war, plump and quick still, who changes the war's loot from every army into clean guild silver at a rate that depends on whose loot it is. In person: plump money changer; sly smile; scales; piles of foreign and house coin; a new gold ring.

Home Ground: Money Counters, After the War at Guild Counting House, Seat of the Debt, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Oily and fast, every sentence a little sum; he smiles at coins, not people. Quick oily sums under his breath, a click of the abacus between phrases, 'honoured customer' said a little too warmly. Tell since the war: he glances behind him between sums, and starts the sum over.
Combat Role: none; he shouts for the guild guards and hides behind the counter.
Relationship Hook: Catch him shaving a coin and he offers you a better rate forever.

Personality

  • Core: weighs coins twice; knows every mint mark; never gives exact change first
  • Quirk: smiles at silver
