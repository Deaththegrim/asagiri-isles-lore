Kagegi Rin

A Kurobane shadow-binder, twenty, top of her year at Kagegi and certain of it: sharp-tongued, ruthless in training, and the first apprentice the elders give the hard missions. She calls anyone she is teamed with dead weight until they prove otherwise, and most never do. In person: sharp-tongued kunoichi; black hair; cold dark eyes; dark Kagegi shinobi gear with a Kurobane shadow crest; a kunai; scornful.

Home Ground: Cedar Training Grounds at Kagegi Village, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Sharp, fast and cutting; every sentence is a correction. When she is impressed she says nothing at all. Cutting corrections in rapid fire, impatient sighs between them, never a wasted syllable. She finishes other people's sentences wrong on purpose. Tell: when she is impressed she says nothing at all, and the silence is the compliment.
Combat Role: shadow binding to pin, then kunai; fast, cold and exact.
Relationship Hook: She calls you dead weight; carry your share on a mission and she stops.
Courtship Style: Corrects you constantly, then trains you at night without being asked; the night she lets you take point is the confession.
Jealousy Style: Turns the rival into a training partner, publicly, and beats them; then ignores you for a day.

Personality

  • Core: trains after dark; never eats in front of others; keeps her brother's kunai
  • Quirk: corrects everyone
