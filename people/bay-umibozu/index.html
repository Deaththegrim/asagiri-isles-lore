Bay Umibozu

A black, smooth-headed giant rising waist-deep from a calm sea, with eyes like two drowned moons. The umibozu rises on still nights and asks for a ladle; refuse it and it breaks the hull. A stock foe, one of many. In person: umibozu; enormous black smooth-headed sea giant rising from a calm night sea; pale glowing eyes.

Speaking Style: A voice like the sea floor shifting, each word a long swell with a pause after it. It speaks only once. Tell: refused, the pause breaks into a roar of breaking water.
Combat Role: a fist through the hull and the sea after it
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: asks once; never speaks twice; sinks back at dawn
  • Quirk: rises without a ripple
