Kocho of the Lantern Boat

Hostess of the finest lantern boat on the lake, thirty, who pours sake, plays the shamisen and listens while scholars and merchants say things they regret. In person: elegant boat hostess; hair; knowing smile; layered pink and gold kimono; a shamisen.

Home Ground: Lantern Boat at Lakeside Pleasure Boats, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Silken, amused and musical; she laughs softly at everything and remembers it all. Silken, musical, soft laughter threaded through every line, repeating secrets as if they were jokes. Tell: when something frightens her, she stops laughing and plays her shamisen instead.
Combat Role: none; the boat leaves the shore.
Relationship Hook: Outbid her buyers and she tells you what the examiner said.

Personality

  • Core: never asks a question directly; plays the shamisen when thinking; keeps her own counsel
  • Quirk: laughs softly at everything
