Star-Reader Enju Kagamine

An onmyoji of the old calendar who keeps a lodge full of charts on the Training Ground and teaches onmyoji to bind a true familiar. Her paper servants are folded so finely they pass for birds. In person: composed; black hair; dark eyes; layered onmyoji robes; a folded paper bird; serene.

Home Ground: Masters' Lodges at The Lodges, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Precise, patient and faintly amused; she speaks in complete sentences and waits for the answer she already knows. Complete sentences, patiently paced, the answer begun before the question ends; faint amusement under all of it. Tell: her familiar's name each morning, said like a greeting.
Combat Role: wards and a true familiar; she stands still and the familiar does the fighting.
Relationship Hook: Bring her a name you learned honestly and she folds a servant for you on the spot.

Personality

  • Core: folds paper while she talks; reads the hour from the sky; names her familiar aloud each morning
  • Quirk: answers before the question ends
