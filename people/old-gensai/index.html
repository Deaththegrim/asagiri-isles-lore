Old Gensai

A scarred old foot soldier, sixty, who drills the Straw Masks' farmers with flails, sickles and stolen spears in a clearing above the rice country. He lost an eye at Sekigane's last skirmish and a lord's pension to a tax reform, and he has no patience left for either. In person: scarred old foot soldier; eyepatch; grey stubble; battered jingasa hat; patched padded armour; a spear; gruff.

Home Ground: Flail Drill Clearing at Straw Mask Hill Camp, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Gravel and barracks humour; he curses the recruits fondly and never repeats an order. Gravel and a smoker's cough, barracks swearing used as punctuation. Calls every recruit 'stone-head'. Tell: when he is moved he grumbles more, and the swearing gets gentler.
Combat Role: spear and shield-wall, the line holds or it dies; a knee to whatever gets close.
Relationship Hook: Drill with his farmers or teach them something real and he trusts you with the camp's plans.

Personality

  • Core: counts drills in the old army cadence; shares his sake with the youngest recruit; sleeps with a spear
  • Quirk: spits when lords are named
