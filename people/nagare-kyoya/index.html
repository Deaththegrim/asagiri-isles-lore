Nagare Kyoya

A masterless swordsman who walks the same roads as the character, takes the same jobs a day earlier, and treats every meeting as a joke until the blades come out. In person: adult man; rangy; unshaven; shaggy hair tied back badly; half-lidded eyes; a faded blue kimono patched; straw sandals; a plain katana with a frayed hilt.

Home Ground: Signing Room at Duelists' Teahouse, Fifty Stations Road district, between meetings; a rival meets the character wherever the character is.
Speaking Style: Lazy and few-worded, a drifter's drawl, a smile that does not reach his eyes. Lazy drawl, few words, a grass stalk between his teeth. Tell: when he stops pretending, the drawl goes and the words come clipped.
Combat Role: lazy guard first, then whatever the bout demands; a ronin rival who has studied the character.
Relationship Hook: He will already have taken the job you came for, and offer to split it if you can beat him for it.

Personality

  • Core: lazy until he isn't; steals your job, then buys you a drink; never draws first
  • Quirk: chews a stalk of grass
