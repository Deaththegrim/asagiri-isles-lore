Mourner Asa, The Wife Who Lit Two Lanterns

A young wife at the Pilgrims' Hostel after the war, twenty-seven, who has lit a mourning lantern for her living husband every night for two years. In person: quiet young woman; hair tied plainly; calm eyes; white mourning kimono; a knife in her sleeve.

Home Ground: Lantern Hall, After the War at Pilgrims' Hostel, Hostel of the Living Lanterns, Veilgate district.
Speaking Style: Soft, steady and strange; she speaks of her husband in the past tense though he lives. Soft and steady, a mourner's practised calm, and she speaks of a living man in the past tense. She folds her sleeves while she talks. Tell since the war: she speaks of two lanterns now, one in the past tense and one not.
Combat Role: a knife in the dark, once.
Relationship Hook: Find her daughter in the Records wing and she puts the knife down.

Personality

  • Core: trims the lantern wick often; smiles at mourners; keeps a knife in her sleeve
  • Quirk: speaks of him in the past tense
