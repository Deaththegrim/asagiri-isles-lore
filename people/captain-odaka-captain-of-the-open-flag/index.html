Captain Odaka, Captain of the Open Flag

Captain Odaka of the Brine Tide after the war, forty-seven, one of its senior captains, laughing in the Drowned Lantern's tap room with nothing left to hide. In person: big laughing smuggler captain; more gold teeth; braided beard; a salvaged navy coat; a boarding axe.

Home Ground: Back Room, After the War at The Drowned Lantern, Lantern of the New Masters, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Rough, laughing and dangerous; he speaks in toasts and threats in the same breath. Rough laughter and toasts, threats in the same breath as a cheer. Bangs his cup. Tell since the war: he raises a toast to the girl in the crate before every other toast, and does not say why.
Combat Role: boarding axe and a crew at his back; he prefers to leave by sea.
Relationship Hook: Find him a way to keep the auction's gold without drowning his own crew and he owes you a ship.

Personality

  • Core: bites coins; laughs at danger; never drinks with a crew he means to drown
  • Quirk: toasts before threats
