Giant Hornet Swarm

Hornets as long as a thumb, droning in a cloud. Giant hornets defend their nest and chase intruders for a mile. A stock foe, one of many. In person: swarm of giant hornets around a marked ancient tree.

Speaking Style: A droning buzz that thickens and thins as the swarm moves, with sharp clicks of mandibles. Tell: when smoke drifts in, the drone goes ragged and high.
Combat Role: a swarm; smoke drives it off
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: chases far; stings in numbers; hates smoke
  • Quirk: drones louder when near
