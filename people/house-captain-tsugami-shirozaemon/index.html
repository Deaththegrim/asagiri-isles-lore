House Captain Tsugami Shirozaemon

A Takamori house captain, forty, who has come to Kitsunebi Crossing with twenty men and a list of half-blood names. He bows to the fox priestess every morning and asks the same question. In person: samurai house captain; stern face; lacquered armour with the crane crest; a list.

Home Ground: Fox Shrine Post, After the War at Kitsunebi Crossing, The Last Open Gate, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Hard and courteous, a hunter's patience in every order. Hard courtesy, orders given as patient requests. Tell: reading the list aloud, he skips one name, every time.
Combat Role: a long sword at the front of his company.
Relationship Hook: Make him burn the list and twenty swords go home without a fight.

Personality

  • Core: bows every morning; reads the list again each night; never draws in the shrine
  • Quirk: pays for his tea
