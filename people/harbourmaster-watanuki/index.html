Harbourmaster Watanuki

The guild's harbourmaster, fifty, thin and immaculate, who keeps the harbour post, the pilots and the anchor fees. Every ship in Saikai bay is in his book, and he knows which ones lie about their cargo. In person: thin immaculate harbourmaster; neat grey moustache; sharp eyes; dark blue guild coat with brass buttons; a spyglass and ledger; stern.

Home Ground: Harbour Post at Saikai, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Precise, dry and pedantic; he corrects your pronunciation of ship names. Clipped, pedantic, precise about ship names and tide tables. Corrects mispronunciations mid-sentence. Tell: when he is afraid, he becomes even more pedantic.
Combat Role: none; the harbour watch answers his whistle.
Relationship Hook: Find the escort that took the humming chest north and he shows you the second ledger.

Personality

  • Core: corrects pronunciation; watches the bay with a spyglass at every meal; distrusts anyone in a hurry
  • Quirk: keeps two ledgers
