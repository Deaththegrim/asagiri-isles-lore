Kirisame Raizo

The matriarch's son, forty, a quiet man with a healer's hands who has walked out of the marsh to the navy anchorage with a box of medicines and a white flag. In person: quiet marsh healer-shinobi; long tied hair; tired calm eyes; dark rain-patterned clothes; wary.

Home Ground: Shipyard at Mizuchi Naval Anchorage, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Calm, careful and tired, a healer's patience; he does not smile. Calm healer's patience, careful instructions, no smile in the voice. Tell: when he speaks of peace with the navy, a tired hope creeps in.
Combat Role: needles and fog, and he would rather not.
Relationship Hook: Get him to the admiral alive and the cure to the son, and the marsh opens to you.

Personality

  • Core: does not smile; washes his hands often; watches the sailors' hands
  • Quirk: keeps his white flag folded neatly
