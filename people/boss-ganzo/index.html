Boss Ganzo

Boss of the plain loggers' camp pushing into the wood for the capital's lumber merchants, fifty, broad and impatient. In person: broad logger boss; beard; rolled sleeves; an axe.

Home Ground: Saw Line at Loggers' Camp, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Loud, impatient and greedy; he talks about deadlines. Loud and impatient, talking in deadlines when work is on, his fist on the table for emphasis. He calls workers by their crew numbers. Tell: when the wood is mentioned he blusters louder, which is how you know he's scared.
Combat Role: an axe and thirty loggers.
Relationship Hook: Tell him what the fox gift means before he insults the Court by ignoring it.

Personality

  • Core: swears at trees; counts logs; keeps the fox gift unopened
  • Quirk: talks about deadlines
