Eagle-Cliff Umibozu

A vast black sea-monk's head rising from the cove at night, asking passing boats for a ladle. Umibozu capsize boats that refuse them; give one a ladle and it fills the boat with seawater. A stock foe, one of many. In person: umibozu; vast black sea monk's head rising from a wreck-strewn cove.

Speaking Style: Slow and vast, one request repeated like the tide. Tell: handed a ladle, it laughs like a wave.
Combat Role: a capsizing wave; a bottomless ladle beats it
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: rises silently; patient; capsizes the rude
  • Quirk: asks for a ladle
