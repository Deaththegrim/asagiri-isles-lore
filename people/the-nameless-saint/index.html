The Nameless Saint

A ronin saint who gave up her name on the terraces and keeps a lodge with no mark on its door. She teaches ronin the school that has no name, which answers every school because it belongs to none. In person: lean and weathered; short grey-streaked hair cut without care; a plain faded kimono with the sleeves tied back; a chipped sword; tired eyes.

Home Ground: Masters' Lodges at The Lodges, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Flat, tired and precise; she speaks as little as a thing can be said in and lets silence do the rest. As few words as a thing can be said in, flat and exact, long silences left to do the rest. Tell: her old name spoken anywhere near and the silence lasts too long.
Combat Role: no stance, no school; she moves after the opponent and arrives first.
Relationship Hook: Fight her once without a stance and lose; she asks what you felt, and if you say 'nothing' she nods.

Personality

  • Core: sits in no stance at all; names every school by its first move; eats standing up
  • Quirk: speaks as little as possible
