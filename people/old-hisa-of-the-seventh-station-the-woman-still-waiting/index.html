Old Hisa of the Seventh Station, The Woman Still Waiting

The innkeeper's mother after the war, bright-eyed still, who meets every rider at the gate. Fewer come. In person: bright-eyed elderly woman; a mourning ribbon in her hair.

Home Ground: Honjin Rooms, After the War at Seventh Station Inn, The Last Inn East, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Warm and teasing, always asking travellers for news. Warm and teasing, always asking travellers for road news. Tell since the war: at the gate she still asks every rider for news, and asks one more question than she used to.
Combat Role: none; a broom and a sharp word.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the couriers of twenty years ago and she smiles at one name.

Personality
