The Lantern Mother

The Lantern Mother, head of the Red Lantern Federation, fifty, broad, scarred and draped in a red-lacquered armour coat, who holds court on a fallen log in a ravine full of red lanterns. Every bandit band on the post road pays her a share, and every inn that hangs her lantern is safe. In person: broad scarred bandit queen; grey-streaked hair; amused eyes; red lacquered armour coat; a cleaver; maternal menace.

Home Ground: Mother's Log at Red Lantern Roost, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Low, amused and maternal; she calls her cutthroats 'my children' and her threats sound like bedtime stories. Low, amused and maternal, threats delivered like lullabies. Calls everyone 'child'. Tell: when she is truly angry she stops calling anyone child.
Combat Role: a heavy cleaver and her children; she fights only after the ambush has already won.
Relationship Hook: Sit on her log and eat her food and she offers you work; cross her code and her children come for you.

Personality

  • Core: calls her bandits 'my children'; never raises her voice; counts lanterns at dusk
  • Quirk: feeds anyone who sits on her log
