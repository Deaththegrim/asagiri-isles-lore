Barricade Captain Tora

The Straw Masks' captain of the Oiwake barricade, twenty-six, a farmer's daughter from the Hundred Fields who took the barrier with Old Gensai and held it when he fell. She stamps travellers' papers with a straw mask seal. In person: young woman; peasant army captain; short tousled hair; straw mask pushed up; patched farmer's clothes; a flail; grinning.

Home Ground: Toll Barrier, After the War at Oiwake Station, Behind the Barricade, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Loud, rough and funny, a farm girl's shout trained into a captain's; she gives orders as jokes and means every one. A farm girl's shout trained into orders, a laugh first and the order after. Tell: when the Regent's raid is mentioned she stops laughing and speaks very quietly.
Combat Role: a flail at the barricade gap, forty farmers behind her.
Relationship Hook: Find the guard who sells papers and the barricade is yours to cross whenever you like.

Personality

  • Core: chews rice straw; salutes Gensai's spear; stamps papers too hard
  • Quirk: laughs before she shouts
