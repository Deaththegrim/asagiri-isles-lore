Old Captain Hamada Gennai, The Captain Who Came Back

The old sea captain after the war, who finally looked toward the reef: he sailed a war junk past it for the Regent and lived. He drinks at the Soldiers' Den now, among men who do not know his name. In person: old white-bearded captain; haunted eyes; a war captain's coat too big for him now.

Home Ground: Lantern Row, After the War at Saikai, The Debt Harbour, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: A rough old sailor's voice that trails off before the end of every story. Tell since the war: he stops drinking mid-cup when anyone mentions a lantern, and puts the cup down.
Combat Role: none any more; a cutlass he has not drawn in years.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the woman on Gibbet Rock and he puts down his cup.

Personality

  • Core: drinks alone; never looks at the reef; tips too much
  • Quirk: tells sea stories with the ending missing
