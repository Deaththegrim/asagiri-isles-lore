Proctor Kagegi Isshin

A Kagegi exam proctor, forty, respected and patient, who runs the rank exams in the cedar valley and is known for fairness. Apprentices like him; their parents trust him. In person: respectable shinobi proctor; neat black hair; warm smile that does not reach his eyes; dark Kagegi uniform with a proctor's sash.

Home Ground: Exam Clearing at Valley of the Rank Exams, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Smooth, patient and helpful; a teacher's voice that says the right thing a little too fast. A smooth, helpful teacher's voice, always ready with the right answer a little too fast. He praises students by name and remembers everyone's weak points. Tell: when the dead examiner comes up he smiles before he speaks, and his helpfulness doubles.
Combat Role: traps he laid himself, then shadow binding.
Relationship Hook: He helps you with the exam; that help costs more later.

Personality

  • Core: praises apprentices loudly; visits the trap grove alone; never meets a Yumeno's eyes
  • Quirk: keeps a northern token in his sleeve
