Senior Diviner Okubo Tomonori

A respected senior diviner who sets the Bureau's examinations and is known for failing students who cheat. In person: stern middle-aged senior onmyoji diviner; nervous eyes.

Home Ground: Examination Hall at Bureau of Yin and Yang, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: A lecturing voice that turns brittle near the subject of old exams. A lecturing voice, confident and clipped. Tell: near old exams or the ward stones it turns brittle.
Combat Role: talismans and rank.
Relationship Hook: Mention Senkichi's name and his lecture stops mid-word.

Personality

  • Core: harsh with cheats; avoids the ward stones; sleeps badly
  • Quirk: proud of his record
