Novice Tomo

A novice of the Curse-Hunter Brotherhood, eighteen, who cleans the record room and reads everything she should not. In person: eager novice; short hair; ink-stained hands; plain dark robes with a few prayer strips; secretive.

Home Ground: Record Room at Curse-Hunters' Chapterhouse, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Quick, eager and whispering; she always sounds like she is telling a secret. Whispery, eager, conspiratorial. Glances over her shoulder. Tell: when the founder's journal comes up she goes pale and stops whispering.
Combat Role: none yet.
Relationship Hook: Read the founder's journal with her and the Brotherhood's secret is yours.

Personality

  • Core: reads everything; carries stones without complaint; hides papers in her sleeves
  • Quirk: whispers
