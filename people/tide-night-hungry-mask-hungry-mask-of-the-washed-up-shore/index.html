Tide-Night Hungry Mask, Hungry Mask of the Washed-Up Shore

A ragged spirit in a white mask with a mouth too wide, fat on the war's dead, climbing from the fog every night now, not just on tides; their old masks wash up behind them by the dozen. A stock foe, one of many. In person: hungry mask; bloated ragged spirit; a white mask with a split mouth; a soldier's ghost sash.

Speaking Style: A hungry scream that drops into chewing mutters. It names what it smells. Tell since the war: it screams in a soldier's voice now.
Combat Role: lunge and devour
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: climbs from the fog nightly; screams; eats the dead
  • Quirk: hungers
