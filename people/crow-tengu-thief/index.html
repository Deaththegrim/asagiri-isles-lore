Crow Tengu Thief

A crow-headed tengu with black wings and a stolen sword. Crow tengu steal anything that glitters and fight for their hoard. A stock foe, one of many. In person: crow tengu; crow-headed yokai with black wings and a stolen sword.

Speaking Style: Cawing laughter and chattering boasts, words chopped short like pecks. It clacks its beak at shiny things. Tell: when it spots treasure it stops mid-taunt.
Combat Role: dive from above, grab, gone
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: caws insults; fights in numbers; hoards
  • Quirk: steals shiny things
