Memory-Seller Jirocho

A dead clerk of the Court of the Dead who sells memories he should have filed: a dead man's last summer, a stranger's wedding, a password nobody living knows. He takes isles coin and Otherworld favours both. In person: adult man; thin and stooped; grey skin; ink-stained fingers; a clerk's grey robe with too many pockets; a small locked box; furtive.

Home Ground: The Ledger Hall at The Court of the Dead, The Far Shore, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Whispery, quick and eager to please; he talks like a man selling something he should not have. A whisper that speeds up when selling, a man offering something he should not have, glances over the shoulder audible in the pauses. Tell: the founding memory mentioned and the whisper stops.
Combat Role: none; he runs, and the dead are hard to catch.
Relationship Hook: Buy a memory and keep its owner's name to yourself; he trusts a buyer who does not talk.

Personality

  • Core: whispers when selling; files nothing; looks over his shoulder at the ledger hall
  • Quirk: tastes a memory before selling it
