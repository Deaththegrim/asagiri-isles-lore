Veil Court Patrol Officer, Officer of the Overrun Fortress

An officer of the Veil Court crewing the ferries day and night now the barracks are emptied; patrols are a memory, and the hungry dead walk while the officers row. A stock foe, one of many. In person: Veil Court officer; pale and hollow-eyed; a black officer's coat salt-stained; a soul-blade; an oar callus on both hands.

Speaking Style: Formal, distant. Formal and distant, Court phrasing, every sentence an entry in a case file. Patrol officers state your status before your name. Tell since the war: he speaks formally, then yawns mid-word and does not apologise.
Combat Role: a soul-blade, exact, on the dead first
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: sees the dead; rows the ferry; sweats now
  • Quirk: speaks formally
