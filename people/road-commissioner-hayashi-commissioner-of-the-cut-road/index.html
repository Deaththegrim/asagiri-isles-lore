Road Commissioner Hayashi, Commissioner of the Cut Road

Hayashi Kenmotsu, Road Commissioner after the war, fifty-seven, who runs a road the war cut in three from a long office under its framed charter. Nine stations are off his maps now. In person: stiff grey official; severe face; Warden's dark blue haori; maps with whole sections inked over.

Home Ground: Register Room, After the War at Road Commissioner's Office, The Office of a Cut Road, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Clipped, official and evasive; he answers every question with a regulation. Clipped and official, answering questions with regulation numbers. Shuffles papers as he talks. Tell since the war: he touches the charter's frame before he answers any question about the tolls.
Combat Role: none; his riders and road guards do it.
Relationship Hook: Help him keep the ghost station quiet and he gives you the road; expose it and he loses everything.

Personality

  • Core: quotes regulations; keeps two maps on his desk; sends riders the long way round
  • Quirk: straightens the charter frame
