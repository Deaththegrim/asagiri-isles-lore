Novice Tomo, Keeper of the Overflowing Record

A novice of the Brotherhood after the war, twenty, who keeps the record room alone: the war's broken curses overflow the scrolls. She reads everything still. In person: novice; short hair; ink-stained hands; dark robes; prayer strips; stacks of scrolls.

Home Ground: Record Room, After the War at Curse-Hunters' Chapterhouse, Chapterhouse Under Siege, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Quick, eager and whispering; she always sounds like she is telling a secret. Whispery, eager, conspiratorial. Glances over her shoulder. Tell since the war: she stops whispering mid-word when she hears a voice she cannot place, and starts again lower.
Combat Role: none yet.
Relationship Hook: Read the founder's journal with her and the Brotherhood's secret is yours.

Personality

  • Core: reads everything; carries stones without complaint; hides papers in her sleeves
  • Quirk: whispers
