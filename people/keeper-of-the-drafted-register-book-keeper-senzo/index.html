Book-Keeper Senzo, Keeper of the Drafted Register

The Book-Keeper of the Hundred-Duel Circuit after the war, sixty-two, who sold the great register to the armies and watched them draft the best duellists from it. Half the names in it are dead. In person: stooped book-keeper; spectacles; ink-stained fingers; grey robes; a register with black ribbons tucked between its pages.

Home Ground: Signing Room, After the War at Duelists' Teahouse, Teahouse of the Last Duels, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Dry, fussy and precise; he quotes duel records by number and sighs at sloppy calligraphy. Fussy, dry, quotes duel numbers and sighs at bad calligraphy. Clears his throat before each number. Tell since the war: reading out a duellist's record, he stops at the black ribbon and says which army took them.
Combat Role: none; he hides under the register table.
Relationship Hook: Bring him a duelist's signed book and he copies it; corner him about the copies and he begs you to keep his name out.

Personality

  • Core: quotes records by number; sighs at bad handwriting; eats alone in the signing room
  • Quirk: flinches when a duelist draws
