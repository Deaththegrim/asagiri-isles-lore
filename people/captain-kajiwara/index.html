Captain Kajiwara

Captain of the toll barrier at Oiwake Station, the busiest on the road, forty, well-fed and friendly, who waves the right people through and makes the wrong ones wait in the rain. In person: plump friendly barrier captain; wide grin; Warden's blue coat stretched; a sheaf of papers.

Home Ground: Toll Barrier at Oiwake Station, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Easy, cheerful and corrupt; he calls everyone friend and charges them for it. Easy and friendly, quick to call anyone 'friend', a cheerful price never far behind. Chuckles at his own bribes. Tell: when a superior is near, the friendliness vanishes into formal grunts.
Combat Role: a spear behind twenty guards.
Relationship Hook: Pay his fee and the road opens; need blank papers and he names a price.

Personality

  • Core: calls everyone friend; eats on duty; never looks at a face he has been paid not to see
  • Quirk: keeps blank papers in his sleeve
