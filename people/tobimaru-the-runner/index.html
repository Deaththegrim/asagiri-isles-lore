Tobimaru the Runner

A shinobi runner who carries letters between the quarters and waits at the Gate Ring for the next one, the only person who knows every seam in the Otherworld's map. He is for hire at the mess keeper's board. In person: young adult male; wiry and quick; short dark hair; a runner's cropped shinobi clothes with a satchel; bare feet; breathless grin.

Home Ground: The Ten Posts at The Gate Ring, The Training Ground, in the Otherworld.
Speaking Style: Breathless, quick and friendly; he talks in bursts between arrivals and knows a shortcut to everywhere. Breathless bursts between arrivals, friendly, a shortcut offered to everywhere. Tell: the undelivered letter mentioned and the bursts stop.
Combat Role: a shinobi's footwork and a thrown knife; he does not stay to fight.
Relationship Hook: Run a letter with him and keep up; he shows you a seam between quarters nobody else uses.

Personality

  • Core: talks in bursts; knows a shortcut everywhere; stretches while waiting
  • Quirk: touches each post as he passes
