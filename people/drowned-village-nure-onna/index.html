Drowned Village Nure-onna

A woman's head and long wet hair on a serpent's body, holding a bundle like a baby on the drowned roofs. Nure-onna ask travellers to hold the baby for a moment; the bundle grows heavy as stone while she feeds. A stock foe, one of many. In person: woman's head with long wet black hair.

Speaking Style: Pleading and wet, begging help for the bundle. Tell: after feeding, she weeps over the stone.
Combat Role: the heavy bundle first, then the coil
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: sings to the bundle; coils slowly; weeps after feeding
  • Quirk: asks for help
