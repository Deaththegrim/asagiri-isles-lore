Jizo Offering Thief

A thin youth in a stolen straw cape who steals the rice-balls and coins left at the roadside statues. Offering thieves rob shrines and travellers both, and run at the first sign of a sword. A stock foe, one of many. In person: thin offering thief; rice-balls; row of small stone jizo statues.

Speaking Style: Fast, guilty patter. Quick, guilty patter in a cracking teen voice, apologising and lying in the same breath. Tell: frightened, he names his little sisters without meaning to.
Combat Role: stones, then his heels
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: apologises to the statues; throws stones; lies badly
  • Quirk: eats while he runs
