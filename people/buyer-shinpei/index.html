Buyer Shinpei

A swordsman soaking in the inn's hot spring, thirty, waiting for a commission, who has recognised another buyer. In person: cool swordsman relaxing; wet hair; faint smile; a sword wrapped.

Home Ground: Steam Bath at Hot Spring Inn, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Easy, cool and amused; he talks to the steam. Easy, cool and amused, talking half to you and half to the steam rising from the bath. He hums a gambler's tune. Tell: when the price of a soul-blade is questioned he laughs, too long.
Combat Role: a fine blade and a buyer's patience.
Relationship Hook: Tell him there is another buyer and he panics.

Personality

  • Core: soaks for hours; watches the other buyer; never names his lord
  • Quirk: talks to the steam
