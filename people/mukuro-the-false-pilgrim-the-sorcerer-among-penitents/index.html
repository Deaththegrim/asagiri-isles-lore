Mukuro the False Pilgrim, The Sorcerer Among Penitents

A pilgrim in grey after the war, walking the penitents' road among a thousand soldiers come to atone, praying for the cursed and helping the weak along. In person: kindly-looking grey pilgrim; shaved head; gentle smile; grey robes; a staff; three bottles hidden now.

Home Ground: Ash Road, After the War at Grey Pilgrims' Road, The Penitents' Road, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Gentle, pious and slow; the voice of a kind pilgrim, with something cold underneath. Gentle, pious, slow blessings, something cold under the warmth. Tell since the war: his blessings run longer for soldiers, and his hand lingers on their shoulders.
Combat Role: a curse whispered into a prayer, then a knife.
Relationship Hook: Catch him at his bottle and the pilgrims' road is safe again.

Personality

  • Core: prays loudly; helps the weak carry their packs; never drinks with others
  • Quirk: touches a bottle under his robe
