Shrine-Keeper Akari

A young shrine-keeper, nineteen, who crosses the stepping stones at low tide every day to sweep a shrine nobody visits, because her mother told her the sea's kami would come back if someone kept the lamp shrine clean. In person: young woman; nineteen; slim; long black hair tied with a white paper ribbon; bright brown eyes; a white shrine attendant's kosode with red hakama; a bamboo broom; a small pouch of salt.

Home Ground: Lamp Shrine at The Shrine on the Rock, on Tomoshibi Isle, the island off Saikai.
Speaking Style: Bright and earnest, a shrine attendant's careful words, quick to laugh at herself. Bright, earnest and careful, apologising to the kami mid-sentence, counting under her breath. Tell: about the lighthouse at night her brightness goes thin and quick.
Combat Role: salt and a broom handle, and running.
Relationship Hook: Cross the stepping stones with her at low tide and she will show you what the keeper wrote about the lamp.

Personality

  • Core: earnest to a fault; apologises to the kami before she sweeps; brave about everything except the lighthouse
  • Quirk: counts the stepping stones aloud, every time
