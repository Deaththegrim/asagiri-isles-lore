Elder of the Kodama

The eldest of the kodama, the small pale tree-spirits of the grove older than the empire. It answers questions by echoing them back, a little changed. In person: small pale tree spirit with a round head and dark hollow eyes; tilting its head.

Home Ground: Echo Path at Kodama Grove, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: A small, rustling echo of your own voice, a little wrong. A rustling echo of your own voice, a little wrong, words returned a heartbeat late. Leaves stir under every phrase. Tell: when the trees are threatened the echo becomes a chorus of many small voices.
Combat Role: none; the grove itself turns you around.
Relationship Hook: Take the loggers' marks off the trees and the echoing logger walks free.

Personality

  • Core: tilts its head; clicks when afraid; sits on the marked trees
  • Quirk: echoes your words
