Watch-Captain Okami Tenzen

A lean, wolf-faced captain of the capital's watch with a pipe always in his teeth, who keeps order at the Tournament Grounds and has one technique he trusts. In person: adult man; lean; sharp wolfish face; slicked-back black hair with a loose strand; dark watch uniform with a haori; a long pipe; katana held level; tournament pavilions behind.

Home Ground: House Pavilions at Tournament Grounds, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Clipped and dry, a pipe in his teeth; he gives orders as statements and never repeats one. Clipped statements around a pipe stem, never a question, never repeated. Tell: the Regent's name and he takes the pipe out to say nothing.
Combat Role: a crouched one-handed thrust stance, the off hand aimed along the blade; one lunge, then another.
Relationship Hook: Take his thrust and stay standing.

Personality

  • Core: speaks in statements; never repeats an order; watches hands, not faces
  • Quirk: pipe always in his teeth
