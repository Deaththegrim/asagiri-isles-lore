Shrine Maiden Hayashida Yae, Keeper of the Mourning Shrine

The sun shrine's maiden after the emperor's death, who keeps the rites alone while the priest searches the isles. She dances the dawn rite for a thin harvest and a dead emperor. In person: young shrine maiden in white over red; a mourning ribbon in her hair; the vermilion torii hung with white.

Home Ground: Dawn Torii, After the War at Sun-Rite Shrine of the Plain, Mourning Shrine, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Calm and bright like early morning, a shrine maiden who greets the sun every dawn. Bright and calm as early morning, a soft bell-like lilt on the sun rite's words. Tell since the war: she hums the dawn rite under ordinary speech, as if afraid to let the thread drop.
Combat Role: a sun sutra and a bell.
Relationship Hook: Help fund the dawn rite and she teaches you a sun sutra.

Personality

  • Core: dances while she sweeps; blesses every sheaf; worries about the masks
  • Quirk: greets the sun
