Priest Mokuren, Priest of Dear News

The salt marsh shrine's priest after the war, seventy-two, thin as a reed, who still trades in news. News is worth more than it has ever been. In person: reed-thin marsh priest; bald; bare feet; white robes; cucumbers and folded letters on the altar.

Home Ground: Boardwalk Altar, After the War at Salt Marsh Shrine, Shrine of Dear News, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Soft, reedy and very slow; he speaks to the water before he answers you. Reedy, very slow, speaks to the water first, then to you. Tell since the war: he asks the water its price before he tells it anything now.
Combat Role: none; the kappa answer him.
Relationship Hook: Bring the kappa a gift worth his news and he tells you where the captain is.

Personality

  • Core: leaves cucumbers; never wears sandals; sleeps on the boardwalk
  • Quirk: speaks to the water first
