Master Sorei, Maker of Borrowed Faces

The mask-maker after the war, thirty-one, whose workshop does a trade it never had: soldiers who want to go home as someone else, and widows who want one more night with a face. Customers still meet the old woman at the counter. In the carving room she is ink-handed, patient and more tired than before. In person: mask-maker woman; long black hair tied back; sharp tired amber eyes; ink-stained fingers; a carving knife; a half-finished face.

Home Ground: Carving Room, After the War at Mask-Maker's Workshop, Workshop of Borrowed Faces, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Low, dry and unhurried; she answers a question with the price of answering it. When she is honest her voice drops and slows. Ink-and-patience cadence, pauses to blow dust off a carving, every price stated softly. A fox's soft laugh in the back of her throat. Tell since the war: asked a price for a face from the war, she quotes it lower than she should, and then looks at you.
Combat Role: a glamour first, a ward second, a carving knife last; she fights only when cornered and then without mercy.
Relationship Hook: Ask her for nothing, keep the Talisman Master off her, and the wall comes down a mask at a time.
Courtship Style: Wary, dry and mercenary to start; she prices every kindness until one day she stops charging you.
Jealousy Style: Gives the rival a mask, as a joke, of your face; takes it back that night and apologises in her own voice.

Personality

  • Core: turns most customers away; answers questions with prices; carves when she cannot sleep
  • Quirk: talks to the fox in her chisel
