Sarumaru of the Spring

A kappa, green and beaked, the size of a ten-year-old with an old man's eyes, who moved into the hot spring cave when the island emptied and considers the water his. In person: small green yokai with a beak; a turtle shell; webbed hands and feet; a water-filled dish; old wise eyes; a loincloth; bowing politely.

Home Ground: The Steaming Pool at The Hot Spring Cave, on Tomoshibi Isle, the island off Saikai.
Speaking Style: Wet and croaking, formal manners from another century, a giggle at the end of every threat. Wet croaking courtesy from another century, a giggle at the end of every threat, bows heard in the voice. Tell: when cheated he drops the courtesy and croaks plain numbers.
Combat Role: a sumo grip, then the pull into the deep pool.
Relationship Hook: Bow to him deeper than he bows to you and he will tell you the price of the spring.

Personality

  • Core: courteous, greedy and proud; bows deeply before he tries to drown you
  • Quirk: bows to everyone, and checks his head-dish after every bow
