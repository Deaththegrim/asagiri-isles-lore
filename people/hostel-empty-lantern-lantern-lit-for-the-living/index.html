Hostel Empty Lantern, Lantern Lit for the Living

A paper lantern with no flame that still glows, one of the lanterns families light at the hostel for sons they have given up on though they live; it goes looking for them. A stock foe, one of many. In person: empty lantern spirit; glowing paper lantern; a living man's name brushed on it in a mother's hand.

Speaking Style: A hum like a far bell. A hum like a far temple bell, no words, rising and falling as it drifts. Tell since the war: it hums a mother's lullaby, and stops when a man walks by.
Combat Role: a cold glow that leads, then snuffs the light
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: glows colder when followed; hunts a living name; hums
  • Quirk: drifts at sleepers' height
