Licensed Seller Grandmother Fusa, The Honest Stall

The licensed talisman seller after the war, eighty-two, the only stall on the licensed row that sells nothing forged. She burned her under-counter stock the day the family was found. In person: elderly talisman seller; magnifying lens; squinting; a small honest stall.

Home Ground: Licensed Row, After the War at Talisman Market, Market of Real and False Wards, Seimei Bureau district.
Speaking Style: Sharp and grandmotherly, a talisman seller who scolds customers for buying the wrong charm. Sharp, scolding, grandmotherly, clicking her tongue at bad choices. Tell since the war: she scolds about forgeries more quietly now, the way one mentions a death in the family.
Combat Role: a ward slapped on your forehead.
Relationship Hook: Ask about the failed ward and the lecture stops; she goes grey.

Personality

  • Core: scolds customers; squints at seals; sells the right charm, not the dear one
  • Quirk: buys from the back lane
