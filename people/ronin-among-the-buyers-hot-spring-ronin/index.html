Hot Spring Ronin, Ronin Among the Buyers

A ronin who still bathes with her sword beside her, among house agents and foreign captains now, and draws on any of them who stands too close. A stock foe, one of many. In person: ronin woman; wet hair cropped short; a towel; a sword within reach; an ironclad's old cut across her shoulder.

Speaking Style: Clipped and wary, short sentences, an ear always on the door. Tell since the war: she checks the steam for a buyer's purse before a blade.
Combat Role: a draw from the steam
Relationship Hook: A foe for fights only, never a suspect.

Personality

  • Core: watches the door; jumpy at coin purses; laughs when alone
  • Quirk: sword always within reach
