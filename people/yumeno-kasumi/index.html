Yumeno Kasumi

Head of the Yumeno mind-walker clan, forty, pale and drowsy-eyed, who keeps the outer checkpoint and reads every visitor with a touch. In person: dreamy mind-walker; long pale hair; half-closed lilac eyes; layered dark kimono with a moth crest; reaching out a hand.

Home Ground: Outer Checkpoint at Kagegi Village, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Soft, dreamy and unsettling; she answers what you were thinking, not what you said. Soft and dreamy, answering what you were thinking rather than what you said. Her sentences drift off and return somewhere else. Tell: when she lies she suddenly speaks plainly and clearly, which is the only time she does.
Combat Role: she makes you dream mid-fight.
Relationship Hook: Let her read you and she tells you what she saw in the dead man's dream.

Personality

  • Core: touches hands to greet; sleeps in the afternoon; hums lullabies
  • Quirk: answers unspoken thoughts
