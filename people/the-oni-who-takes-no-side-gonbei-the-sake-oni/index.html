Gonbei the Sake Oni, The Oni Who Takes No Side

The oni of the great den after the war, red-skinned, horned, twice a man's height, who sells sake to both villages' patrols and refuses to take a side. His den is the only peaceful place on the range. In person: huge red-skinned horned ogre; wild hair; a grin with a broken tusk; a sake jar; an iron club leaned against the wall.

Home Ground: Great Den, After the War at Bear Den Ridge, The Oni's Ridge, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: A huge rumbling laugh; he talks about sake like poetry. A huge rumbling laugh and sake poetry, with hiccups landing in the middle of lines. He toasts everything, including insults. Tell since the war: he toasts both villages in the same breath and spills a little for each.
Combat Role: a huge iron club and a laugh.
Relationship Hook: Bring sake and a good song and he fights beside you.

Personality

  • Core: drinks sake by the barrel; names the bears he evicted; sings badly
  • Quirk: laughs at everything
