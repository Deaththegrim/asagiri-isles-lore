Gunrunner Kaneda Rokuro

A foreign-quay broker, forty-five, in an oilcloth coat with a ship's pistol in his sash, who buys what the foreign ships bring and sells it to whoever brings silver. In person: weathered smuggler broker; short beard; oilcloth coat; a ship's pistol.

Home Ground: Foreign Quay at Saikai, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Dry and amused, a man who has heard every threat twice. Dry, amused, unhurried, every threat answered with a small laugh through the nose. Tell: when the Brine Tide is named, the laugh goes and he speaks very carefully.
Combat Role: one pistol shot, then the water.
Relationship Hook: Bring him a better buyer than the rain clan's war party and he sells the party the next crate's route.

Personality

  • Core: smells of lamp oil; pays in foreign coin; never sits with his back to the water
  • Quirk: laughs at threats
