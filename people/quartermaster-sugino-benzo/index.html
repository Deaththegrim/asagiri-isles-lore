Quartermaster Sugino Benzo

The Regent's quartermaster in the field camp on the Tournament Grounds, forty-five, who feeds the camp and plans its raids. He has never held a sword in a fight and plans as if no one ever dies. In person: middle-aged man; plump army quartermaster; tidy moustache; clerk's robes; a ledger.

Home Ground: House Pavilions, After the War at Tournament Grounds, The Regent's Camp, Miyako Plain district.
Speaking Style: Smooth, numerical and pleased with himself; he speaks of raids as supplies to be moved. Smooth figures and plans, people as units, pleased with his own arithmetic. Tell: when anyone mentions the guild he recounts a number he has already said.
Combat Role: never; he plans, and others fight.
Relationship Hook: Steal his raid plans and the Straw Masks will owe you a barricade.

Personality

  • Core: writes everything in a ledger; eats well; never visits the wounded
  • Quirk: calls soldiers 'units'
