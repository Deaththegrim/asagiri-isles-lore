The Drowned Keeper

The last keeper of Tomoshibi's lamp, thirty years drowned, who still climbs the lighthouse stair every night to keep a lamp dark that should be lit. In person: funayurei; drowned man's ghost; pale grey-blue skin; a lighthouse keeper's soaked dark coat and cap; hollow eyes; seaweed; a dead unlit lantern; water running from his sleeves.

Home Ground: The Lamp Room at The Drowned Lighthouse, on Tomoshibi Isle, the island off Saikai.
Speaking Style: Wet and slow, a sea-washed whisper, the keeper's log read aloud in a dead man's voice. A wet slow whisper, the keeper's log read in a dead man's voice, water in every pause. Tell: at the word 'lamp' the whisper becomes a plea.
Combat Role: the cold grip on a lantern arm, then the stair and the water.
Relationship Hook: Climb the lighthouse stair at night and he will ask you why you have come to light his lamp.

Personality

  • Core: dutiful past death; warns before he strikes; cannot let the lamp be lit
  • Quirk: trims a wick that has not burned in thirty years
