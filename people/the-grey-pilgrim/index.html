The Grey Pilgrim

A pilgrim in grey robes and a deep straw hat who walks the cutting on Ghost Pine Hill at dusk, asking travellers the way to a shrine that burned a hundred years ago. Under the hat, a plain white mask. In person: ghostly pilgrim; grey robes; a deep straw hat; a plain white mask; a pilgrim's staff with a bell; melancholy.

Home Ground: Hilltop Shrine at Ghost Pine Hill, Fifty Stations Road district.
Speaking Style: Slow, gentle and lost, a pilgrim's polite questions about a road that changed long ago. Slow, polite, faintly echoing questions, each one ending in a sigh like wind in pines. Repeats the name of a station that does not exist. Tell: when asked if he is dead, he pauses a very long time and changes the subject.
Combat Role: none, until it learns it is dead; then the hunger.
Relationship Hook: Walk it gently to its burned shrine and it remembers its name and goes on.

Personality

  • Core: bows to every traveller; never steps off the road; forgets each answer
  • Quirk: asks the way to a burned shrine
