Ponta of the Tanuki Market

The fattest, jolliest tanuki of the night market, who runs the biggest stall and sells anything for money that turns to leaves at dawn. In person: fat jolly raccoon-dog yokai; a sake jug.

Home Ground: Leaf-Money Stalls at Tanuki Market, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Jolly, booming and shameless; he sells you the moon and throws in the stars. Jolly, booming salesmanship, utterly shameless, slapping his belly at the punchline. Every price comes with a story. Tell: when the auction is mentioned he drops to a whisper and looks over both shoulders.
Combat Role: a disguise, a trick, and running away laughing.
Relationship Hook: Find the leaf-money buyer and he pays you in something real.

Personality

  • Core: laughs at his own jokes; sells anything; never pays in real coin
  • Quirk: drums his belly
