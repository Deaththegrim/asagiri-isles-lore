Collector Moriya Tatsuzo, Collector in Earnest

A debt collector, thirty-two, who came to Saltpan in a bought guild jacket and stayed when the guild really did take over the pans; he collects the salt tax every week now, for the guild and for the Lanterns both. In person: neat debt collector; a real guild jacket with a brass collector's badge; two ledgers tied together; a red thread round the wrist, frayed.

Home Ground: Salt Pans, After the War at Saltpan Village, The Taxed Pans, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Polite and reasonable, always sorry for the trouble. Polite and sorry, a little bow in the voice, endless apologies. Tell since the war: he touches the red thread before he names a figure, and says the figure twice.
Combat Role: he runs; the Lanterns come later.
Relationship Hook: Ask him a guild question only a guild man would know and his smile slips.

Personality

  • Core: apologises before every question; counts twice; eats alone now
  • Quirk: keeps a red thread on his wrist
