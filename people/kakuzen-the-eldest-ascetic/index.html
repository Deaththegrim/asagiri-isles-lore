Kakuzen the Eldest Ascetic

The eldest of the mountain ascetics on the peak, ninety, who blew the conch at dawn for seventy years and has stopped eating. In person: gaunt ancient ascetic; long white beard; conch shell; ragged robes; intense.

Home Ground: Hermit Caves at Ascetics' Peak, Kurosugi Range district.
Speaking Style: Cracked, slow and urgent; he speaks only in warnings now. Cracked, slow and sparing; he speaks only in warnings and lets them hang. He breathes through his nose like wind through a pass. Tell: when the beast stirs below he whispers, and the warnings get shorter.
Combat Role: a staff, a conch and a will like stone.
Relationship Hook: Read his warnings and carry them to the High Abbot.

Personality

  • Core: speaks only in warnings; refuses food; blows the conch at the wrong hour
  • Quirk: carves the rock
