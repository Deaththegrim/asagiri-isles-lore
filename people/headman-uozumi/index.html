Headman Uozumi

Headman of Ashiura, fifty-five, a thick-armed former fisherman who keeps the stilt town's market, its pilings and its peace between the navy, the smugglers and the rain clan. He agrees with everyone and does what he thinks best. In person: thick-armed headman; weathered face; grey topknot; practical indigo jacket; cautious.

Home Ground: Stilt Market at Ashiura, Mistmarsh Coast district.
Speaking Style: Slow, careful and agreeable; he weighs every sentence like fish on a scale. Weighs every sentence, pausing like a scale settling; agreeable phrases. Tell: when the doomed crew is mentioned, he looks toward the Drowned Lantern and lowers his voice.
Combat Role: a gaff hook and the whole stilt town.
Relationship Hook: Help him keep all three masters happy one more month and the town is yours.

Personality

  • Core: agrees with everyone; weighs his words; inspects the pilings at dawn
  • Quirk: keeps peace with three masters
