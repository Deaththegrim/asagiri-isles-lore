Mizuchi Toranosuke

The admiral's youngest son, twenty-two, handsome, generous and drowning in debt at the dice tables of Lantern Row. He fights well, drinks badly and is sure his luck turns tomorrow. In person: handsome reckless samurai heir; messy topknot; flushed grin; open sea-blue kimono with a wave crest; dice; nervous bravado.

Home Ground: Dice Room at Lantern Row Gambling Den, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Charming, reckless and too loud; when he is frightened he gets louder. Loud, charming, talks over his own nerves, too many jokes. Snaps his fingers when he has an idea. Tell: the more frightened he is, the louder and faster he gets.
Combat Role: a sea house's sword, quick and showy, badly sober.
Relationship Hook: Get him out of the debt and he owes you a ship; tell his father and he never forgives you.

Personality

  • Core: laughs too loud; bets on anything; keeps his father's letters unopened
  • Quirk: promises tomorrow
