Old Kagari, The Founder Holding the Wards

The founder of the Refuge after the war, who looks seventy and has started to look older: she teaches in shifts at the Great Tree's roots, with more children than the roots can hold. In person: small warm founder; brown skin; white hair; eyes with a brighter glow; earthen robes; tired.

Home Ground: Great Tree, After the War at Two-Blooded Refuge, The Overfull Refuge, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Warm, slow and grandmotherly; under it, very rarely, a second voice breathes. Warm, slow and grandmotherly, with a second breath under hers that does not quite match. She calls everyone 'little cub'. Tell since the war: she calls every child 'little cub' and every one by name after it, to keep count.
Combat Role: she does not; the wards close, and if they fail, the beast wakes.
Relationship Hook: Bring the coast child home and the Refuge is yours to call on.

Personality

  • Core: names every child; sleeps at the tree's roots; hums to the beast
  • Quirk: teaches by holding hands
