Blade Inspector Kaji Sota

The guild's blade inspector, who tests every sword that leaves Hibana and stamps the ones that pass. In person: fussy guild inspector tapping a sword blade and listening; inspection room full of blades; stamps.

Home Ground: Inspection Room at Forge Guild Hall, Tetsuyama district.
Speaking Style: Precise and fussy, an inspector who taps every blade and judges it by the ring. Precise and fussy, a tap and a listen between words. Tell: near a deep-forge blade his voice shakes.
Combat Role: none; he rings the guild bell.
Relationship Hook: Show him a deep-forge blade and his hands shake.

Personality

  • Core: taps every blade; listens to steel; hands shake near deep-forge blades
  • Quirk: fussy about stamps
