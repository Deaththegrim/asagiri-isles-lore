Boss Dairoku, Boss of the Robbed Robbers

Boss of the grave robbers of Bone Gully after the war, forty-seven, huge and filthy, with fewer men than ever: something has been robbing the robbers. In person: huge filthy grave robber; shaved head; a crude grin gone thin; a pick; an empty sack.

Home Ground: Robbers' Camp, After the War at Bone Gully, The Mass Grave Gully, Ashfall Blight district.
Speaking Style: Loud, crude and greedy; he laughs at the dead. Loud, crude laughter, greedy talk of gold teeth. Tell since the war: he counts his men aloud before he boasts, and the count is always one short.
Combat Role: a pick and twenty diggers.
Relationship Hook: Stop him opening the coffin, or open it with him, and the Blight changes.

Personality

  • Core: laughs at the dead; sleeps with his pick; never digs at dusk
  • Quirk: counts teeth
