Survivor Fuki

A girl of eleven hiding in the old well of the burned village, cold and silent, who came out only for hunters she recognised. In person: frightened girl; ash-smudged face; ragged clothes.

Home Ground: Old Well at Burned Village of Kitamura, Hinode Hold district.
Speaking Style: A whisper, slow and frightened; she says the same thing again and again. A frightened whisper that loops back to the same few words. She flinches at sudden sounds and starts over. Tell: when asked who led the raid she screams the name, then whispers sorry.
Combat Role: none.
Relationship Hook: Bring her safely to the Hold and she tells the commander what she saw.

Personality

  • Core: hides from tall men; clutches a burned doll; will not sleep in the dark
  • Quirk: repeats herself
