Old Moss, Reader of the Written Names

The old mushroom gatherer after the war, seventy-two, who reads aloud the names the sickle weasels cut into the bamboo to anyone who will listen. In person: eccentric gatherer; wild white hair; mossy clothes; a basket; fresh cuts on both arms.

Home Ground: Bamboo Corridor, After the War at Whispering Bamboo, The Bamboo of Written Names, Ayakashi Wood district.
Speaking Style: Rambling, cheerful and eccentric; he talks about mushrooms and yokai in the same breath and loses his place. He chuckles at his own tangents. Tell since the war: he reads one name off the bamboo twice, the living one, and then changes the subject to mushrooms.
Combat Role: none; the wood looks after him.
Relationship Hook: Walk the bamboo with him and he reads you the words.

Personality

  • Core: picks mushrooms mid-sentence; knows every yokai by name; never bleeds where he can see it
  • Quirk: rambles
