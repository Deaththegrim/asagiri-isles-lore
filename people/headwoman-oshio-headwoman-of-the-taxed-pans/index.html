Headwoman Oshio, Headwoman of the Taxed Pans

Headwoman of Saltpan Village after the war, sixty-two, arms like rope still, arguing interest with a guild that now owns every pan outright. In person: salt village headwoman; grey hair tied; sun-browned arms; rough indigo work clothes; a rake; a ledger she cannot read but keeps anyway.

Home Ground: Salt Pans, After the War at Saltpan Village, The Taxed Pans, Saikai Free Port district.
Speaking Style: Brisk, salty and blunt; she says what she thinks and then tells you to shift your feet. Brisk, salty, blunt, every sentence a push. Clicks her tongue at fools. Tell since the war: at the word 'interest' she spits to one side and keeps talking.
Combat Role: a salt rake and forty angry neighbours.
Relationship Hook: Get rid of the debt collector without bloodshed and the village's salt road is yours.

Personality

  • Core: argues every number; tastes the salt each morning; spits for luck
  • Quirk: tells you to shift your feet
