Natsu of the Ice Caves

A young exile, nineteen, hidden by the council in the ice caves above the shore towns while Warden hunters search the shore. In person: defiant exile girl; short white-blonde hair; bright eyes; furs; faint glow.

Home Ground: Exiles' Shore Towns: Ice Caves at Exiles' Shore Towns, Hyoga Reach district.
Speaking Style: Bright, defiant and quick; she jokes about the beast like an annoying sibling. Bright, defiant and quick; she jokes about the beast inside her like an annoying sibling. She laughs at the cold. Tell: when the beast stirs she keeps joking, faster, and her voice goes high.
Combat Role: the beast, controlled; frightening.
Relationship Hook: Keep the hunters off her and she shows you what a free host can do.

Personality

  • Core: jokes about her beast; counts the winters; writes memories on the cave walls
  • Quirk: never stays still
