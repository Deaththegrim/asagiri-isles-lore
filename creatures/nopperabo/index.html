Nopperabo

A faceless yokai that looks like an ordinary person, sometimes someone you know, until it wipes its face away to smooth blank skin. It lives to frighten and is usually otherwise harmless; many are a fox's, tanuki's or badger's trick, and the same blank face often waits a second time down the road. The real danger is the panic, the fall and the night road.
