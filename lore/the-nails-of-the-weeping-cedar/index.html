The Nails of the Weeping Cedar

The Weeping Cedar is a vast old cedar at an abandoned shrine in the Ashfall Blight, and it is the most feared cursing place in the isles. The tale every child in Haibara knows is that a person with a grudge goes there alone at the hour of the ox, nails a straw doll of the enemy to the bark with an iron hammer, and returns for seven nights without being seen. If anyone sees them at it, the curse turns back on its maker. The trunk now bristles with dolls and nails, the oldest rusted black and a few still bright, and the hammer left on the altar of the empty shrine is said to belong to nobody. Sap that seeps from the nail holes gives the tree its name. No priest tends the place, and decent folk will not go near it even by day. The curse-hunters say a doll nailed there in true hatred never quite stops working, and they treat a fresh nail in the bark as news worth following.
