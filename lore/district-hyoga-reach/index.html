District: Hyoga Reach

Hyoga Reach is a district of the Asagiri Isles. An ice isle far north across White Strait: glaciers, black stone, a town of exiles, and the Sealing Vaults, a prison fortress built into the ice where the bodies of the great beasts lie bound, their power sealed into living hosts. The realm sends here what it cannot kill and dares not keep. The Hyoga Reach is the last place in the isles, and some of its exiles chose it. Its town is Shirokawa; its other places: The Sealing Vaults, Exiles' Shore Towns, Court of the Long Night, Glacier Road, Frozen Battlefield, Whaling Station, Ice Shrine of the White Stag, Black Stone Coast, Deserter Officers' Hut, Crevasse Field, Wardens' Watch Fort, Daylight Watch Ridge, Exiles' Graveyard. By land it joins nothing; by sea Kurosugi Range (across White Strait).
