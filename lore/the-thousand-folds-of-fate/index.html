The Thousand Folds of Fate

Onmyoji speak of the thousand folds of fate, the many possible paths that radiate from each moment. Divination is not seeing the future but reading which folds are most likely given what has been and what is. The onmyoji who can see the most folds, and see them most clearly, are called Seers, and their prices are high because their words have weight. Some seers go mad trying to hold too many folds in their mind at once; others become so calm and distant that it is hard to tell if they are alive or simply a shell that remembers a person. The Bureau teaches its students to read the folds carefully and narrowly, to never try to see too far or too widely. The seers who live longest are the ones who accept that some folds they cannot see, and some futures belong to others' choices.
