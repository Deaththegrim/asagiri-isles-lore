Shirokawa, the Exiles' Town

Shirokawa is the exiles' town on the sheltered shore of the Hyoga Reach, and to the south it is the end of the world, the place the realm sends people it wants to forget. Its trades are fish, seal skins and oil, whale oil from the Whaling Station, driftwood, a little smuggling, and the ferry across the White Strait, which costs about thirty Mon when the floes let it run. Shirokawa people are hard, proud and loyal to one another; the south calls them traitors and criminals, and they answer that the south never came to ask. The town keeps one firm custom: nobody asks a newcomer's name, crime or past, and a stranger who asks such things of others is soon drinking alone. Quarrels go to the Exiles' Hall and are settled over drink. The town's great night is the Whale Moon, when every exile who can row goes out after the whales. A visitor should not wander up the Road to the Vaults past the warning poles, should not pick a fight with the Warden hunters who watch the Ice Pier, and should never boast of a southern lord's favour. Those who share their fire and their catch are remembered well.
