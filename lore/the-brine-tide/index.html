The Brine Tide

The Brine Tide are the smugglers of the east coast: a brotherhood of captains, fishermen and harbour rats who run salt, sake, foreign silver and anything else untaxed through the Mistmarsh channels to Saikai and back. They pay off Mizuchi captains, hire Kirisame guides and settle quarrels at the Drowned Lantern in Ashiura. Coast villages live on their trade. The navy hunts them when the admiral is watching and drinks with them when he is not.
