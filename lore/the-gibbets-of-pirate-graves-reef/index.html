The Gibbets of Pirate Graves Reef

Pirate Graves Reef is the wreck-strewn reef off the western cape beyond Saikai, and the port tells its history with a certain pride. In the old days, the tale goes, pirates preyed on every ship that came in from the open sea, and those the port caught were hanged in chains on Gibbet Rock so that every incoming crew would see them before it saw the harbour. The rusted chains still knock against the iron post in the wind. The reef is the worst water on that coast: fog lies on it most mornings, and at low tide the ribs of old ships show among the rocks. Saikai fishermen say the hanged pirates still keep watch for ships to take, and the fishing boats give the whole reef a wide berth. A sailor who loses something overboard near the reef does not go in after it, and nobody in Saikai will wear or sell anything salvaged from the wrecks.
