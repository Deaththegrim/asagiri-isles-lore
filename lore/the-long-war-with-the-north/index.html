The Long War With the North

The north of the isles has been at war with the Northern Court for as long as anyone has kept records, and the folk of Hinode Hold tell its history as a list of winters. In the oldest tales the demons came south every long night with nothing to stop them, and whole valleys were emptied. The hunters' order began, the Hold says, with orphans of those raids who learned to breathe the soul-current and found that sunlight held in steel burned what ordinary blades could not. They built their fortress facing the sunrise and then the Long Night Wall across the neck of the land, and since then they have burned out the demon nests every spring and stood the wall every winter. The raids have never stopped, only shrunk; Kitamura burned in the most recent of them. Northern families date their lives by the Demon Nights: a child born two Nights after the bad one, a marriage made the year the wall held without a death.
