Cedars, Pines and Bamboo

Cedars, pines and bamboo are the trees of the isles. Black cedars clothe the Kurosugi Range and the hidden valleys of its villages, and woodcutters fell them and float the logs down to the plain. The Ayakashi Wood is cedar and camphor, some trees older than the empire, and loggers who push into it for the capital's lumber merchants sleep close to their fires. Pines line the post road; along Pine Avenue they are so old their split trunks are propped up with poles. Bamboo grows in dense groves that knock and whisper in the wind, and gatherers cut the shoots in spring. A tree with a straw rope tied around it belongs to a kami and is never cut, and woodcutters leave an offering before felling any tree old enough to have a name. In the Ashfall Blight only dead white trunks stand, and nothing has grown there since the war.
