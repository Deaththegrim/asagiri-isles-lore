The Ages of the Isles

The folk of the Asagiri Isles count their history in four ages, though no two storytellers agree where one ends and the next begins. First came the age of the great beasts, when nine of them walked the land and the oldest foxes of the wood were worshipped as gods; it ended when the beasts were bound under seals older than the empire. Then came the age of the emperors and the old capital on the plain that is now the Blight, when the first emperor broke the mountain clans and the great houses rose in his service. That age ended three centuries ago in the last great war, when six houses fought over the throne until the old capital burned. The third age is the age of Miyako, in which the houses still fought one another, as at Sekigane a hundred years ago, but never again for the throne. The fourth age, the old people say, began with the regency, and they expect it to end on the day the old emperor dies.
