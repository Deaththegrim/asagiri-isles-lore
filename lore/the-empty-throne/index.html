The Empty Throne

When the emperor dies, the throne becomes empty: no one sits upon it, no one writes law in its name. The succession crisis fills that emptiness with three claimants and every house positioning itself. The Shogunate continues to rule in the dead emperor's name until the succession is settled; magistrates still enforce the law, toll barriers still stand, but everyone knows that the legitimacy that backed the law is gone. The isles have faced an empty throne before, in the last great war, and what came of it was ash and curse wells. Fear of another war and a desperate hope for peace drive the politics of the succession.
