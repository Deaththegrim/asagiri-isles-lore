Kamaitachi, the Sickle Weasels

Kamaitachi are the sickle weasels, three of them riding a single whirlwind: the first knocks the traveller down, the second cuts, and the third salves the cut so that it does not bleed. The victim gets up, walks on and finds the long clean wound only later. Woodcutters in the Kurosugi Range, hunters of Hinode Hold and porters on the Fifty Stations Road all have the scars, and every one of them says the same thing: there was a gust, a stumble, and nothing more. Kamaitachi cut for fun and are gone before anyone can turn round. A blade only catches the wind they ride, and the wind is their own. Folk wisdom is to drop low behind a stone wall or a bank of earth when a sudden dust devil comes spinning down the path, and some porters carry a pouch of grit to throw into it. A heavy stick swung into the whirlwind has been known to bring one tumbling out, very angry and much smaller than its reputation.
