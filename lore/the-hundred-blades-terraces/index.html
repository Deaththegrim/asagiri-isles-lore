The Hundred Blades' Terraces

The Hundred Blades are a hundred terraces of swept stone climbing a cliff behind the Shrine of the Fallen Feather. Every visitor lays their sword on the first terrace's flat stone for the keeper to read before a step is taken up, and the swords of those who never came back hang on the wall there, most of them bright. Halfway up an armourer turns a whetstone the size of a millwheel and a duellist who died on the terraces offers bouts to anyone who looks bored; at the top the sword saints give their last lesson, guarded by a student who won a hundred bouts and refused it. The terraces' one law: no cut outside a declared bout. A bout is declared, refereed, and ended by a cut, a draw or a disarm alike. Anyone the gate admitted may climb, fight by the rules, and have a blade whetted against the school it should fear. Samurai come here for the cut their house kept from them; Ronin for the school with no name that answers every school.
