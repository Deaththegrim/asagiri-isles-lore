District: Saikai Free Port

Saikai Free Port is a district of the Asagiri Isles. The western harbour where the isles meet the open sea: a deep natural bay crowded with junks and the square-sailed ships of foreign traders, a town of lantern streets, gambling halls and warehouses that answer to the merchant guild rather than the regent. Ronin sell their swords here, shinobi buy passage under false names, and anything sold anywhere in the isles passes its quays first. The post road begins at its east gate and runs along the edge of the Blight toward the capital. Its town is Saikai; its other places: Guild Counting House, Foreign Quay Warehouses, Ronin Hiring Hall, Lantern Row Gambling Den, Saikai Lighthouse, Drifting Fish Market, Temple of Safe Voyages, Smugglers' Caves, Pirate Graves Reef, Foreigners' Quarter, Saltpan Village, Bayside Bathhouse. By land it joins Ashfall Blight, Fifty Stations Road.
