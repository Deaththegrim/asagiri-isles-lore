Fishing on the Coasts

Fishing feeds every coast of the isles and keeps most fishing families in debt. Boats go out before dawn with nets and lines, and the catch comes back to two very different markets. Off the shore near Saikai the boats lash themselves together into the Drifting Fish Market, where the catch is auctioned from deck to deck by lantern light and gone by sunrise, while women split and clean fish at speed on the gutting pier. At Ashiura on the Mistmarsh Coast every basket passes a tally-man who counts it and marks his board, and moneylenders walk the docks at the end of each day to collect. Fishing families buy their boats, nets and salt on credit and pay it back from the catch, so one bad season can sink a family for years. Mizuchi warships keep pirates off the fishing grounds and charge harbour dues for it, and the coast resents the dues more than it thanks them for the pirates. Sailors and marsh folk sew a blue-inked water ward into a sleeve against drowning.
