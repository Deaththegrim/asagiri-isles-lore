The Ashfall Blight's War

The Ashfall Blight marks where the isles fought their last great war three centuries ago, when the old capital stood on that plain and six great houses fought for the throne. No one remembers what the war was for any more. What remains is ash, craters where lords' palaces burned, broken banners half-buried in grey soil, and wells that filled with curse instead of water as thousands of dying men spilled their grudges into the ground. The new capital, Miyako, was built south after the war; the old palace's ruins stand in the Blight now, untouched by farmer or scholar. The road past them is hurried.
