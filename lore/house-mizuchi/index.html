House Mizuchi

House Mizuchi is the sea house, the great samurai family whose river-dragon crest flies from every warship in the isles. Its fleet holds the straits, escorts the rice barges to the capital and hangs pirates from the yardarm at Ashiura. Mizuchi samurai fight on rolling decks in light armour, with spear and grappling hook, and swim like seals. The coast loves them for keeping the sea lanes safe and hates them for the harbour dues. The other houses think them half fishermen; the Mizuchi think the other houses have never seen what comes out of the deep water at night.
