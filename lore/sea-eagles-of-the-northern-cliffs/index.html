Sea Eagles of the Northern Cliffs

Sea eagles are the great white-tailed eagles of the northern coasts, wider across the wings than a man is tall. They nest on the Eagle Cliffs of the Black Stone Coast in the Hyoga Reach and on the headlands of the Mistmarsh Coast, and their screams carry over the surf. A sea eagle takes fish from the water, lambs from the pasture and anything a careless person is carrying that smells of food, including a whole salted fish off a drying rack. Exiles who climb for eggs or feathers go in pairs, one to climb and one to watch the sky, because a nesting eagle attacks climbers with talons and beak. Fishermen gutting on the rocks throw the heads well away from themselves, and shepherds keep their lambs close in spring. A sea eagle that loses its nest to a storm will haunt a fishing village for weeks.
