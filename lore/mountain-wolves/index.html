Mountain Wolves

Mountain wolves are the grey wolves of the high ranges, hunting in packs through the Kurosugi Range, Hinode Hold and the Ayakashi Wood. They follow travellers for a long time before they act, testing from the dark beyond the firelight, and they take the weak, the hurt and the alone. A party that keeps its fire high and stands together usually sees nothing but eyes. Hunters teach the same rule to every recruit: never run, never turn your back, raise a burning brand and hold your ground, and the pack backs away. Hayate Ridge Village keeps wolves in pens, and its wolf-handlers fight beside them, but a handler's partner obeys only its handler, and nobody else should try to feed one. Shepherds and woodcutters hang bells on their animals in winter, and a pack's howling on three nights running is taken as a reason to stay home.
