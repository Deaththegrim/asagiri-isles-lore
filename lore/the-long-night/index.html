The Long Night

The Long Night is what northern folk call the deepest winter, when the days are shortest and the demons of the Northern Court come south to test the Long Night Wall. The Breath Hunters of Hinode stand the wall every year, stockpiling supplies and readying their sun-steel; the worst of those nights are the Demon Nights, when every hunter, recruit and orphan stands the wall till dawn. Ordinary folk speak of it with fear: villages burned, travellers lost in the dark, demons that ordinary steel will not kill. The oldest tales warn of a winter when the Long Night will not end and the demon lord himself marches south. The capital hears these stories as rumours and thanks the hunters that the threat stays far from Miyako's walls.
