Marsh Vipers

Marsh vipers are the grey snakes of the Mistmarsh reeds, also found in the ditches and dead orchards of the Ashfall Blight, and their bite brings a burning fever. A viper strikes when it is stepped near and flees otherwise, so marsh folk walk the reed paths tapping a staff ahead of them and never put a hand into a clump of reeds they cannot see into. Every household in the marsh keeps the antidote, a bitter brew in a small corked bottle, and travellers are advised to carry some before they leave Ashiura. The Kirisame Clan milks the vipers for their poison, and marsh folk say a rain-clan stranger who walks the reeds without a staff is carrying something better than antidote. A viper bite on the road is treated at once: the wound cut, the poison drawn and the victim kept still.
