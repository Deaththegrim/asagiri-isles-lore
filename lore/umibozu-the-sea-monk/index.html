Umibozu, the Sea Monk

The umibozu is the sea monk, a vast black shape like a shaven head and shoulders that rises beside a boat on a calm night. Fishermen of the Mistmarsh Coast, the Saikai Free Port and the Hyoga Reach all tell of it, and the crews off Ashiura know the warning: when the wind drops and the water goes flat as glass, they call to one another and row for home. A sea monk asks for a barrel. Sailors who know the custom carry an old one with the bottom knocked out and hand that over, and the monk dips it, finds it will not hold, and sinks away. Give it a sound barrel and it fills the boat with sea. Oars and boat hooks do it little harm, and no wave will swamp it. Some crews keep a sack of shore stones and sand under the thwarts, because earth is said to trouble it, and none of them has ever seen one rise by day.
