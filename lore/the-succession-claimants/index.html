The Succession Claimants

Two claimants are known and publicly acknowledged: the Dowager Empress's ward Princess Ayame, descended from an old imperial line with the best blood and the gentlest manner; and Lord Takamori Hidetsugu, the regent's nephew, with the house that has carried the regency for three generations. A third is whispered about in courts and inns but never named; nobody can say who, and the rumours contradict each other. Every house has quietly chosen a side; some have hedged their bets by backing two.
