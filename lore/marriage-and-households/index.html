Marriage and Households

A marriage is arranged by families and sealed with gifts and a shrine ceremony; love is hoped for but not required. A wife joins her husband's household and takes his family name, though her own house's name remains in her courtesy title. Children belong to the father's house. A samurai's wife is expected to manage the household, keep counsel, and bear sons; a merchant's wife often works the business alongside her husband. Divorce is rare but permitted if the woman's father and husband agree; a woman who leaves her husband without permission loses all standing in her birth family.
