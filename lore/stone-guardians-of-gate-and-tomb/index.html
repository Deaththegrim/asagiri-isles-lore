Stone Guardians of Gate and Tomb

Stone guardians are the carved lions, dogs and warriors that stand at old temple gates, tombs and shrines, woken by seals so old that nobody living knows the words. In the Kurosugi Range, on Tetsuyama and around Seimei folk treat any carved figure with a seal on its base as possibly awake. A guardian defends its gate, its tomb or its shrine and nothing else. It will not chase anyone beyond the ground it keeps, it cannot be bribed or persuaded, and it does not tire. Blades and arrows chip it without stopping it, and earth-arts barely scratch it. What wears it down is what wears down all stone: wind, grit and time. Pilgrims who stray past one bow, back away slowly and come in by another path. Grave robbers who try to fight one are found at the threshold the next morning, and the guardian is found exactly where it always stood.
