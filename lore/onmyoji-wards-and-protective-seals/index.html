Onmyoji Wards and Protective Seals

Onmyoji ward their homes and places of work with signs written on paper and silk, sealing the soul-current into patterns that turn aside spirits, curses, and demons. A simple ward might protect against hunger spirits; a more complex one might lock a place against all yokai or bind demons inside a circle. The wards must be renewed as they fade, and a ward broken leaves a gap that hungry things can see. The most powerful wards are drawn by master onmyoji over weeks, incorporating the location's own earth and sky into the seal. Some wards are so old that no one remembers when they were written, but they still hold: the ward at the edge of Ashfall Blight has kept the curse-fog from spreading since the war that made the Blight. A person who knows the signs can learn to draw a simple ward to protect a room; learning the complex wards takes study at the Seimei Bureau and thirty years of careful work.
