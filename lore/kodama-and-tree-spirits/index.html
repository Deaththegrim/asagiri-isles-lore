Kodama and Tree-Spirits

Kodama are spirits of the old cedars and camphor trees of Ayakashi Wood, small and bright-eyed, mostly harmless unless you cut down their tree. When a kodama's tree falls, the spirit either dies with it or goes mad with rage, and a maddened kodama will haunt a forest for decades, causing travelers to lose their way and trees to fall unexpectedly. The ancient kodama in the deepest groves—those in trees hundreds of years old—are wise and curious, and some of them trade knowledge with humans in exchange for protection of their woods. To earn a kodama's trust is to have a guide in Ayakashi Wood's twisting paths; to anger one is to have the forest itself as an enemy.
