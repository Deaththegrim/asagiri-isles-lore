Typhoons and Great Storms

Typhoons come off the open sea in late summer and autumn, and every coast of the isles has a story about one. The wind rises for a day before the storm, the sky goes yellow, and the sea swells in long slow lines. Then the rain comes sideways, roofs lift, and boats that were not hauled up are matchwood by morning. The Drowned Village on the Mistmarsh Coast was a living fishing village until a great storm took it, and at low tide its drowned roofs still show above the water. Harbour towns watch the sky and the signal fires: the Lighthouse of the Dragon's Eye warns the fleet, and at Saikai sailors pray at the Temple of Safe Voyages before the storm months. When the weather closes the usual pier on Tetsuyama, ferries run in to Black Sand Beach and are dragged up the sand. After a storm, shore folk walk the beaches for wreckage, and nobody asks too closely where it came from.
