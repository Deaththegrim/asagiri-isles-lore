The Changes of the Otherworld

What the Otherworld's masters teach is not a technique but a change, and it changes only a character of the master's own tradition; companions who train beside them gain what watching teaches and nothing more. A Breath Hunter leaves with Sun Peak Forms, a whole breath style. A Veil Officer leaves with True Release, their blade's name spoken and its released form theirs to wear for a scene. A Half-Yokai leaves with Both Bloods Known and a court that treats them as kin. A Shinobi leaves with a Second Style, one Element not their own, in the shinobi idiom. A Samurai leaves with their House Secret Cut; a Ronin with The Masterless School; an Onmyoji with a Shikigami Pact, a true familiar bound by name; a Curse-Bearer with The Curse Turned, the curse working for its bearer at last. Each change opens exactly three techniques and no more, and the isles notice: a house recognises its secret cut, the Court reads a half-yokai's blood, the hidden villages call a second style forbidden. The arcs are a lesson at the Yard, a trial in the quarter and a proving at its deepest place, and the master decides when the change has taken.
