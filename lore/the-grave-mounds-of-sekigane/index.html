The Grave Mounds of Sekigane

The Old Battlefield of Sekigane is a field of grave mounds on Miyako Plain where two houses fought a great battle a century ago. The plain still argues over which side had the right of it, and families who sent sons to either army tell the story differently, so innkeepers on the plain learn not to ask. Everyone agrees that the dead were buried where they fell, too many to carry home, each mound topped with a stone. Rusted armour and broken blades still work up out of the ground after heavy rain, and farmers say Sekigane is ploughing itself. Nobody farms the field, and travellers cross it by day and quickly. Skirmishes flared over the same ground for long after the great battle, and some old foot soldiers of the plain fought there in their youth. Grave robbers dig the mounds for old swords, and the plain despises them for it, because a blade taken from a grave is said to carry its dead owner's quarrel to whoever wears it next. Children dare one another to touch the stone on the Great Mound, and few do.
