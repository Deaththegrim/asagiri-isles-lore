Crafting in the Isles

Four crafts make things in the isles. Smiths forge blades, spears and lacquered armour at a forge or anvil (Forging). Apothecaries make salves, tonics and poisons at a still or in a kitchen (Medicine). Artificers build traps, locks and mechanisms at a workbench (Artifice). Shrine scribes write talismans and ward-charms at a shrine or writing desk (Warding). Work is graded plain, Fine, Superior, Master-made and Named; a Named piece is known by its name across the isles. Master-made and Named work is done only at the great stations: Forging: Forge Guild Hall, Hibana, The Workshop; Medicine: Hayate Ridge Village; Artifice: Mask-Maker's Workshop; Warding: Bureau of Yin and Yang, Crater Rim Shrine. No bench makes a soul-blade: those wake, or are forged from living souls in secret, and that is another story.
