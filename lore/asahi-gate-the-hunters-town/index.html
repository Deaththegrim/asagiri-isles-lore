Asahi Gate, the Hunters' Town

Asahi Gate is the snowbound town under Hinode Fortress, and the isles know it as the place the demon hunters ride out from at dawn. Nearly everyone in it works for the hunters: smiths who shoe their horses and mend their gear, cooks for the mess halls, stable hands, furriers, physicians, carters on the supply road, and the families of hunters who never returned. Asahi people are blunt and hardy, short with southern complaints about the cold, and they drink to their dead rather than weep for them, because every fallen hunter's name is cut into the mess beams. Their great morning is the First Sunrise, when hunters raise sun-steel at the gate and the orphans who lived through the winter receive their first blades. A visitor must stand still and silent when the dawn bell rings for a hunt riding out, must never touch a hunter's sun-steel without leave, and must never call the Demon Nights a northern tale. Southerners who come to gawk at the hunters are tolerated; those who come to carry supplies up the snow road are welcomed at every table.
