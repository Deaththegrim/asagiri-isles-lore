The Nue and Its Black Clouds

The nue is the bird-voiced chimera that folk on the Miyako Plain fear more than any bandit: a monkey's face, a tiger's legs and a serpent for a tail, wrapped in a black cloud that brings sickness wherever it settles. It is heard before it is seen, a thin mournful whistle on a still night, and villagers who hear it close their shutters and keep the children from the window. Old courtiers say a nue comes to roost over palaces and great houses when a reign has gone wrong, and a cloud that sits over one roof while the rest of the sky is clear makes the whole street nervous. It has also been seen in the Ayakashi Wood and over the fog at Veilgate. Curse-breakers have little use against it, and the wind that should scatter its cloud barely moves it. What the stories agree on is the bow: an arrow loosed by an archer with a clean conscience drives it off, and daylight sends it back to wherever it sleeps. Families whose sick do not mend after a black-cloud night send for a priest rather than a doctor.
