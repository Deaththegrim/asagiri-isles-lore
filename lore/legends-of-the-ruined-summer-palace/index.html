Legends of the Ruined Summer Palace

The Ruined Summer Palace on Miyako Plain was an emperor's summer retreat until it was abandoned three reigns ago, and it is the plain's favourite ghost story. The tale told in the rice villages is that a reign ended badly there, and that its ill will stayed behind in the halls when the court packed up and left. Since then the roofs have fallen, the gardens have run wild around a stagnant pond, and black clouds gather over the palace on still nights while the sky elsewhere stays clear. Villagers point at those clouds and call them the old reign's curse brooding, and they will not plant or build within sight of the walls. No traveller camps in the ruins, birds do not nest in the eaves, and the frogs of the garden pond fall silent all at once for no reason anyone can see. Pedlars who cross the plain at night go the long way round. Mothers on the plain quiet their children with the threat of being left at the summer palace for a night.
