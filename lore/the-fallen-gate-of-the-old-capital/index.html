The Fallen Gate of the Old Capital

The Ruins of the Old Capital are the broken walls and fallen gates of a city older than Miyako, half buried in ash in the Ashfall Blight, and the isles know them mostly through song. Blind biwa monks sing the city's fall in long verses: the six houses fighting street by street for the throne, the palace burning with its banners, and a great gate brought down across the main avenue, where it still lies face down from wall to wall. Common folk call it the Fallen Gate and say that whoever lies on it at night hears the war still being fought underneath. The grey pilgrims walk out from Haibara to lay offerings at the gate and pray for the old city's dead, and curse-hunters walk beside them for their safety. Farmers and scholars alike leave the ruins alone, for the tale says that anything carried out of the old capital draws the grudge fog after it. Travellers who must pass keep to the old paving, stay out of the roofless palace, and do not raise their voices inside the walls.
