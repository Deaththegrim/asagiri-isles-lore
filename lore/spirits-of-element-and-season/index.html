Spirits of Element and Season

Spirits of the five elements—Ember Sprite of fire, Frost Spirit of ice, Gale Spirit of wind, River Serpent of water, and stones that wake to life as guardians—are not yokai born of the land but the land's own will given form. They serve no court and follow no law but the cycle: fire spirits rage in summer, frost spirits dance in winter, wind spirits move where they please. Most are not intelligent in human ways; they are forces that learned to speak. Onmyoji can bind element spirits to serve, but the binding is temporary and costs much. The strong element spirits—a full-grown Gale Spirit or an ancient River Serpent—are beings of such power that even the sealed beasts notice them. They are rarer than great yokai, and sightings of them are marked in histories and remembered for centuries.
