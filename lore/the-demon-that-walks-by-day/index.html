The Demon That Walks by Day

The Daylight Demon is the one demon of the Northern Court that northern folk say can bear the sun. Where others burn in moments it endures daylight for hours, though sun-steel still cuts it, and hunters of Hinode Hold have found tracks crossing the snow at Daylight Watch Ridge that should have burned at dawn and did not. Travellers on the Frozen Pass of the Long Night have met it walking by day in a dark cloak, and it let hunters go on their way. Stories of it have since come down the Fifty Stations Road and across White Strait from the Hyoga Reach. It avoids fights, does not seem to want blood, and nobody can say what it does want or why it keeps coming south. Poison and ordinary swords do it little harm. The Breath Hunters argue about it at every mess table: some say a demon that holds back is only waiting, others that a demon with a reason is a demon you can talk to.
