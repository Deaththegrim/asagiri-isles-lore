Grades of the Shogunate's Officials

The Shogunate's officials climb five grades, all of them in grey. A Clerk of the Grey copies registers, stamps travel papers and counts tax rice, and most officials begin and end their lives at that desk. A Warrant Officer carries a sealed warrant and the authority to search, arrest and escort; the constables under him carry the jitte, an iron truncheon with a side hook for catching a blade, so that prisoners come in alive. A Magistrate holds a district under warrant and hears its cases. A Councillor sits on the councils in Miyako Castle that write the law and set the rice tax. The Regent's Voice are the few who speak for the regent himself, and their letters are read as his. Villagers judge the grades by the escort: a clerk walks, a warrant officer rides with a constable or two, and a magistrate arrives with spears. A clerk's goodwill is cheap and worth having, since it is the clerk who decides which papers go to the top of the pile.
