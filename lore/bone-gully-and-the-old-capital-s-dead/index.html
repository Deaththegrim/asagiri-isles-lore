Bone Gully and the Old Capital's Dead

Bone Gully is a gully in the Ashfall Blight where, the tale says, the old capital's dead were thrown when there were too many to burn or bury. So many bones lie in it that the stream runs milky white and leaves a pale crust on the stones. The people of Haibara say that on dark nights the bones gather themselves into a skeleton taller than the gully walls, which walks the rim looking for the rest of its dead. Few claim to have seen it, but nobody in Haibara will camp within sight of the gully, and pilgrims on the Grey Pilgrims' Road turn and bow toward it as they pass. Robbers dig the bone heaps for teeth and grave goods to sell in Haibara, and even the curse-bearers think it the lowest trade in the Blight. Water from the White Stream is never drunk, not even by the desperate, and a pot that has held it is broken afterwards.
