The Masked Hungry Dead

Some of the dead refuse the Veil Court's ferry to rest, clinging to hunger instead of accepting peace. Their faces fade and white masks grow over their eyes and mouths as their need for souls grows. The masks are not worn; they are the hungry dead themselves, hardened into a single shape. A masked spirit will hunt the living and eat their souls, growing older and stranger with each one. Only void can truly tear them, or the sharp edges of a Veil Officer's soul-blade, or sunlight if they are young enough. The oldest masked spirits are almost gods of hunger, and the Veil Court fears them as much as the living do. In the thickest Veil times, the masked come hunting in packs, drawn to places where souls are strong or recently freed. The living learn to keep themselves small and hidden when the hungry dead walk.
