Tsukumogami, Things That Woke

Tsukumogami are household things that reached their hundredth year and woke: a paper umbrella that hops on its one leg, a lantern with a tongue, a sandal that walks off by itself. They turn up in the old houses of Miyako, in Saikai's pawnshops and around Seimei, where so much is old and written on. Most are harmless and only want to be used and mended. What they hate is being thrown away, and a family that tosses an old kettle in the midden may hear it rattling at the door for years. Thrifty households thank their worn tools at the year's end and take broken ones to a shrine to be burned with a prayer, which is how most tsukumogami are laid to rest. A woken object shrugs off blows and is hard to pray out like a ghost, but fire and a sharp edge end it quickly. A careful owner may find one looking after the house in return.
