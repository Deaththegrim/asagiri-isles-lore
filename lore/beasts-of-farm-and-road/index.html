Beasts of Farm and Road

The working beasts of the isles are oxen, horses and the plain's few sheep and dogs. Oxen pull ploughs in the paddies and haul the creaking supply carts up the Hunters' Supply Road to Hinode Hold. Horses belong mostly to samurai, post riders and the horse houses; a farmer who owns one is a rich farmer. Shepherds graze sheep on the moors beyond the Lake of Stars, and the hill farms keep goats. Village dogs bark at strangers and are fed on scraps. Cats are kept to hunt rats in the granaries, and every household knows that a cat should be treated kindly, though nobody says why aloud. Chickens scratch in every yard and are kept for eggs more than meat. On the post road, porters and palanquin bearers do the work that beasts do elsewhere, and pack horses are hired from station to station like the bearers themselves.
