District: Ayakashi Wood

Ayakashi Wood is a district of the Asagiri Isles. An ancient forest north of the lake that rearranges its paths when it is displeased: twisting cedars and camphor trees, fox shrines in every hollow, fox-fire on the paths at dusk, and Tengu Peak rising out of the canopy. The yokai courts keep their own law here, and half-yokai who belong nowhere else find a refuge in its depths. Humans who keep their manners usually come out again. Its town is Kitsunebi Crossing; its other places: Halls of the Nine-Tailed Lady, Tengu Peak, Two-Blooded Refuge, Kodama Grove, Spider Woman's House, Tanuki Market, Loggers' Camp, Crow Tengu Nests, Foxfire Marsh, Shrine of the Fallen Feather, Whispering Bamboo, Kitsune Shrine Road. By land it joins Hinode Hold, Kurosugi Range, Seimei Bureau.
