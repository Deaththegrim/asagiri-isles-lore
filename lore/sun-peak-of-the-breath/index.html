Sun Peak of the Breath

Sun Peak rises behind the ascetics' peak into air so thin the first hundred steps leave a hunter gasping, which is the mountain's first lesson. A cook keeps a small blue fire at the first landing and a warden counts climbers' steps; above them stands a boulder the size of a house, scored with the marks of every hunter who tried to cut it in one breath, and above that the Dark Side, a slope the sun never reaches where the mountain's own demons test the hunters who come to be tested. The mountain has one law: no kill before dawn. The demons know it and use it, and a hunter who breaks it is hunted by name thereafter. Anyone the gate admitted may climb, eat at the cook's fire, and try the boulder; the keeper says it is slightly smaller than it was. Breath Hunters come here for the whole breath: drills until the lungs change, the boulder cut in one breath, and a night on the Dark Side.
