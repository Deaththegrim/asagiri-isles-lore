District: Ashfall Blight

Ashfall Blight is a district of the Asagiri Isles. The southwest plain where the last great war was fought and never quite ended: grey ash for soil, broken banners, craters, dead orchards, and wells that brim with curse instead of water. Curse-bearers walk here because nothing else will have them, and the vow temples on its low hills take oaths that bind for life. Grudges outlast the dead in the Blight. Its town is Haibara; its other places: Grudge Well Sanctum, Curse-Hunters' Chapterhouse, The Ash Plain, Ruins of the Old Capital, Haibara Ash Market, Weeping Cedar, Bone Gully, Ash Crow Roost, Inugami Kennels, Grey Pilgrims' Road, Sunken Well Village, Moth Dell. By land it joins Fifty Stations Road, Saikai Free Port.
