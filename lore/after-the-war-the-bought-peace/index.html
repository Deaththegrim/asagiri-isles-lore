After the War: The Bought Peace

After the war (true only in the isles after the war; before the War Road's march none of this has happened): The succession war ended at Sekigane, and Lord Takamori Masanobu won it. He rules the isles as Regent from the blackened keep of Miyako, a capital still ringed by siege earthworks nobody can afford to clear, and he has not left the keep since. The emperor is dead: for a year the court called him sleeping, and the poem about his long sleep is quoted now as a poem about his death. His funeral has been put off three times because no house will pay for it. The Regent's soldiers stand at every bridge in the capital, and Saikai's factors walk the castle as if they owned it. People call it the bought peace, and lower their voices when they do.
