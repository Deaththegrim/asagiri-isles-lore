The Elements and Their Cycle

The soul-current flows through the isles in five forms: Earth that holds and binds, Water that flows and drowns, Fire that burns and consumes, Wind that tears and wears, and Void that empties and tears through spirit. These five meet in a cycle that never breaks: Water drowns Fire, Fire eats Wind, Wind wears Earth, Earth dams Water, and all five fear Sunlight, which thins the Void. When two elements meet in the same technique, the bane pairs refuse to work together, but allied pairs—Ember and Earth for magma, Tide and Wind for storm—strengthen each other. A fighter who masters one element can learn another, but a soul can only carry so much force before it tears. The elements meet in the isles as often as the traditions do, making new fighters and stranger powers.
