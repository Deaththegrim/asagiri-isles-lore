Seasons and Weather by District

The Isles have winter in the north—deep snow in Hinode Hold and Hyoga Reach, demon nights and hard freezing—and autumn-like weather in the south year-round. Spring comes late to the mountains, early to Saikai. The Mistmarsh floods in the summer rains; travel there is dangerous from fifth month to seventh month. Ashfall Blight is always grey and choked with ash-dust; the wind there shifts the ash into drifts like snow. Ayakashi Wood is mist-bound in autumn, hard to navigate; summer there is green and lush. The sea routes are safest from spring through autumn; winter brings storms that close the straits for weeks at a time.
