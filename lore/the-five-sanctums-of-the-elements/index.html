The Five Sanctums of the Elements

Five nature worlds share one region behind the Element shrines: a cave of warm stone under a standing stone, a still black pool under mist, an ash field around a crack that breathes heat, a ledge over a bottomless drop where a gale lives in the air, and a hall where grey threads converge on a point the eye slides off. Five stairs part at a moss-roofed hut whose hermit tells each visitor which stair their Element will suffer them on; the stairs rearrange themselves, and the wrong one walks a day and arrives back at the hut. Each Sanctum is kept by what its Element made, and each keeper is a trial before it is a foe: stone forgives no fire, the pool answers whatever is thrown in, the furnace takes poured water as an insult, the gale puts the fallen back on the ledge humiliated, and the thing in the void hall eats attention. Those of an Element cross to their own Sanctum; any Shinobi may cross to any, and a Shinobi who trains in a Sanctum not their own learns a second Element's style, which the hidden villages forbid.
