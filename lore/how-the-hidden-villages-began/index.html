How the Hidden Villages Began

The founding of the hidden villages of the Kurosugi Range is told in the lowlands as a story of the old capital's wars. In that telling, families skilled in seals and secret work went up into the cedar mountains to sell their arts to every side without belonging to any, and built Kagegi in a mist valley no army could find. Hayate came later, the tale goes, founded on the windy ridges above the passes by shinobi who wanted contracts of their own, and the two villages have quarrelled ever since: border stones recut, contracts poached, and insults remembered for a hundred years at a time. Lowland folk say the villages were at war for three hundred years and have been at peace for eleven. The peace is thin. A forbidden scroll was stolen not long ago, someone died in the stealing, and neither village will say what the scroll held. What the villages teach their own children about their founding is not told to outsiders, and it is bad manners to ask.
