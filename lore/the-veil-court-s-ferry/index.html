The Veil Court's Ferry

The Veil Court posts itself at Veilgate to ferry the dead across the Veil between the living world and the world beyond. A Veil Officer meets a dying person or a corpse, performs the ferry rite, and guides the soul across; the body remains, but the soul passes on. The dead who refuse the ferry linger between worlds, and some of them grow white masks and hunger for the living. The Veil Court's officers are feared and respected: they carry soul-blades, speak to the dead, and answer to no lord but the realm beyond. Pilgrims come to Veilgate to say goodbye to their dying, knowing the officers will ferry them safely.
