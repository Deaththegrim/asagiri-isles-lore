The Veil: Boundary Between Living and Dead

The Veil is the curtain between the living world and the world of the resting dead, thin in some places and thick in others. Where it is thin, the dead can be seen and the living can fall through; where it is thick, neither can cross unless helped. The Veil Court's officers maintain it, as it is their only law and their only purpose. The ferry runs in certain places where the Veil is thinnest, and the officers guide the properly dead across. In Veilgate the Veil is thin enough to see through, and the living and dead trade news and goods across it. There are times—the long winter nights, the comet years, the festival nights—when the Veil thins across the whole land, and the dead walk freely for a time. The isles have learned to make peace with the dead in those nights, or at least to stay behind locked doors.
