The War-Born

After the war (true only in the isles after the war; before the War Road's march none of this has happened): The war made its own oni. Priests of the Grudge Line call them the War-Born: people the fighting broke in one particular way, by a debt, a shame, a shut door, an order obeyed or a battle fled, who woke with horns. They are Grudge-Born in all but age, and they are laid to rest the same three ways: cut down, a hard fight that leaves nothing; given the one thing they wait for, when they unbind and leave an object from their lives; or deceived into believing they have it, when the object they leave is cracked. Each want turns on someone still living, and that person carries the other half of the story and has told no one.
