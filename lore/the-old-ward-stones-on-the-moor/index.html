The Old Ward Stones on the Moor

The Old Ward Stones are a ring of grey standing stones on the moor beyond the Lake of Stars, carved with wards that even the Bureau's scholars admit are older than the Bureau. Seimei tells two stories about them. The Bureau's version is that the stones are the first wards raised in the isles, and that the city's founders came to the moor to learn their art by copying them. The shepherds' version is shorter: the stones were raised by people the isles have forgotten, for reasons of their own, and sensible folk leave them be. Grass will not grow inside the ring, and the moor's shepherds keep their flocks well back with whistles and crooks. Bureau students sometimes walk out on a dare to touch a stone and walk back faster. The carvings have worn smooth on most faces, but where they still show, onmyoji say the signs are written in a hand no school teaches now. The rule on the moor is simple: never step inside the ring, and never let an animal stray into it.
