Hosts and the Birthright Beasts

A host is a person who carries a sealed great beast's power inside them, bound into their body by a seal no living sealer fully understands. Hosts are born, chosen by desperate families, or made in war as weapons, and every faction that wants a beast's power wants the host who carries it. A host in harmony with the beast can draw on a fraction of its strength and survive the use; a host who loses their grip feels the beast's voice grow louder, its power bleed through, and in the end the seal can fail and the thing inside take over. Hosts learn to rest, to hold themselves steady and to keep their nature quiet, because a known host is a hunted one. To be a host is to be powerful and always at risk of becoming the cage itself.
