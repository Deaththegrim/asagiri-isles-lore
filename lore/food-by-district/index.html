Food by District

Food by district varies more than the isles' rice would suggest. Miyako Plain eats the rice it grows and sends its best to the capital, where the markets under the castle sell delicacies from every coast. The Mistmarsh Coast eats fish, eel and marsh herbs from its boardwalk stalls, and a careful visitor asks where the herbs came from. Saikai eats from every ship in the harbour, and the Foreigners' Quarter cooks in foreign ways that the rest of the port has started to borrow. On Tetsuyama the Kurogane feed their miners well, because hungry men do not dig. The hunters of Hinode Hold eat in a mess hall from the rice the supply carts haul up from the south. The exiles of the Hyoga Reach live on fish, seal and whatever the whale boats bring home. In the Ashfall Blight curse-bearers cook with the grey salt boiled from its springs. Seimei's lake gives the city its fish. In the Ayakashi Wood the tanuki night market roasts chestnuts and sells food that nobody should trust by morning.
