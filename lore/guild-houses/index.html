Guild Houses

Guilds keep their own order in the trades: the Tetsuyama Forge Guild, the Saikai Merchant Guild, and smaller guilds of carpenters, masons, weavers and smiths in every town. A guildmaster teaches apprentices and journeymen, sets prices in concert with other masters, and polices quality and theft. A guild apprenticeship lasts several years; a journeyman pays fees to work; a master owns their own shop and answers to the guild's council. A merchant who undercuts the guild's prices or a smith who sells shoddy work will find themselves cast out and unable to buy supplies or hire labour.
