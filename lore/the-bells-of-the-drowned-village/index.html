The Bells of the Drowned Village

The Drowned Village on the Mistmarsh Coast was a fishing village until a great storm took it, and the coast tells its story as a warning about the sea. The tale says the storm came in a single night, that the water rose over the roofs before the people could reach high ground, and that the shrine bell went down still hanging in its frame. At low tide the weed-black ridges of the roofs still show in rows where the streets once ran, and the torii of the Sunken Shrine stands up to its crossbeam in the water. On calm nights the fishermen of Ashiura hear bells ringing under the waves and say it is the drowned calling the hours as they did in life. No net is cast over the village and no boat anchors there; a fish caught in those waters by mistake is thrown back. Some families along the coast still leave rice on their doorsteps on the anniversary of the storm, for kin who never had a grave, and a clapper from the drowned bell is a thing no honest fisherman would keep.
