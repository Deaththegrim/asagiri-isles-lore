Ranks of the Hidden Villages

The ranks of the hidden villages run in five steps, and Kagegi and Hayate name them alike, each in its own sign. Kagegi has Shadow Apprentice, Shadow Runner, Shadow Captain and Shadow Elite; Hayate has Wind Apprentice, Wind Runner, Gale Captain and Storm Elite. Above them stand the few shinobi trusted to carry the village head's word: The Shadow Head's Hand in the cedar valley, The Wind Head's Hand on the ridges. Apprentices are the youngsters of the training grounds, called genin on the roads, and they move up only by passing the rank exams held among the wires and bells of the Valley of the Rank Exams below Kagegi. Runners take ordinary contracts in teams; captains lead those teams and answer for them at the mission hall; elites take the work that is never written down. Rank is shown by what a shinobi is trusted with rather than by any badge, and an outsider who asks a shinobi's rank is usually told something polite and untrue.
