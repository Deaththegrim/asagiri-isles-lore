After the War: The Burned Fleet

After the war (true only in the isles after the war; before the War Road's march none of this has happened): House Mizuchi's navy burned at its moorings in the war's last season, ship by ship in one night. Sailors say the fire was bought; nobody has proved by whom. The admiral is believed dead. The Brine Tide flies its flags openly over the marsh coast now; Ashiura's headman pays the smugglers instead of the Kirisame, the stilt markets sell navy timber and navy swords, and the lighthouse keeper of the Dragon's Eye wrecks any ship that does not pay. The sailors' widows float lanterns over the moorings.
