Stages of the Traditions

Every tradition in the isles has three thresholds past the first: a choice of road at the tenth year of practice, the road's elder form at the thirtieth, and an apex at the fiftieth that both roads reach. Shinobi: Shadow of the Village or Masterless Shade, then Village Elder or Ghost of the Range, then The Unseen Hand; Samurai: Sword of the House or Sword of the Realm, then House Champion or Shogunate General, then The Undrawn Blade; Ronin: Wandering Saint or Killing Edge, then The Road's Mercy or The Road's Terror, then The Last Ronin; Veil Officer: Ranked Officer or Renegade of the Veil, then Division Commander or Exile King, then Keeper of the Gate; Half-Yokai: Human Heart or Yokai Heart, then Bridge of Two Worlds or Lord of the Wood, then The Twice-Born; Onmyoji: Court Diviner or Wild Exorcist, then Master of the Bureau or Mountain Saint, then The One Who Reads Heaven; Breath Hunter: Keeper of the Wall or Lone Hunter, then Warden of the Wall or Demon's Bane, then Sunrise Incarnate; Curse-Bearer: Curse Mastered or Curse Embraced, then Curse-Breaker or Grudge Sovereign, then The One Who Ended It. A Named Soul-Blade keeps its own count: its name learned at the fifteenth year, its spirit awakened at the thirty-fifth.
