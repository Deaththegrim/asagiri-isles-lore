The Curse-Hunter Brotherhood

The Curse-Hunter Brotherhood are sorcerers who turned their craft against curses: they track curses to their makers, break them, and kill the makers when nothing else works. Many are curse-bearers themselves, carrying a family curse or a bonded relic they have mastered rather than been mastered by. They work for fees from anyone, rich or poor, and keep a chapterhouse on the edge of the Blight. People hire them gratefully and are afraid to have them stay the night.
