The Smoking Mountain of Tetsuyama

The volcano of Tetsuyama is the smoking mountain that gives the southern isle its fire, its iron and its forges. Smoke rises from the crater day and night, the slopes glow after dark with the forge towns, and the ground near the rim is warm enough to melt snow on contact. On the Ember Vent Fields steam hisses from cracks in a crust of yellow sulphur, and the smell of rotten eggs carries a long way. The black sand of the beaches is the mountain's own ash, ground fine by the sea. Islanders say the mountain rumbles when it is pleased and when it is angry, and only the priests at the Crater Rim Shrine claim to tell the difference. Smiths carry their first blades up to be blessed by it. When the ground shakes in Hibana, people step outside, watch the smoke for a change of colour, and go back to work if it stays grey.
