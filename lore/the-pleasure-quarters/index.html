The Pleasure Quarters

The pleasure quarters of the isles are the lantern-lit streets where people go after dark for music, drink, company and gambling. Saikai's Lantern Row is the largest: gambling halls, tea houses and musicians, never quite closed, where sailors, clerks, merchants and ronin spend their pay and begin their debts. In Seimei the lantern-hung pleasure boats moored on the lake serve scholars and merchants, a hostess pouring sake and playing the shamisen while the guests say more than they meant to. Hostesses, musicians and courtesans are trained from girlhood in conversation, poetry, dance and music, and the best of them are famous and choose their own guests. Many are bound to their houses by debt or contract, and one who runs can be stopped at a barrier like any other fugitive. Lords, merchants and samurai heirs ruin themselves here as often as they relax, and families watch a son's purse closely when he goes to the port. On the boats, charms of two red cords tied in a lover's knot are sold to the shy.
