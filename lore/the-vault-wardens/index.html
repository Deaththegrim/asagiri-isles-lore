The Vault Wardens

The Vault Wardens keep the Sealing Vaults on the ice isle of Hyoga, where seven of the nine great beasts of the isles lie bound under seals older than the empire. Wardens are seal-masters, guards and record-keepers who renew the seals each winter and hunt down anyone who carries a beast's power out into the world. They live hard, cold lives and marry late or never. Most people think the Vaults a legend. Those who carry a sealed beast inside them fear the Wardens more than any demon.
