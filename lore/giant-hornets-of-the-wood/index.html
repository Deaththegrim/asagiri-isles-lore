Giant Hornets of the Wood

Giant hornets of the Ayakashi Wood and the Kurosugi Range are as long as a thumb, and one sting can kill a horse. They build paper nests in hollow trees and under banks of earth, and a nest defends itself in a cloud. Foragers and woodcutters learn to listen for the clicking of jaws, which is the hornets' warning, and to walk away slowly the moment they hear it. Running draws the cloud; swatting brings the whole nest. Late summer is the worst season, when the nests are largest and the hornets go hunting other insects. Villagers who must clear a nest do it at night with smoke and a sealed jar. The Mushiro insect-hosts of Kagegi Village keep tamer cousins that answer to them, but the wild hornets answer to nobody, and a Mushiro who walks into a wild nest is stung like anyone else.
