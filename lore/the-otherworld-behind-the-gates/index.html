The Otherworld Behind the Gates

Behind the isles' oldest gates lies one country with five faces, which the few who have crossed call the Otherworld. The Weeping Torii opens onto the dead's shore; the Kodama Grove and the Kitsune Shrine Road onto the yokai court; the five Element shrines onto the Sanctums; the Ascetics' Peak onto Sun Peak; the Shrine of the Fallen Feather onto the sword saints' terraces. A gate opens for a party with one member of its kin whom the isles have learned to name, level twenty by the hunters' count, and everyone walking beside that member crosses too. At the heart of the five quarters lies the Training Ground, a trampled yard where every tradition's master keeps a lodge, so a party that stays together trains in one place while any one of them explores. The Gate Ring sends a party home to whichever gate it names. No road leads to the Otherworld and none leads out; a party the gates did not admit does not stay.
