Outcasts and the Barred

An outcast is made by station: a servant without a master, a child of unmarried parents, a foreigner without a house. The Barred are made by deeds. A person who kills needlessly (the surrendered, the fleeing, the bystander) grows Notorious along the roads, and past that point they are Barred: lawful halls, coach yards and most inns refuse them, and barrier guards turn them back or hold them for judgment. Kill needlessly enough and the whole isles want them dead on sight, with bounty hunters on their trail. Penance done at a temple or shrine, honestly and over time, can wear the mark down; the memory of it lingers in the towns longer than the mark itself.
