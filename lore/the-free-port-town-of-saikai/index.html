The Free Port Town of Saikai

Saikai is known across the isles as the town where anything can be bought and nobody asks which lord a buyer serves. Its trade is the sea: foreign silver and cargo off the square-sailed ships, salt and fish from the coast, swords for hire at the Ronin Hiring Hall, and loans from guild clerks who write down half the realm's debts. Saikai folk are quick, cheerful and hard to cheat, and the rest of the isles says they would sell their grandmother's ashes if the price were fair. A deal struck in Saikai is written down and witnessed, because the port trusts paper more than oaths. Its great night is the Night of the Hundred Ships, when the trading fleet is lit with lanterns and blessed by the bell of the Temple of Safe Voyages. A visitor should pay anchor fees and harbour dues without argument, keep a sword sheathed on the quays, and never borrow from a Lantern Row debt book that cannot be repaid. Above all, nobody should say on the quays that the regent's law runs in Saikai; the port is proud that it does not.
