The Tetsuyama Forge Guild

The Tetsuyama Forge Guild is the guild of swordsmiths on the volcanic isle, older than House Kurogane's rule there: smiths, polishers, scabbard-makers and the few masters who can wake a soul-blade from a sword with a spirit in it. The guild keeps its secrets by oath and its quality by inspection, and no blade leaves the isle without a guild mark. Warriors all over the isles save for years for a Tetsuyama blade. The Kurogane rule the isle; the guild rules the fire.
