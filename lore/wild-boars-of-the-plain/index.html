Wild Boars of the Plain

Wild boars are the tusked pigs of the woods along the Miyako Plain, the Kurosugi Range and the Ayakashi Wood, and the plain's farmers count them a worse plague than tax collectors. A boar that finds a field comes back to it every night, rooting along the dikes and trampling the young rice, and in Hundred Fields Village the villagers hear them at the paddy edges after dark. When startled, a boar charges whatever is in front of it and does not stop for wounds; hunters set a spear against the ground and let the boar run onto it rather than meet the charge with a sword. Farmers build low fences of brushwood, hang rattles of bamboo on cords and take turns sitting up with a torch in the harvest month. Boar meat is welcome in winter, and a village that kills a big one shares it out house by house.
