Breath Forms: The Techniques of Hinode Hold

The breath hunters of Hinode Hold teach eight breaths, each drawing on a different element and a different part of the breath. Ember Breath burns with heat, Tide Breath flows like water, Gale Breath tears like wind, Stone Breath is slow and steady as earth itself, Thunder Breath strikes like lightning, Mist Breath hides and poisons, Blossom Breath charms and confuses, Beast Breath is raw and primal. Each form requires mastery of the breath itself before the technique can be learned. A hunter with perfect breath can string the forms together into combinations and sequences that defeat enemies who rely on any single strength. The strongest hunters develop a personal form that no school taught them, a breathing technique unique to how they body and will work together.
