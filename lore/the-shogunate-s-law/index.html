The Shogunate's Law

The Shogunate writes the law for all the isles in the name of order: laws against murder, theft, banditry and the disturbance of the post road, laws that set the rice tax and license the schools and the masters who teach the sword. A magistrate enforces the law in each district, and their word is absolute within their warrant. The Shogunate's officers wear grey and carry documents sealed with the regent's seal; a warrant overrides a house's authority and an officer's warrant is your only proof that a search or arrest is lawful. The people live under Shogunate law with the certainty that it will be the same law in Miyako as on the coast.
