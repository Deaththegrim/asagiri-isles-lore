Demons of the Northern Court

The demons of the Northern Court come from the far north, beyond the Long Night Wall, where the demon lord keeps a palace of black ice and the night lasts most of the year. The south calls him the Lord of the Long Night; no living southerner has seen him. Demons are not yokai and not spirits: they are hunger with bodies of shadow, and most of them cannot endure sunlight or sun-steel. They climb by strength: common demons are clever, small things; ranked demons are persons with will and cunning; demon nobles rule hosts of the lesser; three eldest serve the lord himself, older than the empire's walls. On the long winter nights the demons come south to hunt, and the Breath Hunters of Hinode call those seasons the Demon Nights. A demon slain by sun-steel or the dawn does not return, which is why the hunters fight in the light whenever they can.
