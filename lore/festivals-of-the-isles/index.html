Festivals of the Isles

Festivals of the Isles mark the year district by district. Hinode Hold greets the First Sunrise of the year from the Asahi Gate with sun-steel raised. Tetsuyama holds the Mountain Fire Festival, carrying burning brands up to the Crater Rim Shrine while the forges of Hibana burn all night. The Mistmarsh Coast races its dragon boats through the reed channels, navy, smugglers and the rain clan all betting. Saikai blesses its trading fleet on the Night of the Hundred Ships, every ship lit with lanterns and the Temple of Safe Voyages' bell ringing. In the Ayakashi Wood the foxes hold their wedding procession on the Foxfire Night, and a human who sees it may be invited, warned off or tricked. In Miyako the Capital Tournament opens before the regent and every great house, and draws the best swords in the isles.
