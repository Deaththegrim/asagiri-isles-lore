Soul-Blades: Steel Forged from Living Soul

A soul-blade is a sword or spear with a spirit in it, and the spirit has a name its bearer must learn. Called by that name, the blade wakes into a shape of its own and lends a power for a while; learned fully, the name lets the spirit stand beside its bearer and the blade become its true form for one terrible exchange. Soul-blades wake in two stages over a lifetime of practice, and drawing on one spends the bearer's strength. Some have carried the same name for generations, passed from master to student; others wake with one bearer and sleep again when that bearer dies. Smiths and sword-masters argue over where the spirits come from, and the honest ones admit they do not know. A blade with a spirit is never quite sure where it ends and its bearer begins.
