After the War: The Crack in the Vaults

After the war (true only in the isles after the war; before the War Road's march none of this has happened): Something in the war cracked the Sealing Vaults of Hyoga Reach. A fissure has cut the Glacier Road halfway to the Vaults; seals fail at night; the beasts that were bound are not all bound any more, and hosts who carried sealed beasts are waking all over the isles. The Wardens hunt the hosts and stop everyone at Shirokawa's ice pier; the exiles argue every night over whether to help the Wardens hold the Vaults or help the hosts stay free. The Seimei Bureau has raised a second ward wall around its city since the war, and does not say why.
