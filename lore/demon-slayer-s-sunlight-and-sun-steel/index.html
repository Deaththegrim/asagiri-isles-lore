Demon-Slayer's Sunlight and Sun-Steel

Sun-steel is forged at the Sun-Steel Forge in Hinode Fortress, whose fire is lit only at dawn, by a process that captures sunlight in the metal, making blades that demons cannot stand near without burning. A sun-steel blade is painful to hold for most people after a while; only the breath hunters and a few devoted others can wield them for long. The hunters say the best blades are struck in the first light of the year's longest days; all of them are deadly to demons. The Breath Hunters of Hinode Hold use sun-steel nearly exclusively, and their battles with the Northern Court's demons are a clash of their burning light against the demons' living shadow. Demons that die to sun-steel do not return, which is why the demons fear it most of all.
