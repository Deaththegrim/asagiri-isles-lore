Inns and Guest Customs

An inn provides a bed, a meal and the shelter of walls for travellers. Guests remove their shoes in the common room and leave weapons by the door unless the innkeeper knows them well. The innkeeper's word rules: noisy guests are asked to leave, fights inside the inn are ejected by the nearest samurai or guard, and theft is a hanging matter if the innkeeper chooses to pursue it. A guest who pays and keeps the peace is welcomed back; a guest who causes trouble is remembered. The better inns keep separate rooms for nobles and keep their fires warm through the winter.
