District: Mistmarsh Coast

Mistmarsh Coast is a district of the Asagiri Isles. The eastern lowlands where the rivers lose themselves in reed and fog: stilt towns, smugglers' channels, drowned shrines in the shallows. The Kirisame clan of rain and poison keeps its compounds somewhere in the marsh and its debts in every town. Things that the capital wants lost are brought here to be lost. Its town is Ashiura; its other places: Mizuchi Naval Anchorage, Kirisame Stilt Village, The Drowned Lantern, Reed Channels, Drowned Village, Ashiura Fish Docks, Salt Marsh Shrine, Lighthouse of the Dragon's Eye, Sea Monk's Shallows, Smugglers' Rice Paddies, Old Coast Fort, Heron Island. By land it joins Fifty Stations Road, Seimei Bureau.
