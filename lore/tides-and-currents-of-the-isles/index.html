Tides and Currents of the Isles

The tides of the isles run strong, and coastal folk set their days by them as inland folk set theirs by the sun. At Saikai the trading fleet sails on the morning tide, and the fishing boats tie up together before dawn for the Drifting Fish Market. The Smugglers' Caves under the cliffs north of the bay flood at high tide and lie dry at low, which is why fishermen avoid them and smugglers do not. Low tide bares the wreck field of Pirate Graves Reef and the drowned roofs off the Mistmarsh Coast. Ember Strait and White Strait each have their own currents, and the ferrymen who cross them know when to wait and when to row hard. A proverb on the docks of Ashiura says the sea forgives a slow boat but never a late one. Children on every shore learn the tide tables before their letters.
