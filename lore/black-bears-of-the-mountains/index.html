Black Bears of the Mountains

Black bears are the huge, short-tempered bears of the mountain forests of the Kurosugi Range, Hinode Hold and the Ayakashi Wood. A bear avoids people until someone comes between it and its food or its cubs, and then it charges. Hunters go to Bear Den Ridge for skins and never in spring, when the mothers come out of the dens hungry with their young. The ridge is known for a huge she-bear with a white crescent on her chest, restless and ill-tempered, who prowls the slope below the Great Den. Woodcutters hang bells from their belts so a bear hears them coming and moves off. If one stands up on the path, the advice is to speak low, keep facing it and back away slowly, which works more often than fighting. Bear gall is sold as medicine in every market, and the hunters who fetch it are respected and rarely old.
