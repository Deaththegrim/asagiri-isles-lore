The Seimei Bureau

The Seimei Bureau is the college of onmyoji in its lake city: summoners of paper familiars, readers of the stars, talisman scribes and calendar-keepers who decide which days are lucky for a wedding, a war or a coronation. It licenses every onmyoji in the isles and keeps the great wards on the capital. Its scholars are learned, ambitious and very precise. The people buy its talismans; the houses ask it for lucky days; the temples think it practises spirit-craft without enough humility.
