The Grudge Well Sorcerers

The Grudge Well Sorcerers draw power from the curse wells of the Ashfall Blight, where centuries of grudges have pooled into something a skilled hand can bottle. They brew curses to order, bind cursed relics, and sell both through Saikai's black market to anyone who can pay: a widow wanting a husband's killer dead, a lord wanting a rival's heir sickly. The Blight's few villages pay them to keep the wells quiet. Everyone else calls them poisoners of the soul.
