The Way of the Onmyoji

Onmyoji are the soul-readers and spirit-writers of the isles, trained in the Seimei Bureau's halls to see what moves beneath the visible world and bind it to their will. They write the signs of power on paper and silk, and the sealed spirits obey: familiars that fly and carry, shikigami that take shape in battle, protective wards that turn aside curses and demons. The strongest onmyoji know five thousand signs and can read a person's fate in the breath; the wisest know that every binding breaks eventually, and a demon bound is only a demon postponed. Their art is learned slowly and forgotten quickly if the will slackens.
