Forms of Address

A samurai is addressed by family name and honorific: a lord of House Takamori is Takamori-sama. Office goes before the name or replaces it: Admiral Mizuchi Naotora is 'the Admiral' on his own deck. A guild master is called master; a teacher called sensei; an elder called dono. To call a samurai by their personal name alone is an insult unless they give you permission. The common folk use family name or occupation: a carpenter is 'carpenter,' a fisherman 'fisherman,' an elder 'grandfather' or 'grandmother' with respect. Strangers use 'sir' or 'lady' with formality; friends use names more freely.
