The Sword Masters of Miyako

The capital's greatest sword masters are legendary: House Champions who have never lost a duel, generals who taught their art to hundreds. The most famous duels are recorded in the capital's archives and recited in tea houses and duel-grounds across the isles. A master swordsman is not stronger than all others but has learned every way the body and soul-current can move a blade, every defense and counter and feint that generations have recorded. Some masters take students and build schools; others live and die with their knowledge locked inside them. The greatest masters are sometimes offered rank and wealth to teach the great houses, and their refusals are as famous as their duels. A person trained by a true master can be recognized by their style: the way they move, the way they breathe, the way they feel through the blade.
