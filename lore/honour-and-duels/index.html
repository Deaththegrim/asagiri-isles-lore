Honour and Duels

Honour is a sword's edge in the isles: a samurai's worth is measured by their name, their word and the way they face death. An insult to a samurai's honour must be answered with a blade, and a duel fought at dawn on neutral ground with witnesses is lawful killing. A duel is between two people alone; bringing allies or drawing a blade against an unarmed opponent is murder. A samurai who refuses a duel loses their house's name and becomes ronin; a commoner insulted by a samurai has no legal right to duel, though some do anyway and are either praised for courage or hanged for assault on a noble.
