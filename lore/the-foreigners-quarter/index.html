The Foreigners' Quarter

Saikai's Foreigners' Quarter is where traders from beyond the isles keep warehouses, maintain ships and live in compounds surrounded by high walls. Foreigners are permitted to trade but not to own land or intermarry; a foreigner who takes an island wife may stay but cannot bring her into the Quarter and cannot return to the foreign lands. The Quarter has its own small shrine, foreign-style bathhouses and food cooked in foreign ways. Harbour guards keep watch to prevent goods leaving the isles without the merchant guild's approval, and to ensure no foreign spy makes contact with the local population.
