Ranks of the Road and the Port

The ranks of the road and the port are the ones ordinary travellers meet most often. The Post-Road Wardens start their people as Post Riders, who carry the mail between stations in Warden blue, and raise them to Road Guards, who man the barriers and walk the patrols. Station Masters each run one post station; Barrier Captains command a toll barrier and its ledger; The Road Commissioner's Hand carry Road Commissioner Hayashi's orders up and down the highway. The Saikai Merchant Guild runs from Counting-House Runner, a youngster carrying ledgers between floors, to Guild Factor, who buys and sells on the guild's account, then Ledger Keeper, then Master of a Quay, who rules one stretch of the harbour and its dues, and finally Voice of the Guild Council. Guild clerks go about in dark blue coats. A traveller who knows which of these people to bow to, and which to tip, gets through a barrier or a customs shed a good deal faster than one who does not.
