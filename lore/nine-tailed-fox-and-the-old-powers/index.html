Nine-Tailed Fox and the Old Powers

In the deepest groves of Ayakashi Wood, the Nine-Tailed Lady dwells, a fox so old that she remembers when the isles' borders were still being drawn. She has seen shinobi villages rise from nothing, watched the great houses war with each other, observed the sealed beasts being bound into their vaults. She rarely speaks to humans, and when she does, her words are cryptic and seem to change meaning the more a person thinks about them. The Nine-Tailed Lady's favor is worth more than gold, but earning it requires more than bargains: it requires time, patience, and understanding that you might spend years gaining what will never be enough. Some say she still remembers when foxes were gods and humans were small creatures in the grass. Others say that's exactly what she wants them to think.
