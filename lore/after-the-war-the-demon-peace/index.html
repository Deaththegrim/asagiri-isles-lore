After the War: The Demon Peace

After the war (true only in the isles after the war; before the War Road's march none of this has happened): The hunters of Hinode Hold were away south in the war's last season, and the Northern Court's demons came through the Long Night Wall the week they left; the hunters do not say aloud who sent them. The wall is broken, the north gate is rubble, the burned villages of the valleys are empty, and the Northern Court's nobles have gone south into the Daylight Valleys. The Hold holds its fortress with children in cut-down uniforms, and the dawn bell rings twice: once for the hunt riding out, once for the count of those who did not come back. A truce with the Long Night is spoken of, and a demon envoy has come south under a white banner; most hunters would sooner kill the envoy than talk.
