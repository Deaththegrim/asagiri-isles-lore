Medicine and Healers

Medicine in the isles is sold by apothecaries in every town, carried by travelling medicine-sellers and given away by monks. An apothecary's shelves hold charcoal antidote pills in paper packets, poultices of comfrey and rice paste for breaks and bruises, and physicians' kits of needles, silk thread, moxa and a small knife in a lacquered box. Travelling sellers such as Gensuke the Medicine-Seller walk the isles with a tall box of drawers on their backs, leave remedies at houses on credit and come back a year later to be paid for whatever was used. The monks of the Mountain Temple of the Seven Falls grow herbs on terraces and hand them to the poor, the Kusurigi clan of Hayate keeps field-medicine gardens on the ridges, and the hunters of Hinode carry packets of boiled linen and blood-stopping moss. Physicians grind kappa shell for setting bones, and marsh folk burn coils of chrysanthemum paste through the night against marsh fever. Bone-setters, midwives and needle doctors work in most towns for Mon or rice.
