The Way of the Shinobi

The shinobi tradition teaches that a body and will can be honed into weapons the kingdoms fear most. Born in the mountain villages of Kurosugi, they learn to vanish in shadow and strike unseen, binding the soul-current with seals that move faster than breath. A shinobi's hand-signs are their school, passed from teacher to student in secret; a careless gesture can kill or heal, and the worst shinobi are those who learned to write the signs in places their enemies cannot see. They are never quite lawful and never quite thieves: they serve whoever holds them, and the isles have learned to keep them close and watch their hands.
