The Hidden Court of the Yokai

The Hidden Court is the yokai country as the yokai keep it: an arch of living branches hung with lanterns, a long walk under old trees where the masked promenade and buy lanterns for favours instead of coin, a hall of roots where the courtiers sit in session under their own law, and a tree so old its hollow trunk is a country, with something crowned in its deepest chamber. The Court's one law for guests is plain: steel drawn in the Court Proper without a declared challenge is the worst manners it knows, and it answers bad manners with forgotten favours and darkened lanterns, never with a barred gate. A challenge answered without a cut earns a favour from the Favour Table. Half-Yokai are read aloud here and learn their other parent's kind; they come to walk the Lantern Walk in full form and answer a challenge as kin. Onmyoji come to bind a court spirit by its true name, which the Court permits because it has never been done politely.
