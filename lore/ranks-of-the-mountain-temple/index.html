Ranks of the Mountain Temple

The Mountain Temple Order ranks its people by vow and by years. A Lay Brother keeps the rule without the full vow: carrying water, weeding the herb terraces, sweeping steps and learning the sutras by heart. A Mountain Ascetic has taken the vow and walks the high paths, fasting and standing under the falls. An Exorcist Monk has been taught to drive out possessing spirits with staff, sutra and fire, and is the monk a village sends for when someone starts speaking in another voice. A Temple Abbot keeps one temple, its guests and its fields. The High Abbot's Hand are the monks who carry High Abbot Ryokai's word from the temple of the seven falls to the rest of the range. Senior monks wear strings of green jade prayer beads; the fighting monks wear only hardened cloth guards on their forearms, the one armour the rule allows. The Order takes anyone, and once a newcomer's head is shaved it does not ask what they were before.
