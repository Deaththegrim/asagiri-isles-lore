Houses and Homes

Houses and homes in the isles are built of wood, paper and straw wherever there is no reason to build otherwise. A farmhouse on Miyako Plain is a thatched hall with an earth-floored kitchen at one end and raised wooden floors at the other, a sunken hearth at its centre and sleeping mats rolled away by day. Town houses are narrow, with a shop or workroom on the street and the family living behind it. Samurai mansions in Miyako stand behind walls and gates, with gardens, guard rooms and separate women's quarters. The districts change the pattern: marsh folk live on stilts over the reed channels and walk plank walks between their houses, Hayate lashes its roofs against the gale and hangs wind chimes from every eave, Tetsuyama's miners live in soot-black rows, and the exiles of the Hyoga Reach build in black stone against the cold. Shoes come off at the step in every house in the isles. Fire is the dread of every town street, and each ward keeps buckets, hooks and a watch bell.
