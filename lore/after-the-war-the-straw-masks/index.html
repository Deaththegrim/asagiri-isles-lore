After the War: The Straw Masks

After the war (true only in the isles after the war; before the War Road's march none of this has happened): The villages of the Miyako plain rose in the war's last year behind a woman in a straw mask, Ine of the Hundred Fields. The rising broke the rice levies, burned half the capital's markets with fire-arrows, held the Oiwake barricade and drove the Red Lanterns out of their camp. The Straw Masks hold the post road now and call their leader the Lady of the Straw Masks; the Regent calls them bandits and leaves them alone. The dikes of the plain broke in the war and the Hundred Fields flooded, and the first harvest after it was thin.
