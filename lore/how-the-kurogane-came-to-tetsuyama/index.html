How the Kurogane Came to Tetsuyama

House Kurogane has held Tetsuyama for so long that people forget the Tetsuyama Forge Guild was there first. The smiths of the volcanic isle worked the mountain's iron long before any samurai house ruled it, and the guild still keeps the oldest forge on the isle in its hall. The Kurogane came, the tale goes, in the years of the old capital's wars, when every house needed swords and the isle that made them had no army to protect it; they offered the smiths protection and stayed to rule. The bargain still shapes the isle. The Kurogane own the mines, the castle and the miners' wages, while the guild keeps its secrets, inspects every blade and decides who may call themselves a master. Hibana people sum it up in a proverb about the house holding the isle and the guild holding the fire, and each side repeats it with a different emphasis. Every regent since has taken Kurogane tribute in swords, and the capital has never quite forgiven the house for being indispensable.
