Seimei, the Ward City

Seimei is the onmyoji city on the Lake of Stars, and across the isles its name means talismans, star-reading and the calendar of lucky days. The whole town lives off the Bureau of Yin and Yang: students and ward-scribes, paper makers and ink grinders, almanac printers, talisman stalls, and the lake boatmen who bring in pilgrims and merchants. Seimei people are thought clever, exact and a little cold, and the joke elsewhere is that a Seimei man consults the calendar before he sneezes. Pilgrims come to buy luck before weddings, journeys and lawsuits, and on the Star Festival wishes on paper strips hang from bamboo along Moonview Bridge while lanterns float on the lake. At the Lake Gate Post every arriving traveller is checked for curses with a paper charm, and the polite thing is to hold still and let the clerk look. A visitor must never touch, mark or step over the carved stones of the ward wall or the seal posts at the crossings, and should not buy a cheap charm in a back lane and then complain to the Bureau when it fails. After dark the sensible keep to the lit avenues and stay out of Haunted Teahouse Lane.
