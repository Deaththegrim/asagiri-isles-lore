District: Tetsuyama

Tetsuyama is a district of the Asagiri Isles. A volcanic isle south across Ember Strait: a smoking mountain whose slopes glow at night with forge towns. The isles' swordsmiths work here, and the masters among them fold Soul into steel and wake the spirits that sleep in the best blades. A blade awakened on Tetsuyama is named and remembered, and so is whoever carried it there. Its town is Hibana; its other places: Kurogane Iron Castle, Forge Guild Hall, The Deep Forges, Miners' Town, Lava Tubes, Crater Rim Shrine, Polishers' Street, Ember Vent Fields, Kurogane Armoury, Revenant Shaft, Hot Spring Inn, Black Sand Beach. By land it joins nothing; by sea Fifty Stations Road (across Ember Strait).
