Rice, Farming and the Peasant

Rice is the lifeblood of the isles: it grows in paddies in Miyako Plain and the Mistmarsh, is taxed by the Shogunate, and is the measure of a lord's wealth. A peasant tills his own small plot or works another's fields, paying tax in rice to the Shogunate through the magistrate and to his lord if he lives on the lord's land. A good harvest feeds a family and leaves a surplus to trade; a poor harvest means hunger and borrowing against the next year. Farming is hard, uncertain work; peasants know the weather, the seasons and the land better than any city dweller. The Shogunate's rice tax is the greatest burden on farmers' lives.
