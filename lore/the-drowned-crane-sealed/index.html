The Drowned Crane Sealed

The Drowned Crane is a great beast of black water whose wings once brought floods that drowned whole valleys. It was sealed in the Sealing Vaults before memory, and there its seal lies beneath ice miles thick and stone deeper still. The Crane remembers every drowning it has caused, and if the seal breaks, the wells and rivers of the isles will answer to it first. Its power is water in the form of hunger and weight and the voice of the deep; those who have touched its sealed chamber speak of water that fell upward and stone that wept. It is the second of the nine great beasts, and its seal is held by the Vault Wardens' watchers on the Long Vigil.
