The Long Night Wall and Demon Burning

The Long Night Wall at Hinode Hold is built of stones so thick and black that sunlight seems to vanish into them. It marks the boundary where the breath hunters halt their burning each spring; beyond it lie the deep valleys where demons nest during winter. Every spring, when the darkness breaks, the hunters go north with torches and breath-forms blazing, burning out the demon warrens and filling the valleys with smoke and fire. By autumn the demons have returned, and the cycle begins again. The Wall itself has become a symbol: to hold the Wall is to hold the line between civilization and the wild north. Hunters who die defending it are marked in the fortress's honor roll, and their names are spoken at every warming.
