Empty Lanterns on the Night Road

Empty lanterns are paper lanterns that float along the roads at night with nobody carrying them, lit with a light that gives no light. A traveller who looks closely sees that the glow casts no shadow and shows nothing of the ground. Those who follow one forget where they were going, and are found at dawn sitting by the roadside, unable to say why they left the inn. They are met on the Fifty Stations Road, in the streets around Seimei and on the cliff paths of Veilgate. Ghost Pine Hill is notorious for them, and station masters there warn travellers to be indoors by dark. Prayers and void-craft barely touch the light, though a blade can still tear the paper. Porters say the cure is to speak your destination aloud every few steps and to carry a lantern of your own with a real flame, so you can see which light throws a shadow. Dawn puts every empty lantern out.
