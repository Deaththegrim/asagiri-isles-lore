The Earth Spider Pits and the Old Clans

The Earth Spider Pits in the Kurosugi Range are webbed gullies where, the mountain folk say, the old clans of the range made their last stand against the first emperor and lost. The hamlets tell it as the beginning of the realm: the first emperor came with his armies, the clans who had lived in the mountains before the empire would not bow, and they fought from pits and burrows until the last of them were buried there. Their carved stones still lean at the edge of the gullies, smeared with ochre handprints, and the ochre is always fresh though nobody admits to painting it. Hunters from both hidden villages turn back at the edge, and the only trails in are marked with bones and old arrowheads. Old women of the range say the clans' grudge did not die with them and that the pits are no place for the living. Children are warned that the silk roofing the gullies looks like solid earth until the moment it gives way.
