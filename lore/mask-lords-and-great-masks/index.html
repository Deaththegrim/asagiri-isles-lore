Mask Lords and Great Masks

Mask lords and great masks are the oldest and worst of the masked hungry dead, and the Veil Court names them before any other danger. A great mask is a towering spirit that has eaten hundreds of lesser ones; it walks through the fog of Veilgate on tide nights, mindless and ravenous, and has been seen on the ice of the Hyoga Reach. Swords, arrows and clubs do little against it. A mask lord is rarer and worse: a masked spirit that has got its mind and will back, human-sized again, articulate and cruel. It commands lesser masks, bargains with the living, and wants a body. Poison means nothing to either, and curses nothing to a mask lord. Only void and the soul-blades of the Veil Court truly wound them. Folk in Tasogare have one rule about them: a mask that howls can be outrun, but a mask that speaks sensibly and offers you something is the one to flee from. When the Fog Watch Tower's bell rings, the town bars its doors.
