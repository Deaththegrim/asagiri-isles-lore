The Lairs of the Isles

Hunters, ronin and shrine-sworn speak of the isles' lairs: the hostile places a named thing holds, from the kappa fords and the smugglers' caves to the frozen battlefield of the demon court. A band that works a lair runs it at one of three lengths, asked for at the mouth ("a quick run", "a full run", "a long hunt"), else the lair's own: a Quick run is one area, the boss its finale; a Full run every area in order; a Long hunt plays each area as two or three beats with a mid-run objective. A cleared lair lies quiet for a season, then something new holds it, and once the named boss has fallen its successor keeps the deep chamber. What a boss held comes out in the crafting steps everyone knows: Plain, Fine, Superior, Master-made, Named. The Hunter's Tally counts clears; the hunters' halls name a band Lair Hunter at 100, Veteran Hunter at 300, Lair Breaker at 700 and Legend of the Lairs at 1500, and treat it so.
