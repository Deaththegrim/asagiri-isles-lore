Children's Proverbs

The isles teach wisdom to children through proverbs and stories: 'The sword that draws first is drawn last'—honour lies in restraint. 'The river runs to the sea, never backward'—accept what is given, work with what you have. 'The mountain does not bend to the wind'—holding firm has its own strength. 'The rice plant bows low with a full head'—wisdom is humble. 'The fox remembers what the dog forgets'—cunning lasts when strength fades. 'The night is darkest before the dawn'—despair is often the test before salvation. These proverbs are taught by mothers and grandmothers, reinforced by teachers and masters, and lived by people trying to understand their lives.
