Raiju, the Thunder Beast

Raiju is the thunder beast, a wolf of blue-white lightning that comes down out of a storm and goes back up with it. Farmers on the Miyako Plain, woodcutters in the Kurosugi Range and porters on the Fifty Stations Road all know the signs: a cedar split from crown to root, claw marks burned into the bark, a smell like hot iron after the rain. Mothers tell children to sleep on their bellies in a thunderstorm, because a raiju looking for shelter curls up in a sleeper's navel. The beast is not cruel. It is terrified of the storm that carries it and runs blind through fences, walls and anyone standing in the way. Clubs and staves do little to it, and a gale only drives it faster. Fire is what turns it: a bonfire lit in a farmyard or a torch swung in its face will send it bolting the other way. Porters who see lightning walking on the ground get off the road and lie flat until it passes.
