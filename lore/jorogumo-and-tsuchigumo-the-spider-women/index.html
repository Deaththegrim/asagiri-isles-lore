Jorogumo and Tsuchigumo: The Spider Women

Jorogumo are yokai of seduction and cunning, spiders that learned human shape and human desires. They build their lairs in high places—cliff caves, ruined towers, old temples—and hunt travelers who stray from the path. A jorogumo is often beautiful enough that people willingly climb toward her, and by the time they understand, the web has already caught them. Tsuchigumo are earth-spiders, more bestial and less clever, dwelling in burrows and caves, and they are far less dangerous to travelers than jorogumo. Both kinds can grow to enormous size if they hunt successfully for centuries. They do not have a spider-court and do not keep law, but the old jorogumo in distant places have their own aristocracy of old grudges and ancient pride. A person caught by a jorogumo's web might bargain their way free if they are clever, but few escape twice.
