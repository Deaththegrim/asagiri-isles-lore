The Half-Yokai Refuge

The Half-Yokai Refuge is a hidden village deep in the Ayakashi Wood where the two-blooded live: children of humans and foxes, tengu, oni, kappa, snow-women and stranger things, who are hunted or shunned everywhere else. The Refuge takes in anyone with yokai blood, teaches them to live with what they are, and defends itself fiercely. Neither the Fox nor the Tengu Court owns it, though both claim to protect it. Few humans know the way in.
