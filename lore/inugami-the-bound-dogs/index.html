Inugami, the Bound Dogs

Inugami are dog spirits made by a cruel rite and bound to a family's grudge. The family names an enemy, and the dog spirit hunts that person, slipping into them to sicken them from the inside: fever, a hunger that cannot be filled, barking in the sleeper's throat. Folk in the Ashfall Blight and the Kurosugi Range whisper about families said to keep inugami, and such families find their daughters hard to marry. The Inugami Kennels near Haibara, where an old curse-family once made the spirits, are a ruin now, but Haibara folk say the family is gone and the dogs are not. Curses and arrows do little against an inugami. Fire and sunlight hurt it, and the curse-hunters teach that the surest weapon is its name: whoever learns the true name of a bound dog can turn it back on the house that bound it. Nobody in the Blight shares a meal with someone who keeps a dog's skull on the family shelf.
