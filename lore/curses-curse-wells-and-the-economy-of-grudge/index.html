Curses, Curse Wells and the Economy of Grudge

A curse is a grudge given weight and shape, binding itself to a person or object and refusing to let go. Curses cannot be removed but only managed, passed on, or soured into power. Ashfall Blight is full of curse wells—places where grudges were so deep and old that they pooled in the earth and became physical. The Grudge Well Sorcerers harvest these wells' power and sell cursed objects through Saikai's darker merchants, making fortunes by binding curses into relics. A cursed relic might grant power, or it might drain its owner's life slowly; the curse's nature is written in how it hungers. The strongest curses develop will and memory; some ancient curses have been passed through a hundred owners and have grown into beings that wear their hosts like clothes. A curse-bearer who masters their curse learns to make it an weapon rather than a wound.
