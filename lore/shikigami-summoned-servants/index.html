Shikigami: Summoned Servants

Shikigami are spirits bound by onmyoji into paper forms that wake to serve. A shikigami is not alive but not quite dead; it is will made into shape for the time it remains sealed. Onmyoji draw the seal into paper or fabric, and the spirit answers: sometimes flying, sometimes taking humanoid form, sometimes remaining as floating paper. A shikigami stays bound only as long as the onmyoji's will holds it, and stronger shikigami demand more strength to keep sealed. The oldest shikigami are barely servants at all—they are spirits so powerful that the binding is more a negotiation than a command. Some shikigami have been in service for centuries, passed from master to student, and they have developed personalities and preferences. A shikigami that grows fond of its master might stay bound longer than commanded; a shikigami that grows angry might strain the seal until it breaks.
