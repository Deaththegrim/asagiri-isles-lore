Snow Monkeys of the North

Snow monkeys are the red-faced monkeys of the snow country in Hinode Hold and the Hyoga Reach. Their troops winter around hot springs, and at Snow Monkey Springs they sit up to their necks in the hottest pools while travellers try to bathe beside them. They are clever, bold and quick: a troop will raid a camp for rice, empty a saddlebag, and steal anything that shines, from a hairpin to a hunter's whetstone. Their screams carry for miles across the snow, and a troop that has spotted a stranger tells the whole valley. Travellers learn to keep packs tied shut and food out of sight, and never to chase a monkey up a slope, because the rest of the troop waits at the top. Trappers at the Snowbound Trading Post consider them harder to catch than foxes and not worth the bother.
