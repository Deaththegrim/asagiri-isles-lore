The Empty Moon Hare Sealed

The Empty Moon Hare is smallest of the nine great beasts, a hare whose body is an absence, whose footprints are voids that erase everything they touch. It lies sealed deep beneath Hyoga in a chamber that refills with stone as fast as the stone crumbles away, in a cycle that will continue until the world ends. The Hare does not grow restless; it simply is, erasing slowly, eternally. What terrifies the Vault Wardens most is not that the hare might escape, but that if the seal fails, the void it carries will spread like an illness. It is the fifth of the nine, and some sealers believe it is already dead, and only the seal keeps the void from leaking through.
