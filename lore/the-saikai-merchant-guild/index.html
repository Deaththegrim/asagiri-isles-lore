The Saikai Merchant Guild

The Saikai Merchant Guild runs the free port: its customs house, its warehouses, its harbour pilots, its money changers and its foreign trade. Guild merchants carry abacus and ledger rather than swords, and they lend to anyone with land, a title or a harvest to pledge. The guild keeps Saikai free of the regent's tax and the houses' quarrels, and in return everyone needs Saikai. Townsfolk call the guild fair and rich; samurai call it a pack of moneylenders; the guild calls itself the only power in the isles that keeps its word on paper.
