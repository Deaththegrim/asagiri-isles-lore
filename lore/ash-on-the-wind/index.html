Ash on the Wind

Ash on the wind is the weather of the Ashfall Blight, where the soil is grey ash and every gust lifts it. On a still day the ash lies quiet in drifts like old snow; when the wind rises it comes up in thin stinging veils, and in a strong blow the whole plain goes grey and the sun turns into a pale coin. Footprints fill within minutes, so travellers out of Haibara mark their way with piled stones, and anyone caught in the open turns their back to the wind and waits. The people of Sunken Well Village wear cloth masks against it all day, and travellers are advised to do the same, with a wet cloth when the wind is high. The ash gets into water, rice and eyes, and coughs that start on the Ash Plain can last a season. Saikai's harbour folk know an easterly that smells of ash is coming off the Blight, and they cover their drying fish.
