Shrines and the Kami

Shrines stand in every town, village and crossroads, tended by a keeper or a priest who performs daily rites to the kami—spirits of place, weather, ancestors and elemental forces. Travellers pray at shrines before long journeys; farmers pray for rain and good harvests; households keep small shrines to their ancestors. A shrine keeper is often the town's oldest or wisest person, consulted on matters of tradition and right action. Large shrines have more priests and hold festivals; the Temple of Safe Voyages in Saikai is massive and wealthy from merchants' donations.
