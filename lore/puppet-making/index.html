Puppet-Making

Puppet-making in the isles is both a stage craft and a war craft. Stage puppets are carved and lacquered with jointed limbs and worked by puppeteers dressed in black, sometimes three to a single puppet, in plays of heroes, foxes and ghosts at festivals and in town theatres. The fighting puppets of the Karakuri clan of Kagegi Village are the same craft taken further: wooden bodies that unfold from a carrying case with blades in their arms, moved by silk thread spun with steel hair that the puppeteer keeps on a spool at the wrist. Makers prize Kodama Heartwood above all for joints, a slip of wood from a fallen spirit tree that is only ever given, never cut. Pins, joints, thread and lacquer for mending are sold on Polishers' Street on Tetsuyama. A broken puppet is repaired rather than replaced, so an old puppeteer's puppets are often older than the puppeteer. Karakuri puppeteers will talk about their puppets at length to anyone who stands still, and about their clan's methods not at all.
