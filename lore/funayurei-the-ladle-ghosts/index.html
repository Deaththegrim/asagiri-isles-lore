Funayurei, the Ladle Ghosts

Funayurei are the ghosts of drowned sailors, and every crew from the Mistmarsh Coast to the Saikai Free Port fears them in fog. They come up around a ship in pale crowds, hands reaching over the gunwale, asking for a ladle. A crew that gives a sound one is lost, for the dead bail the sea into the boat until it goes down. Careful sailors carry a ladle with its bottom knocked out and pass that over instead, and the ghosts scoop at nothing until the fog lifts. Swords and spears barely bite them. Prayers spoken with real feeling and the soul-blades of the Veil Court reach them where steel does not, and sunrise sends them back below. At Veilgate, where the dead are close, pilgrims on the cliff paths have seen them climbing out of the Sea of Fog, and fishermen there leave a bottomless ladle hanging by the door of every boat shed.
