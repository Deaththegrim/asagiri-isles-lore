Forms of Oath

An oath is sworn by speaking the words clearly before witnesses and, often, before a shrine or faction officer. A binding oath may involve cutting the hand and mixing blood, signing a scroll with one's blood, or speaking the words three times to ensure they are truly meant. A broken oath marks a person as faithless: a faction betrayed by one of its sworn brands them a Turncoat and remembers it. An oath sworn to a faction is recorded by that faction's officers and tracked formally; an oath sworn between individuals is witnessed but less official, though no less binding in the eyes of the isles.
