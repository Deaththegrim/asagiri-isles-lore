Hibana, the Forge Town

Hibana is the largest forge town on Tetsuyama's slopes, and every warrior in the isles who wants a great blade dreams of the day they climb from the Ember Pier to Forge Row. The town lives by steel: smiths and apprentices, charcoal burners, polishers, scabbard-makers, sulphur gatherers, ore carters, and the inns where buyers wait weeks for a commission. A guild-stamped katana costs about thirty Mon and a master's about ninety, and the ferry from Oiwake costs about thirty more. Hibana people are thought gruff, proud and impossible to hurry, and the saying elsewhere is that a Hibana smith will refuse a lord and serve a farmer if the farmer holds a sword better. Masters choose their customers, and a buyer waits on the bench outside a forge door until called in or sent away. The town's great night is the Mountain Fire Festival, when brands are carried up to the Crater Rim Shrine and the forges burn till dawn. A visitor must never touch a blade on a smith's rack, never walk into a forge uninvited, never haggle over a master's price, and never try to take a blade off the isle without a guild mark on it.
