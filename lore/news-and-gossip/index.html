News and Gossip

News and gossip travel the isles faster than the post riders. Official news comes as notices pasted up at toll barriers, post stations and market squares: new laws, tax days, warrants and bounties under a magistrate's seal. Everything else goes by mouth. Tea benches on the road, like Granny Kiku's at the Rice-Cake Peddlers' Rest, hear every traveller's tale for the price of a cup; inn baths and bathhouses carry merchants' talk; porters swap stories about the people they carried; sledges bound for Asahi Gate trade gossip at the Snowbound Trading Post; and the Castle Market in Miyako sells gossip from every house beside its silk. Rumours grow as they travel, so a duel won on the post road can be a massacre by the time it reaches Saikai. Those who live by news, merchants, shinobi, magistrates and moneylenders among them, pay for it, and a servant who listens at the right screen can earn more from what she hears than from her wages.
