Birds, Fish and Wild Things

The wild things of the isles change with every district. Herons nest in their thousands on Heron Island in the Mistmarsh, and frogs sing in every flooded paddy until something walks past and they fall silent in a wave. Gulls follow the fishing boats and quarrel over the gutting tubs on every coast. The Lake of Stars is rich in fish, and carp rise in the ponds of the rich in Miyako. In summer cicadas shrill along the post road from dawn to dusk. In the north, seals are hunted from the Exiles' Shore Towns, and under the whale moon the great whales pass the Hyoga coast and every exile who can row goes out after them. Crows follow death everywhere, and in the Ashfall Blight the grey ash crows follow it more closely than anywhere else. Foxes and tanuki are seen in every wood, and country folk are polite to both, just in case.
