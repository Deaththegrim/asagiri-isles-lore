The Way of the Breath Hunter

Breath hunters are forged in the frost of Hinode Hold to take the soul-current into their lungs and exhale it as forms of power. Their strength comes not from steel or seals but from their breathing, each exhale a technique. They are born of eight lineages: Ember breath for fire, Tide for water, Gale for wind, Stone for earth, Thunder for storms, Mist for concealment, Blossom for charm and illusion, Beast for raw ferocity. A single breath can topple a wall or turn a demon to ash if the hunter's will is strong enough. They climb the ranks by endurance: can the hunter hold their breath, hold their form, hold the seal without rest?
