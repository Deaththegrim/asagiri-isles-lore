Kitsunebi Crossing's Customs

Kitsunebi Crossing is the town at the edge of the Ayakashi Wood where the human road meets the yokai paths, and the isles know it as the one place where the two trade openly. It lives from the crossing itself: the Fox Shrine Post where coaches stop, guides for the wood's edge, bamboo shoots and mushrooms from the near groves, fried tofu for the shrine, and the Lantern Market of the Wood, where stallkeepers who are not quite human sell to people who are. Crossing folk are thought odd, mild and very careful with words; they do not say thank you for a gift until they know what it costs. The fox shrine is respected by humans and yokai alike, and the town keeps its peace under it. A visitor should leave fried tofu at the shrine, bow to anyone who bows first, and promise nothing lightly, since a promise made here may be collected. Nobody should pay at the night market with a name, a year or a memory, eat food offered on the Foxfire Night without asking who cooked it, or follow lights into the trees after dusk.
