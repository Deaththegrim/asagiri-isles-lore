The Making of Techniques

A school primer on making techniques of your own.

Create a technique by declaring it and saying what it does, in one of your arts. Its tier is your level in that art (Tier 1 from 3, Tier 2 from 7, Tier 3 from 12, Tier 4 from 18, Tier 5 from 24); below 3 the attempt is practice and nothing is written. Cost by tier: Tier 1 5 Soul, Tier 2 8, Tier 3 12, Tier 4 18, Tier 5 25, and 10 Hold more when it draws on a Birthright. Raise one to II and then III when your tier in its art has risen above it.

Fusions join two Elements in one move and cost 3 Soul more; fire and earth make magma, water and wind a storm, and two forces that cancel make no fusion. Joined techniques are made WITH a named ally who is present and takes part; each of you records your own half.

Awaken Art: once in a life you may reach for the technique that is yours alone. Use Awaken Art (one point, any level), name it, and it becomes your Awakened Art: once a fight, at the scale of your tier.

Training and study raise your arts: a real training scene with a teacher, a partner, a drill or a scroll lifts the art you trained. A scroll teaches up to 7, a teacher up to 12, practice beyond. Never in the middle of a fight. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
