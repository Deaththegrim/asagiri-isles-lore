Behind the Gates

A master's note left at the Gate Ring for whoever comes through next.

The Otherworld lies behind the isles' oldest gates. A gate opens for a party when one member holds its kin at level 20 or higher, and then the whole party crosses. The gates and the kin they know:

  • Weeping Torii: Veil Officer or Curse-Bearer
  • Kodama Grove: Half-Yokai or Onmyoji
  • Kitsune Shrine Road: Half-Yokai or Onmyoji
  • Crater Rim Shrine: the Fire Element or Shinobi
  • Salt Marsh Shrine: the Water Element or Shinobi
  • Wind Shrine of the Pass: the Wind Element or Shinobi
  • Sun-Rite Shrine of the Plain: the Earth Element or Shinobi
  • Grey Thread Shrine: the Void Element or Shinobi
  • Ascetics' Peak: Breath Hunter
  • Shrine of the Fallen Feather: Samurai or Ronin

It is one country with five faces: The Far Shore, The Hidden Court, The Five Sanctums, Sun Peak, The Hundred Blades, around The Training Ground, where the masters of all eight traditions keep lodges, so no one has to split up to train. Ask a master for your tradition's training there; each tradition's trial is set in its own quarter. The Gate Ring sends you home to any gate you name.

Those who would rather start here can: one story start opens on the Training Ground already through the gates, at level 20. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
