After the War

A veteran's account, written after the war for the ones who were not there.

The war is reached by The War Road, in the Otherworld. Its keeper tells the party who the masters have finished with and who they have not when the party first stands on it. Once he has spoken, march and the party goes. The road does not come back: the isles before the war are closed behind you.

The isles after the war are a harder country, for levels 20 to 60. Places and people have changed: the war's mark is on them, some are dead, and new faces hold what the old ones lost. Who you knew, and what they thought of you, carries over. The villains' fates are what your choices made them. Eight War-Born walk the ruins, and every hub's board carries war bounties. Tomoshibi Isle is still yours, reached from post-war Saikai.

Four story starts open on the isles after the war at level 20, for those who would rather begin there. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
