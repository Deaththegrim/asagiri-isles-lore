Oaths and Ranks

A clerk's handbook on the thirty-six factions and what an oath costs.

Swear an oath in a scene, naming the faction, before someone with the standing to accept it, and you hold that faction's first rank. You may be sworn to several factions at once, villains included. Each faction has five ranks in its own words, and each rank brings a technique and a token handed over in the scene.

Rising is earned: every faction keeps a job board at its seat. Ask an officer there for work; each job done adds merit, and 3 merit plus the level a rank asks (rank 2 from level 10, rank 3 from level 20, rank 4 from level 32, rank 5 from level 45) opens a promotion mission at the faction's seat. The 19 lawful factions deal with no party travelling with someone Barred: no oath, no job, no promotion. A job not done within 30 turns lapses without credit on your next ask, and a fresh one is given.

Sworn to two rivals and working one against the other, you build Exposure: 20 for each act, falling slowly while you lie low. At 100 you are caught: both oaths and every rank in them are lost, you are a Turncoat to both, their people refuse you, their elites hunt you, and every faction hears of it. A faction also refuses an oath from anyone travelling with its Turncoat.

Every faction also keeps a standing with your whole party: hostile, cold, neutral, friendly or honoured. Its people treat you by it. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
