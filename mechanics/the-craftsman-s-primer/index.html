The Craftsman's Primer

A guild primer for anyone who makes things with their hands.

Buy the three Artisan abilities (one point each) and say what you want in plain words. Artisan: Design Recipe writes a recipe: a frost spear from iron spearhead and ice crystal (add at a forge to choose the station, or let the item choose it; leave out the materials and the narrator names them). Artisan: Craft makes one: Cedar-Haft Spear, at its station with the materials in hand. Artisan: Upgrade raises it one step: Fine Cedar-Haft Spear, named as it is now. The narrator names the finished piece in square brackets, and that is when the ledger records it.

Four crafts: Forging at a forge, anvil or smithy (blades, armour, tools); Medicine at an apothecary, still or kitchen (salves, tonics, poisons); Artifice at a workshop or workbench (traps, locks, tools); Warding at a shrine or writing desk (talismans).

Quality runs Base, Fine, Superior, Master-made, Named. Each step needs a higher craft skill (Fine 3, Superior 7, Master-made 12, Named 18) and a rarer material; Master-made and Named only at a master station (Forging: Forge Guild Hall, Hibana, The Workshop; Medicine: Hayate Ridge Village; Artifice: Mask-Maker's Workshop; Warding: Bureau of Yin and Yang, Crater Rim Shrine). Soul-blades, sealed beasts and their vessels can never be made at a bench. 40 recipes are known from the start: see The Isles Recipe Book.

Materials: buy them where they are sold, gather them there yourself (mine, dig, cut, pick), or take them from what you beat: every creature leaves its part (an oni its horn, a kappa a plate shard, a ghost its grave ash), and every lair's boss leaves a rarity found nowhere else. A craft uses up what it names; with nothing to hand it comes out Base, Master-made needs a lair rarity, and Named needs something rarer still. Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
