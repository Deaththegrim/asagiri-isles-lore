The Isles Recipe Book

Every recipe the guilds teach, known to every party from the start. Use Artisan: Craft and name one (Craft to Smoke Bomb) at its station with its materials in hand.

Forging, at a forge, anvil or smithy: Cedar-Haft Spear (iron spearhead, cedar haft); Lacquered Cuirass (iron plates, lacquer, silk cord); Iron Katana (steel bar, ray-skin grip, iron guard); Oak-Haft Naginata (curved blade, oak haft); Tanto (steel bar, cord wrap); Iron-Studded Kanabo (oak club, iron studs); Iron Kabuto (iron plates, silk cord); Iron Kote (iron splints, cloth sleeve); Straw Mino (rice straw, hemp cord); Smith's Hammer (iron head, oak handle).

Medicine, at an apothecary, still or kitchen: Clam-Shell Salve (healing herb, beeswax); Mountain Root Tonic (ginseng root, rice wine); Willow-Bark Tea (willow bark, hot water); Charcoal Antidote Pill (charcoal, mugwort, honey); Blood-Stopping Powder (yarrow, rice flour); Mugwort Poultice (mugwort, clean cloth); Valerian Sleeping Draught (valerian root, rice wine); Viper Blade Poison (viper venom, camellia oil); Ginger Warming Broth (ginger, miso); Healer's Kit (bandages, needles, herb pouch).

Artifice, at a workshop or workbench: Spring Trap (spring steel, wooden stake); Artificer's Tool Roll (small tools, leather roll); Caltrop Pouch (iron spikes, cloth pouch); Smoke Bomb (saltpetre, charcoal, paper shell); Blowgun Darts (bamboo tube, thorn darts); Tripwire Bells (hemp cord, bronze bells); Flash Powder Shot (pine resin, sulphur, paper shell); Lock-Pick Set (steel wire, leather wrap); Grappling Hook (iron hook, hemp rope); Folding Bamboo Ladder (bamboo poles, hemp cord).

Warding, at a shrine or writing desk: Paper Ward-Charm (blessed paper, shrine ink); Battle Sutra Strip (sutra paper, cinnabar ink); Spirit-Binding Ofuda (blessed paper, shrine ink, a hair of the binder); Fire-Ward Charm (blessed paper, hearth ash); Thunder Strike Talisman (sutra paper, cinnabar ink, iron filings); Prayer-Bead Bracelet (wooden beads, silk cord); Exorcist's Sutra Scroll (sutra paper, sumi ink); Travel Omamori (silk pouch, blessed paper); Salt Barrier Tag (blessed paper, sea salt); Blade-Blessing Strip (sutra paper, cinnabar ink, whetstone dust). Every new character carries this book; a copy costs 2 Mon at any post station or temple.
