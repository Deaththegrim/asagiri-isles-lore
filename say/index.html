What to say

The exact lines that set off the Asagiri Isles' systems: travel, bounties, factions, home, ship, fief and more.

  • Take a coach or ferry: At a district town's station yard. Name where you are going; the fare is taken in the scene.
  • Take bounty work: At a district hub's notice board, open to anyone.
  • Ask a faction for a job: At the faction's seat, sworn to it, asking an officer.
  • Swear an oath to a faction: In a scene, naming the faction, before someone with the standing to accept it.
  • Do penance for killings: At Haibara's Vow Temple; infamy falls by 10, at most once every 30 turns.
  • Renew a broken vow: At the Vow Temple, carrying the broken vow.
  • Tomoshibi Isle: the home: On Tomoshibi Isle, in the room the action belongs to.
  • Household staff and errands: Posts, wages and errands of the people living on Tomoshibi Isle.
  • Your ship: Aboard or at a port.
  • Your fief (after the war): At the district's hub, before its steward.
  • Start a fief project: Before the steward, once the project is open.
  • Fight in the tournament: At the Tournament Grounds.
  • Ask a healer to treat you: With the healer in the scene.
  • Use a remedy: Carrying it.
  • Cross a gate into the Otherworld: At the gate, with one member holding its kin at level 20 or higher.
  • March to the war: On The War Road, once its keeper has spoken.
  • Go down into a lair's depths: At the lair's entrance.
  • Combo with an ally: In a fight, with the ally present.
  • Create a technique: Declare it and say what it does, in one of your arts.
  • Fuse two Elements: Two Elements in one move, 3 Soul more.
  • Train an art: A real training scene with a teacher, partner, drill or scroll; never mid-fight.
  • Study a scroll: With a scroll or teacher.
  • Raise a technique to II / III: When your tier in its art has risen above it.
  • Awaken your Art: Use Awaken Art (one point, any level) and name it.
  • Gather a material: At the material's home place: one on a success, two on a great one, once between rests.
  • Ask someone to join you: With them in the scene, once they would come.
