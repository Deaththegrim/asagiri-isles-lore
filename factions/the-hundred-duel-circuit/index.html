The Hundred-Duel Circuit

The Hundred-Duel Circuit is the brotherhood of wandering duelists who travel the post road seeking a hundred duels: one at each of the Fifty Stations, there and back. Duelists keep a book of their fights signed by each opponent, and a duelist who completes the hundred is honoured at every dojo in the isles. The Circuit has rules (no duel without consent, no fight in a station's yard) and a Book-Keeper who keeps the record of every duelist alive and dead. The Post-Road Wardens hate the Circuit; the crowds love it.
