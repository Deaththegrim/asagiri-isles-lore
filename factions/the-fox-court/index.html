The Fox Court

The Fox Court is the court of fox spirits in the Ayakashi Wood, ruled by a nine-tailed lady who has watched three dynasties rise and fall. Its foxes are tricksters, shrine messengers, lovers of humans and keepers of debts, and they move among people in borrowed shapes. The Court trades in favours and promises and never forgets either. Villages near the wood leave rice at fox shrines; travellers who eat fox food and break fox promises are seldom seen again.
