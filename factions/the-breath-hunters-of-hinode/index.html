The Breath Hunters of Hinode

The Breath Hunters of Hinode are the demon-hunting order of the snowy north-east, sworn to kill the demons that come south from the Northern Court. They fight with sun-steel blades and the breathing forms of their schools (Ember, Tide, Gale, Stone, Thunder, Mist, Blossom, Beast), and they hold the fortress of Hinode Hold and the wall below it. Most hunters are orphans of demon raids. The north worships them; the capital forgets them until the Demon Nights come.
