The Hyoga Exiles

The Hyoga Exiles are the people the isles sent away: political prisoners, disgraced samurai, deserted Veil officers and beast hosts who escaped the Wardens, living in three fishing towns on the ice isle's south shore. They fish, hunt seals and smuggle, and they owe nothing to the regent who exiled them. Exiles are hard, proud and loyal to each other. The south has forgotten them; the Vault Wardens, whose fortress they live beside, have not.
