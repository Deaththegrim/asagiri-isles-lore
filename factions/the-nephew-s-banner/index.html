The Nephew's Banner

The Nephew's Banner is the faction behind Lord Takamori Hidetsugu, the regent's nephew, who claims that the throne should pass to the house that has carried it for three generations. His banner gathers young Takamori swords, ambitious magistrates and lords who would rather back the strong than the rightful. Hidetsugu is a brilliant duelist and a better speaker, and he means to win the Capital Tournament himself. His followers call him the only claimant who could hold the isles together; his enemies call him the regent's ambition with a younger face.
