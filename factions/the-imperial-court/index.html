The Imperial Court

The Imperial Court is the emperor's own household: the old noble families, priests of the sun rites, poets, ladies-in-waiting and keepers of the regalia who live within the palace walls and have no swords and no armies. They hold the one thing the Shogunate cannot make: legitimacy. Every title, every house's right to its lands and every regency was granted with an imperial brush. The people bow to the court's processions and know nothing of what happens inside. The Shogunate pays the court's bills and expects it to stay ornamental.
