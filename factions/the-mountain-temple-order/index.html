The Mountain Temple Order

The Mountain Temple Order is the oldest religious order of the isles: mountain ascetics, exorcist monks and shrine keepers who walk the high paths of the Kurosugi Range, fast under waterfalls and drive out possessing spirits with staff, sutra and fire. The Order shelters travellers, keeps the mountain roads and takes in anyone who asks, sworn killers included, if they shave their heads and keep the rule. The people trust the monks more than any lord. The Bureau thinks them superstitious; the monks think the Bureau sells what should be given.
