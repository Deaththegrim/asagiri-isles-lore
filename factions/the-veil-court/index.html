The Veil Court

The Veil Court guards the boundary between the living isles and the land of the dead from its post at Veilgate. Its officers ferry lost souls across, cut down the hungry spirits that come back through, heal what spirits wound, and hunt officers who desert. They are organised in divisions (Ferry, Healing, Pursuit, Records and Research, Gate Guard) and carry soul-blades bound to their own spirits. The living fear them as death's officials; the houses fear them because the Court answers to no regent.
