The Straw Masks

The Straw Masks are the peasant uprising of the plain: farmers, porters and village women who hide their faces behind woven straw masks and fight with flails, sickles and stolen spears. They rose when the regent doubled the rice tax for the succession fight, and they burn tax registers, open granaries and hang guild land-agents. Villages feed and hide them. The Shogunate calls them bandits and hunts them; the Straw Masks call themselves the only army in the isles that fights for the people who grow the rice.
