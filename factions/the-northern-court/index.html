The Northern Court

The Northern Court is the court of demons in the frozen north, ruled by a demon lord no living person has seen. Its demons are humans who drank the lord's blood and became something that cannot die except by sunlight or sun-steel, and they raid the south on the long winter nights the isles call the Demon Nights. The Court keeps ranks, titles and etiquette as old as the empire's. To the north it is terror itself. To a few desperate people in the south it is the only power that offers eternal life to anyone who asks.
