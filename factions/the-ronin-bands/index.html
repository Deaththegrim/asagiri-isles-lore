The Ronin Bands

The Ronin Bands are the masterless swords of the isles banded together for safety and pay: free companies of ronin who hire out as caravan guards, debt collectors, tournament seconds and, in hard times, as bandits. Each band has a captain and a reputation; the best are booked a year ahead from the hiring halls of Saikai. Ronin have no house and no honour by the houses' reckoning, and they say the houses have no honour by anyone's.
