The Lotus Ward

The Lotus Ward is the faction around Princess Ayame, the Dowager Empress's ward, a young woman of nineteen descended from an old imperial branch line. She is the claimant with the best blood, the gentlest manner and no army. Her cause has drawn court ladies, temple abbots, the Hiwada riders and the village elders of the plain, because she has promised to halve the rice tax and dissolve the Shogunate. Her followers wear a white lotus. The houses think her a puppet; the Dowager thinks her the last chance the isles have to be ruled rather than owned.
