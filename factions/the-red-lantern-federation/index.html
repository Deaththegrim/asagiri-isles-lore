The Red Lantern Federation

The Red Lantern Federation is a league of bandit bands along the post road and the plain, named for the red lanterns they hang in the inns that pay them. They rob convoys, kidnap merchants for ransom and sell what they take in Saikai's back streets. Each band has its own boss; the Federation has a code (no robbing a paying inn, no killing a paid-up traveller, and a share for the Lantern Mother). Travellers fear them; innkeepers pay them; Hiwada riders hang them.
