The Post-Road Wardens

The Post-Road Wardens keep the Fifty Stations Road: the station masters, toll clerks, post riders and road guards who run the highway from Saikai to Mistmarsh under the Shogunate's charter. They keep the stables fed, the inns licensed, the mail moving and the barriers manned, and they hate anything that disturbs the road's order: bandits, duelists, ronin bands and rumours of a station that is not on the map. Travellers grumble at their fees and are glad of their patrols.
