House Takamori

House Takamori is the regent's own house, the oldest of the four great samurai houses of Miyako Plain, and its crane crest flies over the capital's inner gate. Takamori swordsmen are famous for the draw-cut: the blade leaves the scabbard and the fight is over. The house holds the regency in the dying emperor's name, keeps the post road's toll barriers, and judges disputes between the other houses. Its retainers are drilled in poetry as hard as in the sword, and they are expected to die well. To the common people of the plain the Takamori are order itself: taxes collected on time, bandits hanged at the crossroads, festivals paid for. To the other houses they are a family that has ruled in someone else's name for three generations and has forgotten it was ever borrowed.
