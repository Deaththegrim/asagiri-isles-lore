House Hiwada

House Hiwada is the horse house of the plain: mounted archers who ride from childhood and shoot from the saddle at full gallop. Their sun-arrow crest guards the open country between the capital and the mountains, and Hiwada riders hunt bandits down the long roads that the post barriers do not reach. The house breeds the best horses in the isles and sells them dear. Farmers of the plain trust Hiwada riders more than any other samurai, because the Hiwada come when the bandits do; the capital thinks them rustic, loud and very hard to command.
