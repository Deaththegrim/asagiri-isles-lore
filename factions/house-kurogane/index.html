House Kurogane

House Kurogane is the iron house: the great samurai family that holds Tetsuyama, its forges and its mines, and arms half the isles. Kurogane warriors fight in heavy lacquered iron that no ordinary blade can open, and they advance slowly and never step back. The house sends the regent its tribute in swords rather than rice, and every house's armoury carries the black iron crest, three interlocking rings in a circle, somewhere. The people of the volcanic isle call the Kurogane hard and fair: they work their miners to the bone and feed them well. In the capital they are called smiths with titles, and Kurogane lords have long memories for that.
