Veilgate

Veilgate is a district of the Asagiri Isles. A shrine city on the northwest cliffs above the Sea of Fog, built around a giant pale torii at the cliff's edge: the gate through which the Veil Court ferries the dead. Pilgrims come to say farewells, Veil Officers keep their living-side post here, and at night lanterns drift out over the fog. When the war dead pile up faster than they can be ferried, masked and hungry spirits climb back up the cliffs. Its town is Tasogare; its other places: Black Ferry Fortress, Grey Thread Shrine, The Great Gate Cliffs, Pilgrims' Hostel, Mask Shore, Hanging Lantern Village, Deserters' Gorge, Ferrymen's Rest, Weeping Torii, Old Battle Barrow, Fog Watch Tower, Moorland Graves. By land it joins Kurosugi Range.
