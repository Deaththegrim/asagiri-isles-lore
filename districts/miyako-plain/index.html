Miyako Plain

Miyako Plain is a district of the Asagiri Isles. The heartland: a broad river delta of rice paddies around Miyako, the walled castle city where the puppet emperor sits in his palace and the regent rules from the castle keep beside it. Samurai houses keep their mansions inside the walls and their quarrels in the streets; duels are fought at dawn on the river bridges. South of the walls the post road crosses at Oiwake, where the road down to the Ember Strait ferry begins. Its town is Miyako; its other places: Miyako Castle, The Inner Palace, Hidetsugu's Manor, Tournament Grounds, Hiwada Horse Fields, Straw Mask Hill Camp, Shogunate Rice Granary, Crossroads Gallows, Old Battlefield of Sekigane, Kappa River Fords, Ruined Summer Palace, Sun-Rite Shrine of the Plain, Hundred Fields Village. By land it joins Fifty Stations Road, Kurosugi Range, Seimei Bureau.
