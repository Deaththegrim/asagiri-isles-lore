Kurosugi Range

Kurosugi Range is a district of the Asagiri Isles. A spine of black cedar mountains north of the capital, mist in every valley, paths that lead nowhere unless you know the sign. Two hidden villages sell their shinobi to whoever pays: Kagegi on the western slopes and Hayate on the eastern. They have been at peace for eleven years and at war for three hundred. The post road reaches only the outer checkpoint, and from a cove on the northern coast the only ferry to the ice isle crosses White Strait. Its town is Kagegi Village; its other places: Hayate Ridge Village, Mountain Temple of the Seven Falls, Valley of the Rank Exams, Hanging Bridge Pass, Ascetics' Peak, Woodcutters' Hamlet, Bear Den Ridge, Village Border Stones, Earth Spider Pits, Wind Shrine of the Pass, Snowline Huts, Monks' Herb Fields, Kagegi Outer Watchpost. By land it joins Ayakashi Wood, Miyako Plain, Veilgate; by sea Hyoga Reach (across White Strait).
