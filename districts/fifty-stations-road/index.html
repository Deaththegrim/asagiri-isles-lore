Fifty Stations Road

Fifty Stations Road is a district of the Asagiri Isles. The great highway from Saikai's east gate along the edge of the Blight, past the capital and on to the marsh coast: post stations a day apart, inns with paper lanterns, tea houses, toll barriers that check papers and station, and the bandits who watch the barriers from the hills. Every traveller in the isles passes along it; at the Oiwake crossroads the road north climbs to the capital and the road south runs down to Ember Strait. Its town is Oiwake Station; its other places: Road Commissioner's Office, Seventh Station Inn, Cedar Pass Barrier, Red Lantern Roost, Duelists' Teahouse, Pine Avenue, River Ferry Crossing, Ghost Pine Hill, Bandits' Bridge, Wayside Jizo Shrine, Rice-Cake Peddlers' Rest, Porters' Lodge. By land it joins Ashfall Blight, Mistmarsh Coast, Miyako Plain, Saikai Free Port; by sea Tetsuyama (across Ember Strait).
