Hinode Hold

Hinode Hold is a district of the Asagiri Isles. The snowbound north of the main island, its highest peaks facing the sunrise, crowned by the breath hunters' fortress. Below it the night valleys hold demon nests that the hunters burn out each spring and that are full again by autumn. From the fortress's wall the hunters watch the sun come up over the eastern sea before every hunt. Its town is Asahi Gate; its other places: Hinode Fortress, The Long Night Wall, Burned Village of Kitamura, Snow Monkey Springs, Orphans' Lodge, Frozen Pass of the Long Night, Hunters' Graveyard, Snowbound Trading Post, Breath Masters' Retreat, Yuki-onna's Valley, Watchfire Hill, Hunters' Supply Road. By land it joins Ayakashi Wood.
