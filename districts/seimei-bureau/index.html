Seimei Bureau

Seimei Bureau is a district of the Asagiri Isles. A lake city laid out on a precise grid inside a star-shaped ward wall, seat of the onmyoji college that reads the stars, names the lucky days, binds paper familiars and keeps the realm's wards. Nothing unwarded passes its gates by night. The Bureau and the shrines quarrel over who may exorcise what; both quarrel with the regent over who pays for it. Its town is Seimei; its other places: Bureau of Yin and Yang, Mask-Maker's Workshop, Lake of Stars, Paper Familiar Workshop, The Old Observatory, Talisman Market, Expelled Scholars' Quarter, Haunted Teahouse Lane, Lakeside Pleasure Boats, Moonview Bridge, Old Ward Stones, Bureau Dormitories. By land it joins Ayakashi Wood, Mistmarsh Coast, Miyako Plain.
