War Bounty: Nest Elder Crow Tengu

Go-Between of the Last Open Gate, Lantern-Market Go-Between Konta posted it on the Kitsunebi Crossing, The Last Open Gate notice board: A crow tengu elder strips travellers of anything with a house crest on it, and the travellers too. The bounty is 90 Mon on proof, counted out by the clerk only when the bounty completes, never before.

Goal: End the Elder of the War Plunder, Nest Elder Crow Tengu's trouble for good
