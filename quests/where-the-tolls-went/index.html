Where the Tolls Went

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

The Shogunate's investigators want to know where the road's tolls went in the war. They went to the Regent's war chest, on the Regent's order, and the commissioner kept the receipt.
