Where the Treasury Went

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

When the Cedar Pass barrier fell, its lieutenant carried the treasury out. It is in Kurokami's fort now, and the Regent's office has told the new captain to leave it there.

Goal: Find the lieutenant at the Roost
