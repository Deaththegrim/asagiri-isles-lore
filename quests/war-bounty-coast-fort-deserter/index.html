War Bounty: Coast Fort Deserter

Stilt-Market Crier Funao, Crier Under the Flags posted it on the Ashiura, Under Smugglers' Flags notice board: Deserters hold the empty coast fort and shoot at anyone in a house's colours. The bounty is 60 Mon on proof.

Goal: End the Coast Fort Deserter, Deserter Who Walked Home's trouble for good
