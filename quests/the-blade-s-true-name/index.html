The Blade's True Name

Gate-Warden Sayo Amagiri teaches an officer to hear the name their blade has carried since it was forged: a lesson in listening, a duel with the blade's own spirit on the Ferry Stones, and a dusk on the Drowned Causeway with the name spoken. Only a Veil Officer's blade answers; companions may hold the line beside them.

Goal: Hold the Drowned Causeway against what comes up at dusk
