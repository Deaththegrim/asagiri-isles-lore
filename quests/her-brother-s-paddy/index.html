Her Brother's Paddy

Oyone's brother worked the paddy by the channel until the smugglers took it to hide their cargo, and he went to the war and did not come back. She wants it planted with plain rice again, in his name.

Goal: Plant the first rice in the paddy with Farmer Oyone
