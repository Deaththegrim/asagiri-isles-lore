Two Stations

Pilgrim Oharu's son rode the post road and did not come back. She walks it shrine by shrine and will not hurry her vow, so she asks the party to go ahead to the last two stations he would have reached and bring back their seals and whatever the keepers remember.

Goal: Bring back the stamp of the Seventh Station Inn
