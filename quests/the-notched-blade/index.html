The Notched Blade

Forge-Widow Akagane notches blades in the back of a polishing shop, which is why the guild pretends not to know her. She teaches the Ember Edge to anyone willing to ruin a good sword for it.

Goal: Cut a straw target to ash in front of Akagane without burning the shop
