War Bounty: Trading-Post Thrall

Keeper of the Double Bell, Mess-Hall Keeper Tetsuji posted it on the Asahi Gate, Under the Broken Wall notice board: Thralls from the Daylight Valleys trade at the snowbound post and carry its news north. The bounty is 75 Mon on proof, counted out by the clerk only when the bounty completes, never before.

Goal: End the Thrall on the Front Post, Trading-Post Thrall's trouble for good
