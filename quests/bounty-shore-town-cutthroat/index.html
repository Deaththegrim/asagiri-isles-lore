Bounty: Shore-Town Cutthroat

Exiles'-Hall Clerk Yukimaro posted it on the Shirokawa notice board: A branded cutthroat on the seal hunters' quay robs and drowns new exiles. The bounty is 110 Mon on proof, counted out by the clerk only when the bounty completes, never before.

Goal: End the Shore-Town Cutthroat's trouble for good
