War Bounty: Sekigane Grave-Robber Band

Scribe of Three Notice Boards, Magistrate's Scribe Oriha posted it on the Miyako, Besieged notice board: Robbers dig the new war mounds at Sekigane for armour and leave the dead uncovered. The bounty is 75 Mon on proof, counted out by the clerk only when the bounty completes, never before.

Goal: End the Mound-Diggers of the Twice-Fought Field, Sekigane Grave-Robber Band's trouble for good
