Bounty: Supply-Road Wolf Pack

Mess-Hall Keeper Tetsuji posted it on the Asahi Gate notice board: A wolf pack is taking the last cart of every supply train on the hunters' road. The bounty is 50 Mon on proof, counted out by the clerk only when the bounty completes, never before.

Goal: End the Supply-Road Wolf Pack's trouble for good
