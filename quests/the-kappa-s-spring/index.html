The Kappa's Spring

Sarumaru has kept the hot spring for twenty-nine years and will share it for a tithe, agreed on terms, or for a sumo bout won fair against a kappa who cheats.

Goal: Pay the tithe, or beat Sarumaru at sumo without letting him cheat
