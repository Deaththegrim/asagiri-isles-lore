The Inn's Old Sign

Jinbei's father carved the Last Inn East's sign, and soldiers took it for firewood the winter the road fell. He says the inn is only half open until it hangs again, and he thinks the sign went up the hill with the deserters.

Goal: Hang the sign over the Seventh Station Inn's door with Jinbei
