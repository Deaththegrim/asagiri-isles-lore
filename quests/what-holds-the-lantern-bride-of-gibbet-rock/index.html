What Holds The Lantern Bride of Gibbet Rock

The Lantern Bride of Gibbet Rock haunts Pirate Graves Reef. It will not say what holds it to the world, and it will not rest until it has it.

Goal: Lay The Lantern Bride of Gibbet Rock to rest: cut it down, give it what it wants, or deceive it
