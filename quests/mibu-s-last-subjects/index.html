Mibu's Last Subjects

After the war. This page is about the isles after the Great War. Pages about what follows the war stay covered until you choose to see them.

Mibu was brought to judgement before the war, but his subjects are still on the Records wing's rolls, and some of them were called up as deputies. Asagi wants them found and freed before anyone reads the rolls.

Goal: Free them from the rolls
