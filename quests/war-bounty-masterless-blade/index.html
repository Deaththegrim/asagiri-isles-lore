War Bounty: Masterless Blade

Keeper of the Debt Board, Notice-Keeper Kagura posted it on the Saikai, The Debt Harbour notice board: A ronin back from the war sells his sword on Lantern Row and has cut down two men who would not pay him. The bounty is 60 Mon on proof, counted out by the clerk only when the bounty completes, never before.

Goal: End the Unpaid Blade, Masterless Blade's trouble for good
