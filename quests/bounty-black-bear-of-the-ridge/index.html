Bounty: Black Bear of the Ridge

Mission-Hall Steward Kiri posted it on the Kagegi Village notice board: A black bear on the ridge has learned that porters carry food, and it no longer fears them. The bounty is 40 Mon on proof, counted out by the clerk only when the bounty completes, never before.

Goal: End the Black Bear of the Ridge's trouble for good
