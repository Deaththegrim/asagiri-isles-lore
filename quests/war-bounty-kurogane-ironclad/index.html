War Bounty: Kurogane Ironclad

Forge-Row Tally-Keeper Hinoe posted it on the Hibana, The Brighter Forges notice board: An ironclad of the victorious house enforces the forges' silence with a war blade. The bounty is 80 Mon on proof, counted out by the clerk only when the bounty completes, never before.

Goal: End the Kurogane Ironclad's trouble for good
