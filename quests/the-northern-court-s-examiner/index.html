The Northern Court's Examiner

Proctor Kagegi Isshin came through the war on the Northern Court's side and holds the old exam valley for it. He runs the rank exams again, for genin of both villages who will swear to the Long Night, and fails the ones who will not by burying them in the trap grove.

Goal: End Isshin's exams: bring him down, or deliver him to Kagegi's judgement
