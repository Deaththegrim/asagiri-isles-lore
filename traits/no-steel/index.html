No Steel

Steel is not for you, by choice. Wood, paper, fist and Soul are enough, and the vow pays you back in power for the steel you gave up. Smiths think you a fool. The vow: you carry and use no metal weapon. Break it and its power leaves you until you atone at a vow temple.

Gives: Spirit +2; Open Hand +1, Soulcraft +1; technique Empty Hand (Your bare hands carry your Soul. Improves the next unarmed technique).
Pairs well with: Bare Fists, Staff, Talismans, Soul. Fists, wood and paper; Soul fills the gap.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
