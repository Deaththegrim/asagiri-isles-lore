Cat-Blooded

A cat parent: night eyes, silent feet, a split tail some days, and the dead's fondness. You see in the dark, land on your feet and hear the dead. Cats follow you, and some of them are not cats.

Gives: Guile +1; Stealth +2, Footwork +2; Soul +5 (maximum and starting); technique Nine Lives' Luck (Land on your feet from a fall or a blow that should have finished you. Improves the next action taken to escape harm); resists curse; weak to piercing.
Pairs well with: Shadow, Void, Kunai and Wire. Night eyes and silent feet; resists curses, weak to points.

Primary Attribute: Guile
