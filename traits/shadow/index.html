Shadow

You fight unseen and unfair: the ambush, the lie, the blade in the back. Whatever your tradition, you settle things before they start. Your free-form moves roll your lane (Stealth, Hidden Arms, Deception) whenever they can be done your way.

Gives: Guile +1; Stealth +2, Hidden Arms +1, Deception +1; technique First Strike Unseen (Strike from where they are not looking. Improves the next attack against a foe who has not seen you); unlocks as you train: Blend In (Stealth 6), Behind You (Stealth 12), Silent Kill (Stealth 18), The Shadow Army (Stealth 24).
Pairs well with: Shinobi, Cut-Cord, Kunai and Wire, Umbrella Blade. Stealth and deception; first strike from hiding.

Primary Attribute: Guile
