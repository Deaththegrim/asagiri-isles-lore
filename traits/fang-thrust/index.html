Fang Thrust

A one-handed charging thrust from a crouched stance, the off hand aimed along the blade like a bowstring. Watchmen and police captains of the capital swear by it; it has one idea, and the idea goes through armour.

Gives: Blade +1; technique Level Fang (The basic charge: a level one-handed thrust from the crouch. Improves the next Blade action that charges a single foe); technique Aim Along the Hand (Sights along the off hand before the lunge and does not miss. Improves the next Blade action that thrusts at a weak point); unlocks as you train: Rising Fang (Blade 6), Downward Fang (Blade 12), Through the Second Man (Blade 18), Zero-Distance Fang (Blade 24).
Pairs well with: Samurai, Spear, Steel. The thrust is a spear's idea in a sword: Steel carries the charge.

Primary Attribute: Body
