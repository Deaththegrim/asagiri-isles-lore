Mountain Ascetic

A yamabushi of the mountain paths: hardship as practice, conch shell and staff, spirits of the peaks as company. You walk the mountain paths with conch and staff and sleep in the cold on purpose. Tengu tolerate you and villages feed you.

Gives:
• Walk Through Fire (technique): Your practice lets you endure what would break others: fire walked, cold slept in. Improves the next Stamina or Resolve action against the elements.
Pairs well with: Steel, Staff, Fire. Stamina and warding on the mountain path; a staff and the fire-walk.

Primary Attribute: Body
