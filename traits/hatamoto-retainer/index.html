Hatamoto Retainer

A sworn bannerman of the regent, owing service directly to the castle and outranking house retainers. You serve the regent directly and outrank house samurai who are older and better born. You are trusted with errands nobody else may know about.

Gives: Guile +1; Intrigue +2, Blade +2; Health +5 (maximum and starting); technique By the Regent's Order (Invoke the regent's authority: officials comply, guards step aside, until someone checks. Improves the next Intrigue action with officials).
Pairs well with: Shadow, Katana, Sworn to One Master. The regent's errand-runner: intrigue, a blade and one master.

Primary Attribute: Guile
