Bare Fists

Wrapped hands and nothing else. Monks, wrestlers, oni-blooded brawlers: you are never disarmed, and nobody at a toll barrier takes your weapons away.

Gives: Open Hand +1; technique Open Palm (Bare fists, open palm: the blow lands where armour does not cover. Improves the next Open Hand action against an armoured foe); starts with Hand Wraps (damage +2 while equipped).
Pairs well with: Oni-Blooded, Exorcist Monk, No Steel. Open hand, never disarmed.

Primary Attribute: Body
