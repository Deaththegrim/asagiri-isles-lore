Onmyoji

Student of the yin-yang arts: wards, talismans, paper familiars, star reading and the calendar of lucky and unlucky days. You write your Soul into paper and the world keeps it.

Gives: Spirit +2, Will +1; Warding +2, Summoning +2, Divination +1, Lore +1; Soul +10 (maximum and starting); technique Paper Servant (Fold, breathe on and throw a paper talisman: it becomes a bird, a dog or a soldier of paper that serves until torn. Improves the next Summoning action. Spends 12 Soul); technique Barrier of Five Seals (Five talismans thrown to the points of a star: a barrier nothing lesser crosses. Strongly improves the next Warding action that holds a place. Spends 25 Soul); cantrip Paper Crane (A folded slip of paper flies where you point and tells you what it saw. A cantrip: free and repeatable, a small edge to the next Divination or Awareness action, never a strike); unlocks as you train: Talisman Volley (Warding 6), Fox-Fire Talisman (Warding 12, with Fox-Fire Kindled), Purification Rite (Warding 12), Purifying Rain (Warding 12, with Water), Star-Path Barrier (Warding 18), Grand Rite of Yin and Yang (Warding 24); starts with Talisman Case (Warding +1 while equipped).
Pairs well with: Soul, Talismans, Earth, Temple Orphan. Warding and summoning want Soul and Talismans; Earth holds a ward line.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
