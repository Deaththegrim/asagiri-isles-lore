Onmyoji

Student of the yin-yang arts: wards, talismans, paper familiars, star reading and the calendar of lucky and unlucky days. You write your Soul into paper and the world keeps it.

Gives:
• Paper Servant (technique): Fold, breathe on and throw a paper talisman: it becomes a bird, a dog or a soldier of paper that serves until torn. Improves the next Summoning action. Spends 12 Soul.
• Barrier of Five Seals (technique): Five talismans thrown to the points of a star: a barrier nothing lesser crosses. Strongly improves the next Warding action that holds a place. Spends 25 Soul.
• Paper Crane (cantrip): A folded slip of paper flies where you point and tells you what it saw. A cantrip: free and repeatable, a small edge to the next Divination or Awareness action, never a strike.
• Unlocks as you train: Talisman Volley (Warding 6), Fox-Fire Talisman (Warding 12, with Fox-Fire Kindled), Purification Rite (Warding 12), Purifying Rain (Warding 12, with Water), Star-Path Barrier (Warding 18), Grand Rite of Yin and Yang (Warding 24)
• Starts with Talisman Case (Warding +1 while equipped)
Pairs well with: Soul, Talismans, Earth, Temple Orphan. Warding and summoning want Soul and Talismans; Earth holds a ward line.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
