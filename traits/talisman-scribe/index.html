Talisman Scribe

A calligrapher of talismans whose ink holds Soul as stone holds heat. Your calligraphy holds Soul like stone holds heat, and every shrine in the isles buys your work. Forgers copy you badly, and you can always tell.

Gives:
• Written Ward (technique): Write a talisman for a specific danger: it holds against that one thing. Improves the next Warding action against a named threat. Spends 18 Soul.
Pairs well with: Soul, Talismans, Earth. Warding and sealwork are the brush's art.

Primary Attribute: Will
