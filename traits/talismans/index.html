Talismans

Paper and ink as weapons: wards thrown like knives, seals slapped on a blade, curses folded small. They run on Soul and skill rather than muscle.

Gives: Warding +1; technique Ink Strike (A talisman thrown flares on touch. Improves the next Warding action against a spirit or a technique. Spends 12 Soul); starts with Battle Talismans (damage +2 while equipped).
Pairs well with: Onmyoji, Soul, Talisman Scribe. Warding as a weapon.

Primary Attribute: Will
