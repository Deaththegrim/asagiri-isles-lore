Yumeno Clan

A Kagegi clan of mind-walkers who enter other people's thoughts through a touch and a held breath. You were taught to keep your own mind locked while you walk in others'. You have seen things in people's heads that you cannot forget.

Gives: Spirit +1; Mindwalk +3, Intrigue +1; Soul +5 (maximum and starting); technique Step Inside (A touch and a held breath: you are inside their mind for a moment, hearing what they think. Improves the next Mindwalk action. Spends 18 Soul).
Pairs well with: Shadow, Water, Umbrella Blade. Mind-walkers win before the fight; a quiet weapon keeps the cover.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
