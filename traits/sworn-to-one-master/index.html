Sworn to One Master

You serve one person and none other. Their word is your law; any other lord's is noise. Name your master in your backstory; the vow makes you stronger at their side and in their cause. The vow: you name one master and never act against them. Break it and its power leaves you until you atone at a vow temple.

Gives: Will +2; Blade +1, Intrigue +1; technique For My Master (Act in your master's service. Improves the next action taken on your master's behalf).
Pairs well with: Hatamoto Retainer, Samurai, Katana. A blade at one master's side.

Primary Attribute: Guile
