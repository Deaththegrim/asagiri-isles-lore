Beast Breath

A breath of the wild hunt: forms that harry a quarry and wear it down like a wolf pack. You can follow a demon's trail for days and strike only when it is too tired to turn. Beast hunters come back last, and they come back with the head.

Gives: Body +1; Breathcraft +2, Tracking +2; Health +5 (maximum and starting); technique Harrying Form (Strike again and again at a wounded quarry. Improves the next attack against a foe you have already wounded).
Pairs well with: Steel, Earth, Chain-Sickle. Wearing a quarry down; a chain to hold it.

Primary Attribute: Body
