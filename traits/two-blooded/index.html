Two-Blooded

Not a half-yokai, but a quarter or an eighth: a yokai grandparent's gift surfaces in you now and then, a pair of ears, a shadow with a tail, a scent of rain. You pass as human most days. Drawing on it spends Hold; a Half-Yokai cannot take it.

Gives:
• Old Blood Stirs (technique): The grandparent's gift surfaces for a moment. Spends 10 Hold.
• Blood Remembered (technique): For a scene the yokai blood shows fully. Spends 25 Hold.
• Unlocks as you train: Grandmother's Ears (Shapeshifting 6), Quarter Change (Shapeshifting 12), Ancestor's Glamour (Shapeshifting 18), Grandparent's Kin (Shapeshifting 24)
Pairs well with: Shinobi, Onmyoji, Soul. A touch of yokai for a human tradition (not for Half-Yokai).

Primary Attribute: Spirit
