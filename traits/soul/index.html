Soul

You fight with the current itself: raw technique, released and shaped. Whatever your tradition, you settle things with what your Soul can do. Your free-form moves roll your lane (Soulcraft, Sealwork, Sensing) whenever they can be done your way.

Gives: Spirit +1; Soulcraft +2, Sealwork +1, Sensing +1; technique Open the Current (Let your Soul run wide for one technique: bigger, louder, more costly. Improves the next Soulcraft action. Spends 12 Soul); unlocks as you train: Current Read (Soulcraft 6), Soul Overflow (Soulcraft 12), Twin Current (Soulcraft 18), The River Beneath (Soulcraft 24).
Pairs well with: Onmyoji, Veil Officer, Curse-Bearer, Talismans. Soulcraft and seals; the traditions that shape Soul directly.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
