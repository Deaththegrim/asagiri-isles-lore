Paired Short Blades

Two short swords instead of one long one: a guard that turns every attack and then turns into a whirl. Bodyguards and secret watchmen favour it for close rooms, rooftops and corridors.

Gives: Blade +1; technique Crossed Guard (Catches a blow between both blades and holds it. Improves the next Blade action that blocks a heavy strike); technique Turn and Answer (One blade turns the attack, the other answers through the gap. Improves the next Blade action that counters in a narrow space); unlocks as you train: Rooftop Flow (Blade 6), Whirling Blades (Blade 12), Six Turns of the Guard (Blade 18), The Whirl That Becomes a Storm (Blade 24).
Pairs well with: Shinobi, Kunai and Wire, Shadow. Close-quarters steel: two short blades suit rooftops, corridors and the ambush.

Primary Attribute: Body
