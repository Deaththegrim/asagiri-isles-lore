Bone Weave

Your bones can be shaped: grown into blades along the forearm, plates under the skin, a spear from the palm. It is horrifying to watch and painful to do. Drawing on it spends Hold.

Gives: Open Hand +1, Stamina +1; technique Bone Blade (A blade of bone slides from the forearm. Spends 10 Hold); technique Ossuary Armour (A full shell of bone covers you for a fight. Spends 25 Hold); unlocks as you train: Bone Spurs (Open Hand 6), Marrow Blade (Open Hand 12, with Steel), Ribcage Shield (Open Hand 12), Spine Whip (Open Hand 18), Ossuary Garden (Open Hand 24).
Pairs well with: Oni-Blooded, Bare Fists, No Steel. Your bones are the weapon, so you need no steel.

Primary Attribute: Body
