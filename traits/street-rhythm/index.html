Street Rhythm

A style with no school, learned in harbour fights and gambling dens: the blade comes from any angle, the feet kick as often as the sword cuts, and the fighter is upside down as often as not. Formal swordsmen hate it because it never does the same thing twice.

Gives: Footwork +1; technique Handstand Cut (Drops to the hands and cuts from below with the blade held in the feet's swing. Improves the next Footwork action that strikes from an unexpected angle); technique Broken Beat (Changes rhythm mid-exchange so a trained opponent's timing lands on nothing. Improves the next Blade action against a schooled fighter); unlocks as you train: Off the Wall (Footwork 6), Spinning Scabbard Kick (Footwork 12), Never the Same Twice (Footwork 18), Last Dance at the Harbour (Footwork 24).
Pairs well with: Ronin, Katana, Bare Fists. Footwork and a light sword, and fists for when the sword is somewhere else.

Primary Attribute: Body
