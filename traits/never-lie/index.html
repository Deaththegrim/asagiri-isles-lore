Never Lie

You do not lie. Not to enemies, not to lovers, not to save your life. People learn that your word is iron, and the wrong people learn to ask you the right questions. The vow: you never say anything you believe is false. Break it and its power leaves you until you atone at a vow temple.

Gives: Will +2; Intrigue +1, Resolve +1; technique True Word (Say a true thing in a way that cannot be doubted. Improves the next action to be believed).
Pairs well with: Noble, Hostage-Ward, Samurai. Intrigue backed by a word that is iron.

Primary Attribute: Will
