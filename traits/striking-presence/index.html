Striking Presence

People look twice when you enter a room. It opens doors, and it makes hiding very hard. Painters ask to draw you; spies hate working with you. You have learned to wear a hood when it matters.

Gives: Artistry +1.
Pairs well with: Noble, Blossom Breath. A presence that is its own art.
