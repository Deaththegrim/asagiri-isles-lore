Okamura Clan

A Hayate clan that fights beside wolves raised from pups, two bodies with one mind in a fight. Your wolf was a pup in your arms and is a killer at your side. Where it cannot go, you do not go either, and inns rarely want it.

Gives: Body +1; Beast Bond +2, Tracking +2; Soul +5 (maximum and starting); technique Two Bodies, One Hunt (You and your wolf strike together from two sides. Improves the next Open Hand or Beast-bond action made alongside your wolf).
Pairs well with: Steel, Earth, Chain-Sickle. You fight beside your wolf up close; beast-bond and tracking keep you on the trail.

Primary Attribute: Will
