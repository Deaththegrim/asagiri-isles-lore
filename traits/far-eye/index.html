Far Eye

An eye that sees through walls, across valleys and into the flow of Soul in a body. The iris pales to frost when it opens. Shinobi clans prize it above gold. Drawing on it spends Hold.

Gives: Sensing +1, Tracking +1; technique Far Glance (See through a wall, across a valley or into a body's Soul for a breath. Spends 10 Hold); technique Eye of the Whole Field (For a scene, nothing within a hundred paces is hidden from you. Spends 25 Hold); unlocks as you train: Soul Map (Sensing 6), Marked From Afar (Sensing 12), Seal Through the Eye (Sealwork 12, with Shinobi), Sight Shared (Sensing 18), The Eye of the Isles (Sensing 24).
Pairs well with: Shinobi, Bow, Pursuit Division. Sensing and tracking at range.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
