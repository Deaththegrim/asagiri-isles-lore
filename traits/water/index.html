Water

Your Soul is water: it flows around what it cannot break and drowns what it can. Water resists water and fears the earth that dams it.

Gives:
• Tide Drawn (technique): Water gathers from air, ground or river and moves as you will. Improves the next Soulcraft action that shapes water. Spends 12 Soul.
• Cup of Rain (cantrip): Water gathers from the air into a cupped hand, or wrings itself out of a soaked sleeve. A cantrip: free and repeatable, a small edge to the next Soulcraft or Medicine action, never a strike.
• Unlocks as you train: Water Whip (Soulcraft 6), Mist Shroud (Soulcraft 12), Purifying Rain (Warding 12, with Onmyoji), Crashing Wave (Soulcraft 18), The Sea Remembers (Soulcraft 24)
Pairs well with: Tide Breath, Kirisame Clan, Dragon-Blooded. Flowing and drowning; weak to Earth.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
