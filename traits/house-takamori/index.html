House Takamori

The regent's own house, masters of the draw-cut, closest to power and most hated for it. You were trained in the castle's own dojo beside the regent's sons. Your name opens doors, and your enemies are the regent's enemies whether you chose them or not.

Gives: Body +1; Blade +2, Intrigue +2; Health +5 (maximum and starting); technique Takamori Draw (The house's draw-cut, taught only to its blood: a cut and a sheathing in one motion. Improves the next Blade action made from the scabbard).
Pairs well with: Steel, Katana, Noble. The draw-cut house: a katana and a court name.

Primary Attribute: Body
