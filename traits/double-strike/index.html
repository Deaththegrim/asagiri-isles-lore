Double Strike

A temple breaker's art: two impacts in one blow, the second landing an instant after the first while the target is still braced against it, so it breaks what the first only struck. Its users carry a sword and rarely draw it.

Gives: Open Hand +1; technique Two in One (A punch that lands twice: the second impact breaks what the first braced. Improves the next Open Hand action against armour or stone); technique Hilt Breaker (The same art through a sword's hilt or pommel, without drawing. Improves the next Open Hand action made while wearing a sheathed blade); unlocks as you train: Through the Guard (Open Hand 6), Breaking the Stone (Open Hand 12), Two-Handed Double (Open Hand 18), The Strike Without a Fist (Open Hand 24).
Pairs well with: Bare Fists, Ronin, Steel. Open-hand and might: bare fists for the art, a ronin's sword for when it is not enough.

Primary Attribute: Body
