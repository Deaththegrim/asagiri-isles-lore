Breath Hunter

Sworn to the hunter fortress in the north and trained to breathe in forms that push Soul through the body for one impossible movement. You cut demons with sun-forged steel, and the Hold expects you back at the wall every Demon Night.

Gives: Body +2, Will +1; Breathcraft +2, Blade +2, Stamina +1, Resolve +1; Health +10 (maximum and starting); technique First Breath Form (Draw the breath your style is built on and move once faster than anything human should. Strongly improves the next Breathcraft or Blade action. Spends 25 Soul); technique Sun-Steel Cut (Your blade was forged in the light of the Hold's wall: a demon's wound from it does not close. Improves the next action that attacks a demon, and the wound stays); cantrip Measured Breath (One measured breath: the heart slows and the hands steady. A cantrip: free and repeatable, a small edge to the next Stamina or Resolve action, never a strike); unlocks as you train: Second Breath Form (Breathcraft 6), Night-Watch Breath (Breathcraft 12), Sunrise Ember Form (Breathcraft 12, with Fire), Breath Between Heartbeats (Breathcraft 18, with Still Eye), Dawn-Seeker Form (Breathcraft 18), Breath of the Wall (Breathcraft 24); starts with Hunter's Sun-Steel Tanto (Breathcraft +1 while equipped).
Pairs well with: Steel, Katana, Fire, Never Retreat. Breathcraft and blade cut demons, and Fire burns what sunlight cannot reach.

Primary Attribute: Body
