Cut-Cord

A deserter who cut the village's cord and runs with its secrets. The village's hunters are somewhere behind you. You burned off the village mark and live on what you took with you: techniques, names, a map of the hidden paths. Every shinobi stranger might be a hunter.

Gives: Guile +1; Stealth +2, Streetwise +2; Soul +5 (maximum and starting); technique Nobody's Shinobi (No village, no mark, no pattern: you fight in no style they have trained against. Improves the next action against a village-trained shinobi).
Pairs well with: Shadow, Outcast, Umbrella Blade. A deserter lives by stealth and streetwise; a hidden blade passes every barrier.

Primary Attribute: Guile
