Sword-for-Hire

You sell your sword by the day: bodyguard, debt collector, duellist for someone else's honour. You have guarded merchants, collected debts and fought other men's duels. You keep your contracts, because a sword nobody trusts earns nothing.

Gives: Guile +1; Streetwise +2, Blade +2; Health +5 (maximum and starting); technique Paid in Advance (A paid job is a promise: you fight harder for the client in front of you. Improves the next action that protects your current employer).
Pairs well with: Steel, Spear, Merchant's Child. Streetwise and blade sell by the day; a merchant's eye reads the contract.

Primary Attribute: Guile
