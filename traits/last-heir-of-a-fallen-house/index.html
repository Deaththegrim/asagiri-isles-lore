Last Heir of a Fallen House

The last of a house that was broken: its lands taken, its name struck from the rolls. You keep the house's sword, its crest hidden inside your collar, and a list of names. Old retainers may still serve you, if you can find them.

Gives: Will +1; Blade +2, Resolve +2; Health +5 (maximum and starting); technique For the Fallen House (Fight in the name of the dead house and its dead. Improves the next Blade or Resolve action against those who broke it, or in front of witnesses who remember it).
Pairs well with: Steel, Katana, Never Retreat. A grudge and a sword, and nowhere to retreat to.

Primary Attribute: Will
