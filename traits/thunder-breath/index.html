Thunder Breath

A breath that cracks like a storm: short, violent bursts and a body that tenses like a drawn bow. You cover a courtyard between two heartbeats. Thunder hunters are loud, fast and hard to keep alive.

Gives: Body +1; Breathcraft +3, Blade +1; Health +5 (maximum and starting); technique Storm Breaks (Burst forward and strike; the sound arrives after the cut. Improves the next Breathcraft action that opens an exchange. Spends 18 Soul).
Pairs well with: Steel, Wind, Katana. Bursts of speed; a katana for the single cut.

Primary Attribute: Body
