Body-Traded

Demons took pieces of you before you were born: an eye, a voice, a heartbeat. Something borrowed fills each gap, and it is not always on your side. Every piece you win back from the demon who holds it returns something of yourself.

Gives:
• What They Took (technique): Fight with the borrowed parts that replaced yours. Improves the next action against a demon that holds a piece of you.
Pairs well with: Steel, Katana, Never Retreat. Stamina and blade to win your pieces back.

Primary Attribute: Body
