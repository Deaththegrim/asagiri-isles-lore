Spear

A straight yari: reach, a point and a shaft that holds a charge or a doorway. Foot soldiers' weapon, master's weapon. Swordsmen who close the distance learn to respect the butt end.

Gives: Blade +1; technique Spear's Reach (The spear keeps them at the point's length. Improves the next Blade action against a foe trying to close); starts with Yari (damage +2 while equipped).
Pairs well with: Steel, House Mizuchi, Gate Guard. Reach to hold a deck or a gate.

Primary Attribute: Body
