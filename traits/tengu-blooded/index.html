Tengu-Blooded

A tengu parent: proud mountain spirits of wind and swordsmanship. You leap like the wind and fight with a swordsman's pride. Tengu Peak would take you in if you bowed.

Gives: Body +1; Blade +2, Footwork +2; Soul +5 (maximum and starting); technique Mountain Wind (A gust carries you up or across further than a leap should. Improves the next Footwork action); resists piercing; weak to slashing.
Pairs well with: Steel, Wind, Katana. Wind and swordsmanship; resists arrows, weak to cuts.

Primary Attribute: Body
