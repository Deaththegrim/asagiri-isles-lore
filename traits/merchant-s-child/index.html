Merchant's Child

Raised in a merchant house, counting mon before you could walk. You can read a ledger, a liar and a price. Guild halls open to you, samurai look down on you, and you know exactly what everyone in the room owes.

Gives: Streetwise +1, Deception +1; starts with Merchant's Abacus.
Pairs well with: Ronin, Gambler of Saikai, Foreign Pistol. Streetwise and deception; money opens the port.

Primary Attribute: Guile
