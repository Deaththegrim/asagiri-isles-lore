Blossom Breath

A breath of grace: flowing, beautiful forms that read an enemy's rhythm and answer it. People stop to watch you fight, and so do demons, to their cost.

Gives:
• Petal Fall (technique): Read the enemy's rhythm and cut between its beats. Improves the next Breathcraft action against a foe you have watched. Spends 18 Soul.
Pairs well with: Steel, Wind, Naginata. Graceful sweeping forms with reach.

Primary Attribute: Will
