Peasant

Born to the rice fields and the tax collector. You can work from dawn to dark and read weather, tracks and soil. Samurai expect you to bow, toll barriers expect you to pay, other commoners trust you, and lords forget your face.

Gives: Stamina +1, Tracking +1; starts with Straw Rain Cape.
Pairs well with: Ronin, Breath Hunter, Staff. Stamina and tracking; a staff passes every barrier.

Primary Attribute: Guile
