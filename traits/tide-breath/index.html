Tide Breath

A breath that flows: forms that turn and redirect like water, so a demon's strength carries it past you. You never meet force with force. Tide hunters live longest.

Gives: Will +1; Breathcraft +2, Resolve +2; Health +5 (maximum and starting); technique Turning Tide (Flow around a blow and turn it back. Improves the next action that counters an attack).
Pairs well with: Steel, Water, Katana. Flowing forms: Water and a blade that turns.

Primary Attribute: Will
