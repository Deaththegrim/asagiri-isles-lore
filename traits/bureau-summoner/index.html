Bureau Summoner

Trained at the Seimei Bureau in paper familiars and contracted spirits. You learned the Bureau's paper arts and hold a contract with at least one spirit. The Bureau's seal opens doors in the capital and closes them in the shrines.

Gives: Spirit +1; Summoning +3, Lore +1; Soul +5 (maximum and starting); technique Contracted Spirit (Call the spirit you hold a contract with; it comes and serves for a scene. Improves the next Summoning action. Spends 18 Soul).
Pairs well with: Soul, Talismans, Earth. Summoning through paper; Earth keeps the circle.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
