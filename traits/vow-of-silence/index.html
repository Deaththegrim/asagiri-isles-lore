Vow of Silence

You do not speak. Your techniques come without a word, and people fill your silence with what they want to hear. You talk with your hands, a brush, or not at all. The vow: you never speak aloud; you gesture, write or stay silent. Break it and its power leaves you until you atone at a vow temple.

Gives: Will +2; Stealth +1, Resolve +1; technique Silent Technique (Your techniques need no word or sound. Improves the next technique used without warning).
Pairs well with: Shinobi, Shadow, Talismans. Silent techniques and silent movement.

Primary Attribute: Will
