Noble

Born to a court family with a name and too many debts. You know the poems, the bows and the scandals. Commoners bow, barriers wave you through with the right papers, and every court in the isles knows something about your family.

Gives: Intrigue +1, Artistry +1; starts with Family Seal.
Pairs well with: Samurai, Onmyoji, War Fans. Intrigue and artistry open court doors.

Primary Attribute: Will
