Samurai

Sworn to a house and its lord, trained from childhood in the sword, the bow and the code that says when to die. Your Soul runs through the blade's edge, and a house's honour runs through you.

Gives: Body +2, Will +1; Blade +3, Intrigue +1, Resolve +1, Horsemanship +1; Health +10 (maximum and starting); technique Drawn Edge (The first cut, from the scabbard, faster than the eye: the draw is the strike. Strongly improves the first Blade action of a fight); technique Name Spoken (You announce your name, house and lineage to an enemy. The lesser hesitate, and your blade arm steadies. Improves the next Resolve or Blade action taken against the one you named yourself to); cantrip Scabbard Word (Hand on the hilt, the blade an inch out: a warning every soldier in the isles understands. A cantrip: free and repeatable, a small edge to the next Intrigue or Blade action that must be taken seriously, never a strike); unlocks as you train: Retainer's Stance (Blade 6), Cut of the House (Blade 12), Oath-Bound Blade (Blade 12, with Named Soul-Blade), Answer in the Stillness (Blade 18, with Still Eye), Mounted Charge (Blade 18), The Lord's Last Sword (Blade 24); starts with House Armour (Resolve +1 while equipped).
Pairs well with: Steel, Katana, Never Draw First, Noble. The blade and resolve skills stack with Steel and a sword; the vow turns your draw into the answering cut.

Primary Attribute: Body
