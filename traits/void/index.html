Void

Your Soul is void: the stillness between the other four, where masked and hungry things come apart. Void resists void and thins in sunlight.

Gives: Soulcraft +1; technique Empty Touch (A touch of nothing: a technique dies in the air, a spirit's mask cracks. Improves the next Soulcraft action against a technique or a spirit. Spends 12 Soul); cantrip Still Air (A patch of air goes silent and dull: a footstep unheard, a candle's glow thinned. A cantrip: free and repeatable, a small edge to the next Soulcraft or Stealth action, never a strike); unlocks as you train: Empty Palm (Soulcraft 6), Empty Curse (Cursecraft 12, with Curse-Bearer), Mask Breaker (Soulcraft 12), Void Ferry (Soulcraft 12, with Veil Officer), Silence Field (Soulcraft 18), The Mask Turned Inside Out (Resolve 18, with Inner Mask), The Space Between (Soulcraft 24); resists void; weak to sunlight.
Pairs well with: Veil Officer, Kurobane Clan, Inner Mask. Tears masked and hungry things apart; thins in sunlight, so it suits night work.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
