Snow-Blooded

A snow-woman or snow-man parent: cold breath, still face, a heart people think is frozen. Your breath can freeze and your face rarely moves. People think you cold, and they are wrong about why.

Gives: Spirit +1; Soulcraft +2, Resolve +2; Soul +5 (maximum and starting); technique White Breath (Breathe frost that slows and numbs whatever it touches. Improves the next Soulcraft action that chills or slows. Spends 18 Soul); resists piercing; weak to bludgeoning.
Pairs well with: Soul, Water, Naginata. Cold breath through Soul; resists points, weak to blunt blows.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
