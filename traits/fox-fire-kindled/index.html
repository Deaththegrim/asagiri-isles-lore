Fox-Fire Kindled

Pale blue fox-fire answers you, though no fox in your family will admit it. It lights the dark, lies to the eye, and in a storm it burns. Fox shrines notice you. Drawing on it spends Hold.

Gives: Soulcraft +1, Deception +1; technique Fox Lights (Pale lights that lead, confuse or burn a spirit. Spends 10 Hold); technique Fox-Fire Storm (A storm of blue fire that burns away illusions, spirits and masks. Spends 25 Hold); unlocks as you train: Fox-Fire Brand (Soulcraft 6), Burning Illusion (Soulcraft 12), Fox-Fire Talisman (Warding 12, with Onmyoji), Foxfire Procession (Soulcraft 18), Nine Tails of Flame (Soulcraft 24).
Pairs well with: Fox-Blooded, Fire, Shadow. Lights and lies.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
