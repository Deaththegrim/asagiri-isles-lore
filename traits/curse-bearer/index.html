Curse-Bearer

You carry a curse, or a grudge given shape, and you have learned to use what is killing you. Your Soul is sour with it, and it answers when you call.

Gives:
• Pass It On (technique): Take a curse, a poison or a wound's sickness from another into yourself, where you can carry it. Improves the next Cursecraft or Resolve action that bears a burden for someone else. Spends 25 Soul.
• Grudge Given Shape (technique): Name someone who wronged you or yours and lay a curse of ill luck on them: their next important act goes wrong. Improves the next Cursecraft action aimed at a named person. Spends 12 Soul.
• Sour Touch (cantrip): A touch leaves a day of small bad luck: a snapped strap, a slipped cup. A cantrip: free and repeatable, a small edge to the next Cursecraft or Deception action, never a strike or a wound.
• Unlocks as you train: Bitter Shield (Cursecraft 6), Curse Fed to the Beast (Cursecraft 12, with Sealed Great Beast), Empty Curse (Cursecraft 12, with Void), Grudge Mark (Cursecraft 12), Curse Off the Leash (Cursecraft 18), The Grudge of a Hundred Years (Cursecraft 24)
• Starts with Bound Relic (Cursecraft +1 while equipped)
Pairs well with: Soul, Void, Talismans, Untouchable. Cursecraft and resolve suit Soul; Void and paper carry a curse well.

Primary Attribute: Will
