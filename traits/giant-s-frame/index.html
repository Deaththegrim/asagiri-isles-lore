Giant's Frame

You can swell to twice your size for a while, and your strength with it: doorways become a problem and walls stop being one. Your clothes rarely survive. Drawing on it spends Hold.

Gives:
• Swell (technique): One limb or the whole body grows for a strike. Spends 10 Hold.
• Twice the Size (technique): For a fight you are a giant: strength, reach and target. Spends 25 Hold.
• Unlocks as you train: Giant's Grip (Open Hand 6), Earth-Shaking Stomp (Open Hand 12), Mountain Body (Open Hand 12, with Earth), Castle-Breaker (Open Hand 18), Shoulder the Gate (Open Hand 24)
Pairs well with: Oni-Blooded, Bare Fists, Steel. Size and open hands.

Primary Attribute: Body
