Shinobi

Raised in a hidden village and sold, by contract, to whoever pays. You shape your Soul through hand seals into tricks a samurai calls cowardice and a lord calls necessary: false faces, vanishing, a wire across the dark.

Gives:
• Borrowed Face (technique): Three seals and a breath: you wear another person's face and voice until struck or until you let it go. Improves the next Deception action taken while disguised. In a fight the foes lose you: your blow next turn is an ambush for 10 more damage to each.
• Cast-Off Husk (technique): As the blow lands you are no longer in the coat it lands on. Turn one hit into a miss and reappear a few paces away. Improves the next Footwork or Stealth action that escapes an attack. In a fight the hit is undone: 10 Health back, and you reappear a few paces off.
• Smoke and Step (cantrip): A pinch of smoke, one hand sign, and you are two paces from where their eyes left you. A cantrip: free and repeatable, a small edge to the next Stealth action, never a strike.
• Unlocks as you train: Silent Seal (Sealwork 6), Mirrored Seals (Sealwork 12, with Mirror Eye), Seal Through the Eye (Sealwork 12, with Far Eye), Smoke-Step Seal (Sealwork 12), Wind-Carried Blades (Hidden Arms 12, with Wind), Stillness Seal (Sealwork 18), Hundred-Seal Rain (Sealwork 24)
• Starts with Shinobi Kit (Hidden Arms +1 while equipped)
Pairs well with: Shadow, Kunai and Wire, Chain-Sickle, Outcast. A shinobi's skills (stealth, hidden arms, seals) all pay off from the ambush.

Primary Attribute: Guile
