Inherited Curse

A curse on your family for something an ancestor did. It took your parents; it is waiting for you. An ancestor's crime cursed your blood, and it has taken your family one by one. You have learned to turn it, and you are looking for the end of it.

Gives: Will +1; Cursecraft +2, Lore +2; Soul +5 (maximum and starting); technique Blood Remembers (The family curse answers its own blood: turn it outward once. Improves the next Cursecraft action. Spends 18 Soul).
Pairs well with: Soul, Void, Talismans. Cursecraft and lore; Void carries the family's curse.

Primary Attribute: Will
