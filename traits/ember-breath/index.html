Ember Breath

A breath that burns: forms of cutting fire pushed down the blade. Your chest burns when you fight, and your cuts leave the smell of a forge. Ember hunters are the Hold's front line.

Gives:
• Ember Arc (technique): A cut trailing fire that keeps burning in the wound. Improves the next Breathcraft attack. Spends 18 Soul.
Pairs well with: Steel, Fire, Katana. Fire breath down a sword.

Primary Attribute: Body
