Sunless

You keep out of daylight. You travel by night, sleep through the day, and fight best where the sun cannot follow. The vow pays you in shadow; daylight weakens you. The vow: you avoid sunlight, and in it you are weakened. Break it and its power leaves you until you atone at a vow temple.

Gives:
• Night's Own (technique): In darkness you are at your best. Improves the next action taken at night or in darkness.
Pairs well with: Shadow, Void, Cat-Blooded. Night work; Void and night eyes.

Primary Attribute: Guile
