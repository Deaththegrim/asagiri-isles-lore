Pursuit Division

The Veil Court's hunters and executioners, who chase rogue officers and the dead who run. You are good at it, and everyone knows. Other officers lower their voices around you, and the guilty run when they see the division's sash.

Gives: Guile +1; Tracking +2, Stealth +2; Soul +5 (maximum and starting); technique Veil Stride (Step half out of the living world and back: three paces become thirty. Improves the next Footwork or Stealth action that closes distance).
Pairs well with: Shadow, Void, Chain-Sickle. Tracking and stealth run the dead down; a chain holds them.

Primary Attribute: Guile
