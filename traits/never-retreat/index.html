Never Retreat

You do not step back. Not in a duel, not in a battle, not in an argument. The vow makes you a wall; it also means you need friends who know when to carry you off. The vow: you never retreat or flee from a fight you have entered. Break it and its power leaves you until you atone at a vow temple.

Gives: Body +2; Stamina +1, Resolve +1; technique Last Step Forward (Wounded and outmatched, you step in. Improves the next action taken while badly hurt).
Pairs well with: Steel, Iron Skin, Stone Breath. A wall that stands.

Primary Attribute: Body
