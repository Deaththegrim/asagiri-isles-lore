Wind

Your Soul is wind: quick, cutting, never where it was. Your techniques slice and scatter and carry far. Wind resists wind and fears the fire that eats its air.

Gives: Soulcraft +1; technique Cutting Gust (A blade of air at range. Improves the next Soulcraft action that cuts or pushes with wind. Spends 12 Soul); cantrip Breath of Wind (A puff lifts a hat, snuffs a lamp or carries a whisper across a room. A cantrip: free and repeatable, a small edge to the next Soulcraft or Stealth action, never a strike); unlocks as you train: Wind Step (Soulcraft 6), Vacuum Cut (Soulcraft 12), Wind-Carried Blades (Hidden Arms 12, with Shinobi), Typhoon Wall (Soulcraft 18), The Sky Splits (Soulcraft 24); resists wind; weak to fire.
Pairs well with: Kazakiri Clan, Gale Breath, War Fans. Quick and cutting; weak to Fire.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
