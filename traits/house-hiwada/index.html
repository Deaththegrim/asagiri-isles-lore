House Hiwada

A house of mounted archers from the plains, the oldest martial line in the isles. You rode before you walked and shot before you wrote. Your house is the oldest and poorest of the great houses and minds both.

Gives: Will +1; Archery +2, Horsemanship +2; Health +5 (maximum and starting); technique Arrow from the Saddle (Loose at full gallop and hit. Improves the next Archery action made while mounted or moving).
Pairs well with: Steel, Bow, Wind. Mounted archers: the Bow and a fast Wind Soul.

Primary Attribute: Will
