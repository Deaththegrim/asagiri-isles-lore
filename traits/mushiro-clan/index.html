Mushiro Clan

A Kagegi clan that lets a hive of insects live in its bodies and fight for it. You grew up with the hive's hum in your bones and fed it from your own Soul. Animals avoid you, and you never sleep entirely alone.

Gives:
• The Hive Answers (technique): A cloud of your insects pours from your sleeves to blind, sting, track or carry a whisper. Improves the next Beast-bond action.
Pairs well with: Soul, Earth, Bare Fists. The hive answers your Soul, and empty hands leave room for it.

Primary Attribute: Will
