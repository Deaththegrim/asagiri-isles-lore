Kurobane Clan

A Kagegi clan that binds with its own shadow: a shadow stretched across the floor holds whoever it touches. You learned to stand still for hours and to read where the light will fall. The clan's elders decide who you may marry, and your shadow sometimes moves before you do.

Gives:
• Shadow Tether (technique): Your shadow runs across the ground to another's and holds them where they stand. Strongly improves the next action that pins or holds someone in place.
Pairs well with: Shadow, Void, Kunai and Wire. A shadow-binding clan fights from the dark; Void deepens it.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
