Living Substitute

A living human handed an officer's power by one who could not finish their duty. You are both, and the court does not know what to do with you. An officer died handing you their power, and you have been both alive and an officer since. The court cannot decide whether you are a crime or a recruit.

Gives:
• Two Bodies (technique): Step out of your living body as an officer and leave it sleeping. Improves the next action taken in your soul-body.
Pairs well with: Steel, Katana, Peasant. A living body with an officer's blade; most substitutes were nobody before.

Primary Attribute: Body
