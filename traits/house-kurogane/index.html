House Kurogane

A house of heavy armour and long blades that holds the passes north of the capital. You learned to fight in armour that would crush a lesser frame and to hold a pass with a handful of men. Your house is loyal, slow, and not forgiving.

Gives: Body +1; Blade +2, Stamina +2; Health +5 (maximum and starting); technique Iron Wall (Plant your feet and take the blow on the armour. Improves the next action that holds ground or protects someone behind you).
Pairs well with: Steel, Odachi, Earth. Heavy armour and long blades; Earth holds a pass.

Primary Attribute: Body
