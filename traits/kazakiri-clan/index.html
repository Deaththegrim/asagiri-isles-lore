Kazakiri Clan

A Hayate clan that cuts with the wind: blades of air thrown from the hand or the edge of a fan. You learned to read the wind in grass and smoke before a fight. Hayate expects you to hate Kagegi, and Kagegi expects you to try.

Gives: Body +1; Soulcraft +2, Hidden Arms +2; Soul +5 (maximum and starting); technique Gale Edge (A crescent of cutting wind leaves your hand or fan and strikes at range. Improves the next Soulcraft or Hidden Arms attack at range. Spends 18 Soul).
Pairs well with: Soul, Wind, War Fans. Wind-blades thrown from a fan are the clan's whole art.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
