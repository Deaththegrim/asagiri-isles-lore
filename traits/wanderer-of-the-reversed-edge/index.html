Wanderer of the Reversed Edge

You walk the road under a vow not to kill, with a blade whose edge faces you. You killed too many once, in a war or for a master, and now you walk the road putting things right without killing anyone. The vow is harder than any fight.

Gives:
• Reversed Edge (technique): Strike with the back of the blade: the enemy falls and lives. Improves the next Blade action that disables without killing.
Pairs well with: Steel, Katana, No Killing. The reversed edge IS the No Killing vow; medicine mends what you break.

Primary Attribute: Will
