Dragon Vein

A vein of a dragon's Soul runs in you: a deep well of power, scales that rise under stress, a roar in the chest. Rain follows your temper. Shrines bow and lords want you. Drawing on it spends Hold.

Gives: Soulcraft +1, Stamina +1; technique Dragon's Breath (Breathe out your Soul as raw force. Spends 10 Hold); technique Dragon Ascendant (Scales cover you and your Soul floods every technique for a fight. Spends 25 Hold); unlocks as you train: Dragon Scales (Soulcraft 6), Rain Calling (Soulcraft 12), Storm-Blooded Change (Shapeshifting 12, with Half-Yokai), Dragon's Roar (Soulcraft 18), Dragon's Well (Soulcraft 24).
Pairs well with: Dragon-Blooded, Water, Soul. A dragon's Soul and rain.

Primary Attribute: Spirit
