Artisan: Upgrade

Raise an item you crafted one quality step: Base, Fine, Superior, Master-made, Named. Click this, then name the item as it is now: Cedar-Haft Spear to make it Fine, Fine Cedar-Haft Spear to make it Superior (square brackets optional). The party's ledger must hold the item at that quality.
Each step needs a higher craft skill (Fine 3, Superior 7, Master-made 12, Named 18), one rarer material (Fine: a refined one, Superior: a rare one, Master-made: an exotic one, Named: a legendary one) and the recipe's station. Master-made and Named need a master-grade station: Forging: Forge Guild Hall, Hibana, The Workshop; Medicine: Hayate Ridge Village; Artifice: Mask-Maker's Workshop; Warding: Bureau of Yin and Yang, Crater Rim Shrine.
Results: a basic success, success or great success raises it one step; a critical success raises it and saves the material; mixed results raises it but costs a second material; a failure changes nothing and keeps the material; a critical failure loses the material, never the item. The upgraded item replaces the old one and the narration names it.
[Level: 1]
