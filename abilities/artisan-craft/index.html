Artisan: Craft

Make an item from a recipe in your party's crafting ledger. Click this, then name the recipe: Cedar-Haft Spear (square brackets optional). You need the recipe's materials in hand and you must be at its station; the roll uses the recipe's craft (Forging, Medicine, Artifice or Warding).
Results: a critical success makes it one quality higher (Fine); a basic success, success or great success makes it at Base; mixed results makes it at Base but uses one extra material; a failure wastes nothing and makes nothing; a critical failure ruins the materials.
When it works, the narration names the finished item in square brackets, and that is the moment the ledger records it. 40 recipes are known from the start, all in The Isles Recipe Book, among them Cedar-Haft Spear, Lacquered Cuirass, Clam-Shell Salve, Mountain Root Tonic, Spring Trap, Artificer's Tool Roll, Paper Ward-Charm and Battle Sutra Strip. Your first crafting action opens The Craftsman's Ledger quest, which tracks your progress.
