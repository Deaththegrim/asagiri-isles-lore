Artisan: Design Recipe

Invent a recipe of your own and add it to your party's crafting ledger. Click this, then say what it is in plain words: a frost spear from iron spearhead, ice crystal and cedar haft. Materials (up to 5) after from, with or using; leave them out and the narrator picks two or three that fit. Add at a forge, at a shrine and so on to choose the station; leave it out and the item's own words choose the craft: talismans, charms and wards are Warding (shrine); traps, bombs, locks and hooks are Artifice (workshop); salves, tonics, teas and poisons are Medicine (apothecary); anything else is Forging (forge). The old form still works: [Frost Spear] = iron spearhead + ice crystal at a forge (never type @: the game turns it into a place name).
A name of at most 48 letters (so its Master-made and Named names still fit), and no quality word (Fine, Superior, Master-made, Named) in a new name. Soul-blades, sealed beasts and their vessels cannot be designed. A designed item starts at Base quality with one theme taken from its words (frost, fire, lightning, venom, shadow, warding, swiftness, healing); its strength is capped by quality, never by the idea, so raising it is how it grows.
Designing costs nothing and you need not be at the station. The narrator names the recipe in square brackets, and that is the moment the ledger records it. A name already in the ledger is refused. Recipes belong to the whole party.
[Level: 1]
